Here are the key items and steps FightinCowboy details to get them early:

1. The Tree Jump Skip
The Method: Head up the interior right stairs of Firelink Shrine, exit out the upper archway, and locate the curved tree trunk near the roof overhang. You need to run directly up the trunk to get a brief vertical lift and perfectly time a sharp jump to the right to land on the edge of the stone roof [00:29].

Note: This trick can take quite a few attempts to master, so be patient with the collision angles [01:06].

2. Covetous Silver Serpent Ring
Location: Once you are safely on the roof, enter the upper doorways to access the wooden rafters overlooking the inside of Firelink Shrine.

The Illusory Wall: Walk along the top of the central rafter toward the back wall. Attack or roll into the blank stone wall directly ahead to reveal an illusory wall [02:32].

The Reward: Drop down to the hidden platform directly behind the broken wall, follow it to the far end, and drop into a small room containing a chest. Inside is the Covetous Silver Serpent Ring, which grants an extra 10-11% Souls from every killed enemy—immensely powerful if grabbed right at the start of the game [02:40].

3. Solaire’s Armor (Armor of the Sun) & Picklepee Nest
The Nest: The roof skip also grants early access to the bird's nest (belonging to Picklepee/Snuggly the Crow) situated directly on the outdoor roof structure [01:39].

Trading for the Armor: To get Solaire’s iconic Armor of the Sun, you must trade a unique beverage called Siegbräu.

How to get Siegbräu: You receive this item from Siegward of Catarina a bit later in the Undead Settlement zone after teaming up with him to defeat the rogue Fire Demon [01:56].

The Trade: Stand inside the nest, open your inventory, select the Siegbräu, and select "Leave". The item will immediately swap into the Armor of the Sun [02:16].

Cowboy's Recommendation: Because you need to wait until the Undead Settlement to get the Siegbräu anyway, it's often easiest to just rush the tree jump early for the soul-boosting ring, and then return to the nest later once you've met up with Siegward [03:00].

Using these techniques helps optimize early characters by maximizing soul generation through the early zones. Let me know if you want to jump into the standard area progression next!

2. General Mechanics & Build Tips
Weight Ratio & Roll Thresholds: At [10:20], Cowboy demonstrates the critical mechanics of equipment load:

Keep your weight ratio below 70% to avoid slow, clunky "fat rolling."

Dropping even lower (around the 30-40% threshold) grants a highly fluid, longer fast-roll.

Ring of Sacrifice Trick: At [06:12], Cowboy explains that equipping a Ring of Sacrifice prevents you from losing your current soul pile if you die. It also preserves any bloodstain already on the ground from a previous death.

Embers as a Full Heal: At [13:53], Cowboy highlights a great combat tactic: save your Embers during tough boss fights and pop them mid-fight after running out of Estus Flasks to instantly restore your health to full.

3. Boss Fight: Vordt of the Boreal Valley
Before entering the fog gate at [14:47], Cowboy consumes an Ember to reveal the NPC summon sign for the Sword Master (assuming you defeated him outside Firelink Shrine earlier).

Strategy Provided:

Phase 1 [16:13]: For massive bosses like Vordt, the safest strategy is to stay directly underneath or behind him. His wide forward swings will miss completely if you wedge yourself between his back legs.

Frostbite Meter: Watch the blue status bar [16:33]. Vordt's attacks build up Frostbite, which chunks your health and slows stamina recovery if filled.

Phase 2 [16:45]: Around 50% health, Vordt will begin to buff and charge aggressively. Dodge roll through the charges rather than blocking. When he pauses to breathe his frost blast [17:35], run in from the side or behind for free, uninterrupted damage to finish the fight.
