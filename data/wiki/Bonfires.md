# Bonfires

**Bonfires** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** serve as checkpoints within the game, providing players with a safe haven that allows players to rest, recover HP, replenish Esus Flasks, and repair equipment. Bonfires also enable fast travel to other lit bonfires, making exploration and backtracking more accessible. Do note that resting at one also resurrects most defeated enemies.

### Dark Souls 3 Bonfire Information

The "Begin Journey x" option only becomes available once an [Ending](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings") has been reached. The value of "x" is based on the current [New Game Cycle +1](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus").

[Undead Match](/Hollow+Arena "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Arena") is a Multiplayer feature that becomes available once the [Champion's Bones](/Champion's+Bones "Dark Souls 3 Champion's Bones") are burned in the Bonfire, similar to an Undead Bone Shard. Upon burning, the [Hollow Arena](/Hollow+Arena "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Arena") becomes available for use, enabling organized PvP matches between other players.

The Bonfire in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") can be upgraded using [Undead Bone Shards](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard"), thus increasing its level and the amount of health regained per [Estus Flask](/Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Estus Flask") drink. You can additionally reinforce the Flask by giving an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") to [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") to increase the number of drinks available. Once ten Bone Shards have been burned (all ten can be acquired in a single New Game Cycle), selecting the option will only give the message 'Cannot kindle further'. However, Bone Shards picked up in further New Game Cycles _can_ be burned if the Bonfire is not already level 11.

In addition to Bonfires, there are three healing stations along the journey called [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup") where you can drink from a cauldron to restore your HP and FP gauges.

Regarding [Multiplayer](/Online "Dark Souls 3 Online"), you may occasionally see small Embers to the right of a [Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations") name. This indicates the presence of a Host of Embers. The location tabs in the travel menu also highlight themselves with a faint glow around the edge if there is any Hosts of Embers there also.
