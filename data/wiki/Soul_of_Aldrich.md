# Soul of Aldrich

**Soul of Aldrich** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Soul of Aldrich  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Aldrich,  
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  
>   
> Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> When Aldrich ruminated on the fading of the fire, it inspired visions of a coming age of the deep sea.   
> He knew the path would be arduous, but he had no fear. He would devour the gods himself.  
  
### Soul of Aldrich Usage

  * Can be consumed for 15000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Darkmoon Longbow](/Darkmoon+Longbow "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Longbow") or [Lifehunt Scythe](/Lifehunt+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Lifehunt Scythe") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition")

### Acquisition

  * Defeat [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods")

### Notes & Trivia

  * A smaller, golden soul can be seen inside Aldrich's dark one. It is probably Gwyndolin's, having been slowly devoured.
  * This soul, along with the [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep"), are the only souls to have a dark blue color to it, possibly showing the effect the Deep has had on them.
