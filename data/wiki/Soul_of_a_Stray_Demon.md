# Soul of a Stray Demon

**Soul of a Demon** is a Boss Soul in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of a Stray Demon  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the stray demon. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. The Stray Demon, now lacking even a trace of flame, was once the gatekeeper of Lothric.  
  
### Soul of a Stray Demon Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring)
  * Can be used to make [Boulder Heave](/Boulder+Heave)

### Soul of a Stray Demon Locations

  * Drops from the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon) above the Old Wolf of Farron bonfire

### Notes

  * Transposing [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon) will give you [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring) and [Boulder Heave](/Boulder+Heave).

### Trivia

  * Stray Demon was first seen in Dark Souls and is the first boss that the player can see right in the beginning of the game.
