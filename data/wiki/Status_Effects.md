# Status Effects

**Status Effects** in **Dark Souls 3** are those which are detrimental to players and NPCs. The following is a list of the status effects in the game, with an explanation of the effect and relevant items.

See also [Status Icons](/Status+Icons "Dark Souls 3 Status Icons"), for a list of the various Icons displayed on the HUD. Please see [Damage Types](/Damage+Types "Dark Souls 3 Damage Types") for information on other types of damage that may be dealt and resisted.

Note that all [Enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") are immune to Curse.

The status bars are represented inconsistently still: the first segment stops at 33, the second at 103, the third at 300 and the final one at 500, still the values can go further. This law applies for every status.

### [  
Hollowing](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing")  
  
---  
Effect: Player becomes progressively more hollow with each death. Unlocks free levels with [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") at certain levels. Also augments the Hollow infusion to increase Luck making Hollowing a way to increase Luck without actually leveling up. Mitigation: N/A. The option is voluntary. Cured: [Fire Keeper Soul](/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul") turned into firekeeper, then pay to be cured. To remove the visual effects, use [Purging Stone](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone"). Praying to the Statue of Velka
