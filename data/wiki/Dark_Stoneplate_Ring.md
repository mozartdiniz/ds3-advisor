# Dark Stoneplate Ring

### Dark Stoneplate Ring  
  
---  
  

> Stoneplates are symbols of true knights, and dark purple stoneplates are granted to Undead Knights.  
>   
> Increases dark damage absorption.  
  
**Dark Stoneplate Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings) in Dark Souls 3.

### Dark Stoneplate Ring Effect

## Increases Dark damage absorption as a function of current absorption

  *     * PVE: damage from non-player sources 
      * +0 = 13%
      * +1 = 17%
      * +2 = 20%
    * PVP: damage from other players 
      * +0 = 4%
      * +1 = 5%
      * +2 = 6%
  * Weight: 0.6

### Where to Find Dark Stoneplate Ring

  * In [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo), near the [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn) bonfire. Just to the left out the door, underneath all the Deacons flinging fire.

([Video](https://youtu.be/T7biHyGjECk?t=42m43s "Video") / )

  * +1 Version (NG+) : [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle), near the big white wyvern. Drop down at the broken railing to the left of the entrance to the Dragonslayer Armour boss fight then enter the building and walk past the small stairs. ()

  * +2 Version (NG++): [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrifices"), the Keep Ruins bonfire. Turn around once you load in and proceed across the bridge past the 3 enemies. Turn left and continue behind the building. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/XmG6ZSmlXC0 "\(video location\)")
