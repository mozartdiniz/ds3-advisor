### Trivia

• Most souls(consumable item) can be farmed by killing [Locusts](/Locust+Preacher "Dark Souls 3 Locust Preacher") in the swamp by [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City"), the amount and quality of the souls obtained will vary depending on which game cycle the player is at.(I.E: they'll start dropping [Soul of a Champion](/Soul+of+a+Champion "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Champion") in [NG+](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus") and beyond as a drop from their corpses). It is advised to max out [item discovery](/item+discovery "Dark Souls 3 item discovery") not only for the higher chance to get a drop but also since it's possible to get multiple items from the same corpse, being this one of the best ways to farm for hard souls that the player can turn in whenever they want.
