# Sunlight Spear

**Sunlight Spear** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Sunlight Spear  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 48  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 40 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Miracle of Gwyn, the First Lord. Hurls a sunlight spear.  
>   
> The tales of Gwyn's Archdragon hunts describe the inception of the Age of Fire.

### How to Get Sunlight Spear in Dark Souls 3

  * Acquired through [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") using [Soul of the Lords](/Soul+of+the+Lords "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Lords") and 10000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Spear Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.6) [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage").
  * Creates a second hitbox at melee range that deals an extra 67% damage.
  * Sped up at Regulation 1.08 by about a second at ~50 Virtual Dex.
  * It takes approximately 1.2 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
  * This spell is an effective upgrade of [Lightning Spear](/Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear") and [Great Lightning Spear](/Great+Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Great Lightning Spear"), but since it's only obtainable after the final boss it won't be much use in a standard playthrough.
