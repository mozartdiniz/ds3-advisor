**Corvian** is a common [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") found in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Corvian Enemy Description

  * Winged fiend protecting the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"). They often appear in groups led by a Corvian Storyteller. Such groups can be encountered at the Farron Keep perimeter, the Untended Graves, and the Grand Archives. A lone Corvian can be found sleeping at a grave outside the Church of Yorshka at Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, and a number of forlorn Corvians are seen praying before Ariandel's Chapel (Ashes of Ariandel DLC).
  * There are 2 variants: [Corvian Greatknife](/Corvian+Greatknife "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Greatknife") and [Great Corvian Scythe](/Great+Corvian+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Great Corvian Scythe"). They drop these weapons.
  * A few, more powerful red-eyed versions can be encountered as well.

### Corvian Combat Information

  * Have 359 HP
  * Corvians behave cowardly until they sprout wings. Once their wings are fully formed they will attack relentlessly.
  * It's best to take them down quickly while they are transforming.
  * Some are found sleeping. These can be woken up by attacking them or having a [Corvian Storyteller](/Corvian+Storyteller "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Storyteller") screech.
  * Can be lured?
  * Can be parried.
  * Are affected by [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport").
  * Are affected by [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"), [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic"), and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed").

### Corvian Notes & Trivia

  * Appear to be related to the Crow People of the Painted World (DS1), and by extension Velka and [Crossbreed Priscilla](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crossbreed+Priscilla "Dark Souls 3 Crossbreed Priscilla"). The descriptions on Corvian Scythe and Storyteller's Staff further the connection.
  * Their name is derived from  _corvus_ , the Latin word for "crow".
  * Corvians met in Painted World of Ariandel attacks only, when provoked.
