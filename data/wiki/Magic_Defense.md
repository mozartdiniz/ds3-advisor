# Magic Defense

## Magic Defense  
  
---  
Type | Defense  
  
**Magic Defense** in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") defines the protection your character has against magic based attacks such as soul arrows and magic enchanted weapons. This statistic does not effect the damage you receive from Physical, Fire, Lightning, and Dark.

### Dark Souls 3 Magic Defense Information

**Magic Defense** is mostly increased by leveling **Intelligence** , which is the main attribute for spell orientated characters.
