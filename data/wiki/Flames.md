# Flames

**Flames** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. These [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") allow the player to cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies"). The base game has only one Flame, with the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC") adding a second one and [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC adding a Curved Sword that doubles as a Flame.
