# Soul of Yhorm the Giant

**Soul of Yhorm the Giant** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Yhorm the Giant  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Yhorm the Giant. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  
>   
> Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> Yhorm is the descendant of an ancient conqueror, but was asked by the very people once subjugated to lead them, serving as both a weighty blade and a stone-hard shield.  
  
### Soul of Yhorm the Giant Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be transposed into [Yhorm's Great Machete](/Yhorm's+Great+Machete) or [Yhorm's Greatshield](/Yhorm's+Greatshield)

### Soul of Yhorm the Giant Locations

  * Granted for the defeat of [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+The+Giant)

### Notes

  * Player note 1
