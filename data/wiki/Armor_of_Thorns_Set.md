**Armor of Thorns Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> (Piece name) of Kirk, the notorious knight of Thorns. A dense patch of thorns grows from its surface.  
>   
> A fitting item for the murderous Kirk, for even the simple act of rolling can damage enemies when wearing this attire.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found on a body by [Rosaria's Bed Chamber](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
  * You have to have been invaded by and vanquished [Kirk](/Longfinger+Kirk "Dark Souls 3 Longfinger Kirk") on the ground floor of the [Cathedral](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
  * Do not defeat [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep") before trying to obtain this armor. Kirk will not invade afterwards.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Wearing any piece of this armor set causes your rolling and [Quickstepping](/Quickstep "Dark Souls 3 Quickstep") to do 2 damage. On top of that, wearing additional pieces causes the damage to stack. For example, equipping two pieces will do 4 damage per roll and equipping all pieces will do 8 damage per roll.
  * Any piece of this set deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage").
  * Damage to enemies and NPCs by falling on them is amplified by each piece of this set.
  * The leg piece of this set slightly increases the damage of kicking and the [Tackle](/Tackle "Dark Souls 3 Tackle") crossbow skill.
  * Useful for finishing low health enemies, or players with [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial").
  * Regardless of stats, no piece, nor combination of pieces will cause [bleed](/bleed "Dark Souls 3 bleed") damage.
  * Enemies that would normally stagger from rolling into them do not stagger while wearing this set.
  * The following is a **tested** list of items that do increase rolling damage: [Red Tearstone Ring](/Red+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Red Tearstone Ring"), [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within"), [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade").
  * The following is a **tested** list of items that do not increase rolling damage: [Flynn's Ring](/Flynn's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Flynn's Ring"), [Lloyd's Sword Ring](/Lloyd's+Sword+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lloyd's Sword Ring"), [Pontiff's Right Eye](/Pontiff's+Right+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Right Eye") ring (The armor set also does not activate [Pontiff's Right Eye](/Pontiff's+Right+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Right Eye")), [Spiked Shield](/Spiked+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Spiked Shield").
  * The amount of damage done varies based on enemy type, as well as armor. Tested on NG+7, at High Wall, unarmored enemies were taking 15 damage. While they don't stun in the traditional way while rolling into them, after enough rolls the enemy will in fact stun. This stun is similar to getting enough hits on Iudex Gundyr, where they stop moving temporarily, and take extra damage for a short time. Unarmored enemies will go as high as 21 damage per roll in this situation. It was taking on average 38 rolls to kill the standard hollows. The knight in High Wall was taking 8 to 9 damage per roll. Multiple enemies can be damaged per roll, assuming they are close together.
