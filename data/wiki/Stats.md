# Stats

**Stats** in **Dark Souls 3** are what determines a player's health, damage, stamina, etc. The Stats in Dark Souls 3 are similar to previous Souls games. If you would like to re-spec your character, you will have to visit [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth).  

  * Please see [Status Icons](/Status+Icons "Status Icons") to understand the icons displayed under your healthbar when you have an active effect.
