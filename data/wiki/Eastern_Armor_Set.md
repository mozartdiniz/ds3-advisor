**Eastern Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Distinctive armor made in an Eastern land.
> 
> The exquisite craftmanship and artistic design made these prized pieces in the collection of any nobleman.
> 
> Offers excellent damage absorption, particularly from slashing attacks from katanas, which are commonly encountered threats in battles fought in the East.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Bought from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 23,000 souls after giving her the [Easterner's Ashes](/Easterner's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Easterner's Ashes") found in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), beneath the Silver Knights.

### Notes and Tips

  * The armor once belonged to Shiva of the East, a character from the original Dark Souls.
