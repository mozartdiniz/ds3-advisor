# Curse

### [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse")  
  
---  
  
###   
  
**Curse** is a [status effect](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

### Dark Sousl 3 Curse Information

  * Effect: Instant death once the status bar fills up.  

    * No lasting penalty beyond one death, compared to the original dark souls.
  * [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") investment provides more curse resistance compared to levelling other attributes.

### Dark Souls 3 Curse Sources

  * [Basilisks](/Basilisks "Dark Souls 3 Basilisks") in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") and [Demon Ruins](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").
  * [Deep Accursed](/Deep+Accursed "Dark Souls 3 Deep Accursed") in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") and [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") inflicts curse.

  * [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep") will summon priests who slowly fills the room with curse clouds.
  * [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") has a crystal dragon breath attack that inflicts curse status.
  * Cursed books (i.e. [Clawed Curse](/Clawed+Curse "Dark Souls 3 Clawed Curse")) in [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). In this area the curse status can also be mitigated by dipping your head in one of the available wax basins.
  * [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") has a curse inducing spit.

### Dark Souls 3 Curse Mitigation

  * [Cursebite Ring](/Cursebite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Cursebite Ring")
  * [Fleshbite Ring](/Fleshbite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fleshbite Ring")
  * [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit")
  * [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection")
  * [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat")
  * Armors with high Curse resistance  

    * [Prayer Set](/Prayer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Prayer Set")
    * [Jailer Set](/Jailer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Jailer Set")

### Dark Souls 3 Curse Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * The [Ring of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice") has no effect if the player dies from exposure to Curse. The ring won't even break when equipped, but a bloodstain will be at the location of death like with any other normal death.
  * Despite what its description may seem to suggest, [Purging Stones](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone") do not remove the curse status buildup. They only remove [Hollowing](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") buildup.
