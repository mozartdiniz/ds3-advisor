# Deep Soul

## Deep Soul  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 6  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 12 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Deep Soul** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery of Archdeacon Royce and his deacons, said to have been imparted to them by McDonnell of the Boreal Valley.   
>   
> Fires dark soul dregs.   
>   
> Souls which swell from the deep pursue their target, drawn towards life. 

### How to Get Deep Soul in Dark Souls 3

  * Transposable from the [Soul of the Deacons of the Deep](/Soul+of+the+Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Deacons of the Deep")

### Dark Souls 3 Deep Soul Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (Spell buff x 1) damage.
  * This spell deals dark damage, and as such will be slightly more powerful when cast with a dark-friendly catalyst with high Intelligence and Faith.
  * A pure Intelligence build with no Faith investment can still cast this spell with any other staff, and it will only deal 30-50 less damage.
  * This spell has a very fast cast time, long range and good homing properties, but deals marginal damage.
  * The dreg starts off slow but moves faster the farther it travels.
