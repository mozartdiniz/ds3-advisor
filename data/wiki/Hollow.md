**Hollow** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Hollow Enemy Description

  * Usually found praying or wandering the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") and the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").
  * Shirtless or wearing ragged robes and are usually unarmed or wielding a [Broken Straight Sword](/Broken+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Broken Straight Sword").
  * Most cower in fear as you approach.

### Hollow Combat Information

  * Can be killed in essentially one hit
  * Can be backstabbed

### Hollow Notes & Trivia

  * Hit by deep axe, supposed AR of 200, the damage was about half, possibly dark resistant. (Dies in 1 hit anyways)
    * Testing using Claymore+3 (295AR) with Human Pine Resin (+95 Dark Damage) at STR-40/DEX-40/INT-10/FAI-10 on v1.35 resulted in 53 Dark absorption, .5 less than an average elemental absorption of 53.5. Therefore, not resistant to Dark in a way that matters
