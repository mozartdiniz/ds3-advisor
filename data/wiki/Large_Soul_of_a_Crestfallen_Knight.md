# Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight

**Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight** is a [Soul](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

### Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight  
  
---  
  

> Large soul found in the corpse of a crestfallen knight. Use to acquire a large amount of souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight Usage

  * Use to gain 20,000 souls.

### Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight Locations

  * 2x at [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")
    * Past the bridge with the final [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") encounter, go up to the room with the [Scholars](/Grand+Archives+Scholar "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives Scholar"), there will be a soul in the back of the room.
    * On the steps with the [Black Hand NPC trio](/Black+Hand+trio "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand trio"), very hard to miss.
  * 1x at [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak")
    * Near the Great Belfry bonfire. In the middle of the area with the [Serpent-Men](/Serpent-man "Dark Souls 3 Serpent-man"). 
  * 1x at The [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel")
    * From the Snowy Mountain Pass bonfire, progress to the cliffside where ground gives out. Where you land, the soul will be next to a tree. 
  * 1x at [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City")
    * Complete the "[Show Your Humanity](/Show+Your+Humanity "Dark Souls 3 Show Your Humanity")" puzzle. Eventually up a ladder.
