# Soul of Rosaria

**Soul of Rosaria** is a Special Soul in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Rosaria  
  
---  
  

> The soul of Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth, stolen by Ringfinger Leonhard. Return this to her extant corpse, and mother Rosaria will spring back to life. As if nothing had ever happened.  
  
### Soul of Rosaria Usage

  * Can be consumed for 5000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Bountiful Sunlight](/Bountiful+Sunlight).

### Soul of Rosaria Locations

  * Dropped by [Leonhard the Ringfinger](/Leonhard), after using [Black Eye Orb](/Black+Eye+Orb) in the room after Aldrich boss fight.
  * The Soul of Rosaria will not drop if Rosaria is killed by the player. She will simply appear dead until the player teleports away and back again.

### Notes

  * There is no benefit to restoring Rosaria using her soul, as her being dead does not prevent you from offering pale tongues, redistributing attributes or changing appearance.
