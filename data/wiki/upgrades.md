# Upgrades

**Upgrades** are how the player improves their [Weapons](/Weapons) and [Shields](/Shields) in Dark Souls 3. The player will need to provide [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre) with [Upgrade Materials](/Upgrade+Materials "Upgrade Materials") in order to be able to [Infuse](/Infusion) or reinforce their equipment. Different items unlock different [Infusions](/Infusion), but the player will be able to reinforce their gear from the beginning. Infusing [Weapons](/Weapons) and [Shields](/Shields) will typically remove any initial scaling the item may have in exchange for a static elemental damage and/or altered scaling based around a specific stat. Players will be able to undo infusions using a [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone) without losing reinforcement levels. Contrary to past installments in the series, [Armor](/Armor) is not upgradeable. Note that to get the achievement (Steam) all infusions must be used on the same character.

### Upgrade Materials

In Dark Souls 3 you can improve the damage/protection (and potential Scaling) on your [Weapons](/Weapons) and [Shields](/Shields). To do this you must have the appropriate amount of the relevant [Upgrade Materials](/Upgrade+Materials), [Souls](/Souls) and access to [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre). Titanite Shards will reinforce a Weapon or Shield up to +3, Large Titanite Shards will reinforce the item from +4 to +6, Titanite Chunks will reinforce the item from +7 to +9, and a Titanite Slab will be required to reinforce to +10. Titanite Scales and Twinkling Titanite are used to reinforce special weapons to +4, but will similarly require a Titanite Slab to reach their final upgrade of +5.

###  Infusions

In Dark Souls 3 you can infuse many different Weapons and Shields with Gems that change the properties, damage type or Scaling. In order to infuse a Weapon or Shield to your chosen type you must: 1) have found and give the appropriate Coal to Blackmsith Andre, 2) have a Gem of the chosen type, and 3) have the required Souls. The exceptions here are Refined, Raw or Fire which require no Coal to be given. You can use a Shriving Stone if you wish to remove the Infusion from your Weapon or Shield (this must also be done at Blacksmith Andre). Again, not all Weapons and Shields can be Infused so check the Weapons pages to verify which can and can't.

These weapons **CANNOT** be infused: Bows, Greatbows, Crossbows, Chimes, Talismans, Staves, Pyromancy Flames and any weapon that upgrades with Twinkling Titanite or Titanite Scale.

**Spell / Item Buffing:** Infusions which add any non-physical damage or status effect remove the ability to buff the weapon with spells or consumables. Only the following infusions allow buffing: Raw, Refined, Heavy, Sharp and Hollow.

### Soul Transposition

**Soul Transposition** in Dark Souls 3 is the process of converting a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") into a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") or [Spell](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic"). In order to create items you must have the specific Boss Soul, you must have the [Transposing Kiln](/Transposing+Kiln "Dark Souls 3 Transposing Kiln") which drops from [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood"), and you must speak with [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Below is a list of items that can be created through Soul Transposition, what Boss Souls are required for them and how much it costs. 

## Soul Transposition List

Note that through Ludleth, [Twin Princes' Greatsword](/Twin+Princes'+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Twin Princes' Greatsword") can be created by fusing [Lothric's Holy Sword](/Lothric's+Holy+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Lothric's Holy Sword") and [Lorian's Greatsword](/Lorian's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Lorian's Greatsword"). 

**Boss Souls** and other special souls in Dark Souls 3 are powerful items that can be converted into new [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Magic](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic"), or [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") from [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland"). They may also be consumed to gain a large amount of souls.
