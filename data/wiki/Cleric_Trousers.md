**Cleric Trousers** is a [Legging](/Leggings) in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Cleric Set](/Cleric+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Trousers worn by a cleric turned undead. Typical attire for blue-robed travelers.
> 
> It is said that they were entrusted with a duty, still its nature is yet to be revealed."

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a corpse at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement): At the graveyard with the falling spears, just ahead of the _Dilapidated Bridge_ bonfire.[ (video location)](https://youtu.be/Hz8WP9D6zQI " \(video location\)")
