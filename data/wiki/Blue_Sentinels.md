# Blue Sentinels

## Blue Sentinels  
  
---  
_Faded sheepskin parchment depicting the Darkmoon and a sword. Equip to pledge oneself to the Blue Sentinels covenant._  
  
_When a member of the Way of Blue is threatened by a dark spirit, the Blue Sentinels, in compliance with an ancient accord, assume the form of blue spirits, and help to root out the leader._  
  
_Summoning takes place automatically while this is equipped._  
  
**Blue Sentinels** is a [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3 "Dark Souls 3"). The covenant can be gained by equipping the item Blue Sentinels which is obtained from [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") and [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed"). While a member of the Covenant, Blue Sentinels will be summoned to help you defend against invaders.

### Blue Sentinels Information

  * You must obtain the item "Blue Sentinels" and equip it.
  * Given by [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") and [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed") at the Halfway Fortress bonfire by the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices").
  * If you missed the opportunity to pick up the covenant from Horace the first time, you may pick it up later at Firelink Shrine after defeating the Deacons of the Deep.
  * Turn in items in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"), to [Company Captain Yorshka](/Company+Captain+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Company Captain Yorshka")

###  Notes

  * When a Darkmoon/Blue Sentinel spawns, a floating red eye will appear locating an invader or Blue spirit, depending on whether the player is an invader or Blue spirit. This floating red eye that locates invaders was previously a miracle in Dark Souls II, called Unveil.

### Effects of covenant

  * Blue Sentinels will be summoned into the worlds of those in the [Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue "Dark Souls 3 Way of Blue") to defend them from hostile invaders. This behavior is shared with the [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon"). 
  * Inexplicably Blue Sentinels will NOT be summoned to protect a player against invading Aldrich Faithful defending Anor Londo or Watchdogs of Farron in the swamps.
  * The Blue Sentinels phantom color is light blue.
  * If the Host of Embers enters a boss room, the Blue Sentinel will be rewarded with a [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept") and be returned to their world. They will NOT remain to challenge the boss.
  * If the invader is killed by the giant with the greatbow (on the high tower) in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), the Blue Sentinel will NOT be rewarded a [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept"). However, they will still get the same victory message. 
  * The Matchmaking Formula is Covenant Dependent:  
  

Condition | Min | Max  
---|---|---  
Summoning a White Phantom, [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight"), [Mound-Maker](/Mound-Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Maker") or no covenant | Host - 10 - (Host * 0.1) | Host + 10 + (Host * 0.1)  
Defending Summons of [Blade of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon") or [Blue Sentinel](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") | Host - 20 - (Host * 0.2) | Host + (Host * 0.1)  
  
### Rank Requirements

This Covenant shares Ranks with the [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon"). To rank up the player will need [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept"). To acquire [Proof of a Concord Kep](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept")[t](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept") a player must be summoned while having their Blue Sentinel badge equipped and successfully defeat an invading phantom. If the player defeats more than one invading phantom while summoned they will acquire more [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept"). The player is sent home when all invaders are banished. They can also be received as a drop from [Silver Knight](/Silver+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight") in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") (Rare). The rank requirements are additive, meaning you only have to earn a total of 30, not 10 and then another 30, to obtain all covenant rewards.

Rank | Requirement | Rewards  
---|---|---  
0 | Initial | None  
1 | 10 | [Darkmoon Ring ](/Darkmoon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Ring") (Grants 2 additional attunement slots.)  
2 | 30 | [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade") (Miracle)  
  
To rank up the player must turn these into [Company Captain Yorshka](/Company+Captain+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Company Captain Yorshka") in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). She is located in a tower across an invisible walkway near the first bonfire there.

### Trivia

  * The Blue Sentinels first appeared in [Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+2+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Dark Souls 2 Wiki"), when they were led by [Targray](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Blue+Sentinel+Targray "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinel Targray").
  * [The DS2 Blue Sentinels](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") served as the replacement for [The Blades of The Darkmoon](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Blade+of+the+Dark+Moon "Dark Souls 3 Blade of the Dark Moon").
