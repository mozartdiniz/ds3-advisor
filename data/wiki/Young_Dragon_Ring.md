# Young Dragon Ring

### Young Dragon Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring given in Vinheim, home of sorcery, when newly ordained as a sorcerer. Boosts sorceries. Apropos to the Dragon School, the seal depicts an everlasting dragon. A young dragon presages the great length of the journey to mastery."  
  
**Young Dragon Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**. 

### Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring Effect

  * Boosts [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") damage by 12%
  * Weight: 0.7

### How to Get Young Dragon Ring in Dark Souls 3

  * Starting equipment for the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer")
  * Given by [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") after purchasing any 3 [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"), giving him any 1 Scroll and playing as any [Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes") but a [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer").

### Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * There is a stronger version ([Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring](/Bellowing+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring")) that grants 20%.
  * This ring stacks multiplicatively with the [Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring](/Bellowing+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Does not benefit weapon buffs in any way (not including the catalyst itself).
