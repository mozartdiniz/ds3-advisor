**Yellowfinger Heysel** is a NPC in **Dark Souls 3**.

## Yellowfinger Heysel Information

_"Heysel appears to be a Scholar of Xanthous and sorcery"_

  * Invades in Crucifixion Woods and Farron Keep swamps.
  * Can give you the "**Proper Bow** " Gesture (see the _Location section_ below _)._
  * "_You can be like Yellowfinger and believe that all share a camaradery, but do not force your views on the rest of us._ "   
-Leonhard in Rosaria's Bedchamber
  * [Great Farron Dart](/Great+Farron+Dart "Dark Souls 3 Great Farron Dart") -Entrusted to the leader of the Legion's acolytes, and apparently a sorcery of his daughter, Heysel, that was refined by a Crystal Sage.

### Location

  * **First Invasion** : the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"). She invades as a dark spirit once you approach the sunlit tree in the deep water near the ruins and the giant crabs. 
    * This invasion can be avoided by not going into the swamp. The alternative route to [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") is through the broken building near [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage"), then go out through the broken wall near a staircase and take the path down to where you find the [Golden Falcon Shield](/Golden+Falcon+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Golden Falcon Shield").
  * **Second invasion** : after the [Farron Keep](/Farron+keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron keep") bonfire. She spawns near the cave where you find the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll"), behind the extinguishable fire near the [Basilisks](/Basilisks "Dark Souls 3 Basilisks"). [Video Example](https://youtu.be/qTXgmv4PFAE "Yellowfinger Heysel's Location in Farron Keep")
  * If you offer a Pale Tongue to [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") (this fails [Sirris'](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") questline), Yellowfinger Heysel will be summonable in the Farron Keep swamp. Her summon sign is after the Keep Ruins Bonfire: Go down the ramp, and to the right- The sign is outside the tower with the five slugs inside. She will give you the "**Proper Bow** " Gesture. 
    * As long as the player has offered at least one tongue to [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth"), she can be summoned as a friendly phantom without having to defeat her when she invades you. (N.b. If you already have a player summoned, Heysel's summon sign may not appear.)
  * Her items can still be obtained if her Red Phantom is not vanquished before defeating the Abyss Watchers. (she will not invade there anymore, and cannot be summoned as a white phantom.) The peaceful worm next to Rosaria, found in Rosaria's Bed Chamber soon after defeating the lords of Cinder, drops both of Heysel's items when killed. 
    * It's possible to get her set twice, the worm will still drop her items even if you've already obtained them by vanquishing her phantom.

### Drops

  * Souls (CW): NG (2,180)
  * Souls (FK): NG (7,196)
  * 2 Estus refills. Sometimes drops 3 or 4.
  * Weapons: [Heysel Pick](/Heysel+Pick "Dark Souls 3 Heysel Pick")
  * Armor: [Xanthous Crown](/Xanthous+Crown "Dark Souls 3 Xanthous Crown")

### Combat Information

  * Wears the [Obscuring Ring](/Obscuring+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Obscuring Ring"), hiding her at long distance.
  * Wields [Heysel Pick](/Heysel+Pick "Dark Souls 3 Heysel Pick") (RH) and [Light Crossbow](/Light+Crossbow "Dark Souls 3 Light Crossbow") (LH).
  * Casts [Great Farron Dart](/Great+Farron+Dart "Dark Souls 3 Great Farron Dart"), [Farron Hail](/Farron+Hail "Dark Souls 3 Farron Hail") and very rarely Chameleon.
  * Weak to Fire Damage.
  * Strong against Magic, Lightning, Frost and Dark damage.
  * Immune to Poison/Toxic.
  * Can heal using Estus 2 times. In co-op she may get unlimited estus as a bug making her potentially the toughest NPC invader.

###  Other

  * Killing Abyss Watchers will fail the gesture part of this questline!
  * It is not possible to both summon Yellowfinger Heysel and continue Sirris of the Sunless Realm's quest, meaning that in any one new game cycle, a player can either obtain the Proper Bow emote; or the Sunless, Sunset, and Chain Mirrah sets, the Dragonslayer Axe, Sunset shield, and the Cat Ring. Therefore, Proper Bow is among the rarer seen emotes, with many players waiting until NG+ to obtain it.

### Trivia

  * After progressing past a certain point in the game, you will find a non-hostile [man-grub](/man-grub "Dark Souls 3 man-grub") monster near Rosaria's bed. When killed, it drops the Xanthous Crown and Heysel Pick, strongly indicating the grub was Heysel herself. The fact that she no longer invades once this grub appears further hints at this. It's possible that Heysel, having been defeated by the player repeatedly, chose to remake herself too many times in an attempt to become stronger and became a mangrub. It's also possible that, due to her sense of camaraderie, she became a mangrub willingly as a final testament of devotion to the covenant.
  * In Dark Souls I, Dusk of Oolacile teaches Proper Bow. Heysel, as a xanthous scholar of Ooacile's magic, likely learned of Proper Bow through her research.
  * Likewise likely acquired through this research is the distinctive mushroom shape of the headdress, which strongly resembles Elizabeth the Mushroom of Oolacile, and the way the yellow robe is worn, as a cloak to one side, which is extremely similar to the yellow cloaks worn by the Scarecrow enemies that tend the Royal Wood of Oolacile in Dark Souls I.
  * This NPC may originate from a similar school of thought to [King Jeremiah](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/King+Jeremiah "Dark Souls 3 King Jeremiah") as they share a similar Xanthous set. 
    * Despite the described nature of Xanthous Scholars as students of the magic of Oolacile in Dark Souls III, it is unclear if King Jeremiah was a Xanthous Scholar himself, as the yellow robes of his set are stated to be of mysterious origin and he himself uses pyromancy, not magic. It is possible that this association of the robes with the study of lost magic came about after King Jeremiah's time.
  * Heysel may also reference the Old Monk boss fight from Demon's Souls as she wears a headwrap resembling the robe.
  * If you max out the amount of pale tongues you can possibly give Rosaria, you will not be able to complete this questline ever again from that point forward (even after your current NG, as that action is irrevocable and is carried on to the next playthrough).
