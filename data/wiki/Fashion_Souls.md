# Fashion Souls

* * *

**Fashion Souls** is a term used to describe aesthetically pleasing and cosplay outfits that show player creativity in approaching [builds](/builds "Dark Souls 3 builds") in Dark Souls 3. This page is dedicated to showcasing such creations. Please add your own by replicating the formatting below.
