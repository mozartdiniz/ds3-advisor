# Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt

**Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Vordt of the Boreal Valley. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extact its true strength. Vordt served as an outrider knight, never far from the fleeting dancer.  
  
### Usage

  * Can be consumed for 2000 Souls.
  * Can be used to make [Vordt's Great Hammer](/Vordt's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Vordt's Great Hammer").
  * Can be used to make [Pontiff's Left Eye](/Pontiff's+Left+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Left Eye").

### Locations

  * Reward for defeating [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Vordt of the Boreal Valley").

### Notes

  * Player note 1
