**Silver Knight Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

  
  
  
  

> (Piece Name) of the Silver Knights allegiant to the royals of old.
> 
> It is said that even after the family's passing, the knights continued to watch over their manor, and the ruined cathedral.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by the [Silver Knights](/Silver+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley ](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")(after [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn")) and [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo").
