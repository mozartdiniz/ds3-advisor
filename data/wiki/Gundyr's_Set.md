**Gundyr's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Ancient armor of a set of cast iron armor, belonging to Champion Gundyr. Modeled after a former king.
> 
> Gundyr, or the Belated Champion, was bested by an unknown warrior. He then became sheath to a coiled sword in the hopes that someday, the first flame would be linked once more.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Bought from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 36,000 souls after defeating [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr").

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The former king mentioned in the armor description could be Old King Doran from Demon's Souls, the helmet being reminiscent of Doran's own helmet and the title of Doran itself.
  * Leaks from quality assurance testers of the alpha version suggest that Iudex Gundyr was originally going to play the role of [Oceiros](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King"), using that name, in [The Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"), with [Oceiroso](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") appearing in a cut area called "God's Grave". Gundyr would still be a character, but look and play like [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") \- (credit darksouls3theorist and Lance McDonald).
