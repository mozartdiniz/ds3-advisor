# Great Magic Shield

## Great Magic Shield  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 60  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Intelligence  
**Duration** | 30 seconds  
**Type** | Buff  
  
**Great Magic Shield** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery which improves upon Magic Shield. Greatly reinforces left shield with magic. 
> 
> Only sorcerer-swordsmen with special duties are permitted the use of this spell, which temporarily grants even the smallest of shields fortitude more akin to that of a greatshield.

### How to Get Great Magic Shield in Dark Souls 3

  * Dropped from the Corpse-grub in its cell inside [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") near the first bonfire. To get there, head straight out from the first bonfire and drop down to the lower level. The cell containing the grub will be to your left, as well as one of the jailers ([video location](http://youtu.be/4pfiioth6f4 "video location")).

### Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Shield Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases [Shield](/shields "Dark Souls 3 shields") Stability by 50%. 
    * Shields with 67 or more Stability will reach 100 Stability, resulting in no stamina loss from blocking attacks.
  * Increases damage absorption of shields to 100%.
  * Lasts 30 seconds and is affected by [ Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"). 
    * With Lingering Dragoncrest Ring, duration increases to 39 seconds.
    * Lingering Dragoncrest Ring +1 = 40.5 seconds.
    * Lingering Dragoncrest Ring +2 = 42 seconds.
    * The buff lasts half as long as [Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield"), holding it back from being a true upgrade.
  * _[Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield") and [Great Magic Shield](/Great+Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Shield") CAN buff [Simple](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem") and [Blessed](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") Infused shields._
  * Can't be used with a catalyst in the left hand to buff a shield equipped in the right hand.
  * Counts as a [Shield Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") and will overwrite [Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield").
  * The effect will dissipate if you two-hand your right weapon or switch the shield out. You can still buff your right-hand weapon with the use of resins.
