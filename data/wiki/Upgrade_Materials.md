# Upgrade Materials

**Ore** in Dark Souls 3 are items that can be used to improve [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") and [Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") via [Upgrades](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"). These are done at the [Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") via the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre"). In contrast to previous _Dark Souls_ games, now [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") isn't upgradable at all.

Although you can drop Titanite and [Gems](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion"), other players cannot take them making trades very difficult.
