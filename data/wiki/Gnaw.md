# Gnaw

**Gnaw** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Gnaw  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 15  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Dark miracle taught in the Cathedral of the Deep.   
>   
> Summons insect swarm to feast on foes.   
>   
> These insects which lurk in the Deep have tiny jaws lined with fangs to tear open the skin and burrow into the flesh in the blink of an eye, causing intense bleeding. 

### How to Get Gnaw in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") or [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 2,000 souls when you give her the [Deep Braille Divine Tome](/Deep+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Deep Braille Divine Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Gnaw Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 0.9) damage.
  * This Spell Inflicts Dark Damage and Bleed Buildup.
  * The Dark Damage scales with the Spellbuff of the chosen Catalyst: 
    * Dark Miracle Catalysts ([Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman") & [Caitha's Chime](/Caitha's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Caitha's Chime")) with high Intelligence and Faith will maximize the Dark Damage.
  * Applies 130 Points of Bleed buildup.  

    * This bleed buildup is a fixed value, and does not scale with Luck, Faith, or Intelligence.
  * Fires a single spinning "swarm" projectile, [Dorhy's Gnawing](/Dorhy's+Gnawing "Dark Souls 3 Dorhy's Gnawing") fires two, although they are not separate projectiles. 
    * Dorhys' Gnawing is an upgrade to this spell.
  * Sped up in regulation 1.08 and again in 1.11.
  * Approx 1.4 secods to cast at max casting speed.
