**Anri's Straight Sword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Sword precious to Anri, another Unkindled. The dullest type of blade found in the ruined land of Astora.  
>   
> Only, it was once the sword of an earnest noble figure, and its attacks are boosted by that elusive, essential property unique to humans: luck.  
>   
> Skill: Stance  
> While in stance, use normal attack to break a foe's guard from below, and strong attack to slash upwards with a forward lunge.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be infused but can be buffed
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Anri's Straight Sword has a motion value of 200 on backstab, 250 on riposte, and 60+225 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a straight sword does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").

  * Anri's Straight Sword has a range of 34.157xl on the 1h R1 33.5592xl on the 2h R1.
  * Anri's Straight Sword has thrusting R2 attacks.
  * Naturally possesses HP regen and Faith scaling for physical damage. 
    * Does 10% bonus damage vs. reanimated. This weapon, like [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") weapons and the [Blessed Weapon](/Blessed+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Weapon") spell, will prevent skeletons from respawning.
    * Regen effect is 2 HP / tick. The tick is 2 seconds long at +5.
    * Equipping the sword will instantly tick the health regen, rapidly equipping and unequipping the sword will offer faster health regeneration.
  * Naturally possesses approximately A scaling in Luck. 
    * This weapon has just under 400 AR at the minimum requirements and 40 Luck.
    * The weapon is used in some Speedruns for its decent damage and health regeneration exploit.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Drops from [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") if killed or if you complete his/her quest line. If you complete Anri's questline, the sword will be given to you by [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Note that killing Anri will disrupt [Yuria's](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") quest line and cause her to leave.
  * If you married Anri in the Darkmoon tomb, it will be in the same location as the cutscene.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
