**Karla's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> (Piece Name) of the dark witch Karla.
> 
> A pointed hat is the signature of a heretical sorceress. Karla, however always kept it close. (Hat)  
>   
> This stained (Piece Name) is torn and odorous, telling of a long journey and even longer imprisonment. It would be madness to wear such a thing. (Coat & Gloves)

  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 23000 souls after giving her [Prisoner Chief's Ashes](/Prisoner+Chief's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner Chief's Ashes") (found in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon")).
  * Also drops if you kill [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla").
