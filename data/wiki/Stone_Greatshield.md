**Stone Greatshield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. [Greatshields](/Greatshields "Dark Souls 3 Greatshields") usually weigh a decent amount and provide the best protection in the game. They are often used by players who have high [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength"), as they require a good amount in order to use.

> A mossy stone disc. Though terribly heavy, it can serve as a shield.  
>   
> The disc is a relic of ancient magic, and one can practically feel the power flowing through it.  
>   
> Skill: Shield Bash  
> Without lowering your guard, strike the enemy with the shield to knock them back or stagger them. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Stone Greatshield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed and Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Provides an average block of 78.4%. Phenominally high block rates for its weight, and also handles lightning damage well. Extremely efficient general-use greatshield. 
  * Has 75 stability at +10.
  * This greatshield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Stone+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Stone Greatshield")_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Has a chance to be dropped by [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru") in the swamp in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep").
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=2h4m3s "Video location") (fextralife video starting at 2h 4m 3s)
  * Can be easily farmed at the Keep Ruins bonfire. Kill the three Ghru on the bridge, then fire at the Elder Ghru from the bridge with a greatbow. An upgraded Greatbow with 40 str/40 dex kills each Elder Ghru in 2 shots. Drop down and collect any items.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
