# Thunder Stoneplate Ring

### Thunder Stoneplate Ring  
  
---  
  

> Stoneplates are symbols of true knights, and yellow stoneplates are granted to those who would become dragons. Increases lightning damage absorption."  
  
**Thunder Stoneplate Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings) in **Dark Souls 3**. 

### Thunder Stoneplate Ring Information

  * Increases Lightning damage absorption as a function of current absorption

    * PVE: damage from non-player sources 
      * +0 = 13%
      * +1 = 17%
      * +2 = 20%
    * PVP: damage from other players 
      * +0 = 4%
      * +1 = 5%
      * +2 = 6%
  * Weight: 0.6

### Where to Find Thunder Stoneplate Ring

  * [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak): From the Dragon-Kin Mausoleum bonfire, proceed upstairs towards the gatehouse and go right. Proceed downstairs and and through the passage with arches and an outside view. In the room at the end, head right to an area with two square pillars and wooden scaffolding. Behind the right pillar there's a ladder that leads up to the ring.
  * +1 Version (NG+) Can be found in the area where there are many pots in [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus) shortly after the first [Skeleton Ball](/Skeleton+Ball "Skeleton Ball")()
  * +2 Version (NG++) Lothric Castle: Enter the room near to the Dragonslayer Armour boss fog and there will be an exit to the left that leads to a balcony. To the right is the Red Tearstone ring, to the left is a hole in the rail. Drop down to the rooftop below and head right. The ring will be at the far end of the roof on the right. ()[ (video location)](https://youtu.be/1YaiwEi7EN4 " \(video location\)")

### 

### Notes

  * Useful to fight against lightning enemies, like the Nameless King and Dragonslayer Armour.
  * In PVP, it can absorb a lot of damage from Faith builds, as most offensive miracles deal Lighting damage and they probably run either a Lothric Knight Greatsword or Lightning infused weaponry.
