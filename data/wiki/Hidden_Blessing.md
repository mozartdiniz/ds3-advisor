# Hidden Blessing

**Hidden Blessing** is a [consumable](/consumables) in Dark Souls 3. It is one of the [Burial Gifts](/Burial+Gifts) available to players.

### Hidden Blessing  
  
---  
  

> Holy water blessed by the Queen of Lothric. Fully restores FP. There is a grave in Lothric that sees no visitors, a dark place where rootless warriors rest. The Queen of Lothric alone cared to wish the poor souls good fortune.  
  
### Hidden Blessing Usage

  * Fully restores FP.

### Where to Find Hidden Blessing

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts).
  * 1x sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid) for 1000 souls (After giving her [Dreamchaser Ashes](/Dreamchaser+Ashes))
  * 1x found in [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Untended Graves") near where the [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Titanite Shard") was found in the tutorial area.
  * 1x sold by [Unbreakable Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Unbreakable Patches") (After sending him to Lothric Castle to look for Greirat on his third outing)

### Notes

  * Can be dropped in [The Crow's](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow) nest for the [Thank You Carving](/Thank+you+carving) item.
