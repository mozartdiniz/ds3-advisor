**Silver Knight Ledo** is a [NPC](/NPCs "NPC") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Silver Knight Ledo Information

  * Killable.
  * Located in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\)). Invades the player, regardless of ember state.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer).

### Location

  * Up a ladder and on a pathway inside a ruined castle tower near the swamp below [Shira](/Shira) in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City). 

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (17,448), NG+ (17,448), NG+2 (??), NG+3 (??), NG+4 (??), NG+5 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+7 (??)
  * [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer)

### Combat Information

  * Wields: [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer) (RH) and [Silver Knight Shield](/Silver+Knight+Shield) (LH)
  * You can lead him to the ledge outside, target lock, and use poison mist/ toxic mist to kill him. Storyteller's Staff, and range weapons work as well, but you must be locked on to hit him.
  * Resistant to fire. Neutral to Dark damage.
  * Backstabs can be hard to land if he uses the WA, as the stone shards can hit and stagger you before the backstab animation starts.

### Dialogue

None. He does not speak.

### Trivia

  * Ledo previously had his own unique armor set, but it was cut from the game.
