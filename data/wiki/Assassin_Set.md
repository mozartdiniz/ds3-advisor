**Assassin Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Soft leather armor worn by assassins who lurk in the shadows.
> 
> The thick leather offers reasonable absorption without creating any undesired noise. A well-crafted piece of protection.
> 
> Critical spots are reinforced with metal.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Assassin](/Assassin "Dark Souls 3 Assassin")
  * This is sold by [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for 6000 souls. After rescuing him from his cell in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") and giving him [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's bone") you will later be able to send him to pillage [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). Check back after defeating your next boss and he will sell it. (see [Greirat's page](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for more details on his questline)
