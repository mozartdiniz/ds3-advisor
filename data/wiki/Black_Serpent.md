# Black Serpent

**Black Serpent** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Black Serpent  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 19  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence/ 15 Faith  
  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Pyromancy discovered from the Abyss by High Lord Wolnir that inspired the black arts of the grave wardens.   
>   
> Releases undulating black flames that trace the ground.  
>   
> Be it sorcery or pyromancy, all techniques that infringe on humanity lead to the same place. That is to say, they all seek a will of their own.

### How to Get Black Serpent in Dark Souls 3

  * Transposed from the [Soul of High Lord Wolnir](/Soul+of+High+Lord+Wolnir "Dark Souls 3 Soul of High Lord Wolnir").

### Dark Souls 3 Black Serpent Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.65) damage.
  * Has some very peculiar homing properties, they will most of the time home fairly well, but can outright swerve away from targets.
  * If an enemy player manages to [Spell Parry](/Spell+Parry "Dark Souls 3 Spell Parry") it, the flames will veer off course and stop homing in on the player (needs further testing)
  * Travels across surfaces, mainly the floor but also up walls, down walls, as well as over and around all obstacles.
  * With the speed buffs given to many pyromancies in Patch 1.05, it is now possible to combo this spell into other pyromancies ([Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Bed Vestiges"), [Great Combustion](/Great+Combustion "Dark Souls 3 Great Combustion"), etc.)
  * The flames can be slightly controlled with the control stick.
  * Similar to [Fire Snake](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Fire+Snake "Dark Souls 3 Fire Snake") from Dark Souls II.
