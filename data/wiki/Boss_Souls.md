# Boss Souls

**Boss Souls** and other special souls in Dark Souls 3 are powerful items that can be converted into new [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Magic](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic"), or [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") from [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland"). They may also be consumed to gain a large amount of souls.
