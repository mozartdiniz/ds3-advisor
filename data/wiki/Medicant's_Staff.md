**Mendicant's Staff** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  

> A staff with a small bowl attached to the tip, similar to a mendicant's crook. Absorb additional souls from fallen foes.  
>   
> The staff's history is unclear. It may be a relic of an ancient serpent's greed, or a ritual artifact used to summon souls.  
>   
> Skill: Steady Chant  
> Boost the strength of sorceries for a very short period. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Notes and Tips

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Trade it to [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") to obtain the [Sunlight Shield](/Sunlight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Shield").
  * Increases souls gained by 20%. Stacks with the [Covetous silver serpent ring](/Covetous+silver+serpent+ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous silver serpent ring") (10%), [Shield of want](/Shield+of+want "Dark Souls 3 Shield of want") (20%), and [Symbol of avarice](/Symbol+of+avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of avarice") (50%). The total increase in souls gained by equipping all of these items is 237.60%. 
  * The staff must be held for the souls to count, this is proven by silver serpent icon in the status bar.
  * [Rusted Coins](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") and full [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") gear are recommended if trying to obtain this Staff, as it is very rare.
  * The most farmable Man-Serpent Summoner is at the Great Belfry bonfire.
  * The word 'mendicant' originally referred to one who belonged to one of the religious orders that originally relied solely on alms for their upkeep; those who are given to begging; a beggar can be said to be a member of a mendicant order. Given that Man-Serpent Summoners rely as heavily as they do on their summons to absorb and inflict damage for them, this might explain the name chosen for the staff that they carry.
  * At 60 INT it has a spellbuff of 208 
  * Can immediately swap to the mendicant staff after killing an enemy to gain the 20% increase in souls.

### Location / Where to Find

  * Drops from [Man-Serpent Summoners](/Man-Serpent+Summoner "Dark Souls 3 Man-Serpent Summoner") in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"), near the [Great Belfry](/Great+Belfry "Dark Souls 3 Great Belfry") and [Dragon-Kin Mausoleum](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
