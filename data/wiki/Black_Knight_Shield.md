**Black Knight Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Shield of the Black Knights who roam the lands. A flowing canal is chiseled deeply into its face.  
>   
> Long ago, the black knights faced the chaos demons, and were charred black, but their shields became highly resistant to fire.
> 
> Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Black Knight Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Highest stability of all medium shields.
  * Second highest Fire Defense after the [Dragon Crest Shield](/Dragon+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Crest Shield") in category [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields").
  * Provides an average block of 73%.
  * This shield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Black+Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Shield")_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Black Knights](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight"). The first [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight") is near the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") Perimeter bonfire. He can drop the entire [Black Knight Set](/Black+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Set"), in addition to the [Black Knight Greatsword](/Black+Knight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Greatsword").
  * Can be farmed in the area after [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr") in the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") as it contains 4 Black Knights and is directly adjacent to the bonfire.
  * Another [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight") is located in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") near the bonfire Demon Ruins.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
