**Mercenary** is a **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , known for its agility and adaptability in combat. Equipped with dual-wielded weapons that share a single slot, this class can seamlessly transition between one-handed and dual-wielded stances, allowing for varied attack strategies. With the highest starting Dexterity of any class, the Mercenary excels in fast, precise attacks, making it ideal for Dexterity-focused builds.

Its balanced Attunement and Intelligence also enable early access to spells like Soul Arrow, providing additional versatility. Starting at level 8, the Mercenary offers a strong foundation for players who favor speed and flexibility in their playstyle.

> A mercenary and veteran of the battlefield. High dexterity allows masterful wielding of dual scimitars.

### Mercenary Starting Equipment in DS3

The Mercenary Class in Dark Souls 3 starts with the following equipment:

**Weapons** :

  * [Sellsword Twinblades](/Sellsword+Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Sellsword Twinblades")
  * [Wooden Shield](/Wooden+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Wooden Shield")

**Armor** :

  * [Sellsword Helm](/Sellsword+Helm "Dark Souls 3 Sellsword Helm")
  * [Sellsword Armor](/Sellsword+Armor "Dark Souls 3 Sellsword Armor")
  * [Sellsword Trousers](/Sellsword+Trousers "Dark Souls 3 Sellsword Trousers")

### Dark Souls 3 Mercenary Notes and Tips

  * The dual weapons take only one weapon slot and act as 1-handed until you press triangle/Y/two-hand: Then you'll dual-wield and be able to perform [Skills](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills").
  * Has the highest Dexterity of all the classes, and has average Attunement and Intelligence, making it possible to wield spells such as Soul Arrow early on.

### Builds That Use Mercenary in DS3

  * Dexterity builds
  * Strength Builds
  * Faith Builds
  * Vigor Builds
