**Sorcerer Gloves** are [Gauntlets](/Gauntlets) in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Sorcerer Set](/Sorcerer+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Attire of a sorcerer from the Vinheim Dragon School. Black-dyed leather gloves embellished with silver medallions.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer)
  * Looted from a corpse in the castle ruin where you can summon [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Eygon") at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrifices"). Jump into the hole on the ground next to [Eygon's](/Eygon+of+Carim "Eygon's") summon sign. *_note: you must be embered to see summon signs._ Also beware the [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Lycanthrope").
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=20m6s "Video Location") (Fextralife video starting at 20m 6s)
