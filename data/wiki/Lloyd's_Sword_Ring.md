# Lloyd's Sword Ring

### Lloyd's Sword Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring given to knights of the Way of White. Depicts Allfather Lloyd's Sword of Law.   
>   
> Boosts attack power when HP is full.   
>   
> Much time has passed since the worship of Lloyd was common in the Way of White. The clerics of Carim had always strongly asserted that Lloyd was a derivative fraud, and that the Allfather title was self-proclaimed.  
  
**Lloyd's Sword Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Dark Souls 3 Lloyd's Sword Ring Effect

  * Boosts **Attack Rating** when HP is full (over 99% HP) 
    * **PVE** = 10% 
    * **PVP** = 10%
  * Weight: 0.9

### How to Get Lloyd's Sword Ring in Dark Souls 3

  * Found at the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") near the first giant you encounter that swings at you. It's on the ground on corpse as you run by.
  * Watch the [Fextra Video](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=2h11m41s "Fextra Video") at 2h 11mins 41 secs in.
  * [Lloyd's Sword Ring Location](https://youtu.be/ZE-BL52HUNY)

### Dark Souls 3 Heal Aid Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Boosts all damage dealt while the buff is active.  
Confirmed with: [Wrath of the Gods](/Wrath+of+the+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Wrath of the Gods"), [Sunlight Spear](/Sunlight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Spear"), [Crystal Soul Spear](/Crystal+Soul+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Soul Spear"). ~12-13% damage increase.
  * This does NOT work with consumable item damage (Tested with Firebombs and Throwing Knives)
  * The boost to actual damage output is generally greater than 10% due to how defense works.  

    * Defense applies a multiplier ranging from .1 to .9 to incoming damage based on the ratio of raw damage to defense, with higher values of damage/defense meaning a higher multiplier, so increasing outgoing damage by 10% will lead to a higher ratio, which means in addition to more raw damage, a higher proportion of that damage will go through. This is true for all cases where your raw damage without the ring is less than 8 times the enemy defense (the enemies in NG generally have between 100 and 130 defense, so any attack with less than ~1000 raw damage will see more than 10% improvement)
  * Tested on Hollows at High Wall of Lothric: 
    * 322 AR (no ring) R1 = 298 damage = 92.5% of AR.
    * 354 AR (with ring) R1 = 336 damage = 94.9% of AR.
