**Dragonslayer's Axe** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Axe favored by Creighton the Wanderer, infamous deserter of the Knights of Mirrah.  
>   
> Called Dragonslayer's Axe for the lightning that pulsates within its blade, but Creighton used it to slay men.  
>   
> Skill: Warcry  
> Let out a spirited warcry that temporarily boosts attack, and pierce through shields to inflict direct damage.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * **Be sure to complete[Sirris](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms")´ questline **to the point, where you fight off an invader ([Creighton](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer")) in her World. The summoning sign to help her is located at the bridge to [Irithyll](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), where you first encounter [Sulyvahn's Beast](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast").
  * Blessed infusion adds Faith scaling to both Physical and Lightning damage.
  * Raw infusion substantially increases this weapon's base power without detracting from its Lightning damage. Because the weapon can still be buffed with resin after infusion, this makes Raw an incredible option for low damage-stat investment or lower-level characters, potentially being stronger than almost any other Raw option for these types of characters.
  * Was previously named the [Dragonslayer Crescent Axe](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dragonslayer's+Crescent+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer's Crescent Axe") in Dark Souls 2.

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * From the Church of Yorshka bonfire that you come across in Irithyll of the boreal valley head into the graveyard area to the left of the bonfire. Here head straight to the item on a tree and go to the right until you can't go any further. If you have an ember active you will be invaded by Creighton and he will drop the axe. This invasion can only occur after progressing through [Sirris of the Sunless Realms' questline](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") and defeating [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer") on the Irithyll bridge. **Invasion is not possible if Pontiff Sulyvahn has been defeated**.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
