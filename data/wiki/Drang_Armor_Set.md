**Drang Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor/Gauntlets/Shoes of the Drang Knights, proclaimed descendants of the land known for the legend of the Linking of the Fire.
> 
> Fine protection that is both light and strong, having been reinforced with rare geisteel.
> 
> The Drang knights were once feared sellswords, until treason meant descending into the abyss, and they were separated forever.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), scattered around the second giant near the altar.
  * Watch the [Fextralife Video](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=2h36m31s "Fextralife Video") (starting at 2h 36m 31s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * It's a good lightweight armor against the elements but it lacks Poise.
  * This armor set lacks a helm. The Thief Hood (sold by Greirat) matches very well with this set.
  * Extremely similar in appearance to the [Llewellyn set](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Llewellyn+Set "Dark Souls 3 Llewellyn Set") from Dark Souls 2, created by the same man that also created Horace The Hushed's shield.
  * Drang is referencing Drangleic From Dark Souls 2 Where this set is from
