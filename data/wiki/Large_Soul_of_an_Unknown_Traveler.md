# Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler

**Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler** is a [Soul](/Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler  
  
---  
  

> A large soul found in the corpse of an unknown traveler. Use to acquire souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler Usage

  * Gives 1000 souls

### Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler Locations

  * 2x Found at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep): right next to one of the chained-Giants. 
  * 1x Found at [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Catacombs of Carthus"): At the bottom of the stairs with the rolling boulder, head left (into a room with a lot of pots with dark magic in them). Head left from there and you will see the Soul on a corpse, guarded by 2 shotel-wielding skeletons. ([Video](https://youtu.be/fQ-oeHS0gYU?t=56m45s "Video"))
