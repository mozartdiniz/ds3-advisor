# Profaned Coal

**Profaned Coal** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Profaned Coal  
  
---  
  

> Coal used for weapon infusion.  
>   
> Remnants of the fire that burned down the Profaned Capital, preserved in an icy skull.   
>   
> Give to the blacksmith in the shrine to allow the use of gems for dark, blood, and Hollow infusion.  
  
### Profaned Coal Usage

  * Give to [Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") to allow the use of [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem"), [Blood Gem](/Blood+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blood Gem") and [Hollow Gem](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") infusions.

### Profaned Coal Location

  * [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"), in a room adjacent to the one with the 6 jailers, who are walking in a circle, right before the shortcut ().

### Notes

  * Player note 1

### Trivia

  * Andre's Dialogue upon giving him the Profaned Coal:   
"Lords... where didye hap'n upon this Coal?"  
"This is much too dark. I see the abyss in it..."  
"Yet, a smith i remain. I won't turn down a request."  
"But forget not, Your fight is for the flame, and y' fellow kin. Just like mine."  
"A cursed fate this may be, but hope remains, does it not?"
