# Chaos Gem

**Chaos Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Chaos Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Relics of land scorched by the Chaos Flame.  
>   
> Used in infusion to create chaos weapons.  
>   
> Chaos weapons inflict fire damage, and scale with intelligence and faith.  
  
### Chaos Gem Usage

  * Used in [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create Chaos weapons
  * Chaos weapons inflict [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), and scale with [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") and [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith").
  * Also adds [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") scaling to innate [lightning damage](/lightning+damage "Dark Souls 3 lightning damage") in weapons ([Dragonslayer's Axe](/Dragonslayer's+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer's Axe"), [Drakeblood Greatsword](/Drakeblood+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Greatsword") and [Lothric Knight Greatsword](/Lothric+Knight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight Greatsword")) and [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") scaling to the Drakeblood Greatsword's innate [magic damage](/magic+damage "Dark Souls 3 magic damage").

### Chaos Gem Locations

  * On a corpse at the far end of the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"), near the left group of big crabs.
  * Crystal lizard at the start of Demon Ruins, down the hole in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"). Drop down and go left around a corner. ([Video](https://youtu.be/V_Q4e7W_5L8?t=1h1m25s "Video"))
  * Low chance drop from a [Smoldering Ghru](/Smoldering+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Smoldering Ghru")
  * Crystal lizard in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). From the bonfire follow the path forward until a dark room with 2 lizards that run up the movable stairs. It is the closest lizard to you, on the left.

### Notes

  * Requires the [Giant's Coal](/Giant's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Giant's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.
  * Weapon is no longer able to be buffed with resin or spells after infusion. (However, weapon can still be buffed with [Warcry](/Warcry "Dark Souls 3 Warcry") Skill.)
  * The scaling on weapons differs depending on the weapon infused. (A corvian greatknife gets E scaling at +0 for both, while a Notched whip gets D scaling)
  * It offers the exact same damage as the [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem"), the only difference being the damage type. This being the general rule with some exceptions like player vs player or the [Murky Hand Scythe](/Murky+Hand+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Murky Hand Scythe").
