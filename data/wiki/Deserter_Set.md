**Deserter Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Common soldier's armor. It's insignia is worn beyond recognition.
> 
> This musty, rusted hunk of metal befits one reduced to thievery.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Chest piece and trousers are starting gear for [Thief](/Thief "Dark Souls 3 Thief")
  * Drops from the [Hollow Soldiers](/Hollow+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Soldier") in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"). [Helm](/Helms "Dark Souls 3 Helms") and [Chest](/Chest+Armor "Dark Souls 3 Chest Armor") only drop from helm-wearing soldiers.
