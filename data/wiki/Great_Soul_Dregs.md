# Great Soul Dregs

## Great Soul Dregs  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  Sorcery  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 40 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Great Soul Dregs** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> A sorcery that fires great dark soul dregs that have stewed for ages, far within the deep.
> 
> This sorcery is the highest form of Deep Soul.
> 
> Some of the murkmen who rise from the depths are possessed by soul dregs, which have a grave likeness to the human form.

### How to Get Great Soul Dregs in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in: [The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap") Area in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC. After you see the first Cursed Angel, proceed forward through the arched doorway ahead. Looking towards the 2 knights at the end of the room, take the arched doorway to the right. Turn right and head up to the back of the area and through another arched doorway. Proceed up the stairs and into through the doorway into a dead end room. Attack the wall on the left to reveal an illusionary wall. Proceed up the stairs until you come to the final room. The sorcery (Great Soul Dregs) is at the back of the room next to the Murkman Sorcerer: [Video Example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b85aBCMJ04c)

### Dark Souls 3 Great Soul Dregs Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.55) damage.
  * This spell deals [dark damage](/dark+damage "Dark Souls 3 dark damage"), and as such will be slightly more powerful when cast with a dark-friendly catalyst with high Intelligence and Faith.
  * The homing and flight speed are extremely similar to [Great Deep Soul](/Great+Deep+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Great Deep Soul"), if not identical.
  * Has a slightly longer range than the Great Deep Soul, making it ideal for sniping enemies.
  * The projectile size is enormous, and its sheer size can make it difficult to roll through.
  * Damage output is roughly 20% higher than Affinity, and can hit 1,000 damage per cast without much effort. 
  * However, with some effort that spell is devastating against most enemies, especially those weak to Dark damage, as it will deal nearly 2,000 damage per shot.
