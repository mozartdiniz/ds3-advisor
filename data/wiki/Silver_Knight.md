### Silver Knight Enemy Description

_"Guards of Anor Londo and the royals of old. It is said that even after the family's passing, the Silver Knights continued to watch over their manor, and the Ruined Cathedral."_

  * There are 3 variants: Sword & Shield, Spear & Shield and Greatbow.
  * Greatbow archers will switch to a sword & shield when within close range.
  * They can be found in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") and [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") just before reaching [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo").

### Silver Knight Combat Information

  * It is best to sprint and roll to avoid the arrows from their Greatbows, or take cover behind the environment.
  * Blocking is not advised against their Greatbow as you'll be pushed back and likely fall down.
  * A good idea to deal with them is to also use a Greatbow of your own to harrass them from a far distance.
  * Attacks deal [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"), [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") and [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage").
  * Weak to [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"), [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") and [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage").
  * Resistant to [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage"), [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage"), [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage"), [Poison/Toxic](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed").
  * [Parryable](/Parry "Dark Souls 3 Parry"): Yes. Parrying them is effective when they swing horizontally.
  * There is a powerful red-eyed version in Anor Londo to the right of the other two Silver Knights at the top of the stairs. The drop rates for the [Silver Knight Set](/Silver+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Set"), including the [Silver Knight Shield](/Silver+Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Shield"), are doubled for this enemy.
  * Spear Silver Knights can't hit you if you're using the Stretch Out, Curl Up or the Prostration [gesture](/gestures "Dark Souls 3 gestures").

### Silver Knight Trivia & Notes 

  * The three Silver Knights in the room with paintings before [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn") do not respawn when killed.
  * Despite wielding Silver Knight Straight Sword and Silver Knight Spear as seen in [Dark Souls I](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Souls+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Dark Souls Wiki"), they cannot be obtained.
  * For some reason some knights have a 4% chance to drop the helmet instead of the 3% found on all other armor pieces and on other knights for the helmet. This is likely an oversight.
