# Lord of Cinder

**Lord of Cinder** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** is a title given to those who were mighty enough to Link the Flame. These are beings that have linked the First Flame and maintained the Age of Fire for centuries. Most of these beings followed the practice of [Gwyn](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwyn+Lord+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Gwyn Lord of Cinder"), [the First Lord](/Gwyn,+the+First+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Gwyn, the First Lord"), there are a number of individuals who became a Lord of Cinder through the use of bad deeds. An example of this is Aldrich, who consumed a high number of humans to become strong and become a lord of Cinder. This also caused the summoning of the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"), who possesses all of the abilities of all Lords of Cinder.

Lords of Cinder have parts of their bodies seemingly alit, in a similar fashion to the player's Lord of Cinder Mode (Embered). At the ending of the game, the player can choose to Link the Flame himself.

## Lord of Cinder Dark Souls 3 Information

### All Lords of Cinder in Dark souls 3

The Lords of Cinder are among the main bosses in Dark Souls 3, playing a crucial role in the game's narrative and progression. The player is tasked with defeating these powerful beings to link the First Flame and prolong the Age of Fire. Each Lord of Cinder has a unique background and significance, making their defeat both challenging and rewarding. The main Lords of Cinder that the player must defeat are:

  * [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland")
  * [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers")
  * [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Saint+Aldrich+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Saint Aldrich of the Deep")
  * [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant")
  * [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince")

### DS3 Lord of Cinder Lore Speculation

  * Unkindled are failed Lords of Cinder, succeeding in linking the flame, but too weak to survive the burst of fire.
  * Lords of Cinder cannot Link the Flame on their own, and all five are needed.
  * The Lords of Cinder have linked the fire long ago, but it still fades and it was deemed "not enough". The new order was to have all 5 of them link it again, this time together for a stronger sacrifice.  
Only Ludleth of Courland was willing to make the sacrifice. He had his legs removed should his will waver, as the first time he linked the fire must had been traumatic. No one else wants to link the flame. It might be that they barely survived the first time or it hurts like hell.
  * The champion of ash, along with many other unkindled are awoken from death, possibly by the fire's call, to bring all mighty Lords back, dead or alive.
  * Despite having served as the fuel for the First Flame for this long of a time the Lords of Cinder still retain a good amount of the fire within their body. This aspect in itself is somehow standing against the purpose of “sacrificing oneself to prolong the inevitable” as it implies that this power is taken from the already dying First Flame and thereby weakening it even further.  
This in fact could be the very reason the Player Character has to find the Lords of Cinder, slay them and thereby retake their souls to then feed them to the First Flame again by Linking it.
  * The young prince Lothric is being called a Lord of Cinder, even though he is implied to have never actually linked the First Flame. He is also not shown to have any of the fire powers the other lords use in their second boss phase. All the fire attacks used are stemming from his older brothers sword (which got its effect from Demon fire).
