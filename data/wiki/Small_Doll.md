# Small Doll

**Small Doll** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in Dark Souls 3. The small doll, is given by Pontiff Sulyvahn to his valued subjects to help them cross barriers when returning home. You can obtain this doll after defeating the Deacons of the Deep. It is essential for progressing in Dark Souls 3, as it is required to pass through the barriers blocking access to Irithyll of the Boreal Valley.

### Small Doll  
  
---  
  

> Small silverwork doll depicting a young squire.   
>   
> In the legendary old city of Irithyll situated in the Boreal Valley, the Pontiff Sulyvahn gave this doll to valued subjects, so that they might use it to cross the barrier when they return home.   
>   
> Listen carefully, and you can hear it say, "Wherever you go, the moon still sets in Irithyll. Wherever you may be, Irithyll is your home."  
  
### Dark Souls 3 Small Doll Usage

  * The Small Doll is used to break the seal which keeps players from passing the bridge which leads to [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").

### Dark Souls 3 Small Doll Location

  * The Small Doll is found after defeating the [Deacons of the Deep ](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep")which is a boss encounter in the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
