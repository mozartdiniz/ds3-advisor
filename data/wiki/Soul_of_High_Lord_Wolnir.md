# Soul of High Lord Wolnir

**Soul of High Lord Wolnir** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of High Lord Wolnir  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the High Lord Wolnir.   
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. Lord Wolnir of Carthus sentenced countless souls to gruesome deaths, keen to outlive them all.  
  
### Soul of High Lord Wolnir Usage

  * Can be consumed for 10,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Wolnir's Holy Sword](/Wolnir's+Holy+Sword "Wolnir's Holy Sword") or [Black Serpent](/Black+Serpent "Black Serpent")

### Soul of High Lord Wonir Locations

  * Obtained from defeating [High Lord Wolnir](/High+Lord+Wolnir "High Lord Wolnir")

### Notes

  * The item in the fight is the [Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome](/Grave+Warden+Pyromancy+Tome). If you do not pick it up, it will appear on a corpse in the corner of the room with the bonfire afterwards. 

### Trivia

  * During the game's inital launch, the description misspelled Wolnir's name as "Wornir" (the misspelling might have been caused by the fact that L's and R's are somewhat interchangable in romanization of Japanese). One of the game's patches corrected this misspelling.
