# Corvian Settler (Enemy)

## Corvian Settler (Enemy)  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Humanoid, Bird  
**Weakness** | Fire, Dark  
**Resistances** | Frost, Poison, Lightning  
**Immune** | n/a  
  
**Corvian Settler (Enemy)** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Corvian Settler (Enemy) Enemy Description

  * Deformed humanoids and inhabitants of the Painted World of Ariandel, met only in the Corvian Settlement.
  * They are a target for Corvian Knights, but only if there is no player nearby.

### Corvian Settler (Enemy) Combat Information

  * Appears in 4 variants: regular, mage, and toxic. It can also appear in the crawling version, which has the same HP level but moves significantly slower. 
  * Appears only in large groups.
  * Regular version attacks with their beak or claws, but only if provoked (normally they run away or/and shout). Can attack from surprise (push player, attack from above) or play dead.
  * Mage casts Homing Mass or Soul Arrow spell. 
  * Toxic version, which can be distinguished by fur, spew toxic.
  * Can be easily stunned with fire. Dark also seems to be effective against them.
  * Crawling one is weak to Physical.
  * Has medium resistance to Frost, Poison/Toxic and Lightning.
  * Can be lured
  * Can be Rapported

### Corvian Settler (Enemy)Notes & Trivia

  * There is a Corvian Settler, which will not run away. Player may meet him met near "Corvian Settlement" bonfire.
  * Corvian are connected to the goddess [Velka.](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Velka the Goddess of Sin")
