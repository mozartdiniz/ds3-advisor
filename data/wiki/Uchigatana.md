**Uchigatana** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. The Uchigatana is one of the earliest katanas available to players in Dark Souls 3. It is dropped by the Sword Master located outside the Firelink Shrine. This weapon features strong Dexterity scaling, making it highly effective for builds focused on Dexterity. Additionally, the Uchigatana has a significant Bleed build-up effect, which can be particularly useful for inflicting high damage over time and overcoming enemies with high health pools.  
  
  
  

> A unique katana characterized by the fine craftsmanship of an eastern land where it was forged.   
>   
> The finely-sharpened blade cuts flesh like butter and causes bleeding, but breaks easily as a result.  
>   
> Skill: Hold   
> Assume a holding stance to rapidly execute a lunging slash with a normal attack, or a deflecting parry with the strong attack.

  
  

### Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Upgraded with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * Earliest [Katana](/Katanas "Dark Souls 3 Katanas") you can get in the game. Due to its early game availability, players who chose the Mercenary class will be able to use this weapon right away without leveling up as long as they two-hand it.
  * It uses slightly less stamina than all other katanas in the game except [Darkdrift](/Darkdrift "Dark Souls 3 Darkdrift").

### Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana Location

  * Dropped by [Sword Master](/Sword+Master "Dark Souls 3 Sword Master") just outside [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Should the Sword Master fall off the edge, simply reload the area and the Uchigatana will be where he fell.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
