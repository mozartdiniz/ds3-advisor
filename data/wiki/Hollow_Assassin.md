**Hollow Assassin** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Hollow Assassin Enemy Description

  * Spies and thieves that are found in [Highwall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"), [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") and [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle").
  * Usually laying in ambush in dark areas

### Hollow Assassin Combat Information

  * Should you leave your back exposed they will trigger a critical.
  * They're capable of unleashing a surprisingly deadly flurry of attacks.
  * Uses [throwing knifes](/Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Throwing Knife")
  * Weak to [Hollowslayer Greatsword](/Hollowslayer+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Hollowslayer Greatsword")
  * Can be lured with [Alluring Skulls](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull")
  * Are affected by [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport")

### Hollow Assassin Notes & Trivia

  * Fast enemies, ready a dodge or block, or come from behind.
  * Low armor.
  * Even though they drop the [dagger](/Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Dagger"), they wield the [bandit's knife](/Bandit's+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Bandit's Knife").
