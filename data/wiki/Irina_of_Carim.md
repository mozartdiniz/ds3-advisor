**Irina of Carim** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **Dark Souls 3**. She can teach and sell [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") to your character in a certain area of the game. She can be found in the Undead Settlement, held as a prisoner in a cell guarded by Eygon of Carim. Once freed, and if your character accepts Irina's services, she can next be found at Firelink Shrine, specifically at the end of the basement corridor on the east wing as a Miracles merchant. Irina originally went to Lothric to become a Fire Keeper, but her journey was cut short.

### Irina of Carim Dark Souls 3 Information

  * Can be killed.
  * Sells [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles"), see Items Sold below.
  * Teaches the "Prayer" gesture.
  * Accepts all divine tomes, but her questline will vary accordingly. 
    * Purchasing any Dark Miracles from her causes her to fall into darkness, and be taken away by [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim"), to be protected from you.
    * Purchasing ALL Dark Miracles causes her to stop being a vendor, and initiates a gloomy dialogue. (If you speak with her while equipping [Morne's Gauntlets](/Morne's+Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Gauntlets"), she will have more things to say.)
    * Purchasing ALL Holy Miracles instead (without buying any dark ones) turns her into a Fire Keeper.

### Irina of Carim Dark Souls 3 Questline

  1. **Start** : You can find Irina in a cell near the entrance to the Road of Sacrifices in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). She can be reached by dropping down the cliff on the left of [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") (via the small gouge in the rocks) or purchasing the [Grave Key](/Grave+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grave Key") from the [Shrine Handmaiden](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 1500 souls (requires [Mortician's Ashes](/Mortician's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Mortician's Ashes")) and using it to open the door halfway through the sewer tunnel with the rats and fog inside it near the Cliff Underside bonfire. [Irina's Location](https://youtu.be/XJSvoUtBup0 "Video Location")   
  

  2. Once you free her and accept her services, she will relocate to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). If you approach [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") and head down the stairs to the right, she can be found at the end of the hallway.  
  

  3. **Dark Path:** If you give her the [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome") or the [Deep Braille Divine Tome](/Deep+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Deep Braille Divine Tome"), and subsequently purchase any Dark [Miracle ](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")from her, [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") will take her from you. They can then be found just after [Iudex Gundyr's](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") bonfire, and [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") will be hostile. If you kill him, he drops [Morne's Great Hammer](/Morne's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Great Hammer") and the [Moaning Shield](/Moaning+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Moaning Shield"). If you cannot find her after killing Eygon warp out and back and she will be where Eygon was standing. Talk to her twice, and she will return to her spot in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").  
  

     1. If you didn't buy ALL of the Dark [Miracles ](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")you can still access her inventory and buy regular [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles"). Her dark questline will not progress until all Dark Miracles have been purchased. Buying a single Dark Miracle locks you into the dark questline and you will have to wait until the next playthrough to attempt her "good" ending if you have purchased any of these.  
  

     2. If you purchase ALL of the Dark [Miracles ](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") from her, she will no longer sell you [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") and you will only be able to speak with her. You can still finish the dark version of her questline but you will need to equip [Morne's Gauntlets](/Morne's+Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Gauntlets").  
NOTE! If you wait to rescue Irina until you have all of the tomes, you can skip conflict with Eygon by giving all dark tomes and purchase the all of her miracles at once. If this happens, Eygon will not remove Irina from Firelink Shrine and subsequently, he will not become hostile. This will prevent the player from obtaining Eygon's equipment and will allow the player to skip ahead to step #3 without conflict (this NOTE needs testing in various game stages and progressions, this could be a bug).  
  

     3. When returned to her usual spot Irina after Eygon has been defeated, she will not sell anything and will only beg the character to touch her. If the player does this without [Morne's Gauntlets](/Morne's+Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Gauntlets"), Irina will not recognize the touch and will ask again for someone to touch her; if the player is wearing the gauntlets, Irina will believe that you are Eygon and will ask you to kill her, as Eygon promised to do so. Killing her drops her ashes which can be used to purchase her miracles from the [Shrine Handmaiden](/Shrine+Handmaiden "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaiden").  
  

  4. **Light Path:** If you instead purchase ALL the other [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  _without_ purchasing any Dark Miracles, she will become a Fire Keeper, and move to the bottom of the Tower outside of Firelink (the one where Sword Master was standing). If you had given her [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome") or the [Deep Braille Divine Tome](/Deep+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Deep Braille Divine Tome") but didn't purchase any Dark Miracles, these will be lying next to her on the ground. You can then give them to [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") to purchase all the Dark Miracles. After this happens, Irina will be able to level you up, much like the Fire Keeper inside the Shrine.

### Irina of Carim Dark Souls 3 Trivia

  * Her name is Slavic/Eastern-European form of Greek name Ειρηνη (Eirene), derived from a word meaning "peace." Ειρηνη was the name of the Greek goddess who personified peace. Also, it was borne by several early Christian saints and is popular among Eastern Christians.
  * Voiced by Sarah Beck Mather, who also voiced Lucatiel of Mirrah in Dark Souls II.
  * Irina's feeling of darkness eating her away like insects corresponds to the item description of [Fire Keeper's Soul](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul") in Dark Souls 1   
"A Fire Keeper's soul is a draw for humanity, and held within their bosoms, below just a thin layer of skin, are swarms of humanity that writhe and squirm."
  * If you kill her while wearing the Morne set, her death dialogue will be different.
  * Upon completing her light questline, she becomes a fully-fledged firekeeper. While some theories claim she isn't a firekeeper due to the fact that she does not have her eyes removed like the primary firekeeper, she does not have her sight due to being blind and therefore meets the required conditions to become a firekeeper.
