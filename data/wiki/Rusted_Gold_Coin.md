# Rusted Gold Coin

### Rusted Gold Coin  
  
---  
  

> A rusty old gold coin that surely has no currency.   
>   
> Crushing the coin gives a large, if temporary, boost to item discovery.   
>   
> A rich man lost his fortune, but it returned to him twofold. He was swift to squander his retrieved fortune, smugly confident that it was bound to double once again.  
  
**Rusted Gold Coin** is an [Item](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is one of the [Burial Gifts](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts") available to players.

### Rusted Gold Coin Effect

  * Increase [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") by 100 points, for 60 seconds.
  * No. Held limit: 10  
  

### Where to Find Rusted Gold Coin

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts"). (7 Coins.)
  * 1x Found in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), hidden behind the wall next to the third extinguishable fire, where [Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") invades.
  * Found at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"): while on the way to the Irithyll Dungeon from Church of Yorshka Bonfire, found on a corpse next to a couple of wolves. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=47m22s "Video"))
  * 2x Dropped by a [mimic](/mimic "Dark Souls 3 mimic") in the left treasure room before [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant").
  * 1x Found at [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"), at the end of the narrow hallway that leads to the toxic area.
  * 3x Sold by [Stone-humped Hag](/Stone-humped+Hag "Dark Souls 3 Stone-humped Hag") when she is alive, or, [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after giving her [Old Woman's Ashes](/Old+Woman's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Old Woman's Ashes"), for 1800 souls each.
  * Very rare drop from Handmaids (white-robed Jailers) in [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital")

### Rusted Gold Coin Notes & Trivia

  * Effect stacks with [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice"), [Covetous Gold Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring"), and [Crystal Sage's Rapier](/Crystal+Sage's+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage's Rapier").
  * Discussion of "optimal" Item Discovery often mentions the Gold coin, but this can be misleading. The Gold coins are minor and limited, especially endgame when Item Discovery gear and unlimited normal [Rusted Coins](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") are available. The "difference" a Gold coin makes might be having 400 instead of 350 Item Discovery, a handful of times. (Or even less than that.) It is not important. Their best use is early-game, for help farming weapons needed for a build. But even then, the early-game difference between 110 and 210 Item Discovery is _less than double, for a very short time_. It's never very important.
  * Does NOT stack with Rusted Coin.
  * The symbol on the coin, as in Dark Souls II, is of an angel, although what implication this has in the lore, again like Dark Souls II, is indecipherable.
  * In reality, gold does not rust (although it does tarnish, losing some of its shine and darkening slightly), making such a coin impossible. Much of gold's value derives from this fact, meaning gold that could rust would, as the item description states, have no currency or value, even were souls not the currency.
