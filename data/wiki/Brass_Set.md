**Brass Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3. It was originally worn by the [Darkmoon Knightess](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Darkmoon+Knightess "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Knightess") from the first Dark Souls.  
  
  
  
  

> Armor of a knight once known as the Darkmoon. It is said that this brass armor hides something hideous within.
> 
> Something about its silhouette suggests femininity.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in the Darkmoon Tomb. (after [Pontiff](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn") but before [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). See [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley#map "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley#map") for walkthrough) Hit the stone statue of [Lord of Cinder](/Lord+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Lord of Cinder") to reveal the [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls").

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This set belongs to Lady of the Darkling, a firekeeper and a Blade of the Darkmoon, from Dark Souls 1.
