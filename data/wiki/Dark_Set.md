**Dark Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> (Piece Name) of the Darkwraiths, relics of a small country that fell to the dark long ago.  
> Looks as if it may crumble to dust at any moment.
> 
> The Darkwraiths were the oldest of the Red Eye Invaders, and rumored to have served a Primordial Serpent.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") and [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"). 

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * In order to complete the set; buy the Dark Hand from [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 12,000 souls. - Equip it on the left hand.
