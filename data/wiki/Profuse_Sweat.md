# Profuse Sweat

**Profuse Sweat** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Profuse Sweat  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 20  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 6 Faith/ 6 Intelligence  
**Type** | Body Buff  
  
> Advanced pyromancy that internalizes flame, associated with Carmina.  
>   
> Profuse sweating temporarily boosts resistance to bleeding, poison, frost, and curses.  
>   
> The yellowish sweat cures all kinds of ailments.

### How to Get Profuse Sweat in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 2,000 souls, after giving him the [Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome](/Great+Swamp+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome")

###  Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Raises [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), [Frost](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") and [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") resistance by 80 points each.
  * Same effect as [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit"). They also stack together, but their durations are different.
  * Does not cure ailments, as the item description suggests, it only increases resistances.
  * Lasts 60 seconds. This duration is affected by [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Overwrites and is overwritten by other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat"), [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within"), [Iron Flesh](/Iron+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh") and [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon").
  * If one wants to benefit from the effects almost immediately, they can attune this spell, use it, and then rest at a bonfire. The buff will maintain active, but the status bar will be set to 0, giving you the increased status resistance for almost the full duration. It's then up to the player if they change the spell, or re-cast it to maintain the buff active for longer time.
