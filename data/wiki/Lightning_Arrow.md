# Lightning Arrow

**Lightning Arrow** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Lightning Arrow

> Draw lightning bow to fire a lightning arrow.

> The lightning arrows offer a great improvement to the range of the spears, and were said to have been used to pierce the eyes of the dragons from afar.  
> But remember, beautiful stories are always marked by embellishment. 

### How to Get Lightning Arrow in Dark Souls 3

  * [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City"): After fighting the dragon after the Shared Grave bonfire, it's just inside the building next to the stairs with crystal lizard, behind 3 illusory walls. You can hear an enemy crumbling noise. [Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E9Z3WWWyxnk)

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Arrow Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.6) damage, making it stronger than lightning spear but weaker than great lightning spear.
  * The spell can be held indefinitely by holding down the button. This makes it great for roll catching and pressuring players in PvP.
  * If the player uses [Unfaltering Prayer](/Unfaltering+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Unfaltering Prayer"), this effect can too be extended indefinitely. 
    * A quick follow-up cast will continue the poise effect. This can be chained indefinitely (L2>L1>L1...).
  * Does not deal any extra point-blank damage. However, it serves as an excellent gun-point spell.
  * The bow itself seems to resemble a [Dragonslayer Greatbow](/Dragonslayer+Greatbow "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Greatbow"), albeit much smaller. 
    * Despite this, the arrow is a full [Dragonslayer Greatarrow](/Dragonslayer+Greatarrow "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Greatarrow").
  * Takes approximately 1.0 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
