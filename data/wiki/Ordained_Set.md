**Ordained Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "DLC").  
  
  
  
  

> Garb of Friede, Sister of the Painted World. A light blue dress sewn with thin fabric.
> 
> After renouncing everything, Friede discovered a people that she wished to protect, and assumed the precise form that they yearned for.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Available after killing [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Sister Friede") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Shrine Handmaid").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Has very low Thrust defense.
