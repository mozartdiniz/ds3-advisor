# Havel Knight

## Havel Knight  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Knight   
**Weakness** |  Backstab, Bleed, Toxic  
**Resistances** |  Physical-type, Fire, Lightning, Dark  
**Immune** |  Rapport  
  
**Havel Knight** (aka Havel the Rock) is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3.**

### Havel Knight Enemy Description

  * Can be found on a roof near the Great Belfry bonfire in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") defending a [Titanite Slab,](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") near the corpse of an ancient Wyvern,
  * Can be summoned by both [Man-Serpent Summoner](/Man-Serpent+Summoner "Dark Souls 3 Man-Serpent Summoner")s, but only while true Havel is still alive.

### Havel Knight Combat Information

  * Medium/high resistance to most type of damage.
  * Can use his [Shield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield")'s [Skill](/Stone+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Stone Flesh"), making him able to tank hits and raising his defense, while forcing him to fat-roll. Skill decreases defense towards Lightning Damage.
  * Attacks deals massive [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage").
  * One-handed attacks can be parried, two-handed can't.
  * Can be guard-broken, flattered and pinned down.
  * Weak to [Bleed.](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed")
  * Most of his attacks are slow, which makes backstabbing him easy.
  * Resistant to most types of damages.
  * Immune to [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport").
  * Can be lured using Alluring Skulls.

### Havel Knight Notes & Trivia

  * While remaining in starting point, he can easily be poisoned or killed with [Pestilent Mist](/Pestilent+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mist").
  * Shield he uses doesn't seem to have the stability as wielded by player. It can be attacked with midweight weapons without having to use it 2-handed.
  * You can lure him to follow you down the ladder. That's your chance to chain some damage (mainly as a sorcerer) on him or use gravity against him.
  * Drops [Dragon Tooth](/Dragon+Tooth "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Tooth") and [Havel's Greatshield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield") once, they even drop from the summoned version.
  * His [armor set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set") can be found on top of the bridge in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), near the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon"), after Havel Knight is defeated. Head to the [Old Wolf of Farron](/Old+Wolf+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf of Farron") bonfire.
  * The [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Stray Demon") can also be transposed into the [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring") or the [Boulder Heave](/Boulder+Heave "Dark Souls 3 Boulder Heave") pyromancy. This hints toward a connection between Havel and the Stray Demon, but it could also be some kind of rock joke.
  * Items related to Havel: [Dragon Tooth](/Dragon+Tooth "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Tooth"), [Havel's Greatshield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield"), [Havel's Armor](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set"), [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring"), [Great Magic Barrier](/Great+Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Barrier").
  * His armor is the second heaviest armor in the game.
  * "Weak" to miracles such as Force, Great Force, and Wrath of The Gods. These miracles can drain his stamina if he is blocking, or thrust him onto the ground, allowing the player breathing room if needed.
