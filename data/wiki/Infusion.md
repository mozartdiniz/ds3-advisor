# Infusion

In Dark Souls 3 you can infuse many different [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") and [Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") with Gems that change the properties, damage type or Scaling. In order to infuse a Weapon or Shield to your chosen type you must:

  1. have found and give the appropriate Coal to [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre"),
  2. have a Gem of the chosen type, and
  3. have the required Souls.

The exceptions here are Refined, Raw or Fire which require no Coal to be given. You can use a Shriving Stone if you wish to remove the Infusion from your Weapon or Shield (this must also be done at Blacksmith Andre). Again, not all Weapons and Shields can be Infused so check the Weapons pages to verify which can and can't.

These weapons **CANNOT** be infused: [Bows](/Bows "Dark Souls 3 Bows"), [Greatbows](/Greatbows "Dark Souls 3 Greatbows"), [Crossbows](/Crossbows "Dark Souls 3 Crossbows"), [Chimes](/Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Chimes"), [Talismans](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), [Staves](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves"), [Pyromancy Flames](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") and any weapon that upgrades with Twinkling Titanite or Titanite Scale
