# Great Heal

**Great Heal** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Great Heal  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**FP** | 65  
**Slots** | 1  
**Faith** | 25  
  
> Glorious miracle used by high-ranking clerics. Restores a large amount of HP for self and those in the vicinity.  
>   
> Only a select few have learned to recite this epic tale in its entirety, but those who do are amply rewarded.

### 

### How to Get Great Heal in Dark Souls 3

  * Under the bridge in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), in the water in the far end. ([Video](http://youtu.be/lNslSGzROQU "Video"))

### Dark Souls 3 Great Heal Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores HP equal to 550% * Spell Buff of used [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") or [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans")
  * Unaffected by miracle boosting rings.
  * When the button is held down you can delay the healing, ideal for co-op partners to get inside the radius.
  * This is a strong spell mid-game for support-oriented builds, as it has considerable healing and a large AoE.
  * Healing range is buffed by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
