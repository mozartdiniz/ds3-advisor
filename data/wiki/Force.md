# Force

**Force** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

### How to Get Force in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") if you've given her [Braille Divine Tome of Carim](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim")
  * Default starting spell for Cleric, along with Heal.

### Dark Souls 3 Force Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * The miracle has two effective ranges. If the enemy's next to you, they'll be sent flying back, and if they're further away, they'll be staggered.
  * Appears to have an innate small window of hyperarmor.
  * Using Unfaltering Prayer (Talisman weapon art) further extends hyperarmor capabilities.
  * Useful for knocking enemies off cliffs, or knocking some bosses down, such as the Watchers or the Deacons, giving players breathing space.
  * Can be used on NPC's at Firelink Shrine without them becoming hostile.
  * Using the [Golden Wing Crest Shield](/Golden+Wing+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Golden Wing Crest Shield") and [Sacred Bloom Shield](/Sacred+Bloom+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Bloom Shield") will prevent the knockback effect due to their Spell Parry skill.
