# Dexterity

## Dexterity  
  
---  
Type | [Attribute](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats")  
Effect | _" Attribute required to wield advanced weapons.__Also improves attack strength, reduces spellcasting time and reduces damage taken when falling. "_  
  
**Dexterity** is a [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3 "Dark Souls 3"). it is an attribute for wielding advanced weapons. Also reduces spellcasting time and damage from falling.

### 

### Dexterity Effects

  * Dexterity's attack damage per point has a soft cap at 40, 60, and stops at 99.
  * Also reduces spellcasting time and damage from falling. 
    * The casting speed cap is at 50 dexterity.
  * Defenses do not scale with Dexterity directly, but rather with overall Soul Level; defense gains are not affected by starting class.
