# Covetous Silver Serpent Ring

### Covetous Silver Serpent Ring  
  
---  
  

> A silver ring depicting a snake that could have been, but never was, a dragon. Fallen foes yield more souls.  
>   
> Snakes are known as creatures of great avarice, devouring prey even larger than themselves by swallowing them whole.  
>   
> If one's shackles are cause for discontent, perhaps it is time for some old-fashioned greed.  
  
**Covetous Silver Serpent Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Covetous Silver Serpent Ring Effect

  * Fallen foes yield 10% more souls, rounded down.
  * +1 Version (NG+): +20%

  * +2 Version (NG++): +30%

  * +3 Version ([The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap")): +35%

  * Weight: 1.2

### Where to Find Covetous Silver Serpent Ring

  * In a chest behind an [illusory wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls") at the top of [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). You need to have bought the key for 20000 Souls from the handmaiden. Take the right upper staircase in Firelink Shrine. Move past the the giant-tree on the right, unlock the door and go up the tower. You will reach a bridge, jump down onto a large roof on the left of the bridge. You will find a nest with an invisible crow baby (Pickle-Pee). Go straight past the nest and drop down onto a ledge. There is a door there that leads to an area with two crossing beams. Go straight towards the opposite wall (past a second nest) and strike down the illusory wall. Head forwards and drop down, the ring is in a chest to your right.

  * If you use the tree shortcut onto the roof of the darkened Firelink Shrine (after the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves")), you can find the ring in the same location as you would at the hub firelink shrine. This is only if you have not collected it already at the Hub [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Conversely, once you have collected the ring from the darkened Firelink Shrine, the chest will be found opened and empty in the hub [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").

  * Watch [Fextra Video](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=1h53m46s "Fextra Video") at 1h 53mins 46secs in

  * +1 Version (NG+): can be found in the elevator near the first bonfire in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon").

  * +2 Version (NG++): [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). In the area after meeting [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") with the Fire Demon, located on the roof of a shack in the far back of the area. Drop off the wooden ledge outside the room with two lady ministers to access it. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/9OC7ijgMPUA "\(video location\)") or watch this [video from the bonfire](https://youtu.be/pAy4axmWla8 "video from the bonfire")

  * +3 Version ([The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap")) After meeting the first angel that shoots at you fall down onto the perch and continue into the open door archway. From there you should see 2 Knight enemies and on the right hand side there is an area you enter. You make a right, climbing the hill and going inside the door, you should be in some small quarter areas. Proceed all the way to the furthest room, in the middle of the wall to your left there is an illusory wall, reveal it. Next, climb the stairs in front of you and just before you obtain Great Soul Dreg on your left, is a balcony. Follow the balcony to the right until it forces you to drop down to another balcony with the ring on the rails.

### Covetous Silver Serpent Ring Notes

  * Can be used efficiently together with a [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice"), a [Shield of Want](/Shield+of+Want "Dark Souls 3 Shield of Want"), and a [Mendicant's Staff](/Mendicant's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Mendicant's Staff")
  * Works multiplicatively with Shield of Want, giving 10% more after the 20%, giving 32% bonus souls.
  * This ring has appeared throughout the series, previously in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covetous+Silver+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Silver Serpent Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Covetous+Silver+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Silver Serpent Ring")_.
