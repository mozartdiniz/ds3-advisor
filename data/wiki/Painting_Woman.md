**Painting Woman** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in Dark Souls 3.

### Painting Woman Information

  * Killable, but respawns after reload.
  * After she moves to the attic and [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede") is killed, talk to her. Afterward, return to corvian village and talk to the [Corvian Settler](/Corvian+Settler "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Settler") outside the house to receive a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab").
  * Cannot be summoned.
  * Will occasionally wave goodbye to the player when leaving after talking to her.

### Location

  * You can find the Painting Woman in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"), in the attic you unlock with the [Contraption Key](/Contraption+Key "Dark Souls 3 Contraption Key") after defeating [Sir Vilhelm](/Sir+Vilhelm "Dark Souls 3 Sir Vilhelm").
  * Relocates to the Ariandel Chapel after unlocking the sliding altar in the lower level of the room filled with flies and corpses. Take the ladder up the wall near the bonfire to find her.
  * If killed, can be seen at the same place you killed her.

### Drops

  * Does not give any souls when killed.
  * No items.

### Combat Information

  * Does not fight back.
