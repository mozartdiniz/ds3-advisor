# Frozen Weapon

## Frozen Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | [Sorcery](/Sorcery "Dark Souls 3 Sorcery")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
**Frozen Weapon** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> One of the spells left behind by the young sorcerer Sulyvahn before leaving the Painted World. Imbues right-hand weapon with frost.
> 
> Sulyvahn was born and raised inside the painting yet had little use for his frigid homeland, since he had not yet experienced loss.

### How to Get Frozen Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in: [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"). Found near the [Giant Flies](/Giant+Fly "Dark Souls 3 Giant Fly") just near the Rope Bridge Cave bonfire.
  * [Fextralife Video: How to acquire Frozen Weapon](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=10m55s "Fextralife Video: How to acquire Frozen Weapon")
  * [Fextralife Video: Frozen Weapon in action](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=9m18s "Fextralife Video: Frozen Weapon in action")

### Dark Souls 3 Frozen Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Lasts 60 seconds. This spell is ** _not_ affected by Lingering Dragoncrest Ring**.
  * Deals 48 [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") Buildup with every hit. The buildup does not scale with Intelligence and does not add any extra AR.
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * Useful as a support spell for characters with a low Intelligence investment.
