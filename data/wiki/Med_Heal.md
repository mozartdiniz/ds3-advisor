# Med Heal

Med Heal is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Med Heal  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**FP** | 55  
**Slots** | 1  
****Faith**** | 15  
  
> Miracle superior to Heal. Restores moderate HP for self and those in the vicinity.  
>   
> Miracles are fruit of the study of divine tales, a blessing received from the gods, through acts of prayer. Miracle strength depends on the caster's faith.

### 

### How to Get Med Heal in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 3500 souls after giving her the [Braille Divine Tome of Carim](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim").

### Dark Souls 3 Med Heal Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores HP equal to 410% * Spell Buff of used [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") or [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans")
  * Not affected by miracle rings.
  * When the button is held down you can delay the healing, ideal for co-op partners to get inside the radius.
  * The moderate faith requirement and ease of access makes this an excellent early-game option for co-op play.
  * Healing range is buffed by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
