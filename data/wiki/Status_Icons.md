# Status Icons

**Status Icons** in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") are icons displayed on the HUD, beneath the stamina bar, when various effects are active. The following is a list of the icons, with an explanation of the effects and the items that causes them.

See [Status Effects](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") page for more information about the harmful status icons and their buildup.

See [Buff Stacking](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") page for more about how different Buffs interact.
