# Covetous Gold Serpent Ring

### Covetous Gold Serpent Ring  
  
---  
  

> A gold ring depicting a snake that could have been, but never was, a dragon. Fallen foes are more likely to drop items. Snakes are known as creatures of great avarice, devouring prey even larger than themselves by swallowing them whole. If one's shackles are cause for discontent, perhaps it is time for some good old fashioned greed.  
  
**Covetous Gold Serpent Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Covetous Gold Serpent Ring Effect

  * Increases [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") +50
  * +1 Version (NG+): +75
  * +2 Version (NG++): +100
  * +3 Version (The Ringed City): +115
  * Weight: 1.2

### Where to Find Covetous Gold Serpent Ring

  * [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"). You can find Covetous Gold Serpent Ring on the quest path of [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") (4th stage). You don't have to complete the quest in order to get the ring, however the quest does take you to the rings location. 1 () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/27JnnW7lNDs "\(video location\)") ([video location from bonfire](https://youtu.be/72vQfOkudx4 "video location from bonfire"))

  * +1 Version (NG+): From the Church of Yorshka bonfire in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), proceed beyond the graveyard to the dark room with all the ghost creatures inside. Just outside the exit, take a right and drop down to a small rocky ledge below, and the ring will be on a corpse. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/Ba8amd8KPrI "\(video location\)")
  * +2 Version (NG++): [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"), after defeating [Nameless King](/Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Nameless King") it's below the boss room. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/7edckn4QqbI "\(video location\)")
  * +3 Version (The Ringed City): All the way down the stairs from the Shira and and left and up the ladder immediately on your left in the swamp. Double back across the bridge, defeating the two Ringed Knights. On a corpse in a room past them with [Lapp](/Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Lapp") inside. ([Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8coY0boMIhk))

### Covetous Gold Serpent Ring Notes

  * Can be used efficiently together with [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice"), [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") or [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin"), and [Crystal Sage's Rapier](/Crystal+Sage's+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage's Rapier").
  * 1 You can still find the ring in the cell at the Profaned Capital (where you would free [Siegward of Catarina,](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") if you would have followed his questline) using the [Old Cell Key](/Old+Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Old Cell Key").
  * This ring has appeared throughout the series, previously in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring")_.
