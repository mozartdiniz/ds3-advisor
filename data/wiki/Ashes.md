# Ashes

**Umbral Ashes** in Dark Souls 3 are used to unlock more items from [Merchants](/Merchants "Dark Souls 3 Merchants"). By giving [Merchants](/Merchants "Dark Souls 3 Merchants") different Ashes, they will sell new and different [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") and [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items"). Below is a list of Ashes that can be found in Dark Souls 3.
