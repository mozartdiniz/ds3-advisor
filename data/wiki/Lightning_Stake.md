# Lightning Stake

**Lightning Stake** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Lightning Stake  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 34   
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 35 Faith  
  
**Type** | Melee Attack  
  
> A lost dragonslaying miracle.  
>   
> Strikes with a stake of lightning.  
>   
> This tale describes the lost practices of ancient dragonslayers, who found that in order to pierce dragonscale, lightning should not be hurled as a bolt, but rather be thrust as a stake directly into the dragon's hide, to be truly effective.

### How to Get Lightning Stake in Dark Souls 3

  * Dropped by the [Carthus Sandworm](/Carthus+Sandworm "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Sandworm") in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Stake Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.3) damage up to 3 times in a single cast.
  * This miracle causes the player to summon a lightning bolt then stab it into the ground in front of them. Has a small AOE radius, but does large amounts of damage if the enemy is struck directly with the stake.
  * Works well with Spear-Type miracles in PVP. Because the initial casting animation is identical to the regular [Great Lightning Spear ](/Great+Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Great Lightning Spear") animation, it can be good for tricking opponents into rolling into a high damage stake, or getting caught by a long ranged spear.
  * Does similar damage to [Lightning Spear](/Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear") on a poor hit. 200% damage on a good hit and 300% damage on a perfect hit. 
  * A perfect Lightning Stake deals 55% more damage than a perfect melee Lightning Spear.
  * Sped up in Regulation 1.08 and in Patch 1.11, and now uses the [Dark Edge](/Dark+Edge "Dark Souls 3 Dark Edge") casting animation. 
  * Approx 1.3 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
  * Very useful for catching foes using Quickstep since the AOE will catch them.
  * This weapon is very powerful with the [Sunlight Talisman](/Sunlight+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Talisman") as you can poise cast the spell with full force.
