# Soul of Sister Friede

**Soul of Sister Friede** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Sister Friede  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Sister Friede.  
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  
>   
> Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> Friede was the first Ash to enter the painting, but together with the good Father, they chose rot over fire.  
  
### Soul of Sister Friede Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Friede's Great Scythe](/Friede's+Great+Scythe "Friede's Great Scythe") or [Rose of Ariandel](/Rose+of+Ariandel "Rose of Ariandel").

### Soul of Sister Friede Location

  * Acquired by defeating [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Sister Friede") in [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Painted World of Ariandel").

### Trivia

  * If the player speaks to [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor) with this soul in their inventory, she will make mention of Friede being one her sisters. She will state the following: "Ahh, our Lord and Liege. Twas the soul of my sister. Elfriede...A poor wench turned to Ash, who would abandon Londor... If thou wouldst, let it nourish they Lordship. And in return, do her one small kindness. Remember those who stayed by her to the end, in the shadows cast by fire... And lastly, my Lord, take up thy rightful mantle, of usurper."
