# Soul of Slave Knight Gael

**Soul of Slave Knight Gael** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Slave Knight Gael  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Slave Knight Gael.  
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. The red-hooded, wandering slave knight Gael sought the blood of the dark soul as pigment for the Painted World. But Gael knew he was no Champion, that the dark soul would likely ruin him and that he had little hope of a safe return.  
  
### Soul of Slave Knight Gael Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Gael's Greatsword](/Gael's+Greatsword) or [Repeating Crossbow](/Repeating+Crossbow).

### Soul of Slave Knight Gael Locations

  * Obtain by defeating [Slave Knight Gael (Boss)](/Slave+Knight+Gael+\(Boss\))

### Notes

  * The soul has a Humanity sprite in the middle of it, similar to the Soul of Manus, which also has a humanity sprite in the middle.

### Trivia

  * Uses a similar sprite to The soul Of Manus, from dark souls 1
