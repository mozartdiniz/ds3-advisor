# Sorceries

**Sorceries** are a type of [Magic](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic") that has the most extensive variety of damage types and effects, and scales primarily with [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence"). The player can currently cast sorceries with all [Staves](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves"), [Immolation Tinder](/Immolation+Tinder "Dark Souls 3 Immolation Tinder"), [Heysel Pick](/Heysel+Pick "Dark Souls 3 Heysel Pick"), [Cleric's Candlestick](/Cleric's+Candlestick "Dark Souls 3 Cleric's Candlestick"), [Golden Ritual Spear](/Golden+Ritual+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Golden Ritual Spear"), and the [Crystal Chime](/Crystal+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Chime"). There are a total of 38 sorceries, some of which are unlocked from [Merchants](/Merchants "Dark Souls 3 Merchants") by giving them unique [Key Items](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") called Scrolls.
