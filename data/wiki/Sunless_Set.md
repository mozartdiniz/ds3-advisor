**Sunless Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of a knight from the Sunless Realms, known for their resistance to both magic and the dark.  
>   
> Formed of a silk veil and silver headband. (Helm)  
> Metal plating and chainmail, treated with silver. (Chest)  
> Iron gauntlets, partially treated with silver. (Gauntlets)  
> Metal leggings and chainmail, treated with silver. (Legs)
> 
>   
> Sunless Knights serve the nameless moon, and perhaps it is for this reason the attire casts a feminine silhouette.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 23000 souls upon completing [Sirris'](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") questline, Or Upon killing [Sirris](/Sirris "Dark Souls 3 Sirris").
