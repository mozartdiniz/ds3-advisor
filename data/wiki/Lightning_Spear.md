# Lightning Spear

**Lightning Spear** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

  

## Lightning Spear  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 23  
****Slots Used**** | 1  
****Requirements**** | 20 Faith  
****Type**** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Miracle allegedly used by Warriors of Sunlight.  
>   
> Hurls a lightning spear.  
>   
> The spears inflict lightning damage, providing an effective counter to magic or fire.   
> Especially powerful against metal armor and dragons.

### How to Get Lightning Spear in Dark Souls 3

  * [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"): From the Old Wolf of Farron Bonfire, take the elevator up and make a right onto the platforms on the right of the gate. Follow that path until the end. The Lightning spear miracle is found next to the dead Dragon.

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.45) damage.
  * It takes little more than a second to cast with ~50 Virtual Dex.
  * Creates a small AoE lightning burst on hit.
  * Creates a second hitbox in melee range that deals an extra 75% of the lightning spear's damage.
  * Has upgrades in [Great Lightning Spear](/Great+Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Great Lightning Spear") and [Sunlight Spear](/Sunlight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Spear")
