**Outrider Knight Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3.**   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of an Irithyll outrider knight. Enveloped in a dimly cool air.
> 
> The knights were given the eyes of the Pontiff, but the eyes transformed them into savage, raving warriors who only knew how to serve as mindless guards.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by a [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight") behind an illusory wall near the top of the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/cUr3lVAzf-E?t=19m24s "Video Location") (Fextralife video starting at 19m 24s)

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Very high [frost](/Frost+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Frost Damage") resistance. Especially useful when fighting enemies that apply [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") like Outrider Knights and [Vordt](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Vordt of the Boreal Valley").
