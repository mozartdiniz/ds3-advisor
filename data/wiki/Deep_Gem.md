# Deep Gem

**Deep Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Deep Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Found in the dregs of the Cathedral of the deep.   
>   
> Used in infusion to deep weapons.   
>   
> Deep weapons inflict dark damage, but lose scaling effects.   
>   
> There is a darkness that lies beyond human ken.  
  
### Deep Gem Usage

  * Used for Deep weapon [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion").
  * Deep weapons inflict [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"), but also lose stat scaling.

### Deep Gem Locations

  * At [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), Bonfire 2, head up the elevator through the first opened shortcut path (on the right if facing the altar). Go down the narrow set of stairs, before the large open room where you encounter the first giant, and you'll find an [Evangelist](/Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Evangelist") peeking over a balcony. It is between the Evangelist and the balcony. 
  * On a body in the Water Reserve bonfire room.
  * Dropped by [Thralls](/Thrall "Dark Souls 3 Thrall") in the Cathedral of the Deep.
  * Dropped by Deacons in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") and [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"), with a droprate of around 1% before item discovery.

### Notes

  * Requires the [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.
  * The Deep Gem, along with [Fire Gem](/Fire+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Fire Gem"), can infuse with shields for increasing elemental defense.
