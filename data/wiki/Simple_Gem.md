# Simple Gem

**Simple Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Simple Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite, said to be an object of infatuation for victims of stunted development.  
>   
> Used in infusion to create simple weapons.  
>   
> Simple weapons inflict magic damage and restore FP very gradually, to help even a simpleton muster some mettle.  
  
### Simple Gem Usage

  * Used for Simple [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion").
  * Simple weapons deal [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") which scales with [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence").
  * Simple weapons also restore [FP](/FP "Dark Souls 3 FP") while the weapon/shield is visible.

### Simple Gem Locations

  * Rare drop from [Jailers](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer") in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") (1%)
  * [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") on a corpse across the bridge, immediately on the left when going down the stairs, next to 3 jailers and some cage spiders.
  * One dropped by crystal lizard above Pontiff Sulyvahn bonfire (which is found by taking the one pathway leading opposite to an elevator shortcut before Anor Londo). It's where the fire-casting undead are at.
  * One in the Painted World of Ariandel, Corvian Settlement. From the Corvian Settlement bonfire, go across the bridge and drop down behind the gated area to the left containing a lever, a Sewer Centipede, and a Simple Gem.

### Notes

  * Simple equipment will restores 1 FP every 5 seconds. Every upgrade level will reduce the tick interval by 0.2 seconds. 

**Upgrade Level** | **Tick Interval** | **Effective FP/second**  
---|---|---  
+0 | 5s | **0.2 FP/s**  
+1 | 4.8s | **0.208 FP/s**  
+2 | 4.6s | **0.217 FP/s**  
+3 | 4.4s | **0.227 FP/s**  
+4 | 4.2s | **0.238 FP/s**  
+5 | 4s | **0.250 FP/s**  
+6 | 3.8s | **0.263 FP/s**  
+7 | 3.6s | **0.278 FP/s**  
+8 | 3.4s | **0.294 FP/s**  
+9 | 3.2s | **0.313 FP/s**  
+10 | 3s | **0.333 FP/s**  
  

  * The simple effect also stacks with other simple equipment. Assuming two +10 simple items are equipped, you will regenerate 2 FP every 3 seconds (effective 0.666 FP/s). 
    * Weapons that come in pairs when two-handed, such as [Fist](/Fist+&+Claws "Dark Souls 3 Fist & Claws") weapons, will not stack the effect. Two individual weapon slots need to be filled in order to stack the effect.
  * Simple weapons cannot be buffed with resins or magical weapon buffs.
  * It may be a good idea to infuse a parrying item (such as the [Buckler](/Buckler "Dark Souls 3 Buckler"), [Parrying Dagger](/Parrying+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Parrying Dagger"), or [Caestus](/Caestus "Dark Souls 3 Caestus") in the offhand) with the Simple gem, as you are unlikely to use these items to block actual attacks in a PvP scenario. Therefore, you sacrifice a very small amount of damage absorption in exchange for a small FP regen buff. 
  * Requires the [Giant's Coal](/Giant's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Giant's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.

### Trivia

  * Has a similar effect to [Crescent Upgrades](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crescent+Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Crescent Upgrades") from Demon's Souls.
