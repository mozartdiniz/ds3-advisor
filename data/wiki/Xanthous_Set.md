### Location/Where to Find

  * The crown is dropped by [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") after killing her invading phantom in either [Road of Sacrifices ](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") or [Farron Keep ](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep")or can be gained by killing the [Man-grub](/Man-grub "Dark Souls 3 Man-grub") that spawns in Rosaria's Bedchamber after both Yhorm and Aldrich are defeated. Defeating The Abyss Watchers boss will cause Yellowfinger Heysel to no longer invade, so the first method must be done before beating the boss. The rest of the armor set can be bought from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 24,000 souls after giving her [Xanthous Ashes](/Xanthous+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Xanthous Ashes") (found in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") in the room with lots of [Jailers](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer")).

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The set is visually drastically different compared to its appearance in Dark Souls 1 and 2. The crown is now shaped like a mushroom and is slightly reminiscent of the Monk's Head Collar from Demon's Souls. The rest of the set also now leaves the wearer fully clothed. Much of the set is stained with blood.
