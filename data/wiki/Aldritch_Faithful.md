# Aldrich Faithful

## Aldrich Faithful  
  
---  
_The holy symbol of the Cathedral of the Deep, and crest of those who see beyond fire to the age of deep waters. Equip to pledge oneself to the Aldrich Faithful covenant._ _The faithful ensure that Aldrich, Devourer of Gods, remains undisturbed, by taking the form of loyal spirits and hunting down those who would trespass the ruined cathedral._ _Summoning takes place automatically while this is equipped._  
  
**Aldrich Faithful** is a [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").The covenant can be gained by equipping the item Aldrich Faithful which is obtained from the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"). While a member of the Covenant, faithful phantoms are blue with a red tint. Will be summoned randomly to defend around the [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") area.

### Aldrich Faithful Information

  * You must obtain the item: Aldrich Faithful and equip it.
  * In [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), from the Pontiff Sulyvahn bonfire, proceed straight through the door, left up the stairs and right through the valley of dead giants and into the door at the far end. Immediately on your left is a wall that can be struck to reveal a ladder. Descend the ladder and fight the two [Sulyvahn's Beasts](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast") that guard [Archdeacon McDonnell](/Archdeacon+McDonnell "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon McDonnell"), who will be located in the north-west corner of the room.

### Effects of covenant

  * Faithful phantoms are blue with a red tint.
  * Will be summoned randomly to defend around the [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") area (you don't have to be in that area to be summoned).
  * If the covenant symbol is flashing-white in your screen, it means you're searching for an intruder.
  * The Matchmaking Formula is Covenant Dependent:  
  

Condition | Min | Max  
---|---|---  
Territorial Invasions by: [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron") or [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful") | Host - 20 - (Host * 0.2) | Host + (Host * 0.1)  
  
### Rank Requirements

Players can increase their rank in the covenant by offering acquired [Human Dregs](/Human+Dregs "Dark Souls 3 Human Dregs") Items to [Archdeacon McDonnell](/Archdeacon+McDonnell "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon McDonnell") in the shrine behind the [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"). These can be acquired via the successful invasion of other players whilst wearing the covenant item or as a drop from the Deacons in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") & around the [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn") bonfire. (Note: invasions using the red eye orb while wearing the covenant item will not yield you a Human Dreg. You will obtain a pale tongue.) The rank requirements are additive, meaning you only have to earn a total of 30, not 10 and then another 30, to obtain all covenant rewards.

Rank | Requirement | Rewards  
---|---|---  
0 | Initial |   
1 | 10 |  [Great Deep Soul](/Great+Deep+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Great Deep Soul")  
2 | 30 | [Archdeacon's Great Staff](/Archdeacon's+Great+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon's Great Staff")  
  
### Trivia
