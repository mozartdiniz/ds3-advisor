# Dark Defense

## Dark Defense  
  
---  
Type | Defense  
  
**Dark Defense** in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") defines the protection your character has against dark based attacks such as [ _Dark Pyromancies_ | _Dark Miracles_ ] and dark infused weapons. This statistic does not effect the damage you recieve from Physical, Magic, Fire, and Lightning.

### Dark Souls 3 Dark Defense Information

Dark Defense is mostly increased by leveling both **Intelligence** and **Faith** equally, which are the main attributes for spellcasters, clerics, and pyromancers.
