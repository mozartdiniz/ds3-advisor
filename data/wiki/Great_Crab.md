# Giant Crab

## Giant Crab  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Beast  
**Weakness** | Fire, Strike, Magic, Lightning  
**Resistances** |  Standard, Slash, Thrust  
**Immune** | Bleed  
  
**Giant Crab** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Giant Crab Enemy Description

  * A giant crab that mainly inhabits the swamp areas of [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") and [Farron Keep](/farron+keep "Dark Souls 3 farron keep")
  * Not to be confused with the Smouldering Giant Crab of the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"), nor the Icy Giant Crab of the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel")

### Giant Crab Combat Information

  * Uses its large pincer to attack the player. Deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage").
  * It can foam at the mouth and spray it at the player to do damage, as well as decrease movement speed. The ones in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") spray frost instead, which can inflict [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Weak point: When both claws are lifted into the air, the greenish egg sac area under the crab's mouth can be riposted for massive damage.
  * Weak to [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage"), [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") and [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage"). Crabs met in Farron Keep and Smouldering Lake are additionaly weak to [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Resistant to [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage") and [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"). 
  * Immune to [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), duo met in Painted World of Ariandel are immune to [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), the Farron Swamp is [Poison/Toxic](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") immune.
  * Can be fooled with [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull"), but is immune to [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport").
  * Its poise can be broken, stunning it for about 2 seconds. A finisher move can be performed by standing at its back after its poise is broken. You may also perforn this while infront of it, near the greenish egg sacks.

### Giant Crab Notes & Trivia

  * Can still be blocked with relative ease, but beware its grab attack.
  * Its right claw will draw back slowly while its mouth foams, then attempt to grab you for major damage if it connects. It can not be blocked.
  * Its left claw is actually faster and is better at comboing whereas its right claw does not drain more stamina and is easier to roll under.
  * If at low health and if the player is a sufficient distance away from it, it will attempt to burrow below ground and escape. It will re-emerge with full health and be cured of status ailments if this occurs. So it can not be killed by attacking only with ranged abilities.
  * [Quickstep](/Quickstep "Dark Souls 3 Quickstep") can be used in deep water, allowing for easy dodging of its attacks.
  * Can not be lured out of water. Once you step foot out of the swampy area, the crab will cease to follow you.
  * Smashing smaller crabs can aggro them
  * When it runs, it turns sideways and moves very fast.
  * Although the crab will not target invaders inadvertantly, all of the Great Crab's attacks can harm all phantoms. This includes Watchdogs of Farron.
  * One of the Great Crabs in Smouldering Lake, the one who walks back and forth near the breakable floor, is not hostile towards the player until provoked.
  * The _h_ _armless child protected by a powerful father_ relation between Great Crab and [Lesser Crab](/Lesser+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Lesser Crab") can be a reference to [Mushroom People](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Mushroom+People "Dark Souls 3 Mushroom People") in Dark Souls 1.
  * Greater Crabs located in Painted World of Ariandel are the only creature that will respawn without refreshing whole area.
  * Can be parried, it has to be on point though.
