# Snap Freeze

## Snap Freeze  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | [Sorcery](/Sorcery "Dark Souls 3 Sorcery")  
**FP cost** | 16  
**Slots used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Snap Freeze** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). To cast a sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> One of the spells left behind by the young sorcerer Sulyvahn before leaving the Painted World. Creates a cloud of near-freezing mist.  
>   
> Sulyvahn was born and raised inside the painting, yet had little use for his frigid homeland, since he had not yet experienced loss.

### How to Get Snap Freeze in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"): From the Depths of the Painting bonfire, head left past the Icy Giant Crab and past the [Tree Women](/Tree+Woman "Dark Souls 3 Tree Woman") until arriving in an enclosed icy area with a single smouldering[ Tree Woman](/Tree+Woman "Dark Souls 3 Tree Woman") guarding a body with the spell on it.

### Dark Souls 3 Snap Freeze Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Releases a cloud of icy mist in front of the caster, which builds up [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") on foes.
  * Frost buildup is unaffected by the caster's Intelligence.
  * The freeze effect will deal damage equal to around 15% of the victim's max health, as well as provide a damage weakness and stamina regeneration debuff for the duration of its effect. This makes it incredibly useful.
  * Because the clouds last for a while, it is possible to apply multiple frostbite procs on a single target if fire damage is used.
  * Since frostbite effect on the target is reset when struck with any kind of fire damage, this spell can be used in tandem with [Fire Surge](/Fire+Surge "Dark Souls 3 Fire Surge") to deal surprising amounts of damage for low FP cost. Do note however that you would need to use two catalysts at the same time. 
    * This can be more succinctly achieved using the [Immolation Tinder](/Immolation+Tinder "Dark Souls 3 Immolation Tinder") and [Farron Flashsword](/Farron+Flashsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Flashsword").
  * Bosses such as [Oceiros](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") and [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") are really susceptible to Frostbite. This spell guarantees its application when used against them.
  * The sound will oddly stop abruptly if the player drinks an Estus Flask immediately after casting this spell, even though this doesn't happen for any other action.
  * "Snap Freeze" is a term for the process of rapidly cooling a substance, typically for the purpose of preservation.
