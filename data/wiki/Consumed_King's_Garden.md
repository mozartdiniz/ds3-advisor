# Consumed King's Garden

**Consumed King's Garden** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. This optional path takes the Ashen One towards Oceiros, who despairs in madness in the far halls. For those wanting to reach Archdragon's Peak, this is a necessary path. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### General Information

  * **Previous:** [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric")/[Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle")
  * **Next:** [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves")
  * **Recommended Levels:** 65 to 75
  * **Bosses:** [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King")
  * **Bonfires:** Oceiros the Consumed King
  * Clearing this area opens Archdragon Peak

## Full Consumed King's Garden Walkthrough

This area can be accessed after defeating [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley") and unlocking [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") via the ladder. If you head left before entering Lothric Castle proper you will reach the area. 

### Looting the Toxic Swamp

Head down the staircase (grab an Item to the right) to come to a [ Cathedral Knight](/Cathedral+Knights "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Knights"), similar to the ones in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"). Defeat it and enter the room to its right and take the lift down & roll out to a landing leading 1/2 way down. Exit the lift and head right down the stairs to find an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") 1x. If you head left, you can drop down to a narrow staircase with a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. The staircase leads down to a toxic mist area where a [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") beast patrols. Like others encountered prior, it is weak to fire. Defeat it and explore the surrounding toxic area for a [Claw](/Claw "Dark Souls 3 Claw"), the [Shadow Set](/Shadow+Set "Dark Souls 3 Shadow Set"), [Black Firebomb](/Black+Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Black Firebomb") 2x, [Human Pine Resin](/Human+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Human Pine Resin") 1x, [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x (up staircase), [Ring of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice"), [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem") 1x. There will be several slugs and Pus of Man beasts in this mist swamp so be cautious when exploring.  
  
There is a doorway here you can take inside. It can be identified by moldy growth on the stairs leading up to it, and a dark beast lurking to its left. Head inside the doorway to find a lift. Take the lift to the top and exit to the outside. Proceed sraight through the doorway across the courtyard and enter. Straight ahead you will find a corpse against a wall with a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Head up the stairs in this room to encounter some thralls. Take the next staircase up to find a body with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Open the door to the right of this corpse to unlock a shortcut to [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle").  
  

### From Shortcut to Hawkwood

Return to the lift and take it down. Around halfway down you will be able to roll off to a ledge where you can exit the tower to the outside. Take the stairs down to find a corpse with the [Dragonscale Ring](/Dragonscale+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dragonscale Ring"). Proceed forward to find an enemy hollow on your left. Down in the next area you will find two iron knights, who can safely be sniped with a bow from this ledge. One will drop the [Magic Stoneplate Ring](/Magic+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Stoneplate Ring"). Head up the stairs on the right and proceed ahead and down the stairs to come to the fog where you will take on the area boss, [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King"). If you head the other direction, you can find [Hawkwood the Deserter's](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") summon sign while in Ember Form, just up the stairs. He's a melee fighter that can tank Oceiros fairly well if you're a magic user.  
  

### Acquiring the Path of the Dragon Gesture

After defeating him, light the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). Step forward and open the double doors. Take the hallway down to encounter a wrapped enemy that can extend an appendage from their head. Enter the room and where you will find a vessel straight ahead and a corpse with [Path of the Dragon](/Path+of+the+Dragon "Dark Souls 3 Path of the Dragon") [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") on it. This gesture will gain you access to [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"). Once you've defeated a summoned Drakeblood Knight in Archdragon Peak, you can find the [Drakeblood Set](/Drakeblood+Set "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Set") where the gesture was. A chest on your left contains a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Head into the upper section of this room to find a chest straight ahead. Loot it for a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Attack the wall behind the chest to reveal an [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls"). Proceed ahead to light the bonfire and discover the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").
