# Soul of a Crystal Sage

**Soul of a Crystal Sage** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3. You obtain it from defeating the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage).

### Soul of a Crystal Sage  
  
---  
  

> Soul of a Crystal Sage. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  The twin Crystal Sages once served as spiritual guides to the scholars of the Grand Archives, and one went on to ally with the Undead Legion.   
  
### Soul of a Crystal Sage Usage

  * Can be consumed for 3,000 Souls
  * Can be used to transpose either [Crystal Sage's Rapier](/Crystal+Sage's+Rapier) or [Crystal Hail](/Crystal+Hail).

### Soul of a Crystal Sage Locations

  * Reward for defeating [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage).

### Notes/Trivia

  * Crystal Hail is said to be one of the weakest sorceries in-game as of now, so those who wish to transpose this soul should go for the Crystal Sage's Rapier.
  * Oceiros' soul, along with this soul, are the only two souls the have a light blue hue to it. This is compareable to Aldrich and the Deacons of the Deep, in which both of their souls are the only ones to a dark blue hue to them.
