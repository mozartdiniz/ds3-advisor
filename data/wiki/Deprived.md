**Deprived** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")****** offers a unique challenge for players who enjoy building their character from the ground up. Starting at Soul Level 1, the Deprived is a blank slate with all stats set at 10, providing a balanced foundation for any build. While this may make the Deprived weaker in the early stages, it offers flexibility, allowing players to tailor the character's progression to suit their preferred playstyle. The Deprived’s starting equipment, like the Master’s Attire and Uchigatana, provides decent early-game utility, though the class is rarely considered optimal for min/maxing due to the even stat distribution.

This is a similar challenge faced by the Deprived class in previous Dark Souls games, where specialized builds with more focused stat distributions often outperform it. However, the Deprived class remains a solid choice for those aiming for an SL1 run or unique challenge builds, such as nudey runs, where minimal equipment and level optimization are key.

> Naked and of unknown origin. Either an unimaginable fool in life, or was stripped of possessions upon burial.

### Deprived Starting Equipment in DS3

The Deprived Class in Dark Souls 3 starts with the following equipment:

**Weapons** :

  * [Club](/Club "Dark Souls 3 Club")
  * [Plank Shield](/Plank+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Plank Shield")

**Armor** :

  * [Loincloth](/Loincloth "Dark Souls 3 Loincloth")

### Dark Souls 3 Deprived Notes and Tips

  * Balanced stat and starts with Soul Level 1.
  * While weak at first, you can eventually develop your desired stat pretty easily.
  * For early weapon and armor, you may go for [Master's Attire](/Master's+Attire "Dark Souls 3 Master's Attire") and [Uchigatana](/Uchigatana "Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana").
  * Good balaced class for trying builds but is rarely optimal for min / maxing. 
    * Having every stat start at 10 means other classes with more focused distribution will often times lead to better optimization depending on builds.
    * This is a similar issue with the deprived class in previous DS games.
