# Standard Shields

[Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in [Dark Souls III](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") are an integral part of survival as you begin to understand enemy mechanics. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, shields are widely available in various sizes which allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.
