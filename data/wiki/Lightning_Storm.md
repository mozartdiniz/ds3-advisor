# Lightning Storm

**Lightning Storm** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Lightning Storm  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 36  
**Slots Used** | 2  
**Requirements** | 45 Faith  
**Type** | Attack  
  
> Miracle of the Nameless King, ally to the ancient dragons.  
>   
> Calls forth furious bolts of lightning.  
>   
> Once a slayer of dragons, the former king and war god tamed a Stormdrake, on which he led a lifetime of battle. This miracle is likely a tale of their bond.

### How to Get Lightning Storm in Dark Souls 3

  * [Transposed](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") with [Soul of the Nameless King](/Soul+of+the+Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Nameless King") and 5000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Storm Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.35) [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage") during the startup explosion, (spell buff x 1.45) for the lightning shockwave that follows
  * Summon lightning to create a wave of sparks crawling across the ground (circle shaped AoE).
  * Sped up in Regulation 1.08 and again in Patch 1.11.
  * Approx 1.5 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
