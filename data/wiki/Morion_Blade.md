**Morion Blade** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. The Morion Blade is a reward from [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor"), given after completing her first questline. To obtain it, you need to retrieve Orbeck's Ashes and deliver them to Yuria. The Morion Blade is well-balanced between Dexterity and Strength, making it versatile for different builds. It also inflicts Bleed buildup, which can be further enhanced by investing in the Luck stat. One of the standout features of this weapon is its unique effect: it increases the wielder's damage by 20% when their health drops to 20% or below, making it a powerful tool for players who can strategically manage low-health situations.

> A twisted sword resembling the towers of Londor's Sable Church. Eight branching blades and thorns induce bleeding.  
>   
> The church's blessing makes the weapon revel in the agony of its owner. Heavy losses of HP boost attack, a curse most befitting its deformed appearance.  
>   
> Skill: Stance  
> While in stance, use normal attack to break a foe's guard from below, and a strong attack to slash upwards with a forward lunge.

### Morion Blade Dark Souls 3 Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * While on critical HP (below 20%) damage from all sources is increased multiplicatively by: 
    * **PVE** = 20% AR
    * **PVP** = 19% AR

  * Stacks with the [Red Tearstone Ring](/Red+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Red Tearstone Ring"), and with another Morion Blade. This can be abused with spells like Homing Soulmass or Seething Chaos to get 3 instances of damage boost stacked together.
  * The stat requirments do not need to be met for the buff to take effect.
  * The Morion Blade has a range of 34.761xl on the 1h R1 and 34.4023xl on the 2h R1.
  * This weapon is reminiscent of a weapon of the same name in _[Demon's Souls](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade")_.

### Morion Blade Dark Souls 3 Location

  * Received from [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") after you give her the ashes of [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim")
  * If Orbeck dies on his own as part of his questline and you loot his [ashes](/Orbeck's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck's Ashes") from the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"), Yuria will still accept them. The only requirement is that the quest where Yuria asks you to murder Orbeck has been previously started.
  * If you don't have enough int (less than 10) Orbeck wont follow you to Firelink Shrine, if you kill him, you'll get his ashes but Yuria won't do anything. To get this weapon, you either need to kill Orbeck in Firelink Shrine after talking to Yuria or progress Orbeck's questline all the way to the end. Doing the latter will let you get his ashes without having to actually kill him.
