# Soul of Champion Gundyr

**Soul of Champion Gundyr** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Champion Gundyr  
  
---  
  

> Soul of champion Gundyr. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  Once, a champion came late to the festivities, and was greeted by a shrine without fire, and a bell that would not toll.   
  
### Soul of Champion Gundyr Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Gundyr's Halberd](/Gundyr's+Halberd) or [Prisoner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain)

### Soul of Champion Gundyr Locations

  * •Obtain from defeating [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr)

### Notes

• If done properly, this is the only soul that can be glitched into being used twice.
