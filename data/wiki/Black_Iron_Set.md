**Black Iron Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor made of black iron, from the set of armor for which Knightslayer Tsorig was infamously known.  
>   
> Offers extensive and particularly effective protection from fire.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 21,500 souls after defeating [Knight Slayer Tsorig](/Knight+Slayer+Tsorig "Dark Souls 3 Knight Slayer Tsorig") in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * A direct reference to the first Dark Souls, where it is worn by an NPC named Black Iron Tarkus.
  * Has excellent slash and fire defense.
  * Grants the second highest fire resistance for an armor set when all pieces are worn, surpassed only by [Havel's Set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set").
