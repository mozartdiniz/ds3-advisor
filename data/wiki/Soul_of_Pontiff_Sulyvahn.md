# Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn

**Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

### Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn.  
> One of the twisted souls, seeped in strength.  
>   
> Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> Pontiff Sulyvahn of Irithyll imprisoned a god of the old royalty in the abandoned cathedral, to be fed to the devourer.  
  
### Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn Usage

  * Can be consumed for 12,000 [Souls](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls").
  * Can be used to make [Greatsword of Judgment](/Greatsword+of+Judgment "Dark Souls 3 Greatsword of Judgment") and [Profaned Greatsword](/Profaned+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Greatsword").

### Soul of Pontiff Sulyvahn Locations

  * Defeat [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn").
