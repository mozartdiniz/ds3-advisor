# The Ringed City

**The Ringed City DLC** is the second and final [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC") for **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. It was announced on January 23rd, 2017 by Bandai Namco and released on March 28th, 2017. It is recommended by the developers for players that have cleared [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). This can be accessed from the Kiln of the First Flame, where you'll need to go to a bonfire near a ramp that leads to the Soul of Cinder Arena. You can also access this from Sister Friede's arena located in the Painted World of Ariandel. These entry points allow players to venture into new areas and face unique challenges within the DLC. 

  * On April 21st, Dark Souls 3: [The Fire Fades GOTY Edition](/Dark+Souls+3+The+Fire+Fades+Edition "Dark Souls 3 The Fire Fades Edition") released which includes the base game as well as both expansions, [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") and The Ringed City.
  * The Ringed City launched March 28th on PS4, Xbox One and PC for 14.99 or is part of the Season Pass.

## The Ringed City DS3 DLC Information

Explore a brand new convoluted region, inhabited by new enemies, mysteries and characters. Fight your fight through [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)") with new armor, weapons and magic!

_Fear not the dark, ashen one. Journey to the world’s end to search for the[Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)") and encounter new lands, new bosses, new enemies with new armor, magic and items. Experience the epic final chapter of a dark world that could only be created by the mind of Hidetaka Miyazaki. This new adventure has players chasing after the [Slave Knight Gael](/Slave+Knight+Gael "Dark Souls 3 Slave Knight Gael") to the literal end of the world as he searches for the Dark Soul of Humanity._

_At the close of the Age of Fire, as the world ends and all lands converge upon themselves, a lone adventurer descends into the madness of the earth and uncovers the secrets of the past. As players make their way to the fabled Ringed City they will encounter ancient beasts, a new cast of[characters](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") teetering on the edge of insanity, new armor, weapons, magic, and at the bottom of it all, a long lost city filled with new horrors for players to overcome._

__

### How to Access the DS3 DLC

  * The Ringed City DLC is accessed by traveling to [Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame"), and finding a new bonfire before [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder") that transports you to the DLC location. There is also a bonfire you can find in the Painted World of Ariandel, in the chapel by [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede"). For this method to work be sure you are porting to the Sister Friede bonfire first. If you last saved there and then start the DLC the bonfire will not be there. Simply port to Firelink and port back and it will be there.

### What's new in the Ashes of Ariandel DLC?

The DLC introduces a new location where the expansion takes place: [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)"). This new location brings new [Bosses](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses"), [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields"), [Spells](/Spells "Dark Souls 3 Spells"), [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor"), [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items"), and [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs"), all of which are listed below. This expansion also delves deeper into the lore of the Dark Souls universe, revealing the fate of the inhabitants of the Ringed City, known as the Pygmies.

### Can I return to the base game after the DLC?

Yes, you can return to the main game or base locations after the DLC by simply going to the bonfires in the Ringed City. The same process applies when you want to go back to the Ringed City. This mechanic in the DLC ensures that players can explore the new content without losing progress in their primary journey.

### What level should I be for the DLC in DS3?

It is recommended to be at least level 100-120 before starting The Ringed City DLC to ensure you are prepared for the high difficulty level of the enemies and bosses. This level range makes sure that you are prepared to take on the new challenges and foes within the expansion.
