# Poison

### [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison")  
  
---  
  
###   
  
**Poison** is a [status effect](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

### Poison Information

  * Lasts 90 Seconds, or until cured
  * In PVE: 
    * Base damage is 3 HP/s
    * Plus an additional amount that scales up with player Max HP
  * In PVP: 
    * Base damage is 7 HP/s
    * Plus an additional 0.07% of player hp/s
    * Total of 630 + 6.3% of player HP over 90 seconds
  * Unlike [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic") and [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), Poison has no effect on stamina regeneration. 
  * Poison inflicted by the [Poison Spores](/Poison+Spores "Dark Souls 3 Poison Spores") weapon art of the [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") is unique, and scales very highly with max HP, making it the most effective form of poison. 
  * Poison total damage is unaffected by [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck")
  * Poison build up is affected by Luck for certain sources (detailed below)

  * [Rotten Ghru Dagger](/Rotten+Ghru+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Ghru Dagger")
  * [Rotten Ghru Curved Sword](/Rotten+Ghru+Curved+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Ghru Curved Sword")

  * Weapons infused with [Poison Gem](/Poison+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Poison Gem")
    * Varies by weapon

    * 99 Luck = 37 Poison buildup

The following do not scale with Luck or weapon upgrade level: 

  * [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") weapon art = 42 Poison buildup per tick in cloud
  * Bow with [Poison Arrow](/Poison+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Poison Arrow") = 30 Poison buildup
  * Pyromancy [Poison Mist](/Poison+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Poison Mist") = 15 Poison buildup per tick in cloud

### Dark Souls 3 Poison Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Poison shares resistance properties with [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic").
  * Poison was overhauled in the 1.11 patch
  * The [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") poison is the unusual exception, with a scaling value so high that at some point it even outperforms Toxic.
  * [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") appears to only speed up the application of poison, allowing you to poison an enemy with fewer hits. It appears to have no effect on poison damage, nor on poison duration.
  * Weapons that have poison naturally on it can be imbued with Poison, or have a Poison Resin used on it, however this appears to only help apply the poison faster, and does not affect poison damage or duration.
  * Tested on Pontiff Sulyvhan (regular NG), most poison effects did 13 damage per second, Dung Pies (Toxic) did 23 damage per second, Storyteller Staff's poison did 54 damage per second
  * Ghru weapon poisons, poison arrows, the Poison Mist pyromancy, Rotten Pine Resin, and Poison Gem infusion all appear to apply the same identical poison (comparable to the Farron Keep swamp poison)
