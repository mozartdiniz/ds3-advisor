# Poison Mist

**Poison Mist** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast it, you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

  

## Poison Mist  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 18  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 0 Intelligence / 10 Faith  
  
> Unique pyromancy crafted by Eingyi, a heretic inhabiting the Great Swamp long ago. Creates a poison mist.  
>   
> Eingyi was driven from the Great Swamp, however his pyromancy continues to succeed him.  
>   
> Perhaps dwellers of the Great Swamp were reminded that their very existence is one rooted in heresy. 

### How to Get Poison Mist in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 2,000 souls after he has been given the [Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome](/Great+Swamp+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Poison Mist Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Applies 15 Poison buildup per tick in cloud.
  * Outclassed by [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") Weapon Art (42 poison buildup per tick in cloud while also doing more poison damage)
