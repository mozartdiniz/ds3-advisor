## Dark Souls 3 Mechanics & Beginner Tips

### Movement & Actions

Below are the basic movement and interacting mechanics that you will be using often while playing the game, you may head over to the [Controls](/Controls "Dark Souls 3 Controls") page for the full control configuration.

  * **Left Stick** : Move your character, pushing it will allow your character to walk and run.
  * **X** (PS4), **A** (Xbox), **E** (PC): To interact with an item or a message.
  * **R1/R2** : Pressing R1 performs a light attack while pressing R2 performs a heavy attack.
  * **L1** : Performs a guard if you have a shield equipped.
  * **□** (PS4) or X (Xbox): Uses your currently equipped [Item](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items").

### Death

If you have died on your journey, your souls appears as a green blood stain and will remain at the location where you died. If you die again before obtaining your lost souls, a new blood stain will appear and you will lose your previously lost souls. You will respawn at the last bonfire you rested at.

### Battle Skills

A new mechanic for the series, [battle skills](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") are weapon abilities executed with L2. Any weapons equipped to the left hand (including any two-handed weaponry) can execute these abilities which consume FP. Battle Skills vary, and can be useful in a number of situations. Mastering when and how to employ certain battle skills can drastically improve your play. 

### Parry System

Understanding the parry system means that there are attacks that cannot be parried. Sometimes, you might end up performing a partial parry instead of a perfect parry. You will still be able to block some damage when doing either parry. Carrying less weight will allow you to perform more parries as it will not take up so much stamina, that being said, make sure to space out techniques that take up stamina to perform. Different [weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") have different timings when performing a parry, so learning the timing of the different weapons while studying the moves of a boss will help you significantly.

### [Bonfires](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires")

Rest at one of the many bonfires found in the world to restore your health and FP (focus points). Bonfires also replenish your Estus Flask uses; bonfires also cure all status effects. (poison, toxic, etc.)  
Resting at a bonfire resurrects most enemies you have killed since your last rest, certain enemies will not respawn.

### Rolling & I-Frames

When you execute a roll, you are temporarily immune from damage. Your roll speed (and the number of frames for which you are invincible) relies on your equipment load. At 70% or greater [equipment load](/Equipment+Load "Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load"), you will "fat roll", a much slower rolling animation in which you are largely vulnerable.

Mastering rolling makes the game substantially easier. Make sure to look into the effects of [Poise](/poise "Dark Souls 3 poise") and [Carthus Milkring](/Carthus+Milkring "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Milkring"). The [combat](/Combat "Dark Souls 3 Combat") page also has useful information.

### Co-Op & PvP

Co-op involves summoning white phantoms or being summoned into another player's world. When you are in Lord of Cinder mode, you will find white summon signs on the ground; activate these signs and you will summon another player into your world until you either kill the area boss or cross a particular time limit (40 minutes). Summoned players keep their souls if they die in another player's world, and receive a portion of all souls acquired through cooperative play (do **not** wear a [Ring of Sacrifice](/ring+of+sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 ring of sacrifice") while summoned, it will still break). In addition, certain covenants make it easier to be summoned in certain situations. To be summoned into another player's world, you must leave a summon sign using the [White Sign Soapstone](/White+Sign+Soapstone "Dark Souls 3 White Sign Soapstone") acquired from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").

PvP can involves being summoned as a Red Phantom invader, using a [Cracked](/Cracked+Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Cracked Red Eye Orb") or [Full ](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb")Red Eye Orb to invade a random player's world, summon a Red Phantom by touching red summon signs or invade automatically if some convenants conditions are met. When invading, area enemies will not attack you as long as the host doesn't use a [Seed of a Giant Tree](/Seed+of+a+Giant+Tree "Dark Souls 3 Seed of a Giant Tree"). The objective of an invasion is to kill the host player (or any phantom if you are in the [Mound Maker](/Mound-Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Maker")), which earns you a number of souls. If you used an orb, you will earn an Ember as well. Some covenants also give additional items.

### Drak Souls 3 Beginner Tips

### Boss Weaknesses

Apart from studying their move sets and seeing what type of attacks they do, they will always have a weakness. Finding the right weapon, attack style, and items to use to go against different [Bosses](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses").

### Experiment with Builds

At the beginning of the game, you pick out your starting class. When you get more experience in the game, you can start to build around your class and experiment by allocating your points around and joining Covenants.

### Obtain a Torch

Torches are normally overlooked and ignored until you need it, they can be used in dark areas and avoid getting ambushed in the dark. When beginning the game, save yourself some time by purchasing it from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").

### Always Explore

Apart from the many [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") you can obtain and the locations you can explore, there are several secrets you can find as well, such as [Illusory Walls](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls"). The more obvious illusory walls have archways built on the top part of the walls, struck them to reveal a passage. Check our [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Wall "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Wall") page to see all the illusory walls you can find in Dark Souls 3.

### Mimic Chests

There are chests in Dark Souls 3 that will either hold loot or will attack you. You can attack every chest before you open it since attacking the chest will not affect the loot inside it or if you observe the chest, you can find that it "breathes" when the lid of the chest slightly opens and closes. This is a sign for you to find out if it is a normal chest or a mimic chest and avoid getting attacked.
