# Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier

**Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier** is a [Soul](/Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier  
  
---  
  

> Large soul found in the corpse of a nameless soldier.Use to acquire souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier Usage

  * Use to gain 3000 souls.

### Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier Locations

  * 5x Found at the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"):  

    * 1x on a corpse next to the Central Irithyll bonfire ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=6m16s "Video"))
    * 1x on a corpse next to the fountain in the courtyard leading towards the Church of Yorshka bonfire. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=14m30s "Video"))
    * 1x on an island in the swamp leading to irithyll dungeons. The swamp is directly below the bridge that you used to enter the Irithyll of the Boreal Valley. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=1h12m4s "Video"))
    * 1x on the stairs leading up to the Irithyll elevator shortcut, towards the distant manor bonfire. The stairs are just after the Silver Knight looking at a picture of [Gwynevere (Dark Souls)](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwynevere "Gwynevere \(Dark Souls\)"). ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=2h5m1s "Video"))
