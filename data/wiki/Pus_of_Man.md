**Pus of Man** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Pus of Man Enemy Description

  * These seemingly ordinary undeads will quickly transform into an abyss-corrupted monstrosity upon spotting the player.
  * They appear in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"), [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"), and [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden").
  * Sweep attacks have a wide range and are very dangerous.

### Pus of Man Combat Information

  * Whenever possible, kill the undead in the 2-3 seconds before it transforms. Its total health will multiply drastically after transforming, and it will be restored to full health upon successful transformation.
  * They are resistant to [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage") but very weak to [fire](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"). After transforming, it will be stunned for several seconds by any source of fire damage, trivializing the encounter. You can easily kill it with Pyromancies, needs only 2 hits with [Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Bed Vestiges"), but be sure that you're aiming the "black serpent" part of the enemy (one hit does about 1500 dmg depending on your build). One [Firebomb](/Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Firebomb") will stun it long enough to get 2-3 free hits in. Fire-infused weapons will achieve the same effect. A [Torch](/Torch "Dark Souls 3 Torch") swing can also do the trick.
  * [Follower Javelin](/Follower+Javelin "Dark Souls 3 Follower Javelin") infused with [Fire](/Fire+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Fire Gem") or [Chaos](/Chaos+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Gem") can set it on fire from a distance with its weapon art, even without FP.
  * Weak to [Farron Greatsword](/Farron+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Greatsword") and [Wolf Knight's Greatsword](/Wolf+Knight's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Wolf Knight's Greatsword").
  * Its large size makes it easy to hit. Avoid the front.
  * Can be fooled with [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull").
  * Can be affected by [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport") both before and after transforming, but applied rapport effect ends immediately upon successful transformation and must be applied again.
  * New players encountering these enemies on the High Wall of Lothric who are unsure of their ability to kill the undead before the transformation can find some black firebombs in the area. 3 of them should render the enemy close enough to kill with a couple of hits or another normal firebomb.

### Pus of Man Notes & Trivia

  * The first time you kill the mutated versions in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") they have a guaranteed drop of 1 [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") and 1 [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember").
  * Dark Souls lore describes the serpent as an imperfect dragon that is a symbol of the undead.
  * Their abyssal growths also look similar to what is consuming [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr").
  * Their attacks can damage all other enemies, including invaders.
