# Rapport

**Rapport** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Rapport  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence / 0 Faith  
  
**Type** | Pyromancy  
  
> Advanced pyromancy of Quelana, a daughter of the Witch of Izalith.  
>   
> Charms the enemy, making them a temporary ally.  
>   
> The living are lured by flame, and this tendency is elemental to the art of pyromancy.

### How to Get Rapport in Dark Souls 3

  * Purchased from [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 7000 souls after giving her [Quelana Pyromancy Tome](/Quelana+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Rapport Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Cast time is roughly 2 seconds.
  * Lasts 30 seconds.
  * Highly effective against red eyed elite mobs.
  * Unlike in Dark Souls 1, Rapport is a ranged spell.
  * Can potentially miss fast-moving enemies.
  * There isn't a limit to the total number of creatures you can have affected by Rapport.
  * There is no known way to increase the time an enemy is influenced.
  * Affected enemies: 
    * Will not target you or any of your allies.
    * Are considered your allies, and can be buffed and healed by [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") that affect allies. And if they are doing a buff while you are rapporting them you will receive said buff i.e [Hollow Priest](/Hollow+Priest "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Priest")'s stronger sacred oath which stacks with normal sacred oath.
    * Cannot be locked-on to, but can still be backstabbed.
    * Can still hurt you if you happen to be in the way of their attacks.
    * Will react to invaders and attack them.
  * Can affect the following enemies: 
    * [Consumed King's Knight](/Consumed+King's+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Knight")
    * [Corvian](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian")
    * [Corvian Storyteller](/Corvian+Storyteller "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Storyteller")
    * [Deacon](/Deacon "Dark Souls 3 Deacon")
    * [Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon")
    * [Cathedral Grave Warden](/Cathedral+Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Grave Warden")
    * [Hollow](/Hollow "Dark Souls 3 Hollow")
      * Includes the lantern-holding ones and the wizard ones just before [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") boss
    * [Hollow Assassin](/Hollow+Assassin "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Assassin")
    * [Hollow Slave](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave")
    * [Hollow Soldier](/Hollow+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Soldier")
    * [Lothric Knight](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight")
    * [Lothric Priest](/Hollow+Priest "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Priest")
    * [Lycanthrope Hunter](/Lycanthrope+Hunter "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope Hunter")
    * [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic")
    * [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man")
      * Works both before and after transforming, but Rapport ends when it transforms
    * [Winged Knight](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight")
    * [Ascended Winged Knight](/Ascended+Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ascended Winged Knight")
    * [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope")
    * Farron Follower
    * Millwood Knight
    * Corvian Knight

  * Cannot affect the following enemies: 
    * Invaders (Players nor NPCs)
    * Any Boss or Wyvern
    * [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight")
    * [Boreal Immolator](/Boreal+Immolator "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Immolator") (Fire Witches in Irithyll)
    * [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith")
    * [Giant](/Giant "Dark Souls 3 Giant")
    * [Greater Crab](/Greater+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Greater Crab")
    * [Jailer](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer") (neither black nor white robed)
    * [Lesser Crab](/Lesser+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Lesser Crab")
    * [Man-Serpent Summoner](/Man-Serpent+Summoner "Dark Souls 3 Man-Serpent Summoner")
    * [Poisonhorn Bug](/Poisonhorn+Bug "Dark Souls 3 Poisonhorn Bug")
    * [Pontiff Knight](/Pontiff+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Knight")
    * Rat (large nor small)
    * [Rotten Slug](/Rotten+Slug "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Slug")
    * [Serpent-Man](/Serpent-Man "Dark Souls 3 Serpent-Man") (large nor small)
