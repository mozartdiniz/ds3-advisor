# Rotten Slug

## Rotten Slug  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Bug  
**Weakness** | Thrust & Fire  
**Resistances** | Strike & Dark  
**Immune** | Poison  
  
**Rotten Slug** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Rotten Slug Enemy Description

  * These giant slugs are found in abundance around [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") and in the [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden").
  * They attack primarily by spitting poison but are capable of melee attacks as well.
  * Poison proof, beware the poisonous environment most live on.

### Rotten Slug Combat Information

  * Very slow, often appear in groups.
  * Move (roll not needed in dry land) to the side as it often tries to spit at you, spit is slow.
  * Its charge is very slow and telegraphed, one hit is all that is needed to interrupt it.

### Rotten Slug Notes & Trivia

  * Drops [Heavy Gem](/Heavy+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Gem") (Rare)
  * Drops [Poison Gem](/Poison+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Poison Gem") (Rare)
  * Drops [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem") (Rare, and only in the Consumed King's Garden)
  * TL:DR, each gem has a 2% chance of dropping from a slug with ~420 item discovery.
  * After doing 150 runs through Farron Keep Ruins with full item discovery setup and 11 luck (427 item discovery) and killing the 8 slugs in the tower and the 1 on the outside for a total of 1350 slugs I found a total of 29 heavy gems and 27 poison gems. So roughly speaking you're looking at a 2% chance for each gem from each slug, or a 18 percent chance to get each gem every run. Using the demon fist and spamming light attacks, this took me an hour and 55 minutes so an average of 46 seconds a run including load times when sword coiling back to the bonfire each time, or roughly 3 minutes and 30 seconds for each gem.
