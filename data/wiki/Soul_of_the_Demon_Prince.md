# Soul of the Demon Prince

**Soul of the Demon Prince** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Soul of the Demon Prince  
  
---  
**New description:**

> The demons, birthed from a common Chaos, share almost everything between them, even the pride of their prince, and his nearly-faded flame. So that the last demon standing may rekindle it.

**Old description:**

> The Demon Prince, after his defeat to Lorian, retreated beneath the earth and lost the memory of the flame that burned within him, marking the end of demonkind.  
  
### Soul of the Demon Prince Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Seething Chaos](/Seething+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Seething Chaos") or [Demon's Scar](/Demon's+Scar "Dark Souls 3 Demon's Scar")

### Soul of the Demon Prince Locations

  * Obtain from [The Demon Prince](/The+Demon+Prince "Dark Souls 3 The Demon Prince").

### Notes

  * The soul bears a resemblance to the Bed of Chaos, especially the white ball in the middle.
