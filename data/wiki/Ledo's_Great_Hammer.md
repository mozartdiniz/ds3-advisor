**Ledo's Great Hammer** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. It was added with [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").  
  
  
  

> Great Hammer wielded by the Silver Knight Ledo. Ledo's hammer is by far the heaviest weapon amongst those wielded by the knights of Anor Londo.
> 
> Ledo, an eccentric who traveled aross the outskirts, became a close friend to the giants and even Havel, the Rock.
> 
> Skill: Call to Stone   
> Spin to lure stones to the steel hammer, and use strong attack to transition into an earth slam that shatters the stones in a shockwave.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Upgraded with Twinkling[ ](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite")Titanite.
  * Cannot be Infused. Can be Buffed with the Weapon Art.
  * Ledo's Great Hammer has a weapon range of 43.1694xl on the 1h R1, and 36.3865xl on the 2h R1.
  * Outweighs Smough's and Morne's Great Hammer by 4 units. This does not only make it the heaviest Great Hammer, but also the heaviest weapon in the game. It's also stronger than Smough's Great Hammer when 2-handed at 66 Strength (668 AR vs 681 AR )
  * This Great Hammer, along with [Smough's Great Hammer](/Smough's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Smough's Great Hammer") and [Morne's Great Hammer](/Morne's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Great Hammer"), have a higher poise health value than the other Great Hammers, with 32.9 poise health instead of the usual 30.8. This means you can have much less poise and still be able to tank through the other Ultras.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Silver Knight Ledo](/Silver+Knight+Ledo "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Ledo") when he invades you in [The Ringed City (Location)](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)") Near the very long staircase where the large enemies (Harald) are patrolling, there is a very long ladder that leads up to one of the friendly locust npc's (white preacher). The Ladder is located to the right of the long staircase (at the very bottom of it) in the swamp near 3 cleric turtle enemies. Once you went up the entire ladder there is a way that leads left to a ringed knight but there is also a way to the right, where you can drop off onto a stoney path. You need to follow the path to a room where silver Knight Ledo invades you.
  * After killing [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church") (or a player boss for the same boss fight) [Silver Knight Ledo](/Silver+Knight+Ledo "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Ledo") will no longer invade. You can however still get his weapon by reviving [Judicator Argo](/Judicator+Argo "Dark Souls 3 Judicator Argo") at the [Purging Monument](/Purging+Monument "Dark Souls 3 Purging Monument") for a varying amount of souls. Once you've revived Judicator Argo, go to the location where Silver Knight Ledo invades you and kill him to get his weapon (if Silver Knight Ledo doesn't invade use a dried finger bought from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid"), it should work).
