# Soul of the Nameless King

**Soul of the Nameless King** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Nameless King  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Nameless King. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.  
>   
> Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> The Nameless King was once a dragon-slaying god of war, before he sacrificed everything to ally himself with the ancient dragons.  
  
### Soul of the Nameless King Usage

  * Can be consumed for 16,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make the [Storm Curved Sword](/Storm+Curved+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Storm Curved Sword") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition")
  * Can be used to make the [Dragonslayer Swordspear](/Dragonslayer+Swordspear "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Swordspear") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition")
  * Can be used to make the [Lightning Storm](/Lightning+Storm "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Storm") via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition")

### Soul of the Nameless King Locations

  * Obtained from defeating [The Nameless King](/The+Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 The Nameless King")

### Soul of the Nameless King Notes

  * This is the only Boss Soul that can be transmuted into three different items, instead of only two. Thus, the Nameless King is the only boss that must be fought a minimum of three times (requiring at least Journey 3, or [NG++](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus")) in order for an individual character to acquire all his associated soul transposed loot. 
    * Alternatively, the player could [Trade](/Player+Trade "Dark Souls 3 Player Trade") for either the Storm Curved Sword or the Dragonslayer Swordspear to acquire the Nameless King's three soul transposed items before NG++ (Lightning Storm is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") and thus cannot be traded).
