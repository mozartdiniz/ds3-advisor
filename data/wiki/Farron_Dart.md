# Farron Dart

**Farron Dart** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

  

## Farron Dart  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 3  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 8 Intelligence  
  
> Variation of Soul Arrow developed by sorcerers of the Undead Legion of Farron.
> 
> Fire a soul dart.
> 
> Well known among the Farron sorceries, it is learned far and wide due to its ease of use.

### How to Get Farron Dart in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") and [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") for 1,000 souls. It is also sold by [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 500 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Farron Dart Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Farron Dart inflicts (Spell Buff x 0.52) magic damage before resistances.
  * Deals low damage, but is the fastest spell in the game and costs little [FP](/FP "Dark Souls 3 FP")
    * Its cast is near instant and combos, using the same "moveset" as [Throwing Knives](/Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Throwing Knife").
  * Has a direct upgrade, the [Great Farron Dart](/Great+Farron+Dart "Dark Souls 3 Great Farron Dart").
