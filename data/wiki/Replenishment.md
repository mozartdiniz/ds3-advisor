# Replenishment

**Replenishment** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Replenishment  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Faith  
**Type** | Heal (Regeneration)  
**Duration** | 60 Seconds  
  
> Miracle once cherished by cleric knights. Gradually restores HP.   
>   
> Replenishment is a relic of the old Lloyd faith, whose cleric knights were unfaltering in battle.

### How to Get Replenishment in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 1000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Replenishment Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores 295 HP in total with 5 HP per second over the course of its duration (first second is not triggered). 
    * The health restored per FP balances out at about 9.83 HP/FP.
    * This is the most HP-FP efficient regeneration [Miracle](/Miracle "Dark Souls 3 Miracle").
  * Stacks with [Gentle Prayer](/Gentle+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Gentle Prayer").
  * Overwritten by [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Light") and [Bountiful Sunlight](/Bountiful+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Sunlight").
  * Unaffected by Spell Buff, [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith"), or Miracle boosting rings.
  * [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") increases its duration. However, it does not change the total amount healed (295 HP). Instead, the spell lasts longer, but the HP per second is reduced.
  * The low faith requirement of this spell means it can be useful on a non-caster build, however the stat investment to use balance isn't especially worth it.
