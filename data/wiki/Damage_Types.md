# Damage Types

**Damage Type** is an important gameplay mechanic of **Dark Souls 3**. Damage is divided into two groups: Elemental and Physical. Please see [Status Effects](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") for information on abnormal statuses that can be inflicted on players and enemies.

All damage types are reflected in your AR (Attack Rating), which shows an average damage that the player may inflict on a target. However several other things are taken into account to calculate actual dealt damage:

  * Weakness/Resistance/Immunity to specific type of damage (more information on pages with specific type of damage);
  * Type of attack (strong/weak/critical strike/weapon art);
  * Hitting weak spots

## Elemental Damage in DS3

###  [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage")

  * Effective against: Armored enemies (Knights, etc.)
  * Ineffective against: Magic-oriented enemies
  * Protective items: [Magic Stoneplate Ring](/Magic+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Stoneplate Ring"), [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring"), [Blue Bug Pellet](/Blue+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Blue Bug Pellet")

###  [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage")

  * Effective against: Unarmored enemies
  * Ineffective against: Demons, Wyverns, Man-serpents, heavily-armored enemies
  * Protective items: [Flame Stoneplate Ring](/Flame+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Flame Stoneplate Ring"), [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring"), [Red Bug Pellet](/Red+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Red Bug Pellet")
  * Note: [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") and [Smouldering Rotten Flesh](/Smoldering+Rotten+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Smoldering Rotten Flesh") are immune to Fire. This is an unique attribute amongst elemental-type damages.

###  [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage")

  * Effective against: Wyverns, Man-serpents, unarmored enemies
  * Ineffective against: Skeletons
  * Protective items: [Thunder Stoneplate Ring](/Thunder+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Thunder Stoneplate Ring"), [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring"), [Yellow Bug Pellet](/Yellow+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Yellow Bug Pellet")

###  [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage")

  * Effective against: Non-Abyssal enemies
  * Ineffective against: Hollow, Abyssal
  * Protective items: [Dark Stoneplate Ring](/Dark+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dark Stoneplate Ring"), [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring"), [Black Bug Pellet](/Black+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Black Bug Pellet")

### Physical Damage in DS3

###  [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage")

  * Effective against: Fragile enemies
  * Ineffective against: Knights
  * Protective items: [Ring of Steel Protection](/Ring+of+Steel+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Steel Protection")

### [ Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage")

  * Effective against: Skeletons, Armored enemies
  * Ineffective against: Wyverns
  * Protective items: [Ring of Steel Protection](/Ring+of+Steel+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Steel Protection")

### [ Slash damage](/Slash+damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash damage")

  * Effective against: Unarmored enemies
  * Ineffective against: Armored enemies
  * Protective items: [Ring of Steel Protection](/Ring+of+Steel+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Steel Protection")

### [ Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage")

  * Effective against: Wyverns
  * Ineffective against: Knights
  * Protective items: [Ring of Steel Protection](/Ring+of+Steel+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Steel Protection")

### Damage Types Notes

  * If the player does not meet the requirements to wield a weapon, they can still use the weapon, but will deal much lower damage and for a few weapons won't be able to use the weapon art.
  * The status bars are represented inconsistently still: the first segment stops at 33, the second at 103, the third at 300 and the final one at 500, still the your values can go further. This law applies for every status.
