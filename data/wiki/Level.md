# Level

**Level** or Soul Level (SL) in **Dark Souls 3** is the progression of your character's attribute growth through the acquisition of souls. As your character earns souls, they may increase their SL by speaking with the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to spend souls for levels. Each level increases the number of souls required to proceed to the next and allows you to raise one of your primary attribute [Stats](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") by 1 point. The maximum Soul Level is 802, where each attribute is at their maximum value of 99 points.

Visit the [Build Calculator page](/Build+Calculator "Dark Souls 3 Build Calculator") for soul leveling calculations.

## Level Cost Increase Per Level

The following table lists the number of souls required for each Soul Level (e.g., a character must spend at least 706 souls to progress from SL 3 to SL 4).  
For levels 2-12, the increase of required souls between each level is about 2.5% from the previous level.  
From level 13 and beyond, the amount of souls needed to level up is expressed by the equation y = 0.02x³ + 3.06x² + 105.6x - 895, where y is the number of souls needed (rounded down) and x is the desired level.
