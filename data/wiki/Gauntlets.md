# Gauntlets

**Gauntlets** are [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3 that protects the player's arms. It is speculated that they will work the same way as previous Souls games, by simply adding their defense values to a total that is then used to calculate how much damage is taken.
