# Equipment Load

## Equipment Load  
  
---  
  
**Equipment Load** is a [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") that determines your mobility based on the weight of the armor, weapons and rings you have equipped.

### Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load Information

Each weapon, armor, and ring you have equipped, even if it is not currently held in your hands, contributes to your current **Equipment Load**. The weights are simply added together. Tools, arrows, and bolts do not weigh anything, and do not contribute to **Equipment Load**.

Your character's current and maximum **Equipment Load** is shown as a fraction on many screens. A percentage called **Weight Ratio** is also shown in the top right corner of many of your character screens. This value is obtained by dividing your current**Equipment Load** (the total weight of your equipped armor, weapons, shields and rings) with your _maximal Equipment Load_ (determined by your [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality")). Depending on your current Weight Ratio, your character's mobility changes as follows:

Weight Ratio % | Effect  
---|---  
29.9% or less | **Lightest state:** Roll farther / cover more distance, but animation has the same total duration as normal, including 13 i-frames.   
30.0% – 70.0% | **Regular state:** Normal roll with 13 i-frames.   
70.1% – 99.9% | **Slow state ("fat rolling"):** Slow and short roll with 12 i-frames. Slow back step. Stamina regenerates about 20% slower.*  
100.0% or higher | **Overburdened:** Cannot roll, back step, or run. Walking animation changes.  
  
_*Please see the[Stamina](/Stamina "Dark Souls 3 Stamina") page for stamina regeneration information._

You can increase your maximum equip load by:

  * Leveling up [Vitality ](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality")(each point increases your base maximal load by 1.0).
  * Equipping [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring") (increases your maximum equipment load by 15%/17%/18%/19%).
  * Equipping the [Ring of Favor](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor") (increases your maximum equipment load by 5%/6%//7%/8%).
  * Equipping the [Prisoner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner's Chain") ring (increases your [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality") by 5. Does not work when your [Vitality ](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality")is already at 99).

### Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * RE: the terms _fast_ and _quick_. These terms describe the _velocity_ of the player character, and not the _duration_ of the roll itself. i.e. Rolls cover more distance in the same time when having a **Weight Ratio** <30%.
  * Equipping [Prisoner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner's Chain") at 99 [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality") will increase your weight ratio as the ring weighs 0.8.
  * Unlike Dark Souls 1, **Equipment Load** has no impact on run speed or sprint speed when it is under 100%.
  * Your character's base maximal**Equipment Load** is 40.0 + 1.0 for each point of [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality").
  * The bonuses of the rings stack. Moreover, [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring") and [Ring of Favor](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor") stack multiplicative. Equipping the normal version of both rings increases your maximal load by a factor of 1.15 * 1.05 = 1.2075 and not by 1.15 + 1.05 = 1.20.
  * Roll speed and distance are both greater when the **Weight Ratio** is <30%. However, the animations for the fast and medium roll take the same amount of time to complete.
  * The maximum obtainable maximal **Equipment Load** is 178.6 and is reached when a character with 99 [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality") equips [Havel's Ring+3](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring") and [Ring of Favor +3](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor"). This means the highest load you can carry while still maintaining the fastest rolls is 53.5.
  * The [Havel Knight](/Havel+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Havel Knight") (and your character) would require a **Vitality** score of 94 to be able to roll normally (<70% _Weight Ratio_) when equipped with the complete [Havel's Set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set"), the [Dragon Tooth](/Dragon+Tooth "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Tooth"), [Havel's Greatshield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield") and [ Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring").
