# Black Eye Orb

**Black Eye Orb** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in Dark Souls 3.

### Black Eye Orb  
  
---  
  

> Arcane orb left on Rosaria's corpse. Have faith her soul can be retrieved, by invading  
> the world of her killer, and returning victorious. The Black Eye is proof of vengeance, but often   
> appears serene as it casts its gaze toward Irithyll.  
  
### Black Eye Orb Usage

  * Use to invade [Ringfinger Leonhard](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard") in Gwynevere's Chamber in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). (Room above Aldrich)

### Black Eye Orb Location

  * Found on [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth")'s corpse after talking to [Leonhard](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard") in Rosaria's chamber.
