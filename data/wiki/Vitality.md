# Vitality

## Vitality  
  
---  
Type | [Attribute](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats")  
  
**Vitality** is a [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") that defines maximum [Equipment Load](/Equipment+Load "Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load") and strongly determines [Physical Defense](/Physical+Defense "Dark Souls 3 Physical Defense") and Poison Resistance. It also affects the other defenses and resistances slightly.

### Dark Souls 3 Vitality Main Effects

_Attribute governing physical defense, though this is also affected by other attributes.__Also governs maximum equip load and poison resistance._

  * **Vitality** increases your maximum [Equipment Load](/Equipment+Load "Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load") by 1.0 per level of **Vitality** , with a hard cap of 139 at 99 **Vitality**.
  * **Vitality** strongly increases the four subtypes of [Physical Defense](/Physical+Defense "Dark Souls 3 Physical Defense") by 2-3 per point from 15 to 25 and 1-2 from 26 to 40. At lower and higher levels, each point of **Vitality** increases [Physical Defense](/Physical+Defense "Dark Souls 3 Physical Defense") by only 0.5-1 points.
  * **Vitality** also increases Poison Resistance by 3 per point of **Vitality** between a score of 30-40. It further increases Poison Resistance by 0.5 per point of **Vitality** between 40-60 and 0.25 per point between 60 and 99.
  * all other defenses by 0.4

### Dark Souls 3 Vitality Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Vitality can be increased by 5 when wearing the [Prisoner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner's Chain")
  * In addition to the above mentioned benefits, each level of **Vitality** also increases all resistances (including Poison Resistance) by a base amount defined by your [Soul Level](/Level "Dark Souls 3 Level") (character level), just like all other attributes do. It increases all resistances by 0.2 per level until [Soul Level](/Level "Dark Souls 3 Level") 60\. Between [Soul Levels](/Level "Dark Souls 3 Level") 60 and 100, each point of **Vitality** increases all resistances by 1. After [Soul Level ](/Level "Dark Souls 3 Level")100, each point of **Vitality** only increases each resistance by 0.3. At even higher Soul levels this might be reduced to 0.1 per level of **Vitality** (_additional testing required_).
