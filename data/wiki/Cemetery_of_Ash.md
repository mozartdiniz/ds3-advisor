# Cemetery of Ash

**Cemetery of Ash** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. This location serves as a starting area for new players to the game. This is where players will find themselves at the start of the game, revived as an 'Unkindled', and will have to face the mighty [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") as a test to prove their worth before heading into [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and being able to explore the lands of Lothric. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

## Full Cemetery of Ash Walkthrough

Click [here](/Cemetery+of+Ash#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash#speedrun") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough. Click [here](/Lore#lorefirelink "Dark Souls 3 Lore#lorefirelink") for Lore and Speculation on this area.   

### Getting Your Feet Under You

After character creation, the game begins with an introductory cutscene of your player character rising from a tombstone. Proceed forward and read the messages on the ground which provide instructions on the game's [Controls](/Controls "Dark Souls 3 Controls").

Follow the path to find your first enemy. After disposing of him, go right and loot a [Soul of a Deserted Corpse](/Soul+of+a+Deserted+Corpse "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Deserted Corpse"). Backtrack to where you found the first enemy and proceed to encounter another enemy in front of a soul-vessel-like fountain. Loot the corpse by the fountain to obtain the [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask").

### Progressing Through The Cemetery

To the right and up, there is a hollow facing away from you. To the left and up the stairs you will find another hollow. If you continue straight past the fountain, you will encounter a hollow with a crossbow. Taking a hard right from where this hollow is, you will see 2 more hollows. As you progress past them down a narrow waist-high waterway, it will open to a wider area where a [Ravenous Crystal Lizard](/Ravenous+Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Ravenous Crystal Lizard") waits. [Soul of an Unknown Traveler](/Soul+of+an+Unknown+Traveler "Dark Souls 3 Soul of an Unknown Traveler") 1x can be located in this area ([Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring") +1 can also be found here in NG+). Defeat the Crystal Lizard to obtain a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. He can be led to the edge by the bonfire to quickly defeat him by making him step/roll off.

Make your way back and continue on to reach a cliffside overlooking a breathtaking view. Head left and you will encounter your first [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). Light the bonfire to trigger the Enkindle Trophy/Achievement and learn the [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") Rest. Continue ahead along the cliff wall, eliminating the hollows in your path. At the edge, a message will tell you how to jump. Jumping here into a small ravine, you can find a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 1x. If you drop down beyond there will be a hollow you can land a jump attack on. It has a chance to drop a [Cleric's Sacred Chime](/Cleric's+Sacred+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Cleric's Sacred Chime"). If you backtrack and follow along the cliff edge, you will encounter several hollows and a path leading down along the cliff. At the end of the path you will find a sword and shield hollow and a crossbow hollow. Behind them, against the wall will be a corpse with [Firebomb](/Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Firebomb") 5x.   
  
In an archway ahead, eliminate the crossbow hollow and enter an open courtyard where you will see a petrified warrior. Approach it.

### Boss Fight: [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr")

Make your way forward and head down the stone steps that open up to a courtyard where you see the statue of a figure with a sword embedded in its chest. Approach the statue and you are given a prompt to remove the sword. Remove it to animate the statue and test your worth against the boss, [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr"). 

Defeat him to receive the [Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword") and earn the Trophy/Achievement Iudex Gundyr. Your ember will now be restored, which is similar to the revival mechanic in Demon's Souls.

Open the large double doors and proceed to the next location, [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").

### Speed Run Walkthrough

A fast walkthrough of how to get to the end of the level the fastest, picking up only essential items goes here. Start from the game's starting point and run straight to the [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask"). From there run to the bonfire which is straight and up the hill. Run past all the enemies in that area and engage [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr"). You may elect to grab the 5 Firebombs to help against Iudex's second form.
