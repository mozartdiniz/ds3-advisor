# Prisoner's Chain

### Prisoner's Chain  
  
---  
  

> Portion of a steel chain used to restrain Gundyr. Gain vigor, endurance, and vitality, but take extra damage.  
>   
> A prisoner is one who has staked everything on a belief, a proclivity most apparent in the greatest of champions.  
  
**Prisoner's Chain** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Prisoner's Chain Effect

  * Adds 15 Virtual Levels: 
    * 5 [Vigor](/Vigor "Dark Souls 3 Vigor")
    * 5 [Endurance](/Endurance "Dark Souls 3 Endurance")
    * 5 [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality")
  * Lowers player [Absorption](/Absorption+ "Dark Souls 3 Absorption ") stat, depending on current Absorption: 
    * **-4.0** when naked ([Video Example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6IYkSNPk4uU)).
    * -**2.588** when wearing full Havel's.
  * Weight: 0.8
  * Has a complex effect on incoming damage & survivability: 
    * Adds 15 Virtual levels increases all of the player's [Defense](/Defense "Dark Souls 3 Defense") stats.
    * Increasing Vigor adds to total HP.
    * Increasing Vitality allows for wearing heavier [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor"), and increases physical defense.
    * Increasing Endurance enables more actions to be performed before resting.
    * Absorption decreases.
  * Unless your defenses are _really_ low, or you've reached softcaps in Vigor/Vitality/Endurance, this ring is an improvement and never a hindrance. 
    * The extra damage you take is almost always balanced out by the increase in defenses and max HP.

### Where to Find Prisoner's Chain

  * [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") using [Soul of Champion Gundyr](/Soul+of+Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Champion Gundyr")

### Prisoner's Chain Notes

  * Equipping this ring will give you 15 Virtual levels, it does not increase your character's real [Soul Level](/Level "Dark Souls 3 Level").
  * At higher **Soul levels** , the Defense Increase from 15 Virtual levels will become proportionally smaller.
  * Prior to 1.13 patch, absorption was decreased by 10.0
  * This ring is similar in appearance to the [Ring of the Embedded](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Ring+of+the+Embedded "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Embedded") from _Dark Souls 2_.
