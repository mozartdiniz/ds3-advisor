# Titanite Chunk

**Titanite Chunk** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. These are used for weapon reinforcements up to +9 weapons, making them more powerful and effective in combat. They can be found throughout the game world, dropped by certain enemies, or purchased from specific merchants after offering the right ashes. These items provide valuable resources, gear and consumables to aid in your journey, making exploration and combat more rewarding.

### Titanite Chunk  
  
---  
  

> Titanite chunk for weapon reinforcement. Reinforces weapons to +9.  
>   
> Few records of chunks exist, and once one was discovered in Lothric, the race to locate Legendary Slabs began, but there have been no reports of success.  
  
### Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk Usage

  * Reinforce regular [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") to +9
  * 12 chunks required in total to get a weapon from +6 to +9 
    * 2 chunks for +7
    * 4 chunks for +8
    * 6 chunks for +9

### Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk Drops

  * Drops from [Cathedral Knights](/Cathedral+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Knight"), [Hollow Slave](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave"), and [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden").
  * Drops from the [Cathedral Grave Wardens](/Cathedral+Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Grave Warden") in [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").
  * Drops from Serpent-man at [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") (rare drop).
  * Drops from [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight"), [Cathedral Knights](/Cathedral+Knights "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Knights") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") (rare drop) and Hollows (extremely rare).
  * Drops from the [Ascended Winged Knight](/Ascended+Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ascended Winged Knight") trio on the roof of the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * Possible drop from various enemies in [Grand Archives.](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")
  * Rare drop from [Corvian Knights](/Corvian+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Knight") and [Millwood Chieftain](/Millwood+Chieftain "Dark Souls 3 Millwood Chieftain") in the village of [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") (DLC).
  * Common drop from [Ringed Knights](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight") and [Harald Legion Knights](/Harald+Legion+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Legion Knight") throughout [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") (DLC). Occasionally, but still quite commonly, the [Harald Legion Knights](/Harald+Legion+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Legion Knight") will drop multiple chunks at a time, with the maximum being 4.
  * Rare drop from [Hollow Slaves](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave") near the Earthen Peak bonfire

### Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk Notes

  * It costs 156,000 souls to purchase 12 Titanite Chunks, which is the required amount to upgrade a weapon to +9

### Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk Trivia

  * The rune on the Titanite Chunk is [Eihwaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eihwaz "Eihwaz").
