# Dark Gem

**Dark Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Dark Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Born of disembodied humanity.  
>   
> Used in infusion to create dark weapons.  
>   
> Dark weapons inflict dark damage, and scale with intelligence and faith.  
  
### Dark Gem Usage

  * Used for [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create Dark weapons.
  * Requires the [Profaned Coal](/Profaned+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Coal") to begin infusing.
  * Dark weapons inflict [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"), and scale with [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") and [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence").
  * Also adds [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") scaling to innate [lightning damage](/lightning+damage "Dark Souls 3 lightning damage") in weapons ([Dragonslayer's Axe](/Dragonslayer's+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer's Axe"), [Drakeblood Greatsword](/Drakeblood+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Greatsword") and [Lothric Knight Greatsword](/Lothric+Knight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight Greatsword")) and [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") scaling to the Drakeblood Greatsword's innate [magic damage](/magic+damage "Dark Souls 3 magic damage").

### Dark Gem Locations

  * Very rare drop from [Rotten Slug](/Rotten+Slug "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Slug") in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden") (0.5%)
  * Rare drop from [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") (2%)
  * Rare drop from [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") and [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden") (1%)
  * Rare drop from [Harald Legion Knight](/Harald+Legion+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Legion Knight") in [The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap") and [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)") (3%)
  * Drops from the second rolling "boulder" in [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"). You must destroy the hat wearing skeleton for the boulder to stop rolling.
  * [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"): Under the left set of stairs leading up to the courtyard full of knights just before the boss room. ([Video location](http://youtu.be/mNtmcolL8rY "Video location"))
  * Located after the Corvian Settlement bonfire (Ashes DLC). Head up toward the large NPC wielding Crow Quills and turn right before the gate at the end of the path, drop down and into the open window and defeat the two fly type mobs inside. Gem is on a corpse here.
  * 1 located on a corpse surrounded by baby locusts at the entrance of the giant swamp in The Ringed City (Ringed City DLC) 

### Notes

  * Requires the [Profaned Coal](/Profaned+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.
  * Some weapons have higher Dark scaling than others (e.g. Corvian's gets E scaling at +0, while a Notched Whip gets D).
  * An alternative to the [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade") miracle for a non-caster character. and an alternative to [Human Pine Resin](/Human+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Human Pine Resin") that scales damage.
  * A variety of enemies (Silver Knights, for example) and a couple of bosses (Vordt, Dancer) are fairly weak to Dark damage.
  * With minimal Intelligence and Faith, it's best to stick to Deep gems.
  * Damage Scaling softcaps at 30/30 INT/FTH
  * It offers the exact samge damage as the [Chaos Gem](/Chaos+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Gem"), the only difference being the damage type. This being the general rule with some exceptions like player vs player or [Murky Hand Scythe](/Murky+Hand+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Murky Hand Scythe").

### Trivia

  * To our dismay, the [Dark Sword](/Dark+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sword") gets renamed to Reinforced Dark Sword when infused with a Dark Gem.
