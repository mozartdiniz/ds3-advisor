#### [](/White+Sign+Soapstone "Dark Souls 3 White Sign Soapstone")[White Sign Soapstone](/White+Sign+Soapstone "Dark Souls 3 White Sign Soapstone")

| Allows you to send a summon sign to other players for them to summon you to their world for cooperative play. Up to 2 phantoms can be summoned to assist a host (3 if using Dried Finger). | The Soapstone can be purchased at the Firelink Shrine from the Shrine Handmaid for 500 souls.  
  
#### [Red Sign Soapstone](/Red+Sign+Soapstone "Dark Souls 3 Red Sign Soapstone")

| Allows you to send a summon sign to other players for them to summon you to their world for competitive play (PvP). | Drops from a white worm casting spells near you outside of the Rosaria's Bed Chamber bonfire.  
  
#### [](/Dried+Finger "Dark Souls 3 Dried Finger")[Dried Finger](/Dried+Finger "Dark Souls 3 Dried Finger")

|  Use to allow the summoning of one additional phantom (for a total of 3 friendly phantoms). However also allows for the summoning of additional invaders and increases the frequency of invasions. | Sold by Shrine Handmaid in Firelink Shrine for 2000 souls.  
  
#### [](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb")[Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb")

| Reusable item used to invade other players. Using it puts you in a queue to invade a player in your area. | Drops from the darkwraith found at the bottom of the lift under the Tower on the Wall bonfire in High Wall of Lothric after following Leonhard's questline.  
  
#### [Cracked Red Eye Orb](/Cracked+Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Cracked Red Eye Orb")

| Consumable item used to invade other players. Using it puts you in a queue to invade a player in your area. |  • Can be chosen as a Burial Gift.  
• Given to you as a gift by Leonhard at Firelink Shrine.  
• Chance to be droped by Darkwraiths. 100% drop rate from Dark Warriors at the Farron Keep perimeter.  
  
#### [](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember")[Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember")

| Ember increases your HP by 30% until death. Also allows for the summoning of phantoms both friendly and enemies and opens up your world for invasions. | Many. See item description.  
  
#### [](/Black+Separation+Crystal "Dark Souls 3 Black Separation Crystal")[Black Separation Crystal](/Black+Separation+Crystal "Dark Souls 3 Black Separation Crystal")

| Use to return home from a host's world or to return a phantom home that you summoned in yours. | Avalible from the start of game  
  
#### [](/Vertebrae+Shackle "Dark Souls 3 Vertebrae Shackle")[Vertebrae Shackle](/Vertebrae+Shackle "Dark Souls 3 Vertebrae Shackle")

| [Mound-Makers](/Mound-Makers "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Makers") covenant rank item. | Reward for killing host as a mound maker.  
  
#### [Way of White Circlet](/Way+of+White+Circlet "Dark Souls 3 Way of White Circlet")

| Restore the link to other worlds. | Available from the start of the game.  
  
#### [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue")

|  [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") covenant rank item. |  • Reward for defeating host after invading with a Red Eye Orb. • Rare drop from Darkwraith  
  
####  [Forked Pale Tongue](/Forked+Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Forked Pale Tongue")

| Proof of a red orb invader's victory over a blue spirit. Consume to acquire 2 Pale Tongues. | Given to dark spirits as proof of killing a blade of darkmoon or a blue sentinel.  
  
#### [Wolf's Blood Swordgrass](/Wolf's+Blood+Swordgrass "Dark Souls 3 Wolf's Blood Swordgrass")

| [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron") covenant rank item. |  Given as a reward for killing host as a Watchdog of Farron. Rare drop from Ghrus  
  
#### [Human Dregs](/Human+Dregs "Dark Souls 3 Human Dregs")

| [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful") covenant rank item. |  Reward to Aldrich Faithfull Covenant. Given as a reward for killing host as a Aldrich Faithfull  
Rare drop from Devout of the Deep  
  
#### [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept")

| [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon") covenant rank item. |  Reward for killing dark spirits as Blue Sentinels or Blade of the Darkmoon Rare drop from Silver Knights  
  
#### [Proof of Concord Well Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Well+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Well Kept")

| Proof of a Blade of the Darkmoon summon's victory over an Aldrich Faithful. Use to acquire 2 [Proof of a Concord Kepts](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept"). | Reward for killing Aldrich Faithfuls as Blades of the Darkmoon.  
  
#### [Roster of Knights](/Roster+of+Knights "Dark Souls 3 Roster of Knights")

| The Roster of Knights is used to check the contributions of Darkmoon Knights, showing the top 10 contributors in a list to the top, as well as your rank at the bottom. |  Just after Yorsha church bonfire on a corpse.  
  
#### [Sunlight Medal](/Sunlight+Medal "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Medal")

| [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") covenant rank item. |  • Reward for defeating a boss as a sunlight phantom. • Rare drop from Lothric Knight
