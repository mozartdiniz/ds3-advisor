# Soul of a Deserted Corpse

**Soul of a Deserted Corpse** is a [Soul](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of a Deserted Corpse  
  
---  
  

> Soul found in a deserted corpse.  
>   
> Use to acquire souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Soul of a Deserted Corpse Usage

  * Use to obtain 200 souls.

### Soul of a Deserted Corpse Locations

  * **1x** at [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash"): At the end of a hidden path immediately to the right of the first enemy you'll encounter after escaping your coffin.
  * **1x** at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"): At the foot of the tower behind the Giant Tree, next to the locked door.
  * **7x** at [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"): 
    * **1x** On a corpse that is on the bridge, to the left of the "_**High Wall of Lothric**_ " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). 
    * **1x** In a dungeon underneath the fallen [Wyvern](/Lothric+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Wyvern"), far to the left of the "_**High Wall of Lothric**_ " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). This is the one just before the blanketing fire breath attack. 
    * **1x** On a corpse on the bridge after the fire breathing [Wyvern](/Lothric+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Wyvern") that leads to the next bonfire. 
    * **1x** Just to the right of the exit of the room that is below the "_**Tower on the Wall**_ " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires").
    * **1x** In the room with the spear-wielding [Lothric Knight](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight"). This is just below the roof with a [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man").
    * **1x** Just outside the top floor of the elevator shortcut. 
    * **1x** To the left, just past the courtyard with the [Lothric Knight](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") bodies and the [Winged Knight](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight"). 
  * Dropped by Rats in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") very close to Dilapidated Bridge bonfire (common drop) (can be farmed safely by shooting fireballs from the high ground)
