**Hand Axe** is a [Weapon](/Fallout+76+Weapons) in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> A crude hand-axe that serves both as a weapon, and a tool of many uses.  
>   
> Favored by pyromancers of the Great Swamp, this axe has a short range but only moderate weight, and reasonable damage. Easily wielded, and more powerful than it appears.  
>   
> Skill: Warcry  
> Let out a spirited warcry that temporarily boosts attack.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Infused") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Titanite").
  * Using the Axe with two hands, press left trigger (default) to activate [warcry](/Warcry "warcry"), changing heavy attacks and increasing damage output.
  * A surprisingly quick weapon, the hand axe is fantastic for trading blows with slower targets and rolling to safety.
  * This weapon is able to roll-catch on reaction provided a decent connection to the opposing player, and as thus it is a most excellent choice for a off-hand weapon.
  * Has a small swing arc that can hit closely grouped targets all at once.
  * It is better to capitalize on the speed of this weapon rather than its damage, stick with the one handed moveset.
  * Given the aforementioned three points, it pairs extremely well with clunkier, less-versatile weapons as it covers the region around you where people may try to roll to.
  * A great starting weapon when paired with the fire gem to almost double its damage out put once you reach Firelink.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting equipment for the [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer).
  * Looted from a corpse just next to where you find [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp) at the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement). (Watch the [Item Location Video Guide](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=2h15m50s))

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
