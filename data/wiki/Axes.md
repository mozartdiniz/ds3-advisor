# Axes

**Axes** are a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") type in Dark Souls 3. Axes usually do decent damage and are best used on Strength/Quality builds. Most of the Axes have the Skill Warcry, which provides the weapon with a damage boost for 30 seconds. Axes are generally considered to be worse than [Straight Swords](/Straight+Swords "Dark Souls 3 Straight Swords"), due to their lower attack speed and therefore lower damage per second, and having an arguably worse moveset and Skill spread. 

However, Axes do have their own positives. Axes deal very high damage and stamina damage per hit relative to their speed.
