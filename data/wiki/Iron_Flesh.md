# Iron Flesh

**Iron Flesh** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Iron Flesh  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 40  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 8 Intelligence  
  
**Type** | Body Buff  
  
> Pyromancy that internalizes flame.   
>    
> Iron flesh boosts damage absorption and resistance, but significantly increases weight.   
>    
> Not advisable for walking in marshlands, perhaps resulting in its obsolescence in the Great Swamp.

### How to Get Iron Flesh in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") Swamp. From Farron Keep bonfire (the one next to the ladder), go directly right, into the swamp. The Pyromancy should be located before the group of [basilisks](/Basilisk "Dark Souls 3 Basilisk") not far from the bonfire.
  * [Video location](http://youtu.be/EFamMNa9E2E "Video location")

### Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Effect does not directly modify weight, but instead forces user to walk as if their weight ratio is above 100% and roll as if their weight ratio is above 70%.
  * Skills like [Quickstep](/Quickstep "Dark Souls 3 Quickstep") can be used to circumvent the slower movement.
  * Increases all physical absorptions by 40%, increases all status resistances by 50, causes most melee attacks to be deflected, and makes the user immune to staggering. However, it significantly lowers Lightning absorption (-60% lightning abdsorption when applied at 0%).
  * Effect lasts about 16s.
  * Is affected by [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Overwrites and is overwritten by other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat"), [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"), [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within"), [Stone Flesh](/Stone+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Stone Flesh") and [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon").
  * The effect is identical to the [Stone Flesh](/Stone+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Stone Flesh") Weapon Art on [Havel's Greatshield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield").
