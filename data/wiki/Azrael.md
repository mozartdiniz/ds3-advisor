### Build Summary

This build is an exceptional stand alone character as well as one that could venture into **New Game+** with ease. It is equipped with the powerful and legendary [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") which has a projectile attack already inherent within the weapon. Although this weapon may not have the same range as other greatswords, the [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") possesses phenomenal base damage while the benefit of its split damage output helps cut down opponents swiftly. For threats that are just outside your range, the build is also equipped with the [Throwing Knife](/Throwing+Knife "Throwing Knife") which allows you to keep the pressure on the enemy while fighting. For oppenents who buff their weapons or constanly heal, the [Duel Charm](/Duel+Charm "Duel Charm") and [Undead Hunter Charm](/Undead+Hunter+Charm "Undead Hunter Charm") are exactly the tools you should employ. If you happen to come across a shield user or someone heavily armored, the [Darkmoon Longbow](/Darkmoon+Longbow "Darkmoon Longbow") is ideal for the situation as it has a [Weapon Art](/Skills "Weapon Art") specifically designed to pierce both armor and shields alike. The [Caestus](/Caestus "Caestus") is mainly used as a defensive tool, either for the quick damaging jab or a parry tool to set up a critical riposte.

  * Build can deal damage at all ranges - [ **Close** | **Mid** | **Long** ]
  * Build has great physical defense - [ **Strike** | **Slash** | **Thrust** ]
  * Build has moderate spell defense - [ [Magic](/Magic+Defense "Magic") ]
  * Build has moderate elemental defense - [ [Fire](/Fire+Defense "Fire") | [Lightning](/Lightning+Defense "Lightning") | [Dark](/Dark+Defense "Dark") ]

  * All attacks have high-priority, thus ensuring damage when trading blows
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") has wide horizontal swings, along with vertical slashes, thus guarenteeing diversity in approach

### Build Weaknesses

  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") has less reach compared to similar weapons within the Greatsword Weapon Class
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") swing animation is complicated by cramped locations
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") projectile attack has semi-decent range
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") may _not_ be engulfed in pyromancy
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") may _not_ be enchanted with sorcery
  * [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Moonlight Greatsword") may _not_ be buffed by resins
  * [Darkmoon Longbow](/Darkmoon+Longbow "Darkmoon Longbow") has slow draw speed compared to similar weapons within the Bow Weapon Class
  * Build has semi-decent resistance - [ [Bleed](/Bleed "Bleed") | [Poison](/Poison "Poison") | [Frost](/Frostbite "Frost") | [Curse](/Curse "Curse") ]
