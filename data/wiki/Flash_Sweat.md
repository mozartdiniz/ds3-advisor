# Flash Sweat

**Flash Sweat** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy, you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Flash Sweat  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
****|  20  
****|  1  
**** /  | 6 / 6   
  
  
> Unique pyromancy of the Great Swamp associated with Carmina.  
>   
> Intense sweating increases fire damage absorption.  
>   
> An influential pyromancy that internalizes flame, likely forming the foundation for many subsequent spells. 

### How to Get Flash Sweat in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 2000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Fire damage absorption is increased by 30% for the duration of the spell.
  * Lasts 30 seconds. This duration is affected by [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Does not stack with other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"), [Iron Flesh](/Iron+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh"), [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within"), [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial") or [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon").
  * Stacks with [Red Bug Pellets](/Red+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Red Bug Pellet"). Cast this spell last, as it has a shorter duration.
  * Best paired with [Flame Stoneplate Ring](/Flame+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Flame Stoneplate Ring") and [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring") for traversing the lava sections in Smouldering Lake.
