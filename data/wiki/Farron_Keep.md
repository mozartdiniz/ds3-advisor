# Farron Keep

**Farron Keep** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. Upon cindering themselves, the former headquarters of Farron's Undead Legion became a vast rotting wood. The swamp hides treasure in many corners, but threatens adventurers with aggresive beasts, knee-deep poison, and even curse. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

## Full Farron Keep Walkthrough

A full walkthrough including all the items, enemies and bosses follows. Click [here](/Farron+Keep#speed "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep#speed") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough. Click [here](/Lore#lorefarron "Dark Souls 3 Lore#lorefarron") to go to the Lore and Speculation page for this location.

### Some starter tips

It is best to leave this area for after you've cleared the [Cathedral](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), where you can find the Poisonbite Ring and Curseward Greatshield. The boost to your poison and curse resistance will help you while exploring the swamp.

This swamp is dangerous, a toxic, stinking bog filled with formidable enemies. Items that boost your poison resistance and cure status effects are required. It is advised to bring Purple Moss Clumps at a minimum. If you are able to withstand the poison waters, there are several notable items you can loot here. Bringing a dagger and using its Quickstep [skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") to dash through the deep water is very helpful; it even works when FP is zero. Some of the enemies you encounter here are vulnerable to fire, and kitting yourself out appropriately will make combat far easier. 

The swamp includes areas of deeper water where you cannot run. Making their accursed return in Fromsoft fashion, there are several [Basilisks](/Basilisk "Dark Souls 3 Basilisk") in the area—lunging reptile-like enemies that spray a deadly yellow mist, causing the buildup of one of the most dangerous status effects in all of Soulsborne games: [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse"). This will kill you instantly if the curse meter on your screen fills up. The best way to deal with those is to equip the [Curseward Great Shield](/Curse+Ward+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Curse Ward Greatshield") you can find in the Cathedral, which gives you very high resistance to cursing, lure them to a safe spot with a bow and take them out one by one. A halberd can catch their jumping attacks in mid-air.

### Lighting the Ritual Fires

There are 3 ritual fires scattered throughout the swamp that must all be extinguished to open the Farron Keep Perimeter gate, the fires looks like a bakery oven with a glowing bowl at the base and a chimney-like structure above.

The fires can be seen as burning chimneys from above, but when you are traversing the swamp the trees will block most of the visibility and it can be hard to see the next fire that needs to be put out. When you extinguish a fire, a corresponding fire will be lit near the gate during a cutscene, and these fires can be used to determine which ritual fire you have yet to extinguish:

If you follow the left hand path with lit bonfires on small islands, after entering the swamp from the Farron Keep bonfire, it will lead you to the first and second ritual fires, both located on top of small hills. The first and second ritual fires corresponds to the first and second fire on the left hand side of the large gate seen in the cutscene, or when standing in front of the gate.  
The third ritual fire is located on a small hill to the right of the large gate, also reached by a couple of small islands with bonfires. The corresponding fire in front of the gate is the lone one on the right hand side.

### Traversing the Swamp

From the first bonfire, you can take two routes in the deadly poisonous swamp.

To the left from the first bonfire, you can find a [Ragged Mask](/Ragged+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Ragged Mask") by a group of leeches, [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 1x, and on a small island [Prism Stone](/Prism+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Prism Stone") 10x. If you continue with this path, you will encounter your first [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") (if you didn't defeat the one carrying the [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb") in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric")). They have a chance to drop several items such as [Dark Sword](/Dark+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sword"), [Cracked Red Eye Orbs](/Cracked+Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Cracked Red Eye Orb") and [Pale Tongues](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue"). Near where you encounter this wraith will be a stone tower and inside will be a corpse with the [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal").

To the right from the first bonfire, you can loot some valuable [Purple Moss Clump](/Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Purple Moss Clump") 3x from a corpse. Ahead on another corpse is the pyromancy [Iron Flesh](/Iron+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh"). Be mindful of the Basilisks here that can inflict curse on you rather quickly. Proceeding along the wall, you will find a [Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Large+Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier"), [Repair Powder](/Repair+Powder "Dark Souls 3 Repair Powder"). Eventually you come to a shore that leads up into a cavern, where you can pillage the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll"). There is also a chest here which contains the [Antiquated Set](/Antiquated+Set "Dark Souls 3 Antiquated Set"). Around this area, you can be invaded by [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel"). There will also be stairs that lead up to an altar lit with fire. It is solely guarded by a Ghrus Leaper, which delivers a lethal grab that grants them a red, glowing buff; this move also bypasses shields. He will be found crouching at the top of the stairs. Nearby there is [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin") 1x.

### Finding the Watchdogs of Farron Covenant

Make your way back towards the center of the swamp to a small island with a structure in its center. Inside you will find a group of poisonous leeches who spew fluid in your direction. They surround a corpse that can be looted for [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") 1x. Outside this building, if you have given a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") to [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth"), will be a summon sign for [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel").

Leaving the island you will see a dilapidated structure with a ladder going up, protected by a group of leeches at the base. Before ascending this ladder, to the left you will find [Wolf's Blood Swordgrass](/Wolf's+Blood+Swordgrass "Dark Souls 3 Wolf's Blood Swordgrass") 1x. Return to the ladder and ascend. At the top ,enter the room through the open doorway to find a [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires") and the [Old Wolf of Farron](/Old+Wolf+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf of Farron"), where you can join the [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron") [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants"). You may pray to the wolf to earn the Legion Etiquette [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). In an alcove partly up the wall there's also an item that can't be reached from inside. Go back outside and follow the path around the structure to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") who drops a [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite"). Right next to the lizard is an illusory wall; hit it to open a path to the Dreamchaser's Ashes, the unreachable item seen from inside the room.

In the corner of this room there is also a lift you can activate via pressure plate which will take you to a bridge where a [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon") mini boss wanders. This mini-boss packs a punch and has a very large reach with its hammer, which it uses to slam and sweep. The demon will also fly into the air a short distance and follow with a ground slam. In addition, the demon can grab you, draining health as it eats you. Defeating it will earn you the [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Stray Demon"). There is also a [Greataxe](/Greataxe "Dark Souls 3 Greataxe") and [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 2x that can be looted in this area. 

On the far side of the area from the Stray Demon on the right hand side, there is an area where you drop down onto a platform. Continue left down the platforms and you will find a path leading behind the large gate. In this area there are three [Crystal Lizards](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), a Dragon corpse and a number of Undead Soldiers. The two [Crystal Lizards](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") near the dragon corpse drop a [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard"). You will also find the [Dragon Crest Shield](/Dragon+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Crest Shield") and [Lightning Spear](/Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear") miracle near the Dragon corpse. Watch out for the lantern carrier who will cause dormant soldiers to aggro you. You can return to the other side of the gate by following the path over and dropping back down into the previous area.

Descend the ladder to return to the swamp. Straight ahead near some burning fires you will find a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 1x. If you follow the fires you will come to an [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru"), a large horned beast that wields a large tree and casts numerous homing missiles. It will cast when at range and up close it will stamp the tree down in an attempt to squash you. Upon defeating it, loot the [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 2x from a corpse by a tree. Continue exploring the swamp, defeating the [Rotten Slugs](/Rotten+Slug "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Slug") you encounter. On a raised mound you will find a [Sunlight Talisman](/Sunlight+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Talisman") and a cauldron of [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup").

You will see the remains of what may have been a stone bridge jutting out of the swamp. Climb up this makeshift ramp to find a [Greatsword](/Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Greatsword") at the end. Back in the swamp to the left of this bridge you will find a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") behind a short stone pillar.

On a small island nearby, where a fire blazes, you will encounter a [Ghru Leaper](/Ghru+Leaper "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Leaper") that jumps in the air to attack. If you can approach it quietly to land a backstab or a combo before it can react, this fight can be much easier. On a tree near here you will find a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 1x on a corpse.

Nearby, you will see a large set of stone stairs going up and at the top another of these beasts. To the right will be a corpse with [Purple Moss Clump](/Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Purple Moss Clump") 2x. Beyond you will see an altar with a fire. Approach it to be given the option to extinguish the flame. Do so to trigger a cutscene and proceed to locate and extinguish the remaining fires.

Continue across a narrow bridge to face two [Ghru Grunts](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt") wielding spears and shields, and a [Ghru Shaman](/Ghru+Shaman "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Shaman"). Past them, inside the structure you will find the Keep Ruins [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). Descend the fallen bridge to reenter the swamp. To your left will be a corpse with a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") 1x.

### Rings, Scrolls and Pharis

Continue exploring the surrounding swamp where you will encounter crawling [Ghru](/Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Ghru"), as well as another giant crab which drops the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"). Be careful when fighting the crab, as you will be slowed down by the deeper water. If you have not befriended the [Giant of the Undead Settlement](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement") yet, he will fire great arrows at you in this area of the swamp. However, if you have befriended the giant, he will fire on the crab, making the potentially difficult fight substantially easier. You can loot several items here such as [Gold Pine Bundle](/Gold+Pine+Bundle "Dark Souls 3 Gold Pine Bundle") 6x, [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x, [Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler](/Large+Soul+of+an+Unknown+Traveler "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of an Unknown Traveler") 1x, [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk"), [Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Nameless Soldier") 1x, [Young White Branch](/Young+White+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Young White Branch") 2x.

In a corner there will be a cluster of dead mushrooms. On a larger mushroom by a tree you can find the [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll"). If you follow this wall here through the swamp you will encounter three [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru") huddled around an item. They can be lured out one by one with projectiles for an easier encounter. The left-most one will drop the [Black Bow of Pharis](/Black+Bow+of+Pharis "Dark Souls 3 Black Bow of Pharis"), and the middle/furthest back one will drop [Pharis's Hat](/Pharis's+Hat "Dark Souls 3 Pharis's Hat"). The item they surround is a [Poison Gem](/Poison+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Poison Gem"). Alongside a fallen bridge, near where a [Rotten Slug](/Rotten+Slug "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Slug") lurks, you will find an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") 1x. The [Nameless Knight Set](/Nameless+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Nameless Knight Set") can also be looted in this swamp, on a corpse in a corner wedged between a structure and natural rock formation. 2 dirty beasts lurk in this region.

Proceed up another stone set of stairs and defeat two [Ghru Grunts](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt") and extinguish the second flame. If you explore the area of dry land around here you will find [Rotten Pine Resin](/Rotten+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Pine Resin") on a corpse along an edge overhanging the swamp. 

Near the Keep Ruins bonfire, you will find another staircase you can ascend on a hill and will discover another flame to extinguish, triggering another cutscene.

Once all 3 fires have been extinguished, a cutscene will play showing a large stone double door opening. The doors can be found from the Keep Ruins bonfire, down the fallen bridge and straight through into the next area.

### The second half of the Keep

This area will features several of the gargoyle type enemies you encountered in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"). Straight ahead as you enter you can find a [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Shriving Stone") near one of these enemies. Explore this wooded area to discover a large ruin which you can enter via an open archway. Inside you will discover the Farron Keep Perimeter [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). If you are in good standing with [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor"), there is a summon sign for [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") and will give you the [Duel Bow Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").

Moving past the bonfire will be 3 of the smaller dirty beasts and at the end of the hallway, you will find a giant [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). Defeat this and open the grated door to unlock a shortcut to Road of Sacrifices. A corpse near here can be looted for [Great Magic Weapon](/Great+Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Weapon"). If you head back towards the bonfire and drop down to your left to land in the wooded area, you will discover a corpse with [Atonement](/Atonement "Dark Souls 3 Atonement") on it. On this same path you can find a [Hollow Gem](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") on a corpse.

From the Farron Keep Perimeter bonfire, if you make your way back into the wooded area, you will encounter a [Dark Wraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith"). The wraith may be engaging other enemies here. If you continue forward you will encounter a small dirty beast and a corpse with an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x. Continue forward until you come to a set of stone stairs with a Darkwraith. In the wooded area around here you will find a [Black Bug Pellet](/Black+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Black Bug Pellet") 3x on a corpse as well as a few of the beasts.

On the stairs, if in ember form you will be able to see and summon [Black Hand Gotthard](/Black+Hand+Gotthard "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Gotthard") and [Sirris of the Sunless Realms](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") depending on how far you have progressed her quest line. 

Once prepared, open the double doors to challenge the boss of this area, a Lord of Cinder, [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"). Defeat them to earn [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord") and [Soul of the Blood of the Wolf](/Soul+of+the+Blood+of+the+Wolf "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Blood of the Wolf").

Light the bonfire that appears here and approach the altar which triggers it sliding back, revealing the entrance to the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus").

### Speed Run Walkthrough

Exit the bonfire room and turn left, follow the fires to find the three flames you have to extinguish to get through this area. These are all located higher up on dry land accessed by stone ramps. Keep an eye out for fires and lanterns. At some point there is ladder by the high wall you see on your left with a bunch of leeches at its base. The ladder is lit by a lantern. Climb up to find an optional area with a big view, a bonfire, a covenant, a miniboss and 3 crystal lizards. You do NOT need to go through the area with the cursing basilisks.
