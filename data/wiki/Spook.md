# Spook

## Spook  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
Focus Cost | 15  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 10 Intelligence  
Duration  | 20 seconds  
  
**Spook** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

  
  

> Sorcery developed by a certain surreptitious sorcerer at Vinheim Dragon School.  
>   
> Masks noises of the caster and prevents fall damage.  
>   
> Their mastery of this sorcery alone allows Vinheim spooks to demand handsome payment for their services. 

### Dark Souls 3 Spook Effects

  * Silences your footsteps and rolls, and allows you to fall from greater heights. However, note that you can still die if you fall from a really great height.

### How to Get Spook in Dark Souls 3

  * The [Assassin](/Assassin "Dark Souls 3 Assassin") class starts out with this spell equipped. 
  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 2,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Spook Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Spook is a combination of the [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") [Hush](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Hush "Dark Souls 3 Hush") and [Fall Control](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Fall+Control "Dark Souls 3 Fall Control") from previous games.
  * Silences one's footsteps and grants immunity to fall damage.
  * Does not silence your footsteps if your equip load is over 70% 
    * The fall damage immunity only works up to a certain point. Falling into a pit or falling from a great height will kill you even with the spell active.
  * The same effect can be achieved with [Silvercat Ring](/Silvercat+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Silvercat Ring") and [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Works great with [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body").
