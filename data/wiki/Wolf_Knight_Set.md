**Wolf Knight Armor** is an [Armor](/Armor "Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  

> Helm of a knight tainted by the dark of the Abyss. The twilight blue tassel is damp, and will ever remain so.
> 
> A vanquished knight left behind only wolf's blood, and legacy of duty. The Undead Legion of Farron was formed to bear his torch, and the armor of these Abyss Watchers suggests their own eventual end.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid) in [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Untended Graves") for 46,000 Souls.

### Lore

  * [Artorias](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Artorias+the+Abysswalker "Artorias") is tied to [The Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "The Abyss Watchers") of [DS3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "DS3").

### Notes and Tips

  * The player MUST kill the Abyss Watchers before this set becomes available.
  * This the same armor worn by [Artorias the Abysswalker](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Artorias+the+Abysswalker "Artorias the Abysswalker") in the first Dark Souls.
