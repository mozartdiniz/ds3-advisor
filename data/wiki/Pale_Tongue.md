# Pale Tongue

**Pale Tongue** is a [Multiplayer Item](/Multiplayer+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Pale Tongue  
  
---  
  

> Proof of a red orb invader's victory over a Host of Embers.  
>   
> Claiming tongues as trophies was originally the practice of an infamous troupe of invaders, who offered them to their speechless goddess.  
  
### Pale Tongue Usage

  * Proof of a red orb invader's victory over a Host of Embers.
  * Covenant item for ranking up in the [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers) covenant.

### Pale Tongue Locations

  * Rare drop from [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Darkwraith") (5%)
  * 1x Found at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement): In the area where you fight the Fire Demon with [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina), on one of the hanging corpses. (Watch the [video](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw) at 3h and 20m)
  * 1x Found at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep): right next to the second chained-Giant, directly in front of the stairs.
  * 1x Found at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Cathedral of the Deep"): outside on the upper level of the roof, at the end of the walkway by the rafters entrance.
  * One is obtained each time the player invades and defeats the host of a world while either covenantless or as a member of [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Rosaria's Fingers").

### Notes

  * Can be used to change character appearance by offering to [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth").
  * Can be used to re-allocate all stats, a maximum of five times per game cycle, by offering to [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth").
  * Show one to [Leonhard](/Leonhard "Leonhard") in Firelink Shrine to get the [Lift Chamber Key](/Lift+Chamber+Key "Lift Chamber Key").
  * One of the best ways to farm Pale Tongues outside of PvP is from the 2 [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Darkwraiths ") you come across when first exiting the Farron Keep Perimeter bonfire (Choosing to let the Darkwraiths weaken themselves or possibly even die while fighting the many [Ghru's](/Ghru "Ghru's") in the area will also double as the best way to farm [Wolf's Blood Swordgrass](/Wolf's+Blood+Swordgrass "Wolf's Blood Swordgrass"))
