**Alva Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Protection of Alva, seeker of the spurned. This (Piece Name) was trimmed to reduce weight.
> 
> To this day, troubadours sing of tales of the Wandering knight Alva and his travels, and of his involvement with the saint and the witch. Needless to say, the songs traditionally end in tragedy.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * You must have been invaded by and defeated Dark Spirit Alva in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley on the staircase leading to the Irithyll Dungeon bonfire. His set will be found on a corpse near [Karla's](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") Cell, in the large cell block full of Jailers.
  * Do NOT defeat [Yhorm](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") before trying to obtain this armor. Alva will not invade afterwards.

  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This set, while its resistances are outclassed by other ~22 weight armor sets, offers more poise than its weight-class competitors.
