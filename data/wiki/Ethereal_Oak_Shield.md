**Ethereal Oak Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Charbronze shield used by Millwood Knights that slowly regenerates HP.
> 
> This shield appears to be blessed by a great, if faded, image of the ethereal oak.
> 
> Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Ethereal Oak Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed but not Infused.
  * Upgrades with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Comes with an innate HP regen much like a [Blessed](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") infusion. It heals 2 HP per tick, each tick interval being 1 second. Upgrading the shield doesn't decrease the tick interval, unlike Blessed infusions. However, the healing over time is equal to a +10 blessed infusion (3 HP per 1.5s) without any upgrades. Stacks with the [Sun Princess Ring](/Sun+Princess+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Sun Princess Ring") and other HP regen items and weapons.
  * The regen occurs as soon as the shield is equipped. So if you swap rapidly between this shield and another item you'll gain health more quickly.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from: [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") \- Corpse located on bottom half of the tower after you go up a ladder then go down the stairs. The tower itself is located in the Millwood Ruins after either of the two giant wolf encounters.
  * [Fextralife Video: Ethereal Oak Shield location](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=22m11s "Fextralife Video: Ethereal Oak Shield location")
  * [Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RP5W0tTHUFk "Video") \- video starts in the Millwood Ruins. Go into the tower past the first enemy in there, up the round stairs. Climb up the ladder there, then follow the round stairs down.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
