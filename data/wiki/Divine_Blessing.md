# Divine Blessing

### Divine Blessing  
  
---  
  

> Holy water blessed by the Queen of Lothric.  
>   
> Fully restores HP and cures ailments.  
>   
> The Queen of Lothric, married to the former King Oceiros, was initially revered as a [goddess of fertility and bounty](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwynevere "Dark Souls 3 Gwynevere"). After giving birth to Ocelotte, her youngest, she quietly disappeared.  
  
**Divine Blessing** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in Dark Souls 3.

### Divine Blessing Usage

  * Use to restore HP and ailments over time. 
    * Fully restores HP.
    * Cures [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic") status.
    * Removes [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic") buildup.
    * Have no effect against [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") buildup.

### Divine Blessing Locations

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts").
  * One is for sale by [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for 8,000 souls, after he goes scavenging.
  * 1x in a chest in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), after you leave the kitchen where you find the [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup"), head up the stairs to find a Silver Knight looking at a painting of [Gwynevere](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwynevere "Dark Souls 3 Gwynevere") head to the left of the knight and up the stairs, turn around at the top and walk past the urns to the three chests along the wall, the Divine Blessing will be in the leftmost chest. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=1h54m55s "Video"))
  * Dropped by the Silver Knight in the same room with the paintings.
  * 1x at [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). [Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUr3lVAzf-E&feature=youtu.be&t=1h48m46s).
  * Dropped by the [Judicator Giants](/Judicator "Dark Souls 3 Judicator") in the Ringed City.

### Divine Blessing Notes

  * Can be dropped in Picklepum the Crow's nest for the [Very Good Carving](/Very+Good+Carving "Dark Souls 3 Very Good Carving") item.
  * This consumable appears throughout the series, including in  _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing")_.
