# Great Lightning Spear

**Great Lightning Spear** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman,](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Great Lightning Spear  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 32  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 30 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Primal account of Lightning Spear, which tells of an ancestral dragonslayer.
> 
> Hurls a giant lightning spear.
> 
> Gwyn, the First Lord, slew dragons with his sunlight spear, a tradition upheld by his firstborn and the greatspear he wielded.
> 
> Much of this resplendent tale of father and son, while epic, remains tragically untold.

### How to Get Great Lightning Spear in Dark Souls 3

  * Acquired by reaching Rank 2 in the [Warriors of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight").. 
    * You must offer 30 [Sunlight Medals](/Sunlight+Medal "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Medal") at the sunlight alter found in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") to reach Rank 2.
    * These are obtained by equipping the [Warriors of Sunlight](/Warriors+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warriors of Sunlight") covenant medal.

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.85) damage.
  * This spell takes little more than a second to be cast with ~50 Virtual Dex.
  * Creates a second hitbox at melee range that does partial damage.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Lightning Spear](/Lightning+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Spear"), and has an upgrade in [Sunlight Spear](/Sunlight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Spear").
