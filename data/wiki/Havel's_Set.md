**Havel's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor as if hewn from a giant boulder. Highly protective, but excessively heavy.
> 
> The warriors who followed Havel the Rock never flinched, nor retreated from battle, crushing any foe that stood in their way.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * In [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep#map "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep#map") on the bridge above the Old Wolf of Farron bonfire where the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon") spawns, only after you defeat the [Havel Knight](/Havel+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Havel Knight") that is located in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"). He is located shortly after the last bonfire of the area, next to the corpse of a wyvern. (This is not to be confused with the [Havel Knight](/Havel+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Havel Knight") that is spawned by the [Man-Serpent Summoners](/Man-Serpent+Summoner "Dark Souls 3 Man-Serpent Summoner"))

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Some of the outright heaviest armor in the game, second only to [Smough's Set](/Smough's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Smough's Set"), with an underwhelming defense rating. 
  * Grants the highest fire resistance in the whole game if all the pieces are worn.
  * Each piece can be sold to [Shrine Handmaid ](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 500 souls.
  * The total weight of Havel's set is 56.9.
