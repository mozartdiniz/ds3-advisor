# Magic Stoneplate Ring

### Magic Stoneplate Ring  
  
---  
  

> Stoneplates are symbols of true knights, and blue stoneplates are granted to royal palace guards. Increases magic damage absorption.  
  
**Magic Stoneplate Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Magic Stoneplate Ring Effect

Increase magic damage absorption as a function of current absorption.

  * PVE: damage from non-player sources 
    * +0 = 13%
    * +1 = 17%
    * +2 = 20%
  * PVP: damage from other players 
    * +0 = 4%
    * +1 = 5%
    * +2 = 6%
  * Weight: 0.6

### Where to Find Magic Stoneplate Ring

  * Dropped from the second Greatsword-weilding knight guarding [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"). ()
  * +1 Version (NG+): [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") bonfire before the swamp. From that bonfire, make a right and proceed to where the Basilisks are. The ring will be in this area. ()
  * +2 Version (NG++): Found in the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"). Climb down the ladder towards the dungeon. There is a hole in the wall. Drop down, turn right, and go down the stairs. The ring is at the end of these stairs on the left. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/vKEA9kNjLgw "\(video location\)")

### Notes

  * Using both this ring and Fire Stoneplate ring is a famous strategy to absorb damage against both the Dancer of the Boreal Valley and Pontiff Sullyvahn in later NG cycles.
  * It can be used to counteract some of the drawback of Dusk's Crown, albeit not very effectively and at the cost of a ring slot.
