**Follower Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "DLC").

> Armor worn by the Farron Followers.
> 
> When a warrior of Farron fell to the Abyss, the tall, lean Followers, with their hollowed eyes, quiety appeared in groups to hunt them down. 
> 
> Farron and its Watchers fell to ruin, but the Followers survived as a wandering pack of Hollows.

### 

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Farron Follower](/Farron+Follower "Farron Follower") soldiers found all around the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Painted World of Ariandel").

### Notes and Tips

  * This armor set can be farmed as soon as you enter Ariandel from the first enemies you encounter.
