# Greatshields

**Great Shields** are a class of [Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. These types of [Shields](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") usually weigh a decent amount and provide the best protection in the game. They are often used by players who have high [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength"), as they require a good amount in order to use.
