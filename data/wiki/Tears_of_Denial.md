# Tears of Denial

**Tears of Denial** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or a Special Weapon that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Tears of Denial  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 100  
**Slots Used** | 2  
**Requirements** | 15 Faith  
**Type** | Self Revive  
  
> Miracle taught by Morne, the Archbishop's apostle.   
>   
> Grants one chance to endure when HP reaches 0.   
>   
> Intended to grant the dying a few moments for a final farewell. Tears are shed for the sake of the living, more so than the deceased.

### How to Get Tears of Denial in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 10,000 souls after she is given the [Carim Tome](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim").

### Known Interactions:

  * [Sunlight Straight Sword](/Sunlight+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Straight Sword") will remove the buff if cast after using [tears](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial"). 
    * Therefore, buff with sword first and then use tears.
    * [Sacred Oath](/Sacred+Oath "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Oath") is unaffected and can be cast in any order.

  * [Saint-Tree Bellvine](/Saint-tree+Bellvine "Dark Souls 3 Saint-tree Bellvine") held in left-hand and casts [Gentle Prayer](/Gentle+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Gentle Prayer") does not overwrite [tears](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial"). 
    * Similarly, it does not overwrite when cast in reserve.
    * Right-hand shows no overwriting.
    * Two-handed shows no overwriting.

  * Overwritten by other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"): 
    * [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within")
    * [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection")
    * [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier")
    * [Great Magic Barrier](/Great+Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Barrier")
    * [Stone Flesh](/Stone+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Stone Flesh")
    * [Sunlight Straight Sword](/Sunlight+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Straight Sword")
    * [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon")
    * [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat")
    * [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat")
  * If in doubt look for  under your stamina bar. This icon denotes the [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial") buff being present on your character.

### Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Unlike its counterpart in Dark Souls II, this miracle lasts indefinitely.
  * When the user reaches 0 HP, the miracle is activated and the player automatically goes back to 1 HP, using up the spell.
  * Saves from lethal fall damage, unless the drop is too high or is a bottomless pit.
  * The "flame" particle floating in your body can be clearly seen, even under the effects of [Young White Branches](/Young+White+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Young White Branch"), [Chameleon](/Chameleon "Dark Souls 3 Chameleon"), the [Obscuring Ring](/Obscuring+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Obscuring Ring") and [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body"). 
    * Some other effects, including those of certain rings such as the [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring"), will mask the particle effect, and if equipped before casting will even disguise the casting sound. Once the provided buff clears, the casting sound and flame will reappear. The icon for the buff will prove that it has worked, however.
  * Changing areas will remove this miracle's buff if it was used beforehand. Additionally, quitting a game with it cast will result in its removal of its buff the next time the game is loaded.
  * Although this is a buff, it is not canceled by the [Duel Charm](/Duel+Charm "Dark Souls 3 Duel Charm")

  * If you've got at least 10 FTH, you can use this spell by doing this: Equip the [Priestess Ring](/Priestess+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Priestess Ring"), now equip this miracle at a bonfire. Use it, then switch your rings around and rest again. This is incredibly useful on all builds and can be the difference between a victory and a death.
