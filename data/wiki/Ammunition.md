# Ammunition

**Ammunition** in Dark Souls 3 are items that can be used with [Bows](/Bows "Dark Souls 3 Bows"), [Greatbows](/Greatbows "Dark Souls 3 Greatbows") and [Crossbows,](/Crossbows "Dark Souls 3 Crossbows") a player can equip two types of bolts and arrows at the same time.  
The damage dealt is '**p'** for Physical, '**m'** for Magic, '**f** ' for Fire, '**l'** for Lightning, '**d'** for Dark, '**bl'** for bleed, '**po'** for poison.
