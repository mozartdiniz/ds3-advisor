**Assassin** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** designed for players who want to blend swift combat with sorcery. With access to the Spook spell right from the start, this class offers an advantage in avoiding fall damage early in the game. The Assassin is ideal for those looking to use Dexterity weapons while also incorporating Intelligence for sorceries.

Starting at level 10, the Assassin is well-suited for Int/Dex builds (also known as Warrior Sorcerers), allowing players to effectively balance ranged magic with agile melee combat. With solid base stats for both Dexterity and Intelligence, the Assassin provides a strong foundation for a versatile playstyle.

> An assassin who stalks their prey from the shadows. Favors sorceries in addition to thrusting swords.

### Dark Souls 3 Assassin Notes and Tips

  * Spook could be considered one of the most useful starting spells, as other means of negating fall damage don't appear until later in the game. 
  * This class is very good to start with if you want to use sorceries as well as normal combat effectively. You can use Dex weapons from the onset and add Int as you go.

### Builds That Use Assassin in DS3

  * Int/Dex builds (Warrior Sorcerers)
  * Dex builds
