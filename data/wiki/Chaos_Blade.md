**Chaos Blade** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  

> A cursed sword of unknown origin bearing uncanny streaks on its blade. Attacks also damage its wielder.  
>   
> The sword is not unlike a thing misshapen, granted life, but never welcome in this world. In other words, chaos itself.  
>   
> Skill: Hold  
> Assume a holding stance to rapidly execute a lunging slash with normal attack, or a deflecting parry with strong attack.

### Chaos Blade Notes and Tips

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Upgrades with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Damages the wielder with each strike (≈1% of HP), but inflicts more bleeding than other [Katanas](/Katanas "Dark Souls 3 Katanas") and has potentially the highest damage.
  * The HP loss can be countered with varying efficiency by the [Sun Princess Ring](/Sun+Princess+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Sun Princess Ring"), [Ring of the Evil Eye](/Ring+of+the+Evil+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Evil Eye"), [Pontiff's Left Eye](/Pontiff's+Left+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Left Eye") and spells like [Replenishment](/Replenishment "Dark Souls 3 Replenishment"), [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Light") and [Bountiful Sunlight](/Bountiful+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Sunlight").
  * Ripostes & Backstabs won't trigger the damage per strike. Useful to keep the [Lloyd's Sword Ring](/Lloyd's+Sword+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lloyd's Sword Ring") buff.
  * This weapon previously appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Chaos+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Blade")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Chaos+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Blade")_.
  * Used by the Sword Master instead of the Uchigatana when the Sword Master is summoned at Untended Graves.

### Location/Where to Find

  * It is located at the top of the path to the left of the dark Firelink Shrine ahead of the [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr")/[Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") bonfire (it parallels the place where you fought the [Sword Master ](/Sword+Master "Dark Souls 3 Sword Master") with the [Uchigatana](/Uchigatana "Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana") in [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash")).

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
