# Hawkwood's Swordgrass

**Hawkwood's Swordgrass** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in Dark Souls 3.

### Hawkwood's Swordgrass  
  
---  
  

> Blood-stained swordgrass of Hawkwood, deserter of the Undead Legion.  
>   
> Traditionally, the Undead Legion of Farron sends the gravest of messages using swordgrass.  
>   
> "Come to the mausoleum in Farron. Only one can take the path of ancient dragons."  
  
##  Acquisition

  * This item becomes available from [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") after acquiring the [Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone1](/Twinkling+Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone"), which is found at the furthest altar in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").

## Hawkwood's Swordgrass Usage

  * This item directs the player to [Hawkwood's](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") location, which is where the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") boss fight occurred. Defeating him will reward the player with the [Twinkling Dragon Head Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Head+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Head Stone").2

### Hawkwood's Swordgrass Locations

  * Given to you by [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") when you obtain the [Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone"), speak to [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") first to receive the [Farron Ring](/Farron+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Farron Ring"). He will leave after reloading at which point you can speak to Andre to receive the item. Losing will result in loss of the Torso stone.3

### Notes

  * 1 Hawkwood becomes unavailable as a summon after picking up this item.
  * 2 This item is **NOT** required to duel Hawkwood. It is simply to help the player locate him.
  * 3 While losing to Hawkwood will lose you the Twinkling Dragon Head Stone, it is not permanently lost. When you are victorious, Hawkwood will drop both Twinkling Dragon Stones.

### Trivia

  * Hawkwood was once a member of the Undead Legion of Abyss Watchers, who belong to the Watchdogs of Farron covenant. This is why he uses swordgrass to deliver this message to the player.
