### Ghru Spearman Enemy Description

  * Ghru Spearman are clothed [Ghru](/Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Ghru") that use a spear and shield to fight.
  * Ghrus are descendants of the Farron guardians. Some event has turned them into goatlike demons.

### Ghru Spearman Combat Information

  * Ghrus are fast and aggressive but their attacks are easily blocked.
  * Like all other Ghru weapons, the spear causes poison buildup.
  * The shield of the Ghru Spearman does not reduce a lot of damage but they can attack while blocking.
