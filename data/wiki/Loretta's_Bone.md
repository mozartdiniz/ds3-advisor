# Loretta's Bone

**Loretta's Bone** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Loretta's Bone  
  
---  
  

> Old, discolored human bone with several holes bored into it.  
>   
> A woman's corpse in the Undead Settlement was found clutching this bone. Her name was Loretta.  
  
### Loretta's Bone Usage

  * Give to [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement) to complete his first quest.

### Loretta's Bone Locations

  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement): On a hanging corpse on the balcony of a house. You will have to hit the corpse from the balcony and then go down outside collect the item. The house is to the left of the Undead Settlement bonfire. The balcony is on the lower floor.

### Notes

  * Can be given to [Pickle Pee](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Pickle Pee") for a [Ring of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Ring of Sacrifice").

### Trivia

  * Loretta's Bone closely resembles an old, broken Homeward Bone when put side-by-side.
