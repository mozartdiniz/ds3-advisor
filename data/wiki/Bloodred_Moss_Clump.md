# Bloodred Moss Clump

**Bloodred Moss Clump** is a [Consumable](/Consumables) in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Bloodred Moss Clump  
  
---  
  

> Red moss clump used as maggot repellent.  
>   
> Reduces bleeding build-up.  
>   
> Bleeding builds up when attacked by sharp blades or spikes, and once triggered causes severe damage. Use this moss clump before it reaches that point.  
  
### Bloodred Moss Clump Usage

  * Reduces [bleed](/bleed "bleed") build-up.
  * Removes bleed-inducing maggots.

### Bloodred Moss Clump Locations

  * Sold by [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for 500 souls.
  * Rare drop from small crabs in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrifices").
  * 3x found in [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Catacombs of Carthus"), on a platform overlooking the long staircase with the boulder, reached from a sidepath at the top of the staircase.
