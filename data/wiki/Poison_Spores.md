# Poison Spores

## Poison Spores  
  
---  
  
##   
  
> Expel noxious spores from the formless, parasitical things that inhabit the staff.  
  
| 20  
  
**Poison Spores** is a [Skill](/Skills) in Dark Souls 3.

### Weapons That Have Skill Name

  * [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff)

### Notes & Information

  * It fuctions similar to the Poison Mist pyromancy. The staff spews a poison cloud in front of the player that builds up fairly quick.
  * Weapon Art applies 42 Poison buildup per tick in cloud
  * Poison from this weapon art is more powerful than Toxic and other sources of Poison
