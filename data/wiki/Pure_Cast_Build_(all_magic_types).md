## Build Strategy

Unfortunately, spells can't afford aggresive playstyle. So that's why we should force our opponent play aggrasive. The best spell combo for it is [Homing Crystal Soulmass](/Homing+Crystal+Soulmass "Homing Crystal Soulmass") and [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow "Lightning Arrow"). Turn back, cast Soulmass and hold your Lightning Arrow. Then target your enemy and shoot after Soulmass releases. 

Other spell for reviwe is [Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges "Chaos Bed Vestiges"). It can deal large amount of damage (~600). However it requires knowledge when to cast it.(Just go and play. There no any tips for it).

If you get pressed, use [Black Flame](/Black+Flame "Black Flame") to deal enourmous amount of damage (~1000). The Pyromancer's Parting Flame will help you to stagger your enemy for 2 casts.

[Fire Surge](/Fire+Surge "Fire Surge") will help you to make final hit to low HP enemy. Also by spamming it, you can make your opponent very aggresive.

Good Luck in battles, Ashen One.
