# Grand Archives

**Grand Archives** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. Scholars endlessly navigate the towering bookshelves of the archives, seeking for and guarding long lost secrets with darkness that can only be countered by submerging oneself in wax. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

## Full Grand Archives Walkthrough

A full walkthrough including all the items, enemies and bosses goes here. You can add links, images. and other details. Click [here](/Grand+Archives#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives#speedrun") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough.   
  

### Facing the Crystal Sage

From the Grand Archives bonfire proceed forward and open the double doors with the [Grand Archives Key](/Grand+Archives+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives Key"), which you can grab after defeating 3 Lords of Cinder. Through the doors head straight and dispatch the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage+\(Mob\) "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage \(Mob\)"). If you do not kill him quickly, he will disappear and teleport around the area. One technique to killing the Sage when first encountered is to equip a weapon laced with poison or rub one down with [Rotten Pine Resin](/Rotten+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Pine Resin") before going through the doors. Pelt the Sage with [Dung Pies](/Dung+Pie "Dark Souls 3 Dung Pie") on approach and then hit it as many times as you can with your poison laced weapon before the Sage disappears. A better strategy may be to use a combination of Toxic Mist and Poison Mist pyromancies, not requiring a heavy investment of FTH to be effective. If the Sage is toxic and poisoned and it's HP knocked down sufficiently enough it may drop dead off scene in which case a pop up that the Crystal Scroll has been obtained will appear. If this doesn't kill the Sage then the second encounter should make for a quick kill. When you eventually kill him he will drop the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll").

### Cover your head in Wax

Make your way back into the room and head left to find a thrall. Kill it and head left up the stairs to loot a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Drop down and continue forward up the stairs to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). Take a staircase up here to find [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x. Take a staircase back around for a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Continue exploring this room where you will encounter a thrall, as well as a red eyed lothric knight. The Knight guards a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1, and a room nearby him has the [Crystal Chime](/Crystal+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Chime"). Near the main portion of this room you can go up a library stepladder to find a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Head to the altar section of the room and take out the wax covered priests. Once defeated, approach the wax pool and accept the prompt to Dunk head in wax. This will cover your character in wax and protect you from being cursed by an enemy you will soon encounter. 

Make a right into the next room to take out 2 Crystal Lizards. Ghostly hands will pop out from the walls here and if not waxed they will inflict curse upon you. In this room you will find a corpse with [Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Nameless Soldier") 1x. Just right after the third book case on the wall in front of you there is a lever which opens a secret room on your left, you will find [Witch's Locks](/Witch's+Locks "Dark Souls 3 Witch's Locks") in a chest and [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within") on a corpse. Head up the stairs and go left to kill a wax priest. Behind him loot a corpse for [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Step out into the adjacent room to kill a wax priest. To the right kick down the ladder. In an alcove in this room you can loot a corpse for [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Continue down this room to take on a crystal lizard. If you continue straight and to the balcony outside you will find [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") 3x on a corpse hanging over the railing. On this balcony you can find a ladder up. Advance into the next room where you may encounter the Crystal Sage if you have not killed him yet. To the right will be a wax priest. Across this path will be more thralls and a wax priest at the end. At the end you can drop down to the next ledge to find a corpse with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x.

If you're feeling athletic you can roll off here to land on top of a bookcase where you can head to the left to find [Avelyn](/Avelyn "Dark Souls 3 Avelyn"). Head back and drop off to the right and take the ladder on the right up. Head right and proceed until you reach the stairs on the right. Take them up to take on 3 thralls. You can loot a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x here. There is a wax pool here if your earlier dip has worn off. 

### Find the Boreal Outrider Knight & Shortcut

Take the stairs down and head to the opposite staircase and go up where you will encounter some thralls and a wax priest and around the corner another wax priest. Loot a corpse in the corner for [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. To the right attack a wall to reveal an [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls"). There will be a [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight") in this next room. Defeating him will earn you the [Outrider Knight Armor Set](/Outrider+Knight+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Outrider Knight Armor Set"). On your way out of this room, head right down the walkway to find [Soul Stream](/Soul+Stream "Dark Souls 3 Soul Stream") on a corpse.

Head out of this room and drop down to the lower level and take the central walkway out and up the stairs. At the top of the stairs to the right you will come to a lift. Take the lift down to the bottom. Exit the lift and straight ahead you will see a lever. Pull it to open the door in front of you, unlocking a shortcut to the main entrance.

### Find the Titanite Slab

Take the lift back up and head out to the main central path, make a right before the next door to find a [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Shriving Stone") 1x. Make your way towards the exit of this room, leading outside. Before you exit turn left to find a lever which will slide the nearby bookcase to reveal a room. In this room you will find the [Scholar Ring](/Scholar+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Scholar Ring") on a corpse and a wax pool. Head down the stairs where thralls will come out and attack. Defeat them and proceed to the left where you will find another level. Pull it to lower a bookcase in the room. The chest is located on the floor with the giant wax pool. Once you reach the giant wax pool, you'll turn AWAY from it to face the railing that overlooks the Crystal Sage spawn point, walk to here and once you reach the railing with a dead body next to it, and a red eyed skeleton to the left behind some book cases, look right, and the chest contains a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab"). 

Make your way back to the central bridge and cross it, exiting via the doorway and up the stairs. Turn left and head up the staircase to encounter 2 wax priests, avoiding the many ghost hands here. Loot [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x. In a corner is a chest with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 3x.

Exit the room via the doorway where a wax priest stands. Head up the stairs to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") to the right. Nearby you will find [Fleshbite Ring](/Fleshbite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fleshbite Ring") on a corpse. Exit the room to outside and drop down to the left to reach the rooftops. To the right you will come onto a large rooftop where a stone gargoyle will fly in and attack. You can loot a corpse at the edge here for a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. You can drop off this roof to a lower level, and enter the archway to find a corpse with [Hollow Gem](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") 1x. Step out the doorway here and head right to find a corpse at the end with a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Make your way back through the room you just came from and head left to encounter some winged foes. Try and take them out one at a time. Proceed onto the next rooftop and if you head left to the corner and look down you will see another roof you can jump to. If you're feeling athletic, make the jump to find a corpse with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 3x as well as [Greirat's Ashes](/Greirat's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Greirat's Ashes"). If not, be prepared to engage a gargoyle guarding a [Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Weary Warrior") 1x. To return to this spot, jump forward over the railing to land on the balcony where you can work your way back up to the roof to find the aforementioned Soul and 2 Crystal Lizards to the left. 3 Gargoyles will engage you on this roof. On an adjacent rooftop you can find a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x.

### Hostile NPC trio

Drop down to enter another building where a wax priest waits in the opening. You can loot an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x on this top level as well as an [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") 1x. You can kick down a ladder on the right to unlock access to and from the lower level. Further across the floor you will encounter a thrall and red eyed lothric knight. To the left on a balcony, you will find a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 2x. Head back inside the building and head right, until you come to an opening on the right. Step outside and into the courtyard with the statue in the middle and head up the large set of stairs to take on 3 NPCs. The one in Faraam armor drops the [Golden Wing Crest Shield](/Golden+Wing+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Golden Wing Crest Shield"), The Mage drops [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff"). The dual katana wielder drops [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi"). A corpse on the stairs can be looted for [Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Large+Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x.   
You can avoid fighting them by equipping the [slumbering dragoncrest ring](/slumbering+dragoncrest+ring "Dark Souls 3 slumbering dragoncrest ring") and casting [hidden body](/hidden+body "Dark Souls 3 hidden body"). Casting [spook](/spook "Dark Souls 3 spook") instead of wearing the ring should work as well, but it's trickier due to hidden body's short duration. Extending the duration with the [lingering dragoncrest ring](/lingering+dragoncrest+ring "Dark Souls 3 lingering dragoncrest ring") helps.

### Major Shortcut & Winged Knights

Continue up the stairs and into the building to find a lift on your right. Activate it to unlock the shortcut back to the Grand Archives bonfire. Take the lift back up and head right out the building and make an immediately left and take the left staircase up. At the top head left to find [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x. Continue to a gap in the railing and hug the railing until you reach a staircase. Go up the stairs and head right and head right through an archway to find a corpse along the railing with an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") 1x. Return to where you first stepped onto the roof and head up the roof, through the staircase and up the roof further, before entering the building you can head right and take a ladder to a tower where you will find [Hunter's Ring](/Hunter's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hunter's Ring"). Head back down and return up the roof to enter the building. Along the way you will encounter 3 [Ascended Winged Knights](/Ascended+Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ascended Winged Knight") who will drop a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") the first time you kill them all. Inside the the building find a hole in the floor where you can drop down to the beam and drop again and then once more inside the cage to find the miracle [Divine Pillars of Light](/Divine+Pillars+of+Light "Dark Souls 3 Divine Pillars of Light"). Step out of the cage to find a [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") on the beam. A [man grub](/Man-grub "Dark Souls 3 Man-grub") caster on a beam below will be casting soul spear at you. Drop down to this beam, kill the grub and take the nearby ladder down to find 2 chests with Divine Blessing 1x and [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") 3x. 

### Boss Fight: Twin Princes

Make your way either back to the bonfire or the lift that connects to it. At the top of the lift, head right out the building and take the main path forward to come to a series of blockades with many hollows behind them. Beyond will be knight enemies. Proceed forward and find a doorway on the right where you can enter a building to find a lift. Take it down to activate a shortcut to [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). If you activate the lift again here and roll off, a second lift platform will rise up. Step on the lift and take it down to find a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") 1x on a corpse here. Take the lift back up and head right to open the double doors. Enter the next chamber to fight the boss, [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince").

Defeat the Twin Princes to obtain [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord") and [Soul of the Twin Princes](/Soul+of+the+Twin+Princes "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Twin Princes"). Light the bonfire and return to Firelink and place the Cinders on the throne to initiate the final battle at [Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame"). Or you may opt to finish exploring the optional areas of [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"), [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") and [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").
