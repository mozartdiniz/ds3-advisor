# Dragon Chaser's Ashes

**Dragon Chaser's Ashes** is an [Ash](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes") in Dark Souls 3.

### Dragon Chaser's Ashes  
  
---  
  

> Umbral ash of the fallen warrior who chose the path of the ancient dragons. With this, the shrine handmaid will prepare new items.  
>   
> Whatever it is, it is sure to grant strength to the most unrelenting warriors.  
  
### Dragon Chaser's Ashes Usage

  * Give it to the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") to get her to sell [Titanite Chunks](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") and [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").

### Dragon Chaser's Locations

  * [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") from the Great Belfry bonfire, exit the room to the left and follow the path leading to the twinkling dragon body stone. Hug the left edge. Dragon Chaser's Ashes are located to the left of the first archway, on a corpse being guarded by a Rock Lizard.

### Notes

  * The path has a large number of enemies, staying to the left makes it easier to run directly to the ashes if you don't want to fight.
