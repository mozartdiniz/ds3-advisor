# Lightning Gem

**Lightning Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Lightning Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Found in the aftermath of dragon hunts.  
>   
> Used in infusion to create lightning weapons.  
>   
> Lightning weapons inflict lightning damage, and scale with faith.  
  
### Lightning Gem Usage

  * Used in [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create Lighting weapons.
  * Adds [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") scaling and [Lighting Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage") to the weapon.

### Lightning Gem Locations

  * 1x at the end of the main road before the altar, near the Church of Yorshka, in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"). ()
  * 1x obtained trading the [Xanthous Crown](/Xanthous+Crown "Dark Souls 3 Xanthous Crown") with [Pickle Pee](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
  * Rare drop from the larger enemies near the Nameless King encounter in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").
  * Very rare drop from smaller enemies thoughout Archdragon Peak.
  * [Video Location in Archdragon Peak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lSE9s9D3LbI&feature=youtu.be&t=17m51s)
  * Very rare drop from Blue Lothric Knights
  * Very rare drop from the [Lothric Thieves](/Lothric+Thief "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Thief") in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)").

### Notes

  * Lightning infused weapons cannot be buffed by spells/miracles.
  * Requires the [Giant's Coal](/Giant's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Giant's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.
