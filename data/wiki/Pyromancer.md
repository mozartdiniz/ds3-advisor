**Pyromancer** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , offering a balanced mix of magic and physical combat. With the ability to wield pyromancy, one of the three forms of magic, this class excels at ranged fire-based attacks that scale with both Intelligence and Faith. Pyromancers can enhance their effectiveness by using Chaos weapon refinements, which boost fire damage while scaling with both stats.

With access to both powerful pyromancies and miracles, Pyromancers are perfect for players who enjoy a "Jack of All Trades" playstyle. Starting at level 8, the class benefits from a strong combination of Intelligence and Faith, allowing for significant spell damage and the option to use both offensive pyromancies and supportive miracles, making it a powerful choice for those seeking flexibility in combat.

> A pyromancer from a remote region who manipulates flame. Also an adept close combat warrior who wields a hand axe.

### Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer Notes and Tips

  * Interesting attack properties and defensive properties while being able to wield pyromancy, the third form of magic in the game.
  * Pyromancies scale based on Intelligence and Faith attributes.
  * [Chaos](/Chaos+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Gem") weapon refinements are particularly useful for pyromancers, due to the fire damage the weapon inflicts, and scaling with both INT and FTH. 
    * However, as with all elemental upgrades, remember that the physical and elemental damage are both affected by defenses.
  * The benefit of having both INT and FTH gives you a wide armament, as you can use both miracles and spells in addition to pyromancies to your benefit, making pyromancers ideal for "Jack of All Trades" players.
  * Most efficient starting class for INT and FTH caster builds statwise, unless you plan on using hollow weapons, due to low starting luck.
