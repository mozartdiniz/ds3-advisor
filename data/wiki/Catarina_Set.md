**Catarina Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Distinctively shaped armor worn by the Knights of Catarina.
> 
> Often ridiculed for its onion-like shape, infuriating the country's proud knights, but the masterfully forged curved design makes it very effective for deflective blows.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Unbreakable Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Dark Souls 3 Unbreakable Patches") for 15,000 souls in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") on the way to [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") near the lever or after Patches has moved to the [Firelink Shrine.](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine")
  * Dropped by [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") upon his death.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * If you kill [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") at the Undead settlement, past Eygon, he drops this set.
  * If you kill Unbreakable Patches, set will be dropped in firelink shrine, in the place where Patches was, after reload.
