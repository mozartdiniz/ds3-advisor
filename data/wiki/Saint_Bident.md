**Saint Bident** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.

> A silver bident decorated by a holy symbol, formerly wielded by Saint Klimt. He discarded this weapon, that draws upon one's faith, on the day that he put his own faith behind him.   
>   
> Skill: Charge   
> Hold spear at waist and charge at foe. Use strong attack while charging to extend the length of the charge.

### Saint Bident Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Resembles the weapon wielded by Archangel Avacyn, a character from the trading card game "Magic: the Gathering"
  * It's actually longer than the [Pike](/Pike "Dark Souls 3 Pike") while still having the same moveset as standard spears (its normal and heavy attacks are much faster than pikes). 
  * Faith scaling was buffed in regulation 1.32. Now has C faith scaling at regular +10 compared to D scaling pre-patch.
  * With 60 Faith and at base requirements for Strength and Dexterity with the Lightning infusion the AR is 504, +10 uninfused with [Lighting Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade") the AR reaches 552.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"): next to one of the chained-Giants, there is a big blue door that leads to the area where this is found.
  * Watch the [Fextra Video](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=2h31m31s "Fextra Video") at 2hr 31mins 31secs in.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
