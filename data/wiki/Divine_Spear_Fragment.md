# Divine Spear Fragment

**Divine Spear Fragment** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Divine Spear Fragment  
  
---  
  

> Online play item.  
> A fragment of the matching ritual spears once held by the knights of Princess Filianore. These Divine Spears of gold were placed in the trusted hands of exalted Spears. When Spears of the Church face unduly treacherous foes, this allows them to draw upon former majesty, and summon a row of upward-thrusting ritual spears.  
  
### Divine Spear Fragment Usage

  * Functions like the [Ritual Spear Fragment](/Ritual+Spear+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Ritual Spear Fragment")
  * Shares the same cooldown as the Ritual Spear Fragment
  * Deals almost double the damage of the Ritual Spear Fragment, while the casting time and effect duration stay the same

Ritual Spear Fragment Function:

  * Summons a row of upwardly thrusting ritual spears
  * Gives the player poise when used
  * Can only be used if the Player is summoned as a Spear of the Church
  * When used, the player can either be surrounded or "throw" the fragment just before them to summon upward thrusting ritual spears that deal lightning damage to foes. Spears will persist for a few seconds before disappearing. An additional line of spears will appear in the direction that the item was originally used, not the direction of the player after using the item.

### Divine Spear Fragment Locations

  * [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") covenant reward for turning in 30 [Filianore's Spear Ornament](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament")
