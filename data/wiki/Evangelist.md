# Cathedral Evangelist

## Cathedral Evangelist  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Undead  
**Weakness** | Frost, Bleed, Thrust, Backstab  
**Resistances** | Fire, Strike  
**Immune** | N/A  
  
**Cathedral Evangelist** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Cathedral Evangelist Enemy Description

Commonly referred to as Evangelists, after the name of their armor set.

Often mocks you or chants, even when not actively attacking.

### Cathedral Evangelist Locations

  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement")
  * [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")
  * [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep")

### Cathedral Evangelist Combat Information

  * The color of the glow from her spellbook shows which spell is coming, pale yellow for [Gnaw](/Gnaw "Dark Souls 3 Gnaw") and smoldering orange for the fiery grab attack.
  * The mace is very dangerous if it connects, but slow and leaves openings. The bible thump comes out much faster and is mostly used in self-defense to interrupt your combo.
  * Deals [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage"), [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage") and [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"). May also cause Blood Loss.
  * When using grapple attack or casting Gnaw, she can easily be backstabbed.
  * Weak to [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed") and [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage").
  * Resistant to [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * Mace and book attacks can be parried and followed up with a riposte.
  * Evangelists have more poise than most enemies, raising the appeal of using heavy weapons in melee combat.
  * One of the only enemies to call out their attacks with spoken words, as introduced in Bloodborne. Specifically the immolation hug, the rest is just mad cackling and mumbling.  

    * "Oh, child... [FWOOSH] **...come to me!** "
    * "I'll cleanse the- [FWOOSH] **...bastard's curse!** "

### Cathedral Evangelist Notes & Trivia

  * The Cathedral Evangelist bears a striking resemblance to the [Fat Officials](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Fat+Official "Dark Souls 3 Fat Official") from [Demon's Souls](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Demons+Souls+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Demons Souls Wiki").
  * Best taken out at range - grapple attack often triggers during your combos, and mace has powerful knockdown/stamina effect.
  * Knocking her down very close to the player will occasionally knock down the player as well, dealing very little damage and causing a long wakeup animation.
  * On her belt are several real-life torture implements, including a [pear of anguish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pear_of_anguish "pear of anguish") [](/file/Dark-Souls-3/Undead_Settlement_Enemies_Concept_Art_Evangelist_Peasant.jpg?v=1521727842680 "Cathedral Evangelist Concept Art - Pear of Anguish").
  * The Evangelist in front of the big tree in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), where the [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") is located, is praying with her hat off.
  * The Evangelists are voiced by [Clare Corbett](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm1352940/?ref_=ttfc_fc_cl_t5 "Clare Corbett"), who also voiced many other characters in the Souls games, such as [Saint Astraea](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Maiden+Astraea "Dark Souls 3 Maiden Astraea"), [Gwynevere](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwynevere "Dark Souls 3 Gwynevere"), [Priscilla](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crossbreed+Priscilla "Dark Souls 3 Crossbreed Priscilla"), and [Alsanna](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Alsanna,+Silent+Oracle "Dark Souls 3 Alsanna, Silent Oracle").
