# Sacred Chimes

**Sacred Chimes** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons) that replaced [Talismans](/Talismans) in Dark Souls 2, but now both have returned in Dark Souls 3. These [Weapons](/Weapons) are used to cast [Miracles](/Miracles) and sometimes [Sorceries](/Sorceries). The most common [Skill](/Skills) they have is [Gentle Prayer](/Gentle+Prayer), differentiating them for the Talismans.
