**Porcine Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in **Dark Souls 3**. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Shield based on a pig's head, depicted with unsettling authenticity.
> 
> Shields such as these were made to shame weak-willed knights, and those who carried these shields were subject to merciless ridicule. No one will defend a craven who abases himself.
> 
> Skill: Shield Bash  
>  Without lowering your guard, give the enemy a good shove with the shameful pig's snout.  
>  Works while equipped in either hand.

### Porcine Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite. 
  * This shield was also featured in _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Porcine+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Porcine Shield")_. It could be obtained from chest located after the [Smelter Demon](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Smelter+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Smelter Demon") boss fight.
  * Returning from _Dark Souls 2_ , it is allegedly a nod to EpicNameBro for his assistance with the _Dark Souls 2_ guidebook and his Marcus Greatshield submission to the Dark Souls 2 Shield Design Contest held by Bandai Namco and FromSoftware.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Trade an [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") with [Pickle Pee](/Pickle+Pee "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee") on top of [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").
  * Due to the fact that you lose one of your Undead Bone Shards in process of obtaining this shield, it is recommended that you don't sacrifice it before getting to NG+, where next Bone Shards become pretty much useless.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
