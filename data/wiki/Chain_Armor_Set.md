**Chain Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Chainmail armor of thin interlinking rings of steel.
> 
> Popular due to its ease of crafting, respectable damage absorption, and light weight.
> 
> Knights may favor imposing armor, but for warriors on the battlefield, that which keeps them alive is armor enough.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") starting from beginning of the game for 2300 souls.
