# HP

## HP  
  
---  
  
**HP** or**Hit Points** in **Dark Souls 3** is the amount of damage a player or enemy can take before being defeated. It is represented by a red bar on the top left of the HUD, and can be replenished via using consumables such as the Estus Flask or Divine Blessing. Some spells and equipment also have regenerative properties.

### Dark Souls 3 HP Information

  * See [Vigor](/Vigor "Dark Souls 3 Vigor") for details on all HP values.
  * Vigor has a [  
](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats")[soft cap](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") at 26.
  * Minimum HP is 323 with the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer") (9 [Vigor](/Vigor "Dark Souls 3 Vigor")) equipped with [Dusk Crown Ring](/Dusk+Crown+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring")
  * Maximum HP is 2122 with 99 Vigor, [Life Ring +3](/Life+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Life Ring"), [Ring of Favor +3](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor") and [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") (2082HP in the base game with the +2 ring versions)

### Dark Souls 3 Items for HP

#### Items that Increase max HP

  * [Life Ring](/Life+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Life Ring") (starter item)
  * [Ring of Favor](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor")
  * [Prisoner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner's Chain") (raises vigor, which affects HP)
  * [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") (if you are unembered)

#### Items that Decrease max HP

  * [Dusk Crown Ring](/Dusk+Crown+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring")
  * [Jailers](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer") in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") has a Lantern and a Branding Iron that will temporarily decrease max HP, down to 1 if you are exposed long enough.

#### Items that Drains HP

  * [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within"): -30%/maxhp
  * [Chaos Blade](/Chaos+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Blade"): -15HP/strike
  * [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice"): -10HP/s
  * [Bloodlust katana](/Bloodlust "Dark Souls 3 Bloodlust")
