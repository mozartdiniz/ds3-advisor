# Deep Protection

Deep Protection is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Deep Protection  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 25  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 20 Faith  
**Type** | Body Buff  
**Duration** | 60 Seconds  
  
> Miracle taught to inaugurated deacons of the Cathedral of the Deep.   
>   
> Slightly boosts attack, damage absorption and resistance, while also increasing stamina recovery speed.   
>   
> The deep was originally a peaceful and sacred place, but became the final rest for many abhorrent things. This tale of the Deep offers protection for those who worship amidst those horrors. 

### How to Get Deep Protection in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") or [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 4,000 souls, after you give either of them the [Deep Braille Divine Tome](/Deep+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Deep Braille Divine Tome")

### Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases all damage types (Both weapons and spells) by 5% multiplicatively (all Damage * 1.05).
  * Grants 5.0 absorption. This causes you to take 5% less damage than currently (damage taken * 0.95).
  * Both the Damage increase and Absorption increase are halved during PvP.
  * Increases [Resistances](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") by 20.
  * Increases [Stamina](/Stamina "Dark Souls 3 Stamina") regeneration speed by +3 per second (base regeneration is 45 per second).
  * Does not stack with other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial"), [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon"), [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within") or ([Great](/Great+Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Barrier")) [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier").
  * Fittingly for a dark miracle, this spell is effectively a more selfish version of [Sacred Oath](/Sacred+Oath "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Oath"), with which this miracle also stacks with.
