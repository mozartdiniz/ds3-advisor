# Soul of Consumed Oceiros

**Soul of Consumed Oceiros** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of Consumed Oceiros  
  
---  
  

> Soul of Oceiros, the Consumed King. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. Oceiros went mad trying to harness his royal blood for a greater purpose, leading him to the heretics of the Grand Archives, where he discovered the twisted worship of Seath the paledrake.   
  
### Soul of Consumed Oceiros Usage

  * Can be consumed for 12,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [White Dragon Breath](/White+Dragon+Breath) or [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword) via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition)

### Soul of Consumed Oceiros Locations

  * Obtained by defeating [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King)

### Notes

  * The [White Dragon Breath](/White+Dragon+Breath "White Dragon Breath") spell seems easily outclassed in cast speed and damage by the [Crystal Soul Spear](/Crystal+Soul+Spear "Crystal Soul Spear").

### Trivia

  * Oceiros' soul, along with the [Soul of a Crystal Sage](/Soul+of+a+Crystal+Sage "Soul of a Crystal Sage") and [Soul of Sister Friede](/Soul+of+Sister+Friede "Soul of Sister Friede"), are the only three souls the have a light blue hue to it. This is compareable to [Aldrich](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Aldrich's") and the [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Deacons of the Deep's"), in which both of their souls are the only ones to a dark blue hue to them.
