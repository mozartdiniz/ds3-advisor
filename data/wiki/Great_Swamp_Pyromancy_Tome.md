# Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome

Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in Dark Souls 3.

### Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome  
  
---  
  

> Pyromancy tome from the Great Swamp containing advanced pyromancies. Give to the old master pyromancer to learn advanced pyromancies of the Great Swamp. Spells of the Great Swamp are passed down from master to pupil. Without a master, there is no pupil, but without a pupil, there is also no master.  
  
### How to Use Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome Dark Souls 3

  * Give to [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") to unlock [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb"), [Bursting Fireball](/Bursting+Fireball "Dark Souls 3 Bursting Fireball"), [Poison Mist](/Poison+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Poison Mist"), [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat")

### Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome Location

  * From Farron Keep bonfire: Turn around and climb up the ladder where you encountered the Exile Knights and walk out the entrance door. From there, hug the wall to the left until you see a big crab. Loot the Tome from a nearby tree right next to the wall.
  * [Video location](http://youtu.be/kxNB7QPDWFY "Video location")
