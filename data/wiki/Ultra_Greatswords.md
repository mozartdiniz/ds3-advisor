# Ultra Greatswords

**Ultra Greatswords** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") returning in **Dark Souls 3**. These Weapons usually do very high damage and have extremely slow attacks along with high [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength") requirements. Ultra Greatswords benefit from "hyper armor" during certain attack animations, allowing you to continue a combo even while being struck. Ultra Greatswords also cannot be parried when two-handed, with the exception of the rolling and running attacks.

There are two types of Ultra Greatswords: Vertical and Horizontal.

Horizontal Ultra Greatswords have the standard Ultra Greatsword moveset when they are 1-handed and 2-handed, but are good for hitting multiple opponents due to its wide range.

Vertical Ultra Greatswords have the standard Ultra Greatsword moveset when they are 1-handed, but when 2-handed, the normal attacks turn into vertical slams and the ability to hit behind the user when the second normal attack is used.
