**Gravewarden Skeletons** are [enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Gravewarden Skeleton Enemy Description

  * Larger skeletons wielding a [Carthus Curved Sword](/Carthus+Curved+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Curved Sword") and [Carthus Shield](/Carthus+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Shield"), a [Carthus Curved Greatsword](/Carthus+Curved+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Curved Greatsword"), or a [Longbow](/Longbow "Dark Souls 3 Longbow").

### Gravewarden Skeleton Combat Information

  * The sword wielding variants inflict [bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"). 
  * Can roll with the effect of the [Carthus Milkring](/Carthus+Milkring "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Milkring"), dealing minor damage. 
  * Those with white eyes will return after their first death. Can be countered with a [blessed](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") weapon. 
  * Their attacks deals [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage") (sword) and [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") (shield).
  * Weak to [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") and Blessed weapons. 
  * Resistant to most types of damages.
