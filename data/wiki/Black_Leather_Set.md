**Black Leather Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Black dyed leather armor. Enables its wearer to hide in the shadows with silent finesse.
> 
> Mask worn by those with something to hide. Used to conceal the face, muffle the voice, and go by cover of darkness. (Thief Mask)
> 
> The wearer of this fine attire was admired by friends and enemies alike, for his skills were unmatched, and his heart was true as gold. As its new owner, you have quite the shoes to fill. (Black Leather Armor/Gloves/Boots)

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Unbreakable Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Dark Souls 3 Unbreakable Patches") after recruiting him at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") tower for 6500 souls. Or sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after killing Patches and giving her [Patches' Ashes](/Patches'+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Patches' Ashes"). The helm (Theif Mask) is sold by [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement").

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The way the description is written makes it sound like [Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Dark Souls 3 Unbreakable Patches") himself wrote it, for he wears this set also.
