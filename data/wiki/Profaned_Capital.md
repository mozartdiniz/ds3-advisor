# Profaned Capital

**Profaned Capital** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. The crumbling citadel is now home to deformed monstrosities and gargoyles, but the Lord of Cinder [Yhorm](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") still awaits at his throne for his long lost friend to come fulfill his promise. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### General Information

  * **Previous:** [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon")
  * **Next:** [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo") or [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle")
  * **Recommended Levels:** 55 to 65
  * **Bosses:** [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant")
  * **Bonfires:** Profaned Capital, Yhorm the Giant

## Full Profaned Capital Walkthrough

Click [here](/Profaned+Capital#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital#speedrun") to go to the speedrun section. At several points in this area's walkthrough you will cross in and out of Irithyll Dungeon.   
  
Click [here](/Lore#loreprofaned "Dark Souls 3 Lore#loreprofaned") for Lore and Speculation on this location. 

  

From Irithyll Dungeon, find the narrow stone bridge where you will enter the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital") and continue your journey towards the Lords of Cinder. On this bridge a [Gargoyle](/Gargoyle "Dark Souls 3 Gargoyle") will drop down to stop your progress. Defeat it and cross the bridge to come to a broken path. Take the ladder down and across the room take the ladder up to the planks where you will find the Stretch Out [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") and [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") 1x and nearby a [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). To challenge the boss early without exploring, see the Boss Fight heading below.

### Path to Siegward & Logan's Scroll

From the bonfire go back down the ladder to the lower floor and step out of the opening here and drop down. Head left to find a ladder on your left. Climb it to find [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") 2x. Head in this room and go to your left to exit and drop down to the ground and continue along the path. A [Gargoyle](/Gargoyle "Dark Souls 3 Gargoyle") will drop and attack here. Head right past him up the plank ramp and into the doorway.

Go straight and jump the hole in the floor to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") on the other side. Another lizard in the left tunnel. Continue down the hallway, and make a right. Step out onto the plank ledge and go right to find [Blooming Purple Moss Clump](/Blooming+Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Blooming Purple Moss Clump") 3x. Head back down the plank ledge and take it to the end and enter the door on the left. Straight ahead you will find [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin") 1x. To the left is a jump across a hole and a short set of stairs down. Straight down the hallway is a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). If you drop down the hole it will land you in a Toxic pool with some of the insect like creatures encountered in the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") waters. If choosing to explore this area prep toxic clearing items like [Blooming Purple Moss Clump](/Blooming+Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Blooming Purple Moss Clump"). Items you can loot in this pool are [Poison Gem](/Poison+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Poison Gem") 1x, [Cursebite Ring](/Cursebite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Cursebite Ring"), [Purging Stone](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone") 1x.

Exit the pool up the sloping shore and climb the ladder and head to your left and continue onto the curved roof. Drop down to a lower ledge and drop down again to the pool, kill the insect like creatures to find a corpse in the corner with a [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Shriving Stone") 1x. You can enter the building here to progress. Alternatively you can reach here via the pool but the other method will save some exposure to the toxicity.

Once in the building, head straight and open the ornate doors where 3 tough [Monstrosities of Sin](/Monstrosity+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Monstrosity of Sin") (hand beasts) lurk. These creatures have a lot of health and feature some powerful attacks so luring and tackling them one at a time is ideal. If they grab you in their mouths, they will also consume some of your souls. If you decide to engage them killing one will net you the [Eleonora](/Eleonora "Dark Souls 3 Eleonora") 1x. Melee users should note that these enemies are weak to bleed—which means they are weak to [Kukri](/Kukri "Dark Souls 3 Kukri") (bleed throwing knives) as well. Behind the hand beasts waits [Purging Stone](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone") x2 against the far wall.

Leave the building and head back up the ladder. Head left down the path again onto the curved roof and head right into the building to find a single [Monstrosity of Sin](/Monstrosity+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Monstrosity of Sin") (hand beast). Behind some pots here you can find a corpse with the [Court Sorcerer Set](/Court+Sorcerer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Court Sorcerer Set"). If you head down the stairs out of this room you will find [Rubbish](/Rubbish "Dark Souls 3 Rubbish") on a corpse. Back up the stairs and to the right you will encounter a [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") which will drop the [Court Sorcerer's Staff](/Court+Sorcerer's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Court Sorcerer's Staff").

Head back outside from where you first entered the building and head right and then right again to go up the stairs. Continue to the top of the stairs where you will encounter a hostile NPC to the right wielding the [Gargoyle Flame Hammer](/Gargoyle+Flame+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Gargoyle Flame Hammer"). He will drop [Logan's Scroll](/Logan's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Logan's Scroll"). Along the roof nearby you can loot a corpse for [Poison Arrow](/Poison+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Poison Arrow") 18x.

From this roof you can jump across to a window and take the staircase up. You can use the [Old Cell Key](/Old+Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Old Cell Key") to open the door. In this room you will find the [Covetous Gold Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring") and if you've been progressing his quest line, [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina"). If you decide to find his cell, retrace your steps back to the roof. Once back on the roof, head into the window next to drop down to a central platform where you will find the [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles"), [Wrath of the Gods](/Wrath+of+the+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Wrath of the Gods").

Drop down to the floor and exit and head right and up the stairs again. Head left up the staircase to find 2 invisible jailers. To kill them attack the far wall, then and head left along the path to find the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring"). If you drop down into the large area, you will land in a giant's room with rats that respawn. The giant can be downed similar to the others, sticking to its ankles and behind, keeping clear of its attacks. There is a tunnel you can use here to duck into to avoid its attacks. Once dead, you can loot the area for [Profaned Flame](/Profaned+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Flame"), [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard") 2x, [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x, [Dung Pie](/Dung+Pie "Dark Souls 3 Dung Pie") 4x. Exit via the doorway to come to a lift. Take it to the top and open the gate, unlocking the shortcut from [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon").

### Using the Jailer's Key Ring and Freeing Karla

Return down the lift and exit the large area through the large opening and head right up the stairs. In the corner of this room is a doorway and a staircase leading up which takes you to a room with another [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic"). Kill it to retrieve [Dragonslayer Lightning Arrow](/Dragonslayer+Lightning+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Lightning Arrow") 10x. Take the ladder in this room up and exit the building to the plank ledge. Jump across the gap to loot [Lightning Bolt](/Lightning+Bolt "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Bolt") 9x. Head into the opening here and head left down the stairs, or right towards a [Jailer](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer"). If you go left you will cross through a room and outside where several [Jailers](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer") wait right at the doorway. Dispatch or run past them and head up the stairs into the shortcut door you opened earlier.

Head down the hallway past the creature who lunges from your right and head to where the caged hollows were. Kill them if they've respawned and head right down the hallway they were protecting. In the next room head up the stairs and proceed forward. Make a left down the hallway and then make your immediate right across the bridge. At the end of the bridge head left and take an immediate right and descend the stairs into the room. Use the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring") to open a gate to a cell, where you can find [Prisoner Chief's Ashes](/Prisoner+Chief's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner Chief's Ashes"). Exit the cell and head right and make a left into the next room and an immediate right to go back over the bridge. Go left down the hallway and drop down near the end and proceed through the opened grated door. Follow this to the right and take the lift down. Exit the left and head left past the petrified dragon person and take the winding stairs down to come to the room where several jailers lurked. Go straight to the second part of this room and head right to go through the hallway inside this room. There will be a jailer in this hallway. At the end of the hall, you can open the cell on the left with the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring") and speak to [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla"), freeing her.

### Boss Fight: Yhorm the Giant 

The boss fog to Yhorm can be accessed very early on, from the Profaned Capital bonfire. If at the bonfire, step just outside and take the ladder down and turn around to cross a crumbling bridge. You can drop down to the right for a small shortcut. If you stay on the bridge, a garygole will descend to attack you. Past him is a corpse with [Onislayer Greatarrow](/Onislayer+Greatarrow "Dark Souls 3 Onislayer Greatarrow") 8x. Cross the bridge to the end and loot a corpse for [Large Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Large+Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Weary Warrior") 1x. Exit the doorway and take the stairs down into the next room where you will encounter 4 jailers wearing white as well as a fire casting gargoyle. Proceed forward and make a right to find more jailers around a large vessel holding a fire. Defeat them and head down the stairs. To the right, in front of a pillar you will find a corpse with [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") 1x. Continue to the end of this large walkway to find another corpse with [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") 1x. 

Head back up the stairs and exit this chamber and make a right to head into another room with many jailers and a gargoyle who lurks up and to the right. There are two chests side by side in here, both are Mimics. Defeat them to obtain [Greatshield of Glory](/Greatshield+of+Glory "Dark Souls 3 Greatshield of Glory") and [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin") 2x. There is also a solitary legitimate chest here which can be looted for an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x. Leave the room and make a right to reach the fog, and beyond the boss [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant"). Defeat him to earn [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord") and [Soul of Yhorm the Giant](/Soul+of+Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Yhorm the Giant").

If this is the 3rd lord of cinder you defeated, a cutscene will play and you will be transported to the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").
