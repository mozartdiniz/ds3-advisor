# Great Heavy Soul Arrow

**Great Heavy Soul Arrow** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

## Great Heavy Soul Arrow  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 14  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Sorcery which improves upon Heavy Soul Arrow. Fires a more powerful heavy soul arrow.  
>   
> Sorceries are a logical discipline: their strength influenced by the caster's intelligence.

### 

### How to Get Great Heavy Soul Arrow in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 4,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Great Heavy Soul Arrow Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Great Heavy Soul Arrow inflicts (Spell Buff x 2.425) magic damage before resistances.
  * Its slow cast speed makes it best used for luring enemies in and killing foes before they can reach you.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Heavy Soul Arrow](/Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Soul Arrow").
