# Bountiful Sunlight

**Bountiful Sunlight** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast Miracles.

## Bountiful Sunlight  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**FP** | 70  
**Slots** | 2  
**Faith** | 35  
**Type** | Heal (Regeneration)  
**Duration** | 60 Seconds  
  
> Special Miracle granted by the Princess of Sunlight.  
>   
> Gradually restores a large amount of HP for self and those in the vicinity.  
>   
> The miracles of Gwynevere, loved as both mother and wife bestow their blessing on a great many warriors.

### How to Get Bountiful Sunlight in Dark Souls 3

  * Transposed from the [Soul of Rosaria](/Soul+of+Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Rosaria") after completing [Leonhard's](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard") questline.

### Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Sunlight Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * A miracle of Gwynevere, strongly implying that Rosaria is in someway linked to her.
  * Restores 10 HP per second over the course of its duration, for a total of 600 health.  

    * The health restored per FP balances out at about 8.57 HP/FP.
    * This makes this the most inefficient regeneration [Miracle](/Miracle "Dark Souls 3 Miracle") when used alone. However, its healing can be multiplied up to four times during co-operative play, something other regeneration Miracles (aside from [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Light")) can't replicate.
    * Given that this spell requires **2 Attunement Slots** in addition to the comparatively worse HP/FP efficiency, a Faith user may want to consider Bountiful Light as an alternative regen buff. 
  * Stacks with [Gentle Prayer](/Gentle+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Gentle Prayer")
  * Overwritten by [Replenishment](/Replenishment "Dark Souls 3 Replenishment") and [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Light").
  * Unaffected by Spell Buff, Faith, or Miracle boosting rings.
  * Lingering Dragoncrest Ring will increase the duration. However, the amount of HP restored will be the same, and the regen will simply be slowed down to compensate.
  * This is the only support-focused regeneration [Miracle](/Miracle "Dark Souls 3 Miracle") in the game.
  * Healing range is buffed by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
