# Lord of Cinder Mode

**Enkindled Mode** in Dark Souls 3 is required in order to become a "host" in multiplayer and summon other players. In order to activate this mode you must acquire and use an [Ember](/Ember). This state is also known as "_Lord of Cinder Mode_ " (from early translations of beta), "_Lord of Embers_ " (from early Namco's materials), "_Host of Embers_ " (from Namco's materials) or "_Embered_ " (community name) mode.

## Enkindled Mode

  * Maximum HP boosted 1.3x
  * Able to summon other players via soul sign
  * Icons next to gauges change
  * 'Cinder' effect on armor
  * Able to be invaded by other players (Only in areas where a boss is still alive)

  
  
**Note:** If you're summoned by another player while Enkindled, your maximum HP will go back to normal.
