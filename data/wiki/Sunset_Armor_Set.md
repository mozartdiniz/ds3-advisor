**Sunset Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> (Piece Name) of Hodrick, holy knight of the Sunless Realms.   
>   
> This light gold (Piece Name), named for its sunset hues, is now faded and wrapped in tainted rags.  
>   
> The sorry fool was known to wander the battlefield as a crazed ghoul, lashing out at friend and foe alike.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * To complete [Sirris'](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") questline kill [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick") at the Pit of Hollows in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). The set will be on his corpse. You may need to reload the area for set to appear.  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This armor bears a lot of resemblance to the [Undead Legion Set](/Undead+Legion+Set "Dark Souls 3 Undead Legion Set"), such as the conical helmet (although it is missing the mask and is tilted to the side), and the left pauldron. 
  * Worn by [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick").
