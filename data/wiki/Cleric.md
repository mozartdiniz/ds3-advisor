**Cleric** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") **that excels in both offensive and defensive support, making it ideal for players who want to wield miracles and use Faith-based builds. With the highest Faith of all the classes, the Cleric can cast powerful healing miracles like Heal and Force right from the start, offering additional healing beyond Estus Flasks. The Cleric’s weapon of choice, the Mace, is effective throughout the game, especially against armored enemies, making it a solid choice for players who enjoy a balance of magic and physical combat.

The Force miracle is particularly useful against human-sized enemies, as it can deplete stamina or knock them down, allowing for added damage opportunities. With a strong starting Faith and good Strength, the Cleric is an excellent choice for players focused on Faith-based builds, particularly Strength/Faith hybrids or Hollow/Faith combinations.

> A traveling cleric who collapsed from exhaustion. Channels high faith to cast many and varied miracles.

### Dark Souls 3 Cleric Notes and Tips

  * Has the highest Faith of all the classes and has a good Strength.
  * Comes equipped with the miracles Heal and Force. This makes this class along with the Herald the only two classes to have a miracle at the start, and have another healing source other than Estus Flasks early on.
  * The [Mace](/Mace "Dark Souls 3 Mace") is an effective weapon throughout the entire game and works especially well against armored opponents.
  * Note: The Force Miracle is exceptionally useful when dealing with human sized NPCs. With a single cast, much stamina can be drained if they attempt to block, or the NPC can be thrust onto the ground. This allows for some damage to be dealt before they recover

### Builds That Use Cleric in DS3

  * STR/FTH builds.
  * FTH builds.
  * Hollow/FTH builds
