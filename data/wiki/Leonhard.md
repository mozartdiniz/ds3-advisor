**Ringfinger Leonhard** is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")****. Leonhard** is a member of Rosaria's Fingers and introduces the PvP mechanic to the player. Born into royalty, he mastered both sorcery and swordsmanship. He initially set out on a quest to cure his scarred body, but upon meeting Rosaria, he chose to abandon his original quest and instead took a vow to serve her. He also gives and teaches you the Applause gesture and can be distracted using Alluring Skulls during an invasion.

### Leonhard Dark Souls 3 Information

  * Introduces players to PvP
  * Quest interacts with [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth")
  * Gives you the [Lift Chamber Key](/Lift+Chamber+Key "Dark Souls 3 Lift Chamber Key")
  * Gives Applause [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  * Can be distracted using [Alluring Skulls](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull") during invasion.

### Leonhard's Dark Souls 3 Questline

  1. **Start** : You will first meet Leonhard after reaching the second bonfire at the [High Wall of Lothric.](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") Return to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to find Leonhard leaning against Holy King Lothric's throne. Speaking to him will grant you five [Cracked Red Eye Orbs](/Cracked+Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Cracked Red Eye Orb").
  2. After obtaining 1 [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue"), and lighting the Cliff Underside bonfire or defeating the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood") in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), Leonhard will be back in Firelink and give you the [Lift Chamber Key](/Lift+Chamber+Key "Dark Souls 3 Lift Chamber Key") to the locked door below the Tower on the Wall bonfire. (Note: [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") must still be in the inventory at this encounter, if you use it, he will not appear)
  3. At the Tower on the Wall bonfire head down two levels and open the locked gate. Take the lift down and defeat the [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") to obtain the [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb"). Then return to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and speak to Leonhard again. Doing so will grant the player the Applause [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  4. Once you reach the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), find [Rosaria´s Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") in Rosaria's Chamber at the second floor of the Cathedral of the Deep and offer at least 1 [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") to [Rosaria](/Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria") (Do NOT rank up in [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") until the next event happens)
  5. Speak to Leonhard at Rosaria's Chamber (over by the entrance to the chamber) and exhaust his dialogue.
  6. After reaching the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital") bonfire, return to Rosaria's Chamber and pick up the Black Eye Orb in front of Rosaria's corpse.
  7. Go to Gwynevere's bed chamber, above the Aldrich, Devourer of Gods bonfire. Here you will receive a prompt to use the [Black Eye Orb](/Black+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Black Eye Orb") to invade Leonhard.
  8. **End** : Kill him here to obtain his hat ([Silver Mask](/silver+mask "Dark Souls 3 silver mask")), [Soul of Rosaria](/Soul+of+Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Rosaria") (use to revive her or to purchase [Bountiful Sunlight](/Bountiful+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Sunlight") miracle from [Ludleth](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") at Firelink Shrine), and the [Crescent Moon Sword](/Crescent+Moon+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Crescent Moon Sword").  
  

**Tips**

  1. This quest can be easily and quickly completed at the end of the game if the player chooses to complete [Sirris](/Sirris "Dark Souls 3 Sirris")' questline first. If the initial stages of receiving his [Cracked Red Eye Orb](/Cracked+Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Cracked Red Eye Orb")s and the [Lift Chamber Key](/Lift+Chamber+Key "Dark Souls 3 Lift Chamber Key") have not been completed, perform these by talking to Leonhard in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Once he tells you to join [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers"), go to Rosaria's Bed Chamber and offer [Rosaria](/Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria") a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue"). Leonhard will then appear at the entrance and you must talk to him and exhaust his dialogue. Then use a [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") or [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment") to return to the last bonfire rested at (Bringing you to same place). At this point, [Rosaria](/Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria") will be dead and you can loot the [Black Eye Orb](/Black+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Black Eye Orb") from her corpse. Now travel to the "Aldrich, Devourer of Gods" bonfire and use the elevator to go up to Gwynevere's bed chamber. Here you can use the [Black Eye Orb](/Black+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Black Eye Orb") to invade Leonhard and kill him. By this process you will get the [Silver Mask](/Silver+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Silver Mask"), [Crescent Moon Sword](/Crescent+Moon+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Crescent Moon Sword") and [Rosaria's Soul](/Rosaria's+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Soul") immediately.
  2. Equipping the [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") covenant item or turning in any number of Pale Tongues will halt you from progressing [Sirris](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms")' questline, although you can finish his questline at any point after you do Sirris' questline. Simply asking to join and obtaining the [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") will not halt the progress of [Sirris](/Sirris "Dark Souls 3 Sirris")' questline, nor will reallocating attributes or changing appearance.
  3. If you kill Leonhard before you have killed the [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") locked in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), the [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb") will be replaced with a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue"). It is recomended to wait until after you have defeated the [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") before you attempt to kill him.
  4. You can still use every one of [Rosaria](/Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria")'s services without returning her Soul, so it is recommended not to return it.

### Dark Souls 3 Leonhard Trivia

  * In the same vein as the Patches line of characters, Leonhard's name is a reference to Leonhardt from  _Armored Core 4_. Ironically, Leonhard is a scoundrel, whereas Leonhardt is virtuous and noble.
  * Very similar to Knight Lautrec of Carim from Dark Souls, with a shady personality, weapon set and even a quest with a Black Eye Orb.
  * Voiced by Edward Bennett.
  * Fact: Changes weapon during quest line. Doesn't have Crescent Moon Sword in Firelink Shrine.
  * Leonhard's attire appears to resemble [The Hunters](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/The+Hunter "Dark Souls 3 The Hunter") of [Bloodborne](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Bloodborne+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Bloodborne Wiki").
  * Warning! If you offer a Pale Tongue to Rosaria, triggering the start of Leonhard's line; It can lockout Sirris content. Depending on what point you're at in the Sirris quest; You can miss the Silvercat Ring, a free Blessed Mail Breaker, Sunless Talisman and some of Sunset gear. Dragonslayer Axe and Mirrah Chain set as well, via trigger that stems from helping her. It's best to complete helping Sirris before triggering Leonhard's content.
