**Unbreakable Patches** is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. **Unbreakable Patches** is a recurring character in the Souls series and also serves as a merchant in Dark Souls 3. Known for his deceitful nature, Patches wields a Winged Spear along with the Twin Dragon Greatshield. True to his character from previous games, Patches will try to deceive and trap the player, often in the hope of killing and looting them.

### Unbreakable Patches Dark Souls 3 Information

Unbreakable Patches can be found within the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") disguised as [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina"). He will spawn once you open the main doors to the cathedral (up the elevator to the left of the boss room), reload the area, and then return, if you've spoken to Siegward after killing the [Fire Demon](/Fire+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Fire Demon") in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). He will trick you into crossing the bridge which he will then lower down to the [giants](/Giant+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Giant Slave"). If you meet him again on the other side of that bridge down the stairs from Rosaria's bed chamber, he will apologize for tricking you, and if you refuse to forgive him you'll earn the [prostration gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") and a rusted coin. He will then become a vendor in that spot until he moves to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). 

  * If you light the Rosaria's Bed Chamber bonfire before he tricks you into crossing the bridge, he will leave the Cathedral Only cross the rafters to the bed chamber _after_ you have beaten the [Fire Demon](/Fire+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Fire Demon") with Siegward in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") and been tricked to cross the bridge in Cathedral of the Deep. After dropping down, he will reappear in his normal attire by the lever that raises the bridge (across from where you first met him in the [Catarina armor](/Catarina+Set "Dark Souls 3 Catarina Set")).
  * If Patches lowers you down to the giants in the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), he will not show up to lock you into the Firelink Shrine tower until you talk to him again on the other side of the bridge in the Cathedral.
  * If you kill the giants in the Cathedral of the Deep before talking to Patches by the bridge, he will be shocked that the giants are gone when he lowers you back down to the cathedral floor. Regardless he will still show up on the other side of the walkway by Rosaria's bedchamber.

An alternative method of meeting him (if you first lighted the [Rosaria's Bedchamber](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") bonfire, before opening the main gate) is to buy the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key") and access the lift above the Firelink Shrine. He will trap you here (after you open the main gate). After escaping his trap he becomes a merchant in the upper floor of Firelink Shrine. Chose to not forgive him for a [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin") and to learn the [gesture Prostration](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). After reloading the area he will be squatting down. Talk to him to learn the [Patches Squat gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). 

### Unbreakable Patches DS3 Patches Questline

Patches can have an important effect on the questlines of NPCs [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") and [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement").

  1. He has [Siegward's Armor](/Catarina+Set "Dark Souls 3 Catarina Set") equipped after trapping [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") and stealing it from him. This armor is needed to save Siegward in the well outside the Cathedral of the Deep. Opening the Cathedral doors is not the trigger for Patches to have the armor, you need to actually reload the Cathedral area. You do not need to talk to Siegward in the well first.You can buy the armor from Patches or just kill him for it.
  2. When [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") leaves on his second scavenger mission Patches will refuse to sell you any items until you tell him where Greirat has gone. If you have not yet bought the Catarina armor off of him and tell him where Greirat has gone he will save him. After killing [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn"), Patches ceases to pester you for Greirat's location. If you have bought the armor off of him and given it to Siegward in the well, Siegward will instead rescue him.
  3. After Greirat has left to scavenge in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"), Patches will ask where he has gone. If you tell him, he will stop selling you items and will leave Firelink Shrine until you defeat a boss. He returns with a [Hidden Blessing](/Hidden+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Blessing") which he sells for 16,000 souls.

**Notes:**

  * If Siegward dies before or during the [fire demon fight](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), Patches will not show up for the rest of the game cycle.
  * Patches has a number of triggers than cause him to appear in a given area. 
    * He will appear in the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") after its front door is opened and you travel away and return to the area.
    * If the Deacons of the Deep are killed or you take the shortcut to the [Rosaria's Bedchamber](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") bonfire, Patches will disappear from the cathedral. He will next appear if you go up the bell tower in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and locks you in.
    * Opening the front door of the Cathedral of the Deep and then going up the bell tower in Firelink Shrine will trigger Patches' event early.
  * If you give Siegward his armor before you meet Patches if you kept it from a previous playthrough he will not spawn in the Cathedral and will spawn in the tower in Firelink Shrine.
  * If you can't find Patches at all, save Siegward and then talk to Siegward inside the well outside the Cleansing Chapel bonfire. This triggers Patches story to continue. 
  * If you have missed Patches triggers by going to far and can't get him to show up in Cathedral of the Deep, you can go back to Firelink and trigger him there. When he shows up as a merchant in Firelink you can buy the Catarina Armor from him there to continue Siegward's quest line in the Chapel.
  * His dialogue at Cathedral of the Deep and the bell tower at Firelink Shrine varies based on your character's gender.
  * If you talk to Patches in the Cathedral and then proceed to the ceiling rafters to the drop off point near Rosaria, the event will no longer occur upon crossing the narrow pathway,and he will not be present till the Firelink event.
  * Attacking Greirat will aggro Patches.
  * If you tell Patches where Greirat has gone after you send him to Lothric Castle, after defeating every boss in the game, you will have no way of getting Patches to return to Firelink. Unfortunately, even if you have _[The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City")_ DLC installed and you reinstate [Judicator Argo](/Judicator+Argo "Dark Souls 3 Judicator Argo") to reset the [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church") boss fight, defeating this boss will **not** make Patches come back.

### Trivia

  * The name "Unbreakable Patches" references his reoccurring appearance in every Miyazaki directed Souls game (Bloodborne included.) In every instance Patches tricks the player into an unfortunate situation only to beg for forgiveness when you outsmart him. Previous names included "Patches the Hyena," "Trusty Patches," and "Patches the Spider."
  * When Patches takes residence in the Firelink Shrine he mentions that Greirat did him a good turn in Lothric Dungeon.
  * Patches will not sell the 'Horsehoof Ring' unless you have met the requirements **and** spoken to him at least once
  * Although Patches' line when he traps you in the Cathedral is listed as differing based on your gender (referring to the player either as a greedy guts or insatiable wench), and the subtitles are for the dialogue reflective of that gender, Patches will refer to you as a Greedy Guts in the vocal line regardless.
