# Seek Guidance

**Seek Guidance** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Miracle, you must use a Chime or Special Weapons that can cast Miracles.

  

## Seek Guidance  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 15  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 12 Faith  
**Type** | Support  
  
> Miracle of stray souls. Displays more help from other worlds and reveals summon signs without using an ember.  
>   
> Faith serves as a guide for clerics, meaning they should have no need for secondhand wisdom.  
>   
> Be that as it may, this miracle has been passed down from soul to soul, providing a tiny ray of hope for the lost.

### How to Get Seek Guidance in Dark Souls 3

  * Looted from a corpse in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"). Just past the first giant and down the stairs to the left. It's a sort of dark ish room above the Cathedral Knight with the mace ([Fextra Video location](https://youtu.be/4LENHGGrMbk "Fextra Video location")).
  * [Seek Guidance Location](https://youtu.be/b1PG_vH6fd0)

### Dark Souls 3 Seek Guidance Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Allows you to see summoning signs, but you cannot summon [White Soapstone](/White+Soapstone "Dark Souls 3 White Soapstone") phantoms unless you are in Ember Form.
  * You can see more summoning signs and messages with this active.
  * Even when playing in Offline mode reveals several messages left by the developers that are hidden until it's used.
  * This spell has the same effect as the [Guiding Light](/Guiding+Light "Dark Souls 3 Guiding Light") [Weapon Art](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills").
