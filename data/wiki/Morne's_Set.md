**Morne's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Unusual helm bestowed upon knights of Carim. (Helmet.)
> 
> Black (armor/gauntlets/leggings) bestowed upon knights of Carim.
> 
> Modeled on Morne, the Archbishop's apostle, cast from a unique mineral resembling stone.
> 
> A Carim knight will dedicate an entire career to attending a single maiden, just as Morne once served one goddess alone.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Bought from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 23,000 souls after either killing [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") or defeating [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour").

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The set can be obtained early by getting Eygon to fall of the cliff.
  * Alternatively, lure the two [Undead Manservants](/Jar+Wielding+Undead "Dark Souls 3 Jar Wielding Undead") from the previous section past the bridge over the ravine and bait attacks from them to hit him, killing him without incurring Sin.
