**Cornyx's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of pyromancers of the Great Swamp, particularly favored by old sages.  
> The large blindfold blocks out unnecessary light, allowing one to observe a pyromancy's true essence.  
> The flame reveals all, and obscures all. (Helm)  
>   
> Attire of Cornyx, pyromancer of the Great Swamp.  
>   
> In the Great Swamp, it was customary to adorn oneself with articles of nature. Cornyx favored the use of raven feathers.  
> Ravens are said to have once been Firelink messengers, guiding the undead to the land of ancient gods. (Chest)  
>   
> The bronze ornamentation serves to ward off evil spirits. (Gauntlets)  
>   
> This long skirt emulates those worn by witches, who were the forebearers of pyromancy. (Skirt)

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): Summon [Great Swamp Cuculus](/Great+Swamp+Cuculus "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Cuculus") (right outside Old Demon King fog) in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") and defeat the [Old Demon King](/Old+Demon+King "Dark Souls 3 Old Demon King"), if he survives the fight you will find this set and the [Spotted Whip](/Spotted+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Spotted Whip") at the cage where you rescued [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").
  * Dropped by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") when killed.
  * The [Helm](/Helms "Dark Souls 3 Helms") to this set is the [Old Sage's Blindfold](/Old+Sage's+Blindfold "Dark Souls 3 Old Sage's Blindfold") which is in the [Catacombs](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") with the [Witch's Ring](/Witch's+ring "Dark Souls 3 Witch's ring") ([video location](https://youtu.be/knzjANGcim4 "video location"))
