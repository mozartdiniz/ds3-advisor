**Evangelist Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  

> Robe of an evangelist sent from the cathedral. These teachers, all women, came to enlighten inhabitants of the Undead Settlement and sent carriers on the path of sacrifice.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Evangelists](/Cathedral+Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Evangelist") in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), and [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").

### Notes and Tips

  * Equipping the chest piece for this set appears to alter the stance of your arms to be wider.
  * Can be easily farmed in NG : From the Cathedral of the Deep bonfire, take the bridge on the right. There is an Evangelist here who can drop the entire set. If you're fast enough to kill it, you can go back to the bonfire before aggroing the two Hollow Manservants.
  * There appears to be a coiled-up [Notched Whip](/Notched+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Notched Whip") hanging off of the belt of the chest piece, along with several other interesting "tools".

### Lore

  * The Cathedral Evangelists also bear a striking resemblance to the [Fat Officials](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Fat+Official "Dark Souls 3 Fat Official") from [Demon's Souls](https://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Demons+Souls+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Demons Souls Wiki").
