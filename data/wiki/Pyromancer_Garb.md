**Pyromancer Garb** is a [Chest Armor](/Chest+Armor "Chest Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Pyromancer Set](/Pyromancer+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Attire of pyromancers of the Great Swamp. Comprised of bronze ornamentation and animal pelt.  
>   
> In the Great Swamp, bronze was used ritualistically to ward off evil spirits and keep darkness at bay.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer)
  * Sold by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp) for 800 Souls or he drops it if killed.
