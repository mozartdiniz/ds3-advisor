# Talismans

**Talismans** are wielded in order to cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles"). These items were not present in Dark Souls 2, but make a return here. The most commonly found [Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") among them, [Unfaltering Prayer](/Unfaltering+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Unfaltering Prayer"), differentiates them from [Chimes](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") and turns them towards PvP use. Not meeting the [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") requirement for the Talisman will drop the spell buff to a flat 60.
