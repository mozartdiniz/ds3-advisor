# Shriving Stone

**Shriving Stone** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials) in Dark Souls 3.

### Shriving Stone  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Also known as stark stone.  
>   
> Reverses weapon infusion.  
>   
> Has the benefit of undoing the effects of infusion without reducing the reinforcement level.  
  
### Shriving Stone Usage

  * Reverses weapon infusion

### Shriving Stone Locations

  * 1x Found at [Road of Sacrifices,](/Road+of+Sacrifices) down a path to the left, near where you first enter the area (Watch the [Item Location Video Guide](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=3h24m37s))
  * 1x Found at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"): On the beams on the ceiling of the dark room with invisible hollow creatures. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=55m25s "Video")) This room is reached by heading left from the Church of Yorshka Bonfire, going towards the swamp/dungeons. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=51m57s "Video"))
  * Drops from the [Corvians](/Corvian "Corvians") that you encounter at the start of the Road of Sacrifice and in Farron Keep beyond the poison swamp.
  * 1x Found in the toxic swamp of [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Profaned Capital"), right next to the chapel entrance.

### Notes

  * The previous infused gem is also lost in the process, but the titanite reinforcement level is unaltered.
  * Changing an infusion to another type does not require a Shriving Stone, it is only needed to remove an infusion.
  * Can be dropped in [Picklepum the Crow's](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow) nest to receive the [I'm sorry Carving](/I'm+sorry+Carving) item.
