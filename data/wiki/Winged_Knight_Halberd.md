Winged Knight Halberd is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Halberd wielded by the Winged Knights, who swore themselves to the Angels.  
>   
> The thick, heavy, bloodstained blade can only be swung by one with inhuman strength.  
>   
> Skill: Chain Spin  
> Sweep foes in a large spinning motion and use strong attack to continue the motion until stamina is exhausted.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Heavy infusion with 66 Strength yields higher AR (**575** AR two-handed, **531** AR one-handed) than Refined infusion with 40 Strength and 40 Dexterity (**542** AR two-handed, **512** AR one-handed). Despite this, it is still the highest AR halberd on a 40/40 build when one-handed, and the second highest behind only Heavy [Splitleaf Greatsword](/Splitleaf+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Splitleaf Greatsword") when two-handed.
  * While Heavy-infused at 66 strength, this is the second highest unsplit AR for the halberd class (the highest being the Splitleaf Greatsword).
  * Despite being called a halberd, this weapon more closely resembles a bardiche.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Winged Knights](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight") wielding this weapon (uncommon)
  * To farm, head backwards from the Dancer of the Boreal Valley bonfire and up the stairs on the right to the winged knight patrolling around a pile of burning bodies.
  * This can alternatively be done by going upstairs and to the left from the Vordt of the Boreal Valley bonfire.
  * [Fextra Video ](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=1h2m4s "Fextra Video ")

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
