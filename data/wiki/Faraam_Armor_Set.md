**Faraam Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> This set is named after a god of war.
> 
> The armor of the Forossa Lion Knights was preserved even after the destruction of their homeland, and is mentioned in numerous legends, alongside the names of those who are said to have gone beyond death.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 18000 souls after defeating [Lion Knight Albert](/Lion+Knight+Albert "Dark Souls 3 Lion Knight Albert") (one of the trio of hostile NPCs) in [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")

###  Trivia

  * This armour set was introduced into the Souls series by Dark Souls 2, being the official armour set of Bearer of the Curse
  * The ending line of the armor's description, "...gone beyond death," is a reference to the tag line used for advertising Dark Souls 2; "Go beyond death"
  * There is a small amount of evidence pointing to the possibility that Faraam is the true identity of the [Nameless King](/Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Nameless King").
