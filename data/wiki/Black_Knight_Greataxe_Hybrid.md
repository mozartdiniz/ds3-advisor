## Build Strategy

If your Enemy has a Fast Weapon and use the Drakeblood Greatsword to Punish rolling or running Attacks, if he's Spamming try to Parry him.

If your Enemy has a Slow Weapon use the BKGA and try to bait him into a Parry.
