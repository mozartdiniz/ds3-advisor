**Sellsword Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> _Metal (Piece Name) interwoven with coarse cloth. Able to endure the hardships of battle and prolonged travel._
> 
> _It is light considering the build, striking a fine balance between absorption and substance._  
>   
> _Frequent shield users lighten the load by wearing a gauntlet on only one arm._  
>   
> _These cloth trousers and leather boots are of no real note, but are durable and allow for easy movement._

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear of the [Mercenary](/Mercenary "Dark Souls 3 Mercenary")
  * [Road of Sacrifice](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") in the area with the [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight") and [Farron Coal](/Farron+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Farron Coal")
  * [Fextra Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=1h5m39s "Fextra Video location") (starts at 1h 5m 39s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * You can also get the sellsword twin blades in this area. Underneath the armor set location, on same level to the right of the black knight.
  * The Sellsword Set boasts fairly balanced protection against both physical and elemental damage. On top of this, its durability is some of the highest in the game. For these reasons, it's a good idea to wear this when one is farming for souls and items, when there is a long trip from one bonfire to the next.
  * This set can be found on a couple of corpses in Irithyll Dungeon near where you do the Path of the Dragon emote.
  * Aside from the helmet, the Sellsword Set bears resemblance to the armor worn by the character Guts in the "Golden Age Arc" of Berserk.
