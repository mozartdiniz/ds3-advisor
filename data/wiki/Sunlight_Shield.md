**Sunlight Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> A large, circular shield made of metal and decorated with a holy symbol depicting the sun.
> 
> This shield was used by the old Knight of Sunlight, and the illustration is his own. Regardless, it has no particular divine powers.

### Sunlight Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Provides an average block of 58.8%.
  * This shield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Sunlight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Shield")_.
  * The old Knight of Sunlight is none other than the righteous [Solaire of Astora](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Solaire+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Solaire of Astora").

### Location/Where to Find

  * Trade [Pickle Pee the Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") a [Mendicant's Staff](/Mendicant's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Mendicant's Staff").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
