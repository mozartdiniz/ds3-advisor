# Lift Chamber Key

**Lift Chamber Key** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Lift Chamber Key  
  
---  
  

> Key to the room of the lift that descends to the deepest dungeon in Lothric. A surviving Darkwraith from the land swallowed by darkness, is said to be there.  
  
### Lift Chamber Key Usage

  * Unlocks an elevator to a [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith) mini-boss just below The Tower on the Wall bonfire in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric). It drops the [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb).

### Lift Chamber Key Locations

  * [Leonhard](/Leonhart) gives you the key.
  * After obtaining 1 [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue), and lighting the Cliff Underside bonfire or defeating the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood) in the Undead Settlement, he will give you the Lift Chamber Key to the locked door below the Tower on the High Wall bonfire.
