**Ringed City Hollow** is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki"). He is part of the [the Ringed City DLC](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City").

## Information

  * Killable
  * First NPC you meet in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)").
  * Informs you of [Filianore](/Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Filianore") and the Dark Soul.

### Location

  * Just before the **Mausoleum lookout** bonfire.
  * Once Filianore is awakened, he will disappear.

### Lore Theories

  * Many believe that he is a pygmy, as he has the same skin color, posture, and voice actor as the pygmy king, being voiced by Ramon Tikaram.
  * Likewise, some theorize that he is an outcast of the Ringed City, given his location, outside the heavily defended city entrance.
  * As a pygmy that despises the gods, some theorize that the Ringed City Hollow may in fact be the Furtive Pygmy himself, however, there is little additional information to corroborate this.
  * The information The Ringed City Hollow actually provides that is not his opinions meant to persuade the player is primarily information identical to the information Shira and Judicator Argo provide regarding Princess Filianore and The Dark Soul. 
  * The exception is his dying statement, which implies that the curse of the Undead in fact originates with the Abyss, or otherwise that there is another curse which stems from the Abyss.
