# Straight Swords

**Straight Swords** are a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") type in Dark Souls 3. Straight Swords are the most basic type of weapon, but have solid reach and damage, fast attack speed and the Skill Stance, along with other versatile Weapon Arts. All these attributes make this weapon class a popular choice for beginners and experts and a good choice for both PvP and PvE.
