# Refined Gem

**Refined Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Refined Gem  
  
---  
  

> "A gem of infused titanite. Forged in the weapons of Lothric knights. Used in infusion to create refined weapons. Refined weapons are difficult to wield properly, and scale effectively with strength and dexterity."  
  
### Refined Gem Usage

  * Used in [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create Refined Weapons.
  * Refined weapons have a balanced, mid range, of [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength") and [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dark Souls 3 Dexterity") scaling.

### Refined Gem Locations

  * x1 guaranteed drop per NG from the Red Eyed Blue Knight to the left of the entrance to [Emma](/Emma "Dark Souls 3 Emma") at her church in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"). Afterwards drop rate decreased greatly but is still farmable here ([Item Location Video Guide](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=1h25m28s)).
  * 1x on a Corpse, in the midst of the blaze, beneath the two dragons who spew fire in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"), near the Dragon Barracks bonfire.
  * 1x from a crystal lizard on the room in [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * 3x sold by the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after giving her the [Captain's Ashes](/Captain's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Captain's Ashes").
  * Rare drop from [Cathedral Grave Warden](/Cathedral+Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Grave Warden") in [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").
  * Rare drop from [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle").
  * Can drop from the Red Eyed Knight in [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). From the bonfire, go in the opposite way of the grand archives, you can find a tougher knight with a sword. 
  * Rare drop from the Red-Eyed [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") outside of the Twin Princes' bonfire. All four knights are almost guaranteed to drop [Large Titanite Shards](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard"), have a chance to drop [Embers](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember"), [Titanite Chunks](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") and rarely [Lightning Gems](/Lightning+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Gem"), and also yield a high amount of souls (an especially high amount for NG), making this an ideal grinding spot. An easy way to farm these knights is to sneak up on them and use [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport"). The [Slumbering Draconcrest Ring](/Slumbering+Draconcrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Draconcrest Ring") can help with this.

### Videos:

  * [Refined Gem Farming Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TN6kd8mkP-I "Refined Gem Farming Guide")

### Notes

  * Refined weapons are often the best Infusion choice for high level characters who have invested heavily in both strength and dexterity. 
  * Most weapons have a scaling leaned to one stat (either str or dex). Compare attack rating loss and gains on different infusions before applying them.
  * No weapon in the game using a refined gem will achieve above B scaling, keep this in mind when choosing a weapon as another gem might benefit the weapon more.

### Trivia

  * Refined weapons are similar to the Quality upgrade in Demon's Souls.
