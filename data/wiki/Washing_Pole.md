**Washing Pole** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This is considered one of the longest katanas in the game. One of its main strengths is its high base damage, bleed build-up, and dexterity scaling. Use this weapon for its long reach and effectiveness against groups of enemies in open areas. Its length and damage make it ideal for controlling space and dealing with multiple foes at once.  
  
  

> A stunning, unusually long katana forged in an eastern land.   
>   
> The extreme length of this blade provides immense range, but also renders the blade extremely fragile.  
>   
> Skill: Hold  
> Assume a holding stance to rapidly execute a lunging slash with normal attack, or a deflective parry with strong attack.

  
  

### DS3 Washing Pole Notes and Tips

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * The longest katana in the game, albeit with a higher stamina cost for attacks, and is incapable of 2-hit light attack combos in PvP. 
  * 18 STR and 60 DEX nets a Sharp Washing Pole 420 AR(nice), making the third best Katana in terms of physical damage. Second place taken by [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Black Blade") (430 AR), and first place being the [Chaos Blade](/Chaos+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Blade") (444 AR).
  * It is named after the weapon of famous swordsman Sasaki Kojiro, who used a larger-than-normal odachi called "Laundry/Drying/Washing Pole". Kojiro was reported to be extremely fast and precise, which may explain why the weapon art (silly fast!) resembles the "Swallow Cut", which Kojiro was famous for using with his 'Laundry Pole'

### Location of Washing pole in DS3

  * Sold by the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after giving her the [Easterner's Ashes](/Easterner's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Easterner's Ashes") for 7,500 souls.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
