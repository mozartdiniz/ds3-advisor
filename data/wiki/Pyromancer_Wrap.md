**Pyromancer Wrap** are [Gauntlets](/Gauntlets "Gauntlets") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Pyromancer Set](/Pyromancer+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Attire of pyromancers of the Great Swamp. Leather wrapped plainly around the arms.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer)
  * Sold by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp) for 500 Souls or he drops it if killed.
