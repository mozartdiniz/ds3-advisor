# Boreal Outrider Knight

## Boreal Outrider Knight  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Humanoid  
**Weakness** | Dark  
**Resistances** | Frost, Fire, Magic, Physical-type  
**Immune** | Toxic/Poison  
  
**Boreal Outrider Knight** is an [enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3.**

### Boreal Outrider Knight Enemy Description

  * A powerful knight that serves as a mini-boss.
  * One can be found before the first [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") bonfire. Just take the elevator all the way down. This one drops the [Irithyll Straight Sword](/Irithyll+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Straight Sword"). Tip: Run past it, get the Road of Sacrifices bonfire, then simply "cheese" it by attacking it from outside the double doors -- it will be unable to pass through them.
  * Another one can be found near the two [Wyverns](/Lothric+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Wyvern") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"), down the stairs in the left building. This one drops the [Irithyll Rapier](/Irithyll+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Rapier").
  * The last one is found past an illusory wall in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). This one drops the [Outrider Knight Set](/Outrider+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Outrider Knight Set").

### Boreal Outrider Knight Combat Information

  * Timing a roll to end up at his side or back, followed by a combo is proven effective.
  * Attacks deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") or [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"). May also inflict [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Can be [parried](/Parry "Dark Souls 3 Parry") and riposted.
  * This [enemy](/enemies "Dark Souls 3 enemies") cannot be charmed by [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport").

### Boreal Outrider Knight Notes & Trivia

  * The Black Fire Orb on a 20-20 INT-FTH build casted on a +9 Pyro Flame, will do 500 damage should you wear both Swamp and Witch's rings +Lloyd's Sword Ring.
  * Outrider Knights have deformed over time, into dog-like [Sulyvahn's Beasts](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast"), [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Vordt of the Boreal Valley") and even the [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley")._\- "Every Outrider Knight one day devolves into a beast, constantly**hounded** by Pontiff Sulyvahn's black eyes."_
  * The Outrider Knight in Lothric Castle drops the [Irithyll Rapier](/Irithyll+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Rapier") regardless of wielding the [Irithyll Straight Sword](/Irithyll+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Straight Sword"), similar to the knight in the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices").
