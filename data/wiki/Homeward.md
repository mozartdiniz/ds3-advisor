# Homeward

**Homeward** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Talisman"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracle](/Miracle "Dark Souls 3 Miracle").

## Homeward  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
****Requirments**** | 18 Faith  
  
  
> Miracle taught to traveling clerics. Returns caster to the last bonfire used for resting, or to the bonfire in the shrine.  
>   
> It would normally link to one's homeland, only the curse of the Undead has twisted its power, redirecting casters to a bonfire. Perhaps for the Undead, the bonfire serves as home.

### How to Get Homeward in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 3,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Homeward Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Same effect as the [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") and [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment").
  * Like with Homeward Bones, it cannot be used when invaded, have any number of phantoms on your world, or during an invasion.
  * Since it is a spell, cast speed also affects its animation. At max cast speed, it takes the same time as a normal Homeward Bone to use. 
    * This also makes it risky to cast in the middle of combat (as a means of escape), unless using a Talisman with [Unfaltering Prayer](/Unfaltering+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Unfaltering Prayer").
  * This spell can be useful if you eat through your homeword bones quickly, but otherwise the investment of 18 faith and a spell slot isn't worth it, especially since this spell will clutter your spell bar during combat.

  * You would need to cast this spell 6 times to outweigh the costs of the spell in Homeward Bones (since they cost 500 souls each).
