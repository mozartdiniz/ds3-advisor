**Black Knight Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of the Black Knights who roam the lands.
> 
> The knights served the First Lord Gwyn, and followed him into the flame upon its linking. They became ash, but still wander the realms to this day.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by the [Black Knights](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight"). One of the first Black Knights is near the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") Perimeter bonfire. He can drop the entire set, in addition to the [Black Knight Shield](/Black+Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Shield") and [Black Knight Greatsword](/Black+Knight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Greatsword"). 
  * Can be farmed in the area after [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr") in the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") as it contains 4 Black Knights and is directly adjacent to the bonfire.
  * Another [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight") is located in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") near the bonfire Demon Ruins.  

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Grants the third highest fire resistance for an armor set when all the pieces are worn, surpassed only by the [Black Iron Set](/Black+Iron+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Iron Set") and [Havel's Set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set").
