# Dead Again

**Dead Again** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman,](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

  

## Dead Again  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 45  
****Slots Used**** | 1  
****Requirements**** | 23 Faith/ 15 Intelligence  
****Type**** | AoE  
  
> Sacrilegious miracle of the Sable Church of Londor.  
>   
> Bless corpses, transforming them into traps.  
>   
> Londor, the Hollow Realm, is a society of undead, comprised of the corpses and shades of those who led unsavory lives. Is such blessing really something one must ponder?

### How to Get Dead Again in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") or [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 5,000 souls, after giving either the [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Dead Again Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Since damage is based directly off your faith stat (See Below), the talisman or chime used, and the spellbuff it has, is entirely irrelevant.
  * Corpses will explode after a short delay, and disappear after exploding. "Traps" is probably not the best word to describe it, as the corpses will explode after casting regardless of whether or not anything is actually in range.
  * Will not work unless character can interact with enemies in the world. For example, an invader will not be able to effectively cast this miracle on corpses unless the host has used a [Seed of a Giant Tree](/Seed+of+a+Giant+Tree "Dark Souls 3 Seed of a Giant Tree").
  * The spell will only affect up to 3 corpses at a time.
  * This spell first appeared in _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dead+Again "Dark Souls 3 Dead Again")_ as a hex.
  * damage dealt is entirely based off of your FTH stat, and is unchanged by your spell buff. see stats below.

**Damage statistics** :

(damage numbers are for before defense and absorption.)

  * 23-29 FTH: 350 damage per body.
  * 30-39 FTH: 450 damage per body.
  * 40-49 FTH: 550 damage per body.
  * 50-59 FTH: 650 damage per body.
  * 60-69 FTH: 710 damage per body.
  * 70-79 FTH: 770 damage per body.
  * 80-99 FTH: 830 damage per body.
