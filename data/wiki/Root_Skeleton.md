# Root Skeleton

## Root Skeleton  
  
---  
|  | **Location**  
148 | 50 | [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement)  
**Enemy Type** | Undead  
**Weakness** | Strike, Blessed   
**Resistances** | Standard, Slash, Thrust, Elemental-type Damage  
**Immune** | Status Effects  
  
**Root Skeleton** is an [Enemy](/Enemies) in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Enemy Description

  * A skeleton that can be found in the sewers of [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement") and in the tower with the Shared Grave bonfire (located within [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "The Ringed City")).
  * Deals physical damage.
  * Roots are attached to them.
  * Only one skeleton can be seen wearing a [Worker Hat](/Worker+Hat "Worker Hat")

###  Drops

  * [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Titanite Shard")

### Combat Information

  * Appears only in groups.
  * Deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage), [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage) or [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage).
  * Weak to [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage) and Blessed weapons.
  * Resistant to [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage), [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage), [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage) and Elemental-type Damage.
  * Immune to [Status Effects](/Status+Effects).
  * Immune to [Rapport](/Rapport).
  * Can't be lured.

### Notes & Trivia

  * In the Undead Settlement you can see many corpses with root overgrowth, possibly having something to do with the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Curse-Rotted Greatwood")
