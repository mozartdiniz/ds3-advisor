# Magic Clutch Ring

### Magic Clutch Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring depicting a hand grasping a blue stone. Increases magic attack, but compromises damage absorption.  
>   
> An old fable in Londor claims that the lure of the clutch ring reaches out to the crestfallen, who might otherwise be overcome by despair.  
  
**Magic Clutch Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring Effect

  * Increases magic attack by 15% in PvE and 8% in PvP

  * Reduces Physical, Strike, Slash, and Thrust Absorptions 10% in PvE and 15% in PvP

  * Weight: 0.8

### How to Get Magic Clutch Ring in Dark Souls 3

  * [Irythyl of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")\- From the Church of Yorshka bonfire, head out of the church the way you came in and head left towards the Pontiff Knights and Fire Witches. In this area is an [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls") on the right. It is behind the wall. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=39m27s "Video") / )

###  Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases the damage on magic weapons, buffs, magic spells, and crystal infusions.
  * Reduction in absorption is calculated by increasing your current absorption by 10% and then subtracting 10 (i.e. New = 1.1xOld- 10; calculated with excel)
  * Although dark sorceries are classified as sorceries, since they do dark damage instead of magic damage, they'll not gain any boost from this ring
