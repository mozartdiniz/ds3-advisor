# Toxic Mist

**Toxic Mist** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Toxic Mist  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 24  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 0 Intelligence/ 15 Faith  
  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Unique pyromancy of Eingyi that never gained currency in the Great Swamp.  
>   
> Creates an intense poison mist.  
>   
> Driven from the Great Swamp, they say that at the end of his journey, Eingyi happened upon a virulent poison and a young lady.

### How to Get Toxic Mist in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").

    * From the Old King's Antechamber bonfire, go through the illusionary wall on the far side of the room that drops down into the hall of fire orbs and pyromancer demons. Follow the far wall left to the end of the room; you should see a corridor with a rat in it.

    * You may follow the rat and drop into a lava pit to pick up the pyromancy, as well as the [White Hair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman"). Alternatively, take the first door on your right into a root-infested catacomb with several rats. One of the far walls is illusionary; grab the [Quelana Pyromancy Tome](/Quelana+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome") if you haven't already and the stairs down into the lava pit. Mind the lava slimes.

  * [Video location](http://youtu.be/hwRXfZ6Jl9c "Video location")

### Dark Souls 3 Toxic Mist Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Drink Estus instead of rolling a lot.
  * Blocking while entering lava blocks the damage of lava, but as soon as you lower your shield or take an item you'll stop blocking damage.
  * Use any combination of high fire resist armor, [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat") (+30%), [Red Bug Pellets](/Red+Bug+Pellet "Dark Souls 3 Red Bug Pellet") (+15%), [Flame Stoneplate Ring](/Flame+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Flame Stoneplate Ring") (+13%), [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring") (+5%) [Blue Tearstone Ring](/Blue+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Blue Tearstone Ring") (+20% at 20%).   
In the End you should get around 66% resistance (73% with [Blue Tearstone Ring](/Blue+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Blue Tearstone Ring")).
  * Note, that, the more you stack up resistance, the more "diminishing returns" hit, which means that resistance will fall more and more. So you don't really need [Havel's Armor Set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set").
  * Applies 12 Toxic buildup per tick in the cloud.
  * This is one of the few ways a player can inflict Toxic.
  * The Toxic Damage totals a fixed 1440 Damage at 24 damage per tick.
