# Burial Gifts

**Burial Gifts** make a return in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , continuing the tradition from previous Souls games. These optional items can be selected during Character Creation to give players a small advantage at the start of their journey. There are 9 different gifts to choose from, each offering unique benefits to help players in various ways. While these items can provide an early boost, all of them can also be found in the game world, meaning they only offer a temporary advantage for players who choose them.

A Burial Gift can only be selected when starting a new game (NG), and players cannot choose another when beginning subsequent playthroughs in New Game Plus (NG+). However, selecting a gift is entirely optional, and players may opt to start their adventure without one if they prefer a more challenging experience or simply wish to rely on what they find naturally as they progress.

### Choosing Gifts in Dark Souls 3

For PvE, the best starting items are the Life Ring, Fire Gem, or Sovereignless Soul.

  * The Life Ring provides a helpful health buffer, which is particularly useful in the early game, such as during the fight against Iudex Gundyr.
  * The Fire Gem is a great choice for those focusing on survival or magic stats. It offers solid damage early on without scaling, making it valuable until weapons with scaling stats become more viable. However, Fire Gems can also be obtained relatively early in the mid-game.
  * The Sovereignless Soul gives 2,000 souls, equivalent to a “[Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Nameless Soldier").” This allows for extra levels or purchases upon reaching Firelink Shrine, which can be more impactful than other starting items.

Other items like the Young White Branch, Divine Blessing, and Rusted Gold Coin have limited early-game usefulness. While the Young White Branch provides instant friendship with the [Giant Archer](/Giant+Archer "Dark Souls 3 Giant Archer") of the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), most players will find the Life Ring or [Sovereignless Soul](/Sovereignless+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Sovereignless Soul") more advantageous for progressing through the beginning stages of the game.

### Fire Gem compared to other Gifts in DS3

The Fire Gem is a Burial Gift that allows players to infuse their weapon with fire damage, providing a flat damage bonus that can be highly effective in the early game. Unlike standard weapon upgrades, fire-infused weapons deal consistent damage without relying on stat scaling, making it a strong option for players who want reliable damage output right from the start.
