# Boss Soul Weapons

**Boss Soul Weapons** in **Dark Souls 3** are obtained by [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition). You may also obtain [spells](/Magic) and [rings](/Rings) from them. To transpose a soul, defeat the respective boss, then take the soul to Ludleth of Courland. He sits on the mid-left throne (looking at Lothric's throne while standing at Firelink) This mechanic is only available after defeating the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotten+Greatwood "Curse-Rotten Greatwood"), and recieving the [Transposing Kiln](/Transposing+Kiln " Transposing Kiln") and giving it to Ludleth.
