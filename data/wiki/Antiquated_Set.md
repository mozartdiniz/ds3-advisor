**Antiquated Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3.**   
  
  
  
  

> Dress sewn in a long-lost fashion.
> 
> The elaborately embroidered, ivory-colored silk is imbued with ancient magic power.
> 
> No protection is offered by this garment, as it was never intended for battle.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * The dress, gloves and skirt are found at [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), in a chest in a small cave housing a deceased Elizabeth Mushroom. See the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") page for a map. The [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk") is found near the Keep Ruins bonfire in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"). See page for more detail.
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=1h19m34s "Video location") (fextralife playthrough video starting at 1h 19 m 34s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The Crown of Dusk is found by the White Birch Tree. The remaining pieces of the Antiquated Set are found in a cave near the last fire required to open the swamp door. This set presumably once belonged to Dusk of Oolacile.
  * Helm: Magic damage inflicted by spells increases by x1.09
