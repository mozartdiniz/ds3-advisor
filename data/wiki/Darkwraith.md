**Darkwraith** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

###  Darkwraith Enemy Description

  * Darkwraith Knights wear the [Dark Set](/Dark+Set "Dark Souls 3 Dark Set") armour.
  * Almost indistinguishable from the Darkwraiths of New Londo.

### Darkwraith Location

  * One can be found at the bottom of the lift under the Tower on the Wall bonfire in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") after following [Leonhard's](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard") questline. Referred as "Special".
  * Another one is located near the Farron Keep bonfire. Head left and follow the great walls to find it. It is guarding a Coal item.
  * Two of them are attacking the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") stronghold, near Farron Keep Perimeter bonfire.

### Darkwraith Combat Information

  * Health (Special/Regular): NG (692), NG+ (~1460), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+4 (??).
  * Uses [Dark Sword](/Dark+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sword") with very fast continuous swings and very high poise. Deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") or [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage").
  * Uses [Dark Hand ](/Dark+Hand "Dark Souls 3 Dark Hand") that serves as a magical shield which allow them a guardbreaking grab attack. Deals [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage").
  * Can be backstabbed, parried, and riposted.
  * Abyssal enemy, therefore weak to [Farron Greatsword](/Farron+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Greatsword") and [Wolf Knight's Greatsword](/Wolf+Knight's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Wolf Knight's Greatsword").
  * Neither weak nor resistant to [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage").
  * Resistant to most types of damages, [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed") and [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Despite their slim posture, they have very high poise.
  * Hitting them before they get hyper armor (they get this during attacks) with very heavy weapons can stun lock them.
  * Cannot be poisoned by the player. The [Ghrus](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt") who cast poison cloud may be able to inflict poison, needs confirmation.

### Darkwraith Trivia and Notes

  * Returning enemy from Dark Souls 1.
  * Darkwraiths will drop 2 Estus refills if they drop any.
  * After killing all the Ghru's in their path if they survive, they will patrol in front of the Abyss Watchers chamber. They can be easily killed because they won't enter the chamber leaving them open to safe attacks.
  * They will attack all other enemies in the area, In Farron Keep, they will attack Ghru's within their aggro radius, which a player can use to his/her advantage.
  * They leave their swords behind when they die.
  * The Special Darkwraith in the High Wall of Lothric does respawn, and can drop all regular Darkwraith items giving an early chance to score the Dark Sword.
