**Livid Pyromancer Dunnel** is an NPC enemy in Dark Souls 3.

## Livid Pyromancer Dunnel Information

  * Killable.
  * Invades in the colosseum above the Champion's Gravetender bonfire.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Pyromancer's Parting Flame](/Pyromancer's+Parting+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer's Parting Flame")

### Location

  * You can find Livid Pyromancer Dunnel at [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"). Take the illusory wall in the bug crypt out to drop through a path of roots. At the bottom, in the colosseum, he will invade you if you are embered.
  * [Fextralife Video: How to get to Livid Pyromancer Dunnel](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=5m46s "Fextralife Video: How to get to Livid Pyromancer Dunnel")
  * [Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5x_Xp2soJko)

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (5,000), NG+ (??), NG++ (11,000), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * Other: [Floating Chaos](/Floating+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Floating Chaos")

Combat Information

  * Wields [Chaos Blade](/Chaos+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Blade") (RH) and [Pyromancer's Parting Flame](/Pyromancer's+Parting+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer's Parting Flame") (LH)
  * Uses a variety of [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies"), including the AoE [Chaos Storm](/Chaos+Storm "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Storm").
  * Weak to Fire Damage.
