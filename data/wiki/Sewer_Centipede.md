**Sewer Centipede** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Sewer Centipede Enemy Description

  * Sewer Centipedes mimic the body of a drowned woman but they're actually spidery fiends.
  * Found in the sewer area of [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), a group of them can be found in the area leading up into the kitchen where you may find [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina"). Also found in [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"). There is also one in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"), behind a gate in the Corvian Settlement.
  * In the river at Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, several dead sewer centipedes give you a false sense of security in approaching the live ones.
  * The creature strongly resembles a house centipede.

### Sewer Centipede Combat Information

  * They use a mixture of melee attacks and toxic spitting.
  * Can be hit by critical strikes from the front if poise is broken.
  * Cannot be Lured.
  * Cannot be Rapported.
  * Will go on patrol mode if you approach them using hidden body + spook, but will not attack you.
