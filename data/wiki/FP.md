# FP

## FP  
  
---  
  
**FP** stands for **Focus Points** is a [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") and is used to cast magic and perform Skills in **Dark Souls 3**. It is the blue bar and stat on the player character's HUD and stat screen respectively. FP is not self regenerating so, in order to refill some of the FP bar, the player must consume an **[Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask")**. FP can only be increased by investing points into [Attunement](/Attunement "Dark Souls 3 Attunement") via Fire Keeper.

### Dark Souls 3 Items for FP

#### Items for FP Regeneration 

  * [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask")
  * [Hidden Blessing](/Hidden+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Blessing")
  * [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire")
  * [Simple Gem](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem") infusion on weapons
  * [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword")
  * [Handmaid's Dagger](/Handmaid's+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Handmaid's Dagger")

#### Items to Reduce FP Consumption

  * [Dusk Crown Ring](/Dusk+Crown+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring"): Reduces consumption of FP but also lowers HP.
  * [Farron Ring](/Farron+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Farron Ring"): Reduces Skill FP consumption.

### Dark Souls 3 FP Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * **T** he maximum amount of Focus Points is 450 at 99 Attunement.
  * The soft cap for FP is at 35 Attunement.
  * Patched with [regulation 1.08](/Patches "Dark Souls 3 Patches"): You can still perform Weapon Arts, even though you have very little FP left. **Does not apply to magic**.
  * Patched with [regulation 1.08](/Patches "Dark Souls 3 Patches"): Weapon Arts can still be performed with no FP, albeit significantly weaker.
