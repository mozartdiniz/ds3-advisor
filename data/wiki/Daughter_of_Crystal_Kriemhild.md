**Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild** is a [NPC](/Invading+NPC+Phantoms "Dark Souls 3 Invading NPC Phantoms") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild

  * Can be killed.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff")

### Daughter of Crystal Location

  * This NPC invades in [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves"), along the cliff path where the _Cemetary of Ash_ bonfire would normally be. 
    * You must be [embered](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember"), and [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr") must be alive, for her to invade.
  * You can also meet her as a hostile NPC, in the trio of Black Hand enemies at the bottom of the stairs that leads up to the Twin Princes boss, in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").

### Daughter of Crystal Drops

  * Souls (phantom): NG (7,543), NG+ (??), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * Souls (human): NG (12,000), NG+ (24,000), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff") (Human version).

### Daughter of Crystal Combat Information

  * Wields: [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff") and [Crystal Sage's Rapier](/Crystal+Sage's+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage's Rapier") (RH), [Parrying Dagger](/Parrying+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Parrying Dagger") (LH). She has equiped the Firekeeper Set.
  * Casts [Crystal Hail](/Crystal+Hail "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Hail"), [Crystal Soul Spear](/Crystal+Soul+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Soul Spear"), [Homing Crystal Soulmass](/Homing+Crystal+Soulmass "Dark Souls 3 Homing Crystal Soulmass").
  * Can parry and riposte player.
  * Her invading phantom can be killed easily by kicking her off the cliff.

### Daughter of Crystal Dialogue

None.

### Daughter of Crystal Trivia

  * Her name is derived from the Germanic elements grim "mask" and hild "battle". Kriemhild was a beautiful heroine in the Germanic saga the 'Nibelungenlied', where she is the sister of Gunther and the wife of Siegfried.
  * The name "Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild" can have two meanings: The daugther of the person known as Crystal Kriemhild, or as Kriemhild - Daughter of Crystal, which is hinted at by the description of the staff she drops. Daughter can also mean apprentice or follower etc.
  * It is unclear why she wears the firekeeper set, but she may have at some point been a firekeeper.
