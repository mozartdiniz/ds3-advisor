# Soul of the Rotted Greatwood

**Soul of the Rotted Greatwood** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Rotted Greatwood  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Curse-rotted Greatwood. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. Ever since its establishment, all manner of curses have managed to seep into the Undead Settlement. The worst of them were sealed away inside a spirit tree, but eventually the curses took their toll.  
  
### Soul of the Rotted Greatwood Usage

  * Can be consumed for 3,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make: The [Hollowslayer Greatsword](/Hollowslayer+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Hollowslayer Greatsword") or alternatively [Arstor's Spear.](/Arstor's+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Arstor's Spear")

### Soul of the Rotted Greatwood Location

  * Dropped by the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood")
