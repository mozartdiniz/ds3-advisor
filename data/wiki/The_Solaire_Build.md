## Build Strategy

This build uses the Sunlight Shield (+10 for stability, optional) Sunlight Straight Sword's (+5) skill to buff your already solid damage absorption and greatly increase your damage. As well as using Lightning Blade to increase the damage once more. Using both the skill and the miracle I was able to make the overall AR 656 with a damage absorption of 30% on physical and the range of 26-30% on the others
