**Shield of Want** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Shield of an ancient king who was cursed by an all-consuming thirst. In the end, he was no king.  
>   
> The residue of the king's lust still smolders within this shield, increasing the number of souls absorbed when enemies are defeated.  
>   
> Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Shield of Want Notes and Tips:

  * Can not be Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").
  * Provides an average damage block of 65.6%. Second highest stability among standard shields, but a whole 2 units lighter. Fields a rather high 18 strength requirement but is remarkably effective, in addition to its passive ability. One of the best general use shields in the game.
  * Increases souls gained by 20%. Stacks with other items (Including another Shield of Want).
  * Wearing [Covetous Silver Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Silver+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Silver Serpent Ring") provides 32% more Souls, if also wearing [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice") you gain 98% more Souls.
  * Shield must be held for the souls to count. Will still count if you dual wield or two-hand a weapon.
  * You do not need to meet the Strength requirement to gain the 20% bonus.
  * Belongs to [King Vendrick](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Vendrick "Dark Souls 3 Vendrick") from _Dark Souls II_. (See [King's Shield](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/King's+Shield "Dark Souls 3 King's Shield")).

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a dead corpse in the lake with a giant worm at [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"). (Video- Part 5 - 1:57:29)

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
