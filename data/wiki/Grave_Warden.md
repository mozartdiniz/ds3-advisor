**Grave Wardens** are an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Grave Warden Background

Grave Wardens are found in the [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash") and [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), the game's beginning areas, and as such are rather weak. They wear dark-coloured robes and cloaks, appearing sluggish and disinterested in their surroundings. They wield various weapons, including: [Light Crossbows](/Light+Crossbow "Dark Souls 3 Light Crossbow") with [Exploding Bolts](/Exploding+Bolt "Dark Souls 3 Exploding Bolt"), [Broken Straight Swords](/Broken+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Broken Straight Sword"), [Shortswords](/Shortsword "Dark Souls 3 Shortsword"), [Spears](/Spear "Dark Souls 3 Spear"), and [Bandit's Knives](/Bandit's+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Bandit's Knife") (only in the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves")).

Some Wardens appear docile and listless; they sit on the pathway, or lean against the mountainous walls adorning the graveyards and the arena. However, they will still attack the player once in proximity, and mindfulness is advised.

### Grave Warden Combat Information

  * Grave Wardens wielding [weapons](/weapons "Dark Souls 3 weapons") such as [spears](/Spear "Dark Souls 3 Spear") or swords will promptly attack the player with the weapon's basic moveset. For instance, those armed with a shortsword will slash or lunge at the player, while light crossbow enemies will simply fire bolts. Their attacks and behavior are identical to many of the enemies found in the next area, but they usually have lower health and defenses, making them a good warm-up for the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").

### Grave Warden Notes & Trivia

  * These enemies are part of the tutorial area. They are ideal targets for new players who are unfamiliar with the game's mechanics.
  * Judging by the fact that these enemies drop the [Cleric's Sacred Chime](/Cleric's+Sacred+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Cleric's Sacred Chime"), they were likely once clerics themselves before going [Hollow](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing").
  * Not to be confused with the much stronger [Cathedral Grave Warden](/Cathedral+Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Grave Warden") found in the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") and the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").
