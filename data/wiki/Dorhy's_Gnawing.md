# Dorhy's Gnawing

**Dorhys' Gnawing** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Dorhys' Gnawing  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 20  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 25 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Miracle of Dorhys the deranged evangelist.   
>   
> Summons great insect swarm to feast on foes.  
>   
> Those who linger too long on the brink of the Deep will often slip. Dorhys is sure to have wallowed in this darkness, intoxicated by its peril. 

### How to Get Dorhys' Gnawing in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"): From the _Church of Yorksha_ bonfire, head out of the main entrance and down the stairs. Take the path going right and then head straight on to an overlook platform that has or had a Crystal Lizard on. Hit the railing on the right to reveal steps going down. Follow the path until you see an item near a tree. Drop down and kill the screaming [Evangelist](/Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Evangelist") to receive Dorhys' Gnawing.

### Dark Souls 3 Dorhys' Gnawing Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.2) dark damage.
  * Fires a pair of spinning swarm projectiles, instead of the single swarm projectile fired by [Gnaw](/Gnaw "Dark Souls 3 Gnaw"). The hit inflicts both dark damage and [bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed") buildup. 
    * This spell is an upgrade of Gnaw.
  * Applies 210 points of bleed buildup. 
    * This is the highest single hit application of bleed in the game, and is sufficient to bleed some enemies with a single cast. Extremely effective even at the minimum required Faith.
    * This bleed buildup is a fixed value, and does not scale with Luck, Faith, or Intelligence.

  * The dark damage scales with the spellbuff of the chosen catalyst. Dark Miracle catalysts ([Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman") & [Caitha's Chime](/Caitha's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Caitha's Chime")) with high Intelligence and Faith will maximize the dark damage.
  * The projectile has lazy homing, it tends to turn most accurately at medium distances. Near the end of the maximum range it shoots off in a straight line.
  * The projectile's hitbox is wider than the particles show. It can be difficult for enemies or players to avoid it in narrow places.
  * Sped up in regulation 1.08 and again in 1.11.
  * Takes approximately 1.4 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
