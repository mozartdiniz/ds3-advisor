# Small Envoy Banner

### Small Envoy Banner  
  
---  
  

> The small banner used by envoys of Great Lord Gwyn in the days of yore.  Face the ringed cliff and hold the banner high to summon facilitators of transport. For the pygmies, who took the dark soul, the Great Lord gifted the Ringed City, an isolated place at world's end, and his beloved youngest daughter, promising her that he would come for her when the day came.  
  
**Small Envoy Banner** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki"). It was added in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Small Envoy Banner Usage

  * Place after you clear The Dreg Heap to be transported to [The Ringed City (Location)](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)").

### 

### Small Envoy Banner Location

  * Corpse after defeating the [Demon Prince](/Demon+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Demon Prince"). Hard to miss but in case; [video location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_DtwGTT-V38).

### Small Envoy Banner Notes & Trivia

  * This item asserts [Filianore](/Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Filianore") as the youngest daughter of [Lord Gwyn](/Gwyn,+the+First+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Gwyn, the First Lord").
