**Ancient Wyvern** is a [Boss](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This **Dark Souls 3 Ancient Wyvern Guide** features locations, strategies and videos on how to defeat the Ancient Wyvern easily, as well as tips, weaknesses, trivia and lore notes for the Ancient Wyvern boss.

The Ancient Wyvern is a large grey wyvern, and it's boss fight is unique in that there is an additional way of killing it besides the regular hit-and-run tactic required for most bosses. Instead of fighting the wyvern head on, players can opt to run up the ruins, fighting regular enemies instead, to get high enough to deliver a plunging attack onto the dragon's head.

> A large Grey Wyvern located at Archddragon peak.

### Ancient Wyvern Boss | Dark Souls 3

This boss is optional and there are no [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") summons for this boss fight. A [non-boss variant](/Ancient+Wyvern+\(Mob\) "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Wyvern \(Mob\)") of this creature also exists, just before a group of [Rock Lizards](/Rock+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Rock Lizard") before reaching the large bell. This one drops 6 [Titanite Chunks](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), 3 [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") and 3 [Twinkling Titanites](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") upon death.

Bosses are unique and challenging [Enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") that drop [Boss Souls](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") capable of being transformed into powerful [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Spells](/Spells "Dark Souls 3 Spells"), and [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") for the player.

### Ancient Wyvern Location | Dark Souls 3

_Where to find the Ancient Wyvern in Dark Souls 3?_

  * Located in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"), immediately after the first bonfire.
  * You must have acquired the [Path of the Dragon Gesture](/Path+of+the+Dragon "Dark Souls 3 Path of the Dragon") from the area directly after the [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") boss fight. Then head to [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") and head to the dragon statue where you picked up the [Dragon Torso Stone](/Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Torso Stone") and use that [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") next to it.

### Ancient Wyvern Notes & Tips | Dark Souls 3

_What should you know about fighting the Ancient Wyvern in Dark Souls 3?_

  * Attacks deal [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") or [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"). 
  * Some attacks can topple player.
  * Hits to the head deals extra damage.
  * Hits to the torso/tail deals normal damage.
  * Hits to the leg deals minor damage.
  * Weak to [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"), [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage") and [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Extremely vulnerable to plunging attack. Any connected plunging attack to the head will instantly kill him, no matter what weapon was used or what amount of hp left.
  * Resistant to [Poison/Toxic](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") and [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage").

### How to fight and beat the Ancient Wyvern

#### Strategy 1 (Plunging Attack)

This strategy is quite easy. You'll need light fire resistance equipment, but make sure you are still able to roll. The weapon doesn't really matter as long as it deals good damage. When the Wyvern arrives, don't bother attacking it directly. Instead, go past it and to the left, where you will find a path. Follow the path and the ladders, ignoring the enemies, until you reach a specific ledge above the Wyvern's head. When it finishes breathing fire, it will lower its head. Simply jump and press R1/RB to deliver the finishing blow and claim victory. This strategy is super easy, although it doesn't include the satisfaction of beating a Wyvern with a bow—it still counts!

#### Strategy 2 (Directly underneath)

Stand directly under the Wyvern so that you are targeted and facing the dragon's stomach. If positioned correctly, the boss will only attack with blockable foot stomps and occasionally fly overhead for a fire breath attack below. Always run away from where you were facing the boss, not towards it, to avoid the fire. Hurl Lightning Spears or any damaging spell, or melee attack its legs whenever possible. Rinse and repeat this process until the Wyvern is defeated.

#### Strategy 3 (Jumping Attack)

This strategy uses a jumping/plunging attack and is quicker than Strategy 1. When you enter the boss area, run straight until you see a bell. Stand to the right of the bell and wait for the Ancient Wyvern to lower its head and breathe fire. Then, perform a jumping attack. This strategy must be executed quickly, or the dragon will move forward, forcing you to revert to Strategy 1.

#### Strategy 4 (Bow/Pyromancy and 'blind spot' cheese)

After starting the fight, immediately run towards the bell. Turn left, and you will see a short bridge with an enemy in front of you. Instead of entering the door, stick to the right barrier and stand in the corner closest to the arena. Apparently, there's a 'blind spot' where the Wyvern will constantly attack the door, ignoring the player. Lock onto the Wyvern and use a bow or pyromancy, though pyromancy has a higher chance of missing due to the Wyvern constantly shaking its head. This strategy takes a lot of time, so it isn't recommended unless you've been struggling for a long time and have become desperate.
