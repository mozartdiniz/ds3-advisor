**Pyromancer Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of pyromancers of the Great Swamp. Comprised of bronze ornamentation and animal pelt.
> 
> In the Great Swamp, bronze was used ritualistically to ward off evil spirits and keep darkness at bay.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer")
  * Sold by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 2300 Souls or he drops it if killed.

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Player notes go here
  * For modders/armor creators, the partsbnd for this is 2800.   
The file should have hd, bd, am, and lg (for head, body, arm and leg respectively) and m or f (for male and female respectively) along with this number.
