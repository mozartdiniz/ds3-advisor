# Titanite Shard

**Titanite Shard** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials) in Dark Souls 3.

### Titanite Shard  
  
---  
  

> "Titanite shard for weapon reinforcement. Reinforces weapons to +3. Titanite shards are fragments of the Legendary Slabs. Titanite is etched into weapons to reinforce."  
  
### Titanite Shard Usage

  * Reinforce regular [Weapons](/Weapons) to +3
  * 12 shards in total required to get a weapon to +3 
    * 2 shards for +1
    * 4 shards for +2
    * 6 shards for +3 

### Titanite Shard Locations

  * Can be purchased infinitely from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid) for 800 souls each after acquiring the [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes) or the [Grave Warden's Ashes](/Grave+Warden's+Ashes "Grave Warden's Ashes").
  * Dropped by [Winged Knights](/Winged+knight) in the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric) and [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Lothric Castle").
  * Dropped by [Evangelists](/Evangelist "Evangelists") in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement")
  * Dropped by [Hollow Manservants](/Hollow+Manservant) (the enemies with large machetes and cages on their backs) in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement).
  * Dropped by most Hollows in the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "High Wall of Lothric"), [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement"), and [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices).
  * Dropped by the [Silver Knights](/Silver+Knights "Silver Knights") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley) (x2 guaranteed from the archer in the painting room).
  * Dropped by the Silver Knights in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Anor Londo") (almost guaranteed drop).
  * Dropped from various enemies in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Smouldering lake") area (common).
  * Dropped by Red-Eyed enemies in the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Catacombs of Carthus").

### Notes

  * For those wishing to farm Titanite Shards, be aware that merely progressing further into the game will cause you to start tripping over piles of them. [Silver Knight](/Silver+Knight "Silver Knight") enemies nearly always drop either a Titanite Shard or [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Large Titanite Shard") each kill (sometimes multiple of them). One of the best farm locations is from the [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Anor Londo") bonfire in the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") area.
