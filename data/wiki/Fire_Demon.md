**Demon** is an [enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Demon Information

The **Demon** is a mini-boss enemy in Dark Souls 3. It is found roaming the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") and [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"). The [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") Demon is tied to [Siegward of Catarina's](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") quest, and the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") Demon drops a special soul that can be used in Soul Transposition.

### Demon Location

  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): Find the tower lift to the right of the area and drop down mid-way onto some rooftops, then further down to meet the demon.
  * [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"): After you break the hanging wooden bridge, go down using it as a ladder to find the demon near a mimic and amongst many skeletons.

### Demon Drops

  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): 
    * Souls NG (5000)
    * Souls NG+ (25,000)
    * [Fire Gem](/Fire+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Fire Gem")
  * [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"): 
    * Souls NG (5000)
    * Souls NG+ (15,000)
    * [Soul of a Demon](/Soul+of+a+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Demon")

### Strategies

#### Melee Strategies

**Catacombs of Carthus** :

  * **Skeleton Strategy** : Run down the stairs to meet the demon and run around the perimeter of the first platform. Two skeletons will rise and chase you. However, they are aggressive to the demon. Run past the demon and the skeletons will start to fight it. They do quite a bit of damage, but are easily killed by the demon. More skeletons can be found on the platform below the demon. Most of the skeletons have more than one life. Allow the skeletons to bludgeon the demon. Usually by the time all the skeletons die the demon is either dead or very close to dead. Mop up the rest of surviving skeletons (if there are any).
  * **Skull Strategy** : Using alluring skulls, bait the demon towards the edge, so that it will drop off to the lower platform. When the demon drops below, use a plunging attack or a jumping attack to deal massive damage very quickly. the skeletons in the lower section should keep it busy if you need to run to the upper platform to repeat this as necessary.

#### Ranged Strategy - Bows

**Undead Settlement:**

  * Run to the building where the dogs are and stand on the balcony. Shoot him until he dies.

**Catacombs of Carthus** :

  * Shoot the demon from the ledge away from the door. It may repeatedly attempt to breathe fire at you, which can be easily walked away from to allow you to regenerate stamina for your volleys.

#### Ranged Strategy - Magic

**Undead Settlement** :

  * Run down towards the shack on the left. There's a narrow path down the left side of the shack. Keep going and get the demon's attention; attack with [Heavy Soul Arrow](/Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Soul Arrow"), etc.. Then quickly run back the way you came. Once you get close enough to the start [Seigward ](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina")will activate. Let him start attacking the demon. Run back down past that shack and attack the demon from behind with [Heavy Soul Arrow](/Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Soul Arrow") until it dies.

#### Other Strategies

**Catacombs of Carthus** :

  * **Mimic** : When you enter the room the demon is in, do not go down to him. Instead, continue on the path you are on until you reach the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") chest. After attracting the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic")'s attention, lure it down to the demon. The Demon and the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") will be hostile to one another and deal heavy damage to one another, usually with the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") killing the demon for you, allowing you to collect the soul of the demon. Be wary of the skeletons in the area, however, as they may swarm and kill you before the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") and the Demon finish with one another.
  * **Cheese** : Lure the Demon up the stairs and towards the doorway you entered from. Once he reaches the top of the stairs, he will slowly walk back down the stairs, becoming hostile only after he has made it all the way back down. During this time, he is open to attack.

### Lore Theories

  * This demon was one of many created by the Chaos Flame, but since said flame has died, along with the other demons born of the Chaos Flame, it is possible that it, along with the one in the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"), and the Old Demon King, are the only demons left.
  * _"Demons were born of the Chaos Flame, but the flame has not survived, and the demons are a dying race."_ \- Soul of a Demon
