# Crystal Magic Weapon

## Crystal Magic Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 45  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 30 Intelligence  
**Duration** | 60 seconds  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
**Crystal Magic Weapon** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Further sharpens Magic Weapon via crystallization. Reinforces right weapon with crystal.  
>   
> After much experimentation, Logan found the crystal medium facilitated a stronger bond between weapon and soul. 

### How to Get Crystal Magic Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 10,000 souls after giving him the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Crystal Magic Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds (Spell Buff * 0.95) in [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage"). 
    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed.
    * This buff is easily identified by the crystal protrusions coming from the weapon
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * When compared to [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade") on the respective highest scaling catalysts (Yorshka's Chime/Saint's Talisman vs Court Sorceror Staff), at 60 of their respective stat (Int or Faith), Darkmoon Blade buff wins out in magic damage increase, even though they have the same Spell Buff multiplier. 
    * This happens because Faith catalysts have a (slightly) higher Spell Buff on 60 Faith, than staves do at 60 Int.
    * However, this spell costs 5 [FP](/FP "Dark Souls 3 FP") less. Meaning, it can be cast 3 times with 18 Attunement, and 4 with 24 Attunement, something Lighting Blade and Darkmoon Blade buffs awkwardly struggle with.
    * Crystal Magic weapon is also much, much easier to obtain (if a bit late, until Grand Archives) than Darkmoon Blade (which is a covenant reward).
  * Magic damage of this buff is boosted by the [Magic Clutch Ring](/Magic+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring") (+15% in PvE, +8% in PvP) and [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk") (+10%).
  * Lasts 60 seconds, and can be lengthened with the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * [Young Dragon Ring](/Young+Dragon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring") and [Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring](/Bellowing+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring")
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit. 
    * Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Twinblades](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.
    * Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
  * This spell is the strongest version of [Magic Weapon](/Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon") and [Great Magic Weapon](/Great+Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Weapon").
