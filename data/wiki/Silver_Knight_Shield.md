**Silver Knight Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in [Dark Souls 3.](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Heavy shield carried by the Silver Knights who served the old royal family. A flowing canal is chiseled deeply into its face.
> 
> The Silver Knights stayed behind to protect the humble manor and ruined cathedral. Though their goddess has long since left, her blessing upon her knights' shields remains.
> 
> Skill: Parry  
> Repel an attack at the right time to follow up with a critical hit. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Silver Knight Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Provides an average damage block of 62.8%. The heaviest parry shield in the game, but boasts good utility for its weight, and great stability.
  * Stability is 63 at +5. 
  * This shield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Silver+Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Shield")._

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by Silver Knights in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
