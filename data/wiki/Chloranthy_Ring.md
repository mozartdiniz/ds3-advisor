# Chloranthy Ring

### Chloranthy Ring  
  
---  
  

> This old ring is named for its decorative green blossom, but its luster is long since faded.   
>   
> Raises stamina recovery speed.  
  
**Chloranthy Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Chloranthy Ring Effect

  * Raises stamina recovery by 7 points per second. 
    * +1: Raises stamina recovery speed by 8 points per second.
    * +2: Raises stamina recovery speed by 9 points per second.
    * +3: Raises stamina recovery speed by 10 points per second.
  * Weight: 0.7

### Where to Find Chloranthy Ring

  * Found in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). Start from the Road Of Sacrifices Bonfire and backtrack to the lift that will take you up. Once you do, hit the switch again to allow it to go down, then you can ride it farther up to meet the Giant who shoots the arrows at you. Take the same lift back down and jump halfway, this is where you can find [Siegward](/Siegward "Dark Souls 3 Siegward") just outside of the inner part of the tower and help him in defeating the demon. After defeating the fire demon, use the staircase of the house on the right. From there use a bridge to enter the house on the left. Go through some enemies until you've passed two Evangelists. You're on the roof now. Go left and run across the roof until you've reached the wooden platform. From here you'll see a stone tower, drop off a ledge to reach the tower. Enter the tower through a door and make use of the beams and platforms within the tower to reach the ground floor safely. You'll find the ring on the ground floor beneath some wooden crates. [(video location)](https://youtu.be/nq8iogVPV5s "\(video location\)")
  * +1 Version (NG+) Behind the pedestal with the [Ring of the Sun's First Born](/Ring+of+the+Sun's+First+Born "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun's First Born"). ()
  * +2 Version (NG++) From the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") bonfire, head past the wagon and to the left of the crow mage there's a ledge that you can drop down to that has the ring. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/YsqquFwwebk "\(video location\)")
  * +3 Version ([The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City")): Ringed Inner wall Bonfire Route: Take the lift down from the Ringed Inner Wall bonfire. This is the shortcut and will be unlocked as you progress this area. As you go down look for the side passage and roll onto it as you go. Find the hole and drop down to the statue of Gwyn to find this ring. ([Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=abGaJiAs9pc))
  * +3 Version ([The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City")): Shared Graves Bonfire Route: From the Shared Grave Bonfire follow the path up past the bridge and into the Church. Take a left up the stairs. On the right there should be an invisible wall/ path to an elevator. Take it up and on your right is a alcove you roll/walk into. Make a right from there and fall through the hole and pick up the Ring. ([Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ELVRMBvnQBg))

### Chloranthy Ring Notes

  * Stacks with the [Grass Crest Shield](/Grass+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Grass Crest Shield") and the various [Green Blossom](/Green+Blossom "Dark Souls 3 Green Blossom") items.
  * This ring has appeared throughout the series, including in  _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Cloranthy+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Cloranthy Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Chloranthy+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Chloranthy Ring")_.
