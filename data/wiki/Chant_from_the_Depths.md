# Chant from the Depths

## Chant from the Depths  
  
---  
  
##   
  
> Works with staff equipped in left or right hand. Briefly boosts the strength of dark sorceries.  
  
| 7(-/-)  
  
**Chant from the Depths** is a [Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") in Dark Souls 3.

### Weapons That Have Chant from the Depths

  * [Murky Longstaff](/Murky+Longstaff "Dark Souls 3 Murky Longstaff")

### Chant from the Depths Notes & Information

  * Basically the same as [Steady Chant](/Steady+Chant "Dark Souls 3 Steady Chant"), but only boosts the power of dark sorceries.
  * Does not cause thrust counter frames.
  * Applies a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") that increases dark sorcery damage by 20%, compared to   
the 12.5% bonus for all [sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") from Steady Chant.
  * Lasts about 15 seconds.
