**Greatsword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.  
  
  

> This ultra greatsword, with its thick blade, is one of the heaviest of its kind.  
>   
> Highly destructive if intolerably heavy.  
>   
> There would appear to be some credence to the rumors that this sword tested the true limits of human strength.  
>   
> Skill: Stomp  
> Use one's weight to lunge forward with a low stance and increased poise, and follow with strong attack for a spinning slash.

### Notes and Tips

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"). 
  * The Greatsword has a range of 40.5346xl on the 1h R1 and 43.9493xl on the 2h R1. It is the longest Ultra Greatsword in the game when two-handed.
  * The Greatsword benefits greatly from Heavy and Hollow infusions.  

    * Hollow has the potential for highest physical AR, but requires extremely high stat investment. It's best to use Heavy unless you're absolutely trying to maximize damage at high levels.
  * Despite being named "Greatsword", it is in fact an [Ultra Greatsword](/Ultra+Greatswords "Dark Souls 3 Ultra Greatswords") and not from the [Greatsword](/Greatswords "Dark Souls 3 Greatswords") class.

###  Softcaps & Scaling

  * 40/40 - STR/DEX - 590 AR 1h, 625 AR 2h - Quality Build, Refined Gem
  * 60/10 - STR/DEX - 619 AR 1h, 657 AR 2h - STR Build, Heavy Gem
  * 28/10/30/30 - STR/DEX/INT/FTH - 235/471 AR 1h, 264/471 AR 2h, - Min Reqs, Dark/Chaos Softcaps  
  
 _All values are for +10._

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a corpse on top of a broken bridge in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"). Go straight ahead from the first bonfire until you reach a broken bridge, look behind you and you will see another piece of broken bridge and a body holding an item, that item is the Greatsword ([video location](https://youtu.be/DE6xDSCGfNc "video location")).

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
