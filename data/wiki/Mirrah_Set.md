**Mirrah Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Mask attached to a ceremonial hat.  
> A Hollow once fought valiantly with this mask, but feared the fading of her self, and implored a comrade remember her name.  
> Perhaps that is why this gentleman's mask is named after a woman. (Helm)  
>   
> Garb worn by Mirrah Knights sent on journeys. This hard leather vest is bestowed only upon proven knights. (Vest)  
>   
>   
> Knights travel afar to fulfill their sacred duties, but few are ever able to deliver on their vows.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), after fighting the [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon") with [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina"). Once you get to the roof past the two [Evangelists](/Cathedral+Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Evangelist") drop onto the Tower near the Boss Room. 
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/nq8iogVPV5s "Video Location")
  * The mask ([Lucatiel's Mask](/Lucatiel's+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Lucatiel's Mask")) can be obtained by trading a [Vertebra Shackle](/Vertebra+Shackle "Dark Souls 3 Vertebra Shackle") with [Pickle-Pee, Pump-a-Rum](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow")
