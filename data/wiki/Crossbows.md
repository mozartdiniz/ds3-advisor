# Crossbows

**Crossbows** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. These [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") usually do moderate damage and have less range than [Bows](/Bows "Dark Souls 3 Bows"). They are generally more close ranged weapons, allowing players to "shoot from the hip" and reload on the move without entering a two-handed stance. These weapons do not scale with stats, and as a result are less useful on consecutive NG+ runs. Crossbows use [Bolts](/Ammunition "Dark Souls 3 Ammunition").
