**High Lord Wolnir** is a [Boss](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") enemy in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This **Dark Souls 3 High Lord Wolnir Guide** features locations, strategies and videos on how to defeat High Lord Wolnir easily, as well as tips, weaknesses, trivia and lore notes for the High Lord Wolnir boss.

High Lord Wolnir is a massive skeleton covered in jewelry, of which the bracelets act as the hitbox of the boss. He mainly attacks the player with his torso and limbs but on occasion may wield a [sword](/Wolnir's+Holy+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Wolnir's Holy Sword"), making the fight more dangerous. This colossal skeleton is imbued with deadly darkness and commands an army of skeletons to his defense.

> Lord Wolnir of Carthus sentenced countless souls to gruesome deaths, keen to outlive them all.

### High Lord Wolnir Boss | Dark Souls 3

This boss fight can be unpredictable - either going very smoothly or being a chaotic mess. It is recommended to destroy the bracelets as quickly as possible to reduce the chances of the boss summoning his sword or army of skeletons. There are no [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") summons available for Lord Wolnir's Boss Fight. You can summon Phantoms before the doors but not in the room with the Goblet. This boss is not optional and must be defeated in order to traverse further into [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").

Bosses are unique and challenging [Enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") that drop [Boss Souls](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") capable of being transformed into powerful [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Spells](/Spells "Dark Souls 3 Spells"), and [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") for the player.

### High Lord Wolnir Location | Dark Souls 3

_Where to find High Lord Wolnir in Dark Souls 3?_

  * [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"), at the very end, up the stairs past the suspension bridge.
  * You need to touch the goblet on the altar in front of the stairs in order to trigger a cut scene and be transported in the arena.

### High Lord Wolnir Notes & Tips | Dark Souls 3

_What should you know about fighting High Lord Wolnir in Dark Souls 3?_

  * Deals [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") and [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"). Permanent cloud dealing dark damage coming out of his ribcage. Getting caught in this almost guarantees instant death.
  * Wolnir is an abyssal creature, therefore it is weak to [Farron Greatsword](/Farron+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Greatsword") and [Wolf Knight's Greatsword](/Wolf+Knight's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Wolf Knight's Greatsword").
  * Weak to [Strike damage](/Strike+damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike damage") and weapons with the Blessed infusion.
  * Very high defense against [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"). 
  * Immune to [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed")
  * Resistant to [Poison](/poison "Dark Souls 3 poison") and [Toxic](/toxic "Dark Souls 3 toxic")
  * May spawn a sword at any time (no bracelets are required to be broken).
  * He will move towards you if you go too far away. After doing this 3-5 times you'll be trapped against the wall and vulnerable to his dark cloud aura. However, he pulls back a good distance every time you break one of his shackles.

### High Lord Wolnir Attacks

Attack Name | Attack Description  
---|---  
Dark Cloud | Raises and releases a dark damage smoke cloud from his mouth that does heavy damage per second.  
Has a safe spot under his head which, unlike running away, is much less likely to trigger him to move forward. Be careful of moving too far forward or to either side and hitting the cloud around his ribcage.  
Summon skeletons | Summons a group of skeletons using his left hand. He spawns different skeletons depending on how many bracelets he has remaining. His normal attacks can harm the summoned skeletons and he usually kills most of them quickly.  
Sword summon | Invokes a gigantic sword from the ground using his right hand and swipes it towards you. He can unsummon the sword by sticking it into the ground, when this happens it deals AOE damage.

Hand slam | Slams his hand down on the player with substantial tracking.  
Hand swipe | Sweeps the arena with his left arm, threatening damage over a large area.  
  
### How to fight and beat High Lord Wolnir

#### Strategy 1 (Melee)

Wolnir is a deceptive boss to fight. On one hand, he can be very easy if you know how to beat him, but conversely, he has several attacks that can kill you very quickly. The key to the fight is breaking the shackles on his wrists. He has a huge pool of HP, and while it's perfectly possible to kill him without breaking the shackles, it's not advisable. Each shackle breaking takes away one-third of his HP.

When you enter the fight, make a beeline for his right hand (your left). This shackle is the hardest to break as he moves this arm a lot and only attacks with it when he has his sword in play. Take a strong weapon and two-hand it to deal maximum damage. He tends to keep his right arm near his rib cage, which is covered with a localized dark cloud. Be careful when attacking this shackle, as you can easily take damage from the cloud. If he somehow manages to corner you on the other side of the arena, don't be afraid to attack the other shackles in an attempt to force him back.

Once you have broken the right shackle, move to the left hand and continue trying to break the other shackles. When you break a shackle, Wolnir will start summoning skeletons of varying types, from regular skeleton soldiers to bonewheels. You can largely ignore these enemies as his melee attacks will normally kill them. Only engage them if they're likely to damage you; focus instead on the shackles.

He spawns different skeletons depending on how many bracelets he has remaining. With all bracelets intact, he will spawn 3-4 normal skeletons. With two bracelets remaining, he will spawn 3-4 normal skeletons and 2-3 bonewheel skeletons. With one bracelet remaining, he will spawn 2 Murakumo-wielding skeletons, 2 wheel skeletons, and 5 normal skeletons. His normal attacks can harm the summoned skeletons, and he usually kills most of them quickly. This phase can be avoided altogether if you manage to kill him quickly.

During the fight, if he lifts his head right up, run to the back of the boss room as he is about to breathe his dark cloud attack. This will deal enormous amounts of damage very quickly. Once he's done this attack, he'll claw his way closer to the back of the room. When he's as far up as he can go, the breath becomes a major issue. Instead of running to the back wall, which will still result in death, run between his head and his ribs. The cloud attack moves forward from his mouth, not backward. There's only a small sweet spot where you won't take damage, so be careful.

Lastly, when he brings his sword out, roll under the swipes to avoid taking damage. When he dismisses it, he will smash it into the ground, causing an AoE attack. This does physical damage and is largely unavoidable, so when you see him go to stab it into the ground, use a 100% physical damage reduction shield. It'll stagger you, but you'll not take damage from the AoE.

#### Strategy 2 (Magic)

Start by moving towards Wolnir to prevent him from pushing you against the back wall early on. Target Wolnir's golden bracelets on either side and move in close. Hit him a few times with Farron Flashsword or Soul Greatsword, then back off as he will likely attack. Roll back to dodge his attacks, and once he places his hands back down, attack his bracelets again. Quickly target the next bracelet and get some good hits in. You can run up and use Farron Flashsword or Soul Greatsword, or cast Heavy/Great Soul Arrow from a distance. Be prepared for his next attack, and watch carefully for his deadly breath attack—get as far away as possible to avoid it. Avoid the skeletons that have now spawned, and wait until Wolnir wipes them away with his attacks. Continue the onslaught from afar or close-up. Rinse and repeat this process: moving in to attack the bracelets, dodging his attacks, and maintaining distance during his breath attack. This strategy will help you manage the fight and steadily wear down Wolnir's health.

#### Strategy 3 (Sorcery with White Dragon Breath on NG+)

All you need for this strategy is the spell White Dragon Breath (WDB) and your standard sorcerer equipment. When you enter the room, run to the far right side and target Wolnir's left hand (your right). Position yourself so that his bracelet is between you and the white target dot, and cast WDB when his arm is down. After two full hits, the bracelet will shatter. Rinse and repeat with the remaining bracelets. If you are quick enough, he won't spawn a single enemy, and the entire fight can be over in about 60 seconds. Unfortunately, this only works on NG+ because you obtain WDB later in the game.

Alternatively, you can do an early run to defeat the Dancer and Oceiros, then transpose Oceiros' soul to enable this strategy on NG.

#### Strategy 4 (Pestilent Mist)

Using Pestilent Mist, you can kill Wolnir without him ever fully waking up. To do so, start the fight as usual by walking up to his left hand (your right) and begin casting Pestilent Mist. Avoid attacking him by any other means. With about five uses of Pestilent Mist, you can kill him without triggering his more dangerous attacks or summoning skeletons.

#### Strategy 5 (Pyromancy)

Using Great Chaos Fire Orb along with pyromancy rings (Great Swamp Ring, Witch's Ring, and Fire Clutch Ring) allows you to utilize both the initial damage burst and the short-lingering lava to break Wolnir's shackles with little to no effort. As you enter the room, grab the tome and head to the left side. Focus on breaking his right (your left) bangle first, as it is the hardest to hit and keep track of. Once you break that bangle, it's likely he will either crawl up the hill or raise his head to use his cursed breath attack. Stay back and move to the right side of the room to target his left hand, then repeat the process on the remaining bangles. If he summons skeletons, your Great Chaos Fire Orb will make short work of them. Once you break two bangles, they become much stronger, so prioritize taking out his right hand first. Make sure to dodge his hand strikes, move away from Wolnir's sword, and avoid his fist strikes and swipes.

#### Strategy 5 1/2 (Pyromancy, with less progression needed)

With Great Combustion, equipped alongside the Great Swamp Ring and Lloyd's Sword Ring, you can employ a similar strategy. Begin by targeting Wolnir's right (your left) bangle first. Approach carefully, as getting too close can result in taking damage from the fog. Use Great Combustion to break the shackles, making sure to maintain a safe distance to avoid being choked by the fog. Once the right bangle is broken, move to the left side and repeat the process on the remaining bangles. This strategy, while requiring caution due to the fog, is effective and allows you to deal significant damage to Wolnir.

#### Strategy 6 (Poison Cheese)

Acquire a Storyteller's Staff from one of the Corvian Storytellers on the Road of Sacrifices and reallocate all or most of your charges to your Ashen Estus Flask. Level up or respec to at least 12 Intelligence to wield the staff. Upon entering the fight for the first time, walk to your right and approach Wolnir's left arm. Use the Storyteller's Staff's weapon art until he is poisoned, and repeat when the poison wears off, restoring FP from your Ashen Estus Flask as necessary. This strategy can be repeated until he is dead, but it cannot be used if Wolnir is "awake," which happens if you attack him directly or get too close to his face. This method is similar to using Pestilent Mist but can be performed with far less Intelligence, at the expense of needing to obtain the staff, which may take a while to drop. It is an excellent strategy for low-level builds attempting to get past the catacombs.

#### Strategy 7 (Lightning Storm NG+)

To efficiently break Wolnir's bracelets, two point-blank casts of Lightning Storm are needed for each bracelet. This spell is also highly effective at clearing skeletons when they spawn. Treat this strategy like a melee approach, as you need to get in close to each bracelet to cast the spell effectively. This method has been tested on NG+ with 60 Faith, a +10 Canvas Talisman, the Lightning Clutch Ring, Morne's Ring, and the Ring of the Sun's First Born.

### High Lord Wolnir Lore Theories

  * Wolnir does not seem to be in control of the Abyss that he is in and instead fears it, as once the bracelets are destroyed, he is devoured by the darkness.
  * "When Wolnir fell to the Abyss, he was gripped by a fear of true darkness, and pleaded to the gods for the first time. This holy sword, together with three armlets stripped from the corpses of clerics, gave him some semblance of comfort." - Wolnir's Holy Sword.
  * Leaks from quality assurance testers of the alpha version suggest that Wolnir was originally going to play the role of [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") \- using that name - in [The Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"). with [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") playing the role of [Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") \- using that name - in [The Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash") \-  _(credit darksouls3theorist)._

### High Lord Wolnir Trivia

  * Exploit: If you run all the way back after you enter the area, Wolnir will follow you until it can no longer do so. Head to either side and stay near Wolnir's wrists, and then continue to attack his bracelets. None of his attacks will hit you, although the skeletons he summons will still target you.
  * The band on his right hand (your left) is harder to hit as Wolnir doesn't leave it down as long. Thus, it's recommended to break that one first during the easier part of the fight.
  * He takes much more damage on his bracelets before waking up (first one was two swings of a fire broadsword+2, others were 6 or so).
  * From footage of older trailer Wolnir was apparently meant to be in a different place, or have a different arena.
  * When Wolnir is asleep, his HP is lower by about 2/3, comparing to state, when he is not asleep.
  * Pestilent Mist does not wake him up, what makes him very easy to kill.
  * Killing Wolnir with at least 1 remaining bracelet will trigger different death animation.
  * Oddly, if the player instantly follows Wolnir once he is killed, they will simply see a wall where the Abyss is supposed to be; Wolnir simply disappears.
