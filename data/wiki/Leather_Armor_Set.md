**Leather Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Broad-brimmed leather hat. Traditionally used by master archers, and especially favored by forest-dwelling hunters.   
>   
> The name Pharis is said to have once belonged to a hero, but is now more widely known as a style of hat.   
>   
> Armor made of smooth leather. Even lighter and more flexible than most leather armors.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 1800 souls, after giving her [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes")
  * Hat is dropped by an [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru") in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Starting armour for the hunter class in the original Dark Souls.
