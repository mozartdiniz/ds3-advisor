# Great Farron Dart

**Great Farron Dart** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

## Great Farron Dart  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  Sorcery  
**Focus Cost** | 4  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 23 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Sorcery which improves upon Farron Dart. Fires a more powerful soul dart.   
>   
> Entrusted to the leader of the Legion's acolytes, and apparently a sorcery of his daughter, Heysel, that was refined by a Crystal Sage.

### How to Get Great Farron Dart in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 2,000 souls after giving him the [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Great Farron Dart Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Great Farron Dart inflicts (Spell Buff x 0.59) magic damage before resistances.
  * The damage is low, but is very fast and costs little FP, making it very spammable and good for harassment 
    * Its moveset is identical to [Farron Dart](/Farron+Dart "Dark Souls 3 Farron Dart"), which itself uses the [Throwing Knife](/Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Throwing Knife") moveset.
