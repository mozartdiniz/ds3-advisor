This is a player-created **Build** for Dark souls 3.

  * **Build Name:** Regen Paladin
  * **Build Level:** 20
  * **Build Focus:** PvP, but also works well for PvE
  * **Build Main[Stat](/Stats):** Vigor + Faith
  * **Link to Build:** [mugenmonkey.com/darksouls3/453676](/mugenmonkey.com/darksouls3/453676 "mugenmonkey.com/darksouls3/453676")

**Note:** Sacred Chime of Filianore is optional as it's the only chime that lets you use the weapon art  _then_ put it away and have the buff still active. If you aren't able to get to the ringed city to get this chime, you can use another chime or even a talisman, since regen miracles are flat healing.

**Another Note:** This build is slightly flexible. Want to use a different shield? Maybe a small shield perhaps? Go ahead. Want to swap out [Replenishment](/Replenishment) for [Force](/Force) or [Med Heal](/Med+Heal)? Sure why not. Just know that the [Kite Shield](/Kite+Shield) was picked specifically for the fashion. As well, while it hasn't been tested at high levels, the build should still work modestly well with higher stats. Swap out [Replenishment](/Replenishment) for [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light), throw a [Lightning Gem](/Lightning+Gem) or [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem) on the [Mace](/Mace), get some more attunement slots and throw on some more miracles like [Sunlight Spear](/Sunlight+Spear) or [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow) or a normal healing miracle like [Med Heal](/Med+Heal) or [Great Heal](/Great+Heal). At that point maybe ditch the [Priestess Ring](/Priestess+Ring) and throw on a [Ring of the Sun's First Born](/Ring+of+the+Sun's+First+Born) or [Morne's Ring](/Morne's+Ring).

## **How to Use**

There's not much to this build. Cast your buffs before going into battle. Swap from your shield to caestus when two handing the mace and/or using perseverance. If you want, swap priestess ring for another ring after casting buffs, such as hornet ring or silvercat ring.
