**Leonhard's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> (Piece Name) of [Ringfinger Leonhard](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard"). Leonhard was born into royalty, which is believed to be the reason for his skill in both sorcery and swordsmanship.  
>   
> In his youth, Leonhard suffered grave burns to his entire body. His face in particular, which he hid beneath his mask, was terribly scalded.   
> He abstained from restoring these injuries, even after becoming a Finger of Roasaria. (Mask)  
>   
> Indeed this dingy garb is in fact embroidered with gold thread, betraying its purpose as military wear designed for a noble. (Chest)  
>   
> Indeed, these dingy gauntlets are in fact decorated with silver, betraying their purpose as military wear designed for a noble. (Gauntlets)  
>   
> Indeed, these dingy trousers are in fact meticulously tanned, betraying their purpose as military wear designed for a noble. (Leggings)  
>   
> 

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Hand](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid")[maid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 10500 souls after killing [Leonhard](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard"). He drops the [Silver Mask](/Silver+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Silver Mask") and [Lift Chamber Key](/Lift+Chamber+Key "Dark Souls 3 Lift Chamber Key") upon death. If finished his [questline](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard") he will also drop [Soul of Rosaria](/Soul+of+Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Rosaria")

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * If you want to kill him to get his set, damage him untill he's around mid-life. Normaly, he shall try to heal himself with his Estus Flask. When he tries to heal, stunlock him by attacking him (Always try to keep your stamina as high as possible). If he tries to heal, he won't attack you untill he healed himself, so stunlock him untill he dies. Then, he shall drop the Silver Mask and his set shall be bought from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").
  * This set pays homage to [The Hunters](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/The+Hunter "Dark Souls 3 The Hunter") of [Bloodborne](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/Bloodborne+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Bloodborne Wiki").
