**Herald Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Steel armor, Leather Gloves and Quilted Trousers said to be worn by Heralds of the Way of White, who deliver commandments of duty.
> 
> Protection made of steel has excellent physical absorption, but is found lacking against blunt strikes and lightning damage.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear of the [Herald](/Herald "Dark Souls 3 Herald").
  * Looted from a corpse at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"), after the Crystal Sage bonfire next to an [Evangelist](/Cathedral+Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Evangelist") and a campfire. [Fextra Video Location](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=38m36s "Fextra Video Location") (starts at 38m 36s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * While most metal armor sets have poor lightning and strike resistance, the resistance disparity for these on the Herald set is extreme.
  * Has very good resistance-to-weight ratio for all resistances besides strike and lightning.
