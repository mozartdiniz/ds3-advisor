# Soul of an Unknown Traveler

**Soul of an Unknown Traveler** is a [Soul](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of an Unknown Traveler  
  
---  
  

> Soul found in the corpse of an unknown traveler.  
>   
> Use to acquire souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Soul of an Unknown Traveler Usage

  * Consume to gain 800 Souls

### Soul of an Unknown Traveler Locations

  * Sold by ??
  * Dropped by Rats, (and more?)
  * 1x at [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash"): next to where you kill the first crystal lizard 
  * 4x at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): 
    * 1x in the area with [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's Bone") near the wall to the left side of the burning tree.
    * 1x close to where you find [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp"). 
    * 1x on the cliff near the Dilapidated Bridge [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires").
    * 1x in the sewer in the area with skeletons.
  * 1x at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"): Overturned coach in the woods
