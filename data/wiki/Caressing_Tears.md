# Caressing Tears

## Caressing Tears

**Caressing Tears** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

> Miracle taught by Morne, the Archbishop's apostle.
> 
> Cures bleeding, poison and frost for self and those in the vicinity.
> 
> Caressing Tears is a tale of the many deaths surrounding the goddess Caitha, of whom Morne was a known follower.

### How to Get Caressing Tears in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 1,500 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Caressing Tears Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Cures the player of [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), and [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") status.
  * Cures the player of the [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic") status, but does not alleviate build up.
  * Also removes Bleed inducing Maggots.
  * The spell has a small AoE range so phantoms can be cured when standing next to the caster.
  * Curing range is buffed by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
  * This miracle first appeared in _Dark Souls 2_ as [Caressing Prayer](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Caressing+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Caressing Prayer").
