# Heal

**Heal** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [miracles](/miracles "Dark Souls 3 miracles").

  

## Heal  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**FP** | 45  
**Slots** | 1  
**Faith** | 12  
  
> Elementary miracle cast by clerics. Restores HP for self and those in the vicinity.  
>   
> To use miracles, equip a talisman or sacred chime and attune a miracle at a bonfire.

### How to Get Heal in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 1000 souls
  * Starting item of the [Cleric](/Cleric "Dark Souls 3 Cleric") class

### Dark Souls 3 Heal Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores HP equal to 300% * Spell Buff of used [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") or [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") (Not affected by int scaling on catalysts with int scaling)
  * Small AoE that also heals phantoms.
  * Not affected by miracle rings.
  * When the button is held down you can delay the healing, ideal for co-op partners to get inside the radius.
  * Its low faith cost makes this a good spell for supporting allies on a non-caster build.
  * Its AoE is NOT affected by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
