**Slave Knight Gael** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in Dark Souls 3 (DS3). **Gael** is one of the NPCs who cannot be killed, and has a special role to play in the story of the game. Gael is encountered in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC and is also a [Boss](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") that players can fight. Gael is also widely considered to be the final boss of the entire Dark Souls series.

### Gael NPC Dark Souls 3 Information

Slave Knight Gael is an aberration created by consuming the Dark Soul. It is unknown which kingdom Gael fought for or in which wars he was active. At some point during his life, he discovered the Painted World of Ariandel and encountered a young woman who wished to paint a new world. This meeting significantly influenced his journey and motivations. Gael is easily recognizable by his noticeably bright red cape, which contrasts with his otherwise ragged and worn appearance. When players first encounter him, Gael moves and fights like a beast on all fours, demonstrating his ferocity and animalistic nature. However, as the battle progresses and he reaches a certain health threshold, he straightens up, adopting a more upright and menacing stance. This transformation marks a shift in his fighting style, becoming more aggressive and dangerous.

  * Can't be killed permanently. Will respawn in the Cathedral, if killed. May take several reloads.
  * Can be summoned in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"), before the [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede") boss fight, in the corner just to the left of the sliding altar. When summoned he will appear for Phase 2 of the boss fight. Can be summoned even if not embered. Note: Summon is blocked, when player defeats Demon Prince in The Ringed City DLC.
  * Can be summoned in The Dreg Heap before the [Demon in Pain & Demon from Below](/Demon+in+Pain+&+Demon+from+Below "Dark Souls 3 Demon in Pain & Demon from Below")/[Demon Prince](/Demon+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Demon Prince") boss fight. Summon sign can be found near Eathern Peak bonfire and dark circle. This time can't be summoned if not embered, but beating Sister Friede first does not block this summon.
  * He wears the [Slave Set](/Slave+Set "Dark Souls 3 Slave Set") and wields [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword").
  * See also: [Slave Knight Gael (Boss)](/Slave+Knight+Gael+\(Boss\) "Dark Souls 3 Slave Knight Gael \(Boss\)")

### Gael NPC DS3 Location

  * You can find Slave Knight at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), Cleansing Bonfire if you have the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). He will be lying prostrate to the right of the bonfire and will give you passage to the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel").
  * If you enter the Ringed City[ ](/Slave+Knight+Gael+\(Boss\) "Dark Souls 3 Slave Knight Gael \(Boss\)")before meeting him at the Cleansing Chapel, an alternate cutscene will play wherein the painting is resting on the altar.
  * His projection can be found near few messages at The Dreg Heap.

### Slave Knight Gael Dark Souls 3 Drops

  * No items.

### Slave Knight Gael DS3 Combat Information

  * Uses [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword") (RH), [Sunset Shield](/Sunset+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunset Shield") and [Canvas Talisman](/Canvas+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Canvas Talisman") (LH).
  * Casts [Way of White Corona](/Way+of+White+Corona "Dark Souls 3 Way of White Corona").

### Slave Knight Gael Dark Souls 3 Trivia

  * When Gael is summoned for the fight against [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede"), his name and health bar will not be present. This is odd because every other NPC has their name and health bar and Gael does have them when he is summoned for the [Demon in Pain & Demon from Below](/Demon+in+Pain+&+Demon+From+Below "Dark Souls 3 Demon in Pain & Demon From Below")/[Demon Prince](/Demon+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Demon Prince") fight.
  * Painting Women calls him an "uncle". However, the Japanese text version clarifies that she treats him like a "mentor" rather than uncle in a biological way.
  * Seeks the dark soul of man.
  * Gael's beard cannot be obtained ingame through legitimate means.
