**Plank Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in **Dark Souls 3**. [Small Shields](/Small+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Small Shields") usually weigh very little and have the least form of protection from attacks. They are often used by players who prefer parrying rather than blocking.

> Makeshift shield cobbled together from wooden planks.  
>   
> Provides minimal protection, and at the cost of moderate humiliation.  
>   
> Skill: Shield Bash  
> Without lowering your guard, strike the enemy with the shield to knock them back or stagger them. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Plank Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * The lightest shield in the game.
  * NOT recommended for use, due to its low damage block and poor stability. This small shield doesn't have a parry, meaning larger shields are a far better choice.
  * However, due to its light weight, it can still be used for Simple or Blessed infusion.
  * If you don't use a shield bash in your fighting style, this shield is not recommended. If you do, defer to the [Pierce Shield](/Pierce+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Pierce Shield").
  * [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") will buy it for 2 souls, the lowest price among all shields.
  * The Plank Shield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Plank+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Plank Shield")_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting equipment for the [Deprived](/Deprived "Dark Souls 3 Deprived")
  * Dropped by Large Undead with planks on their backs.
  * Looted from a corpse next to a weird Caged NPC at the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
