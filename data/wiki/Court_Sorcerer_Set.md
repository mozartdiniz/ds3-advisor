**Court Sorcerer Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor worn by court sorcerers of the Profaned Capital.
> 
> The tall, dark headpiece and formal gold stitching suggests they may have also been oracles.
> 
> There are many sorcerers who claim to heirship to the great sage "Big Hat" Logan, and the Profaned Capital houses on of two leading schools.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a corpse in the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital") near the sleeping [Hand Ogre](/Hand+Ogre "Dark Souls 3 Hand Ogre") (Monstrosity of Sin) that's by itself on the second floor of the church.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Set appears to be worn by Pontiff Sulyvahn
