# Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring

### Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring of a clandestine guild of sorcerers based in Vinheim, home of sorcery. Masks the sound of its wearer. The Dragon School held effective sovereignty over Vinheim, with a great many adept assassins at its disposal.  
  
**Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring Effect

  * Masks the sound of its wearer.
  * Weight: 0.7

### Where to Find Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring

  * Given by [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") after you purchase [Aural Decoy](/Aural+Decoy "Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy"), [Farron Flashsword](/Farron+Flashsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Flashsword"), [Pestilent Mist](/Pestilent+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mist") and [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook"). 
    * This requires giving [Orbeck ](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") a scroll to unlock [Pestilent Mist](/Pestilent+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mist") (any scroll will do) and a total of 8,000 souls to buy all 4 spells.

### Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring Notes

  * Helpful in PvP situations where the player may have to jump down to get to an enemy. This masks their footsteps so that the player won't hear them coming.
  * Can also be used in PvE for getting easier backstabs on enemies as they can't hear your footsteps. In fact, as long as enemies don't see you, they won't aggro on you with this ring.
  * Like [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook"), this ring works great with [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") to allow for repeated backstabs. Hidden Body works in tandem with [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire")
  * Also hides the visual effects of some spells on your character, notably [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial").
  * This masks the sound a player makes when walking in water.
  * This ring previously appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring")_.

### Bugs

  * It seems as though using certain heavy weapons can nullify the sound masking effect for NPCs, allowing them to detect you at a longer distance.
