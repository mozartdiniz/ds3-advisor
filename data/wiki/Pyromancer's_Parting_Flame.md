**Pyromancer's Parting Flame** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> The pyromancy flame of Livid Pyromancer Dunnel that attracts the echoes of death.
> 
> When Dunnel lost his hideous spouse, he gave his own pyromancy flame as an offering, which transformed into a parting flame. Not long after, Dunnel became a mad spirit, cursed to wander the lands.
> 
> Skill: Parting Flame
> 
> Release store of death echoes to gain a modest dose of Estus.

### How to Get Pyromancer's Parting Flame in Dark Souls 3

  * [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel"): Past the Snowy Mountain Pass bonfire, on the bottom floor of the crypt with the giant flies (can also be accessed from the Ariandel Chapel bonfire if the shortcut to here has already been opened). There is a fly facing an illusory wall on the top floor which will lead to the bottom floor. From here, proceed to the right side of the room to a walled-up doorway. This is another illusory wall. Proceed through here until you reach the outside area with giant roots. Carefully descend down the roots until you reach the tower below. The corpse here will contain the Parting Flame.
  * [Fextralife Video: Location of Pyromancer's Parting Flame](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=6m41s "Fextralife Video: Location of Pyromancer's Parting Flame")

### Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer's Parting Flame Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Upgraded with normal [Titanite](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials"), and can only be upgraded by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp").
  * The Pyromancer's Parting Flame leaves a trail of fire during its casting animations, which will damage nearby opponents. This additional damage compensates for its lower spell buff when compared to the Pyromancy Flame, and will out damage the [Pyromancy Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame") on any pyromancy where the fire trail hits as well as the pyromancy. The flame trail can trigger before the pyromancy itself, this is notable on pyromancies like [Black Flame](/Black+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Black Flame") as it makes it difficult to dodge at close range. The following is a list of pyromancies that produce a flame trail that can deal additional damage: [Fireball](/Fireball "Dark Souls 3 Fireball"), [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb"), [Bursting Fireball](/Bursting+Fireball "Dark Souls 3 Bursting Fireball"), [Great Chaos Fire Orb](/Great+Chaos+Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Great Chaos Fire Orb"), [Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Bed Vestiges"), [Great Combustion](/Great+Combustion "Dark Souls 3 Great Combustion"), [Sacred Flame](/Sacred+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Flame"), [Profaned Flame](/Profaned+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Flame"), [Black Flame](/Black+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Black Flame"), [Black Fire Orb](/Black+Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Black Fire Orb"), [Seething Chaos](/Seething+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Seething Chaos"). It should be noted that Sacred Flame does not produce a flame trail, but does deal additional damage as though it did.
  * Has a higher stamina consumption than the regular [Pyromancer's Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame").
  * The weapon art's ability is triggered by killing enemies while the flame is active. After killing 12 enemies, your character will be enveloped by a dull, ghostly gold flame. Activating the WA at this point will cause the player to regain 1 normal Estus flask. The WA must be used before the Flame can accumulate another charge from more deaths, meaning the stored "charge" cannot stack.
  * The Strength requirement not being met has a different effect than other catalysts. Spell buff, Spell damage, Focus cost, Stamina consumption, and the damage from the fire trail all see no penalties for players with less than 15 Str. However, attacks (like Great Combustion and Black Flame) may "bounce" off of shields and some armored enemies at melee range. Additionally, as with many other weapons, lacking the strength requirement renders the Weapon Art unusable.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity, Magic, Fire, Lightning, and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat]. This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks becomes weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an item's durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at its best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
