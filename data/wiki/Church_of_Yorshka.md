# Church of Yorshka

**Church of Yorshka** is a small area with a bonfire and the closest bonfire to the [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn") boss fight. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### NPCs in the area

  * [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") will be here if you have followed her/his questline up to this point, if you have found her/him in her/his two previous locations and after you encounter and kill Horace (If you fail to kill Horris in the smouldering lake Anri will die and never come here.)
  * Lord of Hollows ending will be prematurely ended if you kill the Hollow assassin. If you want the hidden ending simply exhaust Anri's dialogue and kill Sulyvahn.

### Bosses

  * [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn")

### Notes

  * There is an ASSASSIN

## Full Church Of Yorshka Walkthrough

  
Church of Yorshka is more of a sub-location than a fully-fledged area. See [Irithyll Of The Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+Of+The+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Of The Boreal Valley") for a full walkthrough of the surrounding area. 

In the corner of the room, after Anri shows up in the Church there will be a new statue. This statue is an Assassin, they will kill Anri and prematurely end her questline. Simply attack the statue and kill the Assassin, they will not fight back.
