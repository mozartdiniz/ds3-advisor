**Old Sorcerer Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Traditional attire worn by sorcerers of the Vinheim Dragon School.
> 
> Long ago, sorcerers were said to always dress properly, even while on distant journeys of discovery, even before such journeyes were established curriculum.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") on a corpse at the back of a cell; starting from the first bonfire, proceed through the first block of cells and drop down past the Jailer. Turn around and be wary of the hollow prisoners as you grab it.
