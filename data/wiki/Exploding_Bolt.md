# Exploding Bolt

**Exploding Bolt** is an [Ammunition](/Ammunition "Dark Souls 3 Ammunition") in Dark Souls 3.

### Exploding Bolt  
  
---  
  

> Bolts that explode on impact. Deals area fire damage in the immediate vicinity. Due to the risk of detonation, the amount of bolts the user can carry is greatly limited. These are, after all, prototypes created by the weapon craftsman Edmond. You get what you pay for."  
  
### Exploding Bolt Usage

  * Ammo for [Crossbows](/Crossbows "Dark Souls 3 Crossbows").
  * Bolts that explode on impact. Deals area fire damage in the immediate vicinity.
  * Deals 140 Fire damage.

### Exploding Bolt Locations

  * Available to purchase (300 souls) from [Shrine Handmaiden ](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid")after handing in [Greirat's ashes](/Greirat's+ashes "Dark Souls 3 Greirat's ashes") after his third outing.
  * 6x found at Cathedral of the Deep.

### Notes

  * Can only carry 30 at once, less than the 99 for most other bolts.
  * Damage increased from the Fire Clutch Ring.
