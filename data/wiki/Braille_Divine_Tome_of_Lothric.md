# Braille Divine Tome of Lothric

**Braille Divine Tome of Lothric** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Braille Divine Tome of Lothric  
  
---  
  

> A sacred braille tome from Lothric, filled with miracles for use by knights.  
>   
> Give to a storyteller to learn Lothric miracles.  
>   
> It is said that no paladin inside Lothric castle could fall, owing to the divine protection they enjoyed.  
  
### Braille Divine Tome of Lothric Usage

  * Unlocks [Bountiful Light](/Bountiful+Light), [Blessed Weapon](/Blessed+Weapon), [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier)

### Braille Divine Tome of Lothric Locations

  * [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle), courtyard before [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour). Through broken wall on the left near the dragon, drop down into lower balcony. _Be careful of a[Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Pus of Man") that will be on the dragon's claw. _It can be pegged with firebombs from above, with some repositioning every few throws.__ Once it's dead, turn back into the building and up the stairs to the right. The Tome will be found in the curled up corpse.

### Notes

  * This Tome is needed to proceed with the Light Path of [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Irina of Carim")'s quest.
