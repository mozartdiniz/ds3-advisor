# Carthus Beacon

## Carthus Beacon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 35  
**Slots Used** | 2  
**Requirements** | 12 Intelligence/ 12 Faith  
  
**Type** | Body Buff  
**Duration** | 30 seconds  
  
**Carthus Beacon** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

> The most obscure pyromancy developed in Carthus of the Sands.  
>   
> Damage increases with consecutive attacks.  
>   
> Carthus's aggression has been likened to an uncontrollable fire, and since ancient times its beacon has been used as a signal for war.

### Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon Effect

Boosts attacks, as long attacking persists.

  * **PVE:** Initial buff = 7.5%, Max = 15%
  * **PVP:** Initial buff = 3%, Max = 6% 

### How to Get Carthus Beacon in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 8,000 souls after giving him the [Carthus Pyromancy Tome](/Carthus+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Pyromancy Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Will boost attack power after every few hits, depending on weapon type.
  * If attacking stops, the boost will decrease and eventually end.
  * Stacks with [Pontiff's Right Eye ](/Pontiff's+Right+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Right Eye") & [Old Wolf Curved Sword](/Old+Wolf+Curved+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf Curved Sword")
  * Bows and Crossbows seem unable to increase the buff, but benefit from the initial boost.
  * [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") affects the buff duration.
  * Does not stack with other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"), [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat"), [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Iron Flesh](/Iron+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh") or [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within").
