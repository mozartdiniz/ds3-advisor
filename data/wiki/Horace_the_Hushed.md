**Horace the Hushed** is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. He is standing to the right of Anri of Astora and gives you the [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") badge, unlocking the Covenant. He is one of the only prisoners who managed to escape from Aldrich's captivity. Horace is considered by Anri as a very loyal friend and a trustworthy warrior. Upon encountering Horace, you'll notice that he never actually speaks but instead resorts to grunts when talked to. As you progress, Horace goes missing, becoming separated from his friend Anri.

### Horace Dark Souls 3 Questline

Horace is a companion to [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora"), and will turn hostile after you progress into the Catacombs.

To locate Horace again, continue the game past the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"). Head into the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") and find [Anri](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") who will ask you about Horace's location. If you speak to Anri here and tell them "NO", She/He will move further into the Catacombs by an old bridge. You can speak to Anri again here, but it is not necessary. Horace is located in another area across the bridge. Once there cut the rope on the bridge by hitting it with your weapon, wait for it to collapse, and then climb down. Past the [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon") is a bonfire, and past the bonfire is [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"). In [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") hug the right wall until you see a small passageway with two Crystal Lizards. Horace will be at the back of this area and will be hostile. You can see a map [here](/file/Dark-Souls-3/smouldering_lake_map1_dks3.jpg?v=1464332438412 "here").

If you kill him here, he will give you the [Llewellyn Shield](/Llewellyn+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Llewellyn Shield"). If you kill him, it won't matter if you tell Anri where he is. Anri's questline will continue and you will receive the [Ring of the Evil Eye](/Ring+of+the+Evil+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Evil Eye") in the Catacombs or at Anri's next location. If you don't kill him and tell Anri, Anri will be killed and their questline will end. If you don't kill him, but don't tell Anri his location, Anri's questline will continue.

  * If you kill Horace before speaking to Anri in his/her first location in the Catacombs, she/he will only be found at the second location.

### Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed Drops

  * Souls: NG (2000), NG+ (6000), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * Weapons: [Llewellyn Shield](/Llewellyn+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Llewellyn Shield")
  * If killed in the Firelink Shrine, where he will be after the player defeat the [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep"), he will drop 10000 souls, but this action will terminate [Anri's](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") questline, therefore it's not recommended. 

### Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed Dialogue

Horace the Hushed does not have specific dialogue lines. Instead, he only makes what sounds like a low growl or grunt when spoken to.

### Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed Trivia

  * Horace would seem to be a member of the [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels").
  * The shield Horace uses was made by Llewellyn The Smith, who also created the armor worn by the two Drang Knights protecting Aldrich. The Drang Set is from Dark Souls II, where it was called the Llewellyn Set.
  * Horace might have ironically been named after the leading Roman lyric poet under the same name. Roman name (and link to Wikipedia): [Quintus Horatius Flaccus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace "Quintus Horatius Flaccus")
  * Miyazaki, a huge fan of the manga Berserk, may have been inspired by the character Basuzo's armor design in making the Executioner Set.
  * Horace is one of two children to survive Aldrich's appetite
  * If you kill Horace and tell Anri where he is, then return to his location in the Smoldering Lake after Anri has left, there will be a small shrine with his broken halberd, shield, and a red prism stone on top, indicating that Anri found his body and laid him to rest (Anri says earlier that they leave prism stones for each other, to guide their way). Interestingly, if you meet Anri at the Yorshka bonfire, she/he will still say that they never found Horace.
  * The vocalizations Horace makes are like those made by individuals whose tongues have been cut out, indicating that Horace may be a survivor of an encounter with [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers").
