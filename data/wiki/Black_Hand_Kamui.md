**Black Hand Kamui** is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Black Hand Kamui Information

  * Can be killed.
  * Located in [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * Cannot be summoned.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi"), [Black Hand Armor Set](/Black+Hand+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Armor Set"), [Hunter's Ring](/Hunter's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hunter's Ring").

### Location

  * Found among the 3 hostile NPCs at top of the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"), on the staircase that leads to the Twin Princes. 
    * He's accompanied by [Lion Knight Albert](/Lion+Knight+Albert "Dark Souls 3 Lion Knight Albert") and [Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild](/Daughter+of+Crystal+Kriemhild "Dark Souls 3 Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild").

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (12,000), NG+ (24,000), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * Weapons: [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi")
  * Other: After killing him, the [Black Hand Armor Set](/Black+Hand+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Armor Set") will be sold by the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").

### Combat Information

  * Weapons: [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi") (RH), [Composite Bow](/Composite+Bow "Dark Souls 3 Composite Bow") (LH)
  * Other: [Black Hand Armor Set](/Black+Hand+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Armor Set"), [Poison Arrows](/Poison+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Poison Arrow"), 2 [Estus Flasks](/Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Estus Flask").
  * Kamui will use his bow and fire arrows if the player is out of his physical range. In close range, Kamui will use the Onikiri and Ubadachi and will often use the Skill [Onislayer](/Onislayer "Dark Souls 3 Onislayer").

### Npc Information

  * Weapons: [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi") (RH), [Composite Bow](/Composite+Bow "Dark Souls 3 Composite Bow") (LH)
  * Other: [Black Hand Armor Set](/Black+Hand+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Armor Set"), [Poison Arrows](/Poison+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Poison Arrow"), 2 Estus.

### Dialogue

None. He does not speak.

### Trivia

  * The NPC trio is aggroed as a group when you attack one of them. This gives Kamui some kind of super awareness if you come back later after previously killing some of them. When approaching him form the rear, while wearing the Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring, you still have to walk slowly to avoid being spotted.
  * Kamui are divine spirits in the mythology of the Japanese Ainu culture.
  * He has a relation to [Black Hand Gotthard](/Black+Hand+Gotthard "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Gotthard") (being both assassins for the king).
  * The trio he seems to be a part of resembles the fight with the [ Graverobbers](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Graverobber%2C+Varg%2C+and+Cerah "Dark Souls 3 Graverobber%2C Varg%2C and Cerah") in [Crown of the Sunken King DLC](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Crown+of+the+Sunken+King "Dark Souls 3 Crown of the Sunken King") from DS2.
