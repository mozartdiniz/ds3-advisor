# Spin Slash

## Spin Slash  
  
---  
  
##   
  
> Slice into foes with a large spinning motion, and follow with strong attack for a spinning vertical-slash. (Scimitar/Shotel) Slice into foes with a large spinning motion, and continue spinning to transition into a strong attack. (Sellsword Twinblades/Warden Twinblades/Falchion/Carthus Curved Sword/Carthus Shotel/Rotten Ghru Curved Sword/Murakumo/Exile Greatsword/Carthus Curved Greatsword) Slice into foes with a large spinning motion, and use strong attack to lengthen attack and end with a double-bladed thrust. (Gotthard Twinswords) Spin to stoke a fierce chaos flame, and use momentum to transition into a spinning strong attack, creating an evanescent lava chamber. (Demon's Scar)  
  
| Varies

### Notes & Information

  * The light attack and the L1/LB attack can combo into this Skill. This makes it espically deadly when a weapon buff like [Carthus Rouge](/Carthus+Rouge) or [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade) is added.
  * The second slash can still be used without any FP, but the damage will be significantly weaker.
  * It takes about 2 seconds before the actual hitbox activates, so it's best to predict your opponent ahead of time.
  * Cannot be parried.
  * The version of Spin Slash that the Demon's Scar has is a bit different; the strong attack will leave a pool of lava on the ground like the Pyromancy [Great Chaos Fire Orb](/Great+Chaos+Fire+Orb). The pool of lava is not lingering, however, and only lasts for about a second.
