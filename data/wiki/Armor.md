# Armor

**Armor** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** provides the player with protection against various types of damage, resistance to [Status Effects](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects"), and an increase in [Poise](/Poise "Dark Souls 3 Poise"). Players can mix and match different armor pieces for both functional and cosmetic purposes, allowing for a personalized look while maintaining effective defenses. Unlike previous installments in the series, armor in Dark Souls 3 cannot be upgraded, meaning its stats are fixed once obtained. For detailed information on individual pieces, refer to the [Helms](/Helms "Dark Souls 3 Helms"), [Chest Armor](/Chest+Armor "Dark Souls 3 Chest Armor"), [Gauntlets](/Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Gauntlets"), and [Leggings](/Leggings "Dark Souls 3 Leggings") pages.

  * For players interested in creating stylish and unique character appearances, check out the [Fashion Souls](/Fashion+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Fashion Souls"). This page showcases various cosmetic builds that emphasize aesthetics over stats, allowing you to mix and match armor pieces to achieve your desired look in Dark Souls 3.
  * You can use this [Equipment & Spell Checklist](/file/Dark-Souls-3/dark-souls-3-all-equipment-spell-checklist.xlsx) to keep track of the items and spells you've acquired throughout your journey in Dark Souls 3. This checklist helps ensure you don't miss any equipment or spells, making it easier to complete your collection and optimize your build.
