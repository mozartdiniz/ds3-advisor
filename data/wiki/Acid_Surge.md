# Acid Surge

**Acid Surge** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame ](/Pyromancy+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame")or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Acid Surge  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  Pyromancy  
**Focus Cost** | 24  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 0 Intelligence/ 13 Faith  
  
**Type** | AoE Attack  
  
> Pyromancy of Carthus of the Sands. Emits an acid which corrodes weapons and armor.  
>   
> Carthus' swordsmen attached great value to victory, and would not shun the use of such pyromancies. For where is the honor in death and scoured bones? 

### How to Get Acid Surge in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 2,000 souls after he has been given the [Carthus Pyromancy Tome](/Carthus+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Pyromancy Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Acid Surge Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Destroys the durability of Weapons and Armor currently worn by the enemy. The enemy has to be in the mist. Acid Surge will deal approximately 8 damage to equipment durability of the victim stays in the cloud for the whole duration. If the cloud passes through the victim instead, they'll take about 2 durability damage
  * Not recommended for Arena-based PvP, but might prove useful in group fights as to scramble or worry opponents.
  * Curiously, this spell will mark objects on the map with a small acid surge cloud when touching them. This may be the spell trying to break their durability, as the same cloud appears when used on players.
