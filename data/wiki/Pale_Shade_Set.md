**Pale Shade Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire worn by those who kill in the name of the Sable Church of Londor. 
> 
> The Pale Shades of the Sable Church are all undying Hollows, giving rise to much fear and contempt. 
> 
> Their fight is one of neither honor nor exaltation, yielding nothing but withered moans.
> 
> Gold mask worn by those who kill in the name of the Sable Church of Londor.   
>   
> A faint, kindly smile is bound to its surface, giving them the moniker Harlots of Death.   
>   
> What lies beneath that expression, however, is nothing more than the face of a darkly shriveled Hollow. (Sneering Mask)   
>   
> 

  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * After angering [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") (hit her, kill her,kill Anri's Assassin, etc) or killing [Yoel ](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor")including the moment you meet him on the bridge you have to defeat the [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") in both Farron Keep and Irithyll of the Boreal Valley (**you need to be embered and not have defeated the area bosses in order to be invaded**).
  * [Farron Keep:](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") go to Farron Keep bonfire, exit and turn right, by the [basilisks. ](/Basilisks "Dark Souls 3 Basilisks")You have to be invaded here first, otherwise you won't get his set. You need to do this **BEFORE[THE ABYSS WATCHERS](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers").**
  * [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley:](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") go to Distant Manor bonfire, go through the sewers he will invade on a stairway near a shortcut. Then, if you already killed him at Farron Keep, go back to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") (where Yuria/Yoel were) and the set will be there. You need to do this **BEFORE[PONTIFF SULYVAHN](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn")**.
  * Watch [Video](https://youtu.be/enWQmnBQWzQ "Video") on how to get the set
  * Rather resembles Mozgus from Berserk [[1]](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/From+Berserk+to+Bloodborne#1 "Dark Souls 3 From Berserk to Bloodborne#1").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Gaining the armor on NG+ is recommended to not interupt the Londor quest line requiring Draw out Strength from Yoel by killing him the moment you meet him on the bridge ensuring both envasions when embered.
