# Excrement-covered Ashes

**Excrement-covered Ashes** is an [Ash](/Ashes) in Dark Souls 3.

### Excrement-covered Ashes  
  
---  
  

> Unclean umbral ash coated with excrement. Perhaps it's possible the handmaid of Firelink Shrine could turn this into a few new things...   
>    
> "Oh to savor the sweet pungency but once more..."  
  
### Excrement-covered Ashes Usage

  * Unlocks [Blue Bug Pellet](/Blue+Bug+Pellet), [Blooming Purple Moss Clump](/Blooming+Purple+Moss+Clump), [Dung Pie](/Dung+Pie "Dung Pie") and [Stalk Dung Pie](/Stalk+Dung+Pie "Stalk Dung Pie") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid)

### Excrement-covered Ashes Locations

  * [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley): In the sewers near the Distant Manor bonfire. Move past all the [Sewer Centipedes](/Sewer+Centipede), towards where [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina) is, and look to your left before going up the stairs to meet him. The ashes will be in a little cubby area.
