# Large Titanite Shard

**Large Titanite Shard** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials) in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Large Titanite Shard  
  
---  
  

> "Large Titanite Shard for weapon reinforcement. Reinforces weapons to +6.  Large Titanite Shards are fragments of the Legendary Slabs. Titanite is etched into weapons to reinforce."  
  
### Large Titanite Shard Usage

  * Reinforce regular [Weapons](/Weapons) to +6
  * 12 shards required in total to get a weapon from +3 to +6 
    * 2 large shards for +4
    * 4 large shards for +5
    * 6 large shards for +6
