# Fire Keeper

The**Fire Keeper** is an NPC in Dark Souls 3 located in the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). She tends to the bonfire and assists the player character on their long and arduous journey through [Lothric](/Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Lothric"). Interacting with her allows the player to spend souls to level up, and later, heal the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") using the [Fire Keeper Soul](/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul"). She is a blind maiden who has a white crown that covers her eyes and wears a black robe. Just like other Fire Keepers, she can transform souls into power. Players can also converse with the Firekeeper and can expect changes in her Dialogue as progress throughout your ventures in the game.

### Fire Keeper Information | Dark Souls 3

  * The Fire Keeper can be killed but will respawn when the area is reloaded.
  * Allows the player character to spend souls to level up. After the player has discovered the Fire Keeper Soul in the tower above Firelink Shrine and given it to her, the player can 'heal' the Dark Sigils located in their inventory.
  * The [Eyes of a Fire Keeper](/Eyes+of+a+Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Fire Keeper") can be given to her to begin the questline to trigger "[The End of Fire](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings")" ending. When she is in possession of the Eyes of a Fire Keeper, the music that plays in Firelink Shrine will change. 
  * Killing the Fire Keeper while she is in possession of the Eyes of a Fire Keeper will cause the item to drop and be reclaimable by the player character.
  * Wears the [Fire Keeper Set](/Fire+Keeper+Set "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Set").
  * The Firekeeper can react to the gestures that are performed by the player.

### Fire Keeper Location | Dark Souls 3

  * [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") near the bonfire upon arrival, then near the hallway or sitting on the stairs ([Video of Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e8J2lYZFUiw&feature=youtu.be&t=27m38s)).

### Fire Keeper Drops | Dark Souls 3

  * Drops [Eyes of a Fire Keeper](/Eyes+of+a+Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Fire Keeper") upon death if you have given them to her.

### Fire Keeper Notes & Trivia

[](/file/Dark-Souls-3/Fire_Keeper.jpg)

  * She is voiced by Olivia Mace.
  * The Fire Keeper is reminiscent of the Maiden in Black from Demon's Souls, Shanalotte from Dark Souls 2, and the Doll from Bloodborne. 
  * When the player character gives her the Eyes of a Fire Keeper, the theme music playing in Firelink Shrine will change. 
  * Her hands are covered in burns.
  * The Fire Keeper's dialogue mirrors a lot of the dialogue of the Maiden in Black from Demon's Souls: 
    * "_Then touch the darkness inside me. Take nourishment from these sovereignless souls._ " **vs** "_Go forth; touch the Demon inside me. Let these ownerless souls become thine own._ "
  * The Fire Keeper will respond to nearly all of the player's gestures: 
    * When performing the "Path of the Dragon" gesture, the Fire Keeper will continuously spin around.
    * When performing the "toast" gestures, the Fire Keeper will do a little twirl. 
    * When performing the "Patches squat" gesture, the Fire Keeper will twirl.
    * When performing the "bow" gesture, the Fire Keeper will bow back.
    * When performing the "wave" gesture the Fire Keeper will bow back.
    * When performing the "curl up" gesture, the Fire Keeper will appear to hide a smile.
    * When performing the "jump for joy" gesture, the Fire Keeper will turn and bring her hand to her face.
    * When performing the "joy" gesture, the Fire Keeper will turn and bring her hand to her face.
    * When performing the "Applause" gesture, the Fire Keeper will bow back
    * When performing the "prostration" gesture, the Fire Keeper will turn her head and bring her hand to her face.
    * When performing the "legion etiquette" gesture (a threat/duel challenge), the Fire Keeper will look sad.
    * When performing the "sleep" gesture, upon waking, the Fire Keeper will turn her head away.
  * When the player returns to Firelink Shrine still wearing the wax head, or is wearing a ring from Londor (Untrue Dark Ring/Untrue White Ring), she will look away, as if in distaste. It is unknown why she does this.
