**Exile Armor Set** is an Armor in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of the watchdogs of Farron's Keep.
> 
> After the Legion's Watchers became Lords of Cinder, the wolf blood dried up, and Farron was consumed by a festering wood.
> 
> Within the wood, an emaciated old wolf commands watchdogs to defend the sanctity of sleeping warriors.
> 
> Both the exiles were surely watchdogs themselves, for Farron has always been a land of itinerants.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from: [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 13,500 souls; available after defeating the 2 Hostile NPCs just outside [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") wearing said armor.
