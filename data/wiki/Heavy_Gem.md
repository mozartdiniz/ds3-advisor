# Heavy Gem

**Heavy Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Heavy Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite.   
> Famously used to forge Farron greatswords.  
>   
> Used in infusion to create heavy weapons.  
>   
> A warrior can appreciate a heavy weapon, for they scale effectively with strength.  
  
### Heavy Gem Usage

  * Used in [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") along the Heavy upgrade path.
  * Heavy Weapons have lower base damage, but higher [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength") scaling.
  * The [Farron Coal](/Farron+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Farron Coal") is also required to enable Heavy upgrades.

### Heavy Gem Location

  * Drops from normal hollows in The [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") (rare).
  * Drops from the Elder Ghru in the poison swamp of [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") (rare).
  * Drops from slugs in the poison swamp of [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") (rare).
  * Drops from Ghru (unarmed) and Ghru Conjurators in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") (rare). 
  * Gift from Hawkwood after defeating the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood") or [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage"). Dropped by [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") if killed.
  * 1x from a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), down in the ravine near the open sewer entrance in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").
  * 1x from a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), near the dead dragon at the top of the Old Wolf of Farron bonfire in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"). It is found behind the large gate on the opposite side of the Asylum Demon, and is the Crystal Lizard (1 of 3, the other two drop 1 [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard")) found by going up the stairs to the left once on the other side of the bridge. You can access this part of the bridge by a small set of platforms just to the right of the gate nearest to you after going up the elevator.
  * 1x [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") near the watchtower with the archers guarded by a [Millwood Knight](/Millwood+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Millwood Knight") with a [Quakestone Hammer](/Quakestone+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Quakestone Hammer")
  * Drops from [Millwood Knights](/Millwood+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Millwood Knight") in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel")

### Notes

  * This is perfect for builds going for a high investment in strength, since it completely gets rid of the dex scaling, even at +0. 
  * However, even if you have low investment in dex, it might be beneficial to check out a refined path, since some weapons have a terrible scaling even with the heavy gem, such as a [Rapier](/Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Rapier") or [Carthus Curved Sword](/Carthus+Curved+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Curved Sword").
  * Be aware that when comparing infusion damage at [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre"), the AR increase by two-handing a weapon is not displayed, unless the player is two-handing the weapon while in the infusion menu.
