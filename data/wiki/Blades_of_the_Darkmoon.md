# Blades of the Darkmoon

## Blades of the Darkmoon  
  
---  
_A silver pendant depicting the Darkmoon and a sword, the crest of the Darkmoon Knights, the original Blue Sentinels. Equip to pledge oneself to the Blade of the Darkmoon covenant._  
  
_When a member of the Way of Blue faces a dark spirit, the Blades of the Darkmoon, by an ancient accord, help to root out the invader. Summoning takes place automatically when equipped._  
  
**Blades of the Darkmoon** is a [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3 "Dark Souls 3"). The covenant can be gained by equipping its insignia of the same name, which is obtained from [Company Captain Yorshka](/Company+Captain+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Company Captain Yorshka") within [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). While a member of the covenant, you will be automatically summoned to defend those within the [Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue "Dark Souls 3 Way of Blue") covenant whenever they are invaded.

### Blades of the Darkmoon Information

  * The insignia is located in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"), at a tower accessed by walking over an invisible path from the rotating platform. The rotating stairs can be in any position. If it is at the top (allowing access to Anor Londo) go to the bottom of the stairs. If it is at the bottom, go to the top. There is a bonfire on a tower directly to the left of you (if you are facing the Anor Londo bonfire), and a broken platform on the rotating platform facing towards it.   
[(Video Location)](https://youtu.be/7BNpYCuFVy8 "\(video location\)")

  * To obtain the insignia and join formally, the player must have learned the "Darkmoon Loyalty" [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") from [Sirris of the Sunless Realms](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") and perform it in front of Company Captain Yorshka.
  * Alternatively, Company Captain Yorshka can be killed to retrieve the covenant item required to join, but you will be unable to turn-in covenant items for rewards until the next new game cycle.

### Effects of the Covenant

  * While the covenant's insignia is equipped, the player will be automatically summoned into the worlds of those within the Way of Blue covenant whenever they are invaded.
  * The matchmaking formula is covenant dependent: 

Condition | Min | Max  
---|---|---  
Summoning a White Phantom, [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight"), [Mound-Maker](/Mound-Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Maker") or no covenant | Host - 10 - (Host * 0.1) | Host + 10 + (Host * 0.1)  
Defending Summons of Blades of the Darkmoon or [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") | Host - 20 - (Host * 0.2) | Host + (Host * 0.1)  
  

### Notes

  * The covenant is identical in function to the [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels"), with an additional rivalry with the [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful").
  * While searching for worlds to defend, the covenant icon next to your status bars will fade in and out with a white color.
  * You do NOT get an ear from killing Aldrich Faithfuls or Watchdogs of Farron. However, you DO receive a Vertebra Shackle in addition to a Proof of Concord Kept if the invader is a member of the Mound Makers.
  * Blades of the Darkmoon are only summoned in response to players who invaded by using a Red Eye Orb. Hostile players who were summoned through red or white signs, or who invaded using the Aldrich Faithful or Watchdog of Farron covenant items, do not cause Darkmoons to be summoned.
  * The Darkmoon phantom color is dark blue.
  * If the Host of Embers enters a boss room, the Blade of the Darkmoon will be rewarded with a Proof of Concord Kept and be returned to their world.
  * If the invader returns home, is killed by a monster, or dies by fall damage, the Blade of the Darkmoon will NOT be rewarded a Proof of Concord Kept. However, they will still get the same victory message.
  * If the invader dies or returns home before the Blade of the Darkmoon spawns into the world, they will NOT be rewarded a Proof of Concord Kept. However, they will still get the same victory message.
  * If an invader successfully uses the [Black Separation Crystal](/Black+Separation+Crystal "Dark Souls 3 Black Separation Crystal"), it will count as a victory for the player. However, they will not be rewarded with a Proof of Concord Kept.
  * It is possible to be summoned to [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") if the host is fighting one of the NPC summons of the lizard-men there.
  * Summoning will prioritize the area that you are in, but will summon you anywhere after an indeterminate amount of time has passed.
  * If you start new game plus, you CAN be summoned to maps (& bonfires) you have not activated yet on your second playthrough (tested on NG+).
  * When a Darkmoon or Blue Sentinel spawns, a floating red eye will appear, locating an invader. For every invader in any given world, there will be one floating red eye that appears.

### Rank Requirements

Ranks are obtained by offering obtained [Proof of Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept") items to Company Captain Yorshka. These can be acquired by being summoned by the covenant insignia and defeating invaders. One is given for each invader banished and two are given for killing a red [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful").  
[Silver Knights](/Silver+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight") also have a small chance to drop them (approx 1 in 30). See [Proof of a Concord Kept](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept") for farming information. The rank requirements are additive, meaning you only have to earn a total of 30 to obtain all covenant rewards.

Rank | Requirement | Rewards  
---|---|---  
0 | Initial | None  
1 | 10 | [Darkmoon Ring](/Darkmoon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Ring") (Grants 2 additional attunement slots.)  
2 | 30 | [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade") (Miracle)
