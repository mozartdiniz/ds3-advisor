# Bows

Bows have changed a bit in Dark Souls 3. Different Bow types have different attack speeds, ranges and damage. They require arrows and usually a decent amount of Dexterity. It will be possible to use a [Short Bow](/Short+Bow "Dark Souls 3 Short Bow") in melee range. Bows can not be infused.
