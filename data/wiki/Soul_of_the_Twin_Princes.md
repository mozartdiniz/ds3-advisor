# Soul of the Twin Princes

**Soul of the Twin Princes** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Twin Princes  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Twin Princes. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.   
>   
> Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.  
>   
> The two princes rejected their duty to become Lords of Cinder, and settled down far, far away to watch the fire fade from a distance. A curse makes their souls nearly inseparable.  
  
### Soul of the Twin Princes Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Lothric's Holy Sword](/Lothric's+Holy+Sword) or [Lorian's Greatsword](/Lorian's+Greatsword) via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition)
  * If you have both swords you can transpose them to create the [Twin Princes' Greatsword](/Twin+Princes'+Greatsword "Twin Princes' Greatsword")

### Soul of the Twin Princes Locations

  * Gained for defeating [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince) and [Lorian, Elder Prince](/Lorian,+Elder+Prince)
