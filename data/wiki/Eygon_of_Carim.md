**Eygon of Carim** is an NPC in Dark Souls 3.

### Eygon of Carim Information

  * After freeing [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") from her cell in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), Eygon will become allied with you.
  * If you kill [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") he will show up there when you return, hostile.
  * If you aggro him for any reason, and wish to stop him from attacking, visit the statue of [Velka](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Velka the Goddess of Sin") to pardon you for [your level x1000] souls. It is located in the [Undead Settlements](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") sewers next to the Dliapidated Bridge after purchasing the grave key.
  * If you kill Eygon, you can still talk to [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim").
  * Upon his death, his [armor set](/Morne's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Set") will be purchasable from the [Firelink](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").

### Location

  * You can find Eygon of Carim in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") near the lift that leads to your first encounter with [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") and the [arrow-shooting giant](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement").
  * Can be summoned behind a stone pillar for the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") fight. Look to the left side of the room upon entering. 
  * Can be summoned in the chapel to the right of the boss arena for the [Dragonslayer Amour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") fight.
  * He will appear in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after the the player has exhausted his dialogue at [Irina](/Irina "Dark Souls 3 Irina")'s cell and defeated the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"). He will not be available for summoning for boss battles whilst he is here.
  * Can be found hostile just after [Iudex Gundyr's](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") bonfire upon purchasing any Dark Miracle from [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim"). Drops [Morne's Great Hammer](/Morne's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Great Hammer") and [Moaning Shield](/Moaning+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Moaning Shield").
  * Will appear dead in [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim")'s old cell after exhausting all his dialogue and defeating [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") and the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"), with his Hammer and Shield as lootable.

### Trivia

  * Eygon of Carim is played by [Grahame Fox.](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0289018/?ref_=tt_cl_t9 "Grahame Fox")
  * Holds some similarity to Garl Vinland from Demon's Souls, a greathammer and shield wielding knight who guarded the maiden Astrea much like how Eygon guards Irina.
  * His weapon of choice is very similar to the Grant used by Paladin Leeroy from Dark Souls as they are both great hammers with a faith requirement and special area of effect attack. Much like Leeroy, he is summonable for a sorcerer who creates clones of himself.
  * His helm is based on ceremonial/parade helm of Holy Roman Emperor Charles V.
  * Can be summoned for dragonslayer armor even after being summoned for crystal sage. ( Summon can be found in the building to the right of the stairway leading to the boss fog on the right side just beyond the doorway ). 
  * Eygon is the second knight of Carim NPC you can ally yourself with, the first being Lautrec of Carim from Dark Souls 1. Both the knights also has a keen interest in Fire Keepers, maybe it's a cultural thing.
  * Interestingly, he uses a shield in combat, a trait that is very rare among the knights of Carim, who often uses the Parrying Dagger.
  * Within the Ringed City, past Midir and inside the tower that houses the Shared Graves Bonfire, the player will be invaded by the Moaning Knight. This knight is using the same moveset and armor as Eygon, which may lead to speculation that the invader is indeed Eygon gone hollow, or some similar knight within his order. He also drops the Blindfold mask.
  * Building from this information, one can infer that this "Moaning Knight" is the slain Eygon that stole Irina after she was corrupted by the Dark by way of corrupt miracle tomes. Thus, due to a possible player choice, Eygon is left hollowed after the ages waste away, and he guards the bonfire in a place that once held firekeepers. (huge guess)
