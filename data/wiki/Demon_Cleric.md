# Smoldering Ghru

## Demon Cleric  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Demon  
**Weakness** |  Bleed, Poison, Dark, Lightning,  Black Knight Weapons  
**Resistances** | Fire, Blunt, Frost  
**Immune** | Rapport, Alluring Skull  
  
**Demon Cleric** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Demon Cleric Enemy Description

  * A Demon found in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") and Old King's Antechamber, possessing four arms, a crown of horns and a beaked face.
  * Utilizes long and mid-range Pyromancies and wields a Great Machete.
  * Will summon Chaos Fire Orbs.

### Demon Cleric Combat Information

  * Health: NG (1425), NG+ (2038), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * They are armed with the [Great Machete](/Great+Machete "Dark Souls 3 Great Machete") and a [Pyromancy Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame") (not visible). They have four hands and they use a similar moveset to [Yhorm's Great Machete](/Yhorm's+Great+Machete "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm's Great Machete") (1-handed downward chops, 2-handed slam attacks). With the flame, they can use spells similar to [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb"), [Fire Whip](/Fire+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Fire Whip"), and what seems to be the [Floating Chaos](/Floating+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Floating Chaos") spell from the Ashes of Ariandel DLC. 
    * Their balls of fire can spawn on various heights, some of them will be unreachable by all but the furthest-reaching vertical melee attacks.
    * Balls of Fire do not have a lot of health, making bows and crossbows an excellent ranged solution even for non-DEX builds (through observation, they die after inflicting >/= 100 damage).
    * Balls of Fire take no damage from fire-based attacks.
    * If the Demon Cleric is killed, all Floating Chaos orbs that it spawned will disappear.
  * Can be lured?
  * Can't be Rapported.
  * Very weak against bleed. With barbed straight sword (Fully upgraded) +Carthus Rouge, every second hit caused huge bleeding damage.
  * Can be parried/riposted and backstabbed.

### Demon Cleric Notes & Trivia

  * When they are about to create another floating fire orb, they will telegraph this with a high-pitched whine. Use this opportunity to backstab them before they can finish making the fire orb.
  * Despite being titled as 'Clerics', they possess no miracles whatsoever.
  * The description of the [Floating Chaos](/Floating+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Floating Chaos") spell suggests that it may be the same as the floating fire orbs used by these enemies. 
  * The Floating Chaos orbs summoned by the Demon Clerics will disappear when they are killed.
