# Young Grass Dew

**Young Grass Dew** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Young Grass Dew  
  
---  
  

> A small, transparent stone that once graced the green young grass crest. This semi-precious gem honors a Spear of Church after a battle well-fought, and is said to slightly enhance their blessing of protection. (while held, this effect is constant)  
  
### Young Grass Dew Usage

  * This item provides passive bonuses to your character while summoned as Spear of the Church. 
    * Increases the number of Homing Knives by 1.
    * Increases [FP](/FP "Dark Souls 3 FP") by 10%.
    * Slightly increases damage absorption, poise, and stamina regeneration.

### Young Grass Dew Locations

  * [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") covenant reward for turning in 10 [Filianore's Spear Ornament](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament").

### Young Grass Dew Notes

  * Can only be applied if the player is summoned as a member of Spears of the Church.
