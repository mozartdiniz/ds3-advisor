# Homing Soulmass

## Homing Soulmass  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 20  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 20 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Homing Soulmass** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [ Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery developed by Big Hat Logan, the great sorcerer who left the Dragon School in an age long past.  
>   
> Releases a homing soulmass.  
>   
> This sorcery may offer a clue as to what Logan sought, but further investigation suggests its attraction to living things mirrors the nature of the dark.

### How to Get Homing Soulmass in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 6,000 souls after giving him [Logan's Scroll](/Logan's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Logan's Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Homing Soulmass Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Each soulmass inflicts (Spell Buff x 0.49) magic damage before resistances.
  * Increasing your Intelligence increases the amount of Soulmasses. 
    * 20 Int = 3
    * 24 Int = 4
    * 31 Int = 5
  * Throwing an [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull") or casting [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") will cause the Soulmasses to not fire at you. However, they will still damage you if you touch them. The same goes for [Homing Crystal Soulmass](/Homing+Crystal+Soulmass "Dark Souls 3 Homing Crystal Soulmass") and [Affinity](/Affinity "Dark Souls 3 Affinity").
