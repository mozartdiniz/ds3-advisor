# Purging Stone

**Purging Stone** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in Dark Souls 3.

### Purging Stone  
  
---  
  

> Ash-colored stone encasing a skull.  
>   
> Reduces undead curse build-up and cures hollowing.  
>   
> Inhabitants of Londor, the land of Hollows, use this secret treasure to feign normalcy.  
>   
> Occasionally, a Hollow fools even himself, and turns on his own kind.  
  
### Purging Stone Effect

  * Reverses [hollowing](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") without curing [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil"). Upon reversing hollowing, player will revert to human apperance. 
  * Note that it does not remove the buildup of the [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") status effect, it only resets the hollowing counter to zero. The item description is actually referring to the same effect twice.
  * Purging Stones cannot be used while another player is in your world or you are in theirs.

### Purging Stone Locations

  * Purchasable from [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 4500 souls
  * 1x Found in the swamp area of the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital") (below the Dungeons), where the water is [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic") and water spiders lie in wait. Note that these spiders can also drop Purging Stones, making it possible to access in any case.
  * 3x Found in [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"), against the back wall inside the chapel in the toxic swamp.
  * Purchaseable from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 3000 souls after you give her [Hollow's Ashes](/Hollow's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Hollow's Ashes").
  * Purchaseable from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after you give her [Yuria´s Ashes](/Yuria's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Yuria's Ashes").

### Notes

  * If the player heals the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") after [Yoel](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") dies, [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") will no longer converse with the player, thus making it impossible to buy Purging Stones from her. (Historical note: This used to mean the player would have to remain hollow until finding a purging stone later in the game, but in the 1.03 patch, curing the Dark Sigil from the Firekeeper was amended to also remove hollowing, restoring humanity.)
  * If the player decides not to advance into NG+ after completing [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor")'s quest, Purging Stones will no longer be available for purchase from any NPC. 

### Trivia

  * These stones where originally dropped by [Man-Eater Shells](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Man-Eater+Shell "Dark Souls 3 Man-Eater Shell").
