# Ring of the Sun's First Born

### Ring of the Sun's First Born  
  
---  
  

> Ring of the Sun's first born, who inherited the light of Gwyn, the first lord.  
>   
> Greatly boosts miracles.  
>   
> The Sun's first born was once a god of war, until he was stripped of his stature as punishment for his foolishness. No wonder his very name has slipped from the annals of history.  
  
**Ring of the Sun's First Born** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun's First Born Effect

  * Boosts [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") damage by 20%.
  * Weight: 1.0

### How to Get Ring of the Sun's First Born in Dark Souls 3

  * Found at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"). From [Pontiff Sulyvahn's](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn") room, head back out towards the railing and fall straight onto the altar below. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=2h15m50s "Video") / )

### Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun's First Born Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Does not boost healing Miracles or buffing Miracles.
  * Stacks with [Morne's Ring](/Morne's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Morne's Ring")
  * Stacks with [Lightning Clutch Ring](/Lightning+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Clutch Ring") (for lightning Miracles) and [Dark Clutch Ring](/Dark+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dark Clutch Ring") (for dark Miracles)
  * Does not improve the bleed buildup of [Gnaw](/Gnaw "Dark Souls 3 Gnaw") or [Dorhys' Gnawing](/Dorhys'+Gnawing "Dark Souls 3 Dorhys' Gnawing"), but does improve the initial dark damage
  * This ring previously appeared in _Dark Souls_ as the [Ring of the Sun's Firstborn](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Ring+of+the+Sun's+Firstborn "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun's Firstborn").
