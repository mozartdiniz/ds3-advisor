# Soothing Sunlight

**Soothing Sunlight** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Soothing Sunlight  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 80  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 45 Faith  
**Type** | Heal  
  
> Special miracle granted to the maidens of the Princess of Sunlight.
> 
>   
> Restores a large amount of HP for self and those in the general area.
> 
>   
> The miracles of Gwynevere, the princess cherished by all, bestow their blessing on a great many warriors.

### How to Get Soothing Sunlight in Dark Souls 3

  * Crafted via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") from [Soul of the Dancer](/Soul+of+the+Dancer "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Dancer")

### Dark Souls 3 Soothing Sunlight Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * When the button is held down you can delay the healing. This is great for giving allies enough time to get within its range.
  * Area of effect extends roughly 15ft/5m in all directions from the caster.
  * Heals about 700% * Spell Buff. Tested at 1965 Hp; After activating Tears of Denial off a lethal fall in Firelink, a single cast healed for 1725 with a Yorshka's Chime +9 (Spell Buff: 245), at 60 Faith.
  * This spell should be the final goal for high level support builds. It's incredible range and high healing let it easily lift a near-dead ally to very high health.
  * Healing range is buffed by the [Sacred Chime of Filianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
