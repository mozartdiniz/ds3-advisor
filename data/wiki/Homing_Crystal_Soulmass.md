# Homing Crystal Soulmass

## Homing Crystal Soulmass  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 43  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 30 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Homing Crystal Soulmass** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sharpens Homing Soulmass via crystallization.  
>   
> Releases a homing crystal soulmass. Crystal soulmass has piercing qualities.  
>   
> According to the Crystal Sage, old Big Hat achieved enlightenment within the Regal Archives, where he came to find the quintessence of sorcery in the facets of a certain crystal. 

### How to Get Homing Crystal Soulmass in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 10,000 souls after obtaining the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Homing Crystal Soulmass Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Each crystal soulmass inflicts (Spell Buff x 0.65) magic damage before resistances.
  * The number of soulmasses depend on the user's Intelligence stat: 
    * 30 INT: 4 soulmasses
    * 31-99 INT: 5 soulmasses
  * Throwing an [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull") or casting [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") will cause the Soulmasses not to fire at you. However, they will still damage you if you touch them. The same goes for [Homing Soulmass](/Homing+Soulmass "Dark Souls 3 Homing Soulmass") and [Affinity](/Affinity "Dark Souls 3 Affinity").
