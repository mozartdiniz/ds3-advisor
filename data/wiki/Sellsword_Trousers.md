**Sellsword Trousers** is a Leg Armor Piece in Dark Souls 3. It is part of the [Sellsword Set](/Sellsword+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> These cloth trousers and leather boots are of no real note, but are durable and allow for easy movement.  
>   
> It is light considering the build, striking a fine balance between absorption and substance.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear of [Mercenary](/Mercenary).
  * [Road of Sacrife](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrife") in the area of the [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Black Knight") and [Farron Coal](/Farron+Coal "Farron Coal")
