**Vilhelm's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Armor") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "DLC").  
  
  
  
  

> The black (piece name) of Vilhelm, Knight of Londor, and a nice fit for any lean Hollow.  
>   
> This hollow knight, who served the three sisters who founded the Sable Church, was particularly loyal to the eldest, and was known as a cold-blooded hangman.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Painted World of Ariandel"): [Sir Vilhelm](/Sir+Vilhelm "Sir Vilhelm"), Knight of Londor's armor. His armor can be found in the crypt filled with bugs where you turn the crank handle to enable the [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Sister Friede") boss fight.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The ID of Vilhelm's Set once belonged to the Burial Knight Set, an armor set that was cut from the game.
