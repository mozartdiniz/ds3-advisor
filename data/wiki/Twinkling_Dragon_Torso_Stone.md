# Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone

**Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone** is a [Tool](/Tools "Dark Souls 3 Tools") in Dark Souls 3.

### Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone  
  
---  
  

> Stone imbued with the power of the everlasting dragons, offered to a towering dragon. This stone shows signs of a nascent light.  
>   
> Gain the torso of a dragon and roar alongside an archdragon mirage. The transformation is irreversible until death.  
>   
> Yet true imitation will require a dragon head, as well.  
  
### Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone Usage

  * Gain dragon torso until death Must remove all armor to use; Head, Chest, Arm, and Leg armor.
  * Reactivating the stone boosts attack damage for 5 seconds 
    * PVE = + 10% AR
    * PVP = additional modifier = 1.125*damage
  * Summons dragon arms in front of player character that crush enemies
  * Attribute bonus of D for Intelligence and C for Faith
  * Once the player is in dragon form, they gain elemental absorptions (except for lightning). The exact gains are: Magic and Dark absorption by 16.48, Fire absorption by 32.66, but **reduces** lightning absorption by 15.44. It also increases all Resistances by 60.

### Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone Location

  * Located at the very end of [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") by using the "Path of the Dragon" on the altar.

### Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone Notes & Trivia

  * Dragon Torso ability adds Damage buff, which lasts for around 6 seconds
  * Damage buff stacks with [Oath of Sunlight](/Oath+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Oath of Sunlight").
  * Consumes 102 stamina on use.
