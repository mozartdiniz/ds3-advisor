# Pyromancies

**Pyromancies** are a type of [Magic](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic") that deal primarily [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage") or provide resistance to damage, scaling with both Intelligence and Faith. The player can currently cast Pyromancies with all [Flames](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") and the [White Hair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman"). There are a total of 30 pyromancies. The minimum [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") and [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") needed to use all pre-DLC spells is 25 of both [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") and [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith"). The majority of pyromancies can be purchased after giving certain [Merchants](/Merchants "Dark Souls 3 Merchants") a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") known as a Tome.
