**Armor of the Sun Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3, worn originally by [Solaire of Astora](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Solaire+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Solaire of Astora").  
  
  
  

> "Large, durable iron helm, known as a heaume.
> 
> This helm with a red feather is said to have belonged to a Knight of Sunlight in a previous age.
> 
> The heaume has no particular powers, but is of fine quality, and appears to have been looked after with the greatest of care." (Helm)
> 
> "Chainmail armor and white coat featuring a large rendition of the holy symbol of the sun.
> 
> The choice attire of a singular Knight of Sunlight from a previous age. The symbol was painted by the knight himself, but the armor never bore any special power, sacred or otherwise." (Chest)
> 
> "Durable iron bracelets protecting the wearer's wrists." (Gauntlets)
> 
> "Chain leggings combined with iron boots." (Leggings)
> 
> "Said to be the bracelets of a Knight of Sunlight from a previous age.
> 
> Has no particular powers, but are of fine quality, and evidently well taken care of." (Gauntlets / Leggings) 

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Snuggly the Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") (Pickle-Pee). Buy the tower key at the Firelink Shrine, ascend the tower, and jump off the ledge to reach to the trading bird's nest. 
  * Conditions: 
    * Drop a [Siegbräu](/Siegbrau "Dark Souls 3 Siegbrau") at the crow nest to obtain the [Armor of the Sun](/Armor+of+the+Sun "Dark Souls 3 Armor of the Sun").
    * Drop a [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") at the crow nest to obtain the [Iron Bracelets](/Iron+Bracelets "Dark Souls 3 Iron Bracelets").
    * Drop a [Seed of a Giant Tree](/Seed+of+a+Giant+Tree "Dark Souls 3 Seed of a Giant Tree") at the crow nest to obtain the [Iron Leggings](/Iron+Leggings "Dark Souls 3 Iron Leggings").
    * Drop a [Lightning Urn](/Lightning+Urn "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Urn") at the crow nest to obtain the [Iron Helm](/Iron+Helm "Dark Souls 3 Iron Helm").
    * Drop a [Mendicant's Staff](/Mendicant's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Mendicant's Staff") at the crow nest to obtain the [Sunlight Shield](/Sunlight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Shield")

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This is the same armor set that was worn by Solaire of Astora in the first Dark Souls.
