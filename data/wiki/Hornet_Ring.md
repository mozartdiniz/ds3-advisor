# Hornet Ring

### Hornet Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring associated with the Lord's Blade Ciaran, one of the Four Knights of Gwyn, the First Lord.  
>   
> Boosts critical attacks.  
>   
> The masked Ciaran was the only woman to serve in Gwyn's Four Knights, and her curved sword granted a swift death to any and all enemies of the throne.  
  
**Hornet Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Hornet Ring Effect

  * Increases critical damage by 30%, as well as some changes to the Motion Values of certain ripostes and backstabs 
    * Motion Values are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used.
  * Physical damage types are only boosted by 17% in PvE. 
  * Dark damage as a whole does not benefit from this increase in PvP, however it works as it should in PvE.
  * Chaos infusions do NOT gain any bonus damage when compared to other infusions, the fact that Dark damage is bugged coupled with the generally high damage of Dark/Chaos weapons is what makes it a strong option. 
    * For example, if a Sharp Dagger gains more AR than a Chaos Dagger on a specific build, the Sharp Dagger will do more damage on riposte.
  * Weight: 1.1

### Where to Find Hornet Ring

  * Found at Dark Firelink Shrine after [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr") in the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves"). Keep right as you move up, the pathway is about level with the entrance to the Shrine. It is guarded by a Black Knight.
  * Only appears after [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") have been defeated.

### Notes

  * You must defeat the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") for the ring to appear.
  * On enemy players or NPC invaders this ring changes the riposte animation to 2-3 hits instead of just one, the overall damage increase seems to be higher than 30% in that case, needs confirmation.
  * Has no effect on enemies or bosses who cannot be backstabbed. This means only Abyss Watchers, Deacons of the Deep, Champion’s Gravetender, Sister Friede and Halflight, Spear of the Church take extra damage.

### Trivia

  * The ring is found in front of a tombstone with a Farron Greatsword leaning against it. The same location where [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") is praying, and later where his shield can be found, in Firelink Shrine.
