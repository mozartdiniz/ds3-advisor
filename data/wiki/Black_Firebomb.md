# Black Firebomb

**Black Firebomb** is a [Projectile](/Projectiles "Dark Souls 3 Projectiles") in Dark Souls 3.

### Black Firebomb  
  
---  
  

> More powerful bisque urn filled with special black powder. Explodes, inflicting great fire damage.  
>   
> Fire damage, which differs from physical damage, is highly effective against creatures of flesh, beasts, and other foes that might naturally have cause to fear the flame.  
  
### Black Firebomb Usage

  * Throw at enemies to deal fire damage.

### Black Firebomb Locations

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts").
  * Sold by [Unbreakable Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Dark Souls 3 Unbreakable Patches") for 300 Souls.
  * **3x** at [High Wall of Lothric:](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") on the rooftop before the long ladder down to the [Winged Knight](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight"). ()
  * **2x** at [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"): guarded by a group of slugs.

### Notes

  * Deals 140 base Fire damage.
  * Scales with an A in Strength and a C in Dexterity.
  * The damage can be boosted with the [Fire Clutch Ring](/Fire+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fire Clutch Ring").
  * The [Hawk Ring](/Hawk+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hawk Ring") boosts the distance the bomb can be thrown.
  * Can be traded for a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") with [Nestling](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
