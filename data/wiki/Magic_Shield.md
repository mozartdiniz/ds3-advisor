# Magic Shield

## Magic Shield  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirments | 10 Intelligence  
Duration | 60 Seconds  
  
**Magic Shield** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery for casters who wield swords.   
> Reinforces left shield with magic.   
>   
> Shields augmented by magic have higher stability and damage absorption.   
>   
> Even the smallest of shields can be toughened considerably with the use of this spell.

### How to Get Magic Shield in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor")/[Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 4,500 souls or [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 3,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases [Shield](/shields "Dark Souls 3 shields") Stability by 20%. 
    * Shields with 84 or more Stability will reach 100 Stability, resulting in no stamina loss from blocking attacks. 
      * [Greatshield of Glory](/Greatshield+of+Glory "Dark Souls 3 Greatshield of Glory") is the only shield capable of reaching this value with adequate reinforcement.
  * Increases damage absorption of shields by 70% for magic damage and 30% for all other damage types. 
    * Absorption increase is multiplicative with innate absorptions.
    * For example, a shield with 70% damage reduction allows you to receive 30% of incoming damage.  
While this spell is active, incoming damage is reduced from 30% to 21%. 
      * 1 - ((1 - 70%) * (1 - 30%)) = 79% absorption.
  * Lasts 60 seconds and is affected by [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"). 
    * With Lingering Dragoncrest Ring, duration increases to 78 seconds.
    * Lingering Dragoncrest Ring +1 = 81 seconds.
    * Lingering Dragoncrest Ring +2 = 84 seconds.
  * _[Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield") and [Great Magic Shield](/Great+Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Shield") CAN buff [Simple](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem") and [Blessed](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") Infused shields._
  * Can't be used with a catalyst in the left hand to buff a shield equipped in the right hand.
  * Counts as a [Shield Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") and will overwrite [Great Magic Shield](/Great+Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Shield").
  * The effect will dissipate if you two-hand your right weapon or switch the shield out. You can still buff your right-hand weapon with the use of resins.
  * Due to its low requirement and long duration, this spell can be useful on non-mage builds, particularly on parrying shields that normally have very low stability.
