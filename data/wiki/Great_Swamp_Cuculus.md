**Great Swamp Cuculus** is a summonable NPC in Dark Souls 3.

## Great Swamp Cuculus Information

_"Great Swamp Cuculus is a summon for the 'Old Demon King' Bossfight"_

  * Can be killed by enemies.
  * If Cuculus survives the fight with [Old Demon King](/Old+Demon+King "Dark Souls 3 Old Demon King"), the [Spotted Whip](/Spotted+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Spotted Whip") and [Cornyx's Set](/Cornyx's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx's Set") appears in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") where Cornyx was previously located.

### Location

  * You can find Great Swamp Cuculus' summon sign at [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"), just before the [Old Demon King](/Old+Demon+King "Dark Souls 3 Old Demon King") boss gate.
  * Cuculus can be summoned to help for the Demon Ruins if the player does not enter the boss room. Be aware that she doesn't have a large health pool, and she also doesn't know how to use ladders. 

### Combat Information

  * Attacks with a whip and with Pyromancies.
  * She will cast [Poison Mist](/Poison+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Poison Mist"), [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat") and [Great Chaos Fire Orb](/Great+Chaos+Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Great Chaos Fire Orb") (needs confirmation).
  * Strong against fire damage.

### Trivia

  * It is thought that Cuculus is Corynx's pyromancy master or apprentice, which would explain why after summoning Cuculus a body appears at Corynx's cage where he is found in the undead settlement.
  * This is reinforced by the fact that they are both Pyromancers of the Great Swamp, which makes a mentor connection even more possible.
  * Cuculus is a latin name for cuckoos, a type of bird that lays their eggs in other birds nests to raise their young.
