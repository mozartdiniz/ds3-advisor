# Cell Key

**Cell Key** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Cell Key  
  
---  
  

> Key to a cell holding thieves and the like. There is no shortage of brash thieves in Lothric and these particular thieves likely scaled the wall from the Undead Settlement. But they are only willing to practice their thievery on the High Wall, for their fear of Lothric Castle, rumored to devour men, keeps them clear of its grounds.  
  
### Cell Key Usage

  * Opens the cell to Greairat in the Highwall of Lothric, at the bottom of The Tower on the Wall bonfire.

### Cell Key Locations

  * Found inside of the workshop after the the [Lothric Knight](/Lothric+Knight "Lothric Knight") with a greatshield, follow the stairs at the end of the hall down to the base floor. After you have gotten to the base floor look for a flight of stairs a [Halberd](/Halberd "Halberd") weilding [Hollow](/Large+Hollow+Soldier "Hollow") is going up and down, it will be at the bottom with a [Starved Hound](/Starved+Hound "Starved Hound") guarding it. 
  * [Item Location Video Guide](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=59m19s)
  * [Cell Key Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VxpUYGc83Kk)

### Notes

  * Greirat will give you a Blue Tearstone Ring to give to Lorreta if you accept his request. He will then move to the Firelink Shrine if you accept his service.
