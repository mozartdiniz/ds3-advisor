### Build Summary

This build is an incredible stand-alone character as well as one that could easily venture into **New Game+**. It is equipped with a swift and reliable [Katana](/Katanas "Katana") which has the most durability within its respected weapon class. Although this weapon may not have the longest range, the [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") possesses great base damage along with the benefit of the [Refined Gem](/Refined+Gem "Refined Gem") upgrade path allowing it to scale off your ambidextrous character. For threats that are just out of range, the build is equipped with both the [Throwing Knife](/Throwing+Knife "Throwing Knife") and [Kukri](/Kukri "Kukri"), which allows you to keep the pressure on your opponent while fighting. If you happen to come across a person who uses a shield, then the [Dragonrider Bow](/Dragonrider+Bow "Dragonrider Bow") is perfect for the situation as it has a [Weapon Art](/Skills "Weapon Art") specifically designed to pierce both armor and shields alike. 

### Build Strengths

  * Excellent offensive gameplay in offline and online situations
  * Build can utilize three playstyles: _Offensive, Defensive, Supportive_
  * Build can deal damage at all ranges - [ **Close | Middle | Long** ]
  * Build has great physical defense - [ **Strike | Slash | Thrust** ]
  * Build has moderate spell defense - [ [Magic](/Magic+Defense "Magic") ]
  * Emphasive _damage-per-hit_ rather than _damage-over-time_
  * Cramped locations do not complicate swing animation
  * Charged attack with this weapon is high-priority, thus ensuring damage upon trading blows
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") has wide horizontal slashes, along with vertical attacks, thus guaranteeing diversity in approach
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") may be engulfed in pyromancy
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") may be enchanted with sorcery
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") may be buffed with resins
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") has auxiliary [Skill](/Skills "Skill") \- [ [Hold](/Hold "Hold") ]
  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") has auxiliary [Status Effect](/Status+Effects "Status Effect") \- [ [Bleed](/Bleed "Bleed") ]
  * [Dragonrider Bow](/Dragonrider+Bow "Dragonrider Bow") has auxilary [Skill](/Skills "Skill") \- [ [Puncture](/Puncture "Puncture") ]
  * [Dragonrider Bow](/Dragonrider+Bow "Dragonrider Bow") has auxilary damage - [ [Dark](/Dark+Arrow "Dark") | [Magic](/Moonlight+Arrow "Magic") ]
  * [Pyromancy Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Pyromancy Flame") has auxiliary [Skill](/Skills "Skill") \- [ [Combustion](/Combustion "Combustion") ]
  * [Pyromancy Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Pyromancy Flame") has auxiliary damage - [ **Fire** ]

### Build Weaknesses

  * [Black Blade](/Black+Blade "Black Blade") has very short reach compared to similar weapons within its respected katana class
  * [Dragonrider Bow](/Dragonrider+Bow "Dragonrider Bow") has slow draw speed compared to similar weapons within its respected bow class
  * Build has low elemental defence - [ [Fire](/Fire+Defense "Fire") | [Lightning](/Lightning+Defense "Lightning") | [Dark](/Dark+Defense "Dark") ]
  * Build has semi-decent resistance - [ [Bleed](/Bleed "Bleed") | [Poison](/Poison "Poison") | [Frost](/Frostbite "Frost") | [Curse](/Curse "Curse") ]

### Side Notes

  1. While using this build, it is recommended that you upgrade your equipment to maximum for the best quality experience.
  2. While using this build, it is recommended to always have atleast 5,000 souls to resupply consumables after each encounter.
  3. While using this build, the "**Item Section** " is all preference so feel free to change it as you play.
  4. While using this build, remember to have all updates and expansions downloaded and fully installed prior to playing.
  5. While using this build, enjoy the fact that you are cosplaying as my original character Alexander.
