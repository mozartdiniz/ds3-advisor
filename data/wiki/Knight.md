**Knight** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , ideal for newcomers to the series due to its balanced stats, durable armor, and reliable starting equipment. Boasting the highest starting vitality, the Knight can effectively wield heavier gear while maintaining mobility.

With a 100% physical defense shield and a well-rounded weapon, this class excels in both offense and defense. While its Attunement, Faith, and Intelligence stats are modest, they allow for future use of basic spells like Heal Aid or Soul Arrow with minimal investment. Starting at level 9, the Knight offers a strong foundation for players seeking a versatile and resilient build.

> An obscure knight of poor renown who collapsed roaming the land. Sturdy, owing to high vitality and stout armor.

  * [Knight Armor](/Knight+Armor "Dark Souls 3 Knight Armor")
  * [Knight Gauntlets](/Knight+Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Knight Gauntlets")
  * [Knight Leggings](/Knight+Leggings "Dark Souls 3 Knight Leggings")

### Dark Souls 3 Knight Notes and Tips

  * This class has the highest starting vitality to hold its heavier armor.
  * Attunement, Faith, and Intelligence stats are average enough to use spells later on.
  * Starts off with one spell slot and with 1 more point into Faith or Intelligence can use Heal Aid or Soul Arrow respectively (If you buy the sorcerer's staff or talisman from the shrine maiden).\
  * A recommended class for new players of the franchise, due to the 100% physical defense [shield](/Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Knight Shield"), good weapon, and good armor.
