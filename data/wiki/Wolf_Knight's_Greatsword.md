**Wolf Knight's Greatsword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Greatsword of a knight tainted by the dark of the Abyss, and master of the wolf's blood of Farron.   
>   
> The wolf knight was the first Abyss Watcher, and his sword is more punishing against creations of the abyss.   
>   
> Skill: Wolf Sword   
> While in stance, use normal attack for a low spinning slash, or the strong attack to leap forward in a vertically-slashing somersault.

### Wolf Knight's Greatsword Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Buffed or Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").
  * Wolf Knight's Greatsword +5 has **523** AR at 40 Strength and 40 Dexterity.
  * Does 20% bonus damage against Abyssal enemies: 
    * [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith")
    * [Deep Accursed](/Deep+Accursed "Dark Souls 3 Deep Accursed")
    * [Harald Legion Knight](/Harald+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Knight")
    * [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man")
    * [Rotten Flesh](/Rotten+Flesh+of+Aldrich "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Flesh of Aldrich")
    * [Iron Dragonslayer](/Iron+Dragonslayer "Dark Souls 3 Iron Dragonslayer")
  * Does 20% bonus damage against Abyssal bosses: 
    * [High Lord Wolnir](/High+Lord+Wolnir "Dark Souls 3 High Lord Wolnir")
    * [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods")
    * [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour")
    * [Darkeater Midir](/Darkeater+Midir "Dark Souls 3 Darkeater Midir")
  * The Greatsword of [Knight Artorias](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Artorias+the+Abysswalker "Dark Souls 3 Artorias the Abysswalker"), an important character and boss from the original _Dark Souls_.
  * The blade appears to be corrupted by the Abyss, having only one sharpened and uncorrupted side.
  * Unlike in _Dark Souls 2_ , wielding this weapon in your left hand does not grant a unique moveset.
  * The spin move of the weapon art can be parried.
  * Interestingly, the inventory icon resembles the [Greatsword of Artorias](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Greatsword+of+Artorias "Dark Souls 3 Greatsword of Artorias") from the original game, but its in-game model more closely resembles its damaged counterpart, the [Abyss Greatsword](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Abyss+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Greatsword").
  * The Wolf Knight's Greatsword has a range of 35.4179xl on the 1h R1 and 37.8598xl on the 2h R1.

### Softcaps & Scaling:

  * 24/18 = 375 AR = Base Stats
  * 40/18 = 445 AR = Str Softcap
  * 24/40 = 454 AR = Dex Softcap
  * 40/40 = 523 AR = Quality Build

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
