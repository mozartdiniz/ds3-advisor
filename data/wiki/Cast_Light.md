# Cast Light

## Cast Light

**Spell Type** | 

#### [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
  
**Focus Cost  
** | 20  
******Slots Used  
** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence  
  
**Cast Light** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Lost Sorcery from Oolacile, land of ancient golden Sorceries. Casts a light to illuminate surroundings.   
>   
> This light producing magic is elementary, but nonetheless demonstrates the pure nature of golden sorceries;   
> the likes of which have not been developed even at the Dragon School.

### How to Get Cast Light in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 1,000 souls after giving him the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Cast Light Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * The spell is the Sorcery equivalent of the [Weapon Art](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") [Guiding Light](/Guiding+Light "Dark Souls 3 Guiding Light") and the [Torch](/Torch "Dark Souls 3 Torch"). However, the Torch has the advantage of burning off leeches and Guiding Light provides additional messages left by the developers. Cast Light could make up for this by being hands free, however there aren't many dark areas in Dark Souls 3 where such things would be necessary.
  * Lasts for 1 minute.
