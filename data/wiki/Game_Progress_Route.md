# Game Progress Route

**Game Progress Route** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** features the recommended progression course that focuses on the main campaign of **Dark Souls 3**. The progression route for **Dark Souls 3** provides you with important information and a summarized guide to avoid missing any important factors such as [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs"), [Boss](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") encounters, important [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items"), rare equipment, and many more. This page only contains an overview of the game's main quests that will lead the player to reach the end of the game. For a detailed and thorough guide on fully completing the game, please visit the [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") page.

### [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash")

Recommended Levels: Any

  1. Acquire [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask")
  2. Defeat [Ravenous Crystal Lizard](/Ravenous+Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Ravenous Crystal Lizard")
  3. Defeat [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr")

The [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash") serves as the game's tutorial section and is rather straightforward. After awakening and gaining control of your character, familiarize yourself with the controls and take note of the red marks on the ground scattered about. These are messages that offer hints on game mechanics and controls, which you will encounter quite often. Some are also left by other players. The graveyard is patrolled by [Grave Wardens](/Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Grave Warden") who will serve as your introduction to the game's combat, and there are a few consumables you can collect along the way, including several [Soul Items](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls").

Very early on, you will encounter a fountain where you can find the [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask"), used to recover your FP. Go to the narrow pathway to the right where you will encounter a [Ravenous Crystal Lizard](/Ravenous+Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Ravenous Crystal Lizard"). It will drop a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") upon defeat. Return to the fountain and press on towards a hilltop where the first bonfire can be found, overlooking some ruins in the distance. This is your goal. Explore the area some more, defeat more wardens and acquire items. The 5x [Firebombs](/Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Firebomb") located down the hill to the right can prove useful for the upcoming boss fight. Once ready, make your way towards the ruins where you will find the first boss, [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr"), kneeling in the center of the arena. Pull the sword buried in his chest to begin the fight. Upon his defeat, you will receive the [Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword") you pulled from him and you can then proceed through the large set of doors in the back to the hillside path leading to the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Be sure to explore the hillside for some useful items.

### [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine")

Recommended Levels: Any

  1. Enter the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and speak to the inhabitants.
  2. Speak to [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") to receive the Hurrah! [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") (Optional)
  3. Speak to [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").
  4. Speak to [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper").
  5. Speak to [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") to receive the Collapse [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") (Optional)
  6. Embed the [Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword") in the center to light the bonfire.
  7. Explore the Bell Tower (Optional)
  8. Defeat the Sword Master (Optional)
  9. Warp to the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").

The [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") serves as your main hub and safe refuge to return to during your adventures. While mostly empty on your first visit, you will eventually find several friendly [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") to recruit to your cause and add to the shrine's inhabitants. For now, speak to the ones that are here.

You can find [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") down the stairs to the right from the entrance, from whom you can obtain the **Collapse** [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). The [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") stands right in the center and she will assist you with leveling up, by turning in your hard-earned Souls. She will also inform you about the next part of your journey. In the back, right under the main entrance is a corridor where you can find the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid"). She sells an assortment of useful items, including [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") and [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") and her stock will expand as you progress in the game. In the very back of this corridor is [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre"), who can assist you with upgrading your [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") and [Estus Flask](/Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Estus Flask"). Speaking to him for the first time also awards the **Hurrah!** [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). Back in the main hall, you can also speak to [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") who is sitting on the second throne from the left.

To progress forward, you can plant the [Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword") in the center of the room to create a new Bonfire, from which you can warp to other locations. The only one available at the moment is the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric") which is your next goal.

Do note that there are still certain things you can find at the Shrine's exterior:

Access the nearby belltower by purchasing the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key") (20,000 souls) from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid"). The tower leads to some very useful items including an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard"), [Estus Ring](/Estus+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Estus Ring"), [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone"), [Fire Keeper Soul](/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul"), the [Fire Keeper Set](/Fire+Keeper+Set "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Set"), the [Covetous Silver Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Silver+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Silver Serpent Ring"), and a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). Additionally you can access [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") on the roof or inside the rafters of Firelink to exchange items. (It is possible to access part of the roof and get the Estus Shard, Homeward Bone, and Covetous Silver Serpent Ring without the key by jumping on the roof from a nearby tree).

You can also find the [Sword Master](/Sword+Master "Dark Souls 3 Sword Master") on the path to the left outside of the Shrine's entrance. Defeating him awards the [Uchigatana](/Uchigatana "Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana") and his [Armor Set](/Master's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Master's Set"). Leveling up before engaging him is recommended.

Once you're ready, warp to [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").

### [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric")

Recommended Levels: 1-22

  1. Light the first Bonfire.
  2. Pick up the [Longbow](/Longbow "Dark Souls 3 Longbow") (Optional)
  3. Defeat the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") for a [Deep Battle Axe](/Deep+Battle+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Deep Battle Axe") (Optional)
  4. Light the second Bonfire.
  5. Traverse the rooftops and kill the [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard")
  6. Collect the [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") and [Cell Key](/Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Cell Key") (Andre's quest progression)
  7. Free [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement")
  8. Proceed to the Cathedral and meet [Emma](/Emma "Dark Souls 3 Emma") to obtain the [Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue "Dark Souls 3 Way of Blue") Covenant.
  9. Defeat [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Vordt of the Boreal Valley").
  10. Raise the [Small Lothric Banner](/Small+Lothric+Banner "Dark Souls 3 Small Lothric Banner") to get transported to the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").

Exit the room you arrive in via portal from the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and head down the flight of stairs just ahead to light the first Bonfire. From here, the path down the circular stairs to the right leads to a dead end at a battlement at the end of the ramparts, where you can pick up a [Longbow](/Longbow "Dark Souls 3 Longbow") and some arrows. There is also a locked door down and to the left of the ramparts which you can ignore for now. You can also skip this part entirely and head left from the Bonfire instead.

The left path is also a section of ramparts, this time leading to a building with a dead dragon draped over the side of the entrance. To the left, you can climb the battlements to find another building where you can find a pair of [Binoculars](/Binoculars "Dark Souls 3 Binoculars"). Head inside the building under the dragon, and clear the enemies inside. Climb down the ladder to the lower room and exit through the side.

In the next section is a staircase on the left leading to a tower and a walkway on the right. Both are littered with enemies but a dragon will swoop down and perch on the tower as you approach the staircase. Allow it to burn the enemies before you move forward. Once the area is clear, run up the stairs between the wyvern's breaths and quickly enter the tower. Inside, head down the bottom floor and defeat the [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic") disguised as a chest to obtain a [Deep Battle Axe](/Deep+Battle+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Deep Battle Axe") or skip it and climb the ladder to the opposite end, and open the gate.

Enter the building ahead and exit through the door on the right to some stairs leading up to the roof where the second Bonfire can be lit. Head back into the building and go down to the middle level, looking for an exit onto the ramparts. Take the ladder down to some rooftops and look around for a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") to obtain a [Raw Gem](/Raw+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Raw Gem"). From here, there are plenty of paths to take with many items to discover, but only a few are essential. Take the ladder nearby down to the side of the building and enter it. Make your way down to the bottom floor dining area of sorts, clear the area and look for an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") resting on an anvil, and a set of steps around the corner leading to a storage area where you can pick up a [Cell Key](/Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Cell Key").

Make your way back to the tower with the second Bonfire and head to its lower levels to find a cell you can unlock with the key, freeing [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") who will move to the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). This also begins his first quest as he gives you a ring to hand over to a woman named Loretta in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). Return to the rooftops and climb down to the plaza below where the [Winged Knight](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight") patrols. To the right is a path that leads to an elevator where you can unlock a shortcut to the right-side path of the first Bonfire. Continue on down and to the left towards the Cathedral building to meet [Emma, High Priestess of Lothric Castle](/Emma "Dark Souls 3 Emma") and obtain the [Small Lothric Banner](/Small+Lothric+Banner "Dark Souls 3 Small Lothric Banner"), as well as the [Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue "Dark Souls 3 Way of Blue") Covenant. 

Exit the cathedral and head in the opposite direction, down the steps and through the gates to begin the boss battle against [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Vordt of the Boreal Valley"). Defeating him grants the [Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt](/Soul+of+Boreal+Valley+Vordt "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt"). Light the Bonfire here and proceed ahead towards the edge of the cliffs overlooking the settlements and raise the banner Emma gave you to get transported by Gargoyles to the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").

### [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement")

Recommended Levels: 22-35

  1. Speak to [Yoel](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor")
  2. Speak to [Yoel](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") at the Firelink Shrine for the Beckon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") (Yoel's quest progression)
  3. Accept Yoel's offer to Draw Out True Strength (Yoel's quest progression)
  4. Speak to [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") after obtaining 5 [Dark Sigils](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") for the Dignified Bow [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures")
  5. Give [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's Bone") to [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") to keep the [Blue Tearstone Ring](/Blue+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Blue Tearstone Ring") (Greirat's quest progression)
  6. Obtain [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") (Andre's quest progression)
  7. Find the [Warriors of Sunlight](/Warriors+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warriors of Sunlight") covenant
  8. Find the [Mound-Makers](/Mound-Makers "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Makers") covenant
  9. Speak to [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp")
  10. Speak to [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") and [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim")
  11. Obtain [Mortician's Ashes](/Mortician's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Mortician's Ashes") (Shrine Handmaid's quest progression)
  12. Obtain [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") (Firelink Shrine Bonefire progression)
  13. Purchase the [Grave Key](/Grave+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grave Key")
  14. Find [Siegward](/Siegward "Dark Souls 3 Siegward"), defeat the Fire Demon
  15. Defeat [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood")
  16. Speak to [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") to receive the [Heavy Gem](/Heavy+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Gem") (Hawkwood's quest progression)
  17. Obtain [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb") (Leonhard's quest progression)
  18. Obtain Applause [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") (Leonhard's quest progression)

You will be dropped off at the top of a battlement. Light the first Bonfire and follow the long staircase down the ramparts towards the gate of the settlement. The portcullis will rise briefly to let three hounds out who will kill the Hollows in the area. Clear them and then turn around with the gate to your back. Head down this path towards a destroyed bridge where dozens of dead pilgrims are. Look for one still living on the right side and speak with him. This is [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor"). Speak with him to recruit him and he will move to the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").

Return to the portcullis and pull the lever to the left to open it. Head through the gates and light the second Bonfire inside the small hut just ahead. Go back to the main path and head towards the large building. Inside, make your way to the back and head down the stairs. Go to the balcony and cut down the hanging corpse, you will need to loot it later for [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's Bone"). Go back into the building and continue heading down until you exit the building. Once outside, pick up [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's Bone") on the left. Clear the enemies congregating by the large tree surrounded by burning corpses. There is an [Evangelist](/Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Evangelist") praying right next to it and behind her is an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard").

Continue on to the large building behind the burning tree to the left. Inside, look for a hole in the floor on the right side which leads to a hidden room where you can obtain the [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") Covenant. Take note of the pot of [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup") in this room.

Return to the burning tree and head to the path on the right this time, crossing the wooden bridge towards the stables. Clear the stables and open the double doors in the back to find a stone bridge on your left. Before progressing further, return to the entrance of the stables and take the narrow path to its right side. You will eventually come across a ladder to take to the roofs. Look for a hilly section on the right side that you can drop onto, where a [Hollow Manservant](/Hollow+Manservant "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Manservant") with an empty cage on its back is patrolling. This hollow is non-hostile and you can approach it from the back to get transported to a pit where you can speak to an NPC who will give you the [Mound-Makers](/Mound-Makers "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Makers") Covenant. Before doing so, look for a tree with a hanging corpse and cut it down with a ranged attack. You can pick the item up later on. In the pit, pick up the covenant and the [Wargod Wooden Shield](/Wargod+Wooden+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Wargod Wooden Shield") to the side, then use the [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") the NPC gave you to get back to the last bonfire. Note that you can also offer up [Vertebra Shackles](/Vertebra+Shackle "Dark Souls 3 Vertebra Shackle") at the altar to increase your rank in the Mound-Makers Covenant, if you have any.

Find your way back to the stables and to the stone bridge ahead of it. Make a quick run across the bridge before the Hollows start throwing bombs at you, and drop down to the ledge on the right side near the end. Enter the building ahead and light the third Bonfire. Exit through the other side of the building and climb up and around it, defeating the Hollows along the way. Continue along the wooden walkways to find a caged NPC named [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp"). Speak to him and he will teleport to the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") where he can teach you [Pyromancy](/Pyromancy "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy").

  * Get access to the sewers (requires [Grave Key](/Grave+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grave Key") or [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook")) and visit [Velka the Goddess of Sin](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Velka the Goddess of Sin") to cleanse yourself of commited sins. Rescue [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") from her cell and speak with [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim").
  * Help [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") defeat the Demon.
  * Befriend the Giant (who will assist you again later) or kill him for the [Hawk Ring](/Hawk+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hawk Ring"). 
  * Grab the [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") from a corpse in the area where the Giant is shooting arrows. It is on the right-most piece of land and requires you to roll/jump over a small gap onto it.
  * Grab the [Mortician's Ashes](/Mortician's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Mortician's Ashes") from a corpse in the area where the Giant is shooting arrows. It is beside the path you take from the  _Dilapidated Bridge_ bonfire. Make a U-Turn through the trees and keep running forward.
  * Defeat [Curse-rotted Greatwood](/Curse-rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-rotted Greatwood") to acquire the [Transposing Kiln](/Transposing+Kiln "Dark Souls 3 Transposing Kiln"). 

### [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices")

Recommended Levels: 30-45

  1. Obtain [Braille Divine Tome of Carim](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim") for [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") ( Irina's quest progression)
  2. Speak to [Anri](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") and [Horace](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed")
  3. Obtain the [Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome](/Great+Swamp+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome") (Cornyx quest progression)
  4. Obtain [Farron Coal](/Farron+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Farron Coal") (Andre's quest progression)
  5. Obtain [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") (Andre's quest progression)
  6. Obtain [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim")
  7. Defeat [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage")

Find [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") and [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed") at the first bonfire at the ruins and speak to them, speaking to Horace will grant you the [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") Covenant. Continue exploring the ruins until you find a broken stone archway, a bonfire can be found at one of the platforms by a pillar. By the corner past the bonfire on the left, you can loot an [Estus shard](/Estus+shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus shard") and an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") on a corpse by a fire. Make your way to the next structure past the stone bridge, take the stairs down. Continue this path until you reach an archway opening and find [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim"), you can speak with him but he also acts as a merchant who sells you [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"). You can start his questline, but you must have 10 intelligence to progress it.

Continue exploring and make your way to the swamp, there will be several crab enemies nearby and one large crab near the entrance of Farron Keep that drops the [Great Swamp Ring](/Great+Swamp+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Ring"). By the ruins in the swamp, there is a large tree and at the base of the tree you can acquire the [Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome](/Great+Swamp+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome") and the [Conjurator Set](/Conjurator+Set "Dark Souls 3 Conjurator Set"). In the ruins, you will encounter the [Black Knight](/Black+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight") who wields the [Black Knight Greatsword](/Black+Knight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Greatsword"). After defeating it, you can pick up [Farron Coal](/Farron+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Farron Coal") towards the end.

Back at the ruins where you can summon Eygon, head to the path going up to the tall doorway to reach the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") boss arena. Defeat the Crystal Sage and a bonfire can be lit here.

### [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep")

Recommended Levels: 30-45

  1. Extinguish 3 ritual fires
  2. Obtain [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal") in the structure to the left of the first Farron Keep (Andre's quest progression)
  3. Obtain [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") (Andre's quest progression)
  4. Obtain the [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll") (Orbeck's quest progression)
  5. Obtain the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll") and give it to Orbeck **before beating the Abyss Watchers**(Orbeck's quest progression)
  6. Speak with the [Old Wolf of Farron](/Old+Wolf+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf of Farron") for the [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron") covenant (and Legion Etiquette [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"))
  7. Obtain [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes") (Shrine Handmaid's quest progression)
  8. Give [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes") to [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") and speak to [Sirris](/Sirris "Dark Souls 3 Sirris") at the Firelink Shrine to receive the Darkmoon Loyalty [Gestures](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") (Sirris' quest progression)
  9. Give the Pale Tongue to Rosia to be able to summon [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") **(Doing so will lock you out of Sirris' questline)** (Yellowfinger Heysel's quest progression)
  10. Defeat the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers")
     1. Summoning [Black Hand Gotthard](/Black+Hand+Gotthard "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Gotthard") for this fight will reward you with the By My Sword [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures")
     2. Summoning [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") for this fight will reward you with the Duel Bow [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures")

While in the swamp at the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"), you will need to find 3 ritual fires and extinguish them to open the Farron Keep Perimeter Gate. To the left of the first bonfire, you will encounter a [Darkwraith](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith"), nearby is a stone tower with a corpse, you can loot the body to obtain the [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal"). Towards the right of the first bonfire, continue the path until you reach a shore that leads up to a cavern where you can obtain the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll").

In the center of the swamp, you will find an island with a structure in the middle. There will be several poisonous leeches that surround a corpse that contains [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard"). Defeat them and loot the body. Around the swamp, you will find a structure. Take the ladder up and you will find a bonfire as well as the [Old Wolf of Farron](/Old+Wolf+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf of Farron"), interact with the Old Wolf of Farron to join the [Watchdogs of Farron Covenant](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron") and receive the Legion Etiquette [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). Head back out to the other path from the structure and there will be a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), defeat the lizard, and pick up the [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite"), hit the wall by the lizard to open a path to obtain the [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes"). There is a pressure plate to step on to activate a lift that takes you to the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon") mini-boss room, defeat it to obtain the [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Stray Demon").

Return to the swamp, make your way to the burning flames, and pick up a [Titanite Shard](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard"), continue following the flames to encounter the [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru"). After defeating it, 2 [Titanite Shards](/Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Shard") can be obtained by looting a corpse from a nearby tree. Keep exploring the swamp to reach a raised mound where you can pick up a [Sunlight Talisman](/Sunlight+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Talisman") and [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup"). Find a small island to find an altar where you can extinguish a ritual flame, continue across the narrow bridge where you will encounter 2 [Ghru Grunts](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt") and a [Ghru Shaman](/Ghru+Shaman "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Shaman"). After defeating them, continue past them to find the Keep Ruins Bonfire.

Around the swamp, you will find a giant crab that drops the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") when defeated, the [Giant of the Undead Settlement](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement") will attempt to target you if you have not befriended him, but will fire at the giant crab if you have. After defeating the giant crab, in the corner in the same area, there is a cluster of dead mushrooms and larger mushrooms by a tree. There you can obtain the [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll"). Follow the wall through the swamp, you will encounter 3 [Elder Ghru](/Elder+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Elder Ghru") protecting a [Poison Gem](/Poison+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Poison Gem"), defeat them to obtain the [Black Bow of Pharis](/Black+Bow+of+Pharis "Dark Souls 3 Black Bow of Pharis") and [Pharis's Hat](/Pharis's+Hat "Dark Souls 3 Pharis's Hat"). Beside the fallen bridge, you can find an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") with a [Rotten Slug](/Rotten+Slug "Dark Souls 3 Rotten Slug") nearby, and a body that's wedged between a structure and some rocks contains the [Nameless Knight Set](/Nameless+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Nameless Knight Set").

In the next area, you will find a large ruined area surrounded by trees that contains the Farron Keep Perimeter Bonfire, if you progressed [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor")'s questline, there will be a summon for [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") that will give you the Duel Bow [Gestures](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").

  2. Speak with [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") to join the Rosaria's Finger Covenant

Make your way back up the stairs until you reach doorway to the left, pick up the Ember on a corpse near the closed gate. ### [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") (Optional)

Recommended Levels: 40-55

  1. Obtain [Undead Bone Shards](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard")
  2. Obtain [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring")
  3. Light the Old King's Antechamber [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires")
  4. Obtain the [Izalith Pyromancy Tome](/Izalith+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Izalith Pyromancy Tome")
  5. Obtain the [Quelana Pyromancy Tome](/Quelana+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome")
  6. Defeat [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed")

When you reach Smouldering Lake, a ballista will target you as you explore the swamp until you disable it. Follow the path on the left to encounter a [Carthus Sandworm](/Carthus+Sandworm "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Sandworm"), you can use the ballista to take out the worm and it will drop a [Lightning Stake](/Lightning+Stake "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Stake") and an [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard"). Towards the right, there is a hill where you can find a doorway to a staircase that leads to the Demon Ruins Bonfire. Head back up to a man-made stone wall so that the ballista can shoot at it, you will be able to obtain the [Speckled Stoneplate Ring](/Speckled+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Speckled Stoneplate Ring").

From the Demon Ruins Bonfire, continue down the stairs to the next area. Take out the enemies in this area and continue exploring. Through the right side of the hallway, you will encounter a few enemies you've encountered in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"). Take out the enemies and continue the path until you reach a hole in the ground, across is a body that contains the [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard"). If you manage to jump across, pick up the Estus Shard and continue down the hallway, take the path on the right and attack the illusory wall at the end of the hallway. Enter the path to find the Old King's Antechamber Bonfire.

Exit the bonfire room through the door opposite the illusory wall, and continue straight down the path into a room where you will encounter a Ghru. Inside the room, there is a corpse that you can loot to obtain the [Izalith Pyromancy Tome](/Izalith+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Izalith Pyromancy Tome"). Backtrack to the bonfire room and exit through the doorway that leads to the staircase going down, make your way down and follow the hallway to the end and hit the wall to reveal an illusory wall. Enter the room, defeat the Black Knight and loot the [Black Knight Sword](/Black+Knight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Sword"). Into the next room with several enemies, defeat them and obtain the [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard"). Enter the doorway in the corner and follow the hallway to find a room with small rats, hit the wall to reveal an illusory wall, head in and on the left to obtain the [Quelana Pyromancy Tome](/Quelana+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome").

On a stone wall, take the few ladders up, head into the room, and follow the path on the left to find a room with several skeletons, defeat the skeletons, and make your way to disable the ballista. Make your way down the path behind the ballista and drop down until you reach the water. Head left and continue down that path into an open area where you find [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed"), confront him and you will have to kill him and loot the [Llewellyn Shield](/Llewellyn+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Llewellyn Shield"). After this, you can return to Anri and tell her what happened.

Back at the main lake, head to the fog at the stairs past the worm. You may summon the [Great Swamp Cuculus](/Great+Swamp+Cuculus "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Cuculus") to be able to obtain the [Spotted Whip](/Spotted+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Spotted Whip") or the [Cornyx Armor set](/Cornyx's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx's Set"). Enter the fog to take on the [Old Demon King](/Old+Demon+King "Dark Souls 3 Old Demon King"), defeat him and you can light the bonfire.

### [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")

Recommended Levels: 55-65

  1. Summon [Sirris of the Sunless Realms](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") to defeat [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer")

  6. Complete [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora")'s questline

When you get across, there will be a bonfire to the right for you to light. Head up the stairs on the right and continue down the path until you reach a short staircase where you find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). Hit the railing of the staircase to reveal a hidden staircase, make your way down the stairs and continue up head to find an Evangelist, defeat it to obtain [Dorhy's Gnawing](/Dorhy's+Gnawing "Dark Souls 3 Dorhy's Gnawing"). Nearby is a gate to unlock the shortcut back to the first bonfire.

Continue up the path and you will be able to obtain a [Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Large+Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier") from a corpse and the [Church of Yorshka](/Church+of+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Church of Yorshka") bonfire past it. Go towards the left first and continue this path to find an altar, hit the wall behind the altar to reveal an illusory wall where you can obtain the [Magic Clutch Ring](/Magic+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring"). Return to the bonfire and speak with [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") to progress her questline and obtain the Quiet Resolve [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"). In the corner, there is an NPC that is camouflaged as a statue and want to assassinate Anri. Killing this NPC will save Anri and reward you with [Chameleon](/Chameleon "Dark Souls 3 Chameleon") but ending [Yuria of Londor's](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") questline. Past the doorway, take the stairs down until you reach the water to the next area.

On this body of water, towards the underpass is a bonfire and the entrance to [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") and towards the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"). Take the stairs into a room to obtain [Estus Soup](/Estus+Soup "Dark Souls 3 Estus Soup") in a cauldron, and keep the soup for [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina")'s questline, he can be found in the same room if you have been progressing through his questline. A [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") could invade you only if you are on bad terms with Yuria, and defeat them to obtain [Manikin Claws](/Manikin+Claws "Dark Souls 3 Manikin Claws").

You can summon [Black Hand Gotthard](/Black+Hand+Gotthard "Dark Souls 3 Black Hand Gotthard"), [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade"), or [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") depending on your progress on her questline. Enter the arena for your fight against [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn"), once defeated there will be a bonfire in this arena. Make your way to a set of stairs that leads to a platform with a pressure plate to lower the pillar as a shortcut to the Pontiff Sulyvahn bonfire.

Make your way to the building up the stairs and hit the wall on the left to reveal an illusory wall to a ladder, take the ladder down to a flooded chamber with [Sulyvahn's Beasts](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast"), defeat them to obtain the [Ring of Favor](/Ring+of+Favor "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Favor"). In the same room, you will find [Archdeacon McDonnell](/Archdeacon+McDonnell "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon McDonnell"), the covenant leader of the [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful"), along with the bonfire in the center.

From the entrance of the building, take the stairs up and drop down onto a ledge in this area. Exit the tower and return to the landing to find a doorway that leads to a round room, look for the statue and attack it to reveal an illusory wall to a set of stairs. Head into the Darkroom Tomb and look for [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") to complete their questline, and in the same room is a chest that contains the [Reversal Ring](/Reversal+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Reversal Ring").

Once at the top, step onto the bridge and enter [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"), light the bonfire in this area. From the rotating platform, make your way down the spiral steps to the dead end platform. Step off the platform to land on an invisible bridge, drop down to the right onto a tower. There is a bonfire here and follow the left path to meet [Company Captain Yorshka](/Company+Captain+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Company Captain Yorshka") the leader of the [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon").

Head in and use the [Old Cell Key](/Old+Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Old Cell Key"), in this room you can obtain the [Covetous Gold Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring") and also find [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") in a cell. Head down and obtain the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring"). Now that you have the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring"), return to [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") and unlock the door, speak to her and you will be able to free her. Complete this area by defeating the boss [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") past the fog gates, once defeated you will be reward with the [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord"), and [Soul of Yhorm the Giant](/Soul+of+Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Yhorm the Giant").

  2. Defeat [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods")

From the bonfire at the entrance of [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"), enter the castle to progress Anri's questline. Interact with the purple sign on the ground that will make you summonable as a phantom to assist her in defeating [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods"). ### [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric")

Recommended Levels: 55-65

  1. Speak with [Emma](/Emma "Dark Souls 3 Emma")
  2. Defeat [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley")

### [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle")

Recommended Levels: 75-90

  1. Defeat the [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight")
  2. Pray at the altar to receive the Praise the Sun [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") for the [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants")
  3. Obtain the [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric")
  4. Defeat [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour")

Take the stairs on the right then head into the room to light the bonfire. Make your way into the next room to find a chest that contains the [Prayer Set](/Prayer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Prayer Set"). Take the stairs on the left and up the tower and take a left to light the Dragon Barracks Bonfire.

Towards the stone bridge are two [Lothric Wyverns](/Lothric+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Wyvern") that will spew out fire, killing one of them will cause both of them to die. Near the end of the bridge, you can drop down to pick up an [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") from a corpse. Back onto the stone bridge, explore the area and make your way to a set of stairs. Take the stairs and follow the path on the right to encounter a [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight"), there's a chance for this enemy to drop the [Irithyll Rapier](/Irithyll+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Rapier").

Exit the church portion and take the lift up and take the ladder up on the left and explore the rooftops and head into a building and take the ladder down. Cross the bridge and follow the path to pray at the altar to learn the Praise the Sun [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") and join the [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants"). Exit through the double doors and make your way around the fallen wyvern to attack it while taking cover inside a dead wyvern. In this room you can find a mimic with the [Sunlight Straight Sword](/Sunlight+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Straight Sword"), pick up the [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric") that sits on top of the stairs.

Pick up [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric") to advance [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim")'s questline, which could lead to 2 endings.

  * Ending 1: Giving her the [Braille Divine Tome of Carim](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim") and [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric"), or all 4 Tomes and not purchasing any of Dark Miracles. Irina will be a Fire Keeper. She will move to the bottom of the tower, by the left of the Firelink Shrine. If you give her the Londor and Deep Tome instead of giving them to Karla, she will leave them on the floor beside her, you will be able to give them Karla.
  * Ending 2: Giving her the [Braille Divine Tome of Carim](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Carim") and [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric"), and purchasing [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Gnaw](/Gnaw "Dark Souls 3 Gnaw"), [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade"), [Vow of Silence](/Vow+of+Silence "Dark Souls 3 Vow of Silence"), or [Dead Again](/Dead+Again "Dark Souls 3 Dead Again"). She can later be found near the Index Gundyr with Eygon. Eygon will think you are hostile and will try to kill you. Upon defeating him, Irina will return to Firelink Shrine. Buying all the Dark Miracles will cause her to be fully corrupted and no longer act as a merchant for you. Equip Morne's Gauntlets and interact with her, she will ask you to kill her.

Walk through the fog to fight [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour"), you can choose to summon [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") for this fight if you wish. Defeating him will reward you with the [Soul of Dragonslayer Armour](/Soul+of+Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Dragonslayer Armour"). Across this bridge is a bonfire you can light, take the stairs up into the next room and you will find another bonfire to the entrance of the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").

### [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden") (Optional)

Recommended Levels: 65-75

  1. Defeat [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King")
  2. Obtain Path of the Dragon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures")
  3. Reach [Untended Greaves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") through the illusory wall behind the chest

In the toxic swamp, you will find a doorway, make your way up the stairs, and take the lift up to the courtyard. Keep making your way up and open the door that is the shortcut to [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). Return to the lift and head down, midway down roll out of the lift and onto a ledge to excite the tower. Take the stairs down to find a corpse containing the [Dragonscale Ring](/Dragonscale+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dragonscale Ring"). Continue the path and defeat the two Iron Knights, one of them will drop the [Magic Stoneplate Ring](/Magic+Stoneplate+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Stoneplate Ring").

Take the stairs up into a fog gate that leads you to the [Oceiros, the Consumed King](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King") boss arena. Light the bonfire after defeating him, head through the double doors, and continue this path. In the next room, there is a corpse that contains the Path of the Dragon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"), this gesture grants you access to [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"). Defeating the Drakeblood Knight in Archdragon Peak will drop the [Drakeblood Set](/Drakeblood+Set "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Set") where you've picked up the gesture. Make your way to the upper section with a chest that contains a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale"), hit the wall behind the chest to reveal an illusory wall, go through it, light the bonfire, and discover the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").

### [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") (Optional)

  1. Obtain the [Ashen Estus Ring](/Ashen+Estus+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Ring")

  3. Obtain the [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment")

From the bonfire, make your way down the path, defeat the group of [Corvians](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian"), and pick up the [Ashen Estus Ring](/Ashen+Estus+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Ring"). Continue on the left and continue making your way until you reach the arena of [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr"), defeat him to obtain the [Soul of Champion Gundyr](/Soul+of+Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Champion Gundyr"). In the arena you can obtain the [Black Knight Glaive](/Black+Knight+Glaive "Dark Souls 3 Black Knight Glaive") on a corpse by the broken wall, light the bonfire, exit through the double doors, and make your way to the dark [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to pick up the [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment").

### [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")

Recommended Levels: 65-80

  1. Defeat the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") for the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll") (Orbeck's quest progression)
  2. Interact with the wax pool by the altar
  3. Defeat the [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight")
  4. Obtain [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab")
  5. Defeat the 3 hostile [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs")
  6. Defeat [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince")

From the bonfire, open the double doors using the [Grand Archives Key](/Grand+Archives+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives Key"), obtainable by defeating 3 Lords of Cinder. Defeat the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") in this room and it will drop the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll"). Take the stairs on the left side of the room until you reach the library, there is an altar there in the room. Defeat the wax-covered priests and interact with the wax pool to cover your character in wax to avoid being cursed by enemies. Head into the next room on the right, and continue along the bookcases until after you've past the third one. There is a lever that opens to a secret room on the left where you can obtain the [Witch's Locks](/Witch's+Locks "Dark Souls 3 Witch's Locks") in a chest, and [Power Within](/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within") on a corpse. Take the stairs up into the next room and take the ladder down. Look for a balcony and take the ladder up, head into the next room to encounter the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") if you have not killed him yet in your first encounter in the room after the bonfire. At the end of the path on the right, take the stairs on the right and take the staircase on the opposite side. Hit the wall to your right to reveal an illusory wall, a [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight") will be in the next room, defeat it to obtain the [Outrider Knight Armor Set](/Outrider+Knight+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Outrider Knight Armor Set"). Exit the room and drop to the lower level and take the walkway out to reach a set of stairs. Take the stairs up and take the lift up, exit the lift and pull the lever to open the shortcut to the main entrance.

Take the lift back up and turn to the left to find a lever to reveal a secret room where you can find the [Scholar Ring](/Scholar+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Scholar Ring") on a corpse by a wax pool. Make your way down the stairs and to the left is another lever to pull, a bookcase will be lowered which will reveal a chest and another wax pool. Head to the wax pool but turn away from it to face a railing, head to the railings and on the right there's a chest that contains a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab"). Return to the central bridge and exit through the doorway located up the stairs, drop down to the left to reach the rooftops. Continue on this path until you drop down to enter a building that is guarded by a wax priest. 

Make your way into the building and kick down the ladder, go down and make a right to a courtyard with 3 [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") on top of the stairs. Defeat them to obtain the [Golden Wing Crest Shield](/Golden+Wing+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Golden Wing Crest Shield"), [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff"), and the [Onikiri and Ubadachi](/Onikiri+and+Ubadachi "Dark Souls 3 Onikiri and Ubadachi"). You can pick up the [Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Large+Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight"). Continue up the stairs to a building, to the right is a lift, activating it opens the shortcut back to the Grand Archives bonfire. Take the lift up and take the path on the right and up the stairs and through the archway on the right to pick up an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard"). Make your way back to the roof and into the building, walk onto a beam to pick up the [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem"), drop down to the next beam and take the ladder down.

From the shortcut lift, head out and take the main path until you reach a doorway on the right to enter a building to another lift, activating it reveals a shortcut to [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). Open the double doors to the [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") boss arena, defeat him to obtain [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord") and [Soul of the Twin Princes](/Soul+of+the+Twin+Princes "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Twin Princes"). Light the bonfire in this area, return to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), and place the Cinders on the throne to initiate the battle at the[ Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame")

### [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") (Optional)

Recommended Levels: 95-120

  1. Defeat [Ancient Wyvern](/Ancient+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Wyvern")
  2. Obtain [Dragon Head Stone](/Dragon+Head+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Head Stone") (Hawkwood's quest progression)
  3. Use Path of the Dragon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") at the altar to obtain [Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone") (Hawkwood's quest progression)
  4. Obtain [Dragon Chaser's Ashes](/Dragon+Chaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Chaser's Ashes") (Shrine Handmaid's quest progression)
  5. Defeat [The Nameless King](/The+Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 The Nameless King")

Make your way down the path after the cutscene to find the Archdragon Peak bonfire, head up the stairs and enter the building on the left. Head down the hall, to the right you can pick up the [Lightning Clutch Ring](/Lightning+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Clutch Ring"). Make your way to the large gate and pull the lever to enter the [Ancient Wyvern](/Ancient+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Wyvern") boss arena, defeating the Ancient Wyvern will reward you with the [Dragon Head Stone](/Dragon+Head+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Head Stone") and transport you back to the Dragon Mausoleum bonfire.

Go through the doorway to encounter either a Drakeblood Knight or Havel the Rock summoned by a Serpent-man Sorcerer, you will obtain the [Drakeblood Greatsword](/Drakeblood+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Greatsword") upon defeating them. Exit the central doorway up to the building to the right and continue on this path to find a lift to activate that takes you back to the beginning of the area. Go down the stairs to find an altar in front of a dragon statue, you will need to perform the Path of the Dragon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") statue to receive the [Calamity Ring](/Calamity+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Calamity Ring"). Take the stairs up to a large gated door, you will obtain the [Dragonslayer Spear](/Dragonslayer+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Spear") when you examine the door.

Exit the building and take the stairs to the left, at the top of the stairs, a wyvern will linger around the area. It will leave if you leave this area immediately and return if you return and will start to attack you. Defeat it and take the stairs up and enter the building, take the stairs down and through the doorway on the right to the next room. Exit the room after defeating the enemies here and head to the left to find a lever with a large bell, ignore the lever for now and continue the path to find the Great Belfry bonfire.

Continue through the doorway on the right of the bonfire to the crumbling ruins. Take the stairs down and take the path on the left, look for a ladder. Take the ladder down to find a Havel [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") standing in front of a fallen wyvern, and defeat him to obtain the [Dragon Tooth](/Dragon+Tooth "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Tooth") and [Havel's Greatshield](/Havel's+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Greatshield"). Make your way back to the main central courtyard of the ruins, head left from the archway to find a corpse that contains the [Dragon Chaser's Ashes](/Dragon+Chaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Chaser's Ashes") that can be used at the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Enter the archway and take the long stairs up, use the Path of the Dragon [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") at the altar to receive the [Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone").

Back in the room with the lever with the bell, interact with it to see a cutscene. After the cutscene, step into the fog path to face the [Nameless King](/Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Nameless King"). Defeat him to obtain the Soul of the [Nameless King](/Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Nameless King"). Another cutscene will play and you will be teleported back to a bonfire.

### [Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame")

Recommended Levels: 95-120

  1. Defeat [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder")

  * Ending 1: Interact with the [First Flame](/First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 First Flame") for the "To Link the First Flame" ending
  * Ending 2: Obtain the [Eyes of a Fire Keeper](/Eyes+of+a+Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Fire Keeper") for the "The End of Fire" ending
  * Ending 3: Attack the Fire Keeper for an alternate cutscene for the "The End of Fire" ending
  * Ending 4: Have 8 [Dark Sigils](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil"), complete Yoel & Yuri's quest, marry Anri for the "Usurpation of Fire" ending

**Requirements** :

You will need to have defeated the 4 Lords of Cinder and place their ashes on their empty thrones and speak to the Fire Keeper. Activate the bonfire in this area to watch a cutscene and will teleport you to the bonfire at the Kiln of the First Flame which resembles a darker version of the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Head outside to the path to activate the bonfire to proceed to the [First Flame](/First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 First Flame"), light the bonfire and head to the path to encounter the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"), defeat him to obtain the [Soul of the Lords](/Soul+of+the+Lords "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Lords").

After defeating boss, you will then have the choice of the following [endings](/endings "Dark Souls 3 endings"):

  * Use the bonfire resulting in the 'To Link the Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement.
  * If you have obtained the [Eyes of a Firekeeper](/Eyes+of+a+Firekeeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Firekeeper") from the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") area and have given it to the Fire Keeper you can use a summon sign next to the bonfire to trigger the 'The End of Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement. During this scene you have a short window where you can attack the Firekeeper after the cutscene for what is effectively a 4th ending option. (WARNING: If you opt for the 4th option, you WILL NOT get the achievement for The End of Fire)
  * If an optional quest line is completed involving [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") and [Anri](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") to become Prince of Londor, you can usurp the flame by wrestling the flame from its mantle triggering the 'Usurpation of Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement. To progress this option, you must take all 5 levels from [Yoel](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor"), and not cure the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") at any point.
