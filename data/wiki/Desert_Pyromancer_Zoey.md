# Desert Pyromancer Zoey

## Desert Pyromancer Zoey  
  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Pyromancer  
**Weakness** | Vow of Silence, Fire, Strike  
**Resistances** | Frost  
**Immune** | Poison/Toxic  
  
**Desert Pyromancer Zoey** is an [enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in Dark Souls 3 [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). 

### Desert Pyromancer Zoey Enemy Description

  * Zoey is a descendant of the desert pyromancers. She uses the [Flame Fan](/Flame+Fan "Dark Souls 3 Flame Fan"), a pyromancy which conjures and brushes a fan left and right. She possessed true beauty, as did all the desert pyromancers, but hers did not poison, and so she became the unassuming queen of the feeble ones.

  * Can be found in [Dreg Heap](/Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 Dreg Heap")
  * She is accompanied by two [Hollow slaves](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave").

### Desert Pyromancer Zoey Combat Information

  * Wields [Whip](/Whip "Dark Souls 3 Whip") (RH) and [Pyromancy Flame](/Pyromancy+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame") (LH).
  * Casts: [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb") and [Flame Fan](/Flame+Fan "Dark Souls 3 Flame Fan"). 
  * Her whip at melee distance does significant damage. Melee fighters should approach with caution and block and dodge a lot and equip high protective [armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor").
  * Vulnerable to [Vow of Silence](/Vow+of+Silence "Dark Souls 3 Vow of Silence"), which prevents her from casting spells.
  * Uses 2 Estus flasks during the fight.
  * Drops [Flame Fan](/Flame+Fan "Dark Souls 3 Flame Fan").
  * Weak to thrust attacks. Long stick-like weapons are recommended.

### Desert Pyromancer Zoey Drops

**Item** | **Quantity** | **Probability** | **Luck**  
---|---|---|---  
[Flame Fan](/Flame+Fan "Dark Souls 3 Flame Fan") | 1 | 100% | No  
  
### Desert Pyromancer Zoey Notes & Trivia

  * She wears the [Desert Pyromancer Set](/Desert+Pyromancer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Desert Pyromancer Set").
  * Can be killed by [Harald Legion Knight](/Harald+Legion+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Legion Knight").
  * Identically-dressed humans, also called Desert Sorceresses, appeared in Dark Souls II.
  * Players can eliminate her by pushing her off the cliff, at least up to NG+3, even if she has full health.
  * She is immune to swamps's poison, but at least her two companions will die of it.
