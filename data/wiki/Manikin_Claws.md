**Manikin Claws** is a [Weapon](/Weapons) in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Weapon of the Pale Shades, assassins of the Sable Church of Londor. The curved claws cause heavy bleeding.  
>   
> When two handed, claws are equipped to each hand.  
>   
> Skill: Quickstep  
> Instantly step behind or around the side of foes. Especially effective when locked on to target.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed and Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Can be used to parry attacks when equipped in the left hand.
  * Has the most range out of the Claws but is also the weakest.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade) that invades you in [Irithyll of The Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+The+Boreal+Valley) right after the Silver Knights room (The invader will only show up if you're on bad terms with Yuria - i. e. kill the pilgrim in the Church to save Anri). 
  * Dropped by Dark Spirit [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Londor Pale Shade") that invades you in the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Farron Keep "), on the right side near the wall, right after the [Basilisk](/Basilisk "Basilisk"). (Must be on bad terms with Yuria of Londor. I.E. Cured the sigil, attacked, or killed Yuria.)
  * The Manikin claws, [Londor Pale Shade set](/Pale+Shade+Set "Londor Pale Shade set"), AND duel bow [gesture](/Gestures "gesture "), can all be obtained in one playthrough by punching [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Yuria") 3 times to aggravate her, killing the invading [Londor Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Londor Shade") in Farron Keep and Irrythil (BEFORE KILLING [PONTIFF](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "PONTIFF")), and then paying for absolution at the [statue of Velka](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "statue of Velka") in the [undead settlement](/Undead+Settlement "undead settlement"). The Londor Shade will still be summonable, Yuria will be friendly, so you can receive all the items without discontinuing the questline.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
