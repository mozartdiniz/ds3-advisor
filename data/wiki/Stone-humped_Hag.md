**Stone-humped Hag** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") merchant in **Dark Souls 3**. She is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Stone-humped Hag Information

  * Can be killed and drops the [Old Woman's Ashes](/Old+Woman's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Old Woman's Ashes").
  * She is a trader that the player can meet immediately after entering [The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap").
  * The player can ask her about The Ringed City once the player hears about The Ringed City's existence from [Lapp](/Amnesiac+Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Amnesiac Lapp").
  * Once you have awakened [Filianore](/Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Filianore"), the Stone-humped Hag dies.

### Location

  * You can find Stone-humped Hag outside of the room containing [The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap") bonfire sitting by the ledge before the first drop.
  * Once you have completed [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") (waking up Filianore), if you return to the Stone-humped Hag, she will have died and dropped the [Old Woman's Ashes](/Old+Woman's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Old Woman's Ashes").

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (1000), NG+ (2000), NG++ (2200), NG+3 (2250), NG+4 (2400), NG+5 (2450), NG+6 (2500), NG+9 (2550)

### Trivia

  * Looks the same as Londor Pilgrims.
  * Description of [Old Woman's Ashes](/Old+Woman's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Old Woman's Ashes") says that the hag was once wet-nurse of royalty. The [Priestess Ring](/Priestess+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Priestess Ring"), which is associated with the Shrine Handmaiden, makes a similar reference, which could suggest that the hag is the [Shrine Handmaiden](/Shrine+Handmaiden "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaiden").
