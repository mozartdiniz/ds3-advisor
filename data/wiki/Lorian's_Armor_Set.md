**Lorian's Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of Prince Lothric's older brother Lorian.  
>   
>  This black-dyed brass helm is patterned with flame. (Helm)  
> This black-dyed brass armor was passed down to him from the royal family. (chest)  
> These black-dyed brass gauntlets were passed down to him from the royal family. (Gauntlets)  
>   
> 
> 
> Lorian, raised as a knight, is said to have been left mute and crippled by his younger brother's curse. It is also said that Lorian, in fact, wished it so.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 45000 souls after defeating [Lorian, Elder Prince](/Lorian,+Elder+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lorian, Elder Prince")
