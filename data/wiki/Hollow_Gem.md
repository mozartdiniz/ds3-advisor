# Hollow Gem

**Hollow Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Hollow Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. A stone plentiful in Londor, land of Hollows.   
>   
> Used in infusion to create Hollow weapons.   
>   
> Hollow weapons are said to peer into the essence of its wielder, whose luck boosts attack.  
  
### Hollow Gem Usage

  * Used in [Hollow Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion").
  * Requires [Profaned Coal](/Profaned+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Coal").

### Hollow Gem Locations

  * Obtained from [Corvian Storytellers](/Corvian+Storyteller "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Storyteller") in Road of Sacrifices
  * Drops from [Lothric Thieves](/Lothric+Thief "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Thief") in Ringed City DLC.
  * Near the [Farron Keep Perimeter](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") bonfire, there is a hole in the wall, with a corpse holding a Hollow Gem.
  * In the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"), drop down off the rooftop after the first [gargoyle](/gargoyle "Dark Souls 3 gargoyle") to where the group of [Corvians](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian") are, then turn right and go into the hallway ().
  * One can be obtained by trading the weapon [Eleonora](/Eleonora "Dark Souls 3 Eleonora") with [Pickle-pee, Pump-a-Rum](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
  * In [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel"), there's a Hollow Gem near the entrance to the chapel, to the right of [Sir Vilhelm](/Sir+Vilhelm "Dark Souls 3 Sir Vilhelm")'s position.
  * In [Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City"), Ringed Inner Wall bonfire. There is a gem at the bottom of the enclosed staircase, lying on a fence railing just before the first Ringed Knight Encounter.

### Notes

  * Requires the [Profaned Coal](/Profaned+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Coal") to infuse.
  * Hollow weapons can be buffed.
  * Hollow Weapons and Shields scale with [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck"), and also grant a bonus amount to your Luck stat.
  * Hollow weapons grant up to +5 Luck at +10 upgrade level, only when your character is [Hollow](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing").
  * The Luck boost stacks by equipping Hollow weapons in both hands, providing you with 10 extra Luck.
  * The Luck boost does increase your bleed/poison capabilities, although not shown on menu.
  * Hollow scaling is D or E in average but some unremarkable weapons perform slightly better with Hollow.
  * [Rings of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice") and [Purging Stones](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone") can postpone or erase your [Hollow](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") condition. This will negate the bonus Luck.
  * Unlike all physical gems it _negatively_ effects innate damage. This being because it has a penalty for increasing aux effects.
