# Ashen Estus Ring

### Ashen Estus Ring  
  
---  
  

> Gray crystalline ring crafted from shards. Increases FP restored by Ashen Estus Flask.  
>   
> Once a treasure brought before Lothric's Queen, she had it enshrined in the Cemetery of Untended Graves, so that one day an Unkindled might profit from its use.  
  
**Ashen Estus Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Ashen Estus Ring Effect

  * Increases [FP](/FP "Dark Souls 3 FP") restored with [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask") by 20%.
  * Weight: 0.8

### Where to Find Ashen Estus Ring

  * Found at [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves"). From the first bonfire head to the left most path. You will find several Corvian enemies guarding this ring. ()
  * [Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cSlMAhK9e2U "Video Location")

### Ashen Estus Ring Notes

  * Its [HP](/HP "Dark Souls 3 HP") counterpart is the [Estus Ring](/Estus+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Estus Ring").
