# Magic

**Magic** or **Spells** in Dark Souls 3 perform a variety of offensive, defensive and support functions. [Sorceries,](/Sorceries) [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies), and [Miracles](/Miracles) are listed below. Hexes from Dark Souls 2 did not return as a spell category; instead, they appear as dark miracles, dark pyromancies, and dark sorceries. Spells require Attunement Slots to equip obtained through [Attunement](/Attunement "Attunement") or a multitude of [Rings](/Rings "Rings"). Casting spells consumes [Stamina](/Stamina "Stamina") and [FP](/FP "FP"). Cast speed can be increased through [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dexterity") or [Sage Ring](/Sage+Ring "Sage Ring") up to a cap.

You can use this [Equipment & Spell Checklist](/file/Dark-Souls-3/dark-souls-3-all-equipment-spell-checklist.xlsx) to keep track of what you have

Please see the page "[Int-Faith Staff Magic Adjustments](/Int-Faith+Staff+Magic+Adjustments)" to see how these stats interact with one another. You can also check out [this guide for magic users](https://fextralife.com/forums/t52733/how-do-i-magic-the-endall-guide-updated/ "this guide for magic users") for further advice.

**Miracles** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** are a type of [Magic](/Magic "Dark Souls 3 Magic") that typically deal [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage") or provide healing and buffs to the player. They primarily scale with the [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") stat. To cast Miracles, you must wield [Talismans](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or [Chimes](/Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Chimes"). There are a total of 38 Miracles in the game, and some can be unlocked by giving special [Key Items](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") called Tomes to certain [Merchants](/Merchants "Dark Souls 3 Merchants"). Some Miracles in Dark Souls 3 can be found by looting chests or corpses, while others are obtained as covenant rewards. The majority of Miracles focus on buffing and healing the player, enhancing their defenses, or restoring health. However, some Miracles are offensive in nature, dealing Lightning or Dark damage to enemies. This variety allows players to tailor their Miracle usage to suit different combat situations and playstyles. They are as follows:
