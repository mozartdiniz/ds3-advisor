Morne's Great Hammer is a **Weapon** in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Greathammer bestowed upon Carim Knights who demonstrate outstanding strength and unwavering faith. Decorated by a ward of Carim Temple, and imbued with the twisted rage of Apostle Morne.  
>   
> Skill: Morne's Rage  
> Stick weapon into earth and emit a powerful shockwave. Also, similar to Perseverance, temporarily boosts poise and reduces damage received.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Upgraded with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Morne's Great Hammer has a weapon range of 43.466xl on the 1h R1, and 37.9733xl on the 2h R1.
  * Being a weapon with some of the highest stat requirements, this is one of the far less viable weapons on a SL 120 character. 
  * Visually similar to [Bramd](http://demonssouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Bramd "Dark Souls 3 Bramd") from Demon's Souls, and has the same stat requirements as the [Grant](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Grant "Dark Souls 3 Grant") from Dark Souls 1.
  * At +5 with 50 STR/40 FTH, this weapon has 631 AR.
  * Added Faith scaling and had Strength scaling altered in Regulation 1.09 patch, making it a good STR/FTH weapon. Before then, it only had a B scaling in Strength at +5.
  * This Great Hammer, along with [Smough's Great Hammer ](/Smough's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Smough's Great Hammer") and [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Ledo's Great Hammer"), have a higher poise health value than the other Great Hammers, with 32.9 poise health instead of the usual 30.8. This means you can have much less poise and still be able to tank through the other Ultras.

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") if killed.
  * Looted from Eygon of Carim's corpse late-game in the old cell of Irina of Carim.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen).
