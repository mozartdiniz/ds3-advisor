# Crystal Gem

**Crystal Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Crystal Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Introduced to Lothric by the Crystal Sages.   
>   
> Used in infusion to create crystal weapons.   
>   
> Crystal weapons inflict magic damage, and scale effectively with intelligence.  
  
### Crystal Gem Usage

  * Used for Crystal weapon [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion")
  * Crystal weapons inflict magic damage which scales with the [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") stat
  * Requires the [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons

### Crystal Gem Locations

  * 1x Dropped by a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), in the broken building before the Crystal Sage, in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices").
  * 1x Dropped by the first [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") the player encounters in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * 1x Dropped by a [Giant Crab](/Giant+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Giant Crab") near the Depths of the Painting bonfire.
  * (NG+) Dropped by the large undead with a [Great Axe](/Great+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Great Axe"), near the entrance to [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
  * Cloaked Hollows and Hollow Sorcerers in [Road of Sacrifices ](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") before [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") area have a chance to drop it. 
  * Can be dropped by the [Grand Archives Scholars](/Grand+Archives+Scholar "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives Scholar") and the [Hollow Slaves](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave") in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
  * Chance to drop from the [Giant Crabs](/Giant+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Giant Crab") near the Depths of the Painting bonfire.

### Trivia

  * Despite its name it has nothing in common with the brittle Crystal infusion of the first Dark Souls game.
