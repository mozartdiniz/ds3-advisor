# Enemies

**Enemies** are hostile NPCS in **Dark Souls 3** that the player can confront to obtain Souls, upgrade materials and equipment. Enemies have different resistances to the many [Damage Types](/Damage+Types "Dark Souls 3 Damage Types"), and their HP changes depending on your [New Game Cycle](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus"). Please note the names may not be official as the community discovers and records each enemy type. Click on the tabs to see different categories.
