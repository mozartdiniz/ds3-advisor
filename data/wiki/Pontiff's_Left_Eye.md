# Pontiff's Left Eye

### Pontiff's Left Eye  
  
---  
  

> Bewitched ring that Pontiff Sulyvahn bestowed upon his knights.  
>   
> Recovers HP with successive attacks.  
>   
> Knights who peer into the black orb are lured into battles of death, transformed into frenzied beasts. No wonder the Pontiff only provides these rings to those dispatched to foreign lands.  
  
**Pontiff's Left Eye** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This ring allows you to recover 30 HP after successive attacks, and it has a low weight of only 0.9, making it a valuable addition to any build. When you strike multiple enemies in quick succession, the ring's effect can trigger repeatedly, resulting in a significant boost to your character's health. This makes it particularly useful in situations where you're dealing with groups of foes or using fast-hitting weapons. The ring can be obtained by transposing the [Soul of Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Soul+of+Boreal+Valley+Vordt "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt"), adding a strategic element to your decision-making when choosing which boss souls to use.

### Pontiff's Left Eye Dark Souls 3 Effect

  * Recovers 30 [HP](/HP "Dark Souls 3 HP") with successive attacks
  * Weight: 0.9
  * Attacking 2 or more enemies when the effect triggers has a chance to proc a overheal, healing the player massively. 
    * This is speculated to be a coding oversight, or a bug, that never got fixed. 

### Where to Find Pontiff's Left Eye in DS3

  * [Transposed](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") from [Soul of Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Soul+of+Boreal+Valley+Vordt "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Boreal Valley Vordt").

### Dark Souls 3 Pontiff's Left Eye Testing Notes:

  * Weapon Arts count towards the combo counter.
  * Charged attacks count for more points on the combo counter.
  * Healing effect may proc before actual hit connects from input.
  * Invaders may see healing effect before healing effect actually occurs, or when it never occurs in the first place.
  * Weapons with vastly different movesets from their standard may have a bit different results from their weapon class. For example, the weapon "Halberd" has a partial spear moveset, and has different results from the rest of the Halberd weapon class for the most part.
  * Hits that land just after the enemy dies counts towards the combo counter (tested with [Sellsword Twinblades](/Sellsword+Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Sellsword Twinblades"), two handed: two light attacks while target is alive, the third one while the target is dead, completing the combo and healing the player)

### DS3 Pontiff's Left Eye Story & Lore

  * The name Sulyvahn means "little dark eye" or "dark-eyed" in Irish.
  * Cut content suggests that Sulyvahn's character was to appear in The Untended Graves.
