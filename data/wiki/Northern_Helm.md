**Northern Helm** is a [Helm](/Helms "Dark Souls 3 Helms") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of the [Northern Armor Set](/Northern+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Northern Armor Set").

> Iron helm of a stalwart northern warrior. Engraved with an ornate pattern.   
>   
> Iron defensive wear grants superior stability. Being from the north, it should also offer resistance to the cold.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting Armor for the [Warrior](/Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Warrior") class
  * Looted from a hanging corpse in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"). In the area where you fight the [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon") with [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina").
