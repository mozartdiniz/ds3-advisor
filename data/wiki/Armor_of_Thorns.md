**Armor of Thorns** is a [Chest Armor](/Chest+Armor) Piece in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Armor of Thorns Set](/Armor+of+Thorns+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Armor of Kirk, the notorious knight of Thorns.  
> A dense patch of thorns grows form its surface.  
>   
> A fitting item for the murderous Kirk, for even the simple act of rolling can damage enemies when wearing this attire.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found on a body by [Rosaria's Bed Chamber.](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Rosaria's Bed Chamber.")
  * You have to have been invaded by and vanquished [Kirk](/Longfinger+Kirk "Kirk") on the ground floor of the [Cathedral.](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Cathedral.")
  * Do NOT defeat [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Deacons of the Deep") before trying to obtain this armor. Kirk will not invade afterwards.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Does 2 damage when rolling or quickstepping
