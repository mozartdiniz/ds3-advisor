# Fading Soul

**Fading Soul** is a [Soul](/Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Fading Soul  
  
---  
  

> The fading soul of a corpse. Use to gain a smattering of souls. Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for Souls.  
  
### Fading Soul Usage

  * Consume to gain 50 Souls

### Fading Soul Locations

  * Dropped by Undead Cleric at [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash).
  * Dropped by [Cage Spider](/Cage+Spider "Cage Spiders") at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement").
  * 1x at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement) \- In the graveyard with the falling spears, just ahead of the _Dilapidated Bridge_ bornfire. ()
  * 1x at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep)
  * 1x at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices): Near where you can summon [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick)
  * 1x at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"): Starting at the Church of Yorshka bonfire, go down the hallway stairs, and go right along the cliff. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=45m0s "Video"))

### Notes

  * Considered useless as it gives only 50 souls, it is often used as a "bait" item to lure players into a trap or a certain location.
