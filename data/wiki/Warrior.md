**Warrior** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , perfect for players aiming to focus on raw melee power. Boasting the highest starting Strength stat, this class is an excellent choice for pure Strength builds or hybrid Quality builds.

Its low Attunement, Intelligence, and Faith make it less suited for spellcasting, but this trade-off enhances its specialization in physical combat. Starting at level 7, the Warrior is also an optimized starting point for Hollow or Luck builds that incorporate Strength or Dexterity. With its high Vigor and Endurance, the Warrior is designed to endure and dominate in close-range battles.

> Descendant of northern warriors famed for their brawn. Utilizes high strength to wield a heavy battleaxe.

### Dark Souls 3 Warrior Notes and Tips

  * This class has the lowest total of Attunement, Intelligence and Faith, making it an ideal choice for pure melee builds, though knights base Attunement, Intelligence, Faith and Luck are only one level higher total than warrior in exchange for more FP from higher attunement.
  * Starts with highest Strength stat.

### Builds That Use Warrior in DS3

  * 66+ STR builds
  * 40 STR / 40 DEX Quality builds
  * Most optimized starting class for STR or DEX focused Hollow/ Luck Builds
  * 60 INT (or) 60 FTH builds without spellcasting, based on non-physical weapon usage (Moonlight Greatsword, Lightning Lothric Knight Greatsword and etc.)
