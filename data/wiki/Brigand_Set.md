**Brigand Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.  
  
  
  
  

> Armor from a foreign land. Probably belonged to a brigand who met his match.
> 
> In foreign lands, undead were banished to send a message to populace, and when the message was not heard, they banished the living, too.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a corpse behind the hostile NPC wielding the [Butcher Knife](/Butcher+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Butcher Knife") in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"). ([Video Location](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=3h29m5s "Video Location") Fextralife Video starting at 3h29m5s)
