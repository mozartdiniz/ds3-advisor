**Conjurator Set** is an [Armor](/Armor) in **Dark Souls 3.**   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of traveling conjurators.
> 
> Conjurators were the predecessors to pyromancers, and spent their lives roaming the lands. No wonder their attire was designed to protect them from fire, poison, and other threats of nature.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in [The Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "The Road of Sacrifices"), to the right of the entrance to [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Farron Keep"). At the base of a tree, next to a [Great Crab](/Great+Crab "Great Crab"). 
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=1h1m52s "Video location") (fextralife video starting at 1h 1m 52s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Set formerly known as the pyromancer set, in Dark Souls 1&2
  * The elemental resistances and poison resistance on this set are very high, making it a wise choice for magic-based boss fights and poison-covered arenas/areas
  * This set looks completely different between female and male characters.
