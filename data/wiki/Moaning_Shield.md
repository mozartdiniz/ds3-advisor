**Moaning Shield** is a [Greatshield](/Greatshields "Dark Souls 3 Greatshields") in Dark Souls 3. [Greatshields](/Greatshields "Dark Souls 3 Greatshields") usually weigh a decent amount and provide the best protection in the game. They are often used by players who have high [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength"), as they require a good amount in order to use.

> A deformed greatshield given to Eygon of Carim upon being conferred knighthood.
> 
> The giant woman's face that protects Eygon is that of his sister, some years his senior.
> 
> Skill: Moan  
> Offer a gentle prayer to the shield, causing the woman's face to give out a low moan and attract enemies.

### Moaning Shield Notes and Tips

  * Cannot be Infused.
  * Can be buffed with Magic Shield or Great Magic Shield
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Provides an average block of 78.6%. Exceptionally solid blocking, but requires the highest strength of all greatshields at 50 points. Also boasts the second highest stability in game, 82 at +5.
  * Can be given to [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") in exchange for a [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem").
  * Using [Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield") on this shield when maximally upgraded will result in no stamina loss through blocking, however, elemental damage will chip through.
  * At +4 it can survive a fully upgraded, full [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Ledo's Great Hammer") weapon art if you have around 40 endurance.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim").
  * Looted from Eygon of Carim's corpse late-game in the old cell of [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
