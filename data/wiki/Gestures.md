# Gestures

**Gestures** are a game mechanic that allows players to express emotion or direction. There are a total of thirty-three gestures in Dark Souls 3. Collection of all gestures rewards the player with the [Master of Expression](/Trophy+&+Achievement+Guide "Dark Souls 3 Trophy & Achievement Guide") trophy.

Most gestures performed towards the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") will result in different reactions from her, such as 'Dignified bow' responding with a bow, 'Legion Etiquette' with a look of consternation, 'Stretch out' with a small laugh, or 'Path of the Dragon' with a twirl. The only gestures not reacted to are the 3 different point gestures (requires confirmation).

####  Proper bow X

|  Received upon summoning [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"):

  1. Offer a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") to [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth").
  2. [Yellowfinger Heysel's](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") summon sign will appear next to the tower full of slugs near the Keep Ruins bonfire.

Notes:

  * [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") must still be alive.
  * Giving a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") to [Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") will cause [Sirris of the Sunless Realms'](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") questline to fail and you will not be able to receive the [Silvercat Ring](/Silvercat+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Silvercat Ring"), [Sunset Armor Set](/Sunset+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Sunset Armor Set"), [Sunset Shield](/Sunset+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunset Shield"), [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman"), [Dragonslayer's Axe](/Dragonslayer's+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer's Axe"), or the [Mirrah Chain Set](/Mirrah+Chain+Set "Dark Souls 3 Mirrah Chain Set") (and Darkmoon Loyalty, if you didn't get it before) until the next NG cycle. However, she can be killed in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to receive the [Sunless Set](/Sunless+Set "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Set") and [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") if her quest has failed. 
  * There is a cap of 99 [Pale Tongues](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") you can offer. If you reach this limit, you can't offer more, and, consequently, you can't gain this gesture anymore. [Pale Tongues](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") used for respecs don't count toward the 99 limit.

###  Trivia

  * The 'Quiet Resolve' gesture was originally from the first Dark Souls trailer, where the character who uses the gesture in the trailer is wearing the [Elite Knight Set](/Elite+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Elite Knight Set"). The same set is worn by [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora"), who teaches you this gesture.
  * There are several gestures that are performed by NPCs that can not be obtained by the player, most notably the majority of sitting and standing idle poses, the gestures the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") does in response to gestures done towards her, and the standing 'Toast' gesture performed by [Lapp](/Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Lapp").
