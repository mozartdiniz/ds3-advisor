**Master's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> A terribly worn shirt/manchettes.
> 
> Men are fond of weaving tales to explain the raggedness of their garb.
> 
> My sixth sense warned me of danger, and I danced between flurries of blades unscathed, but alas, my clothes went to tatters.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Sword Master](/Sword+Master "Dark Souls 3 Sword Master"), who is in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to the left of the main entrance.

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Featured first in Dark Souls Ⅱ as part of most of the [Prisoner's Set](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Prisoner's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Prisoner's Set"), along with the [Ragged Mask](/Ragged+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Ragged Mask") and [Loincloth](/Loincloth "Dark Souls 3 Loincloth").
  * Really has no redeemable qualities aside from being the lightest armor set.
  * If you trade piece of this set to [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") she will give you only 10 souls for this. Interestingly, despite its back story (and invader NPC and important phantom) it's got one of the worst prices for single armor piece, worse being only e.g. [Loincloth](/Loincloth "Dark Souls 3 Loincloth"), which Handmaid buys for 5 souls.
