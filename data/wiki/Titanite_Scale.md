# Titanite Scale

**Titanite Scale** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Titanite Scale  
  
---  
  

> Titanite altered by a soul. Reinforces soul-transposed weapons to +4. Weapons forged by souls transposition can only be reinforced by titanite of the same kind. In rare cases, Crystal Lizards devour souls, growing to monstrous proportion and leaving these great scales."  
  
### Titanite Scale Usage

  * Reinforces all [Soul-Transposed Weapons](/Boss+Soul+Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Boss Soul Weapons") to +4.
  * Also reinforces some other unique weapons listed at the bottom of the page.
  * 15 scales required in total to get a weapon to +4: 
    * 1 scale for +1
    * 2 scales for +2
    * 4 scales for +3
    * 8 scales for +4

### Trivia

  * It looks very similar to [Demon Titanite](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Demon+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Demon Titanite") from Dark Souls, and also serves the same purpose.
  * In DS2, the function of Demon Titanite was taken over by [Petrified Dragon Bone](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Petrified+Dragon+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Petrified Dragon Bone"), explaining this demon dragon scale.
  * It costs 300,000 Souls to purchase 15 titanite scales, which is the required amount to upgrade to + 4.
