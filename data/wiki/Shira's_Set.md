**Shira's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3.** It is added in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). It is the set worn by [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira").  
  
  
  
  

> Armor of Shira, knight in service to Filianore. A rare women's piece from ancient times.
> 
> With a spun gold shawl draped over the silver breastplate and a green woven skirt, this armor offers a subdued yet refined elegance, apropos to both a handmaiden of the Princess and one whose veins coarse with royal blood.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * After killing Shira in the ruins at Filianore's Rest, head back to the door where you spoke to her before (at the very top of the large set of stairs leading to the swamp) and it will be open. The set is on a corpse in the room.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Provides very good physical and elemental defense for its weight class.
