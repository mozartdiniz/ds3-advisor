# Carthus Flame Arc

**Carthus Flame Arc** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

  

## Carthus Flame Arc  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirments | 10 Intelligence/ 10 Faith  
Duration | 90 Seconds  
  
> Pyromancy of Carthus of the Sands. Reinforces right weapon with flame.   
>   
> Warriors of Carthus favored supplemental pyromancies such as this, which was used to enhance the curved blades of its swordsmen." 

### How to Get Carthus Flame Arc in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 10,000 souls after giving him the [Carthus Pyromancy Tome](/Carthus+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Pyromancy Tome"). 

### Dark Souls 3 Carthus Flame Arc Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds (Spell Buff * 0.9) [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage") to the weapon.  

    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed.
    * The buff can be easily identified by the flaming weapon
  * Good choice for melee characters looking for bonus damage with minimal stats. 
    * Outperforms [Charcoal Pine Resin](/Charcoal+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Charcoal Pine Resin") even at minimum stat requirements.
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * Like all Weapon Buffs, Carthus Flame Ark will only work on weapons with raw, refined, hollow, sharp, heavy, or no infusion.
  * Buff length is extended by the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Uniquely, this buff lasts 90 seconds, 50% longer than any other weapon buffing spell. This, combined with its low requirements and good damage, make it an incredible choice for melee builds.
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit.  
Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Paired Weapons](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.  
Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
