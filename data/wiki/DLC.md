# DLC

**DLC** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** is a full guide to the game's additional downloadable content that is released post launch. Players may purchase a Season Pass to access all DLC, or the [Dark Souls 3 GOTY (Fire Fades) Edition](/Dark+Souls+3+The+Fire+Fades+Edition "Dark Souls 3 The Fire Fades Edition"), that includes the base game and DLC in a bundle.

### About Dark Souls 3 DLC

  * The first DLC is titled [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") and was released on October 25th, 2016. It is priced at 15 USD for the single item.
  * The second DLC is titled [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") and was released on March 28th, 2017. It is priced at 15 USD for the single item.
  * Both DLCs are sold together for a total of 25 USD as Season Pass Bundle, saving players 5 USD.

## Dark Souls 3 DLC Ashes of Ariandel Information

**Ashes of Ariandel** is the first of two [DLCs](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC") in **Dark Souls 3** , released on October 25th. The developers recommend this expansion for players who have cleared [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle").

To access Ashes of Ariandel, players must purchase and download the DLC, then speak to an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") at the Cleansing Chapel in the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"). Talk to him and agree to receive a rotten scrap of painting, which will transport you to the beginning of the DLC. If you have completed the events of The Ringed City beforehand, a piece of a painting will be at the corner of the shrine instead. Note: Completing this DLC is not required to finish the game or obtain any trophies.

## Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City Information

**The Ringed City** is the second and final [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC") for Dark Souls 3. Announced by Bandai Namco on January 23rd, 2017, it was released on March 28th, 2017. The developers recommend this DLC for players who have cleared Lothric Castle.

The Ringed City DLC is accessed by traveling to the [Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame") and finding a new bonfire before the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"), which transports you to the DLC location. Alternatively, you can find a bonfire in the Painted World of Ariandel, in the chapel by [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede"). For this method to work, ensure you are teleporting to the Sister Friede bonfire first. If you last saved there and then start the DLC, the bonfire will not appear. Simply teleport to Firelink Shrine and then back to the Sister Friede bonfire, and it will be there.
