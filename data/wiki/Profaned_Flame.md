# Profaned Flame

## Profaned Flame  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 25 Intelligence / 0 Faith  
  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Profaned Flame** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a Flame or Special Weapons that can cast Pyromancies.

> Pyromancy deriving from the Profaned Flame.  
>   
> Engulfs foes at range and burns them to ashes.  
>   
> The Profaned Capital was consumed by fire after Yhorm the Giant became a Lord of Cinder. The fire, born of the sky, is said to have incinerated naught but human flesh.

### How to Get Profaned Flame in Dark Souls 3

  * [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"), in the pit with a lot of rats at the feet of a giant and where you can talk to [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") outside his cell. The spell is on a body on the ground ([video location](http://youtu.be/RPGXc95uBfY "video location")).

### Dark Souls 3 Profaned Flame Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Profaned Flame is essentially [ Flame Swathe](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Flame+Swathe "Dark Souls 3 Flame Swathe") from Dark Souls II with a minor difference; the explosion isn't as big as it was in Dark Souls II but has much higher knockback.
  * A large AoE pyromancy with decent range and good damage. Good for multiple targets.
  * At good casting speed, the player can use another quick spell before the explosion.
  * Hits through walls.
  * A good wake-up spell after a backstab or a riposte. It the player casts it at the right time, the explosion will go off while they are still in their getting up animation and hit them.
