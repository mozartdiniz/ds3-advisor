**Londor Pale Shade** is a hostile invading NPC in Dark Souls 3, that can also be friendly.

### Londor Pale Shade Information

_"A hostile invader or ally to your cause, wielding the darkness of the Abyss for or against you"_

  * Killable
  * Becomes an ally if your relationship with [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") is good. 
    * Yuria can only be spawned after using [Yoel of Londor's](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") "Draw Out True Strength" mechanic 5 times.
    * Gives the Duel Bow [gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") after being summoned as a white phantom. [Example Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XI4-u0-hezg "Example Video") of summoning him for the gesture.
    * Note: if you use the gesture Darkmoon Loyalty, you will receive Duel Bow right afterwards.
  * Becomes an invader if your relationship with [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") is disrupted. 
    * Your relationship is disrupted by aggroing or killing Yuria, curing the [dark sigil](/dark+sigil "Dark Souls 3 dark sigil"), killing [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") (before he gives 5 level ups), or by killing [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora").
    * Both invasions are needed to get the armor set.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Manikin Claws](/Manikin+Claws "Dark Souls 3 Manikin Claws"), [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade"), [Pale Shade Set](/Pale+Shade+Set "Dark Souls 3 Pale Shade Set"). (references londor pale shades in general)
  * You can recieve his equipment and gesture if you aggro Yuria by hitting her 3 times, run away, kill this invader twice, pay for absolution at Velka's statue, and summon him for Pontiff. Yuria will be friendly afterwards and no quest will be disrupted. Note however, that each invasion must be triggered before defeating the area boss (Abyss Watchers and the Pontiff, respectively) or the Pale Shade will not invade otherwise.
  * If you assisted Sirris in killing [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer"), then you must be invaded by Creighton outside the Church of Yorshka bonfire before the Dark Spirit of Londor will invade you.
  * Does not appear possible after defeating Pontiff Sulyvahn.
  * You must be embered for all dark spirit encounters.
  * His armor is next to Yuria of Londor after he is killed twice.

### Location

#### Summon

  * Can be summoned as an ally for [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"), the summon sign is in the corner next to the _Farron Keep Perimeter_ bonfire.
  * Can be summoned as an ally for [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn"), the summon sign is in front of a statue right outside the boss fog.
  * Can be summoned as an ally for [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"), the summon sign is located halfway between the bonfire and the boss fog.

#### Invasion

  * Invades in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), near the first bonfire, take a right turn outside and follow the wall towards the basilisks. Will not invade if [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") have been defeated. 
  * Invades in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), past the Silver Knight room, at the top of the first set of stairs. 
    * If you assisted [Sirris](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") in killing [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer"), then you must be invaded by Creighton outside the _Church of Yorshka_ bonfire before the Dark Spirit of Londor will invade you.

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (7,196), NG+ (No change)
  * Weapons: [Manikin Claws](/Manikin+Claws "Dark Souls 3 Manikin Claws")
  * The [Pale Shade Set](/Pale+Shade+Set "Dark Souls 3 Pale Shade Set") can be found in Firelink Shrine, near the location of Yoel and Yuria, after the Londor Pale Shade invader has been defeated in both Farron Keep and Irithyll of the Boreal Valley.
  * 2 Estus & 2 Ashen Estus refills
