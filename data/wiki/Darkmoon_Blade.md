# Darkmoon Blade

## Darkmoon Blade  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 50  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 30 Faith  
**Duration** | 60 seconds  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
**Darkmoon Blade** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This allows players to imbue their right-hand weapon with a mystical Darkmoonlight, adding 95% of the player's casting talisman's Spell Buff as magic damage for a duration of 60 seconds. This Miracle is particularly effective for faith-based builds, enhancing the player's offensive capabilities significantly. To obtain the Darkmoon Blade Miracle, players must rank up in the Blade of the Darkmoon covenant. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

> Miracle of those who devoted themselves to the Darkmoon covenant.
> 
> Reinforces right weapon with Darkmoon light.
> 
> Miracles of the Darkmoon are tales of revenge, but Captain Yorshka recites only for the sake of remembering her brother, without knowledge of its meaning.
> 
> Perhaps this is better, as revenge is better left to the Blades.

### How to Get Darkmoon Blade in Dark Souls 3

  * Reward for reaching rank 2 in the covenant [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon"). (You must offer 30 Proof of a Concord Kepts to reach rank 2)

### Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") equal to (spellbuff*0.95)  

    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed
    * Despite applying magic damage, this scales exclusively to Faith
    * This spell and [Lightning Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade") do the same damage in different elements, making them fully interchangeable
  * Buff lasts 60 seconds (affected by the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"))
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * This miracle is particularly powerful on weapons that already have magic damage, such as the [Drakeblood Greatsword](/Drakeblood+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Greatsword")
  * Damage can be increased with the [Magic Clutch Ring](/magic+clutch+ring "Dark Souls 3 magic clutch ring") and the [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk")
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit.  
Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Paired Weapons](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.  
Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
  * Since Faith builds have access to four elements in unique weapon buff spells ([Blessed Weapon](/Blessed+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Weapon"), [Lightning Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade"), Darkmoon Blade, and [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade")), they're the ideal choice for a more physical-focused build investing into magic.
  * This miracle first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade")_.
