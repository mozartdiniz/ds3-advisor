**Dark Sword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. The Dark Sword is a straight sword known for its wide, sweeping attacks, similar to the Broadsword. It is noticeably heavier than most straight swords and scales well with Strength, making it an excellent choice early in the game. What sets the Dark Sword apart from other straight swords is its unique weapon skill, "Stomp," which is more commonly found on greatswords. When used, this skill grants the wielder hyper armor, allowing them to withstand enemy attacks without being staggered during its execution.  
  
  
  

> Pitch-black straight sword of the Darkwraith, survivor of the land swallowed by darkness.  
>   
> The Darkwraiths were the first red orb invaders, and originators of a unique sword technique inspired by their thick, broad blades.  
>   
> Skill: Stomp  
> Use one's weight to lunge forward with a low stance and increased poise, and follow with strong attack for an upward slash.

  
  

### Dark Sword DS3 Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Dark Sword has a motion value of 200 on backstab, 250 on riposte, and 60+225 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a straight sword does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").

  * The Dark Sword has a range of 35.0199xl on the 1h R1 and 34.6102xl on the 2h R1.
  * The Dark Sword has a unique, vertically slashing R2, both 1h and 2h.
  * The only straight sword in the game to have the Stomp [Weapon Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills").
  * Compared to other straight swords, this weapon's main use-case is on Strength builds with enough DEX to use it. Its scaling with Strength when infused with Heavy is massive and it beats all other straight swords besides the extremely short Broadsword on Strength builds.
  * When infused with a Dark Gem, it will be called a "Reinforced Dark Sword" instead of "Dark Dark Sword."
  * There is a glitch where a Dark-infused Dark Sword could be buffed with Dark Blade, which some dubbed a Dark Dark Dark Sword.

  * Dropped by:  
Darkwraith [FK] 8.00%

  * The Farron Keep Perimeter Bonfire is next to two [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") that will wander into and fight a group of [Ghru](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt"). If left to play out the [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith") will usually survive with minimal HP - this allows the player to farm them with little risk.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
