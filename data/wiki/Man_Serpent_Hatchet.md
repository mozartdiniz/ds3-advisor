**Man Serpent Hatchet** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Hatchet of the serpent men guarding Archdragon Peak. Has a unique, curved blade that can sneak past shields.  
>   
> Skill: Warcry  
> Let out a spirited warcry that temporarily boosts attack, and pierce through shields to inflict direct damage.

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * The Man Serpent Hatchet has a weapon range of 36.9138xl on the 1h R1, and 37.9161xl on the 2h R1.
  * This weapon has an innate shield pierce of 20% on light attacks, 45% on heavy attacks, and 65% on charged heavy attacks.
  * Has the same scaling, weight, and damage as the [Battle Axe](/Battle+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Battle Axe"). However, it has more range than the Battle Axe and can penetrate shields like the [Shotel](/Shotel "Dark Souls 3 Shotel") and the [Carthus Shotel](/Carthus+Shotel "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Shotel").
  * Tied with the [Butcher Knife](/Butcher+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Butcher Knife") for the most range out of all the axes.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Drops from [Serpent Men](/Serpent-man "Dark Souls 3 Serpent-man") wielding it in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").
  * To farm, head backwards from the Archdragon Peak bonfire and kill the four nearby enemies.

### Lore

  * Man-Serpents are the [descendants of dragons](/Ancient+Dragon+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Dragon Greatshield"), and were likely inspired by the Berserk manga ([read more](https://bloodborne.wiki.fextralife.com/From+Berserk+to+Bloodborne#Snake "Dark Souls 3 From Berserk to Bloodborne#Snake")).

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
