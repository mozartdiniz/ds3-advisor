**Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow (or "Nestling")** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **Dark Souls 3**.

  * You cannot attack this NPC and you cannot see her.

### Location

  * You can find Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum on the roof of the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), after you have obtained the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").
  * You can get to the crow without a key if you jump on the roof via a tipped tree on the top of [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). ([Screenshot](/file/Dark-Souls-3/bird_jump.png?v=1460504874344 "picture")) 
  * See [this map](/Firelink+Shrine#map "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine#map") for details.

### Trade

In order to trade with her, drop an item (by selecting 'leave' in the item menu) on either the roof nest or the nest in the shrine rafters, and pick up the item she gives you in return. Unlike Dark Souls 1 and Demon Souls, you do NOT need to reload the area (quitting to title screen, resting at a bonfire, etc.) in order to finalize the trade—simply pick up the item on the ground after leaving your tradable item. If you reload the area, both your item and the item she gives in exchange WILL be lost. If she doesn't want the item you offered, she will simply say no and return your item. 

You may only trade one of each item per New Game cycle.

Note: You also learn the [Call Over Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") the first time you trade an item that gives a "Pickle pee" response from the crow. It works with a Homeward Bone, Mendicant's Staff ([Screenshot](/file/Dark-Souls-3/mendicantstafftrade.jpg?v=1646578954293 "Screenshot")) (Eleonora, and Avelyn still needs verification)

Note: The weapons you offer must NOT be upgraded for the trade to be accepted (for example, if you offer a [Avelyn+1 ](/Avelyn "Dark Souls 3 Avelyn")the trade won't go off).

### Trivia

  * "Pickle-pee" and "Pump-a-rum" come from an old children's poem called "The Ceremonial Band" by James Reeves. In the poem, "pickle-pee" is the sound of a fife and "pump-a-rum" is the sound of a drum.
  * The crow is referred to as "Nestling" in the end credits, and is voiced by Clare Corbett. The official strategy guide refers to the crow as 'The Nestling of Firelink Shrine (Snuggly The Crow)" and states the crow is rumored to be named Snuggly.
  * Demon's Souls version is known as Sparkly the Crow, because she only accepted sparkling items.
  * Dark Souls version is known as [Snuggly the Crow](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Snuggly "Dark Souls 3 Snuggly"), because she only accepted warm and soft items.
  * Dark Souls II version is known as [Dyna & Tilo](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dyna+and+Tillo "Dark Souls 3 Dyna and Tillo").
