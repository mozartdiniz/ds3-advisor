# Sage Ring

### Sage Ring  
  
---  
  

> A ring given to Farron's Undead Legion by one of the preacher twins, known more commonly as the Crystal Sages." Shortens spell casting time. The sorcerers of Farron's Abyss Watchers were known to be lonesome warriors who would only rely upon more pragmatic spells.  
  
**Sage Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**. 

### Dark Souls 3 Sage Ring Effect

  * Increases virtual [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dark Souls 3 Dexterity"), effectively decreasing spell casting time for all schools of magic. Maximum Casting Speed is reached at 50 Dexterity. These rings allow low dexterity builds reach the casting speed cap: 
    * +0 Version: 
      * +30 Virtual Dexterity
      * 20 Dexterity to reach Casting Cap
    * +1 Version: 
      * +35 Virtual Dexterity
      * 15 Dexterity to reach Casting Cap
    * +2 Version: 
      * +40 Virtual Dexterity
      * 10 Dexterity to reach Casting Cap
  * Weight: 0.7

### How to Get Sage Ring in Dark Souls 3

  * From the Crucifixion Woods bonfire in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"), facing the two [Exiles of Farron](/Exile+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Exile of Farron"), turn left towards some ruins that are very slightly flooded with water. The Sage Ring will be in those ruins, be wary of a [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope") to your right when you enter the ruins.
  * (NG+) +1 Version found in the rafters above the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). Drop down from the roof where the winged knights are located. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/LpD6UxuZbFw "\(video location\)")
  * (NG++) +2 Version: from the Dancer bonfire go up the ladder and head left to the [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"). When riding the elevator down get ready to roll off halfway. Head right for an Estus Shard, then head left to the broken staircase. When on the staircase look behind you and you'll see the ring. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/M-R27Ege_5Y "\(video location\)")

### Dark Souls 3 Sage Ring Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Virtual Dexterity is [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dark Souls 3 Dexterity") only affecting spell casting.
  * [Witchtree Branch](/Witchtree+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Witchtree Branch") & [Saint-tree Bellvine](/Saint-tree+Bellvine "Dark Souls 3 Saint-tree Bellvine") both give +99 Virtual Dexterity, but the cap is 50 virtual dexterity so there isn't an effective difference between them and Sage Ring +2 for most builds.
  * [Wrath of God ](/Wrath+of+the+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Wrath of the Gods") Testing: 
    * 20 Dexterity, cast time = 2.9 seconds
    * 20 Dexterity & Sage Ring (50 total Virtual Dex), cast time = 2.3 seconds.
