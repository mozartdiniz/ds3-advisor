# Fire Keeper Soul

**Fire Keeper Soul** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. This key item is used by giving it to the Fire Keeper at Firelink Shrine, enabling her to remove the Dark Sigils you acquired after leveling up with Yoel of Londor. You can find this key item on the corpse of a Fire Keeper, located at the top of the Bell Tower in Firelink Shrine. To access the tower, you'll need the Tower Key, which can be purchased from the Shrine Handmaid.

## Fire Keeper Soul  
  
---  
  
> Soul of a Fire Keeper who is said to have returned from the Abyss. This Fire Keeper preserves the bonfire, and serves its champion. She is said to have soothed and accepted the dark sigil, which has tainted her soul. And yet, her soul will one day embed itself in the bosom another Fire Keeper.  
  
### Fire Keeper Soul Dark Souls 3 Use

  * Give to the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") to enable healing of the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil").

### Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul Location

  * Found at the top of the Bell Tower in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), unlocked with the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key").
  * Watch the [Fextra Video](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=1h57m10s "Fextra Video") at 1h 57mins 10 secs in

### Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul Notes

  * After Irina becomes a firekeeper, despite having the Fire Keeper Soul (and the [Eyes of a Fire Keeper](/Eyes+of+a+Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Fire Keeper")) still in the player's possession, no additional dialogue or options appear for Irina.
  * This cannot be reached using the tree jump method. 
  * Giving this to the firekeeper and healing the Dark Sigils afterward will end [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor")'s questline.
