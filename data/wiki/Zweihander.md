**Zweihander** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Ultra greatsword with a large, long blade. Wielded with two hands, yet still requiring great strength due to its weight.   
>   
> Execute powerful attacks that take advantage of its weight, or utilize its sharp blade in thrust attacks.  
>   
> Skill: Stomp  
> Use one’s weight to lunge forward with a low stance and increased poise, and follow with strong attack for an upward slash.

### Notes and Tips:

  * Reinforced using [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * The Zweihander has a range of 38.3551xl on the 1h R1, and 37.2989xl on the 2h R1.
  * Base damage was increased by 5 in 1.04
  * The [Stomp](/Stomp "Dark Souls 3 Stomp") skill greatly increases poise and can interrupt most attacks, making it optimal for countering "spam attacks" such as [Elfriede's Stance](/Elfriede's+Stance "Dark Souls 3 Elfriede's Stance") and [Ringed Knight Paired Greatswords](/Ringed+Knight+Paired+Greatswords "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight Paired Greatswords").

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") or by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after giving her [Greirat's ashes](/Greirat's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Greirat's Ashes") for 6000 souls after he is sent to scavenge the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
