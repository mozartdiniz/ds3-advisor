# Soul Arrow

**Soul Arrow** is a [Sorcery](/Sorcery "Dark Souls 3 Sorcery") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"). 

  

## Soul Arrow  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 7  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 10 Intelligence  
  
> Elementary sorcery.   
> Fires a soul arrow.   
> To use sorceries, equip a staff and attune a sorcery at a bonfire.

### How to Get Soul Arrow in Dark Souls 3

  * Starting spell of the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer") class.
  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 2,000 souls
  * Bought from: [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") for 1,000 souls
  * Sold by [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 1,000 souls
  * Sold by [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 1,000 souls

###  Dark Souls 3 Soul Arrow Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Soul Arrow inflicts (Spell Buff x 1.275) magic damage before resistances.
  * Has an upgrade in [Great Soul Arrow](/Great+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Great Soul Arrow").
