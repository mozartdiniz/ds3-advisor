# Carthus Milkring

### Carthus Milkring  
  
---  
  

> Ring worn by the warriors of the sand kingdom, Carthus. Slightly boosts dexterity and obscures the wearer while rolling.  
>   
> The sword technique of Carthus allows for fluid movement with a curved sword. Masters of the technique are said to dazzle their opponents by moving as weightlessly as a grain of sand. They live for their High Lord Wolnir, conqueror of most kingdoms known to their people.  
  
**Carthus Milkring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Found within the Catacombs of Carthus after the first encounter with the rolling skeleton ball at the large stairs, continue on the path. The ring (and the vases) will be on your right side. Be careful, though, as breaking the vases unleashes flying dark spirit skulls. Only the vases with a lid have spirits in them, so proceed with caution to avoid triggering these dangerous traps. It also increases the dexterity and enhances the user's evasiveness when rolling by turning you invisible for a brief moment.

### Carthus Milkring Dark Souls 3 Effect

  * Gives +3 to [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dark Souls 3 Dexterity") and obscures wearer while rolling.
  * Weight: 0.8

### Where to Find Carthus Milkring in DS3

  * Found within the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") after the first encounter with the rolling skeleton ball at the large stairs, continue on the path, the ring (and the vases) will be on your right side. Be careful though, as breaking the vases unleashes flying dark spirit skulls. (only the vases with a lid have spirits in them)

### Dark Souls 3 Carthus Milkring Notes

  * The player vanishes in a puff of smoke when rolling. However, the effect is purely visual and does not increase the number of invulnerable frames. This can serve as a distraction however in PvP; either offensively, or defensively.

### Dark Souls 3 Carthus Milkring Trivia

  * Dark Souls 2 had a similar ring, the [Simpleton's Ring](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Simpleton's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Simpleton's Ring").
