**Follower Javelin** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").  
  
  
  

> Light spear wielded by the Farron Followers. Balanced to allow for throwing.
> 
> The Followers attack in groups, surrounding foes, shielding themselves, and thrusting their spears at their foes. On a final command, they hurl their spears at their fallen foe, to give the retired warrior an honorable send-off.
> 
> Skill: Hurl Spear
> 
> Emulate the practice of the Followers by throwing an illusory spear that assumes temporary form.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Weapon can be Buffed and Infused.
  * Has A and B scalings for the elemental infusions making it a decent option for Pyromancers, Sorcerers, and Faith users.
  * Has a bug that prevents it from preforming a rolling attack after a one-handed leaping attack like most weapons can, rather it cannot attack at all after performing a leaping attack.

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * From the Snowfield Bonfire, run to the right towards the ice trap. After falling down the ice trap, hug the right wall to avoid the wolves aggroing. After a little bit of walking, you will encounter a path up guarded by a wolf. Kill it quickly before it howls and attracts more. Up at the very top of the path, there is a group of about 5 Farron Followers who will all aggro you at once if you get near, so prepare for a fight. Behind them is a corpse holding the Javelin, and a drop to return you back to the Bonfire.
  * [Fextralife Video: Follower Javelin location](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=28m48s "Fextralife Video: Follower Javelin location")

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
