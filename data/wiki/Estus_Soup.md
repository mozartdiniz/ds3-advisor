# Estus Soup

**Estus Soup** are healing stations you encounter along your travels, where you can stop to drink from a cauldron to heal your HP. 

### Estus Soup Usage

  * A prompt will appear near each cauldron with the option to "Drink Estus Soup". 
  * Estus Soup heals 535 HP

### Estus Soup Locations

  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement"), behind a locked door in the house past the open area with the big tree. Drop down through a hole in the floor above.
  * [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Farron Keep"), near the _Keep Ruins_ bonfire, on a small island in the swamp past the ladder.
  * [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"), near the _Distant Manor_ bonfire, in the kitchen past the sewer entrance.

### Trivia

  * [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Siegward of Catarina") is the one that cooked the Estus soup. Once you enter the Irithyll kitchen and have done his quest line correctly, he ask that you have dinner with him and tell you of the fine estus soup he makes..
