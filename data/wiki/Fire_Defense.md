# Fire Defense

## Fire Defense  
  
---  
Type | Defense  
  
**Fire Defense** in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") defines the protection your character has against fire based attacks such as pyromancies and fire infused weapons. This statistic does not effect the damage you receive from Physical, Magic, Lightning, and Dark.

### Dark Souls 3 Fire Defense Information

**Fire Defense** is increased by leveling **Strength** , and to a lesser extent by leveling other stats. Different armor sets also give varying amounts of fire defense, the highest amount coming from [Havel's set](/Havel's+set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's set").
