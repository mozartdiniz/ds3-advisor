# Mossfruit

**Mossfruit** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in Dark Souls 3.

### Mossfruit  
  
---  
  

> A small clump of mossfruit.  
>   
> Temporarily boosts bleed, poison, frost, and curse resistance.  
>   
> All mossfruit is the same regardless of its color, and has the effects of moss of all colors.  
  
### Mossfruit Usage

  * Boosts [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed"), [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"), [Frost](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), and [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") resistance by 80 for 2 minutes (120 seconds).

### Mossfruit Locations

  * Sold by the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 1,500 souls, after giving her the [Easterner's Ashes](/Easterner's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Easterner's Ashes").
  * Dropped by [Basilisks](/Basilisks "Dark Souls 3 Basilisks") (frogs) in the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") swamp and below the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake")
  * Drop by Skittish Wolf

### Mossfruit Notes & Trivia

  * Same Effect as [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"). They also stack together, but their duration is different.
  * Does not stack with other [Item Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking").
  * Please do take note that due to the way resistances work in the game, the added resistances are initially filled on the status bars.
  * As a result, using this item while in the process of enduring one of the status effects (such as the poison swamps of the Profaned Capital) won't help you to counter it. Use this item when you know you will be subject to bleed/poison/frost/curse.
  * You can however use this item and then rest at a bonfire. The buff will remain active, but the status bar will be set to 0, giving you the increased status resistance for almost the full duration of the effect.
