# Rings

**Rings** are an important category of equipment in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki"). Players may equip up to 4 rings at once and they each grant specific benefits. This page has a list of all of the rings available in the game, separated by base game and [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"). The list is in the order that rings are displayed in your inventory, to make it easy to see which you are missing.

A returning feature from Dark Souls 2, rings are available in more powerful variants that appear with the +1, +2 or +3 suffixes. These enhanced rings are only found in [NG+](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus") or NG++, excluding several +3 rings that can be obtained in the DLC. Their locations and which Journey (NG cycle) they require will be noted here and on the individual ring pages. To obtain the [Trophy/Achievement](/Trophy+&+Achievement+Guide "Dark Souls 3 Trophy & Achievement Guide") for collecting every ring, you must collect all of the different +X variations available, making a total of 107 rings. Do not sell any rings you've obtained.

If you miss a ring on your first playthrough, or even your second or third, you can still obtain them on any following playthrough. Whether it's NG+2 or NG+20, all the rings stay where they originally are placed, they do not go away simply because you've continued playing. In addition to this information, you do not need to have all the rings on you at once. Provided you have picked up the ring, it counts. However, you should always put your rings into your Item Box via the bonfire. This will allow you to keep track and know for a fact what you have.

116 total rings - 9 DLC rings (8 of which are +3, the ninth is the [Chillbite Ring](/Chillbite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Chillbite Ring")) - 1 (+3) ring - 16 (+2) rings - 20 (+1) rings - 70 (+0/regular) rings - 107 rings for the achievement. The [Ring of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice") does count towards the achievement. 

The Trophy/Achievement may be glitched and despite having all 107 rings in your inventory, it may not register. The most common issue is a starter ring not registering. Drop (leave) and then pick back up the [Life Ring](/Life+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Life Ring"), [Young Dragon Ring](/Young+Dragon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring"), and/or [Great Swamp Ring](/Great+Swamp+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Ring") to trigger the achievement. An additional way to get around the glitch is to move a large amount of rings into your storage box and then back out.
