# Ringed Knight

## Ringed Knight  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Knight  
**Weakness** | Strike, Dark, Frost  
**Resistances** | Standard, Slash, Thrust, Fire, Lightning, Bleed, Poison/Toxic  
**Immune** | [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull"), [Aural Decoy](/Aural+Decoy "Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy"), [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport")  
  
**Ringed Knight** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Ringed Knight Enemy Description

  * Powerful knights that guard the streets of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City").

### Ringed Knight Combat Information

  * Appears in 3 variants: sword and shield, spear and greatshield, or paired greatswords. The first 2 variants are met either in pairs or grouped up with  [Hollow Clerics](/Hollow+Cleric "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Cleric"). 
  * The paired greatswords variant can only be met alone, and has more HP than the sword or spear variants. 
  * The sword variant can deal, [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"), and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * The spear variant can deal [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage"), [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"), [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage"), and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * The paired greatswords variant can deal [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage"), [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage"), and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * Some of their attacks can be parried.

### Ringed Knight Notes & Trivia

  * Can be used to farm [Filianore's Spear Ornament](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament") for the [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") covenant. The easiest location to do this is near the Ringed City Streets bonfire. Run through the gated shortcut and jump down off the left side to the swamp below, taking care not to aggro the nearby Judicator. Now move out towards the nearby crumbling building where three knights stand guard. With careful movement, or a few well-aimed projectiles, they can each be lured out to fight one-on-one. Past the left edge of the building is the fourth and last knight, who can be dispatched more easily by starting the fight with a plunging attack. Though they are not the safest source by far, wearing a full set of item discovery/soul farming gear makes the knights a great source not only of [Filianore's Spear Ornaments](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament") but also [Titanite Chunks](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale"), [Embers](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember"), and basic [Souls](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls"). (Efficient runs take about 2 minutes, netting a considerable number of souls in addition to any items they happen to drop.)
  * When the straight sword variant holds its blade upwards and lights it, it will either do a Soul of Cinder-esque 5-hit combo or a lunging thrust from long range.
  * The spear variant's shield roar can knock players back, so be careful when fighting them near cliffs.
