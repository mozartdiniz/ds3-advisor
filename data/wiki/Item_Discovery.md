# Item Discovery

## Item Discovery  
  
---  
  
**Item Discovery** is a derived [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") that measures how likely your character is to receive items from foes. Increasing Item Discovery increases the chance to receive weapons and armor that the enemy is wearing, as well as upgrade materials or tools.

### Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery Information

If an item has a 10% chance to drop then the item's table will  _usually_ have a weighted value of 100 for the item and 900 for nothing. The drop rate is derived from the weight of the item divided by the sum of all weights: 100 / (100 + 900) = 0.1 or 10%. Item Discovery is a multiplier to the weight of the item (Item Discovery / 100 = multiplier).

Examples:

  * An enemy has a base 10% chance to drop an item. With 100 Discovery (100 / 100 = 1), the enemy has 10% chance to drop the item. 
    * (100 * 1) / ((100 * 1) + 900) = 10%
  * An enemy has a base 10% chance to drop an item. With 200 Discovery (200 / 100 = 2), the enemy has ~18.18% chance to drop the item. 
    * (100 * 2) / ((100 * 2) + 900) = ~18.18%
  * An enemy has a base 10% chance to drop an item. With 400 Discovery (400 / 100 = 4), the enemy has ~30.77% chance to drop the item.  

    * (100 * 4) / ((100 * 4) + 900) = ~30.77%

### Dark Souls 3 Items to Increase Item Discover

  * Leveling up [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") permanently increases Item Discovery, 1 point of Discovery per point of Luck.
  * You can temporarily increase Item Discovery by 50 with [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") or 100 with [Rusted Gold Coin](/Rusted+Gold+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Gold Coin").
  * [Crystal Sage's Rapier](/Crystal+Sage's+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage's Rapier") increases Item Discovery by 50. It does not need to be held to function, but must be equipped (i.e. you can equip it in left hand, while two-handing your right hand weapon, and it will still provide the discovery bonus).
  * Holding a +10 Hollow weapon with more than 15 hollowing give +5 luck, increasing the item discovery (Can be done twice if both weapon and shield have +10)
  * [Symbol of Avarice](/Symbol+of+Avarice "Dark Souls 3 Symbol of Avarice") increases Item Discovery by 100 (but it also continually drains your health, so care should be taken when using it).
  * [Covetous Gold Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring") increases Item Discovery by 50 at base, while the +1 and +2 and +3 versions increases by 75 and 100 and 115 respectively.

### Dark Souls 3 Item Discover Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * You can also equip a Crystal Sage's Rapier in each hand for a total boost of 100 Item Discovery when both are equipped.
  * The highest possible Item Discovery is 614, when equipped with two Crystal Sage's Rapiers, the Symbol of Avarice, the Gold Serpent Ring +3, using a Rusted Gold Coin and having 99 Luck.
  * Item Discovery is calculated upon an enemy's death. Changing your Item Discovery after you have killed an enemy, but before looting their corpse (assuming they have dropped something), will not change what they have dropped.
