# Great Magic Weapon

## Great Magic Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 35  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence  
**Duration** | 60 seconds  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
**Great Magic Weapon** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> "Sorcery which improves upon Magic Weapon. Greatly reinforces right weapon with magic.   
>   
> Only sorcerer-swordsmen with special duties are permitted use of this spell. The azure blade is said to cut down heavily armored knights with ease. 

### How to Get Great Magic Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in: [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") near the shortcut door to [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"): From the Farron Keep Perimeter bonfire, turn around 180 and head towards the shortcut gate to Road of Sacrifices. The spell will be on a corpse at the gate near the Ravenous Crystal Lizard.
  * [Video of playthrough showing location](http://youtu.be/Q2rgg0fdMTs "Video location")

### Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds (Spell Buff * 0.85) in [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") to the weapon attack rating. 
    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed.
    * The buff can be easily identified by its deep blue glow.
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * The damage bonus is affected by the [Magic Clutch Ring](/Magic+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring") (+15% in PvE, +8% in PvP) and [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk") (10%).
  * Lasts 60 seconds, and can be lengthened by the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * [Young Dragon Ring](/Young+Dragon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring") and [Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring](/Bellowing+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Bellowing Dragoncrest Ring") do not modify weapon buff spells.
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit. 
    * Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Twinblades](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.
    * Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Magic Weapon](/Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon"), and has an upgrade in [Crystal Magic Weapon](/Crystal+Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Magic Weapon").
