# Soul of the Lords

### Soul of the Lords  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Lords.  
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. Since Lord Gwyn, the first Lord of Cinder, many exalted lords have linked the First Flame, and it is their very souls that have manifested themselves as defender of the flame.  
  
**Soul of the Lords** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Lords Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Sunlight Spear](/Sunlight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Spear") or [Firelink Greatsword](/Firelink+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Greatsword").

### Soul of the Lords Locations

  * Dropped by [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder").
  * [Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cUr3lVAzf-E&feature=youtu.be&t=2h53m57s)

### Soul of the Lords Notes & Trivia

  * Contains the soul of [Gwyn](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwyn+Lord+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Gwyn Lord of Cinder"), [the First Lord](/Gwyn,+the+First+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Gwyn, the First Lord"), as well as the Lords who succeeded him. Including the souls of all pilgrims of old that linked the fire (including the player characters, Chosen Undead and the Bearer of the Curse, had they linked the fire).
