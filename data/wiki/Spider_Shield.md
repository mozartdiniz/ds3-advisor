**Spider Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> A shield bearing a large etching of a spider.  
>   
> Shields of this style are commonly used by savage mountain bandits, and offer high resistance to poison.  
>   
> Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Spider Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Despite its description, it doesn't change poison resistance, nor can it block poison.
  * This shield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Spider+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Spider Shield")_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by the [Brigand](/Brigand "Dark Souls 3 Brigand"), the second hostile NPC after the first bonfire at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
  * [Spider Shield Location](https://youtu.be/IWkHmvxBIQ8)

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
