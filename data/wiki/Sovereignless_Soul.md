# Sovereignless Soul

**Sovereignless Soul** is a [Soul](/Souls) in Dark Souls 3. It is one of the [Burial Gifts](/Burial+Gifts) available to players.

### Sovereignless Soul  
  
---  
  

> The sovereignless soul of one who slept beside you. Use to acquire many souls."  
  
### Sovereignless Soul Information Effect

  * Use to acquire 2,000 souls.

### Where to Find Sovereignless Soul

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts).
  * The actual item received, Soul of a Nameless Soldier can be found in many places.

### Notes

  * Your character actually receives a Soul of a Nameless Soldier
