**Lothric Knight Greatsword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Ultra greatsword of the venerable knights of Lothric. Imbued with the strength of lightning, the trademark of dragon hunting.
> 
> Very few have demonstrated the extreme strength and dexterity required for this weapon, even in the long history of the Lothric Knights.
> 
> Skill: Stomp
> 
> Use one's weight to lunge forward with a low stance and increased poise, and follows with strong attack for an upward slash.

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed and [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") despite its natural lightning AR
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * The Lothric Knight Greatsword has a range of 39.217xl on the 1h R1, and 38.266xl on the 2h R1. It is the longest horizontal type Ultra Greatsword in the game.
  * [Lightning Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade") adds additional lightning AR to the innate lightning AR of the weapon. 
  * For physical damage, [Sharp](/Sharp+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Sharp Gem") works best, followed very closely by [Heavy](/Heavy+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Gem").
  * Blessed competes with Lightning Infusion in terms of AR, but adds HP regen. 
  * Blessed, Lightning, Chaos, and Dark infusions add Faith scaling to its lightning base damage. Additionally, Chaos and Dark provide one of the highest ARs in the game at max character level.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped from [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") carrying this weapon (Rare drop)
  * One can be found walking down the strairs directly south of the Twin Princes boss fight/bonfire. Due to the knight's proximity to a bonfire, this is a very easy place to farm for the weapon.
  * Can be purchased from [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") or [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") if you give her [Greirat's Ashes](/Greirat's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Greirat's Ashes"). Note: Greirat's Ashes will NOT allow purchase from the Shrine Handmaiden if Greirat is killed before he leaves to scavenge the second time.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
