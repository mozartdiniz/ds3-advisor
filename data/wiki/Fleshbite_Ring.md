# Fleshbite Ring

### Fleshbite Ring  
  
---  
  

> One of the bite rings native to Carim. Raises poison, bleed, frost and curse resist.  
>   
> The crafting of these rings is forbidden, perhaps owing to a fear of malleable stone. Clerics, however, dabble freely in the art.  
>   
>   
  
**Fleshbite Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings) in Dark Souls 3.

### Fleshbite Ring Effect

  * Raises [Poison](/Poison "Poison"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Bleed"), [Frost](/Frostbite "Frost") and [Curse](/Curse "Curse") resistance by 40 points each.

  * +1 Version (NG+): +60

  * Weight: 0.9

### Where to Find Fleshbite Ring

  * In the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Grand Archives") after taking the first unlocked elevator up, going up the stairs, past the magician-guys, up another flight of stairs, and on that floor in a corner near bookcases. ([video location](https://youtu.be/cUr3lVAzf-E?t=47m "video location") fextralife video starting at 47m)

  * +1 Version (NG+): Located in the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "High Wall of Lothric"). From the second bonfire, head down towards the roof to the first crystal lizard in the game. To the left of the ladder, there is a roof you have to jump onto to get this ring. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/Bbzo_4h981s "\(video location\)")
