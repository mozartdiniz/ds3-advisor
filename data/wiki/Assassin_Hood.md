**Assassin Hood** is a [Helm](/Helms) in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Assassin Set](/Assassin+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Black cloth covering the face of the assassins who lurk in the shadows.  
>   
> Grants much-needed protection to the head as well as cover from the rain while pursuing a target on the road.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Assassin](/Assassin)
  * This is sold by [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Greirat") for 1500 souls. After rescuing him from his cell in [High Wall of Lothric ](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "High Wall of Lothric ")and giving him [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+bone "Loretta's Bone") you will later be able to send him to pillage [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Undead Settlement"). Check back later after defeating your next boss and he will sell it. (see [Greirat's page](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Greirat's page") for more details on his questline.)
