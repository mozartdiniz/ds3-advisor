# Magic Weapon

## Magic Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 25  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 10 Intelligence  
Duration  | 90 seconds  
  
**Magic Weapon** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery for casters who wield swords.  
> Reinforces right weapon with magic.  
>   
> The power of the sorcerer-swordsmen of Vinheim is predicated upon this and Magic Shield.  
> Many warriors learn sorcery just for this enchantment."

### How to Get Magic Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") / [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 4,500 souls, and [Orbeck](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 3,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds (Spell Buff x 0.75) [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") to your weapon's attack power. 
    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed.
    * The buff can be identified by its bright blue glow on the affected weapon.
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * Scales with [Magic Clutch Ring](/Magic+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Magic Clutch Ring") (+15% in PvE, +8% in PvP) and [Crown of Dusk](/Crown+of+Dusk "Dark Souls 3 Crown of Dusk") (+10%).
  * Lasts 60 seconds, and can be lengthened by the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring")
  * Like all weapon buffs, Magic Weapon will only work on weapons with raw, refined, hollow, sharp, heavy, or no infusion.
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit.  
Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Paired Weapons](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.  
Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
  * Has upgrades in [Great Magic Weapon](/Great+Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Weapon") and [Crystal Magic Weapon](/Crystal+Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Magic Weapon").
