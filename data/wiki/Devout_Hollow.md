**Devout Hollow (Aka Sage's Devout)** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki"). Not to be confused with [Devout of the Deep](/Devout+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Devout of the Deep").

### Devout Hollow Description

  * Hollows that defend the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep")
  * Can be found throughout the perimeter of the Cathedral

### Devout Hollow Combat Information

  * Most of the self-immolating Hollows are found near the Cleansing Chapel bonfire, or near the main entrances to the Cathedral, and will simply mind their own business unless provoked, in which they will ignite and attempt to throw themselves at the player, chasing after them for a few seconds before falling to the ground and dying. Running in circles for a bit will prevent the player from taking damage from their attack. Some use a [Broken Straight Sword](/Broken+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Broken Straight Sword"), others use a [Long Sword](/Longsword "Dark Souls 3 Longsword").
  * Various non-immolating Hollows are also present, and will either wield a [Dagger](/Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Dagger"), a [Spear](/Spear "Dark Souls 3 Spear") and a [Wooden Shield](/Wooden+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Wooden Shield"), a [Light Crossbow](/Light+Crossbow "Dark Souls 3 Light Crossbow") with fire bolts, or an [Arbalest](/Arbalest "Dark Souls 3 Arbalest") with fire bolts.
  * Two larger, bulkier variants will wield either a [Greataxe](/Greataxe "Dark Souls 3 Greataxe") or a [Halberd](/Halberd "Dark Souls 3 Halberd"), and behave much like their [Hollow Soldier](/Hollow+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Soldier") kin

### Devout Hollow Notes & Trivia

  * May have once been the [Grave Wardens](/Grave+Warden "Dark Souls 3 Grave Warden") that can be seen around the cemetery in front of the Cathedral
