# Life Ring

### Life Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring set with a small red jewel. Raises Maximum HP.  
  
**Life Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is one of the [Burial Gifts](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts") available to players.

### Effect

  * Raises the Maximum [HP](/HP "Dark Souls 3 HP") by 7% when equipped.
  * +1 Version: +8%
  * +2 Version: +9%
  * +3 Version: +10%

### Where to Find Life Ring

  * Can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts").
  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 1500 souls after giving her the [Dreamchaser's Ashes.](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes")
  * +1 Version (NG+): On a corpse behind where [Siegward](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") sits, in the white tower in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"), watching the [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon"). () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/4M45qS5HbLw "\(video location\)")
  * +2 Version (NG+): [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") \- From the dark room where you fight the [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") in the wyvern's foot, head outside from the back exit. Turn right and drop down off the ledge. The ring is on a corpse. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/Uu9avhczaro "\(video location\)")
  * +3 Version (NG++): [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") \- Behind the throne of Prince Lothric. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/CFkjVdiVfuo "\(video location\)")

### Life Ring Notes

  * The only ring in the game with an existing +3 version, before [The Ringed City DLC](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City"). Obtained in NG++.
  * Lightest ring in the game, at 0.3 units.
  * This ring previously appeared in _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Life+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Life Ring")_. In _Dark Souls_ it was known as the [Tiny Being's Ring](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Tiny+Being's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Tiny Being's Ring").
