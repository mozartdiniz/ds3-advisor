# Lightning Damage

## Lightning Damage  
  
---  
Category | Elemental Damage  
  
**Lightning Damage** is a [Damage Type](/Damage+Type "Dark Souls 3 Damage Type") in Dark Souls 3. It is one of eight damage types presented in the original game and one of the four Elemental-type damages.

### Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage Guide

Lightning damage can often cause more damage because comparatively few armor sets provide high resistance against it.

### Lightning Damage Trivia & Notes

  * In contrast to Lighting Urn description, there are knights resistant to lightning.
  * In line with Lighting Urn description, all dragons are weak to Lightning.
  * Lightning seems to be effective against bosses using sword/axe/halberd/scythe/vessel (excluding Dragonslayer Armour and Nameless King, as they attack mainly with Lightning) and enemies with no armor.
