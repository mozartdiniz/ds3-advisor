# Purple Moss Clump

**Purple Moss Clump** is a [Consumable](/Consumables) in Dark Souls 3.

### Purple Moss Clump  
  
---  
  

> Medicinal purple moss clump. Reduces poison build-up. Cures poison.  
>   
> Poison builds up in the body, and when it breaks out, it causes gradual damage over a period of time.  
>   
> Poison can be exasperating, so be sure to carry sufficient moss clumps when traveling to a blighted area.   
  
### Purple Moss Clump Usage

  * Reduces [poison](/poison "poison") build-up and cures poison status.

### Purple Moss Clump Locations

  * 6x Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid) for 500 souls. Sells an unlimited supply after giving her [Paladin's Ashes.](/Paladin's+Ashes "Paladin's Ashes.")
  * Rare drop from small crabs in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrifices").
  * 3x Found along the wall in the swamp, to the right of the [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Farron Keep") bonfire exit.
  * 4x Found on top of a broken walkway path near the first extinguishable fire in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Farron Keep").
  * 2x Found right in front of the second extinguishable fire in [farron Keep](/farron+Keep "farron Keep").
