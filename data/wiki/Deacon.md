# Deacon of the Deep

## Deacon of the Deep  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Hollow  
**Weakness** | Hollowslayer Greatsword, ??  
**Resistances** | Magic, Dark, Strike   
**Immune** | -  
  
**Deacon of the Deep** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**

### Deacon of the Deep Enemy Description

  * Not to be confused with the boss [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep").
  * Deacons use melee attacks while in close quarters, and cast fire orbs which track distant enemies. 

### Deacon of the Deep Combat Information

  * Can be Lured.
  * Can be Rapported.
  * Can be Riposted.

### Deacon of the Deep Notes & Trivia

  * The Archdeacon set suggests that the Deacons were once members of the Way of White before falling to the deep along with Aldrich.
  * The head of the [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful") covenant, [Archdeacon McDonnell](/Archdeacon+McDonnell "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon McDonnell"), wears the robes of a blue Deacon rather than the robes of an Archdeacon.

###  Deacon of the Deep Location

  * [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep").
  * [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").
  * [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo").
