# Hawk Ring

### Hawk Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring associated with Hawkeye Gough, one of the Four Knights of Gwyn, the First Lord.  
>   
> Extends the range of arrows.  
>   
> In his later years, the giant Gough was blinded, but this did not prevent him from striking down a calamitous dragon with his Greatbow.  
  
**Hawk Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Hawk Ring Effect

  * Extends the range of [Bows](/Bows "Dark Souls 3 Bows") and [Crossbows](/Crossbows "Dark Souls 3 Crossbows").
  * Reduces damage falloff of [Throwing Knives](/Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Throwing Knife"), [Poison Throwing Knives](/Poison+Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Poison Throwing Knife") and [Kukris](/Kukri "Dark Souls 3 Kukri") at long distances.
  * Raises the throwing arcs for [Firebombs](/Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Firebomb"), [Black Firebombs](/Black+Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Black Firebomb") and [Lightning Urns](/Lightning+Urn "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Urn") when used without lock-on.
  * Extends maximum zoom range of bows, crossbows, and binoculars (minimum zoom remains the same however, so seeing the effect requires further zooming with the directional pad)
  * Weight: 0.7

### Location

  * Dropped by the bow-wielding [Giant](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement") in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement").

  * [Hawk Ring Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xMtTkXlyOXk)

### Hawk Ring Notes

  * Extends effective range by 65%.
  * Projectiles have a gradual drop in performance the further you are away from the target. This ring will increase the distance before damage dropoff, which affects the trajectory and damage dealt with arrows and bolts when shot from far away.
  * Collect all seven [Young White Branches](/Young+White+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Young White Branch") at all three white birch trees, and the Giant will die, leaving the ring behind.
  * You can kill the Giant at any time to receive the ring.
  * At some point in the late game the Giant will die of natural cause. The player can go to the top of the tower and find the giant dead with the ring at his feet.
  * The "calamitous dragon" mentioned in the ring's description is [Kalameet](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Black+Dragon+Kalameet "Dark Souls 3 Black Dragon Kalameet") from the [Artorias of the Abyss](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Artorias+of+the+Abyss+\(Expansion\) "Dark Souls 3 Artorias of the Abyss \(Expansion\)") DLC for _Dark Souls_.
  * This ring previously appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Hawk+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hawk Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Hawk+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hawk Ring")_.
