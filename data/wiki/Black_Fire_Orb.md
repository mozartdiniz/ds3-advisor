# Black Fire Orb

**Black Fire Orb** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Black Fire Orb  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 22  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 20 Intelligence/ 20 Faith  
  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Pyromancy discovered by grave wardens after High Lord Wolnir fell to the Abyss.  
>   
> Hurls a black fireball.  
>   
> The black flame inflicts dark damage, striking targets with weighty force.

### How to Get Black Fire Orb in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 10,000 souls after you retrieve the [Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome](/Grave+Warden+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome").

###  Dark Souls 3 Black Fire Orb Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.4) damage on a direct hit and (spell buff x 2.2) damage with the blast.
  * The projectile is fairly slow with a very wide arc that can cover some distance if aimed properly.
  * This spell deals [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"), not fire. It is a very good spell for damaging enemies weak to Dark.
  * In all other ways, it is simply a stronger version of [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb").
