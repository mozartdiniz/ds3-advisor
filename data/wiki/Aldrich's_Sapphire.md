# Aldrich's Sapphire

### Aldrich's Sapphire  
  
---  
  

> A malformed ring left by Aldrich, Saint of the Deep. Recovers FP from critical attacks.   
>   
> Aldrich, infamous for his appetite for flesh, apparently had the desire to share with others his joy of imbibing the final shudders of life while luxuriating in his victim's screams.  
  
**Aldrich's Sapphire** is a [Ring](/Rings) in Dark Souls 3.

### Aldrich's Sapphire Effect

  * Recovers 15 FP from critical attacks
  * Weight: 0.8

### Where to Find Aldrich's Sapphire

  * Dropped by a [Deep Accursed](/Deep+Accursed "Deep Accursed") in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Cathedral of the Deep").
  * [Fextra Video Location](https://youtu.be/YYbxMQtIwAM?t=1h42m16s)

### Notes

  * Restores 15 FP on any critical attack: backstab, riposte, or guardbreak.
  * There is an HP equivalent ring, [Aldrich's Ruby](/Aldrich's+Ruby "Aldrich's Ruby").
  * Although its uses are quite limited, it can be used effectively against the Deacons of the Deep, they are very easily backstabbed, making for a potentially infinite source of FP during the fight.
  * This ring's usefulness becomes significantly higher when paired with [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring") and the sorcery [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Hidden Body"). Being almost undetectable to enemies, backstabs become easy to deliver, and this ring will keep your FP topped up for that purpose.
