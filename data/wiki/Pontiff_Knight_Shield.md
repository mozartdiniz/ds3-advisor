**Pontiff Knight Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> Shield of the Pontiff's knights, frigid spirits that linger in Irithyll.  
>   
> This blue-gray shield, shrouded in a thinly cold air, is light and brittle.  
>   
> Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Pontiff Knight Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can not be Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Provides an average damage block of 72.4%. It's unfortunate that the shield's stability is so low, as it provides fantastic, well rounded damage block for its weight. A reliable choice to take into any situation.
  * Unfortunately, a large portion of the damage one takes is physical, meaning this shield suffers compared to 100% physical block shields of equal weight.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Drops from [Pontiff Knights](/Pontiff+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Knight") wielding this shield in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").
  * Can be bought from [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for 3000 souls (after sending him to scavenge at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement")).

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
