Name & Icon | Usage | Location  
---|---|---  
  
#### [ Darksign](/Darksign "Dark Souls 3 Darksign")

| Returns you to the last bonfire used at the cost of deleting all of your current souls. | Starting equipment.  
  
#### [ Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword")

| Thrust into the shrine bonfire to restore its power and enable travel between bonfires. |  Obtained after defeating [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr").  
  
#### [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment")

| Return to last bonfire used or to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). Can be used repeatedly. | Found in alternate Firelink Shrine after defeating [Champion Gundyr](/Champion+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Champion Gundyr") in The [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves").  
  
#### [Hello Carving](/Hello+Carving "Dark Souls 3 Hello Carving")

| When used, leaves an audible sound saying ''Hello.'' Has unlimited uses. | Trade an [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull") with Picklepum the Crow  
  
#### [Thank you Carving](/Thank+you+Carving "Dark Souls 3 Thank you Carving")

| When used, leaves an audible sound saying ''Thank you.'' Has unlimited uses. | Trade a [Hidden Blessing](/Hidden+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Blessing") with Picklepum the Crow  
  
#### [Very Good Carving](/Very+Good+Carving "Dark Souls 3 Very Good Carving")

| When used, leaves an audible sound saying ''Very good.'' Has unlimited uses. | Trade a [Divine Blessing](/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing") with Picklepum the Crow  
  
#### [Help me Carving](/Help+me+Carving "Dark Souls 3 Help me Carving")

| When used, leaves an audible sound saying ''Help me.'' Has unlimited uses. | Trade **_any sacred chime_** (not a talisman) with Picklepum the Crow  
  
#### [I'm sorry Carving](/I'm+sorry+Carving "Dark Souls 3 I'm sorry Carving")

| When used, leaves an audible sound saying ''I'm sorry.'' Has unlimited uses. | Trade a [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Shriving Stone") with Picklepum the Crow  
  
#### [Rubbish](/Rubbish "Dark Souls 3 Rubbish")

| Rubbish with no value. | Dropped by [Peasant Hollows](/Peasant+Hollow "Dark Souls 3 Peasant Hollow") and Undead Manservants in Undead Settlement  
  
#### [Binoculars](/Binoculars "Dark Souls 3 Binoculars")

| Used to view longer distances. | Found at the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").  
  
#### [Dragon Head Stone](/Dragon+Head+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Head Stone")

| Used to gain the head of a dragon. |  Obtained after defeating the [Ancient Wyvern](/Ancient+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Wyvern") in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").  
  
#### [Twinkling Dragon Head Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Head+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Head Stone")

| Used to gain a bigger head of a dragon. | Obtained after defeating [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") in a duel, as part of his questline.   
  
#### [](/Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Torso Stone")[Dragon Torso Stone](/Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Dragon Torso Stone")

| Used to gain the torso of a dragon and a dragon's roar. |  Found by the lift in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon")  
  
#### [Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone](/Twinkling+Dragon+Torso+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Dragon Torso Stone")

| Gain the torso of a dragon and roar alongside an archdragon mirage. | Obtained at the very end of [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") by using the ''Path of the Dragon'' on the altar.
