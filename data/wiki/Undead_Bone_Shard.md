# Undead Bone Shard

### Undead Bone Shard  
  
---  
  

> Undead bones that yet burn. Cast it into the shrine bonfire to boost the recovery provided by the Estus Flask. The bonfire's cinders are the bones of Undead, and a bone that still burns is a fresh cinder indeed. Before feeding upon death, one must first pray to it.  
  
**Undead Bone Shard** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Undead Bone Shards are valuable items that grant players a boost to their Estus Flasks, increasing the amount of HP restored by regular Estus Flasks or FP restored by Ashen Estus Flasks. Burning these shards at a bonfire strengthens your flasks, making them more effective and vital for your ventures in Dark Souls 3.

### Undead Bone Shard Dark Souls 3 Usage

  * Burn in the Firelink Shrine bonfire to increase the amount of HP/FP recovered when using the [Estus Flask](/Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Estus Flask") and [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask").
  * Trade with [Picklepum The Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") for the [Porcine Shield](/Porcine+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Porcine Shield") in return.

### Undead Bone Shard Dark Souls 3 Locations

  1. [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") \- Starting at the Dilapidated Bridge bonfire, travel near the white birch tree where the giant fires arrows. To the right of this tree is a platform you can jump to with the Undead Bone Shard on a dead body.
  2. [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep") \- From the Keep Ruins bonfire, head down the walkway towards the door that opens once you light the three bonfires. Before reaching the door head to the right and look for a tower full of slugs. It is in the center of that tower. [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=1h22m27s "Video location") (fextralife video starting at 1h 22m 27s)
  3. [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") \- From the Cleansing Chapel bonfire, head out and through the graveyard. Head across the bridge and up some stairs past a Grave Warden and to the right. The shard is on a tombstone that is laying out over a gap, near a white birch tree.
  4. [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") \- From the Abyss Watchers bonfire go down the stairs and to the right side of the ledge overlooking the level. Drop down on the series of platforms below, be careful of the two archer skeletons. Head through the doorway and kill the skeleton with a hat immediately to the right. This will cause the first [Skeleton Ball](/Skeleton+Ball "Dark Souls 3 Skeleton Ball"), to break on impact with the wall and reveal a shard**.**
  5. [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") \- Kill the [Carthus Sandworm](/Carthus+Sandworm "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Sandworm").
  6. [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake") \- From the Demon Ruins bonfire, head down the stairs into the long room filled with floating fireballs and demons. Head left towards the far end of the room past the enemies and the boneshard is located near the bodies of dead demons. Beware of a Fire Slime that will attempt to fall on you. ([Video](https://youtu.be/V_Q4e7W_5L8?t=1h12m57s "Video"))
  7. [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") \- Starting at the Church of Yorshka bonfire, go down the hallway stairs, and go right along the cliff. The Undead Bone shard is located behind the large tombstone at the end of this path. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=44m40s "Video"))
  8. [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital") \- After you fight the first Gargoyle of the area, it will be on the corpse of [Laddersmith Gilligan](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Laddersmith+Gilligan "Dark Souls 3 Laddersmith Gilligan") next to the bonfire.
  9. [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") \- Starting at the Dragon Barracks bonfire, go up the stairs and to the bridge where the two dragons are. Run almost all the way across the bridge and drop off at the end. The Undead Bone Shard is underneath the bridge.
  10. [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") \- Once you reach the rooftop area, head down the ladder and straight ahead until you can turn left to head up the rooftops. Head up and take a right and remain along the top until you can jump back into the archives. Defeat the Lothric knight and Thralls, walk past the wax pool, and find it on a corpse sitting in a chair. There is a ladder you can kick down here that will go back into the archive area where you will find the corpse of Orbeck (if you buy all his spells/defeat the Princes), and contains a number of Priests. It is also near the doorway that leads to the 3 NPC Hunters.
