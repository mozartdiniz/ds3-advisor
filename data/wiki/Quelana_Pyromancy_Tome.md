# Quelana Pyromancy Tome

**Quelana Pyromancy Tome** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in Dark Souls 3.

### Quelana Pyromancy Tome  
  
---  
  

> A pyromancy tome of Quelaana containing her unique spells. These pyromancies can only be taught by a female master.  
>   
> Give to a female pyromancy master to learn Quelana's pyromancies.  
>   
> Quelana, the sole surviving witch of Izalith, once accepted a human pupil, but after the pupil moved on, she never took another.  
  
### Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome Usage

  * Give to [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") to unlock [Fire Whip](/Fire+Whip "Dark Souls 3 Fire Whip"), [Firestorm](/Firestorm "Dark Souls 3 Firestorm"), [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport")

### How to Get Quelana Pyromancy Tome in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"). 
    * From the Demon Ruins bonfire, continue down the stairs. Take a short right to find the nearest and next set of stairs that lead into a room with two [Demon Clerics](/Smoldering+Ghru "Dark Souls 3 Smoldering Ghru"). Hug the right wall to find a corridor on your right with a rat at the other end. Take the first right to find a root-infested catacomb with rats and interact with the illusory wall directly across from you. On your left will be the corpse resembling Quelaag's Sister with the tome.  
  

    * Alternatively, from the Old King's Antechamber bonfire, take the corridor from the opposite corner of the room to enter the same room with the two Demon Clerics. You'll need to take a left this time to find the corridor with the rat in the right corner.  
  

    * You may follow the rat and drop into a lava pit to pick up two items; [Toxic Mist](/Toxic+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Toxic Mist") and the [White Hair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman"). Take the stairs up out of the pit.  
  
Note: Lava hurts. Bring fire protection (i.e [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat")) and be ready to drink estus quickly. Mind the lava slimes.

### Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * The cloth wrapped around the tome is reminiscent of bandages that may be wrapped around a burn. 
    * It is also reminiscent of the [Gold-Hemmed Black Set](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gold-Hemmed+Black+Set "Dark Souls 3 Gold-Hemmed Black Set") that Quelana wore in Dark Souls 1

  * [Quelana of Izalith](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Quelana+of+Izalith "Dark Souls 3 Quelana of Izalith"), whom the description refers to, is a pyromancer from Dark Souls 1, who taught the three pyromancies given by this tome.
  * The corpse lies before a spider resembling the [Fair Lady](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Daughter+of+Chaos "Dark Souls 3 Daughter of Chaos") from Dark Souls 1. Quelana and the Fair Lady were sisters, both daughters of chaos.
  * Attempting to give the tome to [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") grants dialogue, revealing the fate of Quelana.
