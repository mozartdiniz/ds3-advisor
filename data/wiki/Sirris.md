**Sirris of the Sunless Realms** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Sirris is a member of the Blade of the Darkmoon who is on a quest to end her grandfather, Hodrick. She is a summonable character but can also request assistance from you when she needs help with her opponents. She is first encountered at Firelink Shrine after the Halfway Fortress Bonfire has been ignited.

You won't be able to interact with her the first time because she leaves afterward, but the second time you encounter her, when you deliver the Dreamchaser's Ashes to the Shrine Handmaid, she introduces herself again at Firelink Shrine. During this second encounter, she also teaches you the Darkmoon Loyalty gesture.

### Sirris Dark Souls 3 General Information

Can be killed. Once dead, the player can buy her [Armor Set](/Sunless+Set "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Set") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").

  * Gives "Darkmoon Loyalty" gesture.
  * Equipment and Spells in combat: [Estoc](/Estoc "Dark Souls 3 Estoc"), [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman"), [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade"), [Great Heal](/Great+Heal "Dark Souls 3 Great Heal"), [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier").
  * Can be summoned for [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") and [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep") after meeting her for a second time in Firelink Shrine. Can be summoned for [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") and the [Twin Princes](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") after she swears loyalty to the player.

### Sirris Dark Souls 3 Questline

  1. Start: [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") trigger. Speak with Sirris of the Sunless Realms in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after you speak to [Horace](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed") and [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora").
  2. Find and give the [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes") to the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in the Firelink Shrine.
  3. Speak with Sirris at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and gain the Darkmoon Loyalty [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  4. Kill the [Sulyvahn's Beast](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast") at the bridge near the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") bonfire. Reload the world and find her summon sign on the bridge around where the [Sulyvahn's Beast](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast") spawned. Use it to be summoned into her world and help her kill [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer"). (*note* You have to light the Central Irithyll bonfire to get the sign to appear.) [Sirris's Sign Location](https://youtu.be/AHdkj0iKCeA "Sirris's sign location at boreal valley")
     * For the Creighton the Wanderer interaction you don't need to kill [Curse-rotted Greatwood](/Curse-rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-rotted Greatwood"). You only need to light the second bonfire across the bridge, teleport back to first bonfire from there and you'll see the sign.
     * If her summon sign does not appear killing the Curse-rotted Greatwood makes it appear.
     * If you've already killed the Cursed-rotten Greatwood and her sign doesn't appear, warp back to Firelink after activating the bonfire after the bridge then go back to the beginning of the bridge. Her sign should appear. If you haven't crossed the bridge and lit the bonfire on the other side, her sign may not appear.
     * You don't actually have to kill the [Sulyvahn's Beast](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Dark Souls 3 Sulyvahn's Beast"), just cross the bridge and her sign should appear once you reload the area.
     * If her sign continues to not appear, completely resetting the game has been shown to solve the problem on occasion.
     * Make sure that she left [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after you've given her the [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamchaser's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamchaser's Ashes") and received the gesture, if you travel to Irithyll right after talking to her the sign will not be there yet.
     * You have to kill [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Deacons of the Deep") to receive the "small doll" to pass through the barrier at the end of the starting bridge in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, then reload the area.
  5. Talk to her at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to receive a Blessed [Mail Breaker](/Mail+Breaker "Dark Souls 3 Mail Breaker") and the [Silvercat Ring](/Silvercat+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Silvercat Ring").
  6. After defeating [Aldrich](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods") __ use the summon sign outside the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood") boss area to be summoned into her world and help her kill [Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick").  
Note: For this step, a unique [Budding Green Blossom](/Budding+Green+Blossom "Dark Souls 3 Budding Green Blossom") will appear within the [Shrine Handmaid's](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") shop inventory (Costing 10 souls). The item description has a special notation that reads: "Good-bye, dear Grandmother. I will visit [Grandfather](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick"), in the Pit of Hollows." Sirris probably left the item with the Shrine Handmaid, before confronting [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick"), her Grandfather. Its appearance in the shop can be used to confirm your progress in the questline, but purchasing it is not required. Defeating [Yhorm](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") is also not required. (if the summon sign does not appear outside of the Curse-Rotted Greatwood fight, killing the Curse-Rotted Greatwood will spawn the sign)
  7. When you return to your world, [Holy Knight Hodrick's](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick") armor (the [Sunset Armor Set](/Sunset+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Sunset Armor Set")) will be right by the Pit of Hollows bonfire and the [Sunset Shield](/Sunset+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunset Shield") will be at Hodrick's grave, between Firelink Shrine and the [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") boss bonfire, where the hollow standing at the edge of the cliff can be found. NOTE: You may need to log out and then back in for the items to appear. Picking up the shield may be necessary to make her summon sign appear before the [Twin Princes](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") boss fight.
  8. Speak with her for the final time at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), and she will swear fealty to you.   
If you decline she disappears from the Firelink Shrine and becomes hollow at the Pit of Hollows.   
If you accept her vows, she will not fight back if you choose to kill her, allowing the player to easily get her equipment.
  9. After defeating the [Twin Princes](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince"), her corpse can be found at her grandfather's grave between the Firelink Shrine and Iudex Gundyr bonfires, containing the [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman") and her [armor](/Sunless+Set "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Set") can now be purchased at the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").  
  

### Sirris DS3 Questline Quirks and Failures

  * She leaves [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and becomes hostile if the player gives [Rosaria](/Rosaria,+Mother+of+Rebirth "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth") a [Pale Tongue](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") to increase [her covenant's](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") level and may leave if the player kills [Company Captain Yorshka](/Company+Captain+Yorshka "Dark Souls 3 Company Captain Yorshka") (as of regulation 1.05, simply changing your appearance or reallocating stats does not fail her questline).
  * Doing the above before looting her body will result in her turning hostile.
  * It is no longer possible to speedrun to Rosaria to offer a Pale Tongue before ever meeting Sirris to avoid failing her quest. Sirris will simply not show up, and giving the Dreamchaser's Ashes to the Handmaid does not fix this.
  * She'll skip first encounter dialogue if the player gets the [Dreamchaser's Ashes](/Dreamcatcher's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Dreamcatcher's Ashes") before talking to her once.
  * If you want to speed up the last step, kill her after she pledges herself to you, since this would have the same effect as the last step (for doing Leonhard's quest for the Crescent Moon Sword and other items).
  * Denying her offer for loyalty will result in her becoming hollow. She can be found praying at the Pit of Hollows altar, just like her grandfather. Defeating her will drop the [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman").

### Sirris Dark Souls 3 Notes & Tips

  * If you by any chance failed to retrieve the [Mound Maker](/Mound+Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound Maker") covenant before killing the [Curse-Rotted Greatwood](/Curse-Rotted+Greatwood "Dark Souls 3 Curse-Rotted Greatwood"), you will receive the covenant after defeating [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick") in the Pit of Hollows in Sirris' questline (will be received directly when spawning back in your world).
  * Her summon sign for the Deacons of the Deep battle is next to the fog gate. The Dried Finger will let the player summon Anri, Horace, and Sirris for the battle.
