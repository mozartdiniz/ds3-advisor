# Great Magic Barrier

**Great Magic Barrier** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Great Magic Barrier  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 40  
**Slots Used** | 2  
**Requirements** | 25 Faith  
**Type** | Body Buff  
**Duration** | 70 Seconds  
  
> The tale which was later interpreted as Magic Barrier.  
>   
> Greatly increases magic damage absorption by covering the body in a strong white protective coating.  
>   
> Said to be a tale of Havel the Rock, arch enemy of Seath the Scaleless. Havel despised magic, and was never complacent in preparing means to counter it.

### How to Get Great Magic Barrier in Dark Souls 3

  * [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak") \- Start at the Great Belfry Bonfire and go out the exit and down the stairs. Cross the courtyard and go up the stairs on the opposite side of the ones you just went down. Move into the building. Be aware that there will be a Rock Lizard immediately to your left next to a ladder. Climb the ladder. Once you reach the top of the ladder and can see a dead dragon straight in front of you, turn left. You should see a wall with a huge chunk missing from it. Go to this missing chunk. Drop down from the building. Turn right and follow the path (a rather short path, mind you). The path will lead you down a short flight of stairs and you will encounter another Rock Lizard. Just behind the Rock Lizard is a corpse with Great Magic Barrier in it ([video location](http://youtu.be/7BrIEaV-wmk "video location")).

### Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Does not stack with other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial") and [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier").
  * Increases [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") absorption by 60%.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Magic Barrier](/Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier").
