**Pilgrim from Londor** (Londor Pilgrim) is a [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Pilgrim from Londor Information

Her name is unknown. She's a pilgrim and an assassin in the service of the Sable Church of Londor. She's guiding Anri of Astora, from the shadows, at the request of Yuria of Londor.

  * Tied to [Yuria of Londor's](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") questline, which also involves [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") and [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora").
  * Gives [Sword of Avowal](/Sword+of+Avowal "Dark Souls 3 Sword of Avowal") at the end of Anri of Astora's questline.
  * Drops the [Chameleon](/Chameleon "Dark Souls 3 Chameleon") sorcery.
  * Yuria's questline will end abruptly if you kill the pilgrim before she gives you the Sword of Avowal.

### Location

  * You can first find the Pilgrim from Londor at the _Church of Yorshka_ in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").  

    * She is standing in the corner next to the bonfire, disguised as a statue among other statues.
    * Hit or kick the statue, to break the Chameleon spell, if you have to talk to her.
  * Next you can find her in front of the Darkmoon Tomb, located behind an illusory wall, in the circular room before the revolving staircase that leads to _Anor Londo_. 
    * She will give you the [Sword of Avowal](/Sword+of+Avowal "Dark Souls 3 Sword of Avowal") to initiate the marriage ceremony with [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora").
    * The illusory wall should be gone if you have talked to Yuria after defeating [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn").
  * If you have ignored and or missed Anri's questline the pilgrim will be found dead behind the illusory wall in Darkmoon Tomb, the "Chameleon" sorcery can still be obtained from her body.

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (2,000) NG+ (4,000)
  * The [Chameleon](/Chameleon "Dark Souls 3 Chameleon") sorcery.  

    * Killing her in the _Church of Yorshka_ will break Yuria's questline. If you on the other hand are doing Anri's quest to defeat Aldrich, then she must be killed before progressing further.
    * She dies of natural causes after you go through the marriage ritual with [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora"), dropping the spell without killing her.
    * She can also be killed without any reprecussions after she has given you the ceremonial sword.

### Trivia

  * She is referred to as the "Old Woman of Londor" in the end credits, and is voiced by Jenny Funnell. Her voice can also be heard at the end of the _Usurp the Fire_ [ending](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").
  * It is not explicitly stated anywhere that the Pilgrim from Londor is the one who actually kills [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora"), for all we know she could just be a guide and a spy. There's another npc who is just as likely to be the culprit, namely the [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade"), who we know for sure is an assassin.
