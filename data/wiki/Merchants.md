# Merchants

**Merchants** in Dark Souls 3 provide various services to the player, from selling [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") and [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") to being purveyors and trainers of [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"), [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") and [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles"). As you progress in the game, certain merchants can expand their inventory by giving them obtained [Key Items](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") such as tomes and scrolls. Players may also give [Ashes](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes") they've obtained to the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in Firelink Shrine and she will prepare new items for sale.
