**Alva, Seeker of the Spurned** is a [NPC](/Invading+NPC+Phantoms "Dark Souls 3 Invading NPC Phantoms") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Alva, Seeker of the Spurned Information

  * Can be killed.
  * Invades just before the entrance of [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"), if the player is embered.
  * Invades on the streets of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)"), whether you are embered or not.
  * List of items that refer to this NPC: [Black Witch Set](/Black+Witch+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Witch Set")
  * Cannot be summoned.

### Location

  * You can find Alva, Seeker of the Spurned at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") past the _Distant Manor_ bonfire, on the staircase leading to the [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"). Invades as a dark spirit in the entrance of the doorway leading to the Bonfire. The invasion is triggered the moment you set foot on the bridge outside the manor.
  * Alva does not invade if [Yhorm](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") has already been defeated.
  * From the location of [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira") at the top of the stairs on the streets of the [Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)"), you should head down and to the right, crossing the first bridge over the stairs. If you keep going there will be a [Ringed Knight](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight"). From here, go to the left of the monument and the invasion will be triggered. Alva will be hiding round a corner at the end of this pathway, guarding the [Black Witch Set](/Black+Witch+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Witch Set").

### Drops

  * Souls: NG (3658), NG+ (6058), NG++ (??), NG+3 (??), NG+6 (??), NG+9 (??)
  * Weapons: [Murakumo](/Murakumo "Dark Souls 3 Murakumo")
  * [His armor](/Alva+Set "Dark Souls 3 Alva Set") can be found near [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla")'s cell, in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"), after he has been defeated.

### Combat Information

  * Wields: [Murakumo](/Murakumo "Dark Souls 3 Murakumo") (RH) and a [Arbalest](/Arbalest "Dark Souls 3 Arbalest") crossbow (LH).
  * Mainly fights two-handed with weapon arts up close, switches to wielding one-handed with an off-hand crossbow at distance.
  * Uses the normal curved greatsword moveset and is easily staggered.
  * Alva doesn't heal. (needs confirmation)
  * The doorway back to the _Distant Manor_ will be blocked by a fog wall during the invasion, so he must be fought at the cliff or inside the dungeon.
  * Be wary of falling off the nearby cliff if dodging. 
  * Can be easily killed by tossing an [Alluring Skull](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull") near the edge of the cliff, he'll run right towards it and fall to his death.

### Trivia

  * Is referred to only as “Seeker of the Spurned” in his invasion in the Ringed City.
  * Alva's armor from _Dark Souls 2_ mentions "Alva crossed many a land in search of a cure for Saint Serreta's sickness, but failed and relinquished his knighthood. Alva was once wracked with guilt and remorse, but rediscovered his purpose in life with the aid of the witch who once plotted against him," explaining the change of title from "The Wayfarer" to "Seeker of the Spurned".
