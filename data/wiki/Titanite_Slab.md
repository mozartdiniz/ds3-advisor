# Titanite Slab

**Titanite Slab** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Titanite Slab  
  
---  
  

> Titanite slab for weapon reinforcement, said to once belong to the gods. Reinforces weapons to their highest level.  
>   
> Titanite slabs are smithing materials of the gods, and weapons reinforced with slabs will be admired no less than their previous legendary weapons.  
  
### Titanite Slab Usage

  * Reinforces a weapon to its ultimate form: 
    * +10 for regular weapons
    * +5 for special weapons

### Titanite Slab Locations

  * 2x at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"): 
    * 1x can be obtained by trading the [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment") with the [Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
    * 1x can be bought for 30,000 souls from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") once all the [Cinders of a Lord](/Cinders+of+a+Lord "Dark Souls 3 Cinders of a Lord") are placed on their respective thrones.
  * 1x at [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"): received from [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") after releasing him from his cell.
  * 3x at [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")
    * 1x Found in a chest behind a bookcase on the same floor as the large wax basin. Pull the lever on the floor above, between two haunted bookcases, near the second place the [ Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") flees to. The bookcase and chest are next to the railing overlooking the ground floor; you can see the bookcase move when you pull the lever.
    * 1x is obtained after killing the three [Golden Winged Knights](/Ascended+Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ascended Winged Knight") at the very top of [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). Start from the _Grand Archives_ bonfire and use the elevator. Head outside towards the [Lothric Princes](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") boss area and immediately turn left onto a spiraling staircase. Make your way to the dome shaped roof to find the knights. 
    * 1x found after activating the elevator outside the [Lothric Princes](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") boss fight. Get the elevator to the lower floor, near the [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") boss fight. Trigger the elevator to go up, and jump out immediately - a different elevator platform will rise in its place. Ride this platform down to find the Titanite Slab in a small niche. 
  * 2x at [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak")
    * 1x next to [Havel Knight](/Havel+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Havel Knight").
    * 1x can be found after defeating the [Nameless King](/Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Nameless King"). Walk ahead from the bonfire and loot the nearest corpse.
  * 3x at [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel")
    * 1x near the  _Depths of the Painting_ bonfire. Head left past the [Giant Crabs](/Giant+Crab "Dark Souls 3 Giant Crab") and past several [Tree Women](/Tree+Woman "Dark Souls 3 Tree Woman"), until you come to an icy enclosed area with a [Smoldering Tree Woman](/Tree+Woman "Dark Souls 3 Tree Woman") guarding the [Snap Freeze](/Snap+Freeze "Dark Souls 3 Snap Freeze") spell. After defeating her, head back and you will notice that a ladder has appeard on your left hand side. Climb the ladder, and then climb another ladder to find a body with a Titanite Slab on it. 
    * 1x received after defeating [Sister Friede's](/Sister+Friede+NPC "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede NPC") second phase. Note that she also has a third phase, which will start shortly after receiving the Slab. (only once per NG cycle)
    * 1x in the Corvian Settlement. Received by speaking to the [Corvian Crow](/Corvian+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Crow") NPC after defeating [Sister Friede](/Sister+Friede "Dark Souls 3 Sister Friede"). He will have moved from his previous spot, now standing outside the house, near the ladder leading down to the gulch. (If you kill him before you killed Sister Friede the slab will be on the desk near the location where he was originally.)
  * 4x at [The Ringed City ](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\) "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)")
    * [Lapp](/Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Lapp") will tell you there's treasure in a cavern in Earthen Peak, right at the the swamp before the third Angel. It's guarded by a Harald Knight in the left corner. If you die 3 times or miss it and come back to Lapp he will gift it to you and you'll find the Harald Knight slain.
    * Beating [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church") earns you a slab. (only once per NG cycle)
    * [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira") will give you a slab for killing [Darkeater Midir](/Darkeater+Midir "Dark Souls 3 Darkeater Midir"). You can also get it from her by killing her after the final boss in the chapel to the right of Filianore's Rest's bonfire.
    * Kill the [Ringed Knight](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight") wandering in the final area alone to the left for one. (only once per NG cycle)

### Notes

  * The maximum amount of Titanite Slabs the player can get in a single [New Game](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus") cycle is **8** in the base game, **3** more in the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") DLC, and 4 more in the [Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC, for the total of**15**.
  * It's worth checking the [Farming](/Farming "Dark Souls 3 Farming") page if you want to upgrade a lot of weapons before your NG+, to make max souls.

### Trivia

The runes etched on the Titanite Slab have a possible translation, explained [in this article](https://fextralife.com/the-blacksmiths-tale-deciphering-runes-in-dark-souls-3/ "on this article"). The translation is vague and uncertain, and it is as follows:  
  

_Line 1:_  
_The meek sought God/the divine in the trees (archtrees). Yggdrasil, the tree of life (still archtrees) bestowed the gifts of plenty, joy and health._

_Line 2:_  
_They remained and hardship set in. They found a spark (Lord Souls in the first flame) which turned into a Sun, driving away the cold/ice. Terrible trouble came (conflict with dragons). The Sun (Gwyn and his allies/army) ended this conflict._

_Line 3:_  
_This age (gods in Anor Londo) continued and began an era of wealth and plenty. Man will (or has begun to) ascend. Cold/ice will return._
