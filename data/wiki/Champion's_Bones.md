# Champion's Bones

**Champion's Bones** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Champion's Bones  
  
---  
  

> The charred but warm bones of a champion.  
> Burn at the Firelink Shrine bonfire to participate in Undead Matches.  
>   
> Long ago, an Undead declared a fight.   
> A fight to celebrate their undeath, and so to preserve what remained of their souls.   
> So it was that the Undead Matches were born.  
>   
> The merit of an Undead is measured in deaths.   
> Could there be a greater gift for such a creature, than a fight that has no end?  
  
### Champion's Bones Usage

  * Burn at Firelink Shrine bonfire to unlock access to the Undead Match in the [Hollow Arena](/Hollow+Arena "Hollow Arena")

### Champion's Bones Locations

  * Defeat [Champion's Gravetender & Gravetender Greatwolf](/Champion's+Gravetender+&+Gravetender+Greatwolf "Champion's Gravetender & Gravetender Greatwolf") in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Painted World of Ariandel")
  * Can be obtained from [Lapp's](/Lapp) questline in the [Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\)) if one does not own the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel) [DLC](/DLC)

### Champion's Bones Notes

  * Champion's Bones will be consumed by burning them at the Firelink Shrine's bonfire; however, you keep the option of entering the [Hollow Arena](/Hollow+Arena) in next game cycles
