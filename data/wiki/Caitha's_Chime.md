**Caitha's Chime** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in [Dark Souls 3.](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")

> Sacred chime blessed by Caitha, goddess of tears.   
>   
> Rare even amongst Carim clerics. Affected by intelligence, a rare thing for miracle catalysts, and also agreeable with miracles that lean towards the Dark. Its existence is concealed in the name of the Archbishop, as it is anathema.  
>   
> Skill: Gentle Prayer  
> Recovers HP for a period of time, albeit extremely slowly. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Caitha's Chime Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed. 
  * Can only cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Dark miracles cast with this chime will scale with both Intelligence and Faith up to a 45/45 softcap. Dark miracles cast with any other chime will scale only with Faith up to 60, and will deal only less damage.
  * Contrary to this chime's description, its Intelligence scaling only applies to dark miracles and will have no effect on miracles that deal lightning or physical damage
  * When casting normal miracles with this chime, only the Faith scaling is considered and its softcap is at the normal amount of 60 Faith.
  * The chime has to remain active in your hand for the weapon art to continue regenerating health.
  * At 60 INT and 60 FTH it has a spellbuff of 312.
  * This chime previously appeared in _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Caitha's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Caitha's Chime")_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle")  

    * Start at Dragonslayer Armor Bonfire
    * Go back towards the entrance to the bossfight and turn left. Before you should be a chapel with an elevator in it
    * Enter the chapel, then go up the first flight of stairs within, turn left to find an open doorway and use it to exit the chapel
    * Once on the balcony, turn left to see a break in the guard rail, then very slowly walk off the balcony through the break. Fall damage in Dark Souls 3 scales with your maximum HP, therefore a higher amount of HP is not required, however the [Silvercat Ring](/Silvercat+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Silvercat Ring") and the [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook") [sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") will help if you are not interested in taking fall damage.
    * Upon landing, turn left and make your way to the end of that section of roof. You have a very small drop to make. It should not cause fall damage. Drop down, then pick up Caitha's Chime from the corpse on that ledge.
