# Heal Aid

**Heal Aid** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [miracles](/miracles "Dark Souls 3 miracles").

  

## Heal Aid  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
******FP** | 27  
****** Slots** | 1  
******Faith** | 8  
  
> Miracle imparted as charity to those of little faith. A show of tolerance from the Way of White.  
>   
> Slightly restores HP.  
>   
> To use miracles, equip a talisman or sacred chime and attune a miracle at a bonfire.

### How to Get Heal Aid in Dark Souls 3

  * Starting item for [Herald](/Herald "Dark Souls 3 Herald") class.
  * Bought from: [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 500 Souls

### Dark Souls 3 Heal Aid Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores HP equal to 150% * Spell Buff of used [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") or [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans")
  * Not affected by miracle rings.
  * Can be cast with a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes") or [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") even if you do not meet its Faith requirement.
  * Due to the miniscule requirements and fast casting time, this spell can be quite useful on any build, even ones that don't use spells.
