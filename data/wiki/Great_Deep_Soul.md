# Great Deep Soul

## Great Deep Soul  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 9  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 20 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Great Deep Soul** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery which improves upon Deep Soul. Fires powerful darkened soul dregs
> 
> Archdeacon McDonnell, a sorcerer himself, delighted in the cathedral's stagnating souls. For him, they represented the glorious bedrock of this world. 

### How to Get Great Deep Soul in Dark Souls 3

  * Rank 1 reward from [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful") covenant. (You must offer 10 [Human Dregs](/Human+Dregs "Dark Souls 3 Human Dregs") to reach rank 1)

### Dark Souls 3 Great Deep Soul Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (Spell buff x 1.3) damage.
  * This spell deals dark damage, and as such will be slightly more powerful when cast with a dark-friendly staff with high Intelligence and Faith.
  * A pure Intelligence build with no Faith investment can still cast this spell with any other staff, and it will only deal 30-50 less damage.
  * Only a maximum of 2 dark balls can be present at a time.
  * This spell is great for catching people that are running away from you because of the decent tracking and relatively slow speed which gets faster the farther it travels.
