**Black Iron Greatshield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. [Greatshields](/Greatshields "Dark Souls 3 Greatshields") usually weigh a decent amount and provide the best protection in the game. They are often used by players who have high [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength"), as they require a good amount in order to use.

> Greatshield made of black iron, deeply feared for its association with Knightslayer Tsorig.  
>   
> Black iron offers high defense, and is particularly effective at warding off fire.  
>   
> Skill: Shield Bash  
> Without lowering your guard, strike the enemy with the shield to knock them back or stagger them. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Black Iron Greatshield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed and Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Provides an average block of 74.8%.
  * Has 71 stability when fully upgraded.
  * The maxed(+10) reinforced and Fire(Gem) infused has 99.6% fire defense. However, this does reduce the physical and other elemental defenses. This near-perfect fire defense makes it a good shield for walking through the lava in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").
  * This greatshield first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Black+Iron+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Black Iron Greatshield")_ wielded by [Black Iron Tarkus](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Iron+Knight+Tarkus "Dark Souls 3 Iron Knight Tarkus").
  * The shield was designed as part of a contest FromSoftware had before _Dark Souls_ was released. Adam Ledzion is the name of the creator and the original name for the shield was the “Blood and Souls Shield”. He was credited as a Shield Contest Design Winner in the end credits of _Dark Souls_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Knight Slayer Tsorig](/Knight+Slayer+Tsorig "Dark Souls 3 Knight Slayer Tsorig") underneath the [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"). He can be found beyond the areas with the rats and the basilisks (frog-like creatures whose attacks inflict the [curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") status on players) but before the exit that goes up to the ballista. (Part 5 - 1:36:50)

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
