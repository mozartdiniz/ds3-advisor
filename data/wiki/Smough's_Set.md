**Smough's Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") Set found in **Dark Souls 3**.  
  
  

> Grotesque armor associated with Smough, the last knight to stand in defense of the ruined cathedral.
> 
> Boasts extremely high defense and can be donned by humans, but not without great difficulty.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 31,000 souls after defeating [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods").

  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Smough's Armour Set is extremely heavy, although it makes up for it with the high resistances and damage absorption.
  * Do not expect to be able to mid-roll without having a very high amount of [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality").
  * Lore regarding [Smough](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Ornstein+and+Smough "Dark Souls 3 Ornstein and Smough").
