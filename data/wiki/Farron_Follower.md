# Farron Follower

## Farron Follower  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Hollow  
**Weakness** | [Fire](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"), [Hollowslayer Greatsword](/Hollowslayer+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Hollowslayer Greatsword")  
**Resistances** | [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite"), [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed")  
**Immune** | N/A  
  
**Farron Follower** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") DLC for Dark Souls 3.

### Farron Follower Enemy Description

  * Low-rank members of Undead Legion sent into Ariandel.
  * Common enemy type in that place.

### Farron Follower Combat Information

  * Often met in large groups.
  * Torch and Sabre variant can't use Follower's torch weapon art, but can unleash barrage of slashes. Deals [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage"), [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * Spear variant uses Follower's Javelin weapon art, can also charge at player when in close proximity. Deals [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage")
  * Spear and Shield variant will attack with his Spear while holding his shields up, what allows to guard-break him. Deals [Thrust Damage](/Thrust+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Thrust Damage") and [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage").
  * Torch variant uses Follower's torch weapon art or use that torch like a sword. Can also shout, which attracts other Followers. Deals [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") and [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage").
  * Weak to [Fire Damage](/Fire+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage"), [Poison/Toxic](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Hollowslayer Greatsword](/Hollowslayer+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Hollowslayer Greatsword").
  * Resistant to [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed").
  * All variants can be parried.
  * Can be lured.
  * Can be rapported.

### Farron Follower Notes & Trivia

  * They can't drop their weapons. However, in few spots in Painted World of Ariandel, player can find weapons they use. 
  * Probably sent there because some members of Undead Legion in that region were corrupted by the Abyss.
  * Some of them sleep, so player can approach them and finish with 1 strong hit or poison without waking them up.
