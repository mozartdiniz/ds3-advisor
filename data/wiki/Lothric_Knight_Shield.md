**Lothric Knight Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in **Dark Souls 3**. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.  
  
  

> Shield of the renowned Lothric Knights, decorated with the royal crest.
> 
> The Lothric Knights contended with dragons, and their shields have suitably high lightning absorption befitting a dragonslayer's arms."
> 
> Skill: Parry
> 
> Repel an attack at the right time to follow up with a critical hit. Works while equipped in either hand.

### Lothric Knight Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Provides an average block of 70%.
  * Boasts the highest stability of all medium parrying shields: 66 stability at +10 upgrade level.
  * Infusing with a [Lightning Gem](/Lightning+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Gem") at +10 upgrade level will result in a Lightning damage absorption value of 95.7% while guarding, the highest unbuffed value possible for all shields (narrowly leading the [Dragonslayer Greatshield](/Dragonslayer+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Greatshield") by 0.7%).

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by any [Lothric Knight](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") wielding this shield 
  * Sold by [Greirat](/Greirat "Dark Souls 3 Greirat") for 3000 souls (after sending him to scavenge at Irithyll Valley)

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
