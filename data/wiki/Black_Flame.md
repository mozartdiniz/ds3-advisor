# Black Flame

## Black Flame  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**FP Cost** | 25  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 15 Intelligence / 15 Faith  
Type | Melee  
  
**Black Flame** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast it, you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancy "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy").

> Pyromancy discovered by grave wardens after High Lord Wolnir fell to the Abyss.  
>   
> Creates a giant, black flame in hand.  
>   
> Black flames born from the Abyss bear no shadow. They are said to be the impenetrable fires of humanity." 

### How to Get Black Flame in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 10,000 souls after you give her the [Grave Warden Pyromacy Tome](/Grave+Warden+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Black Flame Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.4) damage on a direct hit and (spell buff x 2.2) damage on its edge.
  * In all other ways, it is simply a stronger version of Great Combustion.
  * This spell gets significantly stronger when used with the [Pyromancer's Parting Flame](/Pyromancer's+Parting+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer's Parting Flame"), thanks to its unique trait of leaving trails of fire during the casting animation, leading to extra damage.
  * Rather than doing fire damage it does dark damage.
  * This spell first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Black+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Black Flame")_.
