**Spirit Tree Crest Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. As they are more than just a barrier used to distance yourself from imminent attacks, [Standard Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") are widely available and allow a player to mesh their main-hand weapons into a personalized playstyle. With offensive applications in addition to their apparent defensive application, strategic use of the shield allows for more than simple damage reduction.

> A knight's shield engraved with a crest depicting a spirit tree.  
>   
> One of the enchanted blue shields. The Spirit Tree Crest Shield greatly reduces lightning damage.

### Spirit Tree Crest Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can not be Infused.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Provides an average damage block of 71.8%. Has high lightning damage block, second to the [Lothric Knight Shield](/Lothric+Knight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight Shield").
  * One of the crest Shields, each one specializes in a different niche.
  * This shield first appeared in _Dark Souls 2_ as the [Spirit Tree Shield](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Spirit+Tree+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Spirit Tree Shield").

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in a chest in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). From the Dragon Barracks bonfire, head toward the narrow bridge guarded by the two dragons. Drop down between the first two stone posts on the left side of the bridge, and walk along the ledge up the stairs and through the doorway on your right(past the crossbow, greataxe, and halberd wielding hollow soldiers). Immediately after exiting the narrow doorway turn to your right and you will see a set stairs going down, the shield is inside one of four chests guarded by an [Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
