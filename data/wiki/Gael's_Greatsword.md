**Gael's Greatsword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3 [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").   
  
  
  

> Greatsword of Slave Knight Gael, the only weapon that he kept with him from beginning to end.
> 
> Originally an executioner's sword made for decapitation, this blade is heavily chipped and stained with the blood of countless battles.
> 
> Skill: Blade of Peril  
> A precarious technique unique to undead Gael. Leap in any direction, slamming the greatsword to the ground, then follow with normal attack for a large spinning slash, or strong attack to back-step and jump foward in an overhead slam.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be infused or buffed. 
  * A true combo for this weapon (while two-handing) is R1+L2+R1.
  * Upgrades with [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").
  * Gael's Greatsword +5 has **504** AR at 40 strength and 40 dexterity.
  * The sword resembles a broken version of the [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword"). Before the boss fight with [Slave Knight Gael](/Slave+Knight+Gael "Dark Souls 3 Slave Knight Gael"), while he is still an NPC, he uses an Executioner's Greatsword.
  * The spin part of Blade of Peril (L2+R1) can be parried.
  * If you have a shield that allows for the use of weapon skills, the shield will still be held as you perform the weapon art.

### Softcaps & Scaling:

  * 2 Handed Stats: 
    * **19/13 = 392 AR = Base Stats**
    * 30/13 = 448 AR = Str Softcap
    * 19/39 = 452 AR = Dex Softcap
    * 40/13 = 463 AR
    * 19/60 = 464 AR
    * 19/99 = 479 AR = Dex Hardcap
    * 66/13 = 495 AR = Str Hardcap
    * **28/39 = 505 AR = Combined Softcap**
    * 40/40 = 525 AR
    * 66/40 = 556 AR
    * 66/99 = 583 AR = Combined Hardcap
  * After softcaps it gets less than 2 AR per level
  * 2 handed Quality weapon 
    * Scales well with both stats
    * Gains more from Strength than Dexterity

### Location/Where to Find

  * Obtained via [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") from the [Soul of Slave Knight Gael](/Soul+of+Slave+Knight+Gael "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Slave Knight Gael") for 20,000 souls.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit
