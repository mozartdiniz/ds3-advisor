# Boulder Heave

**Boulder Heave** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy, you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Boulder Heave  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 17  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 8 Intelligence/12 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Art of a stray demon of a stifled flame.
> 
>   
> Spews a boulder from one's mouth.
> 
>   
> The boulder is heavy, but shatters easily.

### How to Get Boulder Heave in Dark Souls 3

  * Transposed from the [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Stray Demon")

###  Dark Souls 3 Boulder Heave Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.8) damage on a direct hit and (spell buff x 1.1) damage with the burst.
  * This spell deals physical damage, not fire. It also has short to medium range and travels in an arc, heavily affected by gravity.
  * Direct hits knock opponents down if they are not guarding. Useful against [Crystal Lizards](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") and [Hollow Clerics](/Hollow+Cleric "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Cleric"), as they get inmediately flipped on their back, opening them for further damage.
  * Has enough force to guard-break most shields in 1 hit, making it useful for dealing with enemies such as [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") and [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith"). 
  * Has a horizontal range of about 2m and an unlimited vertical range, making it suitable for attacking from above.
  * When the boulder hits the ground, it shatters, spreading chunks that stagger opponents and do heavy poise and stamina damage.

  * Can be used against the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers"). If timed well you can stun-lock the boss in both phases.
  * This pyromancy is affected by [Flynn's Ring](/Flynn's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Flynn's Ring").
  * Casting this pyromancy with [Demon's Scar](/Demon's+Scar "Dark Souls 3 Demon's Scar") causes a small visual effect, centered on the caster's head.
