**Oceiros, the Consumed King** is a [Boss](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") in **Dark Souls 3 (DKS3)**. This **Dark Souls 3 Oceiros Boss Guide** features locations, strategies and videos on how to defeat Oceiros, the Consumed King easily, as well as tips, weaknesses, trivia and lore notes for the Oceiros boss. Oceiros is found in the garden below Lothric Castle, guarding a shrine.

It can be assumed that he is blind (like [Seath](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Seath+the+Scaleless "Dark Souls 3 Seath the Scaleless")), as his eye sockets are empty, and he makes references to this throughout the fight. He is a very vocal boss, and talks of an invisible or imaginary baby which he cradles in his arm for the first half of the fight. Oceiros initially attacks the player with a staff, but after being brought down to a certain health threshold, gets down on all fours and attacks like a wild beast.

> Ahh, you ignorant slaves. Finally taken notice, have you? Of the power of my beloved Ocelotte, child of dragons. Well, I will not give him up. For he is all that I have.

### Oceiros, the Consumed King Boss | Dark Souls 3

This boss fight is optional for "Link the Fire" [ending](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings"); however, it is required for "The End of Fire" ending. You can summon [Hawkwood, the Deserter](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") to help you. His summon sign is located in the center of the platform before fighting the two Church Knights in front of the boss room. If you do not see his sign then you must first defeat the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers") and afterwards talk to him in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") to receive the [Farron Ring](/Farron+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Farron Ring"), you must also defeat the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") and make sure that Hawkwood has left the shrine.

Bosses are unique and challenging [Enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") that drop [Boss Souls](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") capable of being transformed into powerful [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons"), [Spells](/Spells "Dark Souls 3 Spells"), and [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") for the player.

### Oceiros, the Consumed King Notes & Tips | Dark Souls 3

_What should you know about fighting Oceiros, the Consumed King in Dark Souls 3?_

  * Deals [Strike Damage](/Strike+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Strike Damage") (1st phase only), [Standard Damage](/Standard+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Standard Damage") and [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") damage. Can kill player by [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse") and damage FP points.
  * Weak to [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage") and [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite").
  * Resistant to [Magic Damage](/Magic+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Magic Damage") and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed").
  * Immune to [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic").
  * Can be riposted if his head is hit enough times. (Took 4 hits within 60 seconds on ng+3 with a +5 Black Knight Sword)

### How to fight and beat Oceiros, the Consumed King

#### Strategy 1 (Melee)

**First Phase:**

Always stay underneath the boss with your shield up. Only attack after he has completed his attack to minimize the risk of getting hit. This strategy remains effective for the second phase as well. The primary attack to watch out for in this phase is when he stabs his staff into the ground to call up an AoE of white dragon's breath on the floor. This move is easy to dodge but deals significant damage and inflicts curse, so be prepared. The transition to the second phase occurs around the time you hear a baby's cry. The boss will stagger momentarily, shake his head, and then crush his staff down. After this, he will perform two ground attacks, signaling the start of the second phase.

  
**Second Phase:**

In the second phase, aim to get behind or under him, ideally near his bottom half. The boss isn’t extremely easy to stay under, so it may take some practice. If you position yourself near his bottom half or back legs, he won't perform his rush attack, which can easily kill you in one combo. A one-handed weapon for quick attacks is recommended, but if you prefer a two-handed weapon, make sure to time your attacks carefully. The boss can be bled, making an upgraded Bandit's Knife a decent option for this fight, although not the only method. Use it if you're comfortable with the weapon, despite his resistance to it, as the bleed effect still works. The boss also executes flying attacks with white dragon's breath or just plain white dragon's breath attacks. These can be easily avoided if you are constantly pursuing the boss. High stamina is crucial in this fight. You can block some of his attacks, but in the second phase, blocking becomes more challenging. Unless you can block at least three out of his four attacks without losing too much stamina, consider two-handing your weapon. Take advantage of the boss's recovery period after his first move to get in as many attacks as possible. He typically does not have follow-up attacks to his standard attacks, making him straightforward in this regard. The biggest challenge is managing the camera and avoiding walls. Stay in the middle of the arena as much as possible. If you can't see his attacks, he can easily one-hit you. Being up against a wall hinders your ability to dodge effectively. If you find yourself in this situation, roll under his body as quickly as you can.

Just before his charge attack during his second form, you will hear what seems to be the cry of a child, serving as a tell for one of his more dangerous attacks. This boss also struggles with uneven terrain. When on the piles of rubble around the stage, many of his attacks will miss, although he remains capable of overhead slash attacks. While keeping up with his charge attack can be difficult, his very long tail stretches behind him and can be attacked directly with a weapon that has adequate swing range. It is not confirmed if his tail can be cut off, similar to dragon bosses in Dark Souls 1.

#### Strategy 2 (Ranged)

This strategy offers a very easy way to beat him while also allowing you to skip his second phase. First, locate the root system in the back right side of the area. Ensure that throughout the fight, you keep this root system within close range so you can run into it quickly when needed. As soon as the boss begins transitioning into his second phase, sprint into the corner near the roots and bait his charge. Once he charges into the roots, run around them to the other side. This maneuver will cause the boss to keep charging into the roots, effectively getting stuck and unable to reach you. With the boss stuck, pull out your bow or use spells to lay into him from a safe distance. Make sure to watch for his ice attacks, as they can still hit you even when he is stuck. It is crucial to note that the boss can and will eventually use his spin attack, which positions himself behind you. This move will free him from the roots and potentially leave you trapped between the boss and the roots. Always be prepared to adjust your position and avoid getting cornered.

#### Strategy 3 (Pyromancy)

This boss won't be too tricky for pyromancers. During the first phase, you'll have a relatively easy time. Keep your distance, and most of his attacks won't reach you. Utilize powerful pyromancies to whittle down his health from afar. When he transitions to the second phase, you'll have an opportunity to lob some spells while he staggers and prepares his new moveset. The charge attack is the one that will give you the most trouble. It's quick and deals significant damage, so be prepared to dodge it whenever you see him preparing to charge. Maintain your distance to avoid most of his other attacks, including the tail swipe, which won't be much of an issue if you stay back.

#### Strategy 4 (Sorcery)

This boss is one of the most difficult for spellcasters, as Oceiros has high magic resistance, and phase 2 poses a particular challenge for sorcerers. While it is relatively easy to avoid getting hit in the first phase, Oceiros becomes relentless in phase 2, making it difficult for sorcerers to find a window of opportunity to cast spells. A relatively reliable strategy is to cast after Oceiros has charged at you. Make sure to dodge properly, as his damage output is quite high. Once you have maneuvered behind him, he will usually either continue attacking in the direction he is facing, prepare for a tail swipe, or jump away from you. In any case, you should be able to cast a spell during this time window, but be sure to time it so you can dodge a potential tail swipe.

Maintaining a lot of distance from Oceiros is usually a bad idea, as his most deadly attacks are fast charges that may catch you off guard while casting. Additionally, he tends to phase through your projectiles during his charges or simply avoid them with his erratic movements.

It is more beneficial to use spells with shorter casting times in the second phase, such as Great Soul Arrow instead of Great Heavy Soul Arrow. Homing Soulmasses are also less useful, as they often miss Oceiros entirely. A viable strategy is to forgo projectile spells altogether and instead use Farron Flashsword to hack away at Oceiros' back leg, although a decent lightning weapon can deal comparable damage. In any case, Oceiros is much less dangerous in close combat, so if you manage to imitate the common melee strategy of attacking the boss's back legs, you have a good chance of defeating him with magic.

Alternatively, if you have the Ashes of Ariandel DLC, you can use the spell [Snap Freeze](/Snap+Freeze "Dark Souls 3 Snap Freeze"). Use Firebombs, a torch, or [Fire Surge](/Fire+Surge "Dark Souls 3 Fire Surge") to reset the Frostbite meter when it procs. Done quickly, you can trigger three frostbites in a single Snap Freeze cast. However, be cautious, as in the second phase it will be harder to pull off due to Oceiros' aggressive behavior.

### Oceiros, the Consumed King Lore Theories

  * Oceiros was once the King of Lothric and was married to the Queen of Lothric, who was initially revered as the goddess of fertility and bounty, and Ocelotte was the youngest child the queen gave birth to. He went mad trying to harness his royal blood for a greater purpose, leading him to the heretics in the Grand Archives, where he discovered the twisted worship of Seath the paledrake. He may have gone senile or mad with age and isolation. His dialogue mentions there is a child called Ocelotte; and he cradles something, as if he were holding a child, though nothing is seen; yet as you smack him around, he tells Ocelotte to come out, as if just realizing that Ocelotte isn't in his hands, and now he himself doesn't know where Ocelotte is. Perhaps he remembers something painful, as when you smack him around further, he screams the name of Ocelotte and becomes a frenzied beast, no longer human. As you weaken him, you will hear a baby cry...perhaps the child really is invisible? Or maybe the king, consumed by his desire to study and gain the power of dragons, experimented and fused with the dragon child Ocelotte, becoming a twisted dragon man, and the cries heard when Ocelotte takes over are cries of pain? Or maybe the child is just invisible. Speculation can only go so far. Oceiros is part of a series of reincarnations of [Seath the Scaleless](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Seath+the+Scaleless "Dark Souls 3 Seath the Scaleless"), whose essence is entwined with the [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Moonlight Greatsword"). This may have profoundly affected [Lothric's](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") controversial conception.
  * Leaks from quality assurance testers of the alpha version suggest that Oceiros was originally going to be played by the character now known as [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr"), with the dragon character appearing under an unknown name in a cut area called "God's Grave". With [Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") as Oceiros, the part of "Gundyr" would be played by the character model now known as [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant "Dark Souls 3 Yhorm the Giant") \-  _(credit darksouls3theorist and Lance McDonald)._

### Oceiros, the Consumed King Trivia

  * Oceiros is one of the few enemies that can kill by curse. Therefore, Cursebite Ring is recommended.
  * His baby, Ocelotte, like the Emerald Herald from Dark Souls 2, Shanalotte, are both referred as children of dragons (which doesn't necessarily mean they're of the same nature, though).
  * The cry of a baby may be heard during phase 1 of the fight and is heard around when the second phase starts.
  * Seath was blind and eyeless. Oceiros transformation into a Seath like creature would explain his empty eye-sockets.
  * His wings are very similar to the primordial serpent statue's wings. These statues are found throughout Lothric Castle.
  * Oceiros has strange stalks growing out his back. The same stalks can be found on two dead demons in Smouldering Lake.
  * The game files contain a sound file of a baby’s cry for this fight which is not present in the final version of the game released to general audiences. It has been speculated that its removal was facilitated in order to avoid a potential controversy. 
  * Bug (Only when killed with a critical strike): Instead of fading to dust like every other boss, Oceiros instantaneously dissappears with no additional effects.
  * Bug (Very rare): When entering the boss room while being chased by a Cathedral Knight, after passing through the fog wall the knight may glitch through as well, making the fight significantly more difficult. However, he can be Rapported into fighting against Ocerios.
