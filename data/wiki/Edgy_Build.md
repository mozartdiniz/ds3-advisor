## Build Strategy

Be edgy.

Nah, seriously, this build's for fun and memes. Go round spamming Dark Edge like there's no tomorrow. It combos nicely with Affinity, and does huge damage. Throw in the occasional Soul Stream and yell some anime ***** whilst you cast it. You look super edgy when playing this build, so don't forget to spam Legion Etiquette before and after the fight. You can also stab yourself with the [Bloodlust](/Bloodlust "Bloodlust") for bonus edge points before the fight. That's literally it. Have fun being edgelords.
