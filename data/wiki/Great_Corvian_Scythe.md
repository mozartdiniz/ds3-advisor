**Great Corvian Scythe** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  
  

> Great scythe of the forlorn souls guided by heretical storytellers. The Mistress of the Painted World is said to wield a great scythe herself. Great scythes inflict profuse bleeding, such that the blood splatters on the wielder.  
>   
> Skill: Neck Swipe  
> This attack aims for the scruff of a foe's neck, and when successful, functions as a head shot, inflicting heavy damage.

### Notes and Tips

  * Can be Infused and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with Titanite.
  * Each strike that lands on an enemy will cause Bleed build-up to yourself, reminiscent of the Lifehunt Scythe from Dark Souls. Though the self inflicted bleed does not appear to do much damage, the build up will also stack with enemy bleeds. This can be very dangerous as the enemy bleed will deal normal bleed damage.
  * Has the longest range out of all the Reapers and has the highest innate Bleed too.
  * Has innate shield pierce: 40% on all attacks, and 80% on the weapon art.
  * Has a "Sweet Spot" meaning if the player hits with the shaft of the weapon it does 5% less damage, conversely if the player hits with the blade they deal 3% more damage.
  * The WA has hyperarmor

  
  

### Location / Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Corvians](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian") (winged enemies) wielding it in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"), [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves"), and [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") with 2% chance.
  * To farm, travel backwards from the Halfway Fortress bonfire. Two of the five corvians here wield this scythe.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
