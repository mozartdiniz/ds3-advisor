**Black Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of the three mentors of the Sable Church of Londor...  
> ..this billed mask belongs to Yuria, the second eldest. (Helm)  
> ..this pitch-black dress resembles a mourning dress. (Dress)  
> ..these black leggings/gauntlets are hidden by the dress. (Leggings & gauntlets)  
>   
> These maidens of a Primordial Serpent were renowned as founders of the Sable Church, which offered salvation for Hollows. (Helm & Dress)  
>   
> It is apparent that the women in black were highly skilled fencers, capable of founding the Sable Church between just the three of them. (Leggings & Gauntlets)

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") after you have killed [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") and hand in her [ashes](/Yuria's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Yuria's Ashes") (You will need to reload the area) for 18000 souls, or you can complete [Yuria's](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") questline to receive her [Darkdrift](/Darkdrift "Dark Souls 3 Darkdrift") and armor set at her location in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). (You need to summon her to [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder") boss fight and she must survive)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Bears resemblance to the Cainhurst armor set from Bloodborne.
