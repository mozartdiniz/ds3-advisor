# Blessed Weapon

**Blessed Weapon** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Blessed Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 35  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Faith  
**Duration** | 45 seconds  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
> Miracle taught to Lothric Knights. Blesses right weapon, increasing attack power, as well as gradually restoring HP.  
>   
> The Knight is one of the Three Pillars of Lothric, said to have strengthened ties with the High Priestess after the Scholars acquired the Grand Archives.

### How to Get Blessed Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") for 8,000 souls after giving her the [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric").

### Dark Souls 3 Blessed Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Has 3 effects. Health regeneration, a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") that increases Physical Damage and prevents blue-eye [Skeletons](/Skeletons "Dark Souls 3 Skeletons") and [Carthus Swordsman](/Carthus+Swordsman "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Swordsman") from reviving.
  * Both effects last 45 seconds, up to 60 seconds with [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") +2

**Weapon Buff** :

  * Adds a Flat 7.5% boost to the buffed weapon's Physical AR.
  * Does not affect elemental AR (eg, [Dragonslayer's Axe](/Dragonslayer's+Axe "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer's Axe"), [Murky Handscythe](/Murky+Hand+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Murky Hand Scythe"))
  * 20% Damage to Skeletons and will prevent them from resurrecting.
  * Applies a white sparkling glow to weapon (which can be hidden by [Untrue Dark Ring](/Untrue+Dark+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Untrue Dark Ring")), despite the icon showing a light blue weapon similar to [Magic Weapon](/Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon").
  * Will overwrite similar buffs.

**Health Regeneration** :

  * Scales with Spellbuff (SB) of your Chime/Talisman.
  * SB 250+ = 5 hp/s
  * SB 200+ = 4 hp/s
  * SB 150+ = 3 hp/s
  * SB 100+ = 2 hp/s
  * SB <100 = 1 hp/s
  * Unaffected by Faith stat (outside of spellbuff), weapon upgrade level, or specific catalyst. 
    * 40 faith, +0 Talisman, with 152 spellbuff regenerates 3 hp/s
    * 60 faith, +6 Saint Tree Bellvine, with 193 spellbuff regenerates 3 hp/s
    * As both are in the 150 - 200 spellbuff bracket
  * Regeneration breakpoints behave strangely with dual scaling catalysts (Crystal Chime, Caitha's Chime, White Hair Talisman, etc), likely for the same reason higher spellbuff doesn't always mean more damage when dealing with dark catalysts or dark spells.
  * Increasing the duration of the spell with Lingering Dragoncrest rings slows down the regen, resulting in the same total health regained over a longer time.
  * Since faith builds have access to four elements in unique weapon buff spells (Blessed Weapon, [Lightning Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade"), [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade"), and [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade")), they're the ideal choice for a more physical-focused build investing into magic.

Unlike the Lightning Blade miracle, Blessed Weapon does not give the weapon split AR, instead just reinforcing the physical damage of the buffed weapon.

  * The Miracle was updated in an unknown game patch. The change essentially made Blessed Weapon look more in line with the version the [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight") use (getting rid of the blue and making it pure white instead).
