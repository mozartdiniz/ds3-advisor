**Hollowing** is a "statistic" in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** that is accumulated when the player dies while branded with a [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil"). The amount of Hollowing gained per death is equal to the number of Dark Sigils the player has in their inventory. Once the player reaches 15 or more Hollowing, their character becomes visibly Hollow, displaying a withered and decayed appearance. The maximum Hollowing a character can reach is 99, at which point the Hollowed effect is fully manifested.

Being Hollow gives the character a progressively more shriveled appearance, only visible when naked or wearing gear that reveals the body. Hollow characters also receive a bonus to [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") when wielding a [Hollow weapon](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem"). Hollowing does not have any other effect on the character in Dark Souls 3 (it does not reduce weapon damage or maximum HP).

### DS3 Hollowing Branding

By conversing with [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor"), players can acquire a free level-up up to five times, each time being branded with a Dark Sigil. The initial level-up does not require any Hollowing. However, subsequent level-ups demand increasing amounts of Hollowing: the second requires 2 Hollowing, the third needs 6, the fourth demands 12, and the fifth necessitates 15. Once the Hollowing counter reaches 15, the player character fully transitions into a Hollow. Additionally, players can obtain three more Dark Sigils from Anri of Astora as part of Yuria of Londor's questline. Despite acquiring these extra Sigils, they do not grant any further level-ups.

All Dark Sigils are listed under the Key Items section of the inventory. Each Sigil contributes to the player’s Hollowing progression by adding one point of Hollowing for every death. For instance, if a player has accumulated three Dark Sigils in their inventory, they will experience an additional 3 points of Hollowing each time they die.

Once Yoel has given all five Sigils, he dies and [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") appears where he used to be.

### Removing Dark Sigils and Hollowing in DS3

After the player has given the Fire Keeper the Fire Keeper Soul, she can remove the Dark Sigil and clear all accumulated Hollowing. This process requires a payment of souls equal to the cost of the player’s next five level-ups. Importantly, this payment does not result in the loss of the player’s levels; rather, it compensates for the previously acquired “free” levels. Opting to cure the Dark Sigil will have several consequences: it will cancel Yuria of Londor's questline, cause Londor Pale Shade’s summon signs to disappear, and trigger invasions by the Londor Pale Shade in specific in-game locations. Additionally, curing the Dark Sigil in this manner will prevent the player from acquiring any more Dark Sigils during that playthrough and will also obstruct access to the Usurpation of Fire ending.

### Reversing Hollowing in Dark Souls 3

The player can reset their Hollowing counter to zero and restore their appearance by using a [Purging Stone](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone"). It’s important to note that Purging Stones only affect the Hollowing status and do not remove the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") itself. Additionally, using a Purging Stone will not affect [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor")'s questline. Purging Stones can be purchased from [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") directly or from the [Shrine Handmaiden](/Shrine+Handmaiden "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaiden") if you provide her with either the [Hollow's Ashes](/Hollow's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Hollow's Ashes") or [Yuria's Ashes](/Yuria's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Yuria's Ashes"). They can also be farmed from [" target="">Sewer Centipedes](/Sewer+Centipede "Dark Souls 3 Sewer Centipede") found in the toxic swamp of the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital").

Alternatively, Hollowing can be healed by seeking out [Velka](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Velka the Goddess of Sin") or using the [Purging Monument](/Purging+Monument "Dark Souls 3 Purging Monument") and selecting the Dissolution option.

### Hollow Notes & Tips in Dark Souls 3

  * If you die while wearing a [Ring of Sacrifice](/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice") and are not hollow, your character will not become hollow when they revive (though you will still accrue hollowing). You can continue to postpone becoming hollow with more Rings of Sacrifice all the way up to 99 hollowing, at least until you run out of rings. Note that [Hollow](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem")-infused weapons will not give their [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") bonus if you are not hollow, even if you have 15 or more hollowing.
  * As of Patch 1.03, your character loses their shadow and reflection in the water when Hollowed. This is believed to be a bug or a mistake since Hollows in Dark Souls I & II still have their shadow and reflection.
