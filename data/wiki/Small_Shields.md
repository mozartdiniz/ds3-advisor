# Small Shields

**Small Shields** in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") are a type of [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") that usually weigh very little and have the least form of protection from attacks. They are often used by players who prefer parrying rather than blocking.
