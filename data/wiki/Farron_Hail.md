# Farron Hail

**Farron Hail** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

## Farron Hail  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 2 per volly (14 in total)  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 28 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged AoE Attack  
  
> Sorcery which improves upon Farron Dart. Fires a cascade of soul darts.   
>   
> Entrusted to the leader of the Legion's acolytes, and apparently a sorcery of his daughter, Heysel, that was refined by a Crystal Sage.

### How to Get Farron Hail in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") after giving him the [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll") for 5,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Farron Hail Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Each dart inflicts (Spell Buff x 0.58) magic damage before resistances.
  * Farron Hail quickly shoots four bursts of five darts in a wide, consistent spread.
  * There isn't much of a difference between point blank and distant damage, since only one dart can connect at a time.
  * Though it shares the icon of [Soul Spear Barrage](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Soul+Spear+Barrage "Dark Souls 3 Soul Spear Barrage") from Dark Souls 2, the cast is fundamentally different, firing twenty shots in four consistent bursts instead of launching an uneven barrage of projectiles.
