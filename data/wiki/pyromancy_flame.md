**Pyromancy Flame** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3, used to cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").  
  
  
  

> Flame catalyst used by pyromancers.   
>   
> Equip a pyromancy flame to utilize pyromancy. Pyromancies must be attuned at a bonfire before use.  
>   
> Skill: Combustion  
> Creates a powerful flame in the wielder's hand. One of the most basic pyromancies, and for this very reason, the flame created is fierce. Works while equipped in either hand.

### How to Get Pyromancy Flame in Dark Souls 3

  * Starting equipment for the [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer")
  * Given by [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") after rescuing him (excluding pyromancer class).

### Dark Souls 3 Pyromancy Flame Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * A Pyromancy Flame is upgraded with normal [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"), and can only be upgraded by [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp").
  * The effectiveness of Pyromancy scales both with [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") and [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence").
  * The basic [Combustion](/Combustion "Dark Souls 3 Combustion") spell is a Skill feature of the Pyromancy Flame in this game, and not available as an attunable spell.
  * At 40 Intelligence and 40 Faith the flame has a Spell Buff of 207 [(Source)](/Int-Faith+Staff+Magic+Adjustments "Dark Souls 3 Int-Faith Staff Magic Adjustments")
  * At 60 INT and 60 FTH it has a spellbuff of 221.
  * Fireball-type pyromancy spells gain a significant amount of damage at 18 INT.   

    * This applies to Fireball, Fire Orb, Great Chaos Fire Orb and Black Fire Orb
    * Tested with a Pyromancy Flame +6 on the [Corvian](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian") at the beginning of Road of Sacrifices, a 17INT/15FTH Fire Orb did 157 damage. At 18INT/15FTH a Fire Orb did 210 damage.
    * The [White Hair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman") has a similar damage breakpoint at 25 INT.
    * It seems that FTH scaling does not apply until a minimum INT level is met for Fireball-type pyromancies, with the minimum INT based on the type of [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") and its weapon level.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
