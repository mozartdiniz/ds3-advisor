# Dusk Crown Ring

### Dusk Crown Ring  
  
---  
  

> Leaf-colored crown ring bestowed upon the princess of Oolacile, ancient land of golden sorceries.  
>   
> Reduces consumption of FP, but also lowers HP.  
>   
> Oolacile is synonymous for its lost sorceries of which the xanthous sorcerers are dedicated scholars. This crown ring is a rare artifact of great magic heritage.  
  
**Dusk Crown Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring Effect

  * Reduces FP consumption of [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"), [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") and [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") by 25%
  * Reduces maximum HP by 15% (Example: 1000hp to 850hp)
  * Weight: 0.6

### How to Get Dusk Crown Ring in Dark Souls 3

  * Located in the [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"). The ring is in a cell next to [Xanthous Ashes](/Xanthous+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Xanthous Ashes"), in the left-most cell of the room where there are 8 Jailers. ()
  * WARNING: picking up the Dusk Crown Ring will cause the corpse holding it to shriek, causing all of the jailers to come running into the room.

### Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * This ring first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Dusk+Crown+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring")_.
