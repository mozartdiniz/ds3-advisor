# Fire Damage

## Fire Damage  
  
---  
Category | Elemental Damage  
  
**Fire Damage** is a [Damage Type](/Damage+Types "Dark Souls 3 Damage Types") in Dark Souls 3. It is one of eight damage types presented in the original game and one of the four Elemental-type damages.

### Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage Guide

Fire is generally effective against unarmored targets and undead enemies. Most [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") deal Fire Damage.

## Dark Souls 3 Fire Damage Info

### Fire Damage Trivia & Notes

  * Fire Damage causes some enemies to panic, effectively halting all attacks for a short amount of time.
  * Fire damage is very effective against hollows, which use light armor and beasts.
  * Fire damage is ineffective against demons and dragon-type enemies.
