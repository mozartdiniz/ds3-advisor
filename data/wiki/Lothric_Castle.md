# Lothric Castle

**Lothric Castle** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. Once the Ashen One has obtained the sacrificial vessel, the Castle may be entered. Lothric Wyverns, Lothric Knights and Winged Knights closely guard the path to the Twin Princes. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### General Information

  * **Previous:** [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric")
  * **Next:** [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives")
  * **Recommended Levels:** 75 to 90
  * **Bosses:** [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour"), [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") and [Lorian, Elder Prince](/Lorian,+Elder+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lorian, Elder Prince")
  * **Bonfires:** Lothric Castle, Dragon Barracks, Dragonslayer Armour, Grand Archives, Twin Princes
  * Clearing this area opens Flameless Shrine, [Kiln of the First Flame](/Kiln+of+the+First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 Kiln of the First Flame")

## Full Lothric Castle Walkthrough

A full walkthrough including all the items, enemies and bosses goes here. You can add links, images. and other details. Click [here](/Lothric+Castle#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle#speedrun") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough.   
  

### Accessing Lothric Castle

After defeating [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley"), ascend the ladder and proceed forward into the castle. In the first room will be a red eyed knight and a black clothed figure. The cleric in black will heal the knight during the fight so it would be useful to kill it first. Kill them and loot [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x from a corpse in front of the altar.

Take the staircase from the right side of the room up to find a cleric in black on your right and 2 Lothric knights and a cleric ahead. Proceed into the next room and light the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires") to the left. 

Proceed straight into the next room and loot the chest on the right for the [Prayer Set](/Prayer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Prayer Set"). On the left by the railing you will find a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Head to the stairs along the left side to encounter some hollows. At the top of the stairs to your left will be another seated hollow in a tower. Ahead of this to the left is a set of stairs you can take down to a tower top where several hollows will climb over. Loot the corpse here for a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Go back up to the main path and head into a room on the right where winged knight will drop down. Defeat it and loot the corpse here for [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") 1x. Climb the ladder, turn around, and go to the end of the platform to find an [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Wall "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Wall") on your left. Go through to find the [Sacred Bloom Shield](/Sacred+Bloom+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Bloom Shield") and the [Winged Knight Set.](/Winged+Knight+Set "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight Set")

Return to the room with the Winged Knight, exit the room, and head up the stairs to face some crossbow hollows. Up to the left is a tower with another hollow where you can loot [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. To the right you can loot a corpse for [Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Weary Warrior") 1x and another [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 2x. Continue up the long staircase and then head left to light the Dragon Barracks [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). Loot a corpse near for an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x. If you continue through the door closest to the bonfire and then right up the stairs and then head left you will find a red eye knight guarding a corpse with a [Greatlance](/Greatlance "Dark Souls 3 Greatlance"). 

### Navigating Wyvern Fire

Head toward the stone bridge near here where you'll see a [Lothric Wyvern](/Lothric+Wyvern "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Wyvern") perched on the other side, but be aware that a second wyvern will land next to it, and both will begin spraying fire. Killing one wyvern will make them both die. If you decide to run across the bridge, you can drop down near the end to loot [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard") 1x from a corpse underneath the bridge. Drop down again to the right to find a seated hollow. To the left is a doorway you can enter to find some stairs up but first, proceed down the path here to loot [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 2x. Keep going, and you will see a hollow that will turn into a black mass hollow; try to kill it before it can transform, and then continue on to find [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") 2x. Return to the doorway and head up the stairs and break some boxes to the left to find [Lightning Urn](/Lightning+Urn "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Urn") 3x. Step through and exit the doorway and head right. On the stairs you will find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") and a hollow will drop down here.

Continue up the stairs where more hollows will drop down. Continue up the path, where you will find a corpse to the right hanging over a ledge with [Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Large+Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier") 1x. Continue up to the top of a tower and head down the stairs here to encounter a Winged Knight and a hollow to the left. You can loot a corpse near the hollow for [Sniper Crossbow](/Sniper+Crossbow "Dark Souls 3 Sniper Crossbow") and [Sniper Bolt](/Sniper+Bolt "Dark Souls 3 Sniper Bolt") 11x. Return to the tower top and take the other stairs down and take the path back towards the bonfire.

Make your way back to the stone bridge. Near the start of the bridge, you can drop down to the right to a lower ledge where you will find Titanite Chunk 1x. Continue along this ledge to come to a path and a staircase where 3 more hollows will attack. Continue up the stairs dispatching the hollows. At the top if you proceed straight you will encounter 2 Lothric Knights on the left. The one on the right is particularly deadly, he'll two-hand his sword and can bless it. You can aggro one at a time with ranged projectiles. If you head right instead up the short set of stairs you will come to a doorway on the right. Through here you will find a hollow to your right. Straight ahead will be [Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Large+Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier") 1x on a corpse. To the far right of this room you will find a [Raw Gem](/Raw+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Raw Gem") on a corpse.

### Fight the Boreal Outrider Knight

Go down the stairs on the right to encounter a [Boreal Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight") on the lower level who deals some powerful frost damage. This is enemy is quick and relentless so patience and stamina conservation are key. Killing it has a chance to drop [Irithyll Rapier](/Irithyll+Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Rapier"). In this room are 4 chests, 1 with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") 1x, 1 with [Spirit Tree Crest Shield](/Spirit+Tree+Crest+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Spirit Tree Crest Shield") and 1 with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. The 4th chest can be found hidden behind several boxes and contains [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") 2x.

Return up the stairs and head right up the large stairs. The wyverns will breathe fire into this courtyard area. You can dash left or it can be rolled through to head straight into the next room. Head up the stairs here avoiding the fire barrels being tossed and take out the hollows at the top. Head into the proceeding room where you will find hollows and a Mimic chest to the right with Titanite Scale 3x. A corpse in here can be looted for [Pale Pine Resin](/Pale+Pine+Resin "Dark Souls 3 Pale Pine Resin") 1x. If exit to the outside from this room you will find several hollows and a corpse with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. 

Reenter the room and head left down the stairs to take out more hollows and loot [Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier](/Large+Soul+of+a+Nameless+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Nameless Soldier") 1x on a corpse hanging over the edge. If you take the ladder down you can loot a corpse in this room for [Black Firebomb](/Black+Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Black Firebomb") 3x, which will be useful for the next step. Head back up the ladder. To the right you will see a wyvern claw reaching into the room. Attacking it will trigger a dark eruption from which the [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") infecting the wyvern will spill out spitting abyssal sludge that will build up curse. It is weak to fire and can be attacked at range to stay out of harm's way. Killing it will fell both wyverns in the area. At this point you can return to the area across the stone bridge where the wyverns had been guarding to loot corpses for [Lightning Urn](/Lightning+Urn "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Urn") 6x, [Refined Gem](/Refined+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Refined Gem") 1x, and [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 2x.

### Unlock main Gates for a Shortcut

Once the wyverns are slain, exit this room out the opposite doorway and head right down the stairs, down the path to find a corpse on the left with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x and further down the path a Crystal Lizard & a body with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. Return back to the room where you fought the wyvern claw and take the stairs down and head into the nearby room to find a [Large Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Large+Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Weary Warrior") 1x. Pull the lever here and drop down to the right to take on the cleric and red eyed Lothric Knight. Defeat them and head down the main central stairs to face another cleric and knight. A corpse in the corner has an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x.

Head back up the central staircase and take the curved stairs on your left. Head right to exit and right again to enter a church like building. There is an open doorway on the left. Take it and head right to find the [Red Tearstone Ring](/Red+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Red Tearstone Ring"). If you jump down where the rail is broken, you can find [Caitha's Chime](/Caitha's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Caitha's Chime"), beware the long fall. Inside the church you can find [Rusted Coin](/Rusted+Coin "Dark Souls 3 Rusted Coin") 2x on a corpse and behind it, a chest with a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 1x. In the lower portion of this room, you can find the summon sign of [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim"). You can summon him now or wait until ready to tackle the boss if you want to explore some more.

### Find the Sunlight Altar

Exit the church and slightly to the left and ahead will be a corpse with [Sunlight Medal](/Sunlight+Medal "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Medal") 1x. To the left of this corpse enter the room to activate a lift to unlock a shortcut to where you first entered the castle. Take the lift back up to the top and exit to the left and go left around the corner. On the left up the wall will be a ladder. Take it to the top and head left on the roof to find a corpse in the corner with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. If you make your way back along the roof you will come to a long rooftop leading towards some crossbow hollows and an axe wielding hollow. Cross into the building they are in and head to the opposite side of the room and take the ladder down. In this room you will find the [Knight's Ring](/Knight's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Knight's Ring") 1x.

Cross the covered bridge which leads out of this room to encounter an axe hollow and a greatsword wielding lothric knight. Beyond them in the next room is a chest with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") 3x. In this room you will find the Altar of Sunlight, where you can learn the Praise the Sun [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures") and which also serves as the covenant turn in for the [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants"). Open the double door here and exit out.

If you head left and look over the edge you can see the dark mass which has erupted from the fallen wyvern. You can snipe it safely from up here with casting or a lot of arrows. The Onislayer Great arrow seems to do the most damage. It is susceptible to fire and can be taken out from range. If you have no ranged, run past the left side of it and get behind it. You will be inside the dead wyvern and it cannot attack this direction, just be careful attacking since most attacks cause your character to move forward, and getting you in range of it's flailing attacks. Killing it will cause the wyvern to disappear and you can loot [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 2x, [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard") 2x, and [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 2x. A corpse nearby can be looted for [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") 1x. In the adjacent room you will find a Mimic with the [Sunlight Straight Sword](/Sunlight+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Straight Sword") and up the stairs the [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric").

### Boss Fight: [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour")

Once ready to tackle the boss return to where you saw the sign for [Eygon of Carim](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") and summon him if you choose. Head out and to the right to come to the fog, where you will challenge the area boss, [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour"). Kill him to obtain [Soul of Dragonslayer Armour](/Soul+of+Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Dragonslayer Armour"). Proceed along this bridge and light the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"), and continue on. Head to the right and take the stairs down to find [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") 1x. Return up the stairs and head right up the main central stairs. In the next room you will find another [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). Light it and press on into the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives").
