**Crucifix of the Mad King** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**. It was added with [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").  
  
  
  

> A cross spear hung with a malformed corpse. 
> 
> Once, a mad king was born to the pygmy royalty and Shira, knight of Filianore, put him to rest. But Shira's cross spear, unable to kill the undying king, only pinned itself to him. Shira delivered them together to the darkroom, where she stayed and held them close.
> 
> Skill: Mad King's Folly  
> Slam malformed corpse into the ground to briefly awaken it, then fillet with strong attack to trigger a roar, a blessing, or vexation.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Upgrade Material: [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Cannot be infused but can be buffed
  * Despite it's innate dark damage, [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade") does work on this weapon, increasing its damage even further.
  * Both physical and dark damage scale with Faith.
  * It has the Great Club R2. Convenient for groups of enemies or catching rolls.
  * When the weapon art is performed, it will randomly trigger one of three effects:  
_-Roar_ is an AoE blast that covers your front and sides.  
_-Blessing_ works similarly to [Perseverance](/Perseverance "Dark Souls 3 Perseverance").  
_-Vexation_ spawns a following floating sphere, which explodes dealing Dark damage to you and all enemies (it can be dodged).
  * [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira") never triggers the vexation effect when she's using this weapon.

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Drops from [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira") when you face her in the final area of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). **Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit
