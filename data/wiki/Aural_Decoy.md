# Aural Decoy

## Aural Decoy  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 15  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Decoy  
  
**Aural Decoy** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery developed by a certain surreptitious sorcerer at Vinheim Dragon School.   
> Distracts foes with a distant sound.   
> The sound carries with it a strangely infectious resonance that may cause one to stray from their post and expose their back. 

### How to Get Aural Decoy in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [ Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 2000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Functions similarly to [Aural Decoy](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Aural+Decoy "Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy") from Dark Souls 1, but without the laugh sound.
  * Can be used to get the attention of several enemies, which can be used to distract enemies before sprinting past them.
  * Effectively a sorcery version of [Alluring Skulls](/Alluring+Skull "Dark Souls 3 Alluring Skull"), though with some noteworthy differences.  

    * Rather than luring enemies away like a skull, it instead makes them stop and look in its direction. This mitigates its uses significantly.
    * Unlike an alluring skull, it won't affect enemy aggro. This means, if the enemy sees anything it can target (either a player or an enemy of a different alignment), the spell will do nothing, making it nearly useless.
