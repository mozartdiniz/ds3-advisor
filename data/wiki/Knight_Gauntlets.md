**Knight Gauntlets** is a [Gauntlet](/Gauntlets) in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Knight Set](/Knight+Set).  
  
  
  

> Gauntlets of a lowly knight, fashioned from solid iron. These iron gauntlets might be on the heftier side compared to others of their ilk, but as such offer great physical absorption in exchange for its/their imposing weight. Do not think to wear it without the necessary Vitality.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting equipment for the [Knight](/Knight)
  * After sending [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement) out once, you can either buy from him or buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Firelink Shrine") after giving her [Greirat`s Ashes,](/Greirat's+Ashes "Greirat`s Ashes,") for 2000 souls.
