**Millwood Battle Axe** is a [Weapon](/Weapons) in Dark Souls 3. It is part of the [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Ashes of Ariandel") [DLC](/DLC "DLC")  
  
  
  

> Battle axe wielded by Millwood Knights. Its blade is blessed by the symbol of the Ethereal Oak
> 
> A hefty axe normally wielded by the mightiest of warriors.
> 
> Skill: Warcry
> 
> The unique warcry of the Millwood Knights entailed a leap straight toward the enemy and a fearsome roar.

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Buffed and Infused.
  * Upgraded with Titanite.
  * Amazing when used on a Strength build since it's buffable and has an A scaling in Strength when infused with a [Heavy Gem](/Heavy+Gem).
  * The weapon art lunge can be handy for heavy or overburdened character since the leap can be used regardless your [Equipment Load](/Equipment+Load).

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Looted from a corpse at the top of the broken Millwood tower. From the first bonfire (Snowfield bonfire), go down to the ice trap and into the forest full of wolves. After killing the wolves, go down to the edge and follow the path to the left, you will enter an open area with ruins and wandering Millwood Knights, the tower is unmissable. Enter the tower and climb the ladders and climb the stairs until you get to the very top of the tower. From here you have to drop to a plank then fall down to a dead Millwood which the battle axe will be on.
  * [Fextralife Video: Millwood Battle Axe location](https://youtu.be/J2G-2pZXSUc?t=22m11s "Fextralife Video: Millwood Battle Axe location")

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
