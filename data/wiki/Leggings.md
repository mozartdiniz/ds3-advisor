# Leggings

**Leggings** or **Leg Armor** is [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3 that protects the player's legs. They will work the same way as previous Souls games, by simply adding their defense values to a total that is then used to calculate how much damage is taken. Click the table header to sort.
