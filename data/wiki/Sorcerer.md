**Sorcerer** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** tailored for players who want to specialize in powerful ranged magic. With the highest Intelligence and Attunement stats, the Sorcerer is perfect for casting devastating sorceries from a distance. Equipped with Soul Arrow and Heavy Soul Arrow, this class excels at ranged combat from the very start.

While the Sorcerer has the lowest Vigor (resulting in low HP) and Faith, their proficiency in Intelligence and Attunement allows for substantial spell damage and numerous spell slots as the game progresses. Starting at level 6, the Sorcerer is a great choice for those who want to focus on sorcery, with access to a variety of weapons and spells that can be further enhanced by investing in Intelligence, Faith, and Attunement.

> A loner who left formal academia to pursue further research. Commands soul sorceries using high intelligence."

### Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer Notes and Tips

  * Highest [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") and [Attunement](/Attunement "Dark Souls 3 Attunement"). Good [Luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck") and [Dexterity](/Dexterity "Dark Souls 3 Dexterity"). Lowest [Vigor](/Vigor "Dark Souls 3 Vigor"), therefore, lowest amount of [HP](/HP "Dark Souls 3 HP"). Lowest [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith"). Lowest [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength").
  * Starts with Soul Arrow and Heavy Soul Arrow, perfect for range attacks.
  * Starts with [Sorcerer's Staff ](/Sorcerer's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer's Staff"), the strongest staff with Intelligence between 0-49.
  * Invest in [Attunement](/Attunement "Dark Souls 3 Attunement") for more FP and spell slots. Invest in [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") for spell damage. Invest in [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") to gain access to basic [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") and [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").
  * Without leveling investments into Strength and Dexterity, can use the [Mail Breaker](/Mail+Breaker "Dark Souls 3 Mail Breaker"), [Rapier](/Rapier "Dark Souls 3 Rapier"), [Dagger](/Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Dagger"), [Caestus](/Caestus "Dark Souls 3 Caestus"), [Bandit's Knife](/Bandit's+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Bandit's Knife"), [Handmaid's Dagger](/Handmaid's+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Handmaid's Dagger"), and [Dark Hand](/Dark+Hand "Dark Souls 3 Dark Hand"). Can use simple shields, can two-hand some weapons with higher stats, like [Long Sword](/Long+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Long Sword"), Broadsword, Astora Straight Sword, Lothric's Holy Sword, Crescent Moon Sword, [Darkdrift](/Darkdrift "Dark Souls 3 Darkdrift"), Estoc, Golden Ritual Spear, etc., one point investment in Strength will allow you to two-hand wield the [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Moonlight Greatsword"), Astora Greatsword.
  * List of [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"). Many of the strong spells will not be available until you will gain access to certain areas, middle and late game, where you will be able to find the Scrolls. To buy most of the spells, you'll need to find [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim"), relocate him into the Firelink Shrine, and bring him Scrolls of Sorceries.

### Builds That Use Sorcerer in DS3

  * Sorcerer of the Dragon School of Vinheim (implied starting class, with Young Dragon Ring given)
  * Crystal Sage (Crystal Scroll knowledge holder, a branch of sorcery that has been carried on by the Crystal Sages)
  * Sage (Sage's Scroll sorceries knowledge holder)
  * Court Sorcerer (Logan's Scroll knowledge holder)
  * Lothric Spellmaster: A wizard adept at spelling bees and Scrabble
  * Adept Assassin of the Dragon School (Member of a clandestine guild of sorcerers based in Vinheim)
  * Xanthous Scholar (Golden Scroll knowledge holder. In the lost land of Oolacile, the sorceries orchestrated light)
  * Dark Sorcerer (holder of a forbidden dark sorcery knowledge, who uses a dark manifestation of humanity to cast spells).
