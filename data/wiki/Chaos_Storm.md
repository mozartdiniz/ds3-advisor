# Chaos Storm

**Chaos Storm** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Chaos Storm  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 3  
**Slots Used** | 2  
**Requirements** | 0 Intelligence/ 0 Faith  
  
**Type** | AoE attack  
  
> Art of the Flame of Chaos, from Ancient Izalith. Erects multiple chaos fire pillars in vicinity.
> 
>   
> Chaotic flame melts even great boulders, and creates a brief surge of molten lava on impact.
> 
>   
> The all-engulfing Chaos Flame eventually formed a tumultuous seedbed, which birthed the twisted things known as Demons. 

### How to Get Chaos Storm in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 12,000 souls after giving him the [Izalith Pyromancy Tome](/Izalith+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Izalith Pyromancy Tome"). 

### Dark Souls 3 Chaos Storm Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.95) damage per pillar and (spell buff x 0.9) damage for the lava.
  * Uses 3 FP 19 times and ~120 stamina to cast.
  * Can still be cast if you have less than 55 FP for fewer fire pillars, but still uses the full stamina cost.
  * This may be the only viable Storm-type spell, as [Fire Storm](/Firestorm "Dark Souls 3 Firestorm") and [Divine Pillars of Light](/Divine+Pillars+of+Light "Dark Souls 3 Divine Pillars of Light") are easy to dodge and leave the caster quite vulnerable.
