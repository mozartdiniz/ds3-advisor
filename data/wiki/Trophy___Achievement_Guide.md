# Trophy & Achievement Guide

The **Trophy & Achievement Guide** for **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** provides an overview of the various trophies and achievements that players can unlock as they progress through the game. On PlayStation and Steam, there are a total of 43 trophies to collect. This includes 1 Platinum trophy, which is the ultimate reward for unlocking all other trophies. Additionally, there are 3 Gold trophies, 13 Silver trophies, and 26 Bronze trophies. Each of these trophies corresponds to different in-game milestones, challenges, or accomplishments, offering players a sense of achievement and a way to track their mastery of the game. This page contains**heavy spoilers**
