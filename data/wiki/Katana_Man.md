### Strategies  

  * The character model is VERY important, like...if he isnt manly then you have no business playing this build, Ya need thick muscles, an amazingly kissable chin, the sweat of a thousand swings, the sexy beard stubble, THE PONYTAIL. Very important stuff!  
  

  * Little to no armor, glass cannon. You have to be very skilled at maneuvering around attacks to get decent damage in. If you feel you need a bit of armor you can use the firelink/fallen knight trouser armors but I personally only added that for fashion. Be very in and out and never over commit, running attacks are a very good tool but never spam it or you will get parried easily. If you wish to roll punish use the washing pole for the extra range, if ya wanna get in with the dirty deeds then use the dual katanas for the chunky damage. The frayed blade is your balanced medium range damage so stick to that for the most part.
