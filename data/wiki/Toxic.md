# Toxic

### [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic")  
  
---  
  
###   
  
  * Effect: Rapidly inflicts HP damage on the player & lowers Stamina Regeneration
  * Mitigation: [Fleshbite Ring](/Fleshbite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fleshbite Ring"), [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit"), [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"), [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality") investment, Equipment with high Poison resistance
  * Cured: [Blooming Purple Moss Clump](/Blooming+Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Blooming Purple Moss Clump"), [Divine Blessing](/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing")

  
  
**Toxic** is a [Status Effect](/Status+Effects "Dark Souls 3 Status Effects") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki").

### Toxic Effects

  * Lasts 90 seconds
  * 22 HP/s Damage
  * Plus 0.1% Max HP/s Damage
  * -5 Stamina Regen

### Toxic Mitigation:

  * [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit")
  * [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection")
  * [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat")
  * [Vitality](/Vitality "Dark Souls 3 Vitality") investment,
  * Equipment with Poison resistance
  * The [Poisonbite Ring](/Poisonbite+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Poisonbite Ring") does not appear to affect Toxic resistance.

### Toxic Cured:

  * [Blooming Purple Moss Clump](/Blooming+Purple+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Blooming Purple Moss Clump")
  * [Divine Blessing](/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing")
  * [Caressing Tears](/Caressing+Tears "Dark Souls 3 Caressing Tears")

### Toxic Notes

  * Toxic was overhauled in the 1.11 patch
  * Toxic shares resistance properties with Poison
  * The amount of HP lost in PVE with each tick varies from one hazard to another. 
    * The toxic swamp in Profaned Capital deal 14 HP/s
    * Bloated shamans in Demon Ruins deal 18 HP/s

### Toxic Sources

  * Swamp in [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital")
  * Toxic pools in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden")
  * [Ghru Conjurator](/Ghru+Conjurator "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Conjurator") in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake")
  * [Sewer Centipede](/Sewer+Centipede "Dark Souls 3 Sewer Centipede") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") and [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital")
  * The [Toxic Mist](/Toxic+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Toxic Mist") Pyromancy 
    * 12 Toxic Buildup per tick in cloud
  * [Dung Pie](/Dung+Pie "Dark Souls 3 Dung Pie")
    * 60 Toxic Buildup on self
    * 65 Toxic Buildup on target
  * [Stalk Dung Pie](/Stalk+Dung+Pie "Dark Souls 3 Stalk Dung Pie")
    * 60 Toxic Buildup on self
    * 65 Toxic Buildup on target 
  * [Demon in Pain & Demon from Below](/Demon+in+Pain+&+Demon+from+Below "Dark Souls 3 Demon in Pain & Demon from Below")
  * [Corvian Settler](/Corvian+Settler "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Settler")
