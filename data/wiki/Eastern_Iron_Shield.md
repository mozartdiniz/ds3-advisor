**Eastern Iron Shield** is a [Shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") in Dark Souls 3. [Small Shields](/Small+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Small Shields") usually weigh very little and have the least form of protection from attacks. They are often used by players who prefer parrying rather than blocking.

> A thick, round metal shield decorated with the unique crest of an eastern land.   
>   
> Heaviest of the small shields, it is impressively weighty and thus able to deflect attacks with ease.  
>   
>  Skill: Weapon Skill  
> Equipping this shield in the left hand allows one to perform the Skill of the right hand weapon.

### Eastern Iron Shield Notes and Tips:

  * Can be Infused.
  * Reinforced using Titanite.
  * Provides an average damage block of 45.4%.  
Offers the highest physical block of the small shields.
  * Second highest stability at of small shields, at 59 when fully upgraded. Tied with [Golden Falcon Shield](/Golden+Falcon+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Golden Falcon Shield") and [Llewellyn Shield](/Llewellyn+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Llewellyn Shield").
  * One of only two [Weapon Skill](/Weapon+Skill "Dark Souls 3 Weapon Skill") small shields, the other being [Warrior's Round Shield](/Warrior's+Round+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Warrior's Round Shield").
  * It deflects weak enemy attacks the same way [Medium Shields](/Standard+Shields "Dark Souls 3 Standard Shields") do.
  * It appeared as the [Iron Round Shield](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Iron+Round+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Iron Round Shield") in _Dark Souls_ , and as the [Iron Parma](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Iron+Parma "Dark Souls 3 Iron Parma") in _Dark Souls 2_.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 3,000 souls after receiving the [Easterner's Ashes](/Easterner's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Easterner's Ashes").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
