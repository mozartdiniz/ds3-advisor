**Hard Leather Armor Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor made of thick, layered leather.
> 
> Used by warriors of old, but one can still expect sound defense.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Sold by [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement) for 4500 souls after you bring him to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Firelink Shrine").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This armor corresponds to the Warrior's set in Dark Souls 1 and Pate's set in Dark Souls 2
