# Orbeck's Ashes

**Orbeck’s Ashes** is an [Ash](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes") in Dark Souls 3.

### Orbeck’s Ashes  
  
---  
  

> Umbral ash of Orbeck of Vinheim. With this, the shrine handmaid will prepare new items.  
>   
> Orbeck was fascinated with sorcery but without means, so offered to serve as an assassin in exchange for acceptance into the Dragon Academy, believing that one day he could reinvent himself as a sorcerer.  
  
### Orbeck’s Ashes Usage

  * Unlocks [Great Soul Arrow](/Great+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Great Soul Arrow"), [Heavy Soul Arrow](/Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Heavy Soul Arrow"), [Great Heavy Soul Arrow](/Great+Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Great Heavy Soul Arrow"), [Soul Greatsword](/Soul+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Soul Greatsword"), [Farron Flashsword](/Farron+Flashsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Flashsword"), [Magic Weapon](/Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon"), [Magic Shield](/Magic+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Magic Shield"), [Spook,](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook") [Aural Decoy](/Aural+Decoy "Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy") and all other spells unlocked with scrolls at the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid").
  * Also unlocked Orbecks set, the [Clandestine Coat](/Clandestine+Coat "Dark Souls 3 Clandestine Coat").
  * Giving the ashes to [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") after she has asked you to kill Orbeck and she will reward the player with the [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade"). This will also unlock the Clandestine coat at the Shrine Handmaid.

### Orbeck’s Ashes Location

  * Obtained from killing [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim").
  * Looted from his corpse in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") in a chair up the first shortcut elevator inside the Archives, on the large wooden balcony just before the stairs that lead to the roof, next to the shortcut ladder. The player must have purchased all possible spells, including those unlocked from scrolls, from Orbeck.

### Trivia

  * The hand in the icon appears to be wearing the [Sorcerer Gloves](/Sorcerer+Gloves "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer Gloves"). However, Orbeck himself wears the [Old Sorcerer Gauntlets](/Old+Sorcerer+Gauntlets "Dark Souls 3 Old Sorcerer Gauntlets") in-game.
