# Hidden Weapon

## Hidden Weapon  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 25  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 12 Intelligence  
**Type** | Weapon Buff  
  
**Hidden Weapon** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Lost sorcery from Oolacile, land of ancient golden sorceries. Turns right weapon invisible.   
>   
> The weapon itself undergoes no inherent changes. Effective use of this, like any tool, perhaps, is reliant upon the ingenuity of its caster. 

### How to Get Hidden Weapon in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 1,500 souls after giving him the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Hidden Weapon Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * This turns the right-hand weapon fully invisible, which can be useful for disguising what you're wielding in PVP. However, a bundle or spell will typically be more useful.
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * Lasts 45 seconds.
