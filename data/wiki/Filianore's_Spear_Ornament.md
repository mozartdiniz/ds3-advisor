# Filianore's Spear Ornament

**Filianore's Spear Ornament** is a [Tool](/Tools "Dark Souls 3 Tools") in Dark Souls 3. It was added with [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Filianore's Spear Ornament  
  
---  
  

> The Spears of the Church watch over the Princess' slumber, and this serves as proof of their fulfillment of their duty. The embellished gold cloth is woven with a crest of young grass. The Princess' knights proudly displayed these precious flavors, which were most often seen swaying from their identical ceremonial spears.  
  
### Filianore's Spear Ornament Usage

  * Proof of a Church Spear's fulfilment of duty. Used to rank up in the [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church"). 
  * Turn in 10 to [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") altar to receive [Young Grass Dew](/Young+Grass+Dew "Dark Souls 3 Young Grass Dew")
  * Turn in 30 to [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") altar to receive [Divine Spear Fragment](/Divine+Spear+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Divine Spear Fragment")

### Filianore's Spear Ornament Locations

  * See the notes of [Ringed Knights](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight") page to see how they can be used to farm this covenant item.
  * Obtain from defeating [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church") or successfully defending.
  * Obtain from the Ringed Inner Wall, take the left path and take the elevator down. Turn to your left and roll/walk into the pathway there. Make a left and hit the illusory wall on your right hand side and there will be the Filianore Spear. ([Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SLySM6ZABU0)) 
  * Additionally, these can quickly be farmed by continuously defeating and reviving Halflight using Velka, Goddess of Sin or the Purging Monument. The souls you receive from defeating Halflight will more than pay back the cost of reinstating the decree to bring them back.
