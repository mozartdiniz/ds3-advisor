# Fireball

**Fireball** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy, you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

> Elementary pyromancy that is often the first learned. Hurls a fireball.  
>   
> To use pyromancies, equip a pyromancy flame and attune a pyromancy at a bonfire.

### How to Get Fireball in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 1000 souls
  * [Pyromancer](/Pyromancer "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancer") starting equipment

### Dark Souls 3 Fireball Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 1.7) damage on a direct hit, and (spell buff x 1.55) damage with the blast.
  * The projectile is fairly slow with a very wide arc that can cover some distance if aimed correctly.
  * It lasts roughly 2.5 seconds of air time before dissipating.
  * Has an upgrade in [Fire Orb](/Fire+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb").
