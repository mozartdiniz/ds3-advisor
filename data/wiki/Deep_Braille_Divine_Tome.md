# Deep Braille Divine Tome

**Deep Braille Divine Tome** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Deep Braille Divine Tome  
  
---  
  

> A braille divine tome of the Deep, belonging to the deacons of the cathedral.   
>   
> Intended to teach divine protection to the deacons of the deep, but later, dark tales were added to its pages, such that it is now considered a thing profane.  
  
### Deep Braille Divine Tome Usage

  * Give to [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim "Irina") or [Karla](/Karla "Karla") to be able to buy [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Deep Protection") and [Gnaw](/Gnaw "Gnaw").

### Deep Braille Divine Tome Locations

  * At the [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep), drops from a Mimic.
  * [Fextra Video Location](https://youtu.be/YYbxMQtIwAM?t=1h37m55s)

### Notes

  * The miracles in this tome count as dark. Giving this to [Irina](/Irina+of+Carim) and buying one of the dark miracles will fail her questline; therefore, it is advisable to give it to [Karla](/Karla) instead. Not buying any dark miracles from Irina will not fail her questline, and the tome will be beside her once you complete it.
