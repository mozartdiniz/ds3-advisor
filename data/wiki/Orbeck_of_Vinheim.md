**Orbeck of Vinheim** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Orbeck is from Vinheim, the home of the Dragon School. He was once fascinated with sorceries but didn't initially meet the requirements to pursue them. Eventually, he joined the Dragon School as an assassin. When Orbeck died and became undead, he was exiled from Vinheim and traveled to Lothric. You can find Orbeck of Vinheim in the Road of Sacrifices, on the second floor of the room leading to the Crystal Sage boss fight. He will relocate to Firelink Shrine after you talk to him, provided you have at least 10 Intelligence.

### Orbeck Dark Souls 3 Information

  * Sells [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"), see Items Sold below
  * Accepts the Scrolls: [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll"), [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll"), [Logan's Scroll](/Logan's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Logan's Scroll"), and [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll").
  * Can be summoned for the [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") fight.
  * Gives "Silent Ally" [gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  * Gives [Young Dragon Ring](/Young+Dragon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring") and [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring").
  * Wears the [Clandestine Set](/Clandestine+Coat "Dark Souls 3 Clandestine Coat").

### Orbeck Dark Souls 3 Location

You can find Orbeck of Vinheim in the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"), on the second floor of the room leading to the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage") boss-fight. He will relocate to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after talking to him if you have 10 [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence").

  * If you lack points in Intelligence, you can use the [Scholar Ring](/Scholar+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Scholar Ring") to temporarily raise your Intelligence above 10, as he does not continue to check after he has moved to the Shrine.

### Orbeck Dark Souls 3 Directions

From the Crucifixion Woods bonfire, follow the path until you reach the ruins, then turn right and cross the swamp until you reach the narrow set of stairs. Climb the steps and proceed into the ruins. Once you make it inside, continue forward, then turn left and follow the path leading deeper into the building. When you reach the intersection with a single enemy wielding a spear and a shield, keep going straight until you find yourself on a rocky outcropping. There will be a somewhat-obscured path to the right that leads up a flight of stairs towards Orbeck.

### Orbeck Dark Souls 3 Questline

Once Orbeck is at Firelink Shrine, if [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") is also there, she will ask you to _"deal"_ with him and will reward you with the [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade") if you bring her his Ashes. Don't do this if you wish to continue giving him Scrolls to unlock new spells. Orbeck's ashes can be given to Yuria after he dies of natural causes, and she will still reward you with the weapon.

  1. Speak with him after buying three [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"), giving him one Scroll, and being any Non-Sorcerer starting class he will thank you and give you the [Young Dragon Ring](/Young+Dragon+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Young Dragon Ring").
  2. After purchasing [Farron Flashsword](/Farron+Flashsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Flashsword"), [Pestilent Mist](/Pestilent+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mist"), [Aural Decoy](/Aural+Decoy "Dark Souls 3 Aural Decoy"), and [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook") he will ask you if you like gimmicks and will give you [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring"), along with the Silent Ally [gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  3. He will **disappear permanently from the game** if: 
     * You defeat any six bosses after recruiting him without giving him a single scroll for study (in earlier patches, he would leave after defeating [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers "Dark Souls 3 Abyss Watchers")).
     * Only one scroll is required to prevent him from leaving. If he disappears from the game, you will not be able to get his ashes, and won't be able to complete [Yuria of Londor's](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") optional quest. Both the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll") and [Sage's Scroll](/Sage's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Scroll") can be acquired in [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep").
  4. Once all spells are purchased from him (buy them all, not just the ones you don't have), he will acknowledge you as a friend and well-trained sorcerer. He will then leave Firelink Shrine, and you will be able to summon him for the [Lothric, Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince") boss fight, just outside the large gate leading to the boss fog. You will also need to have defeated the Curse-Rotted Greatwood to see his summon sign.
  5. After defeating Lothric, Younger Prince, Orbeck's corpse will show up sitting at a table at the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"), on the upper floor where the four sorcery-using candlehead enemies are. From his corpse, his ashes can be looted. 
     * If you do not purchase all his spells before this boss fight, he will remain in the Firelink Shrine and will not die. However, in NG+ and beyond, if you talk to him after giving him all four scrolls and having learned all of his spells in a prior playthrough, his departure will be triggered. Note that learned scrolls may be sold, so if you want Orbeck to remain in the Shrine, you might want to sell a spell that is of little use to you **_(not tested if this will work, needs confirmation)_**.
     * If you do not proceed directly to the next NG, his corpse can be found at the said location even if you did not summon him for the boss fight.

### Orbeck Dark Souls 3 Notes & Trivia

  * If you are unable to meet Orbeck's intelligence requirement to get him back to Firelink Shrine, you can play the entire game and he will remain in his study at the Road of Sacrifices. You can then use the [Scholar Ring](/Scholar+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Scholar Ring") you find later in the game to artificially boost your intelligence and return him to the Shrine. Be sure to immediately return and hand over any scrolls you have. It is unsure when he leaves after all bosses have already been defeated.
  * You don't have to summon him for the Twin Princes fight to get his ashes.
  * If his corpse doesn't appear at the Grand Archives, teleport back to Firelink Shrine and make sure that he left, then go back and his ashes should be there.
  * Orbeck confesses to being an assassin for Vinheim Dragon School. This lends credence to the theory that Griggs of Vinheim from Dark Souls I is also an assassin as they wear the same black robes associated with sorcerer assassins.
