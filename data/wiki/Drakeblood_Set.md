**Drakeblood Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of the Drakeblood Knights, worshippers of the blood of dragons. The red cloth is said to symbolize their yearning for blood.
> 
> Dragon worship has captured the hearts and minds of warriors across the lands for many ages.  
> Perhaps such warriors are attracted to doctrines of few words.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * After killing the Drakeblood armored enemy at [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"), return to the shrine in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden"), behind [Oceiros](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Dark Souls 3 Oceiros, the Consumed King")' bonfire (where you have found the [Path of the Dragon gesture](/Path+of+the+Dragon "Dark Souls 3 Path of the Dragon")). The full armor set will be lootable from the corpse there.

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * At Archdragon Peak, the [Man-Serpent Summoners](/Man-Serpent+Summoner "Dark Souls 3 Man-Serpent Summoner") will summon several different enemies- one of them wears the Drakeblood set and wields a [Drakeblood Greatsword.](/Drakeblood+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Drakeblood Greatsword") Get a summoner to summon this enemy, then kill it. He will drop his greatsword.
  * This armor was used in Dark Souls 2 DLC [Crown of the Sunken King](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Crown+of+the+Sunken+King "Dark Souls 3 Crown of the Sunken King") by Black Drakeblood Knights, led by Sir Yorgh.
  * The cape's upper part often clips through the armor.
