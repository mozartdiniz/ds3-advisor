# Soul of a Nameless Soldier

**Soul of a Nameless Soldier** is a [Soul](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Soul of a Nameless Soldier  
  
---  
  

> Soul found in the corpse of a nameless soldier.  
>   
> Use to acquire souls.  
>   
> Let the Fire Keeper transform this sovereignless soul into a source of strength, for to be Unkindled is to be a vessel for souls.  
  
### Soul of a Nameless Soldier Usage

  * Used to gain 2000 souls

### Soul of a Nameless Soldier Locations

  * 1x can be obtained as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts").
  * 1x at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): at the top of the tower with the [Giant](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement"), right next to the elevator.
  * 1x at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"): next to one of the chained-Giants (the one who has a big blue door next to him).
  * 2x at [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") :
