**Mail Breaker** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.  
  
  

> A small sword made for thrusting attacks.  
>   
> This hard, edgeless sword can pierce through tough armor, and boasts a deadly critical attack.  
>   
> Skill: Shield Splitter  
> Aim carefully, and attack in a large forward lunge to pierce through enemy shields and inflict damage directly.

### Mail Breaker Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Mail Breaker has a motion value of 375 on backstab, 450 on [riposte](/riposte "Dark Souls 3 riposte"), and 75+437.5 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a dagger does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").
    * This means that aside from a difference in AR, no infusion is strictly better for ripostes, as all infusions will get the same motion value, with the only difference being AR. Use whatever infusion will net the most damage for the best results.
    * Note that Dark damage does not benefit from [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") in PvP.

  * The Mail Breaker has a range of 25.1229xl on the 1h R1 and 28.1449xl on the 2h R1.
  * The Mail Breaker's [Weapon Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") Shield Splitter will combo into a second use of Shield Splitter in PvP.

  * The Mail Breaker is the only dagger with a thrusting heavy attack, all other daggers having a horizontal slash. 
  * The mail breaker has decent damage, decent range, a good moveset, and a better [riposte](/riposte "Dark Souls 3 riposte") at low levels than the [Dagger](/Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Dagger"), making it a very good early game weapon.

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting equipment for the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer")
  * Looted from corpse in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"), This corpse is at the bottom floor of the dungeon directly below the _Tower on the Wall_ bonfire. Just on the way to [Greirat](/Greirat "Dark Souls 3 Greirat").
  * A Blessed Mail Breaker can be given to the player by [Sirris of the Sunless Realms](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after being summoned into her world and fighting [Creighton the Wanderer](/Creighton+the+Wanderer "Dark Souls 3 Creighton the Wanderer") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley ](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
