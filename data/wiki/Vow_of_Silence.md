# Vow of Silence

## Vow of Silence  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 35  
****Slots Used**** | 2  
****Requirements**** | 30 Faith  
  
**Vow of Silence** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman,](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

> Miracle of the Sable Church of Londor.  
>   
> Prevents spells in the vicinity, including one's own.  
>   
> Members of the Sable Church are all trained swordsmen, each sworn only to their weapons as they bear the leaden silence of Londor.

### How to Get Vow of Silence in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") or [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 15,000 souls after giving either of them the [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Vow of Silence Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Works on enemies with any sort of projectile magic attacks, and can even trivialize some bosses like [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods") as it disables his arrow rain attack, magic orbs and magic spears. However, it only lasts 15 seconds so the player has to constantly keep up with the spell.
  * Range can be increased by [Sacred Chime of Filianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
  * It disables the ability to use your spells too.
  * Reveals Mimics and the Londor Assassin in the Church of Yorshka.
  * Is affected by the [Lingering Dragon Crest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"), but only for the caster and other who wear it.
  * This spell first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Vow+of+Silence "Dark Souls 3 Vow of Silence")_.
