# NPCs

**NPCs** or **Non-Player Characters** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** refer to the many pre-designed inhabitants that players meet throughout their journey. These NPCs often provide valuable information that helps players piece together the lore of the game. Some are quest givers or are related to each other in various ways, while many are merchants with whom players can trade for equipment, magic, and items. Below is a list of all these characters, with names taken from the game credits, provided via dialogue, or, if nothing else was available at the time of creation, described by the community. Each NPC contributes uniquely to the player's experience, offering assistance, challenges, or deeper insight into the game's world.

  * Please see the [Side Quests](/Side+Quests "Dark Souls 3 Side Quests") page for an overview of how NPC questlines interact with each other.
  * Please scroll to the bottom of the page to see [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel") and [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") NPCs
