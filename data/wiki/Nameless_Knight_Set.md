**Nameless Knight Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  

> (Piece Name) of a nameless knight. Crafted with thin metal greatly reinforced by a grooved finish.

### Location/Where to Find

  * In [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep#map "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep#map"), from Old Wolf of Farron bonfire, go down the ladder. Go to the campfire in front of you, and then to the other campfire next to it, on the right. You should see the armor in a corner, next to the stone wall.

### Notes and Tips

  * The starting armor set for the Knight in Dark Souls 1
