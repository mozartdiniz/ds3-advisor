# Hidden Body

## Hidden Body  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 25  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence  
**Type** | Turns body nearly invisible  
  
**Hidden Body** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Lost sorcery from Oolacile, land of ancient golden sorceries. Turns body nearly invisible.   
>   
> Although perfect invisibility is unachievable due to the risk of dissipation, the caster need only stand still for a moment to blend in to environs with astounding camouflage. 

### How to Get Hidden Body in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 3,000 souls after giving him the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Lasts 15 seconds. With [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"), this goes up to 19.5 seconds.
  * Enemies can still detect your footsteps and will attack if you get too close to them.
  * Using [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook") or [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring") together with this spell will make you nearly undetectable to enemies.
  * This spell, [Spook](/Spook "Dark Souls 3 Spook")/[Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring") and [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire") work in tandem for easy mob-clearing. One just needs to Backstab with a weapon that has high AR/Critical and move to the next. [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire") will keep your Focus refilled. 
    * 2 backstabs with [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire") will refund the cost of this spell + an additional 10fp
    * If you equip [Aldrich's Sapphire](/Aldrich's+Sapphire "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich's Sapphire") along with the [Dusk Crown Ring](/Dusk+Crown+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dusk Crown Ring") a single backstab will refund all but 3fp. Equipping an unupgraded [simple](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem") weapon will regenerate exactly 3fp over 15 seconds. 
      * Instead of the [simple](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem") weapon, the [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword") can be used so long as the backstab kills the enemy (and leaves you with a 3fp left over). You do not need to use it to kill the enemy; you just need the [Executioner's Greatsword](/Executioner's+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Executioner's Greatsword") visible while an enemy dies.
  * Using [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring") and [Untrue White Ring](/Untrue+White+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Untrue White Ring") together with this spell will allow you to disguise as a ghost.
  * Duration can also be increased with the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") (R+0 = 19,5 sec, R+1 = 20,25 sec, R+2 = 21 sec).
  * Ideal for blockade running, getting to bosses' rooms, etc. Also very helpful for sorcerers to get into the right casting position. Since your cover is not blown while range attacking most enemies, this spell is very useful for one-shotting enemies with ranged attacks (e.g. the Deacons in Irithyll).
  * This spell completly disables [Homing Soulmass](/Homing+Soulmass "Dark Souls 3 Homing Soulmass")/[Homing Crystal Soulmass](/Homing+Crystal+Soulmass "Dark Souls 3 Homing Crystal Soulmass") and [Affinity](/Affinity "Dark Souls 3 Affinity") from launching to the caster, even if they lock on. This can be useful when dealing against players relying on these kind of spells, or NPCs such as [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade").
