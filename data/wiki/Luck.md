# Luck

## Luck

**Luck** is a [stat](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Luck in Dark Souls 3 affects your [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") rate, which determines how frequently enemies will drop multiple items upon their death. While Luck does not influence the damage dealt by Bleed and Poison effects, it does increase the speed at which these status effects apply to enemies. By investing in Luck, you can improve your chances of finding valuable items and enhance your efficiency in applying status effects during combat. Furthermore, increasing your Luck also improves your character's overall defense and enhances resistance to Curse.

### Luck Dark Souls 3 Effects

  * Characters begin with 100 [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery"). For each point of luck you receive one point of Item Discovery, up to a hard cap at 99 Luck (199 Item Discovery).
  * For each point of Luck you receive 0.4 in all defenses, and 0.2 in resistances. At certain Luck values you gain additional Curse Resistance, going all the way up to 99. See the table for which values.
  * The [Man-Grub's Staff's ](/Man-Grub's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Man-Grub's Staff")spell damage, [Anri's Sword ](/Anri's+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Anri's Straight Sword")and [Hollow](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") infused weapons receive damage scaling based on Luck.
  * Regular weapons and weapons infused with a [Blood Gem](/Blood+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blood Gem") scale [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed") buildup based on Luck. Best gains are between 25 and 50, where 50 is the hard cap.
  * [Dorhy's Gnawing](/Dorhy's+Gnawing "Dark Souls 3 Dorhy's Gnawing") is unaffected by the Luck stat as for bleed build-up.
  * Holding a weapon infused with Hollow while at 15 or more [Hollowing](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") increases the player's Luck based on the weapon's upgrade level (+5 to Luck at upgrade +10), and it can be stacked by holding a Hollow [shield](/Shields "Dark Souls 3 Shields") or offhand weapon.

### Luck Table by Anon in DS3

NOTES about Luck scaling

  * Bleeding and poison build-up (innate or from a blood/poison infusion) is always scaling with luck, except when the weapon is infused with a raw, deep or fire gem (erase every scaling).
  * Physical damage (AR) scales with luck when the weapon is hollow-infused (note a hollow weapon gives +5 luck when wielded if your hollow level > 15).
  * For a given weapon, the luck scaling will always be in the following order:  
Blood/poison infusions > hollow infusions > regular weapon > other infusions > raw/deep/fire infusions (=0)

SPECIAL WEAPONS

  * Eleonora axe: its weapon art "Feast Bell" applies 55 bleeding to the weapon, scaling with luck. However note this scaling is almost null (E scaling, only +16% at 99 luck).
  * Anri's straight sword physical AR will naturally scale strongly with luck (A scaling, +110% AR at 99 luck)
  * Man-grub's staff: exceptional S luck scaling, the magic/dark damages from the sorcery spells scale with luck. But not the spell buff, so the magic weapon buffs will not scale with luck.

LUCK SCALING VALUE

For calculation, take the base damage or bleed/poison build-up and multiply it per this value, to know the gain at 99 luck.  
Ex with Pontiff knight great scythe bleeding. Base bleeding 30 x scaling 160/100 : 30x1,6=48 bleeing at 99 luck.

LUCK SCALING LETTER

  * E scaling: from 100 to 120%
  * D scaling: from 120 to 150%
  * C scaling: from 150 to 180%
  * B scaling: from 180 to 200%
  * A scaling: from 200 to 240%
  * S scaling: from 240 to 300% or more

LISTED WEAPON

  * Every innate bleeding/poison weapons, in their regular, blood/poison-infused, hollow-infused form or infused with "other" gems (heavy, sharp, refined, simple, crystal, blessed, lightning, chaos, dark)
  * Every blood/poison and hollow-infused weapons
  * Special weapons (see above)
  * Items with a luck scaling : bleeding Kukris and poison throwing knives
