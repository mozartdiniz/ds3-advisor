# Soul Spear

## Soul Spear  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 32  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 32 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Soul Spear** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff ](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves")or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery developed by Big Hat Logan, the great sorcerer who left the Dragon School in an age long past.  
>   
> Fires a soul spear.  
>   
> The spear boasts superior penetrating power, and is testament to Logan's strength in battle.

### How to Get Soul Spear in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in: [Logan's Scroll](/Logan's+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Logan's Scroll")
  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 5000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Soul Spear Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Soul Spear inflicts (Spell Buff x 2.5) magic damage before resistances.
  * Use only recommended against groups of enemies. For single-target purposes, [Great Heavy Soul Arrow](/Great+Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Great Heavy Soul Arrow") is more cost efficient.
  * Compared to Great Heavy Soul Arrow, Soul Spear costs 2.2x FP, ~10% less stamina, deals ~3% more damage, has roughly the same cast speed, and pierces through enemies. 
  * Has an upgrade in [Crystal Soul Spear](/Crystal+Soul+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Soul Spear").
