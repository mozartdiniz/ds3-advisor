# Covenants

**Covenants** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** allow the player to ally with certain factions within the game to gain access to special items, abilities, or mechanics, especially during [online](/Online "Dark Souls 3 Online") play. To join a Covenant, the player character must equip one of several Covenant insignias discovered throughout Lothric. Select one of the links below for more detailed information regarding each Covenant.

### Matchmaking Formula Varies by Covenant

**Co-op** | Sign Summons | A ： Host's Level |  Upper: A * 1.1 + 10  Lower: A * 0.9 - 10  Remarks: Also affects [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight") and [Mound Makers](/Mound+Makers "Dark Souls 3 Mound Makers")  
---|---|---|---  
[Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue "Dark Souls 3 Way of Blue") (Auto) | B ： Host's Level |  Upper: B * 1.1 + 10  Lower: B * 0.9 - 10  Remarks: Affects [Blue Sentinels](/Blue+Sentinels "Dark Souls 3 Blue Sentinels") and [Blades of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon")  
**PvP** | Dark Spirit Invasion | C ： Invader Level |  Upper: C * 1.1 + 20  Lower: C * 0.9  Remarks: [Red Eye Orb](/Red+Eye+Orb "Dark Souls 3 Red Eye Orb"), etc   
[Mound Maker](/Mound+Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound Maker") Invasion | D ： Invader Level |  Upper: D * 1.15 + 20  Lower: D * 0.9  Remarks: [Mound Makers](/Mound+Makers "Dark Souls 3 Mound Makers")  
Covenant Invasion (defend territory) | E ： Host Level |  Upper: E * 1.1  Lower: E * 0.8 - 20  Remarks: [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron"), [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful")
