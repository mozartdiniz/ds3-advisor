**Ringed City Slave (Aka Hollow Gem)** is an [Enemy](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").

### Ringed City Slave Enemy Description

  * Human often met in The Ringed City wearing red coat similar to Slave's coat.
  * Appears in 2 variants: regular and cursed. Often in groups.

### Ringed City Slave Combat Information

  * Sets traps (e.g. play dead) and attacks from above. 
  * Regular uses lightning spells: Lightning Stake and Spear. May also attack with their stake. Can also break player attack by pushing him or perform a critical strike on him.
  * Cursed one, despite weilding stake, will not attack with it. They try to stay as close as possible to player.
  * Weak to [Slash Damage](/Slash+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Slash Damage"), [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite") and [Bleed](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed").
  * Resistant to [Lightning Damage](/Lightning+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Damage"), [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison") and [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic").
  * Can be lured.
  * Can be rapported.
  * Bows and crossbows are helpful while facing cursed one. If player fight them at close range, cursebite ring is recommended.
  * Using [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring") and [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") at the same time DO NOT prevent player from aggroing them. However, they often appear near the [Harald Legion Knight](/Harald+Legion+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Harald Legion Knight")s, which seems to ignore the player if equipped with mentioned ring and under influence of Hidden Body, so using them both will make some fights easier.
