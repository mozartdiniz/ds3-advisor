# Atonement

**Atonement** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Atonement  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 15  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Faith  
**Type** | Unique Buff  
  
> Miracle given to those cast out from the Sable Church of Londor.  
>   
> Attracts more attention from foes.  
>   
> This is the only tale known by exiles, who believe it carries words of forgiveness. Cursed journeys, too, must ultimately come to an end.

### How to Get Atonement in Dark Souls 3

  * Found in: [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"), near the _Farron Keep Perimeter_ bonfire. From the bonfire walk back and drop where the wall is broken, there will be a body holding the spell.
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=2h36m24s "Video location")

### Dark Souls 3 Atonement Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Lasts 30 seconds.
  * Increases enemy aggro range in addition to making enemies target the caster more often.
  * Same effect as the [Skull Ring](/Skull+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Skull Ring") and [Moaning Shield](/Moaning+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Moaning Shield").
  * Stacks with everything.
