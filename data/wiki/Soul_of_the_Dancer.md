# Soul of the Dancer

**Soul of the Dancer** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls "Dark Souls 3 Boss Souls") in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Dancer  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the dancer.  
> One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength. Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength. The Pontiff Sulyvahn bestowed a double-slashing sword upon a distant daughter of the formal royal family, ordering her to serve first as a dancer, and then as an outrider knight, the equivalent to exile.  
  
### Soul of the Dancer Usage

  * Can be consumed for 10,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Dancer's Enchanted Swords](/Dancer's+Enchanted+Swords "Dark Souls 3 Dancer's Enchanted Swords") or [Soothing Sunlight](/Soothing+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Soothing Sunlight")

### Soul of the Dancer Locations

  * Obtained from defeating [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley")

### Notes

  * Player note 1

### Trivia

  * This boss reappeared in Elden Ring Nightreign.
