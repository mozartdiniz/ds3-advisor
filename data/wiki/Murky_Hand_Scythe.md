**Murky Hand Scythe** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**. It was added in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").  
  
  

> A short-shafted hand scythe wielded by the murkmen who rise from the depths.  
>   
> Enveloped by a black dampness, and imbued with the strength of dark
> 
> Skill: Quickstep  
> Instantly step behind or around the side of foes. Especially effective when locked on to target.

### Murky Hand Scythe Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * Has greater AR with [Chaos Gem ](/Chaos+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Chaos Gem")than with [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem"). Which does more damage will depend on your opponent's defense and absorption.
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Harpe has a motion value of 300 on backstab, 360 on riposte, and 60+350 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a dagger does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").
    * This means that aside from a difference in AR, no infusion is strictly better for ripostes, as all infusions will get the same motion value, with the only difference being AR. Use whatever infusion will net the most damage for the best results.
    * Note that dark damage does not benefit from [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") in PvP.

  * Murky Hand Scythe has a range of 24.3255xl on the 1h R1 and 26.0078xl on the 2h R1
  * The [Weapon Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") Quickstep can be used to lessen the recovery frames of any attack.
  * Despite having the normal dagger moveset, the [Harpe](/Harpe "Dark Souls 3 Harpe") and Murky Hand Scythe have the hit stun properties of a katana on the 2h lunging R1, or first 2h R1 out of neutral.
  * The Murky Hand Scythe loses a lot of physical damage when Dark infused, and loses a lot of dark damage when Chaos infused, due to a previous nerf. 
  * The Murky Hand Scythe gains more AR on all standard builds (66 Strength build, 75-80 Dex build, 60 Int build, 60 Faith build, 30/30 Int/Faith build) when compared to Harpe, and can generally be considered a straight upgrade, aside from the extra requirements in Int and Faith, range, and a few niche builds or infusions (such as Blessed on a 60 Faith build). 
    * The ability to buff this weapon makes it exceptional on a Strength-Faith hybrid build, and gives it an edge with [Dark Blade](/Dark+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade").

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found on a corpse in [The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap"), after dropping through the glass ceiling. It's up some stairs over a collapsed bookcase laying on its side.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
