# Whips

**Whips** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. These [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") usually do little to moderate damage, and perform poorly against well-armored foes, but have long reach and can have Auxiliary effects such as [Bleeding](/Bleed "Dark Souls 3 Bleed") or [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison"). Unlike other melee weapons, whips cannot be parried but cannot backstab or riposte.
