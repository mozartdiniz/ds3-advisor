# Blessed Gem

**Blessed Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in Dark Souls 3.

### Blessed Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. commonly known as a charm kept by saints.   
>   
> Used in infusion to create blessed weapons.   
>   
> Special blessed weapons gradually restore HP and heavily damage reanimated foes.  
  
### Blessed Gem Effect

  * Used for [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") to create Blessed weapons.
  * Blessed Gem have lower base damage, but grants the infused weapon HP regen, [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") scaling for its innate physical and/or lightning damage, and 20% bonus damage to reanimated foes. This applies to the following:  

    * Regular [Skeletons](/Skeleton "Dark Souls 3 Skeleton")
    * [Gravewarden Skeleton](/Gravewarden+Skeleton "Dark Souls 3 Gravewarden Skeleton")
    * [Carthus Swordsman Skeleton](/Carthus+Swordsman+Skeleton "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Swordsman Skeleton")
    * [Skeleton Wheel](/Skeleton+Wheel "Dark Souls 3 Skeleton Wheel")
    * [Reanimated Corpse](/Reanimated+Corpse "Dark Souls 3 Reanimated Corpse")
    * [Infested Corpse](/Infested+Corpse "Dark Souls 3 Infested Corpse")
    * [Corpse Grub](/Maggot-grub "Dark Souls 3 Maggot-grub")
    * Irithyllian Hound
    * [High Lord Wolnir](/High+Lord+Wolnir "Dark Souls 3 High Lord Wolnir")
  * Attacks also prevent reanimated foes from reviving after they've been defeated.
  * Also requires the [Sage's Coal](/Sage's+Coal "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Coal") in order to start infusing weapons.

### Blessed Gem Locations

  * Dropped by [Evangelists](/Cathedral+Evangelist "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral Evangelist")
  * 1x Found at [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep") \- To reach this, take the shortcut to the left of the bonfire at Cleansing Chapel (When coming in from the front), take the elevator into the room with the crossbowman on your left. Go on the ledge, take the ladder there, and then drop off onto another ledge after killing a scholar. Follow this until you reach the rafters, and then it's on the far right side of the rafters ([Video location](http://youtu.be/EScEIIGIHbI "Video location")).
  * Can drop from the [Winged Knight](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight") in the courtyard near the fountain in [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric").
  * One can be found at the very top of [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"), dropping in a hole in the area upstairs at the opposing side of the big bridge leading to Lorian and Lothric room. 
  * One can be obtained from trading the [Moaning Shield](/Moaning+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Moaning Shield") to the Crows nest on the roof of the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").
  * Possible Drop from [Corvian Storyteller](/Corvian+Storyteller "Dark Souls 3 Corvian Storyteller") in Farron Keep.
  * Rare drop from [Hollow Priests](/Hollow+Priest "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Priest") in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle") and in the [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden").
  * Possible Drop from [Ascended Winged Knights](/Ascended+Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ascended Winged Knight") on the roof of the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"), above the shortcut before the bridge leading to the Lorian and Lothric boss room.
  * In the DLC [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel"), one can be located behind the Giant Tower.
  * One can be found in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC. After being kicked down by Lapp inside the tower, walk down a few steps, and the gem will be on a corpse
  * Has a rare chance to drop from the [Hollow Clerics](/Hollow+Cleric "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Cleric") in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") DLC.

### Notes

  * Blessed equipment will regenerate 3 HP per tick. The amount of HP regenerated cannot be increased, but the time between ticks can be decreased. Each upgrade level will decrease the tick interval by 0.25 seconds.  

**Upgrade Leve** l | **Tick Interval** | **Effective HP/second**  
---|---|---  
+0 |  4s | **0.75 HP/s**  
+1 |  3.75s | **0.8 HP/s**  
+2 |  3.5s | **0.857 HP/s**  
+3 |  3.25s | **0.923 HP/s**  
+4 |  3s | **1 HP/s**  
+5 |  2.75s | **1.09 HP/s**  
+6 |  2.5s | **1.2 HP/s**  
+7 |  2.25s | **1.333 HP/s**  
+8 |  2s | **1.5 HP/s**  
+9 |  1.75s | **1.714 HP/s**  
+10 | 1.5s | **2 HP/s**  
  
  * The blessed effect stacks with the [Sun Princess Ring](/Sun+Princess+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Sun Princess Ring"), effectively doubling your HP regeneration at +10.
  * The blessed effect also stacks with other blessed equipment. Assuming two +10 blessed items are equipped, you will regenerate 6 HP/1.5 seconds (effective 4 HP/s). 
    * Weapons that come in pairs when two-handed, such as [Fist](/Fist+&+Claws "Dark Souls 3 Fist & Claws") weapons, will not stack the effect. Two individual weapon slots need to be filled in order to stack the effect.
  * Blessed weapons cannot be buffed with resins or magical weapon buffs.
  * The bonus damage applies to all skeletons, not just the ones capable of reanimating.
  * It is recommended to put this infusion on offhand parrying tools, such as a [Buckler](/Buckler "Dark Souls 3 Buckler") or [Parrying Dagger](/Parrying+Dagger "Dark Souls 3 Parrying Dagger"). The blessed infusion lowers all guard absorption stats except lightning, so using this on something you won't be blocking with is essentially free HP regeneration.

### Trivia

  * This infusion makes a return from Demon's Souls.
