**Lapp's Set** is an Armor in Dark Souls 3. It was added in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City") [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC").   
  
  
  
  

> Steel armor of the amnesiac Lapp.
> 
> This plate-armor body, that covers the torso cleanly without even slight gaps, provides heavy defense.
> 
> Quite fit to conceal a hollowed body, and indeed one's very identity.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Complete the [Lapp Questline](/Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Lapp").
  * Kill [Lapp](/Lapp "Dark Souls 3 Lapp") and purchase the set from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 31,000 souls.
  * He will reside in the cave past where you met Midir on the Bridge. Make a right and you'll find Lapp sitting. Talk to him and you will get a cutscene. To get his armor set you must work your way back to where you receive the cutscene.

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * It is recommended that you do the questline instead of killing him to save souls. It is a very straightforward quest that sheds some light on the game's long building lore.
  * This set was originally cut from the base game and was previously named the Heavy Knight's Set (shown below). It also had a more silver color than Lapp's Set.
  * The overall design of Lapp's Set is based of the _Stechzeug,_ a suit of armor for Jousting.
