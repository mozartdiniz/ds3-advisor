# Carthus Pyromancy Tome

**Carthus Pyromancy Tome** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Carthus Pyromancy Tome  
  
---  
  

> A pyromancy tome from Carthus containing pyromancies crafted for battle.  
>   
> Give to the old pyromancer to learn advanced pyromancies of the Great Swamp.  
>   
> Carthus pyromancies developed in isolation from other forms, explaining their divergent evolution.  
  
### How to Use Carthus Pyromancy Tome in Dark Souls 3

  * Give to [Cornyx](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") to unlock [Acid Surge](/Acid+Surge "Dark Souls 3 Acid Surge"), [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon"), [Carthus Flame Arc](/Carthus+Flame+Arc "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Flame Arc")

### How to Get Carthus Pyromancy Tome in Dark Souls 3

  * [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus"). From the Abyss Watchers bonfire cross the bridge with the Scimitar wielding skeleton. Go up the nearby staircase, head down the hallway to the left, and descend the ladder you find. When you see the red eyed shotel skeleton, there is an illusory wall on the right.
