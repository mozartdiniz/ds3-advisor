# Havel's Ring

### Havel's Ring  
  
---  
  

> A ring for warriors keen on heavy accoutrements. Increases maximum equip load.  
>   
> This ring was named after Havel the Rock, the battlefield compatriot of Gwyn, the First Lord.  
>   
> The art of war has been a constant since ages past, and those who would follow in Havel's footsteps are no fewer now than in his own day.  
  
**Havel's Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Havel's Ring Effect

  * Increases maximum [equip load](/Equipment+Load "Dark Souls 3 Equipment Load") by 15%
  * +1 Version: +17%
  * +2 Version: +18%
  * +3 Version: +19%
  * Weight: 1.5

### Where to Find Havel's Ring

  * [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition"): Created with the [Soul of a Stray Demon](/Soul+of+a+Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Stray Demon") and 5000 souls, both of which the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon") drops upon being killed. The Stray Demon resides at the [Old Wolf of Farron](/Old+Wolf+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Old Wolf of Farron") bonfire, in the topmost area (take the elevator.)
  * +1 Version (NG+): [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"). From the Great Belfry bonfire, proceed down the stairs outside. Turn left towards the stairs leading up to the archway and the giant enemies. After passing the enemies, the ring will be on a corpse on your left.
  * +2 Version (NG++): On a wooden beam below the Prison Tower bonfire in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley")/[Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). ()
  * +3 Version (The Ringed City): Just below where you get invaded by [Dark Spirit Ledo](/Silver+Knight+Ledo "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Ledo"), on the ledges above the swamp. ([Location](http://imgur.com/LPDmdaL)) ([Video Location](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhP1h38RDTI))

### Havel's Ring Notes

  * You can also find [Havel's Set](/Havel's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Set") after defeating the [Stray Demon](/Stray+Demon "Dark Souls 3 Stray Demon") if you have defeated the Havel Knight that spawns in [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak").
  * Havel's Ring has been heavily nerfed; unlike it's Dark Souls counterpart, which raised equip load by a whopping 50%, this Havel's Ring only raises it by 15%.
  * This ring may be useful for fast-roll builds (<= 30% equipment load) as the actual usable weight allowance gained is the equivalent of a good number of points in vitality. _**If aiming for 30% load**_ , this ring allows you to carry the following extra item weight after accounting for the ring's own weight ( extra weight = ((max_weight * ring_bonus/100)-1.5)*0.3 ): 
    * Basic Ring  

      * 10 Vitality: 1.8 (~ +6 vit)
      * 20 Vitality: 2.25 (~ +7.5 vit)
      * 30 Vitality: 2.7 (~ +9 vit)
      * 40 Vitality: 3.15 (~ +10.5 vit)
    * +3 Ring 
      * 10 Vitality: 2.4 (~ +8 vit)
      * 20 Vitality: 2.97 (~ +9.9 vit)
      * 30 Vitality: 3.54 (~ +11.8 vit)
      * 40 Vitality: 4.11 (~ +13.7 vit)
  * This ring first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring")_.
