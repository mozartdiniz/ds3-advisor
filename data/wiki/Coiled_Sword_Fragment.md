# Coiled Sword Fragment

**Coiled Sword Fragment** is a [Tool](/Tools "Dark Souls 3 Tools") in Dark Souls 3.

### Coiled Sword Fragment  
  
---  
  

> Fragment of the coiled sword of a bonfire which served its purpose long ago.  
>   
> Returns caster to last bonfire used for resting, or to the bonfire in Firelink Shrine. Can be used repeatedly.  
>   
> Bonfires are linked to one another irreversibly, retaining their affinity long after their purpose is exhausted.  
  
### Coiled Sword Fragment Usage

  * Same effect as [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone"), only it can be used an infinite number of times. 

### Coiled Sword Fragment Location

  * In the copy of Firelink Shrine in the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves"), where the bonfire would be.

### Notes

  * Can be traded to [Pickle Pee](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") for a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite slab"). If you don't have a problem to spend 500 souls per Homeward Bone it is a really good trade.
  * Does persist into NG+, however does not stack. Upon receiving the second Coiled Sword Fragment, it is sent to your Storage Box.
