### Build Strategy

The build is based on stealth and status effects/damage over time, It means you wont need high level INT or FAITH neither upgrade your build to do nice damage. This is how it works: 

Using Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring masks your steps and [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") makes you invisible ( both together stack performing your stealth combo), therefore enemies won't see you unless you are right next to them (there are unavoidable enemies, whole [bosses](/bosses "Dark Souls 3 bosses") and some high vision range enemies like archers which will see you at closer ranges). 

[Both effects ring and spell stack] 

The key is to keep casting [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") every 14-15 seconds (or you will apear and enemies will aggro you). Thats why we carry so many [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask"). 

All your spells can damage the enemies without getting aggro, except [Dorhy's Gnawing](/Dorhy's+Gnawing "Dark Souls 3 Dorhy's Gnawing") which can help you vs Bosses inmune to [poison](/poison "Dark Souls 3 poison") or [toxic](/toxic "Dark Souls 3 toxic"). 

  
As soon as you can get [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body") and [Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring](/Slumbering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Slumbering Dragoncrest Ring"), all you need is   
[Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") the [White Hair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman") and dot spells(damage over time). Check the Dark Souls 3 Calculator link to see which spell fits you best: 

  
  
  
Basically the goal of your character is to beat the whole game without dealing direct damage or casting scaling spells and instead overusing all the utility spells in the game such as [Rapport](/Rapport "Dark Souls 3 Rapport"), [Twisted wall of light](/Twisted+wall+of+light "Dark Souls 3 Twisted wall of light"), [Force](/Force "Dark Souls 3 Force") and of course dot spells . Therefore you use the [Storyteller's Staff](/Storyteller's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Storyteller's Staff") (because the Weapon Art is a better version of [Poison Mist](/Poison+Mist "Dark Souls 3 Poison Mist") which cause tons of damage to large hp enemies and stacks with [toxic](/toxic "Dark Souls 3 toxic")) and [White Wair Talisman](/White+Hair+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 White Hair Talisman") (this way you can cast all kind of spell without switching weapons) as your only source. 

You can inflict: [Poison](/Poison "Dark Souls 3 Poison")(from weapon art'staff), [Toxic](/Toxic "Dark Souls 3 Toxic"), [Frost](/Frost "Dark Souls 3 Frost"), and [Pestilent Mercury](/Pestilent+Mercury "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mercury") at the same time to a group of enemies WITHOUT EVEN BEING DETECTED!! 

[Once you've inflicted one status switch to another spell, but remember to keep casting [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body")] 

[Don't forget that [Snap Freeze](/Snap+Freeze "Dark Souls 3 Snap Freeze") wont trigger [Frost](/Frost "Dark Souls 3 Frost") for a while after you do it once] 

Depending the boss fight you may need to use differents spells due to the fact that some [bosses](/bosses "Dark Souls 3 bosses") are inmune to status effects like [poison](/poison "Dark Souls 3 poison")/ [toxic](/toxic "Dark Souls 3 toxic"), [bleed](/bleed "Dark Souls 3 bleed") or [frost](/frost "Dark Souls 3 frost"). However you can always use [Pestilent Mercury](/Pestilent+Mercury "Dark Souls 3 Pestilent Mercury")* because there's no enemy inmune to it.
