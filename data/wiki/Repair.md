# Repair

## Repair  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 20  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Intelligence  
**Type** | Equipment repair  
  
**Repair** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Lost sorcery from Oolacile, land of ancient golden sorceries.   
>   
> Repairs equipped weapons and armor. Includes weapons with exhausted durability.   
>   
> While the effects of this spell are rather subtle, its foundations are a well-guarded secret. Light is time, and the reversal of its effects is a forbidden art. 

### How to Get Repair in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 2,000 souls after giving him the [Golden Scroll](/Golden+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Golden Scroll").

### Dark Souls 3 Repair Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Restores durability of all equipped items by 50 points, even if the items have fully broken.
  * Same effect as [Repair Powder](/Repair+Powder "Dark Souls 3 Repair Powder"), although Repair Powder will not restore an item with 0 durability.
  * Can be useful with the [Moonlight Greatsword](/Moonlight+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Moonlight Greatsword") charged attack. The durability damage from 12.5 charge attacks can be repaired with one cast of Repair, making this combination far more FP-efficient than any sorcery with comparable damage.
