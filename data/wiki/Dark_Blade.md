# Dark Blade

## Dark Blade  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 35  
****Slots Used**** | 1  
****Requirements**** | 25 Faith  
****Duration**** | 60 Seconds  
Type | Weapon Buff  
  
**Dark Blade** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman,](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans") or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

> Miracle of the Sable Church of Londor.  
>   
> Reinforces right weapon with dark.   
>   
> The third daughter Liliane, one of the founders of the Sable Church, is said to recount tales that portray the suffering and conflict of Hollows.

### How to Get Dark Blade in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") or [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 10,000 souls, after giving either the [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome").

### Dark Souls 3 Dark Blade Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Adds [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage") equal to (Spell Buff * 0.85).  

    * Damage is applied each time the enemy is hit with the weapon, regardless of the damage of the attack performed.
  * Buff lasts 60 seconds (affected by [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring"))
  * Counts as a [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking"), and will overwrite similar buffs.
  * Only counts the Faith portion of your Catalyst's Spell Buff. This spell gains no advantage with dark catalysts.
  * [Dark Clutch Ring](/Dark+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Dark Clutch Ring") and the [Blindfold Mask](/Blindfold+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Blindfold Mask") increase the buff's damage.
  * Pairs nicely with [Crucifix of the Mad King](/Crucifix+of+the+Mad+King "Dark Souls 3 Crucifix of the Mad King") and [Murky Hand Scythe](/Murky+Hand+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Murky Hand Scythe"), as those weapons already have innate dark damage and are quite powerful already.
  * A few weapons and weapon arts have hidden multipliers that slightly lower the buff's damage per hit.  
Namely, [Daggers](/Daggers "Dark Souls 3 Daggers") and [Paired Weapons](/Twinblades "Dark Souls 3 Twinblades") display this adjustment.  
Regardless, the buffs always remain efficient.
  * Since faith builds have access to four elements in unique weapon buff spells ([Blessed Weapon](/Blessed+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Weapon"), [Lightning Blade](/Lightning+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Blade"), [Darkmoon Blade](/Darkmoon+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Darkmoon Blade"), and Dark Blade), they're the ideal choice for a more physical-focused build investing into magic.
  * This spell first appeared as a hex in _Dark Souls 2_ known as [Dark Weapon](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Dark Weapon").
