# Bursting Fireball

**Bursting Fireball** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Bursting Fireball  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 14  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 18 Intelligence/12 Faith  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> Pyromancy of old restored by Cornyx. One of the Great Swamp's more advanced spells.  
>   
> Hurls an exploding fireball.  
>   
> A rare technique that undergoes change after leaving the caster's hand, making this a spell renowned for its difficulty.

### How to Get Bursting Fireball in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 5,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Bursting Fireball Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 0.71) damage with a limit of 4 hits per cast.
  * Launches a fireball that almost immediately splits into several smaller fireballs. This shotgun effect can hit several enemies and is more likely to hit faster foes.
  * This pyromancy is more cost-efficient compared to other single-shot spells if at least three of the fireballs hit. This can be especially useful against dense crowds or big threats.
  * in general, this pyromancy is powerful at mid to short range, and very cost effective if used right. However, it has generally low damage.
