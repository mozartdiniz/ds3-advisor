# Firebomb

**Firebomb** is a [projectile](/projectiles "Dark Souls 3 projectiles") in Dark Souls 3.

### Firebomb  
  
---  
  

> Bisque urn filled with black powder. Explodes, inflicting fire damage.  
>   
> Fire damage, which differs from physical damage, is highly effective against creatures of flesh, beasts, and other foes that might naturally have cause to fear the flame."  
  
### Firebomb Usage

  * Throw at enemies to deal fire damage.

### Firebomb Locations

  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 100 souls.
  * Sold by [Greirat of the Undead Settlement](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") for 50 souls.
  * **5x** at [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetery+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetery of Ash"): down a small path to the right going downhill of the pathway to the boss. ([Video Location](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=20m38s))
  * **11x** at [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"): 
    * **2x** In a room (with a caged door) underneath the fallen wyvern, far towards the left side of the "**_High Wall of Lothric_** " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). ([Video Location](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=45m35s))
    * **3x** In the room just below the "_**Tower on the Wall**_ " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). It is on a corpse laying at the edge of a long wooden plank. ([Video Location](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=50m43s))
    * **3x** Looted from the remains atop the roof where the [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man") is, past the "_**Tower on the Wall**_ " [bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). ([Video Location](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=53m8s))
    * **3x** In a balcony behind two [Hollow Soldiers](/Hollow+Soldier "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Soldier") with crossbows after the [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man").
  * **6x** at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): on the roof of a building that is reached by heading right from the burning tree with undead worshipping it. Cross the bridge, and at the end of the bridge, there will be a few barrels and boxes to your right. Behind them will be a small path along the side of the hill that leads you above the house where a Flamberge-wielding [Hollow Slave](/Hollow+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Slave") is.

### Notes

  * Deals 100 base Fire damage.
  * Scales with an A in Strength and a C in Dexterity.
  * The damage can be boosted with the [Fire Clutch Ring](/Fire+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fire Clutch Ring").
  * The [Hawk Ring](/Hawk+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hawk Ring") boosts the distance the bomb can be thrown.
  * Can be traded for a [Large Titanite Shard](/Large+Titanite+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Large Titanite Shard") with [Nestling](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
