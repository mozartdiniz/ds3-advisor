**Ringed Knight Set** is an Armor in Dark Souls 3. It was added in [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City) [DLC](/DLC).   
  
  
  
  

> Malformed black armor of the Ringed Knights. The armor of early men was forged in the Abyss, and betrays a smidgen of life.
> 
> For this reason the gods cast a seal of fire upon such armor, and those who possessed them.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Can drop from [Ringed Knights](/Ringed+Knight)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * It's recommended to use a [Covetous Gold Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring) or any of the item discorvery boosting gear when farming for armor peices, as they appear to have a low drop rate.
  * Each piece can be sold to [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Shrine Handmaid") for 600 souls.
