# Red Tearstone Ring

### Red Tearstone Ring  
  
---  
  

> A ring set with a large rare tearstone jewel. Temporarily boosts attack when HP is low.  
>   
> This stone is said to be a tear of mourning of the goddess Caitha, and of course, tears are always more beautiful near death.  
  
**Red Tearstone Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Red Tearstone Ring Effect

  * While on critical HP (below 20%) damage from all sources is increased multiplicatively by 20%

### Where to Find Red Tearstone Ring

  * Found in [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"). Face away from the [Dragonslayer Armour](/Dragonslayer+Armour "Dark Souls 3 Dragonslayer Armour") bossfight entrance. To the left is a doorway lit by torches. Proceed through the door and up the stairs. Head out the door on the left. It will be on a body to the right. () [(video location)](https://youtu.be/1YaiwEi7EN4 "\(video location\)")

### Red Tearstone Ring Notes

  * The effect boosts all damage, including but not limited to Weapons, Spells, Arrows, and Bolts.
  * This ring is the partner ring to the [Blue Tearstone ring](/Blue+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Blue Tearstone Ring").
  * The Red Tearstone range is determined by a percentage of your current maximum health. This means increasing your health pool allows you to have the effect of this ring active with a higher amount of HP remaining.
  * This effect stacks with the effect of the unique weapon: [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade"), resulting in +44% bonus Damage (1.2 x 1.2 = 1.44).
  * This ring does not need to be worn before being dropped to low HP, meaning you can swap to this ring when near death to benefit from its bonus damage.
  * The [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial") miracle can provide a safety net to maintain yourself at low HP and benefit from this ring, and still survive a single instance of damage.
  * This ring previously appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Red+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Red Tearstone Ring")_ and _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Red+Tearstone+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Red Tearstone Ring")_.
