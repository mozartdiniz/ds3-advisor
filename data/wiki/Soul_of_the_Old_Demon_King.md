# Soul of the Old Demon King

**Soul of the Old Demon King** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Old Demon King  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Old Demon King. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.   
>    
> Use to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.   
>    
> The shriveled Old Demon King is now like a clump of burnt ash, but he is the last living witness of the Chaos of Izalith.  
  
### Soul of the Old Demon King Usage

  * Can be consumed for 10,000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges) and [Old King's Great Hammer](/Old+King's+Great+Hammer)

### Soul of the Old Demon King Locations

  * Defeat the [Old Demon King](/Old+Demon+King)

### Notes

  * Player note 1
