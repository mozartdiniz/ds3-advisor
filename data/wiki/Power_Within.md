# Power Within

## Power Within  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies")  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 10 Intelligence/ 10 Faith  
  
**Type** | Body Buff  
**Duration** | 30 Seconds  
  
**Power Within** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

> Forbidden pyromancy amongst those who internalize flame.
> 
> Temporarily boosts attack, but gradually lose HP.
> 
> A pyromancer fears the flame. However, this fear subsides once it is accepted as a substitute for strength.

### How to Get Power Within in Dark Souls 3

  * Found behind a hidden wall in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"). From the first area where you dip your head in wax, head immediately right into the dark room. Hug the left wall until you arrive at the second small staircase. On your left, just behind the second small staircase will be a lever. Pull it and loot the corpse behind the bookshelf that retracts, located on the left side of the room.
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/tkpQBRM_irg "Video Location")

### Dark Souls 3 Power Within Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases all damage types on weapons multiplicatively: 
    * **PVE** = +20% AR
    * **PVP** = +18.8% AR (20 * 0.94 PvP nerf)
  * Improves [Stamina](/Stamina "Dark Souls 3 Stamina") regeneration by +30 per second (base regeneration is 45 per second)
  * Removes ~29% of your maximum HP over the course of the duration.
  * Unaffected by Pyromancy Flame upgrade level or Spell Buff.
  * [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") +2 will increase the duration to 42 seconds, yet total health depleted will stay the same (~29% of max).
  * Does not stack with other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Profuse Sweat](/Profuse+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Profuse Sweat"), [Carthus Beacon](/Carthus+Beacon "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Beacon"), [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Iron Flesh](/Iron+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Iron Flesh") or [Flash Sweat](/Flash+Sweat "Dark Souls 3 Flash Sweat").
  * This spell is useful in Glass Cannon builds as it significantly increases damage and provides extra stamina regeneration for aggressive play.
  * This spell first appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Power+Within "Dark Souls 3 Power Within")_.
