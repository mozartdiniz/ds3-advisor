# Firelink Shrine

**Firelink Shrine** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. After defeating the first boss, the unkindled find this safe haven, where they may level up via the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper"), or interact with other [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") who have sought the safety of the hub. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

## Full Firelink Shrine Walkthrough

Click [here](/Firelink+Shrine#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine#speedrun") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough. Click [here](/Lore#lorefirelink "Dark Souls 3 Lore#lorefirelink") for Lore and Speculation on this area.   
  

### Outsides of Firelink Shrine

After boss-fight with [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr") open the large double doors and proceed out along the cliffs.

You will find a [Broken Straight Sword](/Broken+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Broken Straight Sword") by the graves straight ahead. Along the way, you will find a [Homeward Bone](/Homeward+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Homeward Bone") 1x. Ascend up towards the structure at the top of the hill. To the right, you will encounter an undead dog and beyond will find an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x. Go along the left side to find graves and stairs overlooking a cliff. At the junction between two sets of stairs, there is a corpse in a tree on the left with the [East-West Shield](/East-West+Shield "Dark Souls 3 East-West Shield"). To the stairs on the right, there will be two hollows and another [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember"). Continuing to the left will take you to [Sword Master Saber](/Sword+Master "Dark Souls 3 Sword Master"), an undead wielding the [Uchigatana](/Uchigatana "Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana"); it is recommended that you level up first before trying to fight him. The easier way to engage him is to just lure him at the edge of a cliff and make sure your back is facing the wall with your shield up. Keep attacking him until he falls off the cliff, he will drop master armor and master attire when you reload the area (either by resting at a bonfire or quitting and reloading).

Continue forward and enter the main building of Firelink Shrine, hub of Dark Souls 3.

### Entering Firelink Shrine

Upon entering Firelink Shrine, before you will be 5 large stone thrones, all vacant but one, where [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") sits. The thrones have inscriptions on their backs, identifying the being who occupied them, such as Holy King Lothric, last of his line. Firelink Shrine itself is quite large, similar to the Nexus in Demon's Souls with staircases up and down where you can explore for items and more tutorial messages. Look out for the rare friendly [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") whom you can recruit and add to the shrine's inhabitants.

Merchants, allies, and friends here.

### Lighting the Bonfire

Descend the stairs on the right side towards the center area, speaking to [Hawkwood](/Hawkwood "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood") seated near the bottom to learn the Collapse [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures"), and then speak with the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") at the center. She will make herself available for leveling up and explain the next part of your journey.

Embed the [Coiled Sword](/Coiled+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword") into the fire pit in the center of the room, thereby igniting the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires"). From here you may teleport to other locations. The only one available right now is the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"), so teleport there if you don't want to explore Firelink.

Teleport to new areas with the bonfire

### Meeting the Inhabitants

Explore the area of the shrine to meet and interact with other [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs"). On the ground level, you will find the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") who sells a variety of goods, and [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") who performs [Upgrades](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"). You may purchase the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key") from the Handmaid for 20,000 souls, which will let you access another area here where you can find a [Fire Keeper Soul](/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul"). The Shrine Handmaid also accepts [Ashes](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes"), which will unlock additional items for sale. Move up the stairs behind the thrones and explore to find [Leonhard the Ringfinger](/Leonhard+the+Ringfinger "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard the Ringfinger"), who will give you cracked red eye orbs, an invasion item for multiplayer, as well as progress his questline.

As you progress through the game other NPCs will populate the Shrine, after certain conditions have been met. Look out for additional merchants and quest givers to fill up the shrine.

Check with NPCs from time to time, defeating [Bosses](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses") or discovering areas may trigger extra rewards or new quests.

Black Smith Andre can upgrade weapons with titanite, found from foes or treasures.

### Exploring the Shrine

Heading up the stairs right from the entrance, you can step outside, where you will see a petrified Giant Tree. Examining the tree will give the message "a seed of a Giant Tree is yet to fall". Continuing past the tree and up the stairs, you come to a tower and a corpse where you can loot a [Soul of a Deserted Corpse](/Soul+of+a+Deserted+Corpse "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Deserted Corpse") 1x. The tower gate is locked and can be unlocked with the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key"), which can be purchased for 20000 souls from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid"). At the top of this tower's spiral stairs, you can cross a crumbling stone bridge here. In the next tower, activate the pressure plate on the lift and take it to the top. Ascend the stairs to the top of the tower where you will find a corpse with a [Fire Keeper Soul](/Fire+Keeper+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Soul") 1x. Depending on your progression in the game, [Patches](/Unbreakable+Patches "Dark Souls 3 Unbreakable Patches") may show up here and trap you by closing the grated door leading to the bridge. You can jump down from one side of the bridge to a short lip to find a corpse and loot the [Fire Keeper Set](/Fire+Keeper+Set "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper Set"). From the other side of the bridge, you can drop multiple times to ledges and find the [Estus Ring](/Estus+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Estus Ring") as loot. On this level, you can exit via a grated door to escape his trap. Beware, however, as a katana-wielding master awaits you just beyond. Killing him will earn you an [Uchigatana](/Uchigatana "Dark Souls 3 Uchigatana") and the [Master's Set](/Master's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Master's Set"). The tree ahead of you as you look back towards the entrance to Firelink Shrine contains a corpse holding the [East-West Shield](/East-West+Shield "Dark Souls 3 East-West Shield"). Moving forward there are 2 hollows here and a corpse with an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") 1x.

Halfway across the bridge from the tower, you can drop down to a ledge on the left and from there drop down to the roof. In the center of the roof, you can find a nest where you will meet [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum the Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") with whom you can trade items.

There is a ladder that you can kick down outside on the roof edge, near the tree you can use to get on the roof,

You can also enter back into Firelink from this roof at a higher level and walk across a narrow rafter to loot an [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") 1x. If you proceed down a long rafter you can strike the wall to reveal an [Illusory Wall](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls"). At the end of the rafter, you can drop down to a lower walkway where a chest lies with the [Covetous Silver Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Silver+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Silver Serpent Ring"). 

If you drop down from the roof you can find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard").

When you are ready to proceed, return to the bonfire and travel to the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"). You may return to this hub any time to level up, perform [Upgrades](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"), and more.

### Speed Run Walkthrough

The Firelink shrine is a hub, therefore you do not need to do anything specific. Make sure you get the [Estus Shard](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") at the top beams sections as depicted on the map, and get the [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") for upgrade materials. Everything else is optional.
