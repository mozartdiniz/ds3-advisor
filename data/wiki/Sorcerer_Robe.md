**Sorcerer Robe** is a [Chest Armor](/Chest+Armor) in **Dark Souls 3**. It is part of the [Sorcerer Set](/Sorcerer+Set).  
  
  
  
  

> Attire of a sorcerer from the Vinheim Dragon School. Long, ash-colored robes.  
>   
> Underneath is a deep blue sorcerer's coat; conventional uniform of the academy.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer)
  * Looted from a corpse in the castle ruin where you summon [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Eygon") at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Road of Sacrifices"). Jump into the hole on the ground next to [Eygon's](/Eygon+of+Carim "Eygon's") summon sign. *_note: you must be embered to see summon signs._ Also beware the [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Lycanthrope").
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=20m6s "Video Location") (Fextralife Video starting at 20m 6s)

  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * The hood of the robe will be worn when equipped alongside the following headgear: 
    * [Sorcerer Hood](/Sorcerer+Hood "Sorcerer Hood")
    * [Assassin Hood](/Assassin+Hood "Assassin Hood")
    * [Executioner Helm](/Executioner+Helm "Executioner Helm")
    * [Elite Knight Helm](/Elite+Knight+Helm "Elite Knight Helm")
    * [Old Sage's Blindfold](/Old+Sage's+Blindfold "Old Sage's Blindfold")
