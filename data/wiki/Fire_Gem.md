# Fire Gem

**Fire Gem** is an [Upgrade Material](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") in **Dark Souls 3.**

### Fire Gem  
  
---  
  

> A gem of infused titanite. Found in rare cases inside demons. Fire weapons inflict fire damage, but lose scaling effects.  
  
### Fire Gem Usage

  * Used for Fire weapon [Infusion](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion")
  * Fire weapons have no scaling, reduced base damage, and added fire damage

### Fire Gem Locations

  * Can begin the game with one if you choose it as a [Burial Gift](/Burial+Gifts "Dark Souls 3 Burial Gifts")
  * Drops from the Fire Demon that [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") helps to defeat in [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement")
  * Rare Drop from the [Smoldering Rotten Flesh](/Smoldering+Rotten+Flesh "Dark Souls 3 Smoldering Rotten Flesh") and Ghru Shamans in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake")
  * Rare drop from [Peasant Hollows](/Peasant+Hollow "Dark Souls 3 Peasant Hollow")

### Notes

  * The fire damage portion of weapons can be further increased by wearing the [Fire Clutch Ring](/Fire+Clutch+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Fire Clutch Ring").
  * Fire also has an additional stun, stagger or knockdown effect against most enemies. This is most notable on [Lycanthrope Hunters](/Lycanthrope+Hunter "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope Hunter"), [Pus of Man](/Pus+of+Man "Dark Souls 3 Pus of Man"), [Starved Hounds](/Starved+Hound "Dark Souls 3 Starved Hound") and [Corpse-Grubs](/Corpse-Grub "Dark Souls 3 Corpse-Grub").
  * The Fire Gem, along with [Deep Gem](/Deep+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Deep Gem"), can raise elemental defense for shields.

### Trivia

  * The burial gift description states that it is used for upgrading fire weapons when it is actually used only for the initial infusion._
