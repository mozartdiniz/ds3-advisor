# Magic Barrier

**Magic Barrier** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Miracle you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Magic Barrier  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 30  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 15 Faith  
**Type** | Body Buff  
**Duration** | 45 seconds  
  
> Miracle of clerics who wield weapons.  
>   
> Increases magic damage absorption by covering the body in a white protective coating.  
>   
> There was a short period in history where clerics and sorcerers opposed one another. Thus it became necessary for even simple clerics to have some means of opposing magic.

### How to Get Magic Barrier in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Irina of Carim,](/Irina+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Irina of Carim") after giving her the [Braille Divine Tome of Lothric](/Braille+Divine+Tome+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 Braille Divine Tome of Lothric") for 5,000 souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Magic Barrier Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases magic absorption by 35%, lasts 90 seconds. 
  * Has an upgraded version: [Great Magic Barrier](/Great+Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Barrier").
  * Overwritten by other [Body Buffs](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") such as [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection"), [Tears of Denial](/Tears+of+Denial "Dark Souls 3 Tears of Denial") and [Great Magic Barrier](/Great+Magic+Barrier "Dark Souls 3 Great Magic Barrier").
  * Duration can be increased with the [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring").
