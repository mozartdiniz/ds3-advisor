This is a player-created **Build** for Dark souls 3 by Skripach, based on quality and buffs.

  * Build Name: [Mirrah Weapon Master](/Sword+of+Mirrah "Mirrah Weapon Master")
  * Build Level: 120+
  * Build Focus: PvP, but PvE available
  * Build Main [Stat](/Stats): quality (Strength-Dexterity) + Faith

## Build Strategy

1) Use all your buffs with [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Ashen Estus Flask") help.

2) Try criticals with your [OP ring](/Hornet+Ring "OP ring").

3.1) With Estoc - spam R1 to GIT GUT.

3.2) With Exile Greatsword - say something about oponent`s mother and use R1+3 dat spins ONESHOT COMBO.

3.3) With Gotthard Twinswords - fishing for R1+weapon art ONESHOT, no one can parry your L1, abuse roll attack.
