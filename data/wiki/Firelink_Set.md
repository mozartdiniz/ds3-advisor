**Firelink Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Armor of the Soul of Cinder, a deific manifestation of the Lords of Cinder, who linked the First Flame.  
>   
> It resembles a knight's armor, but bears hideous burns and contortions...
> 
> ..A mishappen crown can be seen upon its rear. (Helm) /  
> ..taking the shape of a deathly ribcage. (Chest)
> 
> It exists as a symbol of the great Lords and the noble act of linking the fire, though it is no more than an empty husk. (Chest, Gauntlets, Leggings)

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 56000 souls after defeating [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder") or after defeating the boss as a phantom in another player's world. 
  * You do not need to enter New Game Plus to be able to buy it, but it will still be available.

### Trivia

  * Much like the Elite Knight Set and the Faraam Set from the previous games, this armor set is considered to be the flagship armor of _Dark Souls 3_ , as it appears in the box art, and is featured as the red knight figurine of the collector's edition and in the Funko Pop line.
