**Sacred Oath** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a miracle, you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

> Miracle of those chosen by the Sunlight covenant.  
>   
> Temporarily boosts attack and damage absorption for self and those in vicinity.  
>   
> This is the tale of the Sun's firstborn, his faithful first knight, and the brave dragonslayer who served them both.

### How to Get Sacred Oath in Dark Souls 3

  * Reward for reaching rank 1 in the covenant [Warriors of Sunlight](/Warriors+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warriors of Sunlight") (you must offer 10 [Sunlight Medals](/Sunlight+Medal "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Medal") to reach rank 1).

### Dark Souls 3 Sacred Oath Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Increases all damage by 10% multiplicatively (Damage * 1.1) and grants 10.0 absorption to all categories (affected by diminishing returns like all armor), these effects are cut in half for PvP.
  * Can be delayed by holding down the button. Good for co-op partners to have time to get in range to get buffed.
  * Unaffected by Spell Buff and Miracle power rings.
  * Duration can be increased with [Lingering Dragoncrest Ring](/Lingering+Dragoncrest+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Lingering Dragoncrest Ring") (60 sec. * 1.3 = 78 sec.)
  * Stacks with most buffs, including [Deep Protection](/Deep+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Deep Protection").
  * Does not stack with [Oath of Sunlight](/Oath+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Oath of Sunlight").
  * Buff range is increased by the [Sacred Chime of Fillianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore").
  * This spell first appeared in _[Dark Souls 2](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Sacred+Oath "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Oath")_.
  * The first two in its description refer to Gwyn's firstborn son (see [Ring of the Sun's First Born](/Ring+of+the+Sun's+First+Born "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun's First Born")) and Dragonslayer Ornstein (see [Leo Ring](/Leo+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Leo Ring")). The brave dragonslayer who served them both has been subject to much speculation.
  * A possible translation from the JP text would be dragonslayers plural, and referring to the warriors of sunlight themselves.
