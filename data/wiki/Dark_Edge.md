# Dark Edge

## Dark Edge  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 26  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 30 Intelligence  
**Type** | Melee Attack  
  
**Dark Edge** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> A forbidden dark sorcery. Strikes with blade formed of humanity's darkness.  
>   
> Passed from mother to daughter, this blade was intended for both protection and as a means of taking one's own life. The young girl never swung it more than once.

### How to Get Dark Edge in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") for 8,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Dark Edge Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (Spell buff x 2.05) damage.
  * This spell deals [dark damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage"), and as such will be slightly more powerful when cast with a dark-friendly spell catalyst with high Intelligence and Faith. 
    * A pure Intelligence build with no Faith investment can still cast this spell with any other staff, and it will only deal 30-50 less damage.
  * The blade is swung vertically as a slam, however, the hitbox is fairly large and is capable of hitting enemies on either side of the blade's impact. 
    * Since this spell consumes FP as soon as the blade appears, the player casting it can be staggered out of the spell animation before the attack can connect, ending it prematurely and still consuming FP without dealing any damage.
  * Unlike the similar [Lifehunt Scythe](/Lifehunt+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Lifehunt Scythe") or [Soul Greatsword](/Soul+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Soul Greatsword"), this spell will continue to track even after casting, though your turn speed slows down. It can be very effective when aimed manually to hit ahead of an opponent.
  * Uses the [Exile Greatsword's](/Exile+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Exile Greatsword") model, surprisingly.
  * This spell is likely an adaptation of the hex in _Dark Souls 2_ known as [Dark Greatsword](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Dark+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Dark Greatsword").
