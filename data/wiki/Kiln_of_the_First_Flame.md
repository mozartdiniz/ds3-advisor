# Kiln of the First Flame

**Kiln of the First Flame** is a [Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations") in **Dark Souls 3**. In order to reach this area, the player must first kill all Lords of Cinder and speak to the Fire Keeper in Firelink Shrine. Interacting with the Firelink bonfire will then warp the player to the first bonfire. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### Area Requirements

Once you have defeated the 4 Lords of Cinder and placed their ashes on their empty thrones, speak to the Fire Keeper, then activate the Bonfire, which will lead to a cutscene. Afterwards, you will be transported to the first [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires") at the Kiln of the First Flame, which looks like a dark copy of [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").

Take the path outside and activate the bonfire to proceed to the [First Flame](/First+Flame "Dark Souls 3 First Flame"). Upon arriving, light the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires") to the right, head up the path and enter the clearing to battle the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"). Defeat him to earn [Soul of the Lords](/Soul+of+the+Lords "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Lords"). Be wary that it is possible to invade at the path, before the fog gate. Activating the [Bonfire](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires") in front warps to[The Dreg Heap](/The+Dreg+Heap "Dark Souls 3 The Dreg Heap")**** instead.

### Endings

After defeating the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder"), you will then have the choice of the following endings:

  * Use the bonfire resulting in the 'To Link the Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement.
  * If you have obtained the [Eyes of a Firekeeper](/Eyes+of+a+Fire+keeper "Dark Souls 3 Eyes of a Fire keeper") from the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Dark Souls 3 Untended Graves") area and have given it to the Fire Keeper you can use a summon sign next to the bonfire to trigger the 'The End of Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement. During this scene you have a short window where you can attack the Firekeeper after the cutscene for what is effectively a 4th ending option, but you will not get the achievement.
  * If an optional quest line is completed involving [Yuria](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") and [Anri](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") to become the Lord of Londor, you can usurp the flame by wrestling the flame from its mantle triggering the 'Usurpation of Fire' ending and the unlocking of said achievement.  
Note: Bonfire will still say "Link the First Flame" even though you're about to usurp the flame.

Once the final cutscene plays the credits will roll. When the credits are complete, the game will prompt you as to whether you would like to stay in your current playthrough or proceed into [New Game Plus](/New+Game+Plus "Dark Souls 3 New Game Plus") (NG+).

### Staying in current Cycle

If you delay proceeding into NG+, you will return to Firelink Shrine where you can tie up any loose ends, explore further or farm any items. [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") is still possible at the throne of [Ludleth of Courland](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland"), and you can also loot the [Skull Ring](/Skull+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Skull Ring") here as well as obtain the [Farron Ring](/Farron+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Farron Ring") from Hawkwood. Reloading and speaking to [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") will earn you [Hawkwood's Swordgrass](/Hawkwood's+Swordgrass "Dark Souls 3 Hawkwood's Swordgrass").
