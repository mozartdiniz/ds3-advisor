## Build Strategy

  * PvE: You have a weapon buff for almost any area in the game. Know your enemies, equip the Pyromancy Flame and re-attune if in an area where [Carthus Flame Arc](https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com/Carthus+Flame+Arc "Carthus Flame Arc") is absolutely necessary. With mundane stats and the right weapon buff, the Dark Sword is capable of insane dps that will make short work of most bosses. Bring [Carthus Beacon](https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com/Carthus%20Beacon "Carthus Beacon") to a boss if you need to hit harder. It stacks with your Pontiff's Right Eye ring for even more impressive burst DPS.
  * PvP: Know your armor and its elemental weakness. If your opponent is wearing lothric armor, buff lightning and etc. If facing an opponent with strong all-around resistance, use [Blessed Weapon](https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com/Blessed%20Weapon "Blessed Weapon") for its raw AR increase. If you aren't sure which one to start with, play defensively and switch your buffs around until you find the one that is most effective against their set up.
  * [Sunlight Straight Sword](https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com/Sunlight%20Straight+Sword "Sunlight Straight Sword") is a great alternative main weapon.
  * If upgrading past 50 faith, use [Yorshka's Chime](https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com/Yorshka's%20Chime "Yorshka's Chime").
