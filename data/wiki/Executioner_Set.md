**Executioner Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Steel armor of Horace the Hushed, who took a liking to its cold, bulky insides.
> 
> The original owner was said to be a corrupt executioner, who was killed and stripped of his armor.
> 
> Horace is one of only two children to escape Aldrich's clutches.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Buy from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") for 18,000 souls after killing [Horace the Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed") as part of [Anri's](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") questline. 

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This armor is most likely based on the character "Bazuso" from the Berserk series
