**Cleric's Candlestick** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in [Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki"). It is a [Straight Sword](/Straight+Swords "Dark Souls 3 Straight Swords") capable of casting [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").  
  
  
  

> Candle stick used as both sword and catalyst. Used for worship by the Deacons of the Deep.   
>   
> The deacons, under the guidance of Archdeacon McDonnell, became both Clergymen and sorcerers.   
>   
> Skill: Guiding Light   
> A candle provides a temporary source of light which reveals additional guidance.

### How to Get Cleric's Candlestick in Dark Souls 3

  * Transposable from [Soul of the Deacons of the Deep](/Soul+of+the+Deacons+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Deacons of the Deep")

### Dark Souls 3 Cleric's Candlestick Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades"). 
    * This is the only boss weapon that upgrades to +10 and is reinforced with regular Titanite.
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Cleric's Candlestick has a motion value of 200 on backstab, 250 on riposte, and 60+225 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a straight sword does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").
  * The [Weapon Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") Guiding Light will ignite the Cleric's Candlestick, similar to a Torch or Follower's Torch, providing light.

  * The Cleric's Candlestick has a range of 33.8792xl on the 1h R1 and 33.496xl on the 2h R1.
  * This weapon functions as both a staff and sword.  

    * The heavy attack is used for casting sorceries.
  * At 60 INT it has a spellbuff of 200.
  * This weapon is reminiscent of the Blue Flame from Dark Souls II.
  * Though the weapon is generally weak, its left-handed moveset is identical to its right-handed moveset, making it a useful sidearm on an elemental build that uses a heavy weapon in the mainhand.

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit
