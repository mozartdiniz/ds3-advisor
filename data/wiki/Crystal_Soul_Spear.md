# Crystal Soul Spear

## Crystal Soul Spear  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 46  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 48 Intelligence  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
**Crystal Soul Spear** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Sorcery you must use a [Staff ](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves")or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Further sharpens Soul Spear via crystallization.  
>   
> Fires a piercing crystal soul spear.  
>   
> According to the Crystal Sages, old Big Hat achieved enlightenment within the Regal Archives, where he came to find the quintessence of sorcery in the facets of a certain crystal.

### How to Get Crystal Soul Spear in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 15,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Crystal Soul Spear Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Kill the Crystal Sage in the [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") for the [Crystal Scroll](/Crystal+Scroll "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Scroll"), and then give this item to [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").
  * Crystal Soul Spear inflicts (Spell Buff x 3.05) magic damage before resistances.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Soul Spear](/Soul+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Soul Spear").
