# Greirat's Ashes

**[Greirat's](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") Ashes** is an [Ash](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes") in Dark Souls 3.

### Greirat's Ashes  
  
---  
  

> Umbral ash of Greirat of the Undead Settlement. With this, the shrine handmaid will prepare new items.  
>   
> Greirat was a thief who fancied himself a martyr for the poor, which is what drove him to climb the wall.  
  
### Greirat's Ashes Usage

[Greirat's](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") item variety will alter when he is allowed to scavenge certain areas, and his ashes will reflect this based on the time he is killed. 

  * Unlocks [Bloodred Moss Clump](/Bloodred+Moss+Clump "Dark Souls 3 Bloodred Moss Clump"), [Throwing Knife](/Throwing+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Throwing Knife"), [Rope Firebomb](/Rope+Firebomb "Dark Souls 3 Rope Firebomb"), [Bandit’s Knife](/Bandit's+Knife "Dark Souls 3 Bandit's Knife"), [Longsword](/Longsword "Dark Souls 3 Longsword"), [Bastard Sword](/Bastard+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Bastard Sword"), [Mace](/Mace "Dark Souls 3 Mace"), [Spear](/Spear "Dark Souls 3 Spear"), [Light Crossbow](/Light+Crossbow "Dark Souls 3 Light Crossbow"), [Buckler](/Buckler "Dark Souls 3 Buckler"), [Elkhorn Round Shield](/Elkhorn+Round+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Elkhorn Round Shield"), [Round Shield](/Round+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Round Shield"), [Kite Shield](/Kite+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Kite Shield"), [Hard Leather Armor Set](/Hard+Leather+Armor+Set "Dark Souls 3 Hard Leather Armor Set"), [Thief Mask](/Thief+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Thief Mask"), [Fire Arrow](/Fire+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Fire Arrow") and [Standard Bolt](/Standard+Bolt "Dark Souls 3 Standard Bolt") from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") if obtained before any of his pillage missions are completed, along with the items unlocked from further completed pillage missions.

### Greirat's Ashes Locations

Obtained following [Greirat's](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") death either from the player's hand or as a result of his questline.

  * If he dies on the Irythill mission (if not saved by Siegward), his ashes will be in the flooded cave by the long-haired centipede monsters in [Irythill of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") just before the kitchen area. ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=1h40m32s "Video"))([Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6laX0xAXQY "Video"))
  * If sent out a third time, ashes can be found on a [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") rooftop; between the first and second Gargoyle encounters, look down for a rooftop with a group of [Corvian](/Corvian "Dark Souls 3 Corvian") worshippers. Drop down and from there, in one direction is a [Hollow Gem](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") and a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), while in the other is a short jump to [Greirat's](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Greirat of the Undead Settlement") corpse and a treasure of 3 [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").()

### Notes

  * If he dies on the Irythill mission, the new items he would have had for sale(uppon success) will be available from the Shrine Handmaid(After giving her his ashes). You will however, be permenantly unable to recieve the items obtained from the Lothric Castle mission.
