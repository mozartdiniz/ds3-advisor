**Thief** is a starting **[Class](/Classes "Dark Souls 3 Classes")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** , ideal for players who want to focus on item discovery and quick, stealthy combat. With the highest starting Luck stat of any class, the Thief excels at finding valuable items, making it perfect for farming and gathering.

This class is also the only one to start with a bow, offering ranged combat capabilities from the outset. Starting at level 5 with a solid Dexterity stat, the Thief is well-suited for bleed or poison-centric builds, allowing players to take advantage of quick attacks and status effects. Its agility and unique starting equipment make it a strong choice for players looking to combine stealth with strategic combat.

> A common thief and pitiful deserter. Wields a dagger intended for backstabs alongside a military-issue bow.

### Dark Souls 3 Thief Notes and Tips

  * Highest [luck](/Luck "Dark Souls 3 Luck"), meaning the most [item discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") for a starting class.
  * The only class that starts with a [bow](/Bows "Dark Souls 3 Bows").
