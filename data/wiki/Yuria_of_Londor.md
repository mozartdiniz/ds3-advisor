**Yuria of Londor** is an **[NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. A very mysterious character, Yuria of Londor, appears after you find Yoel of Londor dead. She is a member of the Darkwraiths and is primarily focused on bringing about the Age of Dark. Yuria is, or once was, a maiden of the primordial serpent, Darkstalker Kaathe. Yuria is one of the three sisters who founded the Sable Church of Londor. Her siblings include Liliane, the youngest sister, and Elfriede, the eldest sister. In the main game, Yuria can be encountered and interacted with, offering unique quests and lore. Elfriede, on the other hand, is found in the Ashes of Ariandel DLC, where she plays a significant role. Liliane appears solely in the cutscene for the "Usurpation of Fire" ending, standing alongside Yuria. Yuria's presence in the game adds depth to the lore of Londor and the overarching narrative of Dark Souls 3, connecting various characters and plotlines.

### Yuria of Londor Dark Souls 3 Information

  * Can be killed.
  * Offers merchant services.
  * Teaches "Dignified Bow" [gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").
  * Gives the [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade"), after giving her [Orbeck's Ashes](/Orbeck's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck's Ashes").
  * If you kill Anri before triggering her arrival at firelink shrine she will not appear.
  * Wears the [Black Set](/Black+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Set") and wields [Darkdrift](/Darkdrift "Dark Souls 3 Darkdrift") and a [Dark Hand](/Dark+Hand "Dark Souls 3 Dark Hand").
  * Replaces [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") when he dies after he levels you up 5 times via the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil"), You must complete that before entering the [catacombs of carthus](/catacombs+of+carthus "Dark Souls 3 catacombs of carthus") or his death will not trigger her arrival. 
  * If player cures the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") before [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") dies, **she will not appear.**
  * After her appearance, If the player cures the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil") at any point, Yuria will abandon [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and her questline will stop for that playthrough. However, the player may use purging stones to reset hollowing to 0 without any negative consequence.
  * Can be summoned for the [Soul of Cinder](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder") fight if you followed her story for the alternative ending. This will also create a second summon sign to summon Pale Shade of Londor for the fight.
  * She will not attack you if you attack her after performing the wedding ritual with [Anri](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora"), and will say a unique line when killed.

### Yuria Dark Souls Location

  * You can find Yuria of Londor at [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") close by [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") after completing his quest line.

### Yuria Dark Souls 3 Drops

If you decide to kill Yuria, be aware of her fast movement speed and high damage output in close range. To make the fight easier, lead her to the main door of Firelink Shrine, as she will be unable to pass through it. This strategy allows you to attack her without risking direct engagement. Ranged attacks do not seem to be very effective against Yuria. Given her agility and the significant damage she deals at close range, using a weapon with reach is recommended. Spears, halberds, or other melee weapons with good range can help you keep a safe distance while still dealing consistent damage.

Angering Yuria will cause the [Londor Pale Shade](/Londor+Pale+Shade "Dark Souls 3 Londor Pale Shade") to invade you in Farron Keep and Irithyll, dropping the Manikin Claws on the first encounter and the Pale Shade Set after both invasions. These invasions will occur as long as you are kindled and have not defeated the bosses of the respective areas. Normally, this action voids the Usurpation of Fire ending. However, if the player hits Yuria three times to aggro her, then seeks Absolution after both invasions, they can experience both the invasions and still achieve the Usurpation of Fire ending. Absolution is performed by visiting the [Statue of Velka](/Velka+the+Goddess+of+Sin "Dark Souls 3 Velka the Goddess of Sin") in the Undead Settlement, located just before the Dilapidated Bridge bonfire. To reach this area, the [Grave Key](/Grave+Key "Dark Souls 3 Grave Key") is required, or a method of surviving the drop into the ravine where Eygon sits. The Grave Key can be purchased from the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 1,500 souls after giving her the Mortician's Ashes.

### Questline - Usurp the Flame Ending

  1. **Start** : Complete the quest line of [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") to get Yuria to appear in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and to begin her quest line.
  2. The player will need to meet with [Anri of Astora](/Anri+of+Astora "Dark Souls 3 Anri of Astora") in the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") and will need to progress Anri's questline. You will need to keep Anri alive and so you must avoid mentioning Horace to Anri at all, or defeat [Horace](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed") in [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake").
  3. After meeting with Anri in the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus") near the wooden bridge before High Lord Wolnir, meet Anri at the Church of Yorksha Bonfire in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"). In this room there is an assassin disguised as a statue tasked with killing Anri. You must exaust all of Anri's dialog and progress to the area's boss fight without killing the assassin.
  4. After defeating [Pontiff Sulyvahn](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Sulyvahn"), talk to Yuria at Firelink Shrine. She will inform you that your spouse waits for you in the Temple of Darkmoon. To access the temple of Darkmoon, the player must break through an illusory wall behind a statue of Lord Gwyn at the bottom of the rotating lift in [Anor Londo](/Anor+londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor londo").
  5. After entering, the assassin will greet the player and offer the option to perform a wedding ceremony at the tomb. If you accept, you must use the sword given to you by the pilgrim assassin to kill your spouse waiting for you at the end of the room. This will marry Anri's Dark Sigils to the player (you may also now acquire Chameleon from the assassin).  
Note: If you kill the assassin camouflaged as a statue in the corner of the Church of Yorshka Bonfire in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, Yuria will depart Firelink Shrine. Saving Anri of Astora from assassination is the equivalent of healing the Dark Sigil.
  6. Afterwards, talk to Yuria in Firelink Shrine. She will congratulate you on your ceremony and tell you that when the time is right, you should take the fire instead of linking it. At this point, you may also return to Darkmoon Tomb to find [Anri's Straight Sword](/Anri's+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Anri's Straight Sword").
  7. **End** : You must now complete the game with all of the [Dark Sigil](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil")s you have obtained. After defeating the final boss, interact with the fire to start the cinematic (you can also return to Yuria's spot before the cutscene, you only need to have killed the final boss).
  8. If you summoned Yuria to help you fight the final boss WITHOUT letting Yuria die, and you have not chosen to continue into a new game, you may find Yuria's [Darkdrift](/Darkdrift "Dark Souls 3 Darkdrift") and [Black Set](/Black+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Set") where she was standing in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"). She will remain in Firelink post-game if she was not summoned for the battle. Should Yuria die during the final battle, she will be found standing in her original spot in Firelink Shrine. 
  9. If you killed the final boss and walk back to Firelink Shrine without linking the fire, you still can find Yuria's Darkdrift and Black Set where she was standing in Firelink Shrine.

### Optional Questline - Assassinate Orbeck of Vinhiem

  1. **Start** : Complete the questline of [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") to get Yuria to appear in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") and to begin this optional quest.
  2. After meeting [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") in the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") the player must send him to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") after obtaining 10 or higher intelligence. This may need to be completed before the "Marry Anri" step of Yuria's main questline for her to offer the player the next step, even if you've already killed Orbeck and taken his ashes. Killing Orbeck prior to him visiting Firelink will also halt this questline. (If you send Orbeck to the Firelink Shrine AFTER the "marry ritual“ the questline doesn‘t fail.)
  3. Yuria tasks the player to kill Orbeck as she fears he may challenge your position as lord of the hollowed.
  4. **End** : After killing Orbeck you must give the ashes to Yuria in order for Orbeck's death to register. She rewards the player with the [Morion Blade](/Morion+Blade "Dark Souls 3 Morion Blade") and praises you.
  5. Note: **You DO NOT have to kill Orbeck at all**. This is done by simply progressing though Orbeck's questline to the very end at which you find his ashes in the Grand Archives after you summon him to help you in the fight against [Lothric Younger Prince](/Lothric,+Younger+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lothric, Younger Prince").

### Yuria Notes & Trivia in Dark Souls 3

  * She is voiced by Pooky Quesnel, who previously voiced Arianna, Woman of Pleasure in Bloodborne. 
  * Her NPC killing sidequest is similar to Mephistopheles in Demon's Souls.
  * Facinatingly, in the original Japanese, Yuria's dying line regarding Kaathe makes direct reference to having failed Kaathe's dying wish, implying that he is no longer alive.
  * If following Yoel/Yuria's questline, Yuria will inform the player that Anri is Hollow and one of her friends is guiding him/her. At this time if Anri is talked to he/she will mention that a [Pilgrim](/Pilgrim+from+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Pilgrim from Londor") told him/her of the path to the Boreal Valley. The pilgrim he/she mentions is likely the friend Yuria mentions, although some speculate that it could be Anri's guardian [Horace The Hushed](/Horace+the+Hushed "Dark Souls 3 Horace the Hushed").
  * She is in part a reference to Yuria, the Witch from Demon's Souls, as both teach the player magics and both are considered "dark" characters (Yuria, the Witch being a witch, and Yuria of Londor being a hollow). Curiously, the witch [Karla](/Karla "Dark Souls 3 Karla") directly resembles the Yuria of Demon's Souls, posessing the same hair, hat, and general attire, the same belief of herself as a vile thing and directly teaching the player dark spells and miracles. Why this dividing up of the name and role exists is unknown.
  * Interestingly enough, despite being a hollow, Yuria does not have the appearance of a hollow under her black dress and mask. One can see images of Yuria without her mask, and she appears as a normal unhollowed female (lacks hair due to developers not needing to add hair as she never removes the mask in game). It is possible that these are simply the developers choosing not to add this detail due to her never removing the mask. However, other hollow characters such as Horace and Anri are hollow under their clothing. Therefore it is fully possible this is intentional, as wearing the black dress unhollowed still shows its "hollow" appearance, meaning the dress doesn't necessarily look that way because of Yuria being hollow underneath, rather the dress is tailored to look like that. She could be using an Untrue Dark Ring or Purging Stones, both of which she sells. 
  * Once the player becomes the Lord of Hollows, they will be able to attack Yuria without her fighting back.

  * Earlier designs suggest that the Pilgrims were once named: "Callers of the Eclipse", and were akin to [Man Serpents](/Serpent-man "Dark Souls 3 Serpent-man") [](/file/Dark-Souls-3/man_serpent_pilgrim_concept_art.jpg?v=1573847601520).
