**Maiden Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  
  
  

> White robe worn by traveling maidens. Part of their formal attire, regardless of rank.
> 
> It is soft and well-made, but ill-suited for use in battle.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Found in [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"), on a pile of corpses near the feet of the first of the two [Giant Slaves](/Giant+Slave "Dark Souls 3 Giant Slave"). Look for the larger and longer of the two sludge pools in the inner Cathedral.
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/YYbxMQtIwAM?t=2h25m24s "Video Location") (Fextralife Video Starting at 2h 25m 24s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * This set was originally worn by Maiden Astraea from Demons Souls, a saint-like demon found at the bottom of the Valley of Defilement. The armor set's location in Dark Souls 3 might be a callback to this; the series often juxtaposes elements of purity with elements of squalor.
