# Ring of Sacrifice

### Ring of Sacrifice  
  
---  
  

> This mystical ring was created in a sacrificial rite of Velka, the Goddess of Sin. Its wearer will lose nothing upon death, but the ring itself breaks. A sacrifice is only worth as much as the life it spares.  
  
**Ring of Sacrifice** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Ring of Sacrifice Effect

  * Prevents soul loss upon death.
  * Prevents becoming [Hollow](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") upon death, even at 15 hollowing or more. 
    * Does not prevent [Hollowing](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") buildup upon death.
  * Does not stop you from losing your [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") upon death.
  * Will not take any effect from death by [Curse](/Curse "Dark Souls 3 Curse").
  * When dying with ring equipped, the ring will disappear from the player's inventory. 
    * Dying as a phantom will break this ring. Unequip upon summon.

### Where to Find Ring of Sacrifice

  * Sold by [Yuria of Londor](/Yuria+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yuria of Londor") for 5,000 souls (maximum of 3)
  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 5,000 souls (maximum of 3 - after giving [Hollow's Ashes](/Hollow's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Hollow's Ashes") or [Yuria's Ashes](/Yuria's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Yuria's Ashes")).
  * Can also be received by trading [Loretta's Bone](/Loretta's+Bone "Dark Souls 3 Loretta's Bone") with the [Crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") in Firelink Shrine.
  * Looted from some remains at [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"): they are on a wooden tarp just above the entrance to the open area with a lot of Red Knight bodies. This is also where you fight the Winged Knight. (Watch the [Fextra Video](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=1h4m18s "Fextra Video"))
  * Found at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices") on a ledge in the castle ruin.
  * One found in the [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley") river ([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=1h11m27s "Video"))
  * Found in [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Dark Souls 3 Consumed King's Garden") in corner by elevator

### Ring of Sacrifice Notes

  * Not only does the ring save your souls but it will also save your bloodstains. For example if you died without ring on you would drop all souls at spot A. Then you equip ring of sacrifice but then die at spot B before making it to spot A. Your bloodstain would still be at spot A (with all you previouse souls) rather than being at spot B.
  * This ring previously appeared in _[Dark Souls](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Ring+of+Sacrifice "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Sacrifice")_.
  * Unlike the [Ring of Life Protection](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/Ring+of+Life+Protection "Dark Souls 3 Ring of Life Protection") in _Dark Souls 2_ , this ring completely breaks upon death.
  * Also unlike the previous versions of Dark Souls, this ring WILL break when you are being summoned and die in another players world. It is unknown at this time if this is an oversight on From Software's part, or if it has some deeper meaning which has yet to be found. Till that time it is advised to remove it before being summoned, as you will needlessly lose it if you perish in the hosts world.
