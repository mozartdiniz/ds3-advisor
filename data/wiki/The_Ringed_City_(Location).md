# The Ringed City (Location)

**The Ringed City** is a **[Location](/Locations "Dark Souls 3 Locations")** in **Dark Souls 3**. It is the name of the main location of the second [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"), [The Ringed City](/The+Ringed+City "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City"). A traditional city for the Pygmies, called the Ringed City. Half covered in foliage, the city looks desolated with ancient ruins. A portion of the bridge is curved, creating a wall on the inner side. Countless tombstones line the top of the wall made of rock. See the Dark Souls 3 [Walkthrough](/Walkthrough "Dark Souls 3 Walkthrough") or [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") for a path to the game's [Endings](/Endings "Dark Souls 3 Endings").

### Full The Ringed City Walkthrough

A full walkthrough including all the items, enemies and bosses goes here. You can add links, images and other details. Click [here](/The+Ringed+City+\(Location\)#speedrun "Dark Souls 3 The Ringed City \(Location\)#speedrun") to go to the Speedrun Walkthrough.

When the gargoyles have dropped you off, there is a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") on a corpse behind you. Grab that and then head down to the left, but take a moment to appreciate the final inspiring vista of the series. Make your way and speak to the NPC along the path and then proceed to light the first Bonfire ahead. Head down the steps and shades will be summoned to fire at you. If you turn around and head to the right corner behind you will come to an alcove where you can find the [Ruin Set](/Ruin+Set "Dark Souls 3 Ruin Set"). Make your way back and use the grave stones along the right as a station to station cover from the attacks.

If you continue straight and stay to the right, you will come to a drop to a lower ledge and ahead you can drop down again. Make an about face and find a corpse at the end with [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") x1. On the opposite end you will find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") that you can chase around a ledge. Around the ledge at the end you'll find a ladder. Take it up to the platform and then take the steps up to find the summoner. His summons will appear here and fire arrows at you. Avoid them and kill him to put an end to the summons and earn a [Divine Blessing](/Divine+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Divine Blessing") and [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"). From here you can go back and check out the path where the summons were to find a corpse with [Large Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Large+Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Weary Warrior"), one with [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") x1, one with [Budding Green Blossom](/Budding+Green+Blossom "Dark Souls 3 Budding Green Blossom") x2 and another with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x2. You can enter a doorway leading beneath where the summoner was and head left to find a corpse with [Hidden Blessing](/Hidden+Blessing "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Blessing") x1.

Back up on the platform where the summoner was, take the stairs on the end down and around and you will come to an NPC on the left. Continue down the path and take out the hollows ahead and beware of the one who drops down. Continue forward towards the corpse but mind the hollow who drops from above and the other who climbs up on the ledge by the body. Take them out and loot the corpse for [Purging Stone](/Purging+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Purging Stone") x2. Make an about face and enter the doorway on the right into a room with a Bonfire. Light it and head up and to the right through the doorway ahead and turn right to find a corpse with [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") x1.

Go back through the doorway and take the next doorway on the right. Take out the hollow and head left down the hallway to a balcony with 2 hollows. Defeat them and head down the steps to find a corpse hanging over a railing with [Hollow Gem](/Hollow+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Gem") x1. Proceed into the flower field where you will encounter your first [Ringed Knight](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight"). Defeat it and to your right you can walk along a narrow ledge to find a hollow and a corpse with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1. You can drop down or circle around to the staircase below.

Take the steps down to encounter a [Hollow Cleric](/Hollow+Cleric "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Cleric"). Defeat it and enter the doorway and head left to find another Cleric and more if you circle around. In the center of this room you can drop/roll to a central gate and then hop to the ledge. Take out the Cleric here and follow the path to the end to find a corpse with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") x1. From here you can drop down into the center where you will be attacked by a formidable [Ringed Knight](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight") with a [Ringed Knight Spear](/Ringed+Knight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight Spear"). Defeat it and in the center of the room you will find a corpse along a pillar with [Shriving Stone](/Shriving+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Shriving Stone") x1. Exit the room through the doorway short starirs going up and cross over the large stairs to the far end to come to a path with some hollows and a corpse at the dead end with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1. Turn around and head straight, beware of all the Harald legion that patrol the stairs. Take out the hollows and head left across the narrow, moss covered bridge to find a corpse by a tree with [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit") x2.

Head through the entrance to the left to come to a field with lavender like flowers and a Ringed Knight. Defeat it and head left up to the next area and you will be invaded by the dark spirit Seeker of the Spurned who will emerge from an alley in the corner. Defeat the invader to earn [Wolf Ring](/Wolf+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Wolf Ring") +3 and continue down the alley to find a corpse with the [Black Witch Set](/Black+Witch+Set "Dark Souls 3 Black Witch Set") (with the hat not the veil).

Go back the way you came and drop down to the large staircase and take out the Harald legion one at a time. Landing jump attacks are particularly useful against them. 

Head up the steps and you will come to a tower and a door. Approach and speak to the person on the other side who will ask for the name of God. It is [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira"). She will ask you to defeat Midir for her. Accept her request and she will give you the [Sacred Chime of Filianore](/Sacred+Chime+of+Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chime of Filianore") somehow magically through the door. 

Head down the stairs and take out the hollows to the left and further on you will find 2 more, one which tosses lightning spears. Loot the corpse in the nook for [Mossfruit](/Mossfruit "Dark Souls 3 Mossfruit") x2. Across the opposite way you can go through a passageway where you will take out more hollows and follow this around. Right before it exits another hollow will drop from the wall on the left. Head outside into the flowered courtyard to take on a Ringed Knight with a spear. Defeat it and loot the corpse in the corner for [Ringed Knight Spear](/Ringed+Knight+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight Spear"). 

Continue forward and take out the hollows here and cross the narrow moss covered bridge to the other side to hear some ominous words. Drop down to the large stairs and take out the Harald legion patrols here if any are left, again dividing them and going one at a time. At the bottom of the steps you will find a corpse with [Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Large+Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") x1. Ahead will be a swamp, because this is Dark Souls.

### The Swamp

Step into the swamp and head left to come to a downed structure with a corpse nearby that you can loot for [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1. Head left to find some Clerics and at the end a corpse with [Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Crestfallen Knight") x1.

  
There is a ladder that can be found turning left once you enter the swamp from the stairs. At the top of the ladder immediately left is the NPC Locust Preacher that was previously above you. Proceed up the short stairs & first go left to encounter a Crystal Lizard (drops [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite")) that will run off the balcony to the large stairs below; across the bridge, you will encounter two ringed knights and then enter a room in which Lapp can be found asking about the location of the [Purging Monument](/Purging+Monument "Dark Souls 3 Purging Monument"), (once found, return here to inform him to progress his questline) alongside with the Covetous Gold Serpent Ring +3. Return to the top of the stairs above the ladder & now go right, drop down to the stone path & proceed up to the tower ahead. You will be invaded by [Silver Knight Ledo](/Silver+Knight+Ledo "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight Ledo") (Who drops [Ledo's Great Hammer](/Ledo's+Great+Hammer "Dark Souls 3 Ledo's Great Hammer") once defeated). Once inside the tower, proceed out the left door to a balcony with a body holding a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), then proceed out the door you first used to enter the tower & drop down a series of ledges to obtain [Havel's Ring](/Havel's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Havel's Ring") +3 & safely return to the swamp.

After returning to the base of the ladder, turn around and head in the opposite direction and head into the corner to find the [White Preacher Head](/White+Preacher+Head "Dark Souls 3 White Preacher Head"). Head back to where you walked in and walk straight ahead to see a group of locusts who will scatter. Straight ahead you will find a corpse with [Dark Gem](/Dark+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Dark Gem"). Head right to encounter a large locust with spikes. Defeat it and head up the stairs. At the top you will find a corpse hanging over the railing with [Prism Stone](/Prism+Stone "Dark Souls 3 Prism Stone") x4. Head into the doorway to find a Bonfire. There is a gate in this room that does not open from this side. Exit the doorway back into the swamp and head straight across to find a larger locust enemy over a corpse with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite"). Stay along the left wall of the swamp and take the hill up and find a hollow to the right and a corpse with the [White Birch Bow](/White+Birch+Bow "Dark Souls 3 White Birch Bow"). Across will be a corpse with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1.

Head back down the hill and to the left will be a tree with corpses you can loot for [Young White Branch](/Young+White+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Young White Branch") x3. Head back into the swamp and go forward and hang left into the structure to find the [Church Guardian Shiv](/Church+Guardian+Shiv "Dark Souls 3 Church Guardian Shiv") on a corpse. Exit the structure and head left along the shore to take on the [Iron Dragonslayer](/Iron+Dragonslayer "Dark Souls 3 Iron Dragonslayer"). Slay him to earn the [Iron Dragonslayer Set](/Iron+Dragonslayer+Set "Dark Souls 3 Iron Dragonslayer Set"). 

Keep following the left shore around. You'll come to 2 corpses in the swamp with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") x2. Keep circling the shore back around and you'll find a corpse along a rock with [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1. Ahead roaming the swamp will be a judicator summoning more warriors. Defeat the judicator to put an end to that nonsense. You'll also encounter some large locusts. 

On an island in the swamp loot a corpse for [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk") x1 and nearby in the abyssal liquid a corpse with [Large Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Large+Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Weary Warrior") x1. Circle around this island to the right and head up the hill to come to a structure and a corpse laying against it with the [Ringed Knight Straight Sword](/Ringed+Knight+Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight Straight Sword"). You'll run into the particularly creepy NPC again.

Head back down into the swamp to a small crumbled ruin ahead and find the [Preacher's Right Arm](/Preacher's+Right+Arm "Dark Souls 3 Preacher's Right Arm") on a corpse. Mind the locusts that emerge. Continue to the building that is partially submerged, stopping to loot the [Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Weary Warrior"). Step onto the submerged roof and prepare yourself for 3 Ringed Knights, good lord. Divide and conquer, and use as much space as you need. Step onto the roof then and head to the other side to drop down to the ground and head right to find another Ringed Knight and a corpse with [Black Witch Veil](/Black+Witch+Veil "Dark Souls 3 Black Witch Veil"). 

Head onto the structure and go up and find a [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") on a corpse. From here if you look to the left across the distance you can get a peak at an imposing foe. Drop down ahead and head to the right of the huge arch to find a narrow path into the building ahead. Go through the doorway and into a room and unlock the gate to open a shortcut to the bonfire. In this room an engraving on the wall reads, "[Show Your Humanity](/Show+Your+Humanity "Dark Souls 3 Show Your Humanity")". If you're stumped visit that page to learn how to unlock this secret!

When following the secret path, drop out of the building to the field below to face a pair of patrolling Ringed Knights, as well as another summoning Judicator sitting on the platform beyond where you first drop down. After dispatching these enemies, descend the ladder further along, & turn right to take on a lightning spear throwing hollow, and loot the adjacent body for the [Dragonhead Shield](/Dragonhead+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Dragonhead Shield"). Return to the ladder, & continue across the bridge to loot a [Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight](/Large+Soul+of+a+Crestfallen+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Crestfallen Knight"), & to the right, the [Purging Monument](/Purging+Monument "Dark Souls 3 Purging Monument") that Lapp has been searching for. If you're feeling athletic, you may attempt the jump past the Monument to reach the bridge where you faced two Ringed Knights and a hollow leading to Lapp's room with the [Golden Covetous Serpent Ring](/Covetous+Gold+Serpent+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Covetous Gold Serpent Ring") +3 (if you did not already loot it earlier). Alternatively, you can walk back around past the ladder to where you faced the hollow to safely drop down into the field directly outside of Lapp's room no jumping required. If followng Lapp's questline, you can now inform him of the Monument's location to continue progressing. Having found all of Humanity's secrets, you can now make your way back to the Bonfire room to continue on the main path.

Head back outside and follow the narrow path down to the right to come to a corpse with a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") x1. Further down the path is a Ringed Knight. Defeat it and continue to find the [Dragonhead Greatshield](/Dragonhead+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Dragonhead Greatshield") on a corpse. Go back up and get ready to head right across the stone bridge. But before you go, be warned that in true Dark Souls fashion the dragon will blaze you when you try. Don't run in recklessly, instead take a few steps on to bait it and then quickly dash back and make your way across after it's flown past. Mind the fire which will hurt.

### Dragon Bridge 

Make your way quickly across and run into the tunnel on the right to escape the flames and take out the enemy in here. You can loot the corpse at the entrance for [Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Soul of a Weary Warrior"). Head back into the tunnel to find a corpse to the right on the edge with [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") x2.

You'll be using this tunnel as your cover while you time the dragon. Let it come towards you and then head out the tunnel and go right when the flames subside. Skip the first corpse as it has [Rubbish](/Rubbish "Dark Souls 3 Rubbish") and keep heading straight and to the left to drop down to a lower ledge to loot [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale") x2. Keep going and take out the hollow and follow the path around and up, and duck into the entranceway ahead to avoid the flames as the dragon passes through. Run out and head left to find a corpse with [Large Soul of a Weary Warrior](/Large+Soul+of+a+Weary+Warrior "Dark Souls 3 Large Soul of a Weary Warrior"). 

### The Tower

Head back in and ahead up the stairs to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"), but the stairs will crumble before you can get to it.

If you tried to go up the crumbling steps and fall you'll land on lower stairs and will be invaded by dark spirit [Moaning Knight](/Moaning+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Moaning Knight"). There will be some vintage animated skeleton action here. Head up the stairs and find another 2 through the entrance to the left. Loot the corpse for [Lightning Gem](/Lightning+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Gem") x1. Step through the room and head up the stairs to be attacked by 2 skeletons and Moaning Knight. Moaning Knight is a beast of a foe and does some heavy melee damage. Defeat him to earn the [Blindfold Mask](/Blindfold+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Blindfold Mask"). Up the stairs past him is a corpse with [Simple Gem](/Simple+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Simple Gem").

Back by the entrance, if you have told Lapp the location of the Purging Monument, he can be found sitting in a room to the right of where you came in (past the little hump of ruble), & will treat you to a scene reminiscent of the Tomb in DS1.

You can avoid the crumbling stairs and just drop from level to level. Eventually you'll come to a corpse with [Blessed Gem.](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") From here you can take the stairs up and come to a room with a [Mimic](/Mimic "Dark Souls 3 Mimic"). Kill it for [Ring of the Evil Eye](/Ring+of+the+Evil+Eye "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Evil Eye") +3. Go up the next level and enter the room and head through and go left up the stairs to take out some skeletons. Keep going up the next staircase and you'll come through an opening where the Crystal Lizard is. Kill it quickly if you can and go left up the stairs and you'll come to a Bonfire.

Assuming you completed Lapp's cutscene, you can drop down though the broken stairs below to retrieve Lapp's Set from the room to the right of the initial entrance. Return to the Bonfire to continue on.

### Dragon Up Close & Personal

Head through the doorway to get outside and it's time for more dragon fire fun. He'll perform a sweeping fire pattern similar to past dragons in the series. You can avoid it in a nook to the left behind a rock. You can time it and get right underneath it to engage it and attack. If you do, you will have to watch for its up close fire and breath attacks as you attack its claws and body, making sure to back away when it breathes its large AoE fire straight down. Eventually it will go berserk and stop slamming the ground, swiping its arms and spreading flames everywhere. When you've done enough damage it will drop and leave itself open for attacks. Take the offensive and knock it out to send it tumbling down to the depths. Cross the bridge up and loot the corpse for a [Titanite Scale](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale").

### Gwyn Statue Room & Shortcut

Enter the doorway ahead and go left up the steps to find a [Crystal Lizard](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard"). To the left of the base of the stairs is a series of 3 illusory walls, the first hiding a Cleric, the second a soul item, & the third the Miracle [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Arrow"). Proceed up the stairs to enter a room with a statue in the center of Gwyn and the Pygmy. You can't loot the item in Gwyn's hand just yet. In a doorway you will come to a room with a pressure plate you can activate to raise an elevator up to a room where you can open the gate to unlock a shortcut to the earlier Bonfire. 

Take the elevator down and look for a side doorway you can walk into on the way down. Head into the room and loot an [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") on the corpse. From here you may access the optional boss of this area, follow the steps detailed at the end of this walkthrough under the heading Optional Boss. Step through the doorway to the stairs and down the next flight and hop down the hole in the floor to land on the platform of the Gwyn statue. You can now loot the [Chloranthy Ring](/Chloranthy+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Chloranthy Ring") +3 from Gwyn's hand. 

### Spears of the Church

Step outside and go forward to find a corpse to the left with [Budding Green Blossom](/Budding+Green+Blossom "Dark Souls 3 Budding Green Blossom") x5. A Ringed Knight wielding [Ringed Knight Paired Greatswords](/Ringed+Knight+Paired+Greatswords "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight Paired Greatswords") will step forward to attack. Defeat it to earn them.

Ahead to the right you will find an altar with the prompt "Pray to the Divine Spear Monument". Do so to discover where you will rank up in the [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants"). Around the backside of the altar will be a [Ritual Spear Fragment](/Ritual+Spear+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Ritual Spear Fragment"). Head up the stairs and drop down to the left to find a corpse with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite") x2.

Head up the steps and approach the door to have a chat with [Judicator Argo](/Judicator+Argo "Dark Souls 3 Judicator Argo"). Open the doors, and step through the fog to face the boss.

### Boss: [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church")

Defeat the boss to earn a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") and [Filianore's Spear Ornament](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament"). Head through the doorway on the left and take the stairs up to find a Bonfire in the room. Head opposite it through the doorway to activate a pressure plate elevator up. Go through the doorway at the top and head up the long flight of stairs and open the double doors to meet [Filianore](/Filianore "Dark Souls 3 Filianore"). If you approach you will get a prompt to touch the embraced shell and a cutscene will play. A Bonfire will now be in front of her. If you step out you will see things have changed dramatically.

Make your way across the barren field to see an NPC dragging itself across the ash. Follow the trail it has left behind. If you veer off to the left you will find a solitary wandering Ringed Knight. Defeat it to earn a [Titanite Chunk](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), [Ember](/Ember "Dark Souls 3 Ember") and [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab"). From the main path leading from the Bonfire, room, if you veer right you can enter another large ruined building to trigger a hostile [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira") to spawn and seek vengeance for awakening Filianore. This can be done either before or after the main Boss fight is finished. Defeating her will yield the [Crucifix of the Mad King](/Crucifix+of+the+Mad+King "Dark Souls 3 Crucifix of the Mad King"), as well as a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") if it was not already earned by completing her questline & slaying [Darkeater Midir](/Darkeater+Midir "Dark Souls 3 Darkeater Midir"); additionally, after defeating her, you can return to the now accessible room at the top of the giant stairs where she previously resided to loot [Shira's Set](/Shira's+Set "Dark Souls 3 Shira's Set"). Resume the course following the main path through the archway and a cutscene will trigger. 

### Boss: [Slave Knight Gael (Boss)](/Slave+Knight+Gael+\(Boss\) "Dark Souls 3 Slave Knight Gael \(Boss\)")

Defeat him to earn [Soul of Slave Knight Gael](/Soul+of+Slave+Knight+Gael "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Slave Knight Gael") and [Blood of the Dark Soul](/Blood+of+the+Dark+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Blood of the Dark Soul"). Light the Bonfire that appears. Make your way across the expanse to the ruins on the hill in the distance. Head through the doorway and enter an arena and you will face off with [Shira](/Shira "Dark Souls 3 Shira"). She is a tough battle and wields the [Crucifix of the Mad King](/Crucifix+of+the+Mad+King "Dark Souls 3 Crucifix of the Mad King") and [Lightning Arrow](/Lightning+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Lightning Arrow") both of which she wields effectively. She will heal a couple of times. Defeat her to obtain her weapon and a [Titanite Slab](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab").

### Wrapping Up

Make your way back to The Dreg Heap's first bonfire and go to where the Stone-humped Hag was to now find [Old Woman's Ashes](/Old+Woman's+Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Old Woman's Ashes") which you can give to the Shrine Handmaid.

To obtain the final dialogue and bring the story to a close, make your way to the Sister Friede Bonfire in the [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") and visit the [Painting Girl](/Painting+Woman "Dark Souls 3 Painting Woman"). Give her the [Blood of the Dark Soul](/Blood+of+the+Dark+Soul "Dark Souls 3 Blood of the Dark Soul") to present her the pigment she sought. She asks your name and offers to paint a new world in your name, or will call it Ash if you refuse your name.

### Optional Boss

To challenge the area's optional boss make your way to the Ringed Inner Wall bonfire and take the elevator shortcut down and step through the hidden side door before you get to the bottom (the room with the Knight statues where you looted the Ember earlier). Attack the statue to the right to reveal the illusion. At the end of the hallway step around the hole in the floor to loot a [Filianore's Spear Ornament](/Filianore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filianore's Spear Ornament") from the corpse. Make your way to the hole and take the long ladder down. Loot the corpse in this room for [Antiquated Plain Garb](/Antiquated+Plain+Garb "Dark Souls 3 Antiquated Plain Garb") and [Violet Wrappings](/Violet+Wrappings "Dark Souls 3 Violet Wrappings"). 

Ahead of the altar, you will see a broken wall with a hole leading down. Take the plunge down to square off against your foe.

### Boss Fight: [Darkeater Midir](/Darkeater+Midir "Dark Souls 3 Darkeater Midir")

Defeat him to earn the [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") covenant item which you can equip to allow you to be summoned to assist [Halflight, Spear of the Church](/Halflight,+Spear+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Halflight, Spear of the Church"), and [Soul of Darkeater Midir](/Soul+of+Darkeater+Midir "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Darkeater Midir"). Light the Bonfire that appears.
