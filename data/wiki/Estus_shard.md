# Estus Shard

**Estus Shard** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Estus Shards are used to upgrade your Estus Flasks or Ashen Estus Flasks, making them essential for your travels around Lothric. Some of these shards are tricky to find, hidden in obscure locations or well-guarded by enemy hordes. Acquiring them is crucial for increasing the number of flasks you can carry, thereby improving your chances of survival in the challenging world of Dark Souls 3.

### Estus Flask Shards  
  
---  
  

> A shard soaked in Estus. Give to the blacksmith at the shrine to increase usages of the Estus Flask. In the old days, it was rare to see an Estus Flask far from its owner, but this shard offers hope, however shattered."  
  
### Estus Shard Dark Souls 3 Usage

  * Increases uses of the [Estus Flask](/Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Estus Flask")/[Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask"), up to a maximum of 15 (combined between the two Estus Flasks), once given to the [Blacksmith](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre") at the [Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine").
  * The blacksmith will not upgrade your flasks until you acquire the [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask") from the [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetary+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetary of Ash").
  * [Undead Bone Shard](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard")s will further upgrade how much HP/FP is replenished with each drink from the flask.

### Estus Shard Dark Souls 3 Locations

  * [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"): On an anvil in the room where you get the [Cell Key](/Cell+Key "Dark Souls 3 Cell Key").
  * [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"): By the burning tree with worshipping undead. This is to the left of the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") bonfire, past the house. It is surrounded by a bunch of undead workers and an [Evangelist.](/file/Dark-Souls-3/evangelist-enemy-dark-souls-3-wiki-guide.jpg?v=1771667995759 "evangelist.")
  * [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"): From the Crucifixion Woods bonfire head towards the [Crystal Sage](/Crystal+Sage "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Sage"). Pass under the small bridge. The shard will be on the left side of the ruins near a fire that has two [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope").
  * [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"): Go straight out from the starting bonfire. The shard is near a fallen tower guarded by slugs.
  * [Cathedral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"): Right outside the Cleansing Chapel bonfire where enemies are praying.
  * [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"): On a body near a hole from the Old King's Antechamber bonfire. Look for an illusory wall in the bonfire room. From there head left inside the hidden hallway past the fire slimes dropping from the ceiling. 
    * Alternatively there is a separate path that leads you to the opposite side of the hole and can be reached if you go down the hall to the right of the one containing a black knight behind an illusionary wall 
  * [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"): From the bonfire, walk forward into the dungeon and turn right. Now go straight, head through the locked gate that requires the [Jailbreaker's Key](/Jailbreaker's+Key "Dark Souls 3 Jailbreaker's Key"), then drop down. Go right into the room and there should be a chest which is a mimic that contains the shard. 
    * Alternatively, as you play through the level you will eventually reach an open area with 3 enemies, one who will shoot a crossbow at you from beside an archway, one with an axe, and one who will ambush you from behind. Kill these enemies, proceed through the archway and there will be a mimic on your right. This mimic will drop the Estus Shard when killed.
  * [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"): In a chest on the first floor of the large dark room with the [Deacons](/Deacon "Dark Souls 3 Deacon") (before the Aldrich fog wall). Enter from the double doors and hug the left wall to find it.
  * [High Wall of Lothric/Consumed King's Garden](/High+Wall+of+Lothric "Dark Souls 3 High Wall of Lothric"): From [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Dancer of the Boreal Valley") bonfire, climb up the ladder and go first left. Proceed down the stairs and kill the knight standing in front of the elevator. Use the elevator and jump out halfway, onto the ledge. The Estus Shard is down the stairs to the right.
  * [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle"): From [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives") bonfire, on the top of Lothric Castle near the [Winged Knights](/Winged+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Winged Knight"). From the ladder that takes you to the [Hunter's Ring](/Hunter's+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hunter's Ring"), you will see an archway that leads to a lower roof/balcony. Estus Shard will be found all the way out near the railing.
  * [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"): Purchase the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key") and get to the bridge that overlooks the Shrine. Drop down and find a window that allows you to get inside and get the shard on the rafters. 
    * Alternatively, go upstairs and outside and find the tree near the raised area with loot on it, then run up the tree and jump onto the raised area - no key needed!

### Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard Notes & Tips

The [Ashen Estus Flask](/Ashen+Estus+Flask "Dark Souls 3 Ashen Estus Flask"), found in the [Cemetery of Ash](/Cemetary+of+Ash "Dark Souls 3 Cemetary of Ash"), will also increase the estus count.

  * All shard upgrades plus the ashen estus makes it possible to get the full amount, 15 flasks, in one playthrough.
  * Upon reinforcing with all Estus Shards you will get the Achievement "Ultimate Estus"

### Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard Trivia

  * Description makes a small reference to the description of the Dark Souls 2 Estus Shard
