# A Pure Pyromancy Build

  * Build Level: 100
  * Build Focus: PvP
  * Build Main Stats: Intelligence and Faith

### Build Equipment

  * **Right Hand** : [Pyromancy Flame +10](/pyromancy+flame "Pyromancy Flame +10") and a [Chaos Thrall Axe +10](/Thrall+Axe "Chaos Thrall Axe +10")
  * **Left Hand** : [Pyromancy Flame +10](/pyromancy+flame "Pyromancy Flame +10") and a shield like the [Target Shield](/Target+Shield "Target Shield") for parrying
  * **Armor: Fashion (but you should choose the[Xanthous set](/Xanthous+Set "Xanthous set") for the flavor)**
  * **Rings** : [Fire Clutch Ring](/Fire+Clutch+Ring "Fire Clutch Ring"), [Great Swamp Ring](/Great+Swamp+Ring "Great Swamp Ring"), [Witch's Ring](/Witch's+ring "Witch's Ring"), Any other good ring for the situation (Like for [more](/Deep+Ring "more") [attunment](/Darkmoon+Ring "attunment") [slots](/Saint%27s+Ring "slots") or [less FP for spell cost](/Dusk+Crown+Ring "less FP for spell cost"))
  * Attuned Spells (8 slots) 
    * [Great Chaos Fire Orb](/Great+Chaos+Fire+Orb "Great Chaos Fireorb") (mainly to freeaim)
    * [Profaned Flame](/Profaned+Flame "Profaned Flame") (or a [Firestorm](/Firestorm "Firestorm"))
    * [Sacred Flame](/Sacred+Flame "Sacred Flame")
    * [Fire Surge](/Fire+Surge "Fire Surge")
    * [Great Combustion](/Great+Combustion "Great Combustion")
    * [Great Chaos Bed Vestiges](/Chaos+Bed+Vestiges "Great Chaos Bed Vestiges")

## Strategy

Make sure to dodge a lot and try to judge your opponents style and play around that. Keep at a distance and always be on the offensive by casting Chaos Orbs, Vestges, and Great Combustions. Use a Profaned Flame or Firestorm to knock down targets and set up for more combos. Fire Surge is there to just ping an opponent whose just circling around. Good Luck
