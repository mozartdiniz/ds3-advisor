# Locations

**Locations** in **Dark Souls 3** 's world are areas with their name prominently displayed upon entry. You may also be interested in the [Side Quests](/Side+Quests "Dark Souls 3 Side Quests") and [Game Progress Route](/Game+Progress+Route "Dark Souls 3 Game Progress Route") pages. Also, check out the [Secrets](/Secrets "Dark Souls 3 Secrets") page to find hidden [Items](/Items "Dark Souls 3 Items") and [Illusory Walls](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls"). See [Painted World of Ariandel](/Painted+World+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Painted World of Ariandel") for the location included in the [DLC](/DLC "Dark Souls 3 DLC"), [Ashes of Ariandel](/Ashes+of+Ariandel "Dark Souls 3 Ashes of Ariandel").
