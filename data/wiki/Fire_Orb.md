# Fire Orb

**Fire Orb** is a [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a Pyromancy you must use a [Flame](/Flames "Dark Souls 3 Flames") or Special Weapons that can cast [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies").

## Fire Orb  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  Pyromancy  
**Focus Cost** | 14  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 8 Faith/ 8 Int  
**Type** | Ranged Attack  
  
> "The signature flame-manipulating spell common to pyromancers. Hurls a giant fire orb.  
>   
> The blazing fire orb explodes on impact, dealing fire damage to the surrounding area.  
>   
> They say that once a pyromancer learns this spell, their understanding of the flame deepens, together with a yearning for it.

###  How to Get Fire Orb in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from: [Cornyx of the Great Swamp](/Cornyx+of+the+Great+Swamp "Dark Souls 3 Cornyx of the Great Swamp") for 3000 souls after giving him [Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome](/Great+Swamp+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Great Swamp Pyromancy Tome")

### Dark Souls 3 Fire Orb Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (spell buff x 2.25) damage on a direct hit and (spell buff x 2.10) damage with the blast.
  * The projectile is fairly slow with a very wide arc that can cover some distance if aimed properly.
  * This spell is an upgrade to [Fireball](/Fireball "Dark Souls 3 Fireball").
