# Greatswords

Greatswords are a high damage, long reach weapon class. Plan to put a decent amount of points into [Strength](/Strength "Dark Souls 3 Strength") if you plan to use these weapons. They have various weapon [Skills](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") ranging from [Stomp](/Stomp "Dark Souls 3 Stomp") to [Stance](/Stance "Dark Souls 3 Stance") to unique Skills.

In terms of damage output per stamina expended, Greatswords are easily the best value weapon category, and they are a good choice for PvE builds as a result. Things to keep in mind when using these in PvP:

  1. They have less range compared to [Spears](/Spears+&+Pikes "Dark Souls 3 Spears & Pikes")/[Halberds](/Halberds "Dark Souls 3 Halberds"), [Greataxes](/Greataxes "Dark Souls 3 Greataxes"), and [Ultra Greatswords](/Ultra+Greatswords "Dark Souls 3 Ultra Greatswords").
  2. The startup to their attacks is markedly slower compared to [Straight Swords](/Straight+Swords "Dark Souls 3 Straight Swords"), [Axes](/Axes "Dark Souls 3 Axes"), and other smaller weapons.
  3. Despite the visual being a thrusting attack, the weapon art heavy deals standard damage (slash for the Flamberge) unless the Greatsword has a thrusting neutral heavy.
