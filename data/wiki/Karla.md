**Karla** is an [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")**. Karla is a merchant in Dark Souls 3 who specializes in selling dark pyromancies, dark sorceries, and dark miracles. She can first be encountered inside a locked cell on the ground floor of Irithyll Dungeon, located next to the main group of Jailers. To free her, you will need to retrieve the cell key, which can be found elsewhere in the dungeon. Once you have the key, return to her cell and unlock it to set her free. After being freed, Karla will relocate to Firelink Shrine. You can find her in an alcove under the stairs to the left of Andre, the blacksmith.

### Karla Information Dark Souls 3

  * Teaches the player Dark [Pyromancies](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies"), [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") and [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").
  * Accepts [Quelana Pyromancy Tome](/Quelana+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Quelana Pyromancy Tome") and the [Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome](/Grave+Warden+Pyromancy+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome"), and hesitantly takes the [Deep Braille Divine Tome](/Deep+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Deep Braille Divine Tome") and [Londor Braille Divine Tome](/Londor+Braille+Divine+Tome "Dark Souls 3 Londor Braille Divine Tome") if the player asks her twice.
  * Killable.
  * Dies if you die talking to her in [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") before being sent to Firelink.
  * Will complain if you give her 2 Pyromancy Tomes in a row, saying that you only bring her Pyromancy Tomes.

### Karla NPC Dark Souls 3 Location

  * Located in a locked cell on the ground floor of [Irithyll Dungeon,](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon") next to the main [Jailer](/Jailer "Dark Souls 3 Jailer") group.
  * The key to her cell is the [Jailer's Key Ring](/Jailer's+Key+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Jailer's Key Ring") and can be found in the [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Dark Souls 3 Profaned Capital"). Within the toxic swamp with the [Sewer Centipedes](/Sewer+Centipede "Dark Souls 3 Sewer Centipede"), go up a ladder and stick to the left side of the roof until you come to some steps, follow the path and the key should be there.
  * Once she is sent back to [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"), you can find her in an alcove under the stairs to the left of [Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre").

### Karla Dark Souls 3Trivia

  * Karla is voiced by Jenny Funnel, who has previously voiced Yuria the Witch (Demon's Souls), Quelana of Izalith (Dark Souls), real Iosefka and Vileblood Queen Analise (Bloodborne)
  * Karla's character appears to be a reference to Yuria from Demon's souls, both characters are found imprisoned, and use dark magic channeled from emotion, both share the same voice actress.
  * Her item and ashes description imply that she is a Child of Dark much like Nashandra, Elana, Nadalia, and Alsanna.
  * Her name is derived from the Anglo-Saxon word "churl" or Old High German "karal" which originally meant "man" and later "commoner", which may be picked as a reference to her "dark" nature connected with humanity in contrast to gods who are referred to, at the least, as royalty or nobility.
