# Faith

## Faith  
  
---  
Type | [Attribute](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats")  
Effect | _" Attribute required to cast miracles and pyromancies. Increases spell potency._  
_(Pyromancy is affected by both faith and intelligence.)__Dark defense is also calculated from this attribute. "_  
  
**Faith** is a [stats](/Stats "Dark Souls 3 Stats") in **Dark Souls 3**. This attribute is required to cast miracles and pyromancies and also boosts Dark Defense.

### Faith Effects

  * For Faith scaling weapons, damage has a soft cap at 40 Faith, a second one at 60, and stops at 99 Faith.
  * Standard [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") effectiveness is governed by Faith.
  * Effectiveness of Dark miracles cast with dark catalysts is governed by Faith and Intelligence (Faith is more effective).
  * [Pyromancy](/Pyromancies "Dark Souls 3 Pyromancies") effectiveness is governed by Faith and Intelligence. 
    * With asymmetrical stats (i.e. 20 INT 40 FTH), Faith provides slightly more damage than intelligence. Otherwise, it is more effective to level both together.
  * Soft caps: 
    * Miracle: 40-45-60 depending on catalyst.
    * Pyromancy: 40 (if using pyromancy flame). They always use intelligence too.
    * Dark spells: When coupled with a dark catalyst, Intelligence is counted too, and the softcap is 45.
  * For each point of Faith you receive 1.1 Dark Defense, 0.4 in other defenses, and 0.2 in all resistances.
  * A character requires 45 Faith to cast every Miracle in the game.
