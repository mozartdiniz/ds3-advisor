**Irithyll Straight Sword** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.   
  
  
  

> Straight sword bestowed upon the Outrider Knights of the Boreal Valley. This weapon is enshrouded in frost, and causes frostbite.  
>   
> Every Outrider Knight one day devolves into a beast, constantly hounded by Pontiff Sulyvahn's black eyes.  
>   
> Skill: Stance  
> While in stance, use normal attack to break a foe's guard from below, and strong attack to slash upwards with a forward lunge.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Cannot be Infused or Buffed.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Being a standard straight sword, an effective combo for PvP is R1+R1+R2+R2. While it doesn't true combo, the delay of the first R2 lines up with the duration of a medium roll, letting the attack connect outside of the opponent's i-frames.
  * All weapons have motion values assigned to each attack, which are modifiers to AR that are associated with the attack being used. 
    * The Irithyll Straight Sword has a motion value of 200 on backstab, 250 on riposte, and 60+225 on [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring") riposte. The motion value of a backstab with a straight sword does not change with [Hornet Ring](/Hornet+Ring "Dark Souls 3 Hornet Ring").

  * The Irithyll Straight Sword has a range of 35.5063xl on the 1h R1 and 35.0193xl on the 2h R1.
  * The Irithyll Straight Sword has thrusting R2 attacks when 1h.
  * Causes [Frostbite](/Frostbite "Dark Souls 3 Frostbite")  

    * Does not scale with Luck or Upgrade level
  * The weapon is powerful at low levels due to its high base damage and frostbite, but can be used effectively later on due to its long reach.

### Softcaps & Scaling:

  * Irithyll Straightsword +5 
    * 1 Handed Stats: 
    * 12/14 = **253 AR** = Base stats
    * 12/38 =**302 AR** = Dex softcap
    * 39/14 =**335 AR** = Str softcap
    * 39/38 = **384 AR =** Overall softcap
    * 40/40 =**388 AR**
  * After Softcaps it gets less than 2 AR per level
  * Quality Weapon 
    * Scales well in both stats
    * Gains more from Strength than Dexterity

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Outrider Knight](/Boreal+Outrider+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Boreal Outrider Knight") in the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement") zone, located at the bottom of the elevator shaft that hosts [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") just before the [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices").

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit
