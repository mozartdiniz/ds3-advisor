**Sorcerer Set** is an [Armor](/Armor "Dark Souls 3 Armor") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  
  

> Attire of a sorcerer from the Vinheim Dragon School. Long, ash-colored robes.
> 
> Underneath is a deep blue sorcerer's coat; conventional uniform of the academy.

  
  
  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Starting gear for the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer")
  * Looted from a corpse in the castle ruin where you can summon [Eygon](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"). Jump into the hole on the ground next to [Eygon's](/Eygon+of+Carim "Dark Souls 3 Eygon of Carim") summon sign._*note: you must be embered to see summon signs._ Also beware the [Lycanthrope](/Lycanthrope "Dark Souls 3 Lycanthrope").
  * [Video Location](https://youtu.be/iZdeJfTOiIU?t=20m6s "Video Location") (Fextralife Video starting at 20m 6s)

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Characters of any sex wearing select helms will put the hood of the coat up while wearing the Sorcerer Robe.
