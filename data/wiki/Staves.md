# Staves

**Staves** are wielded in order to cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries"). Casting Sorceries consumes Focus Points. Their [Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") buffs the power of cast spells for a short duration.
