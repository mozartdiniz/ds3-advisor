# Heavy Soul Arrow

## Heavy Soul Arrow  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 11  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirments | 13 Intelligence  
  
**Soul Arrow** is a [Sorcery](/Sorcery "Dark Souls 3 Sorcery") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Elementary, yet practical sorcery from long ago.   
> Fires a heavy soul arrow.   
> Offers greater power but slower casting time, entailing a vulnerability that makes this sorcery trickier to handle.

### How to Get Heavy Soul Arrow in Dark Souls 3

  * Starting spell of the [Sorcerer](/Sorcerer "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer") class.
  * Bought from: [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") or [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 2,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Heavy Soul Arrow Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Heavy Soul Arrow inflicts (Spell Buff x 2.15) magic damage before resistances.
  * Arguably the most efficient sorcery in the game in terms of damage per FP cost, having a greater efficiency than all the other soul arrows. However, it has a fairly slow casting speed, making it poor for harassment and combat. 
    * Due to the slow cast speed, it's best used at range to lure enemies and finish foes before they can reach you.
  * Has an upgrade in [Great Heavy Soul Arrow](/Great+Heavy+Soul+Arrow "Dark Souls 3 Great Heavy Soul Arrow").
