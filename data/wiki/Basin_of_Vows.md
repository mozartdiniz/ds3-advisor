# Basin of Vows

**Basin of Vows** is a [Key Item](/Key+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Basin of Vows  
  
---  
  

> Chalice used in old ceremony in which Lothric knights take their vows.  
>   
> It is only a formality now, but it remains as an empty practice.  
>   
> Place this basin at a statue of a beheading knight.  
  
### Basin of Vows Usage

  * Place on the altar at the cathedral of the [Way of Blue](/Way+of+Blue) to access Lothric Castle. Once placed, a cutscene will activate and the [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley) will appear for her boss fight. Note: If acquired the conventional way from [Emma](/Emma), the boss fight will start and the Basin must be placed under the statue afterwards to drop the ladder down.

### Basin of Vows Locations

  * Dropped from [Emma](/Emma) on the [High Wall of Lothric](/High+Wall+of+Lothric).
  * Given to the player by [Emma](/Emma) if she is still alive and the player successfully defeats the three lords of cinder, [Yhorm the Giant](/Yhorm+the+Giant), [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods) and the [Abyss Watchers](/Abyss+Watchers)

### Notes

  * If the player wishes, they can kill [Emma](/Emma) upon meeting her and place the basin on the statue without proceeding to the [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley) boss fight and the rest of the game. This will cause the [Dancer of the Boreal Valley](/Dancer+of+the+Boreal+Valley) to appear and her boss fight to occur. As you can imagine, this will be very difficult as the player will not be very strong so early, but it is possible to proceed to [Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle) without fighting [Vordt of the Boreal Valley](/Vordt+of+the+Boreal+Valley) and reaching the [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement).
