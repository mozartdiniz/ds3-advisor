# Homeward Bone

**Homeward Bone** is a [Consumable](/Consumables "Dark Souls 3 Consumables") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Homeward Bone  
  
---  
  

> Bone fragment reduced to white ash.  
> Return to last bonfire used for resting, or to the shrine bonfire. Bonfires are sustained by bones of the Undead.  
> In rare cases, their previous owner's strong urge to seek bonfires enchant their bones with a homeward instinct.  
  
### Homeward Bone Usage

  * Use to teleport the player to Firelink Shrine or to the last bonfire the player rested at.
  * Drop at the [crow nest](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") for the [Iron Bracelets](/Iron+Bracelets "Dark Souls 3 Iron Bracelets") and "Call Over" [Gesture](/Gestures "Dark Souls 3 Gestures").

### Homeward Bone Locations

  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid") for 500 souls.  
  

  * 5x Found in [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine"): 
    * 1x at the end of the cliff path straight ahead of the door, after you defeat [Iudex Gundyr](/Iudex+Gundyr "Dark Souls 3 Iudex Gundyr").
    * 1x behind some coffins, at the end of the ledge path, above the shrine entrance.
    * 3x on the lower roof of the shrine. Jump up to it from the tree or buy the [Tower Key](/Tower+Key "Dark Souls 3 Tower Key").
  * 8x found at [Undead Settlement](/Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Undead Settlement"):

    * 2x on the roof of a building that is reached by heading right from the burning tree with undead worshipping it. Cross the bridge, and at the end of the bridge, there will be a few barrels and boxes to your right. Behind them will be a small path along the side of the hill that leads to this house. ([Video](https://youtu.be/e8J2lYZFUiw?t=2h11m48s))
    * 1x beside a tilted wagon near the [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon"), near the top of the sniper tower.
    * 2x on top of a tower after area with [Fire Demon](/Demon "Dark Souls 3 Demon").
    * 1x gift from [Holy Knight Hodrick](/Holy+Knight+Hodrick "Dark Souls 3 Holy Knight Hodrick"), or can be picked up from his corpse, in the pit of hollows.
  * 2x found at [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices"): On a corpse hanging over the railing, past the ladder that leads down to Farron Keep.
  * 2x found at [Cathehdral of the Deep](/Cathedral+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Cathedral of the Deep"): On a balcony outside, behind the big blue door next to the first chained-Giant.
  * 2x found at [Farron Keep](/Farron+Keep "Dark Souls 3 Farron Keep"): Behind the "third" extinguishable fire, the fire guarded by the elder ghru.
  * 2x found at [Smouldering Lake](/Smouldering+Lake "Dark Souls 3 Smouldering Lake"): Before to the bone-wheel enemies near the giant ballista. ([Video](https://youtu.be/V_Q4e7W_5L8?t=1h40m42s))
  * 4x found at [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley"): 
    * 1x on a corpse near the end of the bridge, in front of the barrier.
    * 3x located behind a large tombstone. Starting at the Church of Yorshka bonfire, go down the hallway stairs, and go right along the cliff.([Video](https://youtu.be/mpMrxkgTv9M?t=44m40s))
  * 2x found at [Irithyll Dungeon](/Irithyll+Dungeon "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll Dungeon"): In the outside area before the room with the estus shard mimic.
  * 3x found at [Grand Archives](/Grand+Archives "Dark Souls 3 Grand Archives"): On a corpse hanging over the railing on the second floor balcony.
  * 2x found at [Archdragon Peak](/Archdragon+Peak "Dark Souls 3 Archdragon Peak"): On a corpse near the elevator shortcut, at the start of the level.

### Notes

  * Same effect as the [Homeward](/Homeward "Dark Souls 3 Homeward") miracle and the [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment").
  * The [Darksign](/Darksign "Dark Souls 3 Darksign") also has the same effect, but at the additional cost of all your current souls.
  * If you light a bonfire without resting then use the bone, it teleports you to the lit bonfire
