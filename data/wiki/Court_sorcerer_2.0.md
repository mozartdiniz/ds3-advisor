## Build Strategy

How to play this build

The point of this build is to be able to play like a true sorcerer without using any weapons. As a mage you'll be weak to physical attacks, make sure to evade a lot and keep your distance so you can cast a lot of spells, while out of immediate combat. If an ennemie come close use black flame to either kill them or force them to back away. As a main attack use black fire orb for trash, homing souls mass to keep ennemies on their toes, crystal soul spear for anything harder to kill, hidden body is your best friend in PvE if you need to hide and last but not least use warmth instead of your estus potions as much as you can so you're not too much estus dependant.
