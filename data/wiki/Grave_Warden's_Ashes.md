# Grave Warden's Ashes

**Grave Warden's Ashes** is an [Ash](/Ashes) in Dark Souls 3.

### Grave Warden's Ashes  
  
---  
  

> Umbral ash of the grave warden of the catacombs of Carthus. With this, the shrine handmaid will prepare new items.  
>   
> The old man began as a squire, carrying his master's accoutrements. He ended his days a grave warden, and carried them still.  
  
### Grave Warden's Ashes Usage

  * Makes [Yellow Bug Pellet](/Yellow+Bug+Pellet "Yellow Bug Pellet"), [Kukri](/Kukri "Kukri"), [Carthus Rouge](/Carthus+Rouge "Carthus Rouge"), and [Titanite Shards](/Titanite+Shard "Titanite Shards") available for sale from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid).
  * Also adds 3 [Embers](/Ember "Embers") to her stock.

###  Grave Warden's Ashes Locations

  * From the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus) bonfire, take the first left. When in the sewers, take another left. From here, go right into the first open door you see. Take the stairs up, and then make a u-turn towards the back wall. The ashes will be on a corpse.
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/McZS8wmoK4Q "Video location")
