# Sun Princess Ring

### Sun Princess Ring  
  
---  
  

> Ring associated with Gwynevere, princess of sunlight and eldest daughter of Gwyn, the First Lord.   
>    
> The ring is vaguely warm, like a beam of sunlight, and gradually restores HP.   
>    
> Gwynevere left her home with a great many other deities, and became a wife and mother, raising several heavenly children.  
  
**Sun Princess Ring** is a [Ring](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") in Dark Souls 3.

### Sun Princess Ring Effect

  * Gradually restores HP (2 per second)
  * Weight: 0.6
  * Stacks with the [Ancient Dragon Greatshield](/Ancient+Dragon+Greatshield "Dark Souls 3 Ancient Dragon Greatshield"), which also heals 2 HP/second.

### Where to Find Sun Princess Ring

  * Found at Gwynevere's Chamber after [Aldrich's](/Aldrich%2C%2BDevourer%2Bof%2BGods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich%2C%2BDevourer%2Bof%2BGods") boss room in [Anor Londo](/Anor+Londo "Dark Souls 3 Anor Londo"). ()

### Sun Princess Ring Notes

  * Can be equiped/unequiped for fast hp regeneration.
  * This ring previously appeared, albeit with a slightly different appearance and effect,   
in the original  _Dark Souls_ as the [Ring of the Sun Princess](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Ring+of+the+Sun+Princess "Dark Souls 3 Ring of the Sun Princess").
  * This ring is found in front of the bed on which [Gwynevere](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Gwynevere "Dark Souls 3 Gwynevere") lays in  _Dark Souls_.
