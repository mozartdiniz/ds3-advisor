# Hollow Priest

## Hollow Priest  
  
---  
**Enemy Type** | Humanoid Hollow  
**Weakness** | Hollowslayer Greatsword  
**Resistances** | ??  
**Immune** | ??  
  
**Hollow Priests** (Aka Lothric Priest) are [enemies](/Enemies "Dark Souls 3 Enemies") in **Dark Souls 3**.

### Hollow Priests Enemy Description

  * Hollows that wear a long tattered robe with a hood.
  * Wields a [Straight Sword](/Straight+Sword "Dark Souls 3 Straight Sword") and a [Priest's Chime](/Priest's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Priest's Chime").
  * Some of them appear to be praying or sitting before they've been aggroed, similar to other Hollows.
  * Accompanied by [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight").

###  Hollow Priests Location

[Lothric Castle](/Lothric+Castle "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Castle")

  * South of the **Lothric Castle Bonfire.**

###  Hollow Priests Drops

**Item** | **Quantity** | **Probability** | **Luck**  
---|---|---|---  
[Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") | 1 | 0.5% | Yes  
  
### Hollow Priests Combat Information

  * Casts something similar to [Sacred Oath](/Sacred+Oath "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Oath") constantly.
  * Casts [Great Heal](/Great+Heal "Dark Souls 3 Great Heal") when an ally is damaged or when there are no enemies close by.
  * Recklessly swings its sword 6 times in a flurry whenever an enemy is very close to them.
  * They will attempt to get within range of their allies before casting their [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").
  * Slowly strafes around enemies rather than attacking them.
  * Can be lured?
  * Can be Rapported. When done, they can also buff and heal you.

### Hollow Priests Notes & Trivia

  * Their armor doesn't appear to be available to players.
  * They don't drop any items.
    * Low chance to drop [Blessed Gem](/Blessed+Gem "Dark Souls 3 Blessed Gem") (tested with 2 priests near first bonfire in Lothric Castle)
