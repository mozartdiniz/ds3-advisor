# Blooming Purple Moss Clump

**Blooming Purple Moss Clump** is a [Consumable](/Consumables) in Dark Souls 3.

### Blooming Purple Moss Clump  
  
---  
  

> Potently medicinal moss clump with a flower.  
>   
> Reduces poison and toxin. Cures ailments.  
>   
> A toxin is a more vicious form of poison which quickly leads to death.Moss clumps without blooms are useless against toxin, and those who neglect to carry this flowered variety are simply courting an early demise.  
  
### Item Usage

  * Reduces [poison](/poison "poison") and [toxin](/Toxic "toxin") build-up. Cures poison and toxin status.

### Item Locations

  * Sold by [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid) for 1000 souls. (After giving her [Excrement-Covered Ashes](/Excrement-Covered+Ashes))
  * 3x Found in [Profaned Capital](/Profaned+Capital "Profaned Capital"), on a plank walkway overlooking the toxic swamp.
  * Dropped by [Lesser Crab](/Lesser+Crab "Lesser Crab") in [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Catacombs of Carthus"). (very rare)
