*Why so much strength and only 15 dexterity (minimum dex for Gundyr's Halberd)?

The answer is quite simple. The Gundyr's Halberd has B scaling on strength and only E scaling on dexterity, making the dex AR minimal. Here's some examples of how the AR would be lower:

  1. STR 60 + DEX 15 = 481 AR
  2. STR 55 + DEX 20 = 479 AR
  3. STR 50 + DEX 25 = 478 AR
  4. STR 45 + DEX 30 = 476 AR

### About the Gear

On this build you are free to choose any piece of gear that fits you, unless the 70% weight is exceeded. I recommend to use at least one piece of the [Armor of Thorns Set](/Armor+of+Thorns+Set "Armor of Thorns Set"), since the roll damage can make a little poise damage and free you from tight spaces (You don't have to wear all set, just one piece gives the damage. Having more pieces gives more damage). I'm currently using the [Lucatiel Mask](/Lucatiel+Mask "Lucatiel Mask"), [Armor of Thorns](/Armor+of+Thorns "Armor of Thorns"), [Dark Gauntlets](/Dark+Gauntlets "Dark Gauntlets") and [Dark Leggings](/Dark+Leggings "Dark Leggings"), but there are no reason to use this equipment otherwise that I like it.

### Explaining the Weapons and Rings

  * Gundyr's Halberd

Well, for start I'll explain why I'm using Gundyr's Halberd. First of all, halberds in general have a nice range, high damage and quite fast attacks, they can be used to strike multiple enemies (behind each other or surrounding you) and do both horizontal and vertical attacks. The Gundyr's Halberd has a [Weapon Skill](/Skills "Weapon Skill") called [Champion's Charge](/Champion's+Charge "Champion's Charge"), similiar to [Charge](/Charge "Charge ") but with one big difference, you can engage a strong attack on the end of the charge, making the combo dangerous (you charge into ones direction then make a spin). Other than that, Gundyr's Halberd is one of the highest AR halberds (481 without buffs on this build).

  * Target Shield

The Simple Target Shield has two functions, regenerate FP, so you can use Gundyr's skill (Only takes 1 FP to use), and parry attacks. I used the Target Shield on this build because it's one of the best parry itens on the game, having an extended parry animation (more parry frames) making the parry easier. (**NOTE** : some weapons and movements can't be parried, for more information see [here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FNHEH4qgtiZz6xj5PVx0SoI8QfDha9lxkkjGWGOASmQ/edit#gid=0 "here").)

  * Hornet Ring

This ring shouldn't sound strange on a PvP and parry build, since it raises your critical damages in 30%. Not much more to be explained.

Hornet Ring can be replaced by [Dragonscale Ring](/Dragonscale+Ring "Dragonscale Ring") (that reduces backstab damage by 30%).

  * Chloranthy Ring

The Chloranthy Ring raises your stamina recover, making you be able to dodge a lot and have stamina enough for several attacks, or even a back stab. We can't always count with parrying.

The Green Blossom also raises your stamina recover, making a perfect combination with the Chloranthy Ring. (**NOTE** : you can also use the [Grass Crest Shield](/Grass+Crest+Shield "Grass Crest Shield") on that combo, but remember you can't infuse it with Simple Gem.)

  * Ring of Favor

This ring is just broken. It gives you 5% more health (stacking with Life Ring), 10,5% more stamina (giving you more dodges, more attacks and more parries) and also gives you 7% more weight limit. Now the question, why the Ring of Favor and not the [Prisioner's Chain](/Prisoner's+Chain "Prisioner's Chain")? Simple, Prisioner's Chain = more damage (10%) = death, and no one likes to die, I mean, no one at all. (NOTE: this ring is extremely necessary with my gear since the weight of my gear exceed the limit and with the Ring of Favor +2 you will be at 68,31%, if you copy my gear please, wear that ring or havel's).

  * Life Ring

Well, the Life Ring +3 raises your maximum HP on 10%, with the Ring of Favor it stacks 15%, making your maximum HP, with 40 vigor, as invader of 1401 and as embered host of f*cking 1821, that's a lot as hell. The reason why you need so much HP is that there are PvP builds that focuse on kill you with one hit, being it a critical or a Weapon Skill (I did once 1350 on a backstab with Dragonslayer Greataxe). Even if you find someone that can hit kill you it's gonna be one in a hundred. 

### Gameplay

  * Getting Started

I recommend you to start with the [Warrior Class](/Assassin "assassin class"), since you won't need any attunement, faith or intelligence. But you can start with any class since the core itens are only available on end game. If you are not very acquainted with parrying try to practice a little bit (you can do it on common mobs or even on Iudex Gundyr), you are gonna lose some time here but it is going to be worth later. After Iudx Gundyr don't by the halberds that the Shrine Handmaid sell, you are going to buy [Glaive](/Glaive "Glaive") from [Greirat](/Greirat+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Greirat") after you send him to pillage the Undead Settlement. I also don't recommend you to spend you shards on estoc, since you are not going to need the soon. The Glaive is going to be the weapon you are going to use until you have the Gundyr's Halberd. (**NOTE** : remember to get the caestus from the Undead Settlement Sewer, under the Cliff Underside Bonfire).

  * Getting to Untended Graves

To get the [Untended Graves](/Untended+Graves "Untended Graves") (area where Gundyr is the boss) you have to kill the Dancer and Oceiros, usually this can only be done after you kill 3 Cinder Lords, but you can rush that by killing [Emma](/Emma "Emma") (the old lady that gives you the Small Lothric Banner and the Way of Blue covenant), by doing that you will face the Dancer (a tough enemy). Kill it if you are an experienced player of use the mas [Sword Master](/Sword+Master "Sword Master") glitch (if you don't know what it is keep reading). Once you kill it go upstairs and go to the left area, it is going to be the [Consumed King's Garden](/Consumed+King's+Garden "Consumed King's Garden"), there beat [Oceiros](/Oceiros,+the+Consumed+King "Oceiros") and hit the fake wall behind a chest to find the Untended Graves.

  * Sword Master Glitch (Still working at 2016-05-29 10:56:19 PM)

To use the Sword Master glitch you have to follow some steps:

  1. Kill him at Firelink Shrine. Can be a tough fight so try to push him from the cliff.
  2. Summon him at the entrance to the Vordt boss fight. (**NOTE:** Vordt must be alive to summon Sword Master)
  3. Kill Emma, trigger the boss fight and let Sword Master die. After that quit the game and enter it again.
  4. Once you enter summon him again and let him kill the Dancer. Be patient it can take a while.

**NOTE** : Remember that even if you kill the dancer you still have to kill Oceiros and Gundyr, so don't expect to get his weapon that early.
