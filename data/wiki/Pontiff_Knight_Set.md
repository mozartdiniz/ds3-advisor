### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by the [Pontiff Knights](/Pontiff+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Pontiff Knight") in [Irithyll of the Boreal Valley](/Irithyll+of+the+Boreal+Valley "Dark Souls 3 Irithyll of the Boreal Valley").

  
  
  
  

### Notes and Tips

  * Can be farmed in NG : From the Central Irithyll bonfire, go up the stairs where three Pontiff Knights are patrolling. It is possible to fight them one by one. First, stand still at the top of the stairs until the first patrolling Knight can see you and he will either rush on you or cast a spell and then rush. Once he's dead, run to the second Knight hidden at the top of a small staircase on the right and you can kill it before it reacts. The third Knight should have aggroed you at this point, but he will be casting spells into the railing of the staircase you're standing on. Kill the third one and go back to the bonfire.
