**Heysel Pick** is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in **Dark Souls 3**.

> Choice weapon of Yellowfinger Heysel, a Finger of Rosaria and xanthous scholar.  
>   
> It`s both a weapon and sorcery staff. The gold specks are remnants of lost sorceries.  
>   
> Skill: Steady Chant  
> For a short moment, boost the strength of sorceries.

### How to Get Heysel Pick in Dark Souls 3

  * Dropped by invading dark spirit [Yellowfinger Heysel](/Yellowfinger+Heysel "Dark Souls 3 Yellowfinger Heysel") in [Road of Sacrifices](/Road+of+Sacrifices "Dark Souls 3 Road of Sacrifices").
  * [Video location](https://youtu.be/ZKR1kW4LFQ4?t=43m58s "Video location") (Fextralife video starting at 43m 58s)
  * Alternatively, will drop from the worm-like enemy in Rosaria's room if it appears.

### Dark Souls 3 Heysel Pick Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Cannot be Infused but can be Buffed by using Steady Chant.
  * Reinforced with [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite").
  * Functions as a weapon and a catalyst for [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").
  * At 60 INT it has a spellbuff of 208 when fully upgraded.
  * Due to doubling as a catalyst, this weapon has no heavy attacks, but can perform a jumping attack.
  * Using Steady Chant both increases the damage of the spells and the weapon itself.
  * Can true combo into [Soul Greatsword](/Soul+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Soul Greatsword") or [Old Moonlight](/Old+Moonlight "Dark Souls 3 Old Moonlight"), provided the player's cast speed is fast enough.
  * Also combos into Great Soul Arrow, Farron Flashsword, and Great Farron Dart, but not anything equivalent to or slower than Heavy Soul Arrow.
  * Can technically combo into Deep Soul and Great Deep Soul, but you need to stand close.
  * [Heysel Pick complete moveset](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MExvDSH1og4 "heysel pick moveset")

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit
