# Human Dregs

**Human Dregs** is a [Multiplayer Item](/Multiplayer+Items) in Dark Souls 3.

### Human Dregs  
  
---  
  

> Proof of a duty fulfilled by the Aldrich Faithful, who patiently await the Devourer of Gods' return.  
>   
> Dregs are the heaviest things within the human body, and will sink to the lowest depths imaginable   
  
### Human Dregs Usage

  * Covenant item for ranking up in the [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful)

### Human Dregs Locations

  * Rare drop from the [Devout of the Deep](/Devout+of+the+Deep " Devout of the Deep") (1%, 2% from tall deacons)
  * Very rare drop from [Murkmen ](/Murkman)in the [Dreg Heap](/Dreg+Heap) (0.3%, 1% from summoners)
  * Behind an illusory wall in the tower underneath the stairs, across the courtyard of the sleeping [Giant Slave](/Giant+Slave "Giant Slave") enemies in Anor Londo. Down the ladder behind the illusory wall are two [Sulyvahn's Beast](/Sulyvahn's+Beast "Sulyvahn's Beast") enemies. At the center of this room is a [Human Dreg](/Human+Dreg "Human Dreg") and in the corner is [Archdeacon McDonnell](/Archdeacon+McDonnell "Archdeacon McDonnell").
  * ([Online](/Online "Online") Use) Obtained as a reward upon successfully defeating a trespasser while in the [Aldrich Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Aldrich Faithful") covenant.

### Notes

  1. These are easiest to obtain via drops by taking the elevator up from the [Pontiff Sulyvhan](/Pontiff+Sulyvahn "Pontiff Sulyvhan") bonfire, as there are nine [Devout of the Deep](/Devout+of+the+Deep "Devout of the Deep") along the courtyard overlook.
  2. Alternate route: From Aldrich, Devourer of Gods bonfire, after defeating him you can backtrack to the entrance of room where three Devout of the Deep are facing backwards. 

### Trivia

  * Human Dregs are speculated to be the same thing as [Humanity](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Humanity\(Consumable\) "Humanity") in previous parts of the game series.
