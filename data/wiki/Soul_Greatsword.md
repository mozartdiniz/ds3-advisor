# Soul Greatsword

## Soul Greatsword  
  
---  
**Spell Type** |  [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries")  
**Focus Cost** | 23  
**Slots Used** | 1  
Requirements | 22 Intelligence  
  
**Soul Greatsword** is a [Sorcery](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries") in Dark Souls 3. To cast a sorcery, you must use a [Staff](/Staves "Dark Souls 3 Staves") or Special Weapons that can cast [Sorceries](/Sorceries "Dark Souls 3 Sorceries").

> Sorcery developed primarily for sorcerer-swordsmen.   
> Attacks with a greatsword formed from souls.   
>   
> The ephemeral blade only exists as an extension of the caster, but its power is said to rival that of physical greatswords. Even the most obstinate magic purists may resort to this spell in times of crisis.

### How to Get Soul Greatsword in Dark Souls 3

  * Bought from [Yoel of Londor](/Yoel+of+Londor "Dark Souls 3 Yoel of Londor") or [Orbeck of Vinheim](/Orbeck+of+Vinheim "Dark Souls 3 Orbeck of Vinheim") for 5,000 souls

### Dark Souls 3 Soul Greatsword Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Soul Greatsword inflicts (Spell Buff x 2) magic damage before resistances.
  * At max cast speed (50 dex), this spell can true combo out of a two-handed [Heysel pick](/Heysel+pick "Dark Souls 3 Heysel pick") R1. It might also combo out of a one-handed L1 (needs testing).
  * Has wide horizontal range, making it good for crowd control.
  * Fairly easy to dodge if the opponent sees the attack coming.
  * It pairs decently with [Farron Flashsword](/Farron+Flashsword "Dark Souls 3 Farron Flashsword"), as both spells add to a staff's moveset. Furthermore, since Flashsword can skip its cast animation so easily, it may combo cleanly out of Greatsword (needs testing)
