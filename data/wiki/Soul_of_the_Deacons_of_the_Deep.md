# Soul of the Deacons of the Deep

**Soul of the Deacons of the Deep** is a [Boss Soul](/Boss+Souls) in Dark Souls 3.

### Soul of the Deacons of the Deep  
  
---  
  

> Soul of the Deacons of the Deep. One of the twisted souls, steeped in strength.   
>   
> Use it to acquire many souls, or transpose to extract its true strength.   
>   
> After Aldrich left for the Boreal valley, Archdeacon Royce remained in the cathedral with the high priests, to keep eternal watch over their master's coffin.  
  
### Soul of the Deacons of the Deep Usage

  * Can be consumed for 20000 Souls
  * Can be used to make [Deep Soul](/Deep+Soul) or [Cleric's Candlestick](/Cleric's+Candlestick)

### Soul of the Deacons of the Deep Locations

  * Drops from [Deacons of the Deep](/Deacons+of+the+Deep)

### Notes/Trivia

  * This soul, along with Aldrich's, are the only souls that have a dark blue color, possibly showing the effect the Deep has had on them.
