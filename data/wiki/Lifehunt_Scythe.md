# Lifehunt Scythe

**Lifehunt Scythe** is a [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles") in **Dark Souls 3**. To cast Miracles you must use a [Chime](/Sacred+Chimes "Dark Souls 3 Sacred Chimes"), [Talisman](/Talismans "Dark Souls 3 Talismans"), or Special Weapons that can cast [Miracles](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles").

## Lifehunt Scythe  
  
---  
**Spell Type** | 

#### [Miracle](/Miracles "Dark Souls 3 Miracles")  
  
**Focus Cost** | 20 (25*)  
**Slots Used** | 1  
**Requirements** | 22 Faith  
**Type** | Melee Attack  
  
> Miracle of Aldrich, Devourer of Gods.  
>   
> Steals HP of foes using an illusory scythe.  
>   
> Aldrich dreamt as he slowly devoured the God of the Darkmoon. In this dream, he perceived the form of a young, pale girl in hiding. 

### How to Get Lifehunt Scythe in Dark Souls 3

  * [Soul Transposition](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") of [Soul of Aldrich](/Soul+of+Aldrich "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Aldrich") for 5,000 Souls.

### Dark Souls 3 Lifehunt Scythe Notes, Tips & Trivia

  * Deals (Spell Buff _x_ 2.0) [Dark Damage](/Dark+Damage "Dark Souls 3 Dark Damage").
  * This spell will be more powerful when cast with a Dark catalyst such as the [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman"), or [Caitha's Chime](/Caitha's+Chime "Dark Souls 3 Caitha's Chime") with high [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence") and [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith").
  * When cast with any other talisman or chime, and/or on a character with high [Faith](/Faith "Dark Souls 3 Faith") and no [Intelligence](/Intelligence "Dark Souls 3 Intelligence"), it will deal less damage.
  * ~1.2 seconds to cast at max casting speed.
  * The swing has short, yet wide range, and can hit through walls.
  * (v. 1.13) Health recovered is now determined by spell buff. For example, Yorshka's Chime +10 with 60 faith will restore 215 health for each target hit. Catalysts which scale with both Faith and Intelligence, such as Caitha's Chime, only take into account the faith scaling when determining the healing aspect of this miracle.
  * Casting Lifehunt Scythe costs a minimum of 20 FP regardless of which hand it is cast from. (Casting from the left hand will drain up to **25 FP** , but still only requires 20 as stated previously. If the player only has 20 FP, they may still cast the miracle from the left hand). (In both cases, casting with [Unfaltering Prayer](/Unfaltering+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Unfaltering Prayer") will incur its normal FP cost).
  * The miracle is best used when attacking multiple enemies at once to maximize health gained.
  * Due to its slow startup animation, it is recommended to cast Lifehunt Scythe using a talisman with [Unfaltering Prayer](/Unfaltering+Prayer "Dark Souls 3 Unfaltering Prayer"), preferably the [Sunless Talisman](/Sunless+Talisman "Dark Souls 3 Sunless Talisman"). This makes it effective as a tool to punish aggressive players by means of trading blows.

  * This is a reference to the [Lifehunt Scythe](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lifehunt+Scythe "Dark Souls 3 Lifehunt Scythe") obtained by transforming the soul of [Crossbreed Priscilla](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Crossbreed+Priscilla "Dark Souls 3 Crossbreed Priscilla") in DS1.
  * This miracle emulates the attack used by [Aldrich, Devourer of Gods](/Aldrich,+Devourer+of+Gods "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich, Devourer of Gods"), though striking with only one slash instead of spinning around for a consecutive swipe.
