This is a player-created **Build** for Dark souls 3.

  * Build Name: Masked Mutineer
  * Build Level: 120
  * Build Focus: PvE or PvP
  * Build Main [Stat](/Stats): Dex (and sort of a quality build)

### Build Equipment

  * **Right Hand** : Sharp Crow Quills +10 (DLC Item From Ashes Of Ariandel)
  * **Left Hand** : Witchtree Branch, Silver Knight Sheild
  * **Head** : Silver Mask
  * **Chest** : Wolf Knight Armor
  * **Hands** : Ringed Knight Gauntlets
  * **Legs** : Ringed Knight Leggings
  * **Rings** : Havel's Ring +3, Ring Of Favor +3, Lingering Dragoncrest Ring, Hunter's Ring
  * **Items** : Dual Charm
  * **Sorceries** : Great Magic Weapon

## Build Strategy

This build is meant to be played as a quick attacking build. It is not a go in there type build, but rather an opportunity based build. The weapon art of the Crow Quills is very handy especially against any spellcasting enemies, follow up with a strong for a powerful lunge attack. The Great Magic Weapon is to be used on the sword at the beginning of a battle. This is a quick class with low poise, be careful about heavy hitters. NOTES:

  * The Crow Quills is a very fast hitter, can be good to stun lock foes, however, it is a thrusting sword so you can't just go in attacking or you will most likely miss, and very likely be pounded to dust.
  * Use the weapon art to stop low poise spell casts etc.
  * There is a lot of leeway in this build, so feel free to change it up how you like!

Get out there and have fun!
