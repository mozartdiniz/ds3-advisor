# Secrets

**Secrets** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** highlight the hidden treasures and mysteries scattered throughout the game. These include rare items, weapons, armor, and elusive locations that are often difficult to discover. This page provides a comprehensive list of all the secrets that can be found and unlocked.

## Dark Souls 3 Secrets Guide

### Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls Guide

**[Illusory Walls](/Illusory+Walls "Dark Souls 3 Illusory Walls")** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki")** are concealed walls that hide secrets such as hidden rooms, treasures, or pathways. To reveal them, the host of the world must either strike the wall with an attack or roll into it. Note that spirits, whether friendly or hostile, cannot interact with or uncover illusory walls.

### Dark Souls 3 Rare Items Guide

**Rare Items** in **[Dark Souls 3](/Dark+Souls+3+Wiki "Dark Souls 3 Wiki") **are weapons, consumables, and other items that are easily missable or can only be obtained once per playthrough.

All [boss soul items](/Boss+Soul+Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Boss Soul Weapons") may only be obtained once per playthrough ([see this page](/Soul+Transposition "Dark Souls 3 Soul Transposition") for a chart organized by boss). A nearly complete set of them can be obtained after two playthroughs. However, the [Soul of the Nameless King](/Soul+of+the+Nameless+King "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Nameless King") can be traded for three items, so that extends the timeline to three playthroughs to obtain all items. Furthermore, the [Twin Princes' Greatsword](/Twin+Princes'+Greatsword "Dark Souls 3 Twin Princes' Greatsword") requires two playthroughs to complete, as it is obtained by fusing Lothric and [Lorian's](/Lorian,+Elder+Prince "Dark Souls 3 Lorian, Elder Prince") swords, each of which must be transposed from the [Soul of the Twin Princes](/Soul+of+the+Twin+Princes "Dark Souls 3 Soul of the Twin Princes"). Also, there is one non-boss soul which may be transposed by [Ludleth](/Ludleth+of+Courland "Dark Souls 3 Ludleth of Courland") \- the [Soul of Rosaria](/Soul+of+Rosaria "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Rosaria"), used to obtain the [Bountiful Sunlight](/Bountiful+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Bountiful Sunlight") miracle. Rosaria's soul may only be obtained by completing [Leonhard's questline](/Leonhard "Dark Souls 3 Leonhard"). While the [Catarina Set](/Catarina+Set "Dark Souls 3 Catarina Set") is not that hard to get, it can only be acquired once during various steps of the [Siegward of Catarina](/Siegward+of+Catarina "Dark Souls 3 Siegward of Catarina") questline.

There are numerous items that can only be obtained by [trading with the crow](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow"). The items that are exclusive to the crow are: the entire [Armor of the Sun set](/Armor+of+the+Sun+Set "Dark Souls 3 Armor of the Sun Set") (including the [Sunlight Shield](/Sunlight+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Shield")), [Lucatiel's Mask](/Lucatiel's+Mask "Dark Souls 3 Lucatiel's Mask"), the [Porcine Shield](/Porcine+Shield "Dark Souls 3 Porcine Shield"), and all five Carvings. The crow only makes each trade once per playthrough, so these items each become one per playthrough.

All of the [covenent rewards](/Covenants#rewards "Dark Souls 3 Covenants#rewards") can only be obtained by leveling up in the corresponding covenant.

Almost all rings can only be obtained once, except for a few which can be purchased or found in multiple locations. This is also made more complicated by the presence of +1, +2, and +3 rings, most of which are only available in NG+ and NG++. Please consult [the list of rings](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") for details on all rings' availability.

The following list includes one-time weapons and armor, as well as notably rare drops. While most starting equipment can be purchased, some starting items can only be obtained by finding them in specific places (unless the player started in the corresponding class). These are included in the list as well.
