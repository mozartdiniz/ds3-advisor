# Steady Chant

## Steady Chant  
  
---  
  
##   
  
> Boost the strength of sorceries for a very short period. Works while equipped in either hand.  
  
| Varies  
  
**Steady Chant** is a [Skill](/Skills "Dark Souls 3 Skills") in Dark Souls 3.

### Weapons That Have Steady Chant

  * [Heretic's Staff](/Heretic's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Heretic's Staff")
  * [Court Sorcerer's Staff](/Court+Sorcerer's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Court Sorcerer's Staff")
  * [Heysel Pick](/Heysel+Pick "Dark Souls 3 Heysel Pick")
  * [Sage's Crystal Staff](/Sage's+Crystal+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sage's Crystal Staff")
  * [Izalith Staff](/Izalith+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Izalith Staff")
  * [Witchtree Branch](/Witchtree+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Witchtree Branch")
  * [Golden Ritual Spear](/Golden+Ritual+Spear "Dark Souls 3 Golden Ritual Spear")
  * [Archdeacon Great Staff  
](/Archdeacon+Great+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Archdeacon Great Staff")
  * [Sorcerer's Staff](/Sorcerer's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Sorcerer's Staff")
  * [Man-Grub's Staff](/Man-Grub's+Staff "Dark Souls 3 Man-Grub's Staff")

### Steady Chant Notes & Information

  * For most staves, boosts spell damage by 10%. For the Sage's Crystal Staff, it boosts spell damage by 30%.
  * [Weapon Buff](/Buff+Stacking "Dark Souls 3 Buff Stacking") that affects the catalyst used.
  * Steady Chant also boosts the staff's melee damage by 10%.
  * Does **not** extend buff duration (like [Magic Weapon ](/Magic+Weapon "Dark Souls 3 Magic Weapon") or [Hidden Body](/Hidden+Body "Dark Souls 3 Hidden Body")).
  * Lasts about 16 seconds.
