Falchion is a [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3.   
  
  
  

> Standard curved sword and widely-used slashing weapon.  
>   
> The falchion's flesh-rending slash attacks are greatly compromised against armor and tough scale-covered hides.  
>   
> Skill: Spin Slash  
> Slice into foes with a large spinning motion, and continue spinning to transition into strong attack.

  
  

### Notes and Tips:

  * Can be [Infused](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") and Buffed. 
  * Reinforced using [Titanite](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades").
  * Has the highest AR (338) when infused with Raw of all the curved swords.

### Softcaps and Scaling

  * 10/16 = 274 = Base Stats
  * 10/40 = 352 = Dex Softcap
  * 40/16 = 327 = Strength Softcap
  * 40/40 = 405 = Combined Softcap
  * This weapon receives irregular strength scaling, ranging from 1-3 per level until the soft cap.

  
  

### Location/Where to Find

  * Dropped by [Falchion skeletons](/Minor+Skeleton "Dark Souls 3 Minor Skeleton") in the [Catacombs of Carthus](/Catacombs+of+Carthus "Dark Souls 3 Catacombs of Carthus").
  * Starting from the [High Lord Wolnir](/High+Lord+Wolnir "Dark Souls 3 High Lord Wolnir") bonfire, head straight out the entrance of the boss room and backtrack through the area and across the rickety bridge (this will not work if the bridge is in its ladder form) and down into the gathering of awakening skeletons. There are many Falchion Skeletons here that are farmable.
  * An easier grinding spot is from the Abandoned Tomb bonfire. Backtrack and target the two skeletons/pile of bones closest to the stairway. They will reanimate, so use a blessed weapon. The rest of the skeletons in this room have Scimitars.
  * Drops from Falchion skeletons in Undead Settlement sewers, near Velka Statue. Requires Grave Key

**Parameter Bonus** : Strength, Dexterity,Magic, Fire, Lightning and Dark bonuses - The scaling multiplier applied to the [Attack: stat]. Scaling quality is from highest to lowest as follows: S/A/B/C/D/E.The higher the player's [Str, Dex, Mag, Fire, Light] stat, the higher the [Attack Bonus: Stat] is (found on the player status screen). The higher the scaling letter, the higher the percent multiplier applied to the [Attack: Stat].This resulting bonus damage is added to the base physical damage of the weapon and is shown in the equipment screen in blue numbers as a "+ X".   
**Durability :** The weapon's HP, when the durability hits 0, the effectiveness of its attacks become weakened to the point of almost uselessness. When an items durability is low, a message will come up saying "Weapon At Risk!" at this point the weapon does not perform at it's best.  
**Weight :** How much the item weights when equipped.   
**Stability :** How well the player keeps stance after being hit  
**Attack Type :** Defines what kind of swing set the weapon has: Regular(R), Thrust(T), Slash(Sl), Strike(St)
