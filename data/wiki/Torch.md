_"A simple, makeshift torch made from an oily rag wrapped around the end of a stick._

_Torches such as these are the easiest way to illuminate one's surroundings, but their use comes at the cost of occupying a hand that would otherwise be free._

_Can be used as a rudimentary weapon to inflict fire damage. It possesses no Skill, being an ordinary, basic tool."_

### **Location/Where to Find**

  * Purchasable from [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Shrine Handmaid") for 300 Souls

### **Hints and Tips:**

  * Holding the guard button with the torch in the left hand will raise the torch and increase its brightness.
  * While held up in this way the torch can be used to reveal the irithyllian servants that turn invisible in Anor Londo and The Boreal Valley
  * Equipping the torch while afflicted wih leeches destroys them, preventing further bleed build up.
  * The torch cannot perform backstabs, ripostes or plunging attacks.
  * Upgrades with regular Titanite.
  * CANNOT be infused or buffed
