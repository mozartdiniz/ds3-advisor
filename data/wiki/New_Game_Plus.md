# New Game Plus

**New Game Plus** is a feature of **Dark Souls 3** , wherein once the [Final Boss](/Soul+of+Cinder "Dark Souls 3 Soul of Cinder") is defeated, players have the option of replaying the game with their current stats and gear. After the credits, players are presented with an option to head straight into the next playthrough; if they opt not to, they can start the next playthrough later, from the [Firelink Shrine](/Firelink+Shrine "Dark Souls 3 Firelink Shrine") bonfire. The consecutive playthroughs bring new challenges as enemies have increased health and damage, and there are new [Souls](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") and [Rings](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings") hidden for players to find in order to continue developing their character.

### Changes with Game Plus

  * Enemies have more hit points, defenses, resistances, and damage. In NG+2 through 7 enemies also receive less poise damage.
  * Access to upgraded [Rings](/Rings "Dark Souls 3 Rings"): +1 for NG+ and +2/+3 for NG++. (base rings also still obtainable)
  * [Soul ](/Souls "Dark Souls 3 Souls") pickups are changed to higher level versions which give more souls when consumed or sold.
  * From NG+3, the only changes are enemy health, defense, resistance, damage, stamina damage, poise damage received, and the souls gained for killing enemies and NPC's. There are an unlimited number of NG+, but enemies stop getting stronger after NG+7.

  * NG+ is variable based on the location of the game the enemy is in and NG+2 through NG+7 multipliers use NG+ as a base.

### What carries over to New Game Plus

  * [Covenant](/Covenants "Dark Souls 3 Covenants") rank status/all covenants and covenant rewards.
  * All your current stats, weapons, armor, and consumables. (including the [Coiled Sword Fragment](/Coiled+Sword+Fragment "Dark Souls 3 Coiled Sword Fragment"))
  * [Estus ](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") and [Bonfire Upgrades](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard").
  * [Hollow](/Hollowing "Dark Souls 3 Hollowing") level.
  * Friendship with [Giant of the Undead Settlement](/Giant+of+the+Undead+Settlement "Dark Souls 3 Giant of the Undead Settlement"). (if you kept the unique [Young White Branch](/Young+White+Branch "Dark Souls 3 Young White Branch") he gave you)
  * [Undead Match](/Hollow+Arena "Dark Souls 3 Hollow Arena").

### What doesn't carry over to New Game Plus

  * All [Key Items](/Key+Items "Dark Souls 3 Key Items"). This includes [Dark Sigils](/Dark+Sigil "Dark Souls 3 Dark Sigil"), Tomes, Sorcery Scrolls and [Ashes](/Ashes "Dark Souls 3 Ashes") (and their respective vendor ware unlocks). With the exception of [Champion's Bones](/Champion's+Bones "Dark Souls 3 Champion's Bones").
  * All [Coals](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") reset.
  * Unlocked shortcuts.
  * Discovered [Bonfires](/Bonfires "Dark Souls 3 Bonfires").
  * All [NPC](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") states. (i.e. NPC quest progression resets, dead NPCs come back to life, NPC invaders will invade again, etc.)
  * Sky changes. (no more black sun and red clouds)
  * [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow") trade state - all trades are reset except for the head carvings, of which you can only have one of each.

### To-do Checklist before New Game Plus

  * Obtain all [Titanite Slabs](/Titanite+Slab "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Slab") \- there are fifteen (eight in the base game, three in the Ashes of Ariandel DLC, and four in The Ringed City DLC) available per playthrough so it is highly recommended to get as many as possible per run.
  * Trade everything you want to trade with [Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum](/Pickle+Pee,+Pump-a-Rum+Crow "Dark Souls 3 Pickle Pee, Pump-a-Rum Crow").
  * Kill off all [Crystal Lizards](/Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Crystal Lizard") and [Ravenous Crystal Lizards](/Ravenous+Crystal+Lizard "Dark Souls 3 Ravenous Crystal Lizard").
  * Collect all [Estus Shards](/Estus+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Estus Shard") and [Undead Bone Shards](/Undead+Bone+Shard "Dark Souls 3 Undead Bone Shard").
  * Collect all available [Rings](/rings "Dark Souls 3 rings") (note - all lower versions of rings are still available in all subsequent playthroughs, so don't worry if you missed any).
  * Defeat all [Bosses, and Mini-Bosses](/Bosses "Dark Souls 3 Bosses").
  * Clear out the harder areas, like the Ringed City, to bring up your skills.
  * Buy any [Ore and Gems](/Upgrade+Materials "Dark Souls 3 Upgrade Materials") that you need from upgraded vendors. It's recommended to stock up on [Titanite Chunks](/Titanite+Chunk "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Chunk"), [Twinkling Titanite](/Twinkling+Titanite "Dark Souls 3 Twinkling Titanite"), and [Titanite Scales](/Titanite+Scale "Dark Souls 3 Titanite Scale"), as these will not be available until late into your next playthrough.
  * [Infuse](/Infusion "Dark Souls 3 Infusion") your weapons and [Upgrade](/Upgrades "Dark Souls 3 Upgrades") them as much as required.
  * Kill off [NPCs](/NPCs "Dark Souls 3 NPCs") to obtain their equipment and souls. Note that the [Shrine Handmaid](/Shrine+Handmaid "Dark Souls 3 Shrine Handmaid"), [Blacksmith Andre](/Blacksmith+Andre "Dark Souls 3 Blacksmith Andre"), and the [Fire Keeper](/Fire+Keeper "Dark Souls 3 Fire Keeper") can be killed, but they will respawn without dropping any souls or items. There are no benefits to killing them, only drawbacks - killing the Handmaid will only increase the price of her wares by 20%, and Andre will refuse to help you anymore. The Fire Keeper doesn't change.
  * Grind your [Covenant Items](/Multiplayer+Items "Dark Souls 3 Multiplayer Items"), for Achievements/Trophies, in either your first journey (since it is easier to defeat the enemies), or during New Game +2 (to have the best [Item Discovery](/Item+Discovery "Dark Souls 3 Item Discovery") possible). The [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers") covenant should be accessed with caution, because any offerings to Rosaria will stop [Sirris'](/Sirris+of+the+Sunless+Realms "Dark Souls 3 Sirris of the Sunless Realms") questline and, by extension, lock off access to the [Mound-Maker](/Mound-Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Maker") covenant if you missed the first way. Here are the required items for getting all covenant rewards:  

    * 30 [Sunlight Medals](/Sunlight+Medal "Dark Souls 3 Sunlight Medal") for [Warrior of Sunlight](/Warrior+of+Sunlight "Dark Souls 3 Warrior of Sunlight"). These may be farmed from [Lothric Knights](/Lothric+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Lothric Knight").
    * 30 [Proof of a Concord Kepts](/Proof+of+a+Concord+Kept "Dark Souls 3 Proof of a Concord Kept") for [Blade of the Darkmoon](/Blades+of+the+Darkmoon "Dark Souls 3 Blades of the Darkmoon"). These may be farmed from [Silver Knights](/Silver+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Silver Knight").
    * 30 [Vertebrae Shackles](/Vertebra+Shackle "Dark Souls 3 Vertebra Shackle") for [Mound-Maker](/Mound-Maker "Dark Souls 3 Mound-Maker"). These may be farmed from [Carthus Swordsmen](/Carthus+Swordsman+Skeleton "Dark Souls 3 Carthus Swordsman Skeleton").
    * 30 [Human Dregs](/Human+Dregs "Dark Souls 3 Human Dregs") for [Aldritch Faithful](/Aldrich+Faithful "Dark Souls 3 Aldrich Faithful"). These may be farmed from [Devout of the Deep](/Devout+of+the+Deep "Dark Souls 3 Devout of the Deep").(10 needed for the Achievements/Trophies)
    * 30 [Wolf's Blood Swordgrasses](/Wolf's+Blood+Swordgrass "Dark Souls 3 Wolf's Blood Swordgrass") for [Watchdogs of Farron](/Watchdogs+of+Farron "Dark Souls 3 Watchdogs of Farron"). These may be farmed from [Ghru Grunts](/Ghru+Grunt "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Grunt") and [Ghru Shaman](/Ghru+Shaman "Dark Souls 3 Ghru Shaman").
    * 30 [Pale Tongues](/Pale+Tongue "Dark Souls 3 Pale Tongue") for [Rosaria's Fingers](/Rosaria's+Fingers "Dark Souls 3 Rosaria's Fingers"). These may be farmed from [Darkwraiths](/Darkwraith "Dark Souls 3 Darkwraith").(10 needed for the Achievements/Trophies)
    * 30 [Filanore's Spear Ornament](/Filanore's+Spear+Ornament "Dark Souls 3 Filanore's Spear Ornament") for [Spears of the Church](/Spears+of+the+Church "Dark Souls 3 Spears of the Church") (DLC Ringed City). These may be farmed from [Ringed Knights](/Ringed+Knight "Dark Souls 3 Ringed Knight").
