# Thrusting Swords

**Thrusting Swords** are a type of [Weapon](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") in Dark Souls 3. These [Weapons](/Weapons "Dark Souls 3 Weapons") usually do little to moderate damage per swing, but attack rather quickly, have good reach, and are capable of punishing counter-attacks. Many can also attack from behind a raised shield. Thrusting Swords replace the kick with a quick retreating attack that does minor damage.
